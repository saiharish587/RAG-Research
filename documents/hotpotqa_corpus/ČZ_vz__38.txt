ČZ vz. 38: The vz.  38 is a Semi-automatic pistol manufactured from 1939 until 1945 chambered in .380 ACP (in Europe called 9 × 17 mm Browning Short).  The barrel is attached to the frame by a hinge, allowing for very easy disassembly.  Certain aspects of the pistol are covered by Czechoslovakian patent 65558 which may also be found as Finish patent FI18533(A) from 1939.
