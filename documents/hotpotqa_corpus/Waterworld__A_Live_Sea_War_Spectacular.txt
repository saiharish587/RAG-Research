Waterworld: A Live Sea War Spectacular: Waterworld: A Live Sea War Spectacular is an attraction based on the 1995 film "Waterworld" found at Universal Studios Hollywood (1995), Universal Studios Japan (2001), and Universal Studios Singapore (2010).  The original attraction opened at the same time as the film.  Although the film was considered a critical and financial disappointment, the show was highly praised, winning a 1996 Thea Award from the Themed Entertainment Association.  The attraction remains highly rated by park guests.
