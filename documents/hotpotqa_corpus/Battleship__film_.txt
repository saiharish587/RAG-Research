Battleship (film): Battleship is a 2012 American military science fiction action film loosely based on the board game of the same name.  The film was directed by Peter Berg and starred Taylor Kitsch, Rihanna, Tadanobu Asano, Alexander Skarsgård, and Liam Neeson.  Filming took place in Hawaii and on the USS "Missouri".  In the film, a fleet of ships are forced to do battle with an armada of extraterrestrial origin in order to thwart their destructive goals.
