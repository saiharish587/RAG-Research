Kiskiack (Lee House): Kiskiack (Lee House) is the name of an early 17th-century brick building, originally built as a private residence, which still stands at the Naval Weapons Station Yorktown in York County, Virginia.  This brick structure, the oldest building owned by the U.S. Navy, is listed on the National Register of Historic Places.  It was named for the historic Kiskiack, an Algonquian-speaking tribe of the Powhatan Confederacy, who occupied this area at the time of English colonization.
