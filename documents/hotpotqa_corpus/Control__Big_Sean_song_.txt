Control (Big Sean song): "Control" is a song by American hip hop recording artist Big Sean, featuring fellow American rappers Kendrick Lamar and Jay Electronica.  The song was originally intended to be included on Big Sean's second studio album "Hall of Fame" (2013), but was ultimately removed from the final track-listing due to sample clearance issues.  "Control" impacted American mainstream urban radio on August 14, 2013 as a promotional single for "Hall of Fame".  The track contains samples such as "Where I'm From" (1997) by Jay-Z, "El Pueblo Unido Jamás Será Vencido" (1974) by Quilapayún and Sergio Ortega and an interpolation of "Get Bizy" (2011) by Terrace Martin, which also features fellow rappers Kendrick Lamar and Jay Electronica.
