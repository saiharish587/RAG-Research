Pelham Road: Pelham Road, also commonly referred to as Shore Road, is a historic 4.1-mile east-west arterial road that runs along the Long Island Sound shoreline through the southern Westchester County, New York communities of New Rochelle, Pelham Manor, and the Pelham Bay Park section of the Bronx in neighboring New York City.  The thoroughfare had its beginning as an Indian trail linking the important Indian villages on Davenport Neck to those on Pelham Neck in Pelham.  Between these points along the shore line there was an almost continuous chain of small Indian villages and camps.  This waterfront area was especially advantageous, with many small coves in secure harbors and protected by adjacent islands and many small streams of water and abundant springs.
