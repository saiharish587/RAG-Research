St. Mary of Blachernae Church, Berat: St. Mary of Blachernae Church (Albanian: "Kisha e Shën Mëri Vllahernës" ) is a church inside Berat Castle, in Berat, Albania, named after the famous Church of St. Mary of Blachernae, near the Palace of Blachernae in Constantinople.  It dates from the 13th century and contains 16th-century murals.  It became a protected cultural monument in 1948.
