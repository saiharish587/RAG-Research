Halmstad: Halmstad ] (Danish: "Halmsted" ) is a port, university, industrial and recreational city at the mouth of Nissan in the province of Halland on the Swedish west coast.  Halmstad is the seat of Halmstad Municipality and the capital of Halland County.  The city had a population of 92,797 in 2012, out of a municipal total of over 90,000 (18th most populous - 2012).  Halmstad is Sweden's 20th-largest city by population and located about midway between Gothenburg (the second most populous) and Malmö (the third).  It is Europe's northernmost city with a lot of timber framing architecture.
