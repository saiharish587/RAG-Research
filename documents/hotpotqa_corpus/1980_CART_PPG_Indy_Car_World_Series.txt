1980 CART PPG Indy Car World Series: The 1980 CART PPG Indy Car World Series season was the second in the CART era of U.S. open-wheel racing.  It consisted of twelve races, beginning in Ontario, California on April 13 and concluding in Avondale, Arizona on November 8.  The PPG Indy Car World Series Drivers' Champion and Indianapolis 500 winner was Johnny Rutherford.  Rookie of the Year was Dennis Firestone.  The entire season, including the 64th Indianapolis 500, was to be co-sanctioned by both the USAC and CART under the banner of the Championship Racing League (CRL).  However, USAC withdrew from the arrangement after five races.
