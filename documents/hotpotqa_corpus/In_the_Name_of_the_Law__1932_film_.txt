In the Name of the Law (1932 film): In the Name of the Law (French: Au nom de la loi ) is a 1932 French crime film directed by Maurice Tourneur and starring Marcelle Chantal, Régine Dancourt and Gabriel Gabrio.  It was based on a novel by Paul Bringuier.  The film was well received by critics. " Variety" considered Marcelle Chantal's performance "her best so far in talkers".
