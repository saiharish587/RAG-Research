Market towns of Østfold and Akershus counties: The Market towns of Østfold and Akershus counties (Norwegian: "Kjøpstedene i Østfold og Akershus fylker" ) was an electoral district for parliamentary elections in Norway.  It comprised the market towns (Norwegian: "kjøpsteder" ) of Fredrikstad, Halden (until 1928 named Fredrikshald), Moss and Sarpsborg in Østfold county and Drøbak in Akershus county.
