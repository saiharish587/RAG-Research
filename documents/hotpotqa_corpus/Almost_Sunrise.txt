Almost Sunrise: Almost Sunrise is a 2016 American documentary film directed by Michael Collins.  It recounts the story of two Iraq veterans, Tom Voss and Anthony Anderson, who, in an attempt to put their combat experience behind them, embark on a 2,700-mile trek on foot across America.  It made its world premiere on the opening night of the Telluride Mountainfilm Festival on 27 May, 2016.
