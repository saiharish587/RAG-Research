Paul Feig: Paul Samuel Feig ( ; born September 17, 1962) is an American actor, film director, producer, and screenwriter.  He is best known for directing the 2011 film "Bridesmaids", featuring Kristen Wiig and Melissa McCarthy and the 2016 film "Ghostbusters" also starring Wiig and McCarthy.  Feig also directed the comedy films "The Heat" (2013) starring McCarthy and Sandra Bullock, and "Spy" (2015) which stars McCarthy, Jason Statham, and Jude Law.
