Bird strike: A bird strike—sometimes called birdstrike, bird ingestion (for an engine), bird hit, or bird aircraft strike hazard (BASH)—is a collision between an airborne animal (usually a bird or bat) and a manmade vehicle, especially an aircraft.  The term is also used for bird deaths resulting from collisions with structures such as power lines, towers and wind turbines (see Bird-skyscraper collisions and Towerkill).
