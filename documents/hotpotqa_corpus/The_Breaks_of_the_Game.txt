The Breaks of the Game: The Breaks of the Game is a 1981 sports book written by Pulitzer Prize winning reporter David Halberstam about the Portland Trail Blazers' 1979–1980 season.  The Trail Blazers are a professional basketball team which plays in the National Basketball Association (NBA).  Aside from a recap of the Blazers' season, the book attempts to give a detailed history of the NBA, the 1976–77 Portland Trail Blazers championship team, the injuries faced by departed star Bill Walton, and the life of Kermit Washington after his two-month suspension for punching Rudy Tomjanovich.  The book also puts basketball into a social context and contains extensive discussion on race in the NBA.
