Phantasy Star (video game): Phantasy Star (ファンタシースター , Fantashī Sutā ) is the first installment in Sega's renowned series of the same title.  It was released for the Sega Mark III/Master System, in Japan on December 20, 1987 , and then for the Master System in North America and Europe in 1988.  It is considered one of the pioneers amongst role-playing video games, both for its advanced graphics technology, and for being one of the first story-driven games released in the West.  It is also notable for being one of the first games featuring a female protagonist after Ms. Pac-Man.
