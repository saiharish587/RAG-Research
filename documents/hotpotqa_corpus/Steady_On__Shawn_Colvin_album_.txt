Steady On (Shawn Colvin album): Steady On is the 1989 debut album by American singer-songwriter Shawn Colvin.  In addition to launching Colvin's career the album won the award for Best Contemporary Folk Album at the 33rd Grammy Awards in February 1991. " Allmusic" calls the album, "a must have for anyone who loves acoustic music created in the grand tradition of Joni Mitchell and James Taylor."  Notable guest appearances include Suzanne Vega and Bruce Hornsby.
