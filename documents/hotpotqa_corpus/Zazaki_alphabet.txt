Zazaki alphabet: The Zazaki alphabet is writing system of Zaza language which used in history and in use today.  Zazaki alphabet is an alphabet derived from the Latin alphabet used for writing the Zaza language, consisting of 32 letters, seven of which (Ç, Ğ, I, İ, Ü, Ş, and Ê) have been modified from their Latin originals for the phonetic requirements of the language.
