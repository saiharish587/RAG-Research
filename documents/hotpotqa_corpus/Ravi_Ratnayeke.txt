Ravi Ratnayeke: Joseph Ravindran "Ravi" Ratnayeke (born 2 May 1960), is a former Sri Lankan cricketer and ODI captain, who batted left-handed and bowled right-arm medium pace.  Ratnayeke played 22 Tests and 78 ODIs from 1982 to 1990, his Test best bowling performance of eight wickets for 83 runs at Jinnah Stadium (Sialkot) Pakistan was a Sri Lankan Test record at the time, and was also vice captain to Arjuna Ranatunga.
