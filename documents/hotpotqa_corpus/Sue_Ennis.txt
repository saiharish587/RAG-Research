Sue Ennis: Sue Ennis is a Seattle songwriter whose songs include Classic Rock radio staples "Straight On", "Even It Up" and "Dog & Butterfly" by the rock band Heart.  She has co-written over 70 songs with Ann and Nancy Wilson of the band.  and co-wrote the theme from "The Golden Child" starring Eddie Murphy with James Bond composer, John Barry, Ann and Nancy Wilson.  Her songs have also appeared in "Thomas and the Magic Railroad", a feature film from 2000.  In 2004, she and co-writer Hummie Mann won a Soundie award (Puget Sound Advertising Award) for their jingle for State Roofing.
