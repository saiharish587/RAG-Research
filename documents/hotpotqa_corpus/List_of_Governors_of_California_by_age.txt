List of Governors of California by age: The youngest person to become governor was J. Neely Johnson (30 years, 160 days).  The oldest person to become governor is Jerry Brown on January 3, 2011, who became governor at 72 years, 271 days old.  Brown had previously been the sixth-youngest person to become governor in 1975, and the youngest since the 1860s, at 36 years, 275 days old (his third term began 28 years to the day after his second term expired).  The median age of accession is roughly 48 years, which falls between John Bigler (19th youngest) and William Irwin (20th).
