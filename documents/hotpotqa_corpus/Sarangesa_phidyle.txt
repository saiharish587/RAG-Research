Sarangesa phidyle: Sarangesa phidyle, the orange flat or small elfin, is a butterfly of the Hesperiidae family.  It is found from Botswana, south-west Africa, Saudi Arabia, Sudan.  In South Africa it is found from the eastern Cape to Swaziland, KwaZulu-Natal, Mpumalanga and the Limpopo Province, the North West Province and Gauteng.
