ICCREA Banca: ICCREA Group known formerly as Istituto Centrale delle Casse Rurali ed Artigiane, is a central institution of Italian credit unions and rural saving banks.  The holding company of the group was ICCREA Holding (from 1995–2016), while ICCREA Banca, ICCREA BancaImpresa and several companies were subsidiaries .  However, ICCREA Banca revered merger with ICCREA Holding in mid-2016, as part of the banking reform of Italy.  The co-operative banks of Italy would create strong central banks (Cassa Centrale Banca had also applied to be another central bank) with power to recapitalize individual co-operative bank.
