Park Min-ha (actress): Park Min-ha (born July 2, 2007) is a South Korean actress.  Park began her career as a child actress in 2011, and has appeared in such productions as the South Korean disaster film "Flu" (2013), the Korean drama television series "King of Ambition" (2013), and the music video for K.Will's song "Love Blossom" (2013).  Her father is SBS anchorman Park Chan-min.
