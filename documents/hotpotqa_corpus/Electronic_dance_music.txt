Electronic dance music: Electronic dance music (also known as EDM, dance music, club music, or simply dance) is a broad range of percussive electronic music genres made largely for nightclubs, raves, and festivals.  EDM is generally produced for playback by disc jockeys (DJs) who create seamless selections of tracks, called a mix, by segueing from one recording to another.  EDM producers also perform their music live in a concert or festival setting in what is sometimes called a live PA.  In Europe, EDM is more commonly called 'dance music' or simply 'dance'.
