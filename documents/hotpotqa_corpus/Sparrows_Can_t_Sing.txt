Sparrows Can't Sing: Sparrows Can't Sing is a 1963 British film.  Based on a 1960 play, "Sparrers Can't Sing", it was directed by Joan Littlewood and was from a story by Stephen Lewis.  The producer was Donald Taylor and the original music by James Stevens, incidental music was composed by Stanley Black.  The play, also by Stephen Lewis, was first performed at Joan Littlewood's Theatre Workshop in the Theatre Royal Stratford East.
