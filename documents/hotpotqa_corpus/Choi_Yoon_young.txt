Choi Yoon-young: Choi Yoon-young (born September 25, 1986) is a South Korean actress.  After passing the 21st KBS actors' auditions in 2008, Choi began playing supporting roles in the network's dramas, notably in "King of Baking, Kim Takgu" (2010) and "My Daughter Seo-young" (2012).  She then appeared twice on the big screen in 2012: in the short film "Endless Flight" in omnibus "Horror Stories", and the table tennis sports film "As One".
