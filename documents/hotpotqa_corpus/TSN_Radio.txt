TSN Radio: TSN Radio is a semi-national sports radio brand and part-time network in Canada carried on AM radio stations owned by Bell Media Radio.  The TSN Radio brand, and some of the stations' content, is shared with Bell Media's television sports channel, The Sports Network.  With the American sports media company ESPN being a minority shareholder in TSN, most of the stations also air some ESPN Radio programming, usually on weekends and/or overnight.
