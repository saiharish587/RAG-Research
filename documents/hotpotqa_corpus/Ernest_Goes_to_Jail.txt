Ernest Goes to Jail: Ernest Goes to Jail is a 1990 comedy film directed by John R. Cherry III and starring Jim Varney.  It is the fourth film to feature the character Ernest P. Worrell.  It was shot in Nashville and Tennessee State Penitentiary.  This is the second most successful of the Ernest films, behind "Ernest Saves Christmas".  It was in third place during its opening weekend, earning $6,143,372.  Total gross was $25,029,569.
