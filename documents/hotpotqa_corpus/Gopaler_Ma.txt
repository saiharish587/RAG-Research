Gopaler Ma: Gopaler Ma (translation: Mother of Gopala, an epithet for Sri Krishna) (1822 – 8 July 1906) was a devotee and a householder disciple of Sri Ramakrishna, the saint and mystic from Bengal.  Her birth name was Aghoremani Devi, but she came to be known as Gopaler Ma among the devotees of Sri Ramakrishna, owing to her intense motherly love for Sri Ramakrishna as "Gopala" or baby Krishna.  She was famous for her divine visions of Lord Krishna as a baby and her devotion to the ideals of Sri Ramakrishna.  In her later years she was very close to Swami Vivekananda and Sister Nivedita.  She spent the last few years of her life with Sister Nivedita.
