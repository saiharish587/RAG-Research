Partula (gastropod): Partula is a genus of air-breathing tropical land snails, terrestrial pulmonate gastropod mollusks in the family Partulidae.  Many species of "Partula" are known under the general common names "Polynesian tree snail" and "Moorean viviparous tree snail".  Partulids are distributed across 5000 sqmi of Pacific Ocean islands, from the Society Islands to New Guinea.
