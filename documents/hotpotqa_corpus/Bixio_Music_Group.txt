Bixio Music Group: Bixio Music Group, a New York corporation, is the American branch of Gruppo Editoriale Bixio , or Bixio Publishing Group, the first Italian music publishing company.  The Bixio Publishing Group, currently based in Rome, Italy, was established in Naples during the 1920s by composer Cesare Andrea Bixio.  Bixio Music Group, an ASCAP member since 1992, was established to facilitate licensing of the Bixio Publishing Group catalogue that includes not only musical compositions but also sound recordings by related labels under the Group in the territories of North and South America.
