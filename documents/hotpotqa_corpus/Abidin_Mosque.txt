Abidin Mosque: The Abidin Mosque (Malay: "Masjid Abidin") is Terengganu's old state royal mosque built by Sultan Zainal Abidin II between 1793 and 1808.  The mosque, which is also known as the White Mosque or the Big Mosque, is located in Kuala Terengganu, Terengganu, Malaysia.  The old Royal Mausoleum is situated near the mosque.
