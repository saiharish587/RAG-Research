Ub Iwerks: Ubbe Eert "Ub" Iwerks, A.S.C. ( ; March 24, 1901 – July 7, 1971) was an American animator, cartoonist, character designer, inventor, and special effects technician, who created Oswald the Lucky Rabbit and Mickey Mouse with Walt Disney.  The works Iwerks produced alongside Disney went on to win numerous awards, including multiple Academy Awards.
