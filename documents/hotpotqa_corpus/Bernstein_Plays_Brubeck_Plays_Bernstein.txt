Bernstein Plays Brubeck Plays Bernstein: Bernstein Plays Brubeck Plays Bernstein is a 1961 studio album by The Dave Brubeck Quartet.  Its title refers to the fact that it consists of both a Brubeck composition conducted by Leonard Bernstein (though the "Brubeck" there is Howard Brubeck, Dave Brubeck's brother) and Bernstein compositions played by the Dave Brubeck Quartet.  The title is also an echo of Dave Brubeck's 1956 solo debut album, "Brubeck Plays Brubeck".
