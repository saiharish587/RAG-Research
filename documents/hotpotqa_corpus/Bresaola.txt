Bresaola: Bresaola (] ) is air-dried, salted beef (but also horse, venison and pork) that has been aged two or three months until it becomes hard and turns a dark red, almost purple colour.  It is made from top (inside) round, and is lean and tender, with a sweet, musty smell.  It originated in Valtellina, a valley in the Alps of northern Italy's Lombardy region.
