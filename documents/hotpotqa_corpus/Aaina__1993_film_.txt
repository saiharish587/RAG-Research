Aaina (1993 film): Aaina (English: "Mirror" ), is a 1993 film directed by Deepak Sareen and produced by Yash Chopra and independently produced by his wife Pamela Chopra.  The film starred Juhi Chawla, Jackie Shroff and Amrita Singh in lead roles with Deepak Tijori in a supporting role.  The film was a blockbuster hit in India, and solidified Juhi Chawla's career as a leading lady in the 1990s.  The film was remade in Telugu as "Aayanaki Iddaru", in Tamil as "Kalyana Vaibhogam" and in Kannada as "Yare Nee Abhimani".
