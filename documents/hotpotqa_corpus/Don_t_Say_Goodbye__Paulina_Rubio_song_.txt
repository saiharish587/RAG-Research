Don't Say Goodbye (Paulina Rubio song): "Don't Say Goodbye" is a song by Mexican recording artist Paulina Rubio, from her sixth studio album "Border Girl" (2002).  It was written by Cheryl Yie and Joshua 'Gen' Rubin and was produced and arranged by Gen Rubin himself.  "Don't Say Goodbye" was released as the album's first single by Universal Records on April 30, 2002.
