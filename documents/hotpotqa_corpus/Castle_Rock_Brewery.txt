Castle Rock Brewery: Castle Rock Brewery is a brewery and pub group based in Nottingham, Nottinghamshire.  It was originally set up as a joint venture with Bramcote Brewery.  Bramcote Brewery was wound up and a new company was set up, 50% owned by Tynemill and 50% owned by the previous owners of Bramcote Brewery, on a new site next to Tynemill’s pub, The Vat and Fiddle, located on Queensbridge road close to Nottingham railway station.  The Vat and Fiddle became the ‘Brewery Tap’, serving over 10 real ales at any one time, several of which are Castle Rock's.
