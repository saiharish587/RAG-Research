Babe I'm Gonna Leave You: "Babe I'm Gonna Leave You" is a folk song written by Anne Bredon (then known as Anne Johannsen) in the late 1950s.  It was recorded by Joan Baez (credited and became widely popular as "traditional") and released on her 1962 album "Joan Baez in Concert, Part 1"; and by the English rock band Led Zeppelin, who included it on their 1969 debut album "Led Zeppelin".
