Millmerran Power Station: The Millmerran Power Station is a coal-fired power station south of Millmerran in the Darling Downs region of Queensland, Australia.  The power station was commissioned in 2002 and cost $1.5 billion to build.  The power station is owned and operated by InterGen, a multinational company owned by the Ontario Teachers' Pension Plan and AIG Highstar Capital II, a private equity fund sponsored by a subsidiary of American International Group, Inc.
