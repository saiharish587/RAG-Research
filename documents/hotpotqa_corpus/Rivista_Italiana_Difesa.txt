Rivista Italiana Difesa: Rivista Italiana Difesa ("RID"; meaning "Italian Defence Magazine" in English) is an Italian military magazine published by Coop Giornalistica La Riviera, from Chiavari (Liguria).  Founded in 1982, has recently reached 25 years of activity with a silver cover.  The magazine is published eleven times a year.  The field of interests are about military and geostrategical analysis.  Many articles were published with over 10 pages length, among them many aircraft designs analysis (such as MiG-31 in April 1992, Tu-22M in 1995, Su-24 in October 1996, AMX in 1993 and 1998, C-27J in April 2000).  The magazine's former director was Giovanni Lazzari; the present director is Pietro Batacchi.
