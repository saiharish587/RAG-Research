Rocco's Dinner Party: Rocco's Dinner Party is an American reality competition series which premiered on June 15, 2011, on the Bravo cable network.  Each week, three chefs are challenged to craft the perfect dinner party for celebrity chef Rocco DiSpirito and his guests.  The contestants are judged on their cooking, service, and décor.  The winner of each episode wins $20,000 (USD).
