Daburiyya: Daburiyya (Arabic: دبورية‎ ‎ ; Hebrew: דַבּוּרִיָּה‎ ), also "Deburieh" or "Dabburieh", is an Arab village ca. 8 km.  east of Nazareth in Israel's Northern District.  Daburriya gained local council status in 1961.  Its jurisdiction extends over 7,200 dunams.  In 2016 it had a population of 10,039 .
