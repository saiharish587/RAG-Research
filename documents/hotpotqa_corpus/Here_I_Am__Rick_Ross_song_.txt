Here I Am (Rick Ross song): "Here I Am" is the third single from Rick Ross's second album "Trilla".  It features Nelly and Avery Storm.  This song, produced by Drumma Boy, contains an interpolation of Stevie Wonder's "Lately."  Video appearances made are DJ Khaled, Birdman, Pitbull, Dre, Felicia Pearson, Ace Hood and others.  After the song ends, the video cuts to Ross's other song, "Maybach Music".  The single debuted at 99 on the Billboard Hot 100, and has peaked at #41.  The original third single was going to be "Luxury Tax", but it was changed to "Here I Am".
