Brandywine Village Historic District: Brandywine Village Historic District is a national historic district located along Brandywine Creek at Wilmington, New Castle County, Delaware.  It encompasses 12 contributing buildings, 7 contributing sites, and 2 contributing structures.  Brandywine Village developed in the late-18th century as a group of flour mills, the homes of prosperous millers, mill workers, shop keepers and artisans.  Located in the district are a set of mill owner built homes of granite.  Notable buildings include the Gothic Revival style St. John's Episcopal Church (1857-1858) designed by noted Philadelphia architect John Notman, Brandywine Methodist Episcopal Church (1857), and Brandywine Academy (1798).  In 1788, Brandywine Village was the site of the first mechanized mill designed by Oliver Evans.
