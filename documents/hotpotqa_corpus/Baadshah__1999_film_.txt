Baadshah (1999 film): Baadshah (translation: "King") is a 1999 Indian action comedy film directed by Abbas-Mustan.  The film stars Shah Rukh Khan opposite Twinkle Khanna in lead roles.  It was released on 27 August 1999.  Shahrukh Khan earned a Filmfare Award nomination for Best Performance in a Comic Role.  It is inspired from films such as "Nick of Time", "Rush Hour", "If Looks Could Kill", "Mr Nice Guy", "The Mask".
