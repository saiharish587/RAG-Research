Holden: Holden, formally known as General Motors Holden, is an Australian automobile manufacturer with its headquarters in Port Melbourne, Victoria.  The company was founded in 1856 as a saddlery manufacturer in South Australia.  In 1908 it moved into the automotive field, before becoming a subsidiary of the United States-based General Motors (GM) in 1931.  After becoming a subsidiary of GM, the company was named General Motors-Holden's Ltd, becoming Holden Ltd in 1998 and General Motors Holden in 2005.
