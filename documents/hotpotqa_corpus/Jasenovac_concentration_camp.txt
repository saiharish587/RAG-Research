Jasenovac concentration camp: The Jasenovac concentration camp (/Логор Јасеновац, ] ; Yiddish: יאסענאוואץ‎ ) was an extermination camp established in Slavonia by the authorities of the Independent State of Croatia (NDH) during World War II.  The camp was established by the governing Ustaše regime and not operated by Nazi Germany.  It was one of the largest concentration camps in Europe and the camp has been referred to as "the Auschwitz of the Balkans" and "the Yugoslav Auschwitz".
