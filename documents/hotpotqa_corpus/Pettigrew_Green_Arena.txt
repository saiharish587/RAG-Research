Pettigrew Green Arena: Pettigrew Green Arena is a multi-purpose indoor sports and entertainment centre in Taradale near Napier, New Zealand.  The centre opened in April 2003, and regularly hosts volleyball, basketball and netball matches for Hawke's Bay representative teams.  The main court has a capacity of 2,500.  The centre hosted an ANZ Championship match for the Central Pulse against the Adelaide Thunderbirds in Round 14 of the 2008 season.  The Arena is also home to the Hawke's Bay Hawks.
