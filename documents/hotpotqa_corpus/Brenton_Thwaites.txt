Brenton Thwaites: Brenton Thwaites (born 10 August 1989) is an Australian actor who is known for his portrayal of Luke Gallagher in the Fox8 teen drama series "Slide" (2011), and later Stu Henderson in the soap opera "Home and Away" (2011–2012).
