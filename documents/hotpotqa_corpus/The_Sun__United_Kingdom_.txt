The Sun (United Kingdom): The Sun is a tabloid newspaper published in the United Kingdom and Ireland.  Since "The Sun on Sunday" was launched in February 2012, the paper has been a seven-day operation.  As a broadsheet, it was founded in 1964 as a successor to the "Daily Herald"; it became a tabloid in 1969 after it was purchased by its current owners.  It is published by the News Group Newspapers division of News UK, itself a wholly owned subsidiary of Rupert Murdoch's News Corp.
