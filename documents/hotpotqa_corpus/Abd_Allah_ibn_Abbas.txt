Abd Allah ibn Abbas: Abd Allah ibn Abbas (Arabic: عبد الله ابن عباس‎ ‎ ) or ′Abd Allah ibn al-′Abbas otherwise called (Ibn Abbas; Al-Habr; Al-Bahr; The Doctor; The Sea) was born c. 619 CE.  He was the son of Al-‘Abbas ibn ‘Abd al-Muttalib, an uncle of the Islamic prophet Muhammad, and a nephew of the Maymunah bint al-Harith, who later became Muhammad's wife.  He was one of Muhammad's cousins and one of the early Qur'an scholars.
