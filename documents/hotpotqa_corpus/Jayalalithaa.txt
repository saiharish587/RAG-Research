Jayalalithaa: Jayaram Jayalalithaa (born Komalavalli, 24 February 1948 – 5 December 2016) was an Indian actor and politician who served six terms as the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu for over fourteen years between 1991 and 2016.  From 1989 she was the general secretary of the All India Anna Dravida Munnetra Kazhagam (AIADMK), a Dravidian party whose cadre revered her as their "Amma" (mother), "Puratchi Thalaivi" (revolutionary leader) and "Thanga Tharagai" (golden maiden).  Her critics in the media and the opposition accused her of fostering a personality cult, and of demanding absolute loyalty from AIADMK legislators and ministers who often publicly prostrated themselves before her.
