Michael K. Williams: Michael Kenneth Williams (born November 22, 1966) is an American actor, dancer, and reporter.  He is best known for his portrayal of Omar Little on the HBO drama series "The Wire" and Albert "Chalky" White on the HBO series "Boardwalk Empire".  He was also acclaimed for his role as Jack Gee, husband of Bessie Smith, in the HBO telefilm biopic "Bessie".  He has acted in supporting roles in a number of films and television series, including "The Road", "Inherent Vice", "The Night Of", "Gone Baby Gone", and "12 Years a Slave".
