Speed Demos Archive: Speed Demos Archive (commonly known as simply SDA) is a website dedicated to video game speedruns. SDA's primary focus is hosting downloadable, high-quality speedrun videos, and currently has runs of over eleven hundred games, with more being added on a regular basis.  SDA also hosts two annual speedrunning charity marathons, Awesome Games Done Quick and Summer Games Done Quick.  It has so far hosted thirteen marathons, and has raised over $10 million for various charities, with the most successful so far being Awesome Games Done Quick 2017, which raised over $2.2 million for the Prevent Cancer Foundation.
