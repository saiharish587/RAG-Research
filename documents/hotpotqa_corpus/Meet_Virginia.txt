Meet Virginia: "Meet Virginia" is a song written and recorded by American roots rock band Train.  It was released in October 1999 as the second single from their eponymous first album, "Train".  The song reached a peak of number 20 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 chart in January 2000, becoming their first top 20 hit, and their first single to chart on the Hot 100.  It also reached the Top 30 of Billboard's rock charts, Hot Mainstream Rock Tracks (number 21) and Alternative Songs (number 25).
