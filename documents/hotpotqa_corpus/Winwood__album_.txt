Winwood (album): Winwood is the first compilation album of music featuring Steve Winwood.  This two-record set was issued in 1971 by United Artists Records and features music which Winwood performed with The Spencer Davis Group, Powerhouse, Traffic and Blind Faith.  UA Records issued this album after Winwood's band Traffic left UA when their home label Island Records set up their own American operation.  Issued without Winwood's authorization as catalogue number UAS-9950, it was taken off the market after legal action by Winwood and Island Records.  It was then reissued with minor changes as catalogue number UAS-9964.  Currently out of print, it was issued on CD by Universal Music of Japan for the Japanese market.
