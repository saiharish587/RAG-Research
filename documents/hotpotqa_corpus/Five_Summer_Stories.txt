Five Summer Stories: Five Summer Stories is a 1972 surf film by Jim Freeman and Greg MacGillivray, starring David Nuuhiwa, Eddie Aikau, Gerry Lopez, and Sam Hawk.  Its VHS re-release was in 1994, followed by a DVD release.  "The Original Sound Track from Five Summer Stories" was composed and recorded by the Southern California native band, Honk.  The soundtrack was released on LP in 1972 and re-released on CD in 1992.
