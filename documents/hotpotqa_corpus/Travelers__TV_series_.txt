Travelers (TV series): Travelers is a science fiction television series created by Brad Wright, starring Emmy Award-winning actor Eric McCormack.  The series is a co-production between Netflix and Showcase.  The first season comprises 12 episodes and premiered on Showcase on October 17, 2016; the entire series premiered globally (outside of Canada) on Netflix, on December 23, 2016.  On February 8, 2017, Netflix and Showcase renewed the show for a second season.  Season 2 production began in March 2017, ahead of the Canadian premiere on Showcase scheduled to air on October 16, 2017, which will be followed by a Netflix release.
