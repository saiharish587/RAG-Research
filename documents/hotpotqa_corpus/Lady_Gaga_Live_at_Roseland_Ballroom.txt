Lady Gaga Live at Roseland Ballroom: Lady Gaga Live at Roseland Ballroom was the first residency show by American singer Lady Gaga.  Performed at the Roseland Ballroom in Manhattan, New York, the residency show began on March 28 and concluded on April 7, 2014, after completing seven shows.  It was the final event hosted by the venue after it was announced that it was being closed down and being replaced with a 42-story skyscraper.  Gaga revealed that Roseland was the only venue in New York City that she had never played, although she had visited there previously to watch shows.  A poster announcing the event was released, showing an old image of Gaga taken before the time she became successful as a recording artist.
