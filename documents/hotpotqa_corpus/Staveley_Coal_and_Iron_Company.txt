Staveley Coal and Iron Company: The Staveley Coal and Iron Company Limited was an industrial company based in Staveley, near Chesterfield, North Derbyshire.  The company was registered in 1863, appearing in provincial stock exchange reports from 1864.  It exploited local ironstone quarried from land owned by the Duke of Devonshire on the outskirts of the village.  It developed into coal mining, owning several collieries and also into chemical production, first from those available from coal tar distillation, later to cover a wide and diverse range.  Part of the plant at Staveley was a sulphuric acid manufacturing unit making use of the Contact Process.
