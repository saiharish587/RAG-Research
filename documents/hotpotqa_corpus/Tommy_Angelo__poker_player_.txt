Tommy Angelo (poker player): Tommy Angelo (born August 25, 1958) is an Oakland, California professional poker player, writer, and coach.  Angelo was a career musician in the 1980's, performing rock and country on drums and piano.  In 1990, he became a full-time professional poker player.  Since then has since written 100 magazine articles, written and produced 18 poker training videos, and written and published three books on poker.
