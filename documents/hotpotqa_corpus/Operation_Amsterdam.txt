Operation Amsterdam: Operation Amsterdam is a 1959 British action film, directed by Michael McCarthy, and featuring Peter Finch, Eva Bartok and Tony Britton.  It is based on a true story as described in the book "Adventure in Diamonds", by David E Walker.  The action of the story covers a few days in May 1940 when the Germans invaded the Netherlands.  The composer Philip Green composed two original pieces of music for the film, the "Pierement Waltz" and the "Amsterdam Polka".
