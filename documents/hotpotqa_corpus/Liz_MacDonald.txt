Liz MacDonald: Elizabeth MacDonald is the stocks editor for "Fox Business" and "Fox News".  MacDonald also covered the markets, corporate accounting scandals, taxes and the IRS for the "Wall Street Journal" and "Forbes" Magazine, where she created Forbes' top-rated annual ranking, "The World's 100 Most Powerful Women".  MacDonald appears on Fox Business shows Opening Bell with Maria Bartiromo", "Cavuto" and "Varney & Co.", "Fox News'" "Forbes on Fox", "Your World With Neil Cavuto", "Outnumbered", and "Happening Now".
