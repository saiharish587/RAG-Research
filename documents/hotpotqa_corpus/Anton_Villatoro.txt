Anton Villatoro: Anthon Villatoro (born June 10, 1970 in Boulder, Colorado) is a Guatemalan former professional cyclist.  He attended the University of Colorado, where he raced with future US Postal teammate Tyler Hamilton.  Villatoro won the 1991 Junior Tour of Guatemala, a gold medal at the 1994 Central American Games (team time trial) and placed fourth at the 1995 Pan American Games (time trial).  In 1996, he represented Guatemala at the Olympic Games in Atlanta.
