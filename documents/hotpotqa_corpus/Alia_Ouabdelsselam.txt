Alia Ouabdelsselam: Alia Ouabdelsselam (born 20 April 1978) is a French former competitive ice dancer.  She competed for most of her career with Benjamin Delmas.  They teamed up in 1997 and split in 2002.  During their career, they won the 2002 French Figure Skating Championships and placed as high as 13th at the European Figure Skating Championships.
