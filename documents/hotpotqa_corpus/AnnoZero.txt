AnnoZero: AnnoZero is an Italian television talk show hosted by the Italian journalist Michele Santoro and has been broadcast on Rai 2 since 2006.  Beatrice Borromeo was a collaborator on this show from 2006–2008.  Journalist Marco Travaglio is a permanent guest on the show.  The show officially ended its run on 9 June 2011 with a last episode being shown on 28 June.
