Bon Appétit Management Company: The Bon Appétit Management Company is a Palo Alto, California-based on-site restaurant company, owned by Compass Group, that provides café and catering services to corporations, colleges and universities.  The company operates over 500 cafes in 32 states.  Princeton Review has named Bon Appétit the "No. 1 College Food Service in the Country" for several years.
