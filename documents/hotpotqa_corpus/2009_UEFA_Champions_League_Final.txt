2009 UEFA Champions League Final: The 2009 UEFA Champions League Final was played on 27 May 2009 at the Stadio Olimpico in Rome, Italy.  The match determined the winners of the 2008–09 season of the UEFA Champions League, a tournament for the top football clubs in Europe.  The match was won by Barcelona of Spain, who beat England's Manchester United 2–0.  Samuel Eto'o opened the scoring in the 10th minute, and Lionel Messi added another goal 20 minutes from the end to earn Barcelona a historic treble of La Liga, the Copa del Rey and the Champions League, a feat never before achieved by a Spanish club.  The match was refereed by Swiss referee Massimo Busacca.
