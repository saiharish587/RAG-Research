Alex Heffes: Alex Heffes is a British film composer (born 1971 in Beaconsfield, Buckinghamshire).  His film scores include those for the BAFTA-winning "Touching the Void", and Oscar-winning movies "One Day in September", "The Last King of Scotland", and "Inside Job".
