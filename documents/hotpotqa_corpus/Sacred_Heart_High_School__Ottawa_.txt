Sacred Heart High School (Ottawa): Sacred Heart Catholic High School is a secondary school of the Ottawa Catholic School Board in Ottawa, Ontario Canada.  It serves as a middle school and also as a high school, having classes from grades seven to twelve.  The team name is the Sacred Heart Huskies with the colours white, red and black.  Some of Sacred Heart's feeder schools include: Guardian Angels Catholic Elementary School, Holy Spirit Catholic Elementary School, St. Phillips Catholic Elementary School, St. Stephen's Catholic Elementary school, Sittsville Public School and Westwind Public School, among others.  The school is run in the district of John Curry the local trustee of zone 1.  Sacred Heart is the largest high school in Ontario according to student population.
