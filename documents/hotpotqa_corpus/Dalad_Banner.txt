Dalad Banner: Dalad Banner (Mongolian: ᠳᠠᠯᠠᠳ ᠬᠣᠰᠢᠭᠤ Далад қосиу "Dalad qosiɣu"; ) is a banner of western Inner Mongolia, People's Republic of China, lying on the southern (right) bank of the Yellow River.  It is under the administration of Ordos City, although it is closer to the city of Baotou, 33 km to the north-northwest.
