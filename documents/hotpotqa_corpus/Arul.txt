Arul: Arul (English: "Grace") is a 2004 Indian Tamil action family drama film written and directed by Hari starring Vikram, Jyothika, Pasupathy, Vadivelu, Sarath Babu, Vinu Chakravarthy and Vaiyapuri.  The music is composed by Harris Jayaraj.  The story tells how a man is rejected by his family but then finds his way back.  The film opened to mixed reviews from both critics as well as audience.
