Oops (Oh My): "Oops (Oh My)" is a song by American R&B-soul singer–songwriter Tweet, featuring American rapper Missy "Misdemeanor" Elliott.  It was written by Tweet and Elliott and produced by Timbaland for Tweet's debut album, "Southern Hummingbird" (2002).  Released as the album's lead single in February 2002, the song received heavy airplay from U.S. and UK radios, becoming a top ten hit in both countries.  Remixes of the song feature rappers Bubba Sparxxx and Fabolous.
