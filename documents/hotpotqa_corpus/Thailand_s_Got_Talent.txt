Thailand's Got Talent: Thailand's Got Talent ไทยแลนด์ก็อตทาเลนต์, (also known as TGT) is a Thai reality television series on the Channel 3 television network, and part of the global British "Got Talent" series.  It is a talent show that features singers, dancers, sketch artists, comedians and other performers of all ages competing for the advertised top prize of 10,000,000 Baht (approximately $325,000).  The show debuted in March 2011.  Thailand is also the fifth country in Asia to license Got Talent series.  The three judges Nirut Sirijanya, Benz Pomchita Na Songkla, and Pinyo Rutham join hosts Krit Sribhumisret and Ketsepsawat Palagawongse na Ayutthaya.
