Love Me Like You Used To (song): "Love Me Like You Used To" is a song written by Paul Davis and Bobby Emmons, and recorded by American country music artist Johnny Cash for his 1985 studio album "Rainbow".  The song was later recorded by American country music artist Tanya Tucker in 1987.  The song was released in July of that year as the first single and title track from Tucker's album "Love Me Like You Used To".  The song reached number two on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles chart.
