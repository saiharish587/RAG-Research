Silver Lake (Cook County, Minnesota): Silver Lake is a 22 acre lake in Cook County, Minnesota which is tributary to the Poplar River.  Silver Lake is six feet deep and has clarity down to 4.5 feet.  It has one inflow from Bulge Lake, and drains into Rice Lake South of the inflow on the northwest shore, most of the west shore is a brush bog.  Silver Lake has a shoreline of 1.1 miles.
