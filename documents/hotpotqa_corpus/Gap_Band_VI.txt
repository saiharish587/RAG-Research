Gap Band VI: Gap Band VI is the eighth album (contrary to the title) by The Gap Band, released in 1984 on Total Experience Records.  It was originally intended to be lead singer Charlie Wilson's first solo album, and the first Gap Band album released under Total Experience's new distribution deal with RCA Records.  The album reached #1 on the Black Albums chart for 2 weeks in March 1985.  In October 29, 2012 the remastered and expanded album including 5 bonus tracks was released by Big Break Records.
