Sujan Mukhopadhyay: Sujan Mukhopadhyay (also known as Neel Mukherjee) is a Bengali film, television and theatre actor.  In 2012 he made his directorial debut with the film "Ghete Gho".  He is an active Member of the renounced Theatre Group Chetana.  On February 22, 2016 his directorial debut, in the field of Theatre, "Ghashiram Kotwal",which is an adaptation on Marathi play, was premiered.
