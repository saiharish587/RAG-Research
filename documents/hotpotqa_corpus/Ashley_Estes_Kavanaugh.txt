Ashley Estes Kavanaugh: Ashley Estes Kavanaugh served as Personal Secretary to the President for US President George W. Bush between 2001 and 2004.  She had previously served since 1996 as an assistant for Bush during the Bush-Cheney Presidential campaign and his tenure as Governor of Texas.  Both President Bush and the then First Lady attended Kavanaugh's (née Estes) wedding ceremony to Brett Kavanaugh, a federal judge on the United States Court of Appeals for the D.C. Circuit, in Georgetown.
