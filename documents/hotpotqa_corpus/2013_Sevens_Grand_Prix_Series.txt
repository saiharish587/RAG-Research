2013 Sevens Grand Prix Series: The 2013 Sevens Grand Prix Series was the 12th year of the annual rugby Sevens Grand Prix Series (formerly known as the European Sevens Championship) for rugby sevens organised by the FIRA – Association of European Rugby.  The 2013 Series consisted of two tournaments, held in Lyon, France and Bucharest, Romania.  England won both legs, winning the overall 2013 Series.
