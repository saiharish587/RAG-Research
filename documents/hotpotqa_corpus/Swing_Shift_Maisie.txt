Swing Shift Maisie: Swing Shift Maisie (a.k.a. Swing It, Maisie) is a 1943 romantic comedy film directed by Norman Z. McLeod.  It is the seventh in a series of 10 films starring Ann Sothern as Maisie, preceded by "Maisie Gets Her Man" (1942) and followed by "Maisie Goes to Reno" (1944).  Her co-stars are James Craig and Jean Rogers.
