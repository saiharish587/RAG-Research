2014 US Open – Women's Singles: Serena Williams was the two-time defending champion and successfully defended her title, defeating Caroline Wozniacki in the final, 6–3, 6–3.  Williams did not lose a set in the entire tournament and did not lose more than three games in any set.  This was Williams' 18th Grand Slam singles title, tying the number won by Martina Navratilova and Chris Evert.  With the victory, Serena tied the open era record of six US Open titles held by Evert.  This was Williams' third US Open championship that she competed in without losing a set, also tying Evert.
