1951 Pan American Games medal table: The 1951 Pan American Games, officially known as the I Pan American Games, were a continental multi-sport event held in Buenos Aires, Argentina, from February 5 to March 9, 1951.  At the Games, 2,513 athletes selected from 21 National Olympic Committees (NOCs) participated in events in 18 sports.  Seventeen nations earned medals during the competition, and ten won at least one gold medal.
