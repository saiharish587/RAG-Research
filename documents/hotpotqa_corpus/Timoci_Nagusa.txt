Timoci Nagusa: Timoci Nagusa (born 14 July 1987 in Nadi, Fiji) is a Fijian rugby union footballer.  He plays as a wing.  He plays his rugby for French club, Montpelier.  He is 1.88m (6'2) and weighs 88 kg.  He worked as a Police constable in Fiji before his rugby career took off.  He married his sweetheart, Losalini Tuilevuka on February 17, 2009 and has a child with her.
