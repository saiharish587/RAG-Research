House of Angostura: The House of Angostura ( ), also known as Angostura Limited, is a Trinidad and Tobago company famous for the production of Angostura bitters, invented by the company's founder.  The company is also a distiller and is the major producer of rum in Trinidad and Tobago.  The company also has been used as a vehicle for international expansion by its parent company, CL Financial.  As a result of these acquisitions, the company owns distillers in the United States, Canada, The Bahamas and Suriname.
