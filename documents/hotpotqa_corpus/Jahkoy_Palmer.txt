Jahkoy Palmer: Jahkoy Palmer (known mononymously as Jahkoy or JAHKOY) is a Canadian R&B singer and rapper currently signed to Def Jam Recordings.  He has released several projects, including the mixtape, "Forward Thinking," and the singles Still In Love, Hold Your Hand, and Odd Future.  His most recent single, "Odd Future," premiered on Zane Lowe's Beats 1 radio show in January 2016 and played by Drake on OVO Sound Radio.
