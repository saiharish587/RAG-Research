San Miguel de Tucumán: San Miguel de Tucumán (usually called simply Tucumán) is the capital of the Tucumán Province, located in northern Argentina 1311 km from Buenos Aires.  It is the fifth-largest city of Argentina after Buenos Aires, Córdoba, Rosario and Mendoza and the most important of the northern region.  The Spanish Conquistador founded the city in 1565 in the course of an expedition from present-day Peru.  Tucumán moved to its present site in 1685.
