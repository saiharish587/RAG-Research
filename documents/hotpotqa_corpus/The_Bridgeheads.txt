The Bridgeheads: The Bridgeheads was a London-based alternative band, formed in 2007, originating from Slovakia, although they described themselves as "expressionism".  The band decided to stop after the singer, Tomas dAsK, died on 27 September 2010.  The line up consisted of Tomas (vocals, guitar, piano, songwriting), Jozef Lemee (guitar, piano) and Michal Wisp (drums).  The line-up did not include a bass guitar - this was replaced by dAsK’s specific octave guitar played without the B-string.
