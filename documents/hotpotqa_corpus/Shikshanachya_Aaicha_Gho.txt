Shikshanachya Aaicha Gho: Shikshanacha Aaicha Gho is a 2010 Indian Marathi film directed by Mahesh Manjrekar starring Sachin Khedekar, Bharat Jadhav, Saksham Kulkarni, Gauri Vaidya, Siddharth Jadhav and Kranti Redkar.  The film was released on 15 January 2010.  Films music composed by trio Ajit-Atul-Sameer.  After Astitva with 9 years long gap Mahesh Manjrekar directed Marathi film.This film was later remade in Tamil & Telugu as "Dhoni" and in Bengali as "Chalo Paltai" starring Prosenjit Chatterjee.
