Deborah Stipek: Deborah Stipek is the Judy Koch Professor of Education in the Stanford Graduate School of Education (GSE) and a professor by courtesy of psychology.  She also serves as the Peter E. Haas Faculty Director of the Haas Center for Public Service at Stanford.  From 2001 to 2012 and then again from 2014 to 2015 she served as the I James Quillen Dean of the GSE at Stanford.  Prior to Stanford she was a faculty member at UCLA where she served for 10 of her 23 years there as the Director of the Corinne Seeds University Elementary School and the Urban Education Studies Center.  During this period as a faculty member at UCLA she took a year off to work for U.S. Senator Bill Bradley.
