War risk insurance: War risk insurance is a type of insurance which covers damage due to acts of war, including invasion, insurrection, rebellion and hijacking.  Some policies also cover damage due to weapons of mass destruction.  It is most commonly used in the shipping and aviation industries.  War risk insurance generally has two components: War Risk Liability, which covers people and items inside the craft and is calculated based on the indemnity amount; and War Risk Hull, which covers the craft itself and is calculated based on the value of the craft.  The premium varies based on the expected stability of the countries to which the vessel will travel.
