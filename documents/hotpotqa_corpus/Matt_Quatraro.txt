Matt Quatraro: Matthew John Quatraro (born November 14, 1973) is an American professional baseball player, coach, and manager.  He is the assistant hitting coach for the Cleveland Indians of Major League Baseball.  He played college baseball for Old Dominion University from 1993 through 1996, where he was named an All-American. Quatraro played professionally from 1996 through 2003, without reaching the majors.  He began coaching in 2004, and was enshrined in the Old Dominion University Sports Hall of Fame that year.
