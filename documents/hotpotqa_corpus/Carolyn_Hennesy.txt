Carolyn Hennesy: Carolyn Lee Hennesy (born June 10, 1962) is an American soap opera actress, author, and zoo advocate.  She is known for her role as Diane Miller on the daytime television series "General Hospital", for which she earned a Daytime Emmy Award nomination.  Miller's 2011 novel "The Secret Life of Damian Spinelli", featuring characters from "General Hospital", reached #16 on the "New York Times" Best Seller list.  She is also known for her work promoting AZA zoos and aquariums, and accredited marine parks like SeaWorld.
