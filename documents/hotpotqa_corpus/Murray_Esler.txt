Murray Esler: Professor Murray David Esler, AM (born in 1943 in Geelong, Australia) is a clinical cardiologist and medical scientist, based at the Baker IDI Heart and Diabetes Institute and the Alfred Hospital in Melbourne, where he is the Associate Director of the Heart Centre.  He is a Professor of Medicine at Melbourne's Monash University.  As Associate Director of the Baker, Professor Esler leads the institute’s research into the relationship between the brain and heart health.  He studied medicine at the University of Melbourne and received a PhD from the Australian National University (Department of Clinical Science).  His chief research interests are the causes and treatment of high blood pressure and heart failure, the effects of stress on the cardiovascular system, and monoamine transmitters of the human brain.
