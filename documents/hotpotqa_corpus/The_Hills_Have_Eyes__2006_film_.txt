The Hills Have Eyes (2006 film): The Hills Have Eyes is a 2006 American horror film and remake of Wes Craven's 1977 film of the same name.  Written by filmmaking partners Alexandre Aja and Grégory Levasseur of the French horror film "Haute Tension", and directed by Aja.  The film follows a family that is targeted by a group of murderous mutants after their car breaks down in the desert.
