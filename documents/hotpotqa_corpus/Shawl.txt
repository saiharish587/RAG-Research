Shawl: A shawl (from Persian: lang-Urdu شال‎ ‎ "shāl", which may be from Hindi: दुशाला duśālā, ultimately from Sanskrit: शाटी śāṭī) is a simple item of clothing, loosely worn over the shoulders, upper body and arms, and sometimes also over the head.  It is usually a rectangular or square piece of cloth, which is often folded to make a triangle but can also be triangular in shape.  Other shapes include oblong shawls.
