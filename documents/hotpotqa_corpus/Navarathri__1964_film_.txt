Navarathri (1964 film): Navarathri ("Nine Nights") is a 1964 Tamil Drama film by A. P. Nagarajan.  The film is well known for starring Sivaji Ganesan in nine distinct roles: the basic emotions - wonder, fear, compassion, anger, peace, love, courage, repulsion and happiness.  It was Sivaji's 100th film he acted.  "Navarathri" traces Nalina's (Savitri) experience of these nine emotions on nine consecutive nights.  The record of playing most number of roles in one Indian film was made by Sivaji Ganesan followed by Telugu with the same title and in Hindi as "Naya Din Nai Raat" (1974)with Sanjeev Kumar and Kamal Haasan in "Dasavathaaram" (2008).
