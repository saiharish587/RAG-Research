Golf course: A golf course is the grounds where the game of golf is played.  It comprises a series of holes, each consisting of a teeing ground, a fairway, the rough and other hazards, and a green with a flagstick ("pin") and hole ("cup").  A standard round of golf consists of 18 holes.  Most courses contain 18 holes; some share fairways or greens, and a subset has nine holes, played twice per round.  Par-3 courses consist of nine or 18 holes all of which have a par of three strokes.
