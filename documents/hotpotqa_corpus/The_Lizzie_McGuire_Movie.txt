The Lizzie McGuire Movie: The Lizzie McGuire Movie is a 2003 American teen comedy film released by Walt Disney Pictures on May 2, 2003.  The film serves as the finale of the Disney Channel television series of the same name, and was the first theatrical film based on a Disney Channel series.  The film stars Hilary Duff, Adam Lamberg, Robert Carradine, Hallie Todd and Jake Thomas, and tells the story of Lizzie's graduation trip to Rome.  At its release, the film peaked at number two at the domestic box office behind "X2: X-Men United".  "The Lizzie McGuire Movie" was released on August 12, 2003 on VHS and DVD.  "The Lizzie McGuire Movie" was directed by Jim Fall.
