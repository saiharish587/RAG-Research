From Denver to L.A.: "From Denver to L.A." is a song sung by Elton John, appearing on the soundtrack of the 1970 movie, "The Games".  The song was released as a single in the U.S. in July 1970, miscredited on the record label to "Elton Johns".  The single was issued just as John's career was starting to take off, but was quickly withdrawn because both John and his then-current record company objected to its release.  It is now an extremely rare collectors' item.
