Brains Brewery: Brains (S. A. Brain & Company Ltd) is a regional brewery founded in 1882 in Cardiff, Wales, by Samuel Arthur Brain.  The company controls more than 250 pubs in South Wales (particularly in Cardiff), Mid Wales and the West Country.  The company took over Crown Buckley brewery in Llanelli in 1997, and Hancock's Brewery in 1999.  Brains moved to the former Hancock's brewery just south of Cardiff Central railway station in 2000.  The Old Brewery, in Cardiff city centre, has been developed into a modern bar and restaurant complex.
