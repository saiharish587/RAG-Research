Femininity: Femininity (also called girlishness, womanliness or womanhood) is a set of attributes, behaviors, and roles generally associated with girls and women.  Femininity is socially constructed, but made up of both socially-defined and biologically-created factors.  This makes it distinct from the definition of the biological female sex, as both males and females can exhibit feminine traits.  People who exhibit a combination of both masculine and feminine characteristics are considered androgynous, and feminist philosophers have argued that gender ambiguity may blur gender classification.  Modern conceptualizations of femininity also rely not just upon social constructions, but upon the individualized choices made by women.
