Charcoal Tank Nature Reserve: The Charcoal Tank Nature Reserve is a protected nature reserve in the central western region of New South Wales, Australia.  The 86.4 ha reserve is situated 10 km south of West Wyalong and may be accessed via the Newell Highway and The Charcoal Tank Road.  The reserve is an important refuge for native flora and fauna in a highly fragmented landscape, one in which the majority of the original vegetation has been removed.
