Birkenhead Town Hall: Birkenhead Town Hall is a town hall and former civic building in Birkenhead, on the Wirral Peninsula, England.  The building was the former administrative headquarters of the County Borough of Birkenhead, and more recently, council offices for the Metropolitan Borough of Wirral.  Birkenhead Town Hall remains the location of the town's register office.  However, since the closure of the Wirral Museum in 2010, the future purpose of the Grade II* listed building is uncertain.
