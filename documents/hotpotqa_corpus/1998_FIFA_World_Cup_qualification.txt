1998 FIFA World Cup qualification: The 1998 FIFA World Cup qualification competition was a series of tournaments organised by the six FIFA confederations.  Each confederation — the AFC (Asia), CAF (Africa), CONCACAF (North, Central America and Caribbean), CONMEBOL (South America), OFC (Oceania), and UEFA (Europe) — was allocated a certain number of the 32 places at the tournament.  A total of 174 teams entered the 1998 FIFA World Cup qualification rounds, competing for a total of 32 spots in the final tournament.  The 1998 FIFA World Cup featured 32 teams, with two places reserved for France and Brazil as host nation and defending champions, respectively.  The remaining 30 places were determined by a qualification process, in which the other 174 teams, from the six FIFA confederations, competed.
