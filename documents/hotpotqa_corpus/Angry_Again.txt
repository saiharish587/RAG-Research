Angry Again: "Angry Again" is a 1993 heavy metal song by Megadeth, written by frontman Dave Mustaine.  The song was written exclusively for inclusion in the 1993 satirical action film "Last Action Hero", directed by John McTiernan which starred Arnold Schwarzenegger, and appeared on the film's soundtrack.  "Angry Again" never appeared on any of the band's official studio albums, but appeared first on their 1995 EP "Hidden Treasures" and several later compilations.
