St. Luke's Church, Aarhus: St. Lukas Church (Danish: "Skt.  Lukas Kirke" ) is a church in Aarhus, Denmark.  The church is situated in the Frederiksbjerg neighbourhood on Skt.  Lucas Kirkeplads by Ingerslevs Boulevard.  St. Lukas Church is a parish church under the Church of Denmark, the Danish state church, under the Diocese of Aarhus.  It is the parish church of St. Lukas Parish which has some 11.000 parish members.  The church was designed by architects Anton Frederiksen and Kaj Gottlob in neoclassical style with a 35 meters tall tower.  It was constructed between 1921 and 1926 but the crypt under the church, with room for 200 graves, was opened before the church itself, in 1923.
