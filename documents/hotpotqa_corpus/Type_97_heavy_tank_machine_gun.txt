Type 97 heavy tank machine gun: The Type 97 heavy tank machine gun (九七式車載重機関銃 , Kyū-nana-shiki shasai jū-kikanjū ) was the standard machine gun used in tanks and armored vehicles of the Imperial Japanese Army during World War II, a heavy machine gun by infantry forces, This weapon was not related to the Type 97 aircraft machine gun used in several Japanese Navy aircraft including the A6M Zero.
