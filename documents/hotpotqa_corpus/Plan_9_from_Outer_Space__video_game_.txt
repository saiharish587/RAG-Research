Plan 9 from Outer Space (video game): Plan 9 from Outer Space is a point and click adventure game developed by Gremlin Graphics at their Irish development office for the Amiga and Atari ST.  It was released in 1992 and published by Gremlin in Europe and by Konami in the United States.  A DOS version was made but only released in the USA and Europe.  There were two editions of the game.  The rarer of the two came packaged by itself, while the other edition came with a VHS copy of the film of the same name.
