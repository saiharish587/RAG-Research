Neil Smith (writer): Neil Smith is a fiction writer who lives in Montreal, the city where he was born.  His most recent book, a novel called "Boo", came out in May 2015 with Random House imprints in America, Britain, and Canada.  The book is narrated by a young science geek named Oliver Dalrymple who finds himself in a heaven reserved exclusively for 13-year-olds.  In addition to English, "Boo" has been published in traditional Chinese, Czech, Dutch, French, German, and Portuguese. " Publishers Weekly", "Booklist", and "Quill & Quire" all gave the book starred reviews.  The novel won the Hugh MacLennan Prize for Fiction and was nominated for a Sunburst Award, an Alex Award, and the Canadian Library Association Young Adult Book Award.
