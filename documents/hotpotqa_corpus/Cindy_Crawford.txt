Cindy Crawford: Cynthia Ann "Cindy" Crawford (born February 20, 1966) is an American model and actress.  Her years of success at modeling made her an international celebrity that has led to roles in television and film, and to work as a spokesperson.  In 1995, "Forbes" magazine named her the highest paid model on the planet.  One of the original five supermodels, she is known for her trademark mole just above her lip, and has appeared on hundreds of magazine covers throughout her career.
