New World String Quartet: The New World String Quartet was a classical music string quartet formed in the city of Grand Rapids, Michigan, United States, in 1975 and active through the early 1990s.  Founding members were: Yosef Yankelev and William Patterson, violins; Yuri Vasilaki, viola; and Ross Harbaugh, cello.  These were also the members in a 1981 Minnesota Public Radio interview and performance.  As of 1983, members were: Curtis J. Macomber and William Patterson, violins; Robert Dan, viola; and Ross T.
