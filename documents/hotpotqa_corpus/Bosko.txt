Bosko: Bosko is an animated cartoon character created by animators Hugh Harman and Rudolf Ising.  Bosko was the first recurring character in Leon Schlesinger's cartoon series, and was the star of 39 "Looney Tunes" shorts released by Warner Bros. He was voiced by Carman Maxwell, Johnny Murray, and Ruby Dandridge during the 1920s and 1930s, and once by Don Messick during the 1990s.
