Alexander Manly: Alexander (or Alex) L. Manly (1866–1944) was notable as an African-American newspaper owner and editor in Wilmington, North Carolina in the late 19th century.  With his brother Frank G. Manly as co-owner, he published the "Daily Record (Wilmington, North Carolina)", the state's only daily African-American newspaper and possibly the nation's only black-owned daily newspaper.  At the time, the port of Wilmington had 10,000 residents and was the state's largest city; its population was majority black, with a rising middle class.
