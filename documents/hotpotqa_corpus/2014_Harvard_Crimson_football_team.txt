2014 Harvard Crimson football team: The 2014 Harvard Crimson football team represented Harvard University in the 2014 NCAA Division I FCS football season.  They were led by 21st year head coach Tim Murphy and played their home games at Harvard Stadium.  They were a member of the Ivy League.  They finished the season 10–0, 7–0 in Ivy League play to be crowned Ivy League Champions.
