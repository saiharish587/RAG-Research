The Shame of the Cities: The Shame of the Cities is a book written by American author Lincoln Steffens.  Published in 1904, it is a collection of articles which Steffens had written for "McClure’s Magazine".  It reports on the workings of corrupt political machines in several major U.S. cities, along with a few efforts to combat them.  It is considered one of several early major pieces of muckraking journalism, though Steffens later claimed that this work made him "the first muckraker."
