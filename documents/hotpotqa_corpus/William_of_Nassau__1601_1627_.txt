William of Nassau (1601–1627): Willem van Nassau, Lord of De Lek (also "Willem van Nassau-LaLecq", or in French "Willem LaLecq"; 18 August 1601 – 18 August 1627) was a Dutch soldier from 1620 until 1627.  He was the illegitimate son of stadholder Maurice of Nassau, Prince of Orange and his mistress Margaretha van Mechelen.
