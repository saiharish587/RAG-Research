We Want Eazy: "We Want Eazy" is a single by the American gangsta rapper Eazy-E, from his 1988 debut album, "Eazy-Duz-It".  The song features fellow N.W.A members Dr. Dre and MC Ren and was produced by Dr. Dre and DJ Yella.  "We Want Eazy" also appears on his greatest hits, "Eternal E"; a 12-inch remix of this song was released as a single in 1989 and appeared on the rapper's posthumous compilation, "Featuring...Eazy-E".
