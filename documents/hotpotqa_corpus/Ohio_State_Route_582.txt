Ohio State Route 582: State Route 582 (SR 582) is an east–west state highway in the northwestern portion of the U.S. state of Ohio.  The western terminus of SR 582 is at a T-intersection with SR 65 nearly 2 mi west of Haskins.  Its eastern terminus is also at a T-intersection, this time with SR 105 less than 0.50 mi southwest of Woodville.
