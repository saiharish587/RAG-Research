Minnesota State High School League: The Minnesota State High School League (MSHSL) is a voluntary, non-profit association for the support and governance of interscholastic activities at high schools in Minnesota, United States.  The association supports interscholastic athletics and fine arts programs for member schools.  As of 2010, the organization cited a membership of nearly 500 public and private schools, including home schools, charter schools and 435 high schools.  The State High School League is an affiliate of the National Federation of State High School Associations.
