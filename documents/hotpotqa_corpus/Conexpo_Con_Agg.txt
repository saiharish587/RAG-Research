Conexpo-Con/Agg: CONEXPO-CON/AGG is a trade show for the construction industry that takes place every three years.  CONEXPO-CON/AGG is a result of the merger of CONEXPO and CON/AGG in 1996.  It is held at the Las Vegas Convention Center with the next show held March 10-14, 2020.  The International Exposition for Power Transmission (IFPE) is held in conjunction with Conexpo-Con/Agg.
