The Process (collective): The Process is an art and philosophy collective formed in the early 1990s.  The idea was initially birthed at the same time as, and with a subset of the same people from, the studio work for the Skinny Puppy album "The Process", though the direct interrelation ends there.  Some of the early contributors included Nivek Ogre, Genesis P-Orridge, William Morrison, and Loki der Quaeler.  Members are known as Processians or Processors
