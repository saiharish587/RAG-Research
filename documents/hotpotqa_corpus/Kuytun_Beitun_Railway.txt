Kuytun–Beitun Railway: The Kuytun–Beitun Railway or Kuibei Railway () is a single-track railway in Xinjiang, China between Kuytun and Beitun.  The railway branches from the Northern Xinjiang Railway at Kuytun and heads north across the Junggar Basin to Beitun, near Altay, passing through Karamay, Urho, and Fuhai (Burultoqay).  The railway is 468.5 km in length The railway opened to freight traffic on December 29, 2009 and passenger traffic on June 1, 2011.
