Near and Far: Near and Far is a board game for 2 to 4 players designed by Ryan Laukat and published by Red Raven Games in 2017.  In this map based storytelling board game, players seek fame and fortune and the eventual discovery of a mysterious last ruin.  The gameplay blends resource management with a storybook, with players controlling heroes that equip and provision themselves in a town, and then set out to explore the map, setting up camps and completing quests.  The game is a sequel to Laukat's Above and Below, which is set in the same fictional universe.
