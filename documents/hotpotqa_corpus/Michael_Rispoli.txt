Michael Rispoli: Michael Rispoli (born November 27, 1960) is an American character actor.  He was in the cast of the HBO television series "The Sopranos" as Jackie Aprile, Sr. Rispoli reunited with "Sopranos" co-star James Gandolfini in the 2009 thriller "The Taking of Pelham 123".  Rispoli was a contender for the role of Tony Soprano that ultimately went to Gandolfini.
