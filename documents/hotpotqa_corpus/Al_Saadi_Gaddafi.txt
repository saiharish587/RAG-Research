Al-Saadi Gaddafi: Al-Saadi Muammar Gaddafi (Arabic: الساعدي معمر القذافي‎ ‎ ; born 25 May 1973), is the third son of former Libyan leader Muammar Gaddafi.  He is a Libyan former association football player.  In 2011, he was the commander of Libya's Special Forces and was involved in the Libyan Civil War.  An Interpol notice has been issued against him.  On 5 March 2014, he was arrested in Niger and extradited to Libya, where he faces murder charges.  In August 2015, video surfaced allegedly showing Gaddafi being tortured.
