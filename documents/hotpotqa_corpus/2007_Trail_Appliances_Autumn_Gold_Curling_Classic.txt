2007 Trail Appliances Autumn Gold Curling Classic: The 2007 Trail Appliances Autumn Gold Curling Classic was the 30th annual edition of the event.  It marked the first Grand Slam event of the Women's World Curling Tour.  It was held October 5-9 at the Calgary Curling Club in Calgary, Alberta.  The total purse for the event was $56,000 with $14,000 going to the winning team of Jennifer Jones, Cathy Overton-Clapham, Jill Officer and Dawn Askin.  They beat Shannon Kleibrink's rink in the final.
