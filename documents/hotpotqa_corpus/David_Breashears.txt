David Breashears: David Finlay Breashears (born December 20, 1955) is an American mountaineer, filmmaker, author, and motivational speaker.  In 1985, he reached the summit of Mount Everest a second time, becoming the first American to reach the summit of Mount Everest more than once.  He is perhaps most famous for guiding Richard Bass to the summit of Everest, thus completing Bass's ascent of the Seven Summits (the highest summit on each of the seven continents).
