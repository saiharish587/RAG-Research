2013 Adelaide Film Festival: The 6th Adelaide Film Festival took place in Adelaide, Australia, from 10 to 20 October 2013.  This was Amanda Duthie's first year as Festival Director (after eight years at the Australian Broadcasting Corporation and eight years at the Special Broadcasting Service during the 1990s), having taken over from Katrina Sedgwick.  Margaret Pomeranz and David Stratton served as the festival’s patrons.
