Denmark–Norway: Denmark–Norway (Danish and Norwegian: "Danmark–Norge ") was an early modern multi-national and multi-lingual real union consisting of the Kingdom of Denmark, the Kingdom of Norway (including Norwegian regions Faroe Islands, Iceland, Greenland, et cetera), the Duchy of Schleswig, and the Duchy of Holstein.  The state also claimed sovereignty over two historical peoples: Wends and Goths.  In addition, the state included colonies: St. Thomas, St. John, St. Croix, Ghana, Tharangambadi, Serampore, and Nicobar Islands.  The state's inhabitants were mainly Danes, Norwegians (along with Inuit and Sami), and Germans.  The state's largest cities were Copenhagen, Altona, Bergen, Trondheim, and Christiania (Oslo).
