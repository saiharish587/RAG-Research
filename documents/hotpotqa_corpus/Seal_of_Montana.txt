Seal of Montana: The Great Seal of the State of Montana was adopted in 1865, when Montana was a United States Territory.  When it became a state in 1889, it was decided to use the same seal.  In 1891, proposals were made to make changes or adopt a brand new seal.  None of these proposals passed legislation.  The outer ring of the seal contains the text "The Great Seal of the State of Montana".  The inner circle depicts a landscape of mountains, plains and forests by the Great Falls on the Missouri River.  A plow, a pick and a shovel are depicted on the front, representing the state's industry.  The banner at the bottom of the seal reads the territorial motto of "Oro y Plata", meaning "Gold and Silver" in Spanish.
