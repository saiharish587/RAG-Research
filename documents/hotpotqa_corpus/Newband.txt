Newband: Newband is a contemporary music ensemble devoted to the performance of microtonal music.  The group was founded in 1977 by musicians Stefani Starin and Dean Drummond.  As a youth, Drummond performed with maverick composer Harry Partch in a unique ensemble of microtonal instruments that Partch designed and built himself; Drummond performed in the premieres of Partch’s "Daphne of the Dunes", "And on the Seventh Day Petals Fell in Petaluma", and "Delusion of the Fury", as well as on both Partch Columbia Masterworks recordings made during the late 1960s.
