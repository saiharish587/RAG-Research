Swan Song (Supernatural): "Swan Song" is the fifth season finale of The CW television series "Supernatural".  It is the 22nd episode of the fifth season, and is the show's 104th episode overall.  Steve Boyum directed the episode with teleplay written by series creator Eric Kripke and story written by Eric Gewirtz.  The episode aired on Thursday, May 13, 2010, and concluded the series' originally slated storyline.  The narrative follows the series' protagonists Sam (Jared Padalecki) and Dean Winchester (Jensen Ackles)—brothers who travel the continental United States hunting supernatural creatures—as they attempt to stop the Apocalypse.
