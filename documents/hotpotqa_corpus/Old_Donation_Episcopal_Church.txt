Old Donation Episcopal Church: Old Donation Church is the third Lynnhaven Parish Church and is the oldest Episcopal Church in Virginia Beach.  Records show that the parish’s first church services were held in 1637 in the home of Adam Thoroughgood.  The first church building was constructed on Mr. Thoroughgood’s land in 1639 on the location later known as "Church Point."  The vestry, or governing body of the church, was established in 1640.
