ShondaLand: ShondaLand is an American television production company founded by television writer/producer Shonda Rhimes.  She founded it to be one of the production companies of her first series "Grey's Anatomy" in 2005.  It has since gone on to produce Rhimes's other creations, "Grey's" spinoff "Private Practice" and the widely popular political drama "Scandal", and her other productions—the short-lived "Off the Map", the Viola Davis-starring legal thriller "How to Get Away with Murder", and the crime thriller "The Catch"—all of which are co-produced with ABC Studios and air on ABC.
