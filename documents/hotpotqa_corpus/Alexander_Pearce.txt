Alexander Pearce: Alexander Pearce (1790 – 19 July 1824) was an Irish convict who was transported to Van Diemen's Land for seven years for theft.  He escaped from prison several times.  During one of these escapes he allegedly became a cannibal, murdering his companions one by one.  In another escape, with one companion, he allegedly killed him and ate him in pieces.  He was eventually captured and was hanged and dissected in Hobart for murder.
