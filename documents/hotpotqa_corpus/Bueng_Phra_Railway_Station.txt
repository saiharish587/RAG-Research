Bueng Phra Railway Station: Bueng Phra Railway Station is a railway station located in Bueng Phra Subdistrict, Phitsanulok City, Phitsanulok.  It is located 381.875 km from Bangkok Railway Station and is a class 1 railway station.  It is on the Northern Line of the State Railway of Thailand.  The station opened on 24 January 1908 as part of the Northern Line extension from Pak Nam Pho to Phitsanulok.  PTT Public Company Limited operates a crude oil depot adjacent to the site and the railway operates several oil freight services from this station (to Mae Nam Station).
