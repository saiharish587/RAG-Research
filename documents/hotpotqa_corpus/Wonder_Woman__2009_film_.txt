Wonder Woman (2009 film): Wonder Woman is a 2009 direct-to-DVD animated superhero film focusing on the superheroine of the same name.  The plot of the film is loosely based on George Pérez's reboot of the character, specifically the "Gods and Mortals" arc that started the character's second volume in 1987.  It is the fourth in the line of DC Universe Animated Original Movies released by Warner Premiere and Warner Bros.  Animation.
