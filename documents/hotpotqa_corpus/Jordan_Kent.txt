Jordan Kent: Jordan Russell Kent (born July 24, 1984 in Dhahran, Saudi Arabia) is a former American football wide receiver.  He was drafted by the Seattle Seahawks in the sixth round of the 2007 NFL Draft.  Jordan began playing football when he was a junior in college at the University of Oregon.  While at the University of Oregon, Jordan also played Basketball and ran Track for the Oregon Ducks.  Today Jordan is a broadcaster for Comcast Sportsnet, and he covers the Portland Trailblazers and Pac-12 football and basketball.  He also runs popular youth sport camps throughout the state of Oregon.
