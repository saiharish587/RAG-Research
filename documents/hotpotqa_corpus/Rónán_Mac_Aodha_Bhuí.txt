Rónán Mac Aodha Bhuí: Rónán Mac Aodha Bhuí is an Irish-language broadcaster known particularly for his popular magazine programme "Rónán Beo" on RTÉ Raidió na Gaeltachta.  He was born on 6 May 1970 in Cork, but was brought up in Gweedore, in the Donegal gaeltacht, where he attended Bunscoil Bhun Bhig and Pobalscoil Ghaoth Dobhair.  He is the youngest son of the author Fionntán Mac Aodha Bhuí and comes from a family of eight.
