Aranyélet: Aranyélet (] , English: "Golden Life" ) is a Hungarian television series based on the Finnish "Helppo elämä" crime drama.  The show tells the story of a newly rich Hungarian family whose existence is based on crime and lies.  The series premiered in Hungary on November 8, 2015 on the cable network HBO Hungary.  The series was renewed for a second season which premiered on November 6, 2016.
