AMU Literary Festival: AMU Literary Festival is an annual celebration of all the things from the vast world of literature by the Central University of India, Aligarh Muslim University.  The Cultural Education Center(CEC) of AMU which embodies the multicultural and diverse spirit of the university, propitiates art and literature holding true to the envisioned nature of the university by its founder Sir Syed Ahmad Khan.  The University Debating and Literary Club (UDLC) has been organizing this event for the past three years where CEC becomes the nexus of all the panels and speeches by illustrious names of journalism and literature.  Some of the previous invitees include news anchor and author Rajdeep Sardesai, poet Keki N. Daruwalla, politician Mani Shankar Aiyar and independent journalist Rana Ayyub.
