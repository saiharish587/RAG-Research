Gennifer Flowers: Gennifer Flowers (born January 24, 1950) is an American model and actress who obtained notoriety after revealing a sexual encounter with U.S. President Bill Clinton.  In January 1998, Clinton testified under oath that there had been a sexual encounter between Flowers and him.  Before Clinton's presidency, she posed nude for "Penthouse" magazine and was an actress in two films and one TV show.
