Hot Potato (1976 film): Hot Potato also known as Twist the Tiger's Tail is a 1976 action film written and directed by Oscar Williams.  The film was a Fred Weintraub and Paul Heller production starring Jim Kelly.  Jim Kelly also arranged his own fight scenes.  "Hot Potato" was filmed on location in Chiang Mai, Thailand.  It is a follow-up to "Black Belt Jones".  The film was distributed by Warner Bros.  Pictures.
