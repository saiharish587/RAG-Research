Stony Brook Sports Complex: Stony Brook Sports Complex is a multi-purpose complex located in Stony Brook University, Stony Brook, NY.  The Complex houses the Stony Brook Arena on the west end, the Pritchard Gymnasium, a 25 yard long swimming Pool, an athletic performance center and many other athletic facilities within.  Currently, the Stony Brook Seawolves basketball and volleyball programs home games are played in the Complex, specifically in the Pritchard Gymnasium.  The Stony Brook Arena on the west end is currently undergoing $20 million renovation.  During this renovation period, the Pritchard Gymnasium will be used to host the basketball and volleyball games.
