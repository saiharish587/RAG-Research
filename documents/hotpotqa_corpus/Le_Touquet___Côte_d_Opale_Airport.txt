Le Touquet – Côte d'Opale Airport: Le Touquet - Côte d'Opale Airport (French: "Aéroport de Le Touquet - Côte d'Opale" ) (IATA: LTQ, ICAO: LFAT) is 2.9 km east-southeast of Le Touquet, a commune of the Pas-de-Calais department in the Nord-Pas-de-Calais region of France.  It is on Côte d'Opale, the northern coast of France
