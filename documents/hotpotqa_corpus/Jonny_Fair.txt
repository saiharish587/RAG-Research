Jonny Fair: Jonny Fair is an American jazz/folk singer/crooner, musician, actor and composer.  Fair composed and performed the music and acted in the 2007 motion picture "Gordon Glass" starring Omar Benson Miller.  Fair also acted as the sidewalk cafe Maitre D' in the opening sequence of the 2002 motion picture "Collateral Damage" starring Arnold Schwarzenegger and as the post match reporter in the 2001 made-for-TV movie "When Billie Beat Bobby" starring Ron Silver.
