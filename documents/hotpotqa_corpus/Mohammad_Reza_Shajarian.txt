Mohammad-Reza Shajarian: Mohammad-Reza Shajarian (Persian: محمدرضا شجريان‎ ‎ ; ] ) (born 23 September 1940, Mashhad, Razavi Khoarasan, Iran) is an internationally and critically acclaimed Iranian classical singer, composer and "Ostad" (master) of Persian traditional music.  He has been called "Iran's greatest living maestro of Persian classical music."  Shajarian is also known for his skills in Persian calligraphy, and humanitarian activities.
