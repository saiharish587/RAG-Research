William Preston (actor): William Preston (August 26, 1921 – July 10, 1998) did not start his acting career until he was at the age of 47 but subsequently appeared in more than sixty productions of Shakespeare's plays.  He had a Master's degree in English literature from Penn State.  He is perhaps best known for his role as recurring character Carl "Oldy" Olson on "Late Night with Conan O'Brien".  Among his many movie roles, he played John, the bum, from "The Fisher King" (1991), a blacksmith in "Far and Away" (1992), and the flask mourner in "Family Business" (1989).  He later appeared in "Waterworld" (1995), "Reckless" (1995), "Blue in the Face" (1995), and "The Crucible" (1996).
