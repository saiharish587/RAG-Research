Andrew Garfield: Andrew Russell Garfield (born 20 August 1983) is a British-American actor.  Born in Los Angeles and raised in Epsom, Surrey, Garfield began his career on the UK stage and in television productions.  He made his feature-film debut in the 2007 ensemble drama "Lions for Lambs".  Garfield first came to international attention in 2010 with supporting roles in the drama films "The Social Network", for which he received Golden Globe and BAFTA nominations for his portrayal of Eduardo Saverin, and "Never Let Me Go", for which he received a Saturn Award and another BAFTA nomination.
