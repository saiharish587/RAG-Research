Angel of Retribution: Angel of Retribution is the fifteenth studio album by British heavy metal band Judas Priest, released in 2005.  It is the band's first album since "Painkiller" (1990) to feature Rob Halford.  The album debuted at #13 on the US "Billboard" chart, which made it the third highest chart of a Judas Priest album (the second being "Nostradamus" and the highest being "Redeemer of Souls").  The album was produced by Roy Z, who co-wrote the song "Deal with the Devil".
