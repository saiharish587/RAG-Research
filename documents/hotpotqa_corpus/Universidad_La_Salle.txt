Universidad La Salle: Universidad La Salle (ULSA) is a private institution of higher education with 15 campuses in Mexico.  It is part of the Institute of the Brothers of the Christian Schools.  It offers high school, bachelor, master and Ph.D degrees.  It has had an expansion in the country, creating its own university national system.  Its main campus is located in Mexico City, and has a presence in Ciudad Obregon, Chihuahua, Gomez Palacio, Monterrey, Ciudad Victoria, Leon, Morelia, Pachuca, Ciudad Nezahualcóyotl, Cuernavaca, Puebla, Oaxaca, Cancun and Saltillo.
