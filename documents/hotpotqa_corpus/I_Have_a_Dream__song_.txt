I Have a Dream (song): "I Have a Dream" is a song by Swedish pop group ABBA.  It was featured on the group's sixth studio album "Voulez-Vous" and released as a single in December 1979.  The single became a big hit, topping the charts in many countries and peaking at No. 2 in the UK over the Christmas week of 1979.  Twenty years later, Irish pop group Westlife released a version of the song which reached No. 1 in the UK over the Christmas week of 1999.
