Now and Zen: Now and Zen is the fourth solo album by Robert Plant, released in 1988 (see 1988 in music) under the label Es Paranza.  The album made the top 10 in the US (No. 6) and UK (No. 10).  The album was certified triple platinum by the RIAA on September 7, 2001.  The album was produced by Tim Palmer, Robert Plant and Phil Johnstone.
