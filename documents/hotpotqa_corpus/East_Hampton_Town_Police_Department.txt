East Hampton Town Police Department: The East Hampton Town Police Department, commonly referred to as EHTPD, is a professional police organization responsible for primary jurisdictional law enforcement for the town of East Hampton, New York.  East Hampton Town is located in Suffolk County.  The East Hampton Town Police Headquarters are located in Wainscott, with an address of 131 Wainscott Northwest Road, Wainscott, NY 11975 (631) 537-7575.  The department is a New York State Accredited Agency.
