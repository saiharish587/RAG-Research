Harbor Square: Harbor Square, formerly Shore Mall, is a shopping plaza (formerly a shopping mall) in Egg Harbor Township, New Jersey on U.S. Route 40/U.S. Route 322 originally known as "Searstown".  The plaza is accessible from Exit 36 off the Garden State Parkway.  The plaza is owned by Aetna Realty.  The plaza has a gross leasable area of 337,423 ft², formerly 620,000 ft² when it was a mall, located on 73 acre of land.  The plaza's anchor stores include Boscov's and Burlington Coat Factory.
