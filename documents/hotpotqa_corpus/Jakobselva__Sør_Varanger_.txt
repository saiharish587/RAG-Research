Jakobselva (Sør-Varanger): Jakobselva or Grense Jakobselv River (English: Jacob's River , Russian: Ворьема , Finnish: "Vuoremijoki" , ) is a river that runs along the Russia-Norway border.  The river runs along the border of Sør-Varanger Municipality in Finnmark county, Norway, and Pechengsky District in Murmansk Oblast, Russia.  The river discharges into the Varangerfjorden, a bay off the Barents Sea.
