Theophilus Hastings, 7th Earl of Huntingdon: Theophilus Hastings, 7th Earl of Huntingdon PC (10 December 1650 – 30 May 1701) was an English politician.  He was the son of Ferdinando Hastings, 6th Earl of Huntingdon, born in the 27th year of his parents' marriage, and became Earl of Huntingdon on 13 February 1656 on his father's death.  He married, firstly, Elizabeth Lewis, daughter of Sir John Lewis, 1st Baronet, on 19 February 1671/2.  She died 24th December 1688.  He married, secondly, Mary Frances Fowler, daughter of Francis Leveson Fowler, on 8 May 1690.  With his first wife he had two sons and six daughters, including Thomas (1674–1675), George and Elizabeth.  With his second wife he had two more sons and five more daughters, including Theophilus, Catherine, Maria and Margaret.
