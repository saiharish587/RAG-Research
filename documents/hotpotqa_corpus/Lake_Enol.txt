Lake Enol: Lake Enol is a small highland lake in the Principality of Asturias, Spain.  It is located in the Picos de Europa Western Massif, Cantabrian Mountains.  It is next to Lake Ercina and together, they form the group known as Lakes of Covadonga, inside the Picos de Europa National Park.  Lake Enol is the larger of the two.  It is situated approximately 10 km from Covadonga and 25 km from Cangas de Onís.  A curving road is available from Arriondas to the lake.  Measuring 0.1 km2 , it is one of the biggest lakes in the area.  Lake Enol is situated 1070 m above sea level, in the Picos de Europa).  It was formed by the withdrawal of a front glacier.
