Marie Losier: Marie Losier (born in 1972 in France) is a French filmmaker and curator who has been living and working in New York City for 20 years.  She has shown her film and video work at a number of museums, galleries, festivals and biennials internationally.  Losier has studied literature at the University of Nanterre (France) and fine arts at Hunter College in New York City.  She has made many film portraits based on directors, musicians and composers, including George Kuchar, Guy Maddin, Richard Foreman, Tony Conrad, Genesis P-Orridge and Alan Vega.  Her films are distributed by Video Data Bank.  Additionally, Losier has been the film curator at the French Institute Alliance Française since 2000.
