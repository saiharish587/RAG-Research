Masahiko Takeshita: Lt. Col. Masahiko Takeshita (竹下正彦 , Takeshita Masahiko ) was the head of the domestic affairs section of the Military Affairs Bureau of the Imperial Japanese Army during World War II.  In August 1945, he helped plan a "coup" along with Major Kenji Hatanaka and a handful of others , with the intent of preventing the Emperor's announcement of Japan's surrender from being broadcast.
