Shweta Munshi: Shweta Munshi is an Indian television actress, played the leading role of Avni in Maayke Se Bandhi Dor on Star Plus TV channel.  Previously, she played the role of Arpita in the most famous Zee TV show, Punar Vivah.  Currently Shweta Munshi is playing the character of Aditya Redij’s wife Prabha in Zee TV show Bandhan
