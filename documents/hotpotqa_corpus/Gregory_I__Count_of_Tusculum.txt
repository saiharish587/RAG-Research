Gregory I, Count of Tusculum: Gregory I was the Count of Tusculum sometime between 954 and 1012.  Consul et dux 961, vir illustrissimus 980, praefectus navalis 999.  He was the son of Alberic II (son of Alberic I of Spoleto and Marozia), and Alda of Vienne (daughter of Hugh, King of Italy and his second wife, Alda (or Hilda)).  His half-brother was Pope John XII.
