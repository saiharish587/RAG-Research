Kanako Momota: Kanako Momota (百田 夏菜子 , Momota Kanako , born July 12, 1994) is a Japanese idol singer.  She is best known as the leader of the female idol group Momoiro Clover Z.  Momota was ranked 12th most popular Japanese idol of 2013 by "Nihon Keizai Shimbun".  She is represented by Stardust Promotion talent agency.
