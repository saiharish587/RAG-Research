Camille (1915 film): Camille is a 1915 American silent film based on the story "La Dame aux Camélias" ("The Lady of the Camellias") by Alexandre Dumas, "fils", first published in French as a novel in 1848 and as a play in 1852.  Adapted for the screen by Frances Marion, "Camille" was directed by Albert Capellani and starred Clara Kimball Young as Camille and Paul Capellani as her lover, Armand.
