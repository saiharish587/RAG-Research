The Biggest Loser UK (series 1): The Biggest Loser UK 2005 was the first season of the reality television series entitled "The Biggest Loser".  The season first aired on 6 October 2005, with the final episode on 15 December 2005, where 12 overweight contestants competed for a cash prize of £25,000.  Vicki Butler-Henderson was featured as the host, with trainers Angie Dowds and Mark Bailey.  Aaron Howlett was named as the winner after losing 9 st 12 lb .
