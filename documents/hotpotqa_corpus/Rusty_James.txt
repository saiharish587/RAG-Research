Rusty-James: Rusty-James is a fictional character in author S. E. Hinton's 1975 novel Rumble Fish.  The book was adapted to film and directed by Francis Ford Coppola in 1983.  In the film, Rusty-James is played by Matt Dillon.  In the book, Rusty James is a tall 14 year old kid in Junior High School with dark red hair and Hershey brown eyes.
