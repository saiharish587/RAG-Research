Lahori Gate (TV series): Lahori Gate (Urdu: لاہوری گیٹ‎ ) was a Pakistani comedy drama serial, aired on PTV Home.  Serial's stars were Iftikhar Thakur, Sardar Kamal, Naseem Vicky, Abid Kashmiri, Waseem Abbas and Qavi Khan.  This show is written by Waseem Abbas who also acts in it.  He is a son of Pakistan's late famous film playback singer Inayat Hussain Bhatti.  Shahid Aziz is the director of this comedy drama.
