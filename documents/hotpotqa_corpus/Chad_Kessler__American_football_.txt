Chad Kessler (American football): Chad Stephen Kessler (born June 24, 1975) is a former American football punter who played college football at Louisiana State University and attended Lake Mary High School in Lake Mary, Florida.  He was a consensus All-American in 1997.  Kessler was named first-team All-SEC in 1995 and 1997.  He was also a member of the Tampa Bay Buccaneers of the National Football League.
