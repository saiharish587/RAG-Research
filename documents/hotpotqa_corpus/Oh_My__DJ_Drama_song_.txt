Oh My (DJ Drama song): "Oh My" is a song by American hip hop artist DJ Drama, released on May 13, 2011, as the lead single from his third studio album "Third Power".  The song was produced by frequent collaborator Drumma Boy and features rappers Fabolous, Roscoe Dash and Wiz Khalifa.  The song peaked at #18 on the "Billboard" and #12 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop Songs, making it the most successful song for DJ Drama to date.
