Fate of Nations: Fate of Nations is Led Zeppelin singer Robert Plant's sixth solo album.  It was released in 1993 and re-released in a remastered edition on 20 March 2007.  It features former Cutting Crew guitarist Kevin Scott MacMichael.  The lead singer of Clannad, Máire Brennan is featured on the track "Come Into My Life".  The song "I Believe" is a tribute to Robert Plant's late son, Karac.
