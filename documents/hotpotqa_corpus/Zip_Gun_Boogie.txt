Zip Gun Boogie: "Zip Gun Boogie" is a 1974 single, originally credited in the UK as a solo single by Marc Bolan of the British glam rock band T. Rex.  In other territories, the single was credited to "T. Rex" or "Marc Bolan and T. Rex"; almost all reissues of the track credit it solely to T. Rex.  The track and its B-side feature on the 1975 T. Rex album Bolan's Zip Gun.
