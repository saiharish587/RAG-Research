Rupert Jee: Rupert Jee (born July 16, 1956) is an American entrepreneur and television celebrity who has gained fame through frequent appearances on "Late Show with David Letterman".  He first appeared on the "Late Show" during a "Meet the Neighbors" segment on September 20, 1993 (his Hello Deli is located at 213 W 53rd St, near the Ed Sullivan Theater in New York City, where the "Late Show" is taped), and was a frequent accomplice of host David Letterman during the show's many comedic segments.
