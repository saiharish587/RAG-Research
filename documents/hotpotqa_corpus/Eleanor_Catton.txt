Eleanor Catton: Eleanor Catton MNZM (born 24 September 1985) is a Canadian-born New Zealand author.  Her second novel, "The Luminaries", won the 2013 Man Booker Prize.  In January 2015, she created a short-lived media storm in New Zealand when she made comments in an interview in India in which she was critical of "neo-liberal, profit-obsessed, very shallow, very money-hungry politicians who do not care about culture."
