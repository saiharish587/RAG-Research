Sheena Duncan: Sheena Duncan (7 December 1932 – 4 May 2010) was a South African anti-Apartheid activist and counselor.  Duncan was the daughter of Jean Sinclair, one of the co-founders of the Black Sash, a group of white, middle-class South African women who offered support to black South Africans and advocated the non-violent abolishment of the Apartheid system.  Duncan served two terms as the leader of Black Sash.
