Southern crowned pigeon: The southern crowned pigeon ("Goura scheepmakeri") is a large, terrestrial pigeon confined to southern lowland forests of New Guinea.  It has a bluish-grey plumage with elaborate blue lacy crests, red iris and very deep maroon breast.  Both sexes have a similar appearance.  It is on average 70 cm (28 in) long and weighs 2,250 grams (5 lbs).
