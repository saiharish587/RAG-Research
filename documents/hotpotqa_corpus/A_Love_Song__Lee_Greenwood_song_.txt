A Love Song (Lee Greenwood song): "A Love Song" is a song written by Lee Greenwood and recorded by American country music artist Kenny Rogers.  It was released in October 1982 as the second single from his album "Love Will Turn You Around".  The song reached number 3 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles chart and number 1 on the "RPM" Country Tracks chart in Canada."  "A Love Song" was originally recorded by Greenwood on his 1982 album "Inside Out".
