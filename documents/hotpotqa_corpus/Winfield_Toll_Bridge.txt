Winfield Toll Bridge: Winfield Toll Bridge, also known as the Ross Booth Memorial Bridge, is a historic three-span cantilever Warren Truss bridge located at Winfield and Red House, Putnam County, West Virginia.  It was built in 1955, and spans the Kanawha River, carrying West Virginia Route 34.  The cantilever through-truss consists of two anchor spans each 245 feet in length and the main span 462 feet in length between pier center lines.  The main span consists of two 128 feet, 4 inch, cantilever arms and a 205 feet, 4 inch, suspended span.
