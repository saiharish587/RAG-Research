Celal Kandemiroglu: Celal Kandemiroglu is a graphic artist in the German computer games industry.  He was born in 1953 in Turkey and made his first comic when he was five years old.  After graduating at the fine arts academy in Istanbul he started to offer his comics to the Bastei-Verlag in Germany around 1978.  Since 1985 he has created many video and movie covers until he became a cover artist for the German computer game industry in 1988.
