Frank Buck (politician): Frank Forrest Buck (born September 26, 1943, in Trousdale County) was a Tennessee politician and a member of the Tennessee House of Representatives for the 40th district, which is composed of DeKalb, Smith, and Macon counties.  He was the son of John and Georgia Baird Buck.  He has one brother, John William Buck.  His father, John, started out as a sharecropper and eventually owned over 100 acre of land in Wilson, Smith and Trousdale counties.  Neither of his parents completed the eighth grade, as they dropped out of school to work in the Great Depression.  His father was stricken with polio while Buck was at Lebanon High School.  His father spent many months at Vanderbilt Medical Center recovering, but never walked again.
