The Seat Filler: The Seat Filler is an American romantic comedy musical film produced by the Company Production of Magnolia Home Entertainment, Seat Filler Productions, Shake Martin Films and Strange Fruit Films, which released in 2004.  It stars Kelly Rowland, Duane Martin, Mel B, Kyla Pratt and Shemar Moore.  The movie received mainly average reviews.  The film is also recognized as Duane Martin's first (and to date, only) appearance in a musical film and Kelly Rowland's most successful film in a leading role performance.
