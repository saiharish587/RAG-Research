Fingerprint File: "Fingerprint File" is the closing track from the Rolling Stones' 1974 album "It's Only Rock 'n Roll".  It is one of their first attempts to branch out into dance or electronic music, and the song resembles music by Sly and the Family Stone.  Key ingredients of the song are the rhythm guitar played by Mick Jagger, which features heavy phasing due to the use of the MXR Phase 100 effects pedal, and the highly jazz/funk-oriented bass guitar played by Mick Taylor.  Keith Richards uses the wah-wah pedal for his guitar part.  Bill Wyman is on synthesiser, Charlie Watts on drums, Billy Preston on clavinet, and Nicky Hopkins on piano.  Charlie Jolly Kunjappu is featured on the tabla.
