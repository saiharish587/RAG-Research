We the Best: We the Best is the second studio album by Miami-based DJ and fellow Terror Squad member DJ Khaled.  It was released on June 12, 2007, by Terror Squad Entertainment, distributed by Koch Records.  DJ Khaled alongside fellow rapper Fat Joe and Allah, collectively handled as the executive producers on this album.  The album was produced by Cool & Dre, Jim Jonsin, The Diaz Brothers and The Runners; as well as the guest appearances from Bone Thugs-n-Harmony, Trina, Paul Wall and Bun B, among others.
