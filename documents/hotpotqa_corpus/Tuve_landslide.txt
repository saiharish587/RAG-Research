Tuve landslide: The Tuve landslide was a large landslide in Tuve, Gothenburg, Sweden on November 30, 1977.  Some 67 houses were destroyed, killing 9, injuring about 60 and making around 600 people homeless.  The slide began at 16.05 and lasted 5–6 minutes.  The slide affected 270 000 square meters (27 hectares).  About 600 people lived in the area; of these, approximately 200 were in the area at the time of the slide.  About 100 needed help by rescue workers.  It was the most severe landslide in the modern history of Sweden.
