Principal Skinner: Principal W. Seymour Skinner (born Armin Tamzarian) is a fictional character in the American animated sitcom "The Simpsons", who is voiced by Harry Shearer.  He is the principal of Springfield Elementary School, which he struggles to control, and is constantly engaged in a battle against its inadequate resources, apathetic and bitter teachers, and often rowdy and unenthusiastic students, Bart Simpson being a standout example.
