Nexon: Nexon (Hangul: 넥슨 ; RR: "Nekseun " ) Co., Ltd. is a South Korean global leader in online games for PC and mobile.  It services over 100 titles in over 190 countries.  Nexon was founded in Seoul, South Korea in 1994 by Kim Jung Ju and Jake Song.  In 2011, Nexon moved its headquarters from Seoul, South Korea to Tokyo, Japan.
