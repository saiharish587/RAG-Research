The Young Doctors (film): The Young Doctors is a 1961 film directed by Phil Karlson and starring Ben Gazzara, Fredric March, Dick Clark, Ina Balin, Eddie Albert, Phyllis Love, Aline MacMahon, George Segal (in his first movie) and Dolph Sweet.  The film is based on the 1959 novel "The Final Diagnosis" by Arthur Hailey.  Ronald Reagan was the narrator in the film.
