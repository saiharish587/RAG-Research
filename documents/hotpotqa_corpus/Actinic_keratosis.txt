Actinic keratosis: Actinic keratosis (AK) is a pre-cancerous patch of thick, scaly, or crusty skin.  These growths are more common in fair-skinned people and those who are frequently in the sun.  They usually form when skin gets damaged by ultraviolet (UV) radiation from the sun or indoor tanning beds. AKs are considered potentially pre-cancerous; left untreated, they may turn into a type of cancer called Squamous cell skin cancer.  Untreated lesions have up to a 20% risk of progression to squamous cell carcinoma, so treatment by a dermatologist is recommended.
