China in Your Hand: "China in Your Hand" is a song by the British band T'Pau, released from their album "Bridge of Spies".  A re-recorded version was released as a single in October 1987, spending five weeks at number one in the UK and is arguably the song for which the group is best known in their native Britain, though their debut single "Heart and Soul" was a much bigger hit in the United States.  "China in Your Hand" was the 600th single to top the UK charts and kept George Harrison's "Got My Mind Set on You" from hitting the top spot.  In 2015, the power ballad was voted by the British public as the nation's 11th favourite 1980s number one in a poll for ITV.
