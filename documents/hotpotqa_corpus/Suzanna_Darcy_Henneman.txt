Suzanna Darcy-Henneman: Suzanna Darcy-Henneman is a Boeing test pilot and lead test pilot of Boeing 777 program.  She was a pilot of a record breaking 21,602.22 km flight from Hong Kong to London over the Pacific Ocean, America, and the Atlantic on November 9, 2005 in a Boeing 777-200LR.  She joined Boeing in 1974, spent seven years as engineer, became trainer of airline pilots, and in 1985 was named Boeing Production Test pilot.
