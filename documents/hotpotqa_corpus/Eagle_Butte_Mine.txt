Eagle Butte Mine: The Eagle Butte mine is a coal mine located 7 mi north of Gillette, Wyoming in the United States in the coal-rich Powder River Basin.  The mine is an open pit, "truck and shovel", mine producing a low-sulfur, sub-bituminous coal from the Roland and Smith seams that is used for domestic energy generation.  Coal produced by the mine is shipped to its customers via railroad.  The mine is currently owned and operated by Alpha Natural Resources after being acquired in a merger with Foundation Coal in 2009.
