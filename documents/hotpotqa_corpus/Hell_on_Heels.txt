Hell on Heels: Hell on Heels is the first studio album by American country girl group Pistol Annies.  The group consists of Miranda Lambert, Ashley Monroe, and Angaleena Presley.  They released their single, "Hell on Heels," in May 2011 and released their debut album on August 23, 2011.  Pistol Annies debuted at Number 1 on Billboard’s Country Album Chart with their introductory album, Hell On Heels.  With more than 44,000 albums sold in the first week, with little to no promotion, the group lands at Number 5 on the Billboard 200 Chart.  As of June 5, 2013, the album has sold 488,000 copies in the United States.
