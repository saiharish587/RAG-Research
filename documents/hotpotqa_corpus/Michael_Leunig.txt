Michael Leunig: Michael Leunig (born 2 June 1945), typically referred to as Leunig (his signature on his cartoons), is an Australian cartoonist, poet and cultural commentator.  His best known works include "The Adventures of Vasco Pyjama" and the "Curly Flats" series.  He was declared an Australian Living Treasure by the National Trust of Australia in 1999.
