North Creek Railroad Station: The North Creek Railroad Station is a historic railroad station complex located at North Creek, Warren County, New York.  The complex consists of the railroad station, the freight house, round house, turntable, and horse barn.  The station was built in 1874 and is a simple, rectangular, gable roofed building with a broad, overhanging strut-supported roof in the Stick-Eastlake style.  Its exterior is covered with vertical boards.
