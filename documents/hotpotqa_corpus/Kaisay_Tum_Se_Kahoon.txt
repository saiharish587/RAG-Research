Kaisay Tum Se Kahoon: Kaisay Tum Se Kahoon; is a 2015 Pakistani romantic drama serial airing on Hum TV based on the novel of Maha Malik.  The series is directed by Fahim Burney and produced by Concepts & Fahim Burney, including a stellar cast of Saba Qamar, Adeel Chaudhary, Farhan Ahmed Malhi and Aleezay Tahir.  Veteran actor Irfan Khoosat is also a part of the show.
