Roman Toi: Roman Toi (born 18 June 1916 in Kõo Parish, Estonia) is an Estonian composer, choir conductor, and organist.  Influenced by Ralph Vaughan Williams, Toi's music is melodic, lyrical, and melancholic in style.  His compositional output includes nine cantatas (composed 1953–77), three symphonies (1969, 1972, 1974), and more than 80 choral works.  Many of his compositions have become part of the standard Estonian choral repertoire.
