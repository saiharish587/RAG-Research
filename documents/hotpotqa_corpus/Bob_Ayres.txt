Bob Ayres: Bob Ayres (born December 27, 1953) is an American entertainment industry executive, known among other things as the Founder of former San Francisco comedy club The Other Café, and for his involvement in San Francisco Bay Area projects such as The Other Cafe Comedy Club, The TED conference, "The Next Twenty Years series", and Comedic Ventures.  Bob is now the Licensee/Executive Producer of TEDxMarin, one of the longest running TEDx's in the country.
