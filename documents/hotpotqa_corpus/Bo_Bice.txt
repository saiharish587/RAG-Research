Bo Bice: Harold Elwin "Bo" Bice, Jr. (born November 1, 1975) is an American singer and musician who was the runner-up against Carrie Underwood in the fourth season of "American Idol".  Prior to auditioning for "American Idol", Bice released a solo album as well as a few albums with his bands while performing in the night club circuit.  In 2005, Bice charted at number two on the "Billboard" Hot 100 with a rendition of "Inside Your Heaven" from "American Idol".  He released the album "The Real Thing" after "American Idol" to minor success before being dropped by RCA Records.  He started his own record label Sugar Money and subsequently released two more albums, "See the Light" and "3".  As of 2014, he is also the lead singer of the touring version of Blood, Sweat & Tears.
