Mircea David: Mircea David (16 October 1914 – 12 October 1993) was a Romanian football player, who played as a goalkeeper.  After a match between Romania and Italy, played in Rome, he was nicknamed by the Italian football fans "Il Dio", because of his incredible saves.  He was a member of the Romania national football team which competed at the 1938 FIFA World Cup, but did not play any match.
