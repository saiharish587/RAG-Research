Carmine Appice: Carmine Appice (born December 15, 1946) is an American drummer and percussionist most commonly associated with the rock genre of music.  He has received classical music training, and was influenced early-on by the work of jazz drummers Buddy Rich and Gene Krupa.  Appice is best known for his associations with Vanilla Fudge, Cactus, the power trio Beck, Bogert & Appice, Rod Stewart, King Kobra, and Blue Murder, which also featured John Sykes of Whitesnake and Thin Lizzy fame, and Tony Franklin of The Firm.  He was inducted into the "Classic Drummer" Hall of Fame in 2013, and the "Modern Drummer" Hall of Fame in 2014.
