Hard Core (Lil' Kim album): Hard Core is the debut studio album by American rapper Lil' Kim.  The album was released on November 12, 1996 by Undeas Recordings, Big Beat Records, and Atlantic Records.  After achieving a success with the hip hop group Junior M.A.F.I.A. and their album "Conspiracy" (1995), Kim began working on her solo album with The Notorious B.I.G. serving as the executive producer (besides this, he performed on four songs).  She collaborated with a number of producers, such as Sean "Puff Daddy" Combs, Stevie J., David "Ski" Willis and Jermaine Dupri, among others.  Other rappers, including Jay Z, Lil' Cease and Puff Daddy were featured on the album.  Recording for the album took place from 1995 to 1996, mainly at The Hit Factory studio, in New York City.
