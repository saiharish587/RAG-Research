Plastic optical fiber: Plastic optical fiber (POF) (or Polymer optical fibre) is an optical fiber that is made out of polymer.  Similar to glass optical fiber, POF transmits light (for illumination or data) through the core of the fiber.  Its chief advantage over the glass product, other aspect being equal, is its robustness under bending and stretching.  Optical fiber used in telecommunications is governed by European Standards EN 60793-2-40-2011.
