Kim Min-seo: Kim Min-seo (born March 16, 1984) is a South Korean actress.  She debuted as Kim Se-ha in the short-lived K-pop three-member girl group Mint, which was active in 1999-2000.  When Mint disbanded, she turned to acting.  Kim is best known for her role as a villainous queen consort in the period drama "Moon Embracing the Sun" (2012).
