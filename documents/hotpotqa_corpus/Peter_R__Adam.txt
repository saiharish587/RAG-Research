Peter R. Adam: Peter R. Adam (born May 29, 1957 in Pirmasens, Germany) is a film editor.  He has worked on such films as "An American Werewolf in Paris", "Good Bye, Lenin! ", and "Anonymous".  Adam was one of the first to use digital editing tools.  He won the "Deutscher Filmpreis" for editing in 1998 for his work on "Comedian Harmonists".
