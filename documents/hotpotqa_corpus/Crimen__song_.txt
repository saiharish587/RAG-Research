Crimen (song): "'Crimen'" (Spanish for "Crime") is a song written and recorded by Argentine rock musician Gustavo Cerati.  It is one of the most popular songs from his solo career and it's recognized as one of his signature songs.  It was released as the twelfth track of the album Ahí vamos (Spanish for "There we go"), in 2006.  The song won the Latin Grammy in 2007 and the MTV Awards in 2006 for "Best Rock Song" among other awards ("See "Crimen" Awards").
