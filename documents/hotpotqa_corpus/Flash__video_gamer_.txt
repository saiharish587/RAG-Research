Flash (video gamer): Lee Young-ho (born 5 July 1992 이영호) is a South Korean "" and "StarCraft II" player who played Terran for the Korean pro-gaming team KT Rolster under the alias By.FlaSh or simply Flash.  He is widely considered the best "StarCraft" player of all time.  Lee began playing "StarCraft II" competitively in 2011, until his retirement in December 2015.  He subsequently returned to playing "Starcraft: Brood War", and started his personal broadcast in February 2016 on the AfreecaTV personal broadcasting platform.  Since returning to "Brood War", Lee has won first place in both Season 2 and Season 3 of the Afreeca Starleague.
