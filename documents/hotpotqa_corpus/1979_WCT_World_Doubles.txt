1979 WCT World Doubles: The 1979 WCT World Doubles, also known by its sponsored name Braniff Airways World Doubles Championship, was a men's tennis tournament played on indoor carpet courts at Olympia in London, England that was part of the 1979 Colgate-Palmolive Grand Prix.  It was the tour finals for the doubles season of the WCT Tour section.  The tournament was held from January 3 through January 7, 1979.
