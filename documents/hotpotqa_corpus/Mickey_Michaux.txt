Mickey Michaux: Henry McKinley "Mickey" Michaux Jr. (born September 4, 1930) is a Democratic member of the North Carolina General Assembly representing the state's thirty-first House district since 1983.  He previously served from 1973 through 1977.  His district includes constituents in Durham County.  s of 2014 , Michaux is the longest-serving member of the North Carolina General Assembly.  In the 2007-2008 session, Michaux served as senior chairman of the House Appropriations Committee and chairman of the House Select Committee on Street Gang Prevention.
