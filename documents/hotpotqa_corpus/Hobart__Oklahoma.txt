Hobart, Oklahoma: Hobart is a city and the county seat of Kiowa County, Oklahoma, United States.  It was named for Garret Hobart, the 24th Vice President of the United States.  The population was 3,756 at the 2010 census, a decline of 6.0 percent from 3,997 at the 2000 census.  It is served by Hobart Regional Airport.  It also has 2 museums: the General Tommy Franks Museum and the Kiowa County Museum.
