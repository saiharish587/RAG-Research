Bell Sensplex: The Bell Sensplex is a four-pad ice facility, located in the city of Ottawa, Ontario, Canada.  It is the primary practice facility of the Ottawa Senators NHL team.  In a partnership with the City of Ottawa, it is also used for minor hockey and hosts the annual Bell Capital Cup ice hockey tournament.  The Sensplex is located at 1565 Maple Grove Road in the Kanata district of Ottawa.  It is also the home arena for the Ottawa Senators of the Canadian Women's Hockey League.
