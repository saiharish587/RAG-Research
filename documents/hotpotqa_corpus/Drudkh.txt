Drudkh: Drudkh is a Ukrainian black metal band.  It currently consists of Roman Saenko (former member of Hate Forest and Dark Ages), Thurios (former member of Astrofaes), Krechet, and Vlad.  All four also belonged to Blood of Kingu until it was disbanded.  Their lyrics embrace Slavic mythology.  Many of the band's lyrics are derived from the works of nineteenth- and twentieth-century Ukrainian poets, especially Taras Shevchenko.
