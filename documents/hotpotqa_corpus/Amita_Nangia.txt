Amita Nangia: Amita Nangia (also known as Ameeta Nangia) is a TV actress and character artist in Bollywood.  Her initial popularity was from her role as Sheena in TV serial "Tara", broadcast on Zee TV in 1993.  She played roles in many A, B and C grade Bollywood movies, and was among the five girls of popular TV serial Hum Paanch (sitcom), also on Zee TV.  She has also acted in Bollywood B movies like "Purani Haveli" and "College Girl".
