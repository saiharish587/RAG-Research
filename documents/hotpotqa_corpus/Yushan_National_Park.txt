Yushan National Park: Yushan National Park () is one of the nine national parks in Taiwan and was named after the summit Yushan, the highest peak of the park.  The park covers a total of 103,121 hectares including large sections of the Central Mountain Range.  The park contains over thirty peaks more than 3,000 meters in elevation, and two-thirds of the area within the park is above 2,000 meters.  The elevation difference in the park is 3,600 meters, and there are many canyons, cliffs, and valleys.
