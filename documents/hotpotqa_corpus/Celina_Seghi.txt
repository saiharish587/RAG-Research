Celina Seghi: Celina Seghi (born 6 March 1920) is a former Italian alpine skier.  Born in Abetone, Tuscany, she was the youngest child in a family of nine and earned her first Italian championship medal, a bronze in the slalom, in 1934.  In 1937 she earned three national titles, her first victories at that level, by winning the slalom, downhill, and combined events.  In total she won a total of 25 gold, 6 silver, and 3 bronze medals at the Italian National Championships: gold seven times, silver thrice, and bronze twice in the downhill, gold and silver once each in the giant slalom, gold ten times, silver twice, and bronze once in the slalom, and gold seven times in the combined.
