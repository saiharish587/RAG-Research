Marin Mazzie: Marin Joy Mazzie (born October 9, 1960) is an American actress and singer known for her work in musical theater.  She was nominated for the Tony Award, Drama Desk Award and Olivier Award for her role as Lilli/Katharine in "Kiss Me, Kate", and won the Outer Critics Circle Award.  In addition to appearing in many musical stage productions, Mazzie also performs in concert with her husband, Jason Danieley.
