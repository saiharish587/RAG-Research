Airplane II: The Sequel: Airplane II: The Sequel (titled Flying High II: The Sequel in Australia, New Zealand, South Africa, Japan, and the Philippines) is a 1982 American parody film.  It is the sequel to the 1980 film "Airplane! ".  Released on December 10, 1982, the film was written and directed by Ken Finkleman and stars Robert Hays, Julie Hagerty, Lloyd Bridges, Chad Everett, William Shatner, Rip Torn, and Sonny Bono.  The team who wrote and directed the original "Airplane!"  (Jim Abrahams, David Zucker, and Jerry Zucker) had no involvement with this sequel.
