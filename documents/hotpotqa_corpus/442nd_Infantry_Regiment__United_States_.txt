442nd Infantry Regiment (United States): The 442nd Regimental Combat Team is an infantry regiment of the United States Army, part of the Army Reserve.  The regiment was a fighting unit composed almost entirely of American soldiers of Japanese ancestry (mostly from Hawaii) who fought in World War II.  Most of the families of mainland Japanese Americans were confined to internment camps in the United States interior.  Beginning in 1944, the regiment fought primarily in Europe during World War II, in particular Italy, southern France, and Germany.
