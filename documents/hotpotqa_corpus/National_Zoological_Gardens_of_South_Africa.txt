National Zoological Gardens of South Africa: The National Zoological Gardens of South Africa (also informally known as The Pretoria Zoo) is an 85 ha zoo located in Pretoria, South Africa.  It is the national zoo of South Africa, and was founded by J. W. B. Gunning in 1899.  Pretoria zoo is one of the top 8 largest zoos in the world and one of the most highly rated zoo's in the world.
