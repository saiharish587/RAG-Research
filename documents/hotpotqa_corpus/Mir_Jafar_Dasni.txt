Mir Jafar Dasni: Mir Jafar bin Mir Hasan Dasni (Kurdish: Mîr Ceferê Dasnî‎ , Arabic: الأمير جعفر بن الأمير حسن الداسني‎ ‎ ), also known as Jafar bin Faharjis, was a Yazidi and was member of the ethno-religious group of the Yazidis leader who in 838 launched an uprising against Abbasid Caliph al-Mutasim in the area north of Mosul.  After being defeated at Babagesh he resided in castles in Dasin.  Mutasim in response sent an army under command of Abdullah bin Ans al-Azdi, resulting in an armed confrontation between the Arab army and Kurds.  Due to the difficulty of the terrain, the Arab army proved unsuccessful, suffered heavy casualties and a number of its commanders including Abdullah's uncle Ishaq bin Ans and his father-in-law were killed.
