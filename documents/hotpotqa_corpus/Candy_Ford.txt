Candy Ford: Candy Ford (born May 1, 1975) is an American comedian and television actress, best known for starring in the sketch comedy, "The Rerun Show", Ford has also appeared in other TV programs including: "Curb Your Enthusiasm", "Will & Grace", and she provided voicework for the "Law & Order" videogame, and starred on the short-lived NBC sketch comedy, "The Rerun Show" and voiced Trixie in the film "The Country Bears" and later starred in "Girls Behaving Badly".
