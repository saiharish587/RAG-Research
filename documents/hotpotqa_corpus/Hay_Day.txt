Hay Day: Hay Day is a freemium mobile farming game developed and published by Supercell.  Hay Day was released for iOS on June 21, 2012 and Android on November 20, 2013.  According to a 2013 report, Supercell earned $30 million a month from Hay Day and Clash of Clans, another game made by Supercell.  In 2013, Hay Day was the 4th highest game in revenue generated.
