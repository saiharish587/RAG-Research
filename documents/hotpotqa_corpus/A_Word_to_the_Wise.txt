A Word to the Wise: A Word to the Wise is a 1770 comedy play by the Irish writer Hugh Kelly.  It was his second work after the 1767 hit "False Delicacy".  Kelly was known as a supporter of the government, and an opponent of the radical John Wilkes.  During the second performance of the play a riot broke out amongst Wilkes' supporters at the Drury Lane Theatre and the play was subsequently withdrawn.  When it was published instead Kelly wrote a long introduction defending himself and complaining about political prejudice.  It was the first play performed at The Theatre, Leeds when it was opened on 24 May 1771.  It was later staged by the American Company in North America.
