Peter Pan (1950 musical): Peter Pan is a 1950 musical adaptation of J. M. Barrie's play "Peter Pan, or The Boy Who Wouldn't Grow Up" with music and lyrics by Leonard Bernstein; it opened on Broadway on April 24, 1950.  This version starred Jean Arthur as Peter Pan, Boris Karloff in the dual roles of George Darling and Captain Hook, and Marcia Henderson as Wendy.  The show was orchestrated by Hershy Kay and conducted by Benjamin Steinberg.  The show ran for 321 performances, closing on January 27, 1951.
