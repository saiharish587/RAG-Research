Helena Andrews: Helena Andrews (born October 28, 1980) is an author, journalist and pop culture critic.  Her first book, "Bitch is the New Black", was published by HarperCollins in June 2010.  "Bitch is the New Black" is a collection of essays chronicling her experiences as a single black female in Washington, D.C. First conceptualized as a daily blog documenting the sad state of dating among educated African Americans, "Bitch is the New Black" evolved to describe all the influences and impacts on the modern black woman.  Andrews is currently writing a screenplay for the movie version of the book.  The film rights have been optioned by "Grey's Anatomy" creator/executive producer Shonda Rhimes, who will serve as executive producer for the project.
