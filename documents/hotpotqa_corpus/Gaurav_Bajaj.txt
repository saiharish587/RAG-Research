Gaurav Bajaj: Gaurav Bajaj (born 16th November 1990) is an Indian film actor and producer "Notorious Owl Pictures".  He is a very well known actor in Indian Film and TV Industry and has appeared in several TVCs for brands like Mentos, HSBC bank, Snickers, Close-up, Pepsi, Microsoft, Adidas, Minute Maid, Cornetto, TVS, 8PM and many more.  He has done films such as "Phir Kabhi", "Tor Naam", "Kirkit" and "Vroom".  He is now actively working in various Tv shows, known for their experimental content.  Gaurav has worked with some of the biggest names in the industry like Pradeep Sarkaar, Shad Ali, VKP, Abhinay Deol etc.  His production house successfully made India's first youth based Webshow "BTW".  They are also engaged in producing short films, and have co-produced show named "Bad Company" with Lost Boy Productions.
