Lucie Memba: Lucie Memba was born (Lucie Memba Bos,in 1987) is a Cameroonian actress, movie producer who have starred in both series and movies in French and English language.She was honored for best lead actress in Cinema of Cameroon for French speaking actress at Cameroon Movies Merit Award (CMMA) 2013 edition.  She did her International debut with Nollywood stars in the movie Pink Poison featuring Jim Iyke and Far starred along side Nigerian Dakore Akande
