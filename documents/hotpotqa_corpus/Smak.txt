Smak: Smak (Serbian Cyrillic: Смак; trans.  "The end time") is a Serbian and former Yugoslav rock band from Kragujevac.  The group reached the peak of popularity in the 1970s when it was one of the most notable acts of the former Yugoslav rock scene.  The band's leader, guitarist Radomir Mihailović, nicknamed Točak ("The Wheel"), is considered one of the most influential guitarists on the former Yugoslav rock scene.
