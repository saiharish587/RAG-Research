The Captain from Connecticut: The Captain from Connecticut is a novel by C. S. Forester, the author of the novels about fictional Royal Navy officer Horatio Hornblower.  "The Captain from Connecticut" is set at the tail end of the Napoleonic Wars, and the War of 1812.  It was written at the beginning of World War II.  Forester wanted to write a novel where both American and British sailors could be heroic and admirable.
