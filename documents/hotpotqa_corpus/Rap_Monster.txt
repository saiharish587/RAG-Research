Rap Monster: Kim Nam-joon (Hangul: 김남준 ; born September 12, 1994), better known as Rap Monster, is a South Korean rapper, songwriter, and record producer.  He is the main rapper and songwriter of the South Korean boy group BTS, managed under Big Hit Entertainment.  In 2015, he released his first solo mixtape, "RM".  To date, he has recorded with artists such as Wale, Warren G, Gaeko, Krizz Kaliko, MFBTY, and Primary.
