Tha Chomphu Railway Station: Tha Chomphu Railway Station is a railway station located in Tha Pladuk Subdistrict, Mae Tha District, Lamphun.  It is a class 3 railway station located 691.898 km from Bangkok Railway Station.  The station is the railway station closest to Tha Chomphu Bridge, or also known as the "White Bridge" as it was built of white concrete.
