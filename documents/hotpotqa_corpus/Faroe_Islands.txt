Faroe Islands: The Faroe Islands ( ; Faroese: "Føroyar" ] ; Danish: "Færøerne" , ] ), sometimes called the Faeroe Islands, are an archipelago between the Norwegian Sea and the North Atlantic, about halfway between Norway and Iceland, 200 mi north-northwest of Scotland.  The islands are an autonomous country within the Kingdom of Denmark.  Their area is about 1,400 km2 with a population of 50,030 in April 2017.
