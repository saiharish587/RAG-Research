American Ninja Warrior (season 7): The seventh season of "American Ninja Warrior" premiered on NBC on May 25, 2015.  Hosts Matt Iseman & Akbar Gbaja-Biamila returned for their respective sixth and third seasons while newcomer Kristine Leahy joined as sideline reporter, replacing Jenn Brown.  In addition, this season's grand prize was increased from $500,000 to $1,000,000.  The season concluded on September 14, 2015.
