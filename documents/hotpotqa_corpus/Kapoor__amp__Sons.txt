Kapoor &amp; Sons: Kapoor & Sons, also known as Kapoor & Sons (Since 1921), is an Indian Hindi-language buddy romantic drama film directed by Shakun Batra and produced by Karan Johar, Hiroo Yash Johar, and Apoorva Mehta under the banners of Dharma Productions and Fox Star Studios.  The film stars an ensemble cast featuring Rishi Kapoor, Ratna Pathak, Rajat Kapoor, Sidharth Malhotra, Fawad Khan and Alia Bhatt.  "Kapoor & Sons" tells the story of two estranged brothers who return to their dysfunctional family after their grandfather suffers a cardiac arrest.
