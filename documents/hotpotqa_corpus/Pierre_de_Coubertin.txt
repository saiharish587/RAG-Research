Pierre de Coubertin: Pierre de Coubertin (] ; 1 January 1863 – 2 September 1937, born Pierre de Frédy, also known as Baron de Coubertin) was a French educator and historian, and founder of the International Olympic Committee.  He is considered the father of the modern Olympic Games.  Born into a French aristocratic family, he became an academic and studied a broad range of topics, most notably education and history.
