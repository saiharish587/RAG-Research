2008 Bank Austria-TennisTrophy: The 2008 Bank Austria-TennisTrophy was a tennis tournament played on indoor hard courts.  It was the 34th edition of the event known that year as the Bank Austria-TennisTrophy, and was part of the International Series Gold of the 2008 ATP Tour.  It took place at the Wiener Stadthalle in Vienna, Austria, from October 6 through October 12, 2008.
