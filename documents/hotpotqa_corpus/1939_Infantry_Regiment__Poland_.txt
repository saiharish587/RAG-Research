1939 Infantry Regiment (Poland): The Polish Infantry Regiment during World War 2 (in Polish Pulk Piechoty) comprised on average some 2,900 men and 60 officers organised around 3 rifle battalions armed with the Mauser 98k 7.92mm bolt-action rifle.  Each 19-man squad was also issued the RKM wz.28 light machine gun.  Other regimental weapons included the Polish version of the French Model 1897 75-mm field gun, the Wz.  35 anti-tank rifle, the Ckm wz.30 heavy machine gun, the wz.31 81 mm mortar, and the wz.36 46mm light mortar/grenade launcher.
