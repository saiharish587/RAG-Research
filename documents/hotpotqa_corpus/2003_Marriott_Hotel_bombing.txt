2003 Marriott Hotel bombing: The 2003 Marriott Hotel bombing occurred on 5 August 2003 in Mega Kuningan, South Jakarta, Indonesia.  A suicide bomber detonated a car bomb outside the lobby of the JW Marriott Hotel, killing twelve people and injuring 150.  Those killed were mostly Indonesian, with the exception of one Dutch man.  The hotel was viewed as a Western symbol, and had been used by the United States embassy for various events.  The hotel was closed for five weeks and reopened to the public on 8 September.
