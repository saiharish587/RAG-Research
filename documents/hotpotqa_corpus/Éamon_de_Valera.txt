Éamon de Valera: Éamon de Valera ( ; ] ; first registered as George de Valero; changed some time before 1901 to Edward de Valera; 14 October 1882 – 29 August 1975) was a prominent politician and statesman in 20th-century Ireland.  His political career spanned over half a century, from 1917 to 1973; he served several terms as head of government and head of state.  He also led the introduction of the Constitution of Ireland.
