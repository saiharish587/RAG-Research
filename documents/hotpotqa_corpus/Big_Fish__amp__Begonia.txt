Big Fish &amp; Begonia: Big Fish & Begonia (original title: "Da Yu Hai Tang"), is a 2016 Chinese animated epic fantasy film written, produced and directed by Liang Xuan and Zhang Chun.  The first animated feature film of B&T Studio collaborated with Studio Mir, it is joint invested by B&T and Enlight Media.  It was released in both 2D and 3D formats in China by Enlight Media on 8 July 2016.
