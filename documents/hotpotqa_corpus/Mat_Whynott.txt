Mat Whynott: Mathew Whynott is a Canadian politician, who was elected to the Nova Scotia House of Assembly in the 2009 provincial election.  From 2009 to 2013, he represented the electoral district of Hammonds Plains-Upper Sackville as a member of the New Democratic Party.  He is the youngest person ever elected as MLA in Nova Scotia.  Whynott is majoring in political science at St. Mary's University.  He lives in Lucasville with his wife Charlotte and daughter Morgan.
