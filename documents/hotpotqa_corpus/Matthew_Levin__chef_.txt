Matthew Levin (chef): Matthew Levin is an American celebrity chef based in Philadelphia.  He was the executive chef at Lacroix in the famed Rittenhouse Hotel until December 9, 2008.  From 2010 to July 24, 2011, he was the chef at Adsum, a Queen Village bistro where he gained notoriety for dishes including Tastykake sliders and his Four Loco dinner.  In March 2012, along with Cuba Libre owners Barry Gutin and Larry Cohen, he will take over as chef and co-owner of Square Peg, at the former location of Marathon Grill at 10th and Walnut St.
