Sailing at the Youth Olympic Games: Sailing has featured as a sport at the Youth Olympic Summer Games since its first edition in 2010.  The Youth Olympic Games are multi-sport event and the games are held every four years just like the Olympic Games.  With sailing limited to four events sailing has chosen to feature athlete under 16 in two disciplines.  This allows older youth competitors in sailing to focus on the Olympic disciplines through events like the ISAF Youth Sailing World Championships as the games could permit sailors up to 18 years old.
