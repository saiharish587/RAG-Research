Saint Etienne (band): Saint Etienne are an English band from London, formed in 1990.  The band consists of Sarah Cracknell, Bob Stanley and Pete Wiggs.  They became associated with the UK's indie dance scene in the 1990s, beginning with the release of their debut album "Foxbase Alpha" in 1991.  Their work has been described as uniting 1990s club culture with 1960s pop and other disparate influences.
