Aniruddha Bahal: Aniruddha Bahal (born 1967) is an Indian journalist, author, founder and editor of the online magazine "Cobrapost.com".  Born in Allahabad, Bahal worked as a journalist and editor for "India Today" and "Outlook".  In 1999, he along with Tarun Tejpal co-founded "Tehelka", an investigative news website.  While at Tehelka, Bahal conceived and carried out a sting operation which caught members of Indian Cricket Team accepting bribes to throw matches in camera.  It resulted in a series of articles on match fixing in Indian Cricket, which were eventually published as a book -" Fallen Heroes".  Bahal is also known for his part in Operation West End, another sting operation.  In 2003, he left "Tehelka".
