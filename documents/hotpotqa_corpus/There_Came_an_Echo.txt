There Came an Echo: There Came an Echo is a real-time tactics video game developed for the PlayStation 4, Microsoft Windows through Steam and Xbox One platforms, and was released on 24 February 2015.  The player can use a voice control system to direct their units around the battlefield to ensure a tactical advantage over the enemy.  The game features voice actors Wil Wheaton, Ashly Burch, Yuri Lowenthal, Laura Bailey and Cassandra Morris.
