Pierrot (band): Pierrot (stylized as PIERROT) was a Japanese visual kei rock band formed in 1994 in Nagano.  After changing their name from Dizy-Lizy to Pierrot and several member changes, the final lineup was completed in 1995 with Kirito on vocals, Jun and Aiji on guitar, Kohta on bass and Takeo on drums.  After roughly ten years together, Pierrot disbanded in 2006.  Their final single was named "Hello", an apt title for a band who started their major career with an album called "Finale".
