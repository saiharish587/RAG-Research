Mists of Dawn: Mists of Dawn is a juvenile science fiction novel by science fiction writer and anthropologist Chad Oliver first published in 1952 by John C. Winston, Co. as a part of the Winston Science Fiction series of juvenile novels.  The story follows the adventures of adolescent Mark Nye when he is accidentally transported to the stone age by his uncle's time machine.  It includes a factual foreword on the science of anthropology and how Oliver uses this science in the telling of his story.
