Virulence (journal): Virulence is a peer reviewed medical journal that covers microbiology and immunology specifically, microorganism pathogenicity, the infection process and host–pathogen interactions.  It is published 8 times per year by Taylor & Francis.  It was previously published 6 times per year by Landes Bioscience.  The journal was established in 2010 by Eva M. Riedmann, and Eleftherios Mylonakis.  The editor-in-chief is Eleftherios Mylonakis.
