GEICO advertising campaigns: GEICO advertising campaigns are known for using surreal humor and satire, often featuring distinctive characters such as the company's mascot, the GEICO gecko.  The advertising strategy incorporates a saturation-level amount of print (primarily mail circulars) and television parody advertisements, as well as radio advertisements.  A common line used by GEICO is "15 minutes could save you 15% or more on car insurance."
