Tabcorp Park Menangle: Tabcorp Park Menangle is a harness racing track in Sydney.  Their main races are the Miracle Mile Pace, the NSW Derby, the NSW Oaks, the Len Smith Mile and the qualifiers for the Miracle Mile, the Allied Express Sprint and the Canadian Club Sprint.  It replaced Harold Park as Sydney's premier harness track in 2010.  Fred Hastings and Anthony Manton are two callers that have called at the track before.
