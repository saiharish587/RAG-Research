San Diego Community College District: The San Diego Community College District (SDCCD) is a public community college district in the city of San Diego, California.  The district is one of the five community college districts in San Diego County; part of the greater California Community College system.  Under the California Master Plan for Higher Education, the California Community College system is a part of the state's three-tier public higher education system, which also includes the University of California system and California State University system.
