Dangerous (book): Dangerous is a book by British media personality and journalist Milo Yiannopoulos, published on July 4, 2017.  The book was originally due for release on June 13, 2017 by Threshold Editions, a division of Simon & Schuster, but its release was canceled on February 20 due to an audio release that heard Yiannopoulos praise aspects of pedophilia having a positive impact on children.  A day after its announcement, pre-sales for the book briefly elevated it to first place on Amazon.com's list of best-sellers.  It returned to number one on February 1, 2017, a day after a violent disturbance occurred at UC Berkeley which deterred Yiannopoulos from speaking there.
