Joe Odom: Joe Edward Odom (born December 14, 1979 in Alton, Illinois) is an American football linebacker.  He was selected in the sixth round of the 2003 NFL Draft with the 191st overall pick by the Chicago Bears out of Purdue University.  On August 23, 2006, Odom was claimed off of waivers by the Buffalo Bills.  He was released on September 2, 2006.  He is married to Aline Odom from Brazil and has two daughters Stella Odom and Luna Odom.
