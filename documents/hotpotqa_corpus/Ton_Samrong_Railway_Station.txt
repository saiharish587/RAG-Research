Ton Samrong Railway Station: Ton Samrong Railway Station is a railway station located in Thammasala Subdistrict, Nakhon Pathom City, Nakhon Pathom.  It is a class 3 railway station located 44.301 km from Thon Buri Railway Station.  Originally, the station was built 300 metres from the present-day location, near a curve.  But it was moved possibly because it was difficult to expand the railway station into a railyard, and there was not enough space near a curve for building the double-track section.
