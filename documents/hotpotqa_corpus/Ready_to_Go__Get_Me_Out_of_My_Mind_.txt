Ready to Go (Get Me Out of My Mind): "Ready to Go (Get Me Out of My Mind)" (commonly referred to as simply "Ready to Go") is a song by American rock band Panic!  at the Disco, released June 6, 2011, as the second single from the group's third studio album "Vices & Virtues" (2011).  A clip of the song was used on the band's short film, "The Overture".  The song received positive critical reviews on its release.
