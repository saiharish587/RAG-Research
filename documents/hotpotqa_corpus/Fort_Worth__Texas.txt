Fort Worth, Texas: Fort Worth is the 16th-largest city in the United States and the fifth-largest city in the state of Texas.  The city is in North Central Texas and covers nearly 350 sqmi in the counties of Denton, Parker, Wise, and Tarrant, of which it is the county seat.  According to the 2016 census estimates, Fort Worth's population is 854,113.  The city is the second-largest in the Dallas–Fort Worth–Arlington metropolitan area (the "DFW Metroplex").
