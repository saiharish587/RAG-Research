NGC 7016: NGC 7016 is an elliptical or lenticular galaxy located about 480 million Light-years away from Earth in the constellation Capricornus.  NGC 7016's calculated velocity is 11,046 km/s.  NGC 7016 has an estimated diameter of about 140 thousand light years.  NGC 7016 was discovered by American astronomer Francis Preserved Leavenworth on July 8, 1885.
