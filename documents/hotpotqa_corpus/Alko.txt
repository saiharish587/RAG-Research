Alko: Alko is the national alcoholic beverage retailing monopoly in Finland.  It is the only store in the country which retails beer over 4.7% ABV, wine (except in vineyards) and spirits.  Alcoholic beverages are also sold in licensed restaurants and bars but only for consumption on the premises.  Alko is required by law to sell drinks with lower alcohol content than 4.7% and non-alcoholic alternatives, but in practice carries a very limited stock of low alcohol beer, cider and non-alcoholic drinks and mixers as supermarkets sell the same products at a lower price.  By law, alcoholic drinks may only be sold to those aged 18 or above.
