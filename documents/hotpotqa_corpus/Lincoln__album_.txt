Lincoln (album): Lincoln is the second album by the band They Might Be Giants.  It was released by Bar/None in 1988.  The album is named after the capital city of Lincoln, Nebraska and John Linnell and John Flansburgh's boyhood home of Lincoln, Massachusetts.  The album produced three singles — "Ana Ng", "They'll Need a Crane", and "Purple Toupee".  It is included on "", a compilation of the band's early material, in its entirety.
