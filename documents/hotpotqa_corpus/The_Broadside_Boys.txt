The Broadside Boys: The Broadside Boys are a folk band that formed in 2010.  Originally consisting of two long term friends, Mat Bayfield and Eric Sedge, the band currently play at events throughout their home county of Suffolk, although they have occasionally also ventured to other parts of East Anglia.  The 'Broadside' part of the band's name comes from Broadside Ale, brewed by Adnams in Southwold, Suffolk.
