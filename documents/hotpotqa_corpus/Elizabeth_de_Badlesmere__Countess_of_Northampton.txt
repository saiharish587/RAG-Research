Elizabeth de Badlesmere, Countess of Northampton: Elizabeth de Badlesmere, Countess of Northampton (1313 – 8 June 1356) was the wife of two English noblemen, Sir Edmund Mortimer and William de Bohun, 1st Earl of Northampton.  She was a co-heiress of her brother Giles de Badlesmere, 2nd Baron Badlesmere.
