The Chris Isaak Show: The Chris Isaak Show is a television sitcom which follows a fictionalized version of the life of American rock musician Chris Isaak.  The show portrays Isaak and his band members as everyday people with everyday problems.  The series was produced for the Showtime channel from 2001–2004, and was filmed in Vancouver, British Columbia, Canada, which stands in for the show's home setting of San Francisco.
