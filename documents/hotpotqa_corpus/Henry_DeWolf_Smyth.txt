Henry DeWolf Smyth: Henry DeWolf "Harry" Smyth ( ; May 1, 1898 – September 11, 1986) was an American physicist, diplomat, and bureaucrat.  He played a number of key roles in the early development of nuclear energy, as a participant in the Manhattan Project, a member of the U.S. Atomic Energy Commission (AEC), and U.S. ambassador to the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA).
