Alberte Winding: Elin Alberte Leonora Winding (born August 23, 1963), commonly known as Alberte, is a Danish singer and actress, and the daughter of Thomas Winding and Lulu Gauguin.  She grew up in Copenhagen and on the island of Ærø.  She worked together with her brothers Kasper Winding and Aske Bentzon on their father's programs for Denmark Radio.
