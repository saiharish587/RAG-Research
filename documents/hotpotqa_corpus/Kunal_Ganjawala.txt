Kunal Ganjawala: Kunal Ganjawala (born 14 April 1972) is a popular playback singer whose songs are mostly featured in Hindi and Kannada films.  He has also sung in Marathi, Bengali and other official languages of India.  Kunal began his career by singing jingles.  He came to limelight in Hindi with the song "Bheege Honth Tere" from the film "Murder" in 2004.  It was his first biggest hit.  The song earned him Zee Cine Award as Best Playback Singer in 2005.  He came to limelight in Kannada with the song "Neene Neene" from the film "Akash" in 2005.
