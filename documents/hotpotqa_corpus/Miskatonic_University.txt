Miskatonic University: Miskatonic University is a fictional university located in Arkham, a fictitious town in Essex County, Massachusetts.  It is named after the Miskatonic River (also fictional).  After first appearing in H. P. Lovecraft's 1922 story "Herbert West–Reanimator", the school appeared in numerous Cthulhu Mythos stories by Lovecraft and other writers.  The story "The Dunwich Horror" implies that Miskatonic University is a highly prestigious university, on par with Harvard University, and that Harvard and Miskatonic are the two most popular schools for the children of the Massachusetts “Old Gentry”.  The university also appears in role-playing games and board games based on the mythos.
