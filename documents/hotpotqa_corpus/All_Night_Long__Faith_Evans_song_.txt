All Night Long (Faith Evans song): "All Night Long " is a song by American recording artist Faith Evans, featuring guest vocals by Puff Daddy.  It was produced by Evans, Ron "Amen-Ra" Lawrence, and Sean Combs for her second album "Keep the Faith" (1998).  it features a sample of Unlimited Touch's 1980 hit "I Hear Music in the Streets".  Upon its release, the song reached number 9 on the US "Billboard" Hot 100 and number 3 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart.
