Bloody Tourists: Bloody Tourists is the sixth studio album by the English rock band 10cc, released worldwide by Mercury Records and in North America by Polydor Records in September 1978.  Recorded at Strawberry Studios South in Dorking, the album was produced by Eric Stewart and Graham Gouldman.  The cover art was created by Hipgnosis with graphics by George Hardie.  The cover photograph was taken by Aubrey Powell of Hipgnosis.
