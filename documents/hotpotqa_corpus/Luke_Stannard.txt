Luke Stannard: Robert Luke Stannard is an American gymnast.  He competed for Illinois from 2007 to 2010.  In 2009, he won the gold medal on pommel horse at the National championships.  In 2010, he placed fourth at NCAA championships on pommel horse and thus earned all-American honors.  That year, he was awarded the Nissen-Emery, the gymnastics version of the Heisman.
