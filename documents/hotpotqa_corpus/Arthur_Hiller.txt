Arthur Hiller: Arthur Hiller, OC (November 22, 1923 – August 17, 2016) was a Canadian-American television and film director, having directed over 33 films during his 50-year career.  He began his career directing television in Canada and later in the U.S. By the late 1950s he began directing films, most often comedies.  He also directed dramas and romantic subjects, such as "Love Story" (1970), which was nominated for seven Oscars.
