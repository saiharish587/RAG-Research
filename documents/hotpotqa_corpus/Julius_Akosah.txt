Julius Akosah: Julius Pongla Akosah ( "Akosah", born (1982--)16 1982 ) is a Cameroonian-Hongkonger professional association football player, his position is striker.  He was called for Cameroon but he is eligible to play for Hong Kong in international tournaments.  Hong Kong football fans commonly use Agumon as a nickname for him because of his transcriptional name.  He is currently playing for Hong Kong First Division club Happy Valley.
