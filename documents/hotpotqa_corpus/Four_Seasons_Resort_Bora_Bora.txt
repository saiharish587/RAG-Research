Four Seasons Resort Bora Bora: Four Seasons Resort Bora Bora is a luxury resort located at Motu Tehotu on the island of Bora Bora, in the Society Islands of French Polynesia.  The Resort is a part of Four Seasons Hotels and Resorts, a Toronto-based hotel management company.  Four Seasons Resort Bora Bora is the company's first and only property in French Polynesia.  The Resort was named the #1 Resort in French Polynesia in 2012, according to Condé Nast Traveler.
