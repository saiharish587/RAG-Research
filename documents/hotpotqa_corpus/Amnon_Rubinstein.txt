Amnon Rubinstein: Amnon Rubinstein (Hebrew: אמנון רובינשטיין‎ ‎ , born 5 September 1931) is an Israeli law scholar, politician, and columnist.  A member of the Knesset between 1977 and 2002, he served in several ministerial positions.  He is currently dean of the Interdisciplinary Center (IDC) in Herzliya and a patron of Liberal International.
