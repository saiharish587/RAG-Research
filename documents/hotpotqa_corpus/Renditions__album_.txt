Renditions (album): Renditions is the first studio album by Amelia Warner under the name Slow Moving Millie.  The album features the songs 'Please Please Please Let Me Get What I Want' which was the song for the John Lewis 2011 Christmas advert, and 'Beasts' which was used in a Virgin Media TV advert.  The first eight tracks on the album are covers but 'Beasts' and 'Hart With A Crown & Chain' were written by Amelia Warner and are original tracks for the album.  After the first week of release, the album entered the UK Albums Chart at number 89.
