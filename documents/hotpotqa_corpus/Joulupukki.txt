Joulupukki: Joulupukki is a Finnish Christmas figure.  The name ""Joulupukki"" literally means "Christmas goat" or "Yule Goat" in Finnish; the word "pukki" comes from the Teutonic root "bock", which is a cognate of the English "buck", and means "billy-goat".  An old Scandinavian custom, the figure eventually became more or less conflated with Santa Claus.
