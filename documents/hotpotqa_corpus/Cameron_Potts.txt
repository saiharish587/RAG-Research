Cameron Potts: Cameron Potts (born 10 November 1971 in Subiaco, Western Australia), is a musician based in Melbourne.  Since 1999, he has toured regularly to the United States, Canada, Europe, Mexico, Japan, Taiwan, China, Malaysia, Scandinavia and Iceland with both Ninetynine, playing drums, and Baseball, playing violin.  His band Cuba Is Japan toured the far east in 2009, playing the Music Terminals Festival in Taiwan with Tricky, Placebo and a number of Australian acts including Mick Turner, The Stabs and The Holy Soul.  and a 5 date tour of China supporting Angie Hart.  He is not to be confused with Sydney musician Cameron Potts, lead singer and guitarist of Dead Letter Chorus.
