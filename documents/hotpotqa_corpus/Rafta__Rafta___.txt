Rafta, Rafta...: Rafta, Rafta... is a comedy by British Pakistani playwright Ayub Khan-Din adapted from the 1963 Bill Naughton play, "All in Good Time".  The play is set in the working class English town of Bolton, and examines a story of marital difficulties within an immigrant Indian family.  Eeshwar Dutt is a first-generation immigrant and patriarch of the family.  He has a troubled relationship with his newlywed son Atul, whose married life with Vina Patel has got off to a rocky start.  The play deals with setting out and resolving these various conflicts.
