Paul Postma: Paul Edward Postma (born February 22, 1989) is a Canadian professional ice hockey defenceman currently playing for the Boston Bruins of the National Hockey League (NHL).  He was drafted 205th overall in the 2007 NHL Entry Draft by the Atlanta Thrashers.  During his major junior career with the Swift Current Broncos and Calgary Hitmen of the Western Hockey League (WHL) Postma won the WHL Plus-Minus Award and was a WHL East First Team All-Star in 2009.
