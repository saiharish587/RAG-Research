Andrew Stanton: Andrew Stanton (born December 3, 1965) is an American film director, screenwriter, producer and voice actor based at Pixar.  His film work includes writing and directing Pixar's "A Bug's Life" (1998) (as co-director), "Finding Nemo" (2003), and "WALL-E" (2008), and the live-action film, Disney's "John Carter" (2012).  He also co-wrote all three "Toy Story" films and "Monsters, Inc." (2001)
