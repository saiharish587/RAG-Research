National Space Olympiad: The National Space Olympiad (NSO) is a science competition conducted in India.  The competition is conducted at the Department of Physics, Cochin University of science and Technology.  Students from class 8 to class 10 can participate in the competition.  The National Space Olympiad is conducted in remembrance of Indian astronaut Kalpana Chawla.  All students participating in NSO are given a Certificate of Participation.
