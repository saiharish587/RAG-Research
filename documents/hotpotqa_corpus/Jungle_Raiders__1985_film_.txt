Jungle Raiders (1985 film): Jungle Raiders, also known as Captain Yankee (in original Italian, La leggenda del rubino malese/ "Legend of the Blonde Malaysian") is a 1985 Cannon Film.  This film by Antonio Margheriti stars Christopher Connelly and Lee Van Cleef.  The film was the third of three films Antonio Margheriti made in which he attempted to copy the success of "Raiders of the Lost Ark" (the first films he made were "Hunters of the Golden Cobra" and "Ark of the Sun God").
