Montana Highway 35: Montana Highway 35 is an approximately 51 mi state highway in the west of the US state of Montana.  It begins at U.S. Route 93 in Polson in the Flathead Indian Reservation and ends at U.S. Route 2 in Evergreen, the eastern portion of the city of Kalispell.  As well as serving the townships along the eastern shore of Flathead Lake, it also acts like an alternative route of U.S. Route 93 between Polson and Kalispell and is the terminus for both Highways 82 and 83.
