Opera Noire of New York: Opera Noire of New York is a performing arts company, as well as a resource and network for African-American artists.  ONNY is an organization which has performed in multiple venues in the New York City metropolitan area.  Opera Noire was founded by leading New York City Opera tenor Robert Mack, baritone Kenneth Overton and tenor Barron Coleman.  The group consists of all African American opera singers.
