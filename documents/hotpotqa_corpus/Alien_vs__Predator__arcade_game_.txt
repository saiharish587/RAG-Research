Alien vs. Predator (arcade game): Alien vs. Predator (エイリアンVSプレデター) is a beat 'em up video game developed and released by Capcom for the CPS-2 arcade game system in 1994.  In the game, the players take control of up to three out of four human and Predator characters in a battle against the Alien hordes and rogue human soldiers.  The game was very well received by the public and by media publications, becoming a classic title for many fans of the beat 'em up genre, but was never ported to any home system.
