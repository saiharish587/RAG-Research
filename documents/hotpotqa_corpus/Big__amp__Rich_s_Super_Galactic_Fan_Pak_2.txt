Big &amp; Rich's Super Galactic Fan Pak 2: Big & Rich's Super Galactic Fan Pak 2 is the second EP and DVD set released by the American country music duo Big & Rich.  The compilation, released in late 2008, is a followup to their 2004 EP/DVD combo "Big & Rich's Super Galactic Fan Pak".  This set is composed of a three-song CD and a live DVD featuring a full-length concert.
