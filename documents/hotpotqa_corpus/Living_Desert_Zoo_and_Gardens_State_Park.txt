Living Desert Zoo and Gardens State Park: The Living Desert Zoo and Gardens State Park, formerly the Living Desert Zoological and Botanical State Park, is a zoo and botanical garden displaying plants and animals of the Chihuahuan Desert in their native habitats.  It is located off U.S. Route 285 at the north edge of Carlsbad, New Mexico, at an elevation of 3200 ft atop the Ocotillo Hills overlooking the city and the Pecos River.  It is open every day except Christmas.
