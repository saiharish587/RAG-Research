Kelsey Griffin: Kelsey Michelle Griffin (born July 2, 1987) is an American-Australian professional women's basketball player who last played for the Connecticut Sun of the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA).  She was drafted 3rd overall in the 2010 WNBA Draft.  Griffin played college basketball with the Nebraska Cornhuskers.  Griffin was named to many All-America teams and was the winner of the Lowe's Senior CLASS Award.  Griffin is currently playing for the Bendigo Spirit in the Australian WNBL.  Having obtained Australian citizenship in November 2015, Griffin pledged her allegiance to Australia before the 2016 Summer Olympics in Rio de Janeiro.
