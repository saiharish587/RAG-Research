Bendomino: Bendomino is a tabletop strategy game similar to dominoes, created by Thierry Denoual and published by Blue Orange Games in 2007.  It is a set of double-6 dominoes with a 120-degree curve.  The main difference from dominoes is the curved shape of the pieces, which introduces a new level of strategy to the game.  There is also a version of the game for younger players with pictures instead of numbers and symbols on the bendomino tiles.
