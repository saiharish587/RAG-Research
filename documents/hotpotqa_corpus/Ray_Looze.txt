Ray Looze: Ray Looze is an American swimming Coach.  In 2016 Looze was selected to be an assistant coach for the 2016 US Women's Olympic Swimming Team.  At the 2016 Olympic Swimming Trials, Looze coached Cody Miller, Blake Pieroni, and Lilly King who all qualified for the team.  He is a swim coach for the IU Hoosiers.  In 2016, he was named Big 10 Coach of the year.
