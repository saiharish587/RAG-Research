Norwegian County Road 774: Norwegian County Road 774 (Norwegian: "Fylkesvei 774" ) is a 4.2 km long county road in the municipality of Levanger in Nord-Trøndelag county, Norway.  It intersects with European Route E6 at Mule and heads into and through parts of the town center of Levanger.  There, the road continues as the Levanger–Hokstad Ferry operated by Tide Sjø to Hokstad on the island of Ytterøya.  After the ferry, the road changes to Norwegian County Road 135.
