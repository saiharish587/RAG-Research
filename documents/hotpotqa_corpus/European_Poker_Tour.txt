European Poker Tour: The European Poker Tour (EPT) was a series of poker tournaments similar to those in the World Poker Tour (WPT), created by John Duthie, winner of the inaugural Poker Million tournament.  It began in 2004 as part of the worldwide explosion in Texas Hold 'em popularity.  Since 2011 the EPT has been sponsored and wholly owned and controlled by PokerStars the online casino and taped by Sunset + Vine for television broadcast across Europe.
