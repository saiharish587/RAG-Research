List of awards and nominations received by Secret: Secret is a four-member South Korean girl group formed by TS Entertainment in 2009.  Secret debuted with the song "I Want You Back" on October 13, 2009.  In 2010, the group released their hit single "Magic" which was nominated at the 12th Mnet Asian Music Awards for "Best Dance Performance by a Female Group".  The same year, the group released their number one hit single "Madonna" which won them a "Bonsang" award at the 20th Seoul Music Awards.  With the success of "Magic" and "Madonna", the group received the "Newcomer award" at the 25th Golden Disk Awards.
