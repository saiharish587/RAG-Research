The Blitzkrieg Button: "The Blitzkrieg Button" is the fourth episode of the first season of the American television series "Agent Carter", inspired by the films "" and "", and the Marvel One-Shot short film also titled "Agent Carter".  It features the Marvel Comics character Peggy Carter as she must hide the fugitive Howard Stark while she retrieves the titular device for him, and is set in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), sharing continuity with the films of the franchise.  The episode was written by Brant Englestein and directed by Stephen Cragg.
