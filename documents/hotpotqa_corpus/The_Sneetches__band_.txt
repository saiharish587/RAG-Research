The Sneetches (band): The Sneetches were an American indie pop/power pop band formed in San Francisco, California, United States, in 1985, who released several albums before splitting up in the mid-1990s.  The band was described by "Trouser Press" as "one of the most tasteful, consistently tuneful pop bands on the American scene".  In 2017 All Music described and Sneetches as "one of the best classic guitar pop bands in the late '80s/early '90s."
