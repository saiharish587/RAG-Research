We Are All Completely Beside Ourselves: We Are All Completely Beside Ourselves is a 2013 novel by the American writer Karen Joy Fowler.  The novel won the 2014 PEN/Faulkner Award for Fiction<ref name="The Guardian - 3 April 2014 - Karen Joy Fowler wins PEN/Faulkner award for fiction"> </ref> and was also short-listed for the 2014 Man Booker Prize.
