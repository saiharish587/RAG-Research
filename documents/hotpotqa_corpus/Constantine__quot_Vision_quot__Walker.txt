Constantine &quot;Vision&quot; Walker: Constantine "Vision" Walker, also known as "Vision" or "Dream" (born Constantine Anthony Walker, Jr., 19 October 1951, Jamaica), is a singer songwriter and musician.  He was an original member of reggae group The Soulettes, with his cousin Rita Anderson (Marley) and Marlene "Precious" Gifford in the early 1960s, and was briefly a member of The Wailers along with Bunny Wailer and Peter Tosh.  He is engaged in the California-based group The Rastafarians.
