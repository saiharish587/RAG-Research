Paschim Banga Gramin Bank: Paschim Banga Gramin Bank (PBGB) is a Regional Rural Bank established on 26 February 2007 in exercise of the powers conferred by Sub-section (1) of Section 23A of the Regional Rural Bank Act, 1976 (21 of 1976).  The Bank is established by the amalgamation of Howrah Gramin Bank, Bardhaman Gramin Bank and Mayurakshi Gramin Bank.
