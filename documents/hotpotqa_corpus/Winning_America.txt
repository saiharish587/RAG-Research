Winning America: Winning America is a documentary television film about the Canadian band Said the Whale.  It follows the band on their first US tour down through California, and then to South by Southwest.  It premiered on CBC Television on July 23, 2011.  The film was directed by Brent Hodge and Thomas Buchan, and was produced by Brent Hodge, Jon Siddall and Sheila Peacock.  It was nominated for a Leo Award in 2012.
