Hooker Jim: Hooker Jim (1851–1879) was a Modoc warrior who played a pivotal role in the Modoc War.  Hooker Jim was the son-in-law of tribal medicine man Curley Headed Doctor.  After white settlers massacred Modoc women and children contemporaneously with the Battle of Lost River, Hooker Jim led a group of Modocs overland to Captain Jack's Stronghold.  During their march, Hooker Jim and his warriors killed several white settlers in revenge.
