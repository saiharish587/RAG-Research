Shannon Doyle: Shannon Doyle is a Canadian-born women’s ice hockey player with the Connecticut Whale of the NWHL.  At the NCAA level, she accumulated 25 points with the Colgate Raiders women's ice hockey from 2010–12, and 53 points with the Boston University Terriers women's ice hockey program over the course of two seasons from 2012-15 (she missed the 2013-14 season due to injury).
