Acme-McCrary Hosiery Mills: Acme-McCrary Hosiery Mills, also known as Acme Hosiery Mills, McCrary Hosiery Mills, and Asheboro Grocery Company, is a historic textile mill complex located at Asheboro, Randolph County, North Carolina.  The complex includes six buildings and a smokestack—erected between 1909 and 1962.  The mill buildings were designed by noted architect Richard C. Biberstein and the oldest section is a two-story, heavy-timber-frame mill with load bearing brick walls.  The Acme-McCrary-Sapona Recreation Center was built in 1948-1949, and is a two-story, Art Moderne style brick recreation center.
