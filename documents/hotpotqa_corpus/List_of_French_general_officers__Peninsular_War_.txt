List of French general officers (Peninsular War): The following list of French general officers (Peninsular War) lists the "générals" ("général de brigade" and "général de division") and "maréchals d'Empire", that is, the French general officers who served in the First French Empire's "Grande Armée" in Spain and Portugal during the Peninsular War (1808–1814).  The rank given refers to the ones held until 1814.  The list includes foreign nationals who fought in French military units.
