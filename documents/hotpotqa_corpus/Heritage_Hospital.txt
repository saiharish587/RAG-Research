Heritage Hospital: Heritage Hospital is a hospital located in Tarboro, North Carolina.  It is a part of the University Health Systems of Eastern Carolina (UHSEC).  Edgecombe General Hospital opened as a county-owned hospital in 1916.  It succeeded Pittman Hospital, which opened in 1901.  In 1959, the Hill-Burton Act helped combine Edgecombe General Hospital, with three other facilities.  Edgecombe County sold the hospital to Hospital Corporation of America (HCA) in 1982.  HCA opened a 127-bed facility in 1985, named Heritage Hospital.  UHSEC bought Heritage Hospital in 1998 from HCA.  The hospitals focus is as a community hospital.  The hospital has 101 general and 16 rehabilitation hospital beds.  It has five Shared Inpatient/Ambulatory Surgery, two Endoscopy, and one C-Section operating rooms.
