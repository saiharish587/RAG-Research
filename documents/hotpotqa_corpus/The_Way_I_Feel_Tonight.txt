The Way I Feel Tonight: "The Way I Feel Tonight" is a pop ballad by the Bay City Rollers from their 1977 album "It's a Game".  The tune, written by Harvey Shield, and featuring a lead vocal by Les McKeown, is a slow, dramatic ballad with a heavily orchestrated arrangement.  It was released as a 7" vinyl single in numerous territories, and had a peak position of #24 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 chart.  It was the Rollers' final charting US single.
