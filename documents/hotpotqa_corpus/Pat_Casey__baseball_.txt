Pat Casey (baseball): Patrick Michael Casey (born March 17, 1959) is the head coach for the Oregon State Beavers baseball team.  He is best known for winning the 2006 College World Series for the Beavers' first-ever baseball National Championship.  The following year, despite losing all but two starters on the team and being the final team selected in the NCAA College World Series bracket, he led the Beavers to a repeat championship in the 2007 College World Series, the first unranked team in history to accomplish this feat.
