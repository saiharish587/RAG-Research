Strange Interlude (film): Strange Interlude is a 1932 American pre-Code drama film directed by Robert Z. Leonard and released by Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer.  The film stars Norma Shearer and Clark Gable, and is based on the play "Strange Interlude" by Eugene O'Neill.  It is greatly shortened from the play: the stage production lasts six hours and is sometimes performed over two evenings, while the film runs the usual two hours.
