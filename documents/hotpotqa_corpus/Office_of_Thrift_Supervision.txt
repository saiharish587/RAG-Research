Office of Thrift Supervision: The Office of Thrift Supervision (OTS) was a United States federal agency under the Department of the Treasury that chartered, supervised, and regulated all federally chartered and state-chartered savings banks and savings and loans associations.  It was created in 1989 as a renamed version of another federal agency (that was faulted for its role in the savings and loan crisis).  Like other U.S. federal bank regulators, it was paid by the banks it regulated.  The OTS was initially seen as an aggressive regulator, but was later lax.  Declining revenues and staff led the OTS to market itself to companies as a lax regulator in order to get revenue.
