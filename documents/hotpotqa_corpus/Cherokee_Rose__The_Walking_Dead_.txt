Cherokee Rose (The Walking Dead): "Cherokee Rose" is the fourth episode of the second season and 10th episode overall of the post-apocalyptic horror television series "The Walking Dead", which aired on AMC in the United States on November 6, 2011.  The episode was written by Evan Reilly and directed by Billy Gierhart.  In the episode, the group of survivors move to the home of Hershel Greene (Scott Wilson).  While the group contemplates on what to do, Daryl Dixon (Norman Reedus) continues to search for Sophia Peletier (Madison Lintz).
