Stomil Olsztyn (company): Stomil Olsztyn was a tyre manufacturer based in Olsztyn, Poland.  The company came into existence when the tyre plant OZOS „Stomil”, founded in 1967, was privatized in 1992.  In 1995 Michelin acquired the majority share in Stomil.  From 1995 to May 28, 2004 Stomil was quoted at the Warsaw Stock Exchange.  In 2005 Michelin gained full control of Stomil and renamed it to "Michelin Polska S.A.".  With roughly 4,000 employees "Michelin Polska" is one of the largest Michelin plants and the largest tyre plant in Poland.
