Maria Antonia of Austria: Maria Antonia of Austria (Maria Antonia Josepha Benedicta Rosalia Petronella; 18 January 1669 – 24 December 1692) was the eldest daughter and only surviving child of Holy Roman Emperor Leopold I and his wife Margaret Theresa of Spain.  She became Electress of Bavaria when she married in 1685, but died prematurely in 1692.
