Chittagong (film): Chittagong is a 2012 Indian historical war drama film directed by Bedabrata Pain.  It stars Manoj Bajpayee in the lead role and is based upon events of British India's (now in Bangladesh) Chittagong Uprising.  The film features music by trio Shankar Ehsaan Loy and sound by Resul Pookutty.  The world premier of film was on 10 April 2012.  "Chittagong" released on 12 October 2012 and nett grossed Rs 3.1 million at the Indian box office.  This movie won the 60th National Film Award for the Best Debut Film of a Director.
