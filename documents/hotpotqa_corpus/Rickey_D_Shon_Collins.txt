Rickey D'Shon Collins: Rickey D'Shon Collins (born January 17, 1983) is an American voice actor, most notable for providing the voice of Vince LaSalle in Disney's hit show "Recess".  He also voiced Tucker Foley in the Nickelodeon show "Danny Phantom".  He has done voice overs for other television shows such as "Static Shock" and "Justice League".  He also voiced Vince LaSalle in the "Recess" film "".  In 2006 he reprised his role as Vince LaSalle once again, in a special crossover episode of "".  Most recently, he is best known as the associate producer in the 2009 comedy short "Brotherlee" and the writer in the 2011 thriller short "Undiagnosed".
