Kawasaki ZXR400: The Kawasaki ZXR400 was a Kawasaki motorcycle introduced in 1989.  It was one of the first and most popular of the 400 cc sport bikes that swept across Japan and later Europe in the 1990s.  It was discontinued in 2003.  The H model was produced first, and was superseded by the L series in 1991.  The L series had increased power output, but less torque, and updated slimmer rear styling.
