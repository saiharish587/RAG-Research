ERuf Model A: The eRuf Model A is an all-electric sports car made by German automobile manufacturer Ruf Automobile.  The car is powered by a UQM Technologies propulsion system (a UQM PowerPhase 150).  The car has a top speed of 225 km/h and it’s capable of making 150 kW and 479 lbft of torque .  Estimated range per charge is 250 kilometers to 320 kilometers, depending on performance level, using iron-phosphate, lithium-ion batteries built by Axeon plc of Great Britain.  The power and torque produced by the 3-phase motor can be used to recover almost as much power as it can put out.  During coasting the engine works as a generator producing electricity to charge the batteries.
