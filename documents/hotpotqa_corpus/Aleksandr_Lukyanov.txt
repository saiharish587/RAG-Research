Aleksandr Lukyanov: Aleksandr Viktorovich Lukyanov (Russian: Александр Викторович Лукьянов , born August 19, 1949) is a Russian rower who competed for the Soviet Union in the 1976 Summer Olympics, in the 1980 Summer Olympics, and in the 1988 Summer Olympics and for Russia in the 1996 Summer Olympics and in the 2000 Summer Olympics.
