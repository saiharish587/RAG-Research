Get Cape. Wear Cape. Fly (EP): Get Cape.  Wear Cape.  Fly is the debut EP of Sam Duckworth, who performs under the name Get Cape.  Wear Cape.  Fly.  It was released in tandem with a video for "Whitewash is Brainwash", which sees Get Cape.  Wear Cape.  Fly travelling around on the London Underground and performing a secret show in Bank Tube Station.
