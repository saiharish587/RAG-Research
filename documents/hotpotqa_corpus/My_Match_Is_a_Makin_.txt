My Match Is a Makin': My Match Is A Makin' (English translation of opening track title) is a music album by Irish musicians Moya Brennan and Cormac de Barra.  This is Moya's eighth studio album to be released.  It was released on 14 April 2010 exclusively to concert goers on her Spring 2010 tour of the Netherlands.  The album was re-released to a wider audience under the title Voices & Harps.
