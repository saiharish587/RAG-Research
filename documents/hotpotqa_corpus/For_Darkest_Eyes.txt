For Darkest Eyes: For Darkest Eyes is the first video release by death/doom metal band My Dying Bride.  Created first as a VHS in 1997, then later being reproduced as a DVD in 2002.  Live footage from Willem II, The Netherlands in 1993 on November 3, the picture gallery and the live song "Sear Me" done in Simplon 1992 do not appear on the video version due to time constraints of the old VHS format.
