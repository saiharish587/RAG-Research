F. B. Rogers Silver Co.: F. B. Rogers Silver Co. was a silversmithing company founded in Shelburne Falls, Massachusetts in 1883.  It was acquired by Edmund W. Porter and L.B. West, who incorporated the company and moved manufacturing operations to Taunton, Massachusetts in 1886.  For several years, the company became known as West Silver Company, and was producing silver products for the William Rogers Manufacturing Company of Hartford, Connecticut.
