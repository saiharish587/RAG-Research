DC Rivals HyperCoaster: DC Rivals HyperCoaster is a steel hypercoaster at Warner Bros.  Movie World in Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia.  Manufactured by Mack Rides, it officially opened on 22 September 2017, being promoted as the longest, fastest, and tallest hypercoaster in the Southern Hemisphere.  It is the first new attraction added to Warner Bros.  Movie World since Doomsday Destroyer in 2016 and the first new roller coaster added since Green Lantern Coaster opened in 2011.  The roller coaster reaches a height of 61.6 m with a maximum speed of 115.1 km/h and has a total track length of 1,400 metres (4,593.2 ft).
