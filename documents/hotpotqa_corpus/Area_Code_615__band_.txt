Area Code 615 (band): Area Code 615 was the name of a Nashville country rock band active in the late 1960s and early 1970s, taking their name from the telephone area code, which at the time covered all of Central and Eastern Tennessee.  The band was made up of session musicians, recording only two albums before resuming normal session work.  Several of the members were backing musicians for Bob Dylan's "Blonde on Blonde" and "Nashville Skyline" albums and on recordings by Billy Swan.
