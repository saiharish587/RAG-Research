Jamie Campbell (author): Jamie Campbell (born March 21, 1950, in Carshalton, Surrey) is an East Anglian author and journalist.  Campbell's home is Gorleston-on-Sea, Norfolk.  Campbell's non-fiction works cover traditional East Anglian waterways livelihoods, yachts and the other commercial sailing craft with emphasis on The Broads special area with legal protection equivalent to a national park — a major European sailing and canal boat centre.
