2015 Big Ten Conference Men's Lacrosse Tournament: The 2015 Big Ten Men's Lacrosse Tournament took place April 30 to May 2 at Capital One Field at Byrd Stadium in College Park, Maryland.  The winner of the tournament received the Big Ten Conference's automatic bid to the 2015 NCAA Division I Men's Lacrosse Championship.  Four teams from the Big Ten conference competed in the inaugural event of the single elimination.  Big Ten Conference Men's Lacrosse Tournament.  The seeds were based upon the teams regular season conference record.  Johns Hopkins University won the event, despite playing its first season in the new conference.
