A. Edward Sutherland: Albert Edward Sutherland (January 5, 1895 – December 31, 1973) was a film director and actor.  Born in London, he was from a theatrical family.  His father, Al Sutherland, was a theatre manager and producer and his mother, Julie Ring, was a vaudeville performer.  He was a nephew of both Blanche Ring and Thomas Meighan, who was married to Frances Ring, another of his mother's sisters.
