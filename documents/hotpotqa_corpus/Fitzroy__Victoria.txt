Fitzroy, Victoria: Fitzroy is an inner-city suburb of Melbourne, Victoria, Australia, 3 km north-east of Melbourne's Central Business District in the local government area of the City of Yarra.  At the 2016 Census, Fitzroy had a population of 10,445.  Planned as Melbourne's first suburb, it was later also one of the city's first areas to gain municipal status.  It occupies Melbourne's smallest and most densely populated suburban area, just 100 ha.
