Duff Goldman: Jeffrey Adam "Duff" Goldman (born December 17, 1974) is a pastry chef and television personality.  He is the executive chef of the Baltimore-based Charm City Cakes shop which was featured in the Food Network reality television show "Ace of Cakes", and his second Los Angeles-based shop Charm City Cakes West, which is featured in Food Network's "Duff Till Dawn" and "Cake Masters" series.  His work has also been featured on the "Food Network Challenge", "Iron Chef America", "Oprah", "The Tonight Show with Jay Leno" and "Man v. Food".
