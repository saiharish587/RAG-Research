KDWN: KDWN (720 AM) is an American radio station owned by Beasley Broadcast Group, Inc. based in Las Vegas, Nevada.  It broadcasts full-time at 50,000 watts, and is directional at night.  It can be heard throughout most of the Western United States, north into Canada and south into Mexico.  KDWN is primarily a news-talk radio station featuring a local morning talk show host Alan Stock and syndicated talk show host Jerry Doyle.  KDWN also broadcasts brokered programs for Las Vegas casinos, local businesses, and other groups.  It is the station where famous late-night talk show host Art Bell first broadcast his show, Coast to Coast AM.  It also has a local news alliance with Channel 8 KLAS-TV, the CBS TV Network affiliate in Las Vegas.
