RMS Arundel Castle: RMS "Arundel Castle" was a British ocean liner and Royal Mail Ship which entered service in 1921 for the Union-Castle Line.  A previous vessel of the same name was built in 1864 by Donald Currie & Co. (a predecessor to Union-Castle) and sold in 1883, whereupon it was renamed "Chittagong".  Originally laid down as the "Amroth Castle" in 1915, building was delayed by the First World War.  She was eventually launched on 11 September 1919.
