Capitale-Nationale: Capitale-Nationale (] ; English: National Capital region ) is one of 17 administrative regions of Quebec, Canada.  Quebec City, Quebec's centre of government, is located in this region.  It has a land area of 18,796.86 km.  It reported a total resident population of 700,616 as of the Canada 2011 Census, with Quebec City having 73.7 percent of the total.
