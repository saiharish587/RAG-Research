Darryl Hunt: Darryl Hunt (February 24, 1965 – March 13, 2016) was an African-American man from Winston-Salem, North Carolina, who, in 1984, was wrongfully convicted for the rape and the murder of Deborah Sykes, a young white newspaper copy editor, and sentenced to life in prison.  After being convicted in that case, Hunt was tried in 1987 for the 1983 murder of Arthur Wilson, a 57-year-old black man of Winston-Salem.  Both convictions overturned on appeal in 1989.  Hunt was tried again in the Wilson case in 1990; he was acquitted by an all-white jury.  He was tried again on the Sykes charges in 1991; he was convicted.
