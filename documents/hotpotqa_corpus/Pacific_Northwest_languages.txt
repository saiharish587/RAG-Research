Pacific Northwest languages: The Pacific Northwest languages are the indigenous languages of the Pacific Northwest of North America.  This is a geographic term and does not imply any common heritage for these languages.  In fact, the Pacific Northwest is an area of exceptional linguistic diversity and contains languages belonging to a large number of (apparently) unrelated families.  However, the close proximity of multiple languages has created many opportunities for mutual interaction, with the result that the Pacific Northwest forms a linguistic area, with many areal features that are shared across language families.
