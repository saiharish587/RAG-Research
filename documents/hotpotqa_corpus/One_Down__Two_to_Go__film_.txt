One Down, Two to Go (film): One Down, Two to Go is a 1982 American blaxploitation film written and directed by Fred Williamson and starring Fred Williamson, Jim Brown, Richard Roundtree and Jim Kelly.  The plot revolves around a pair of tough cops Cal (Fred Williamson) and J (Jim Brown) who go after the mob, who jinxed the martial arts tournament and injured their friend, Chuck (Jim Kelly).
