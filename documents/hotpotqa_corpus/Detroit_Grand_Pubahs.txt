Detroit Grand Pubahs: The Detroit Grand Pubahs are an American musical group from Detroit, Michigan, comprising Paris the Black Fu (real name Mack Goudy, Jr.) and The Mysterious Mr O. (real name Oliver Way).  One of the founding members, Dr. Toefinger (real name Andy Toth), left in 2004.  They are best known for their 2000 single "Sandwiches", which reached number 29 in the Hot Dance Club Songs chart.
