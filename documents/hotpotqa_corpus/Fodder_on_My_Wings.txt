Fodder on My Wings: Fodder on My Wings is an album by singer/pianist/songwriter Nina Simone.  It is part of her later works, and can be regarded alongside "Baltimore" (1978) as one of her better achievements of that period.  It is however a rather obscure album and not widely distributed.  The album is one of Simone's most introspective and personal works, with songs about her father's death and her (not always pleasant) stay in Liberia, Trinidad and Switzerland.  There is some confusion about the actual title of the album and the song with almost the same title on the album, being called "Fodder on My Wings", "Fodder in My Wings", "Fodder in Her Wings" interchangeably.
