Argent Corporation: Argent Corporation was a company in Las Vegas that at one time controlled the Hacienda Hotel/Casino, the Stardust Resort & Casino, the Fremont Hotel and Casino and the casino in the Marina Hotel.  The company was owned by Allen Glick, a San Diego real estate investor.  The name Argent came from the three initials of his name, combined with the first three letters of the word "Enterprises".  However, state and local officials in Nevada believed that the casinos were controlled by organized crime families in the Mid-West and that a huge skimming operation was conducted within the casinos.
