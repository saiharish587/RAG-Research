Darby Stanchfield: Darby Leigh Stanchfield (born April 29, 1971) is an American actress.  She is best known for her role as Abby Whelan in the ABC political drama series "Scandal" (2012–present).  Stanchfield is also known for roles as April Green in the CBS post-apocalyptic drama series "Jericho" (2006–07), and as Helen Bishop in the AMC period drama series "Mad Men" (2007–08).
