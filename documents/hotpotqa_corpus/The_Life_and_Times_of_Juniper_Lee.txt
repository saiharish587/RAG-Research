The Life and Times of Juniper Lee: The Life and Times of Juniper Lee is an American animated television series, created by former MTV reality star Judd Winick for Cartoon Network.  The show was produced by Cartoon Network Studios.  It premiered on Cartoon Network on May 30, 2005, and ended its run on April 9, 2007.  The show currently reruns on Pogo in India, Boing in Nigeria, South Africa and France and Boomerang in Thailand.
