John Butler, 15th Earl of Ormonde: John Butler, 15th Earl of Ormonde and 8th Earl of Ossory ("de jure") was born before 1744 and died on 24 June 1766.  He was the son of Thomas Butler of Garryricken and Lady Margaret Burke who was the daughter of William Burke, 7th Earl of Clanricarde.  Upon the death of Charles Butler (14th Earl of Ormonde), his only surviving sister, Lady Elizabeth Butler inherited her brother's estate in 1760.  Lady Elizabeth was the daughter of Thomas Butler, 6th Earl of Ossory.  John Butler succeeded his cousin as Earl of Ormonde, but he did not assume the honours.  He married Bridget Stacey in 1763 but had no children; she remarried Allen Walter.
