The Testimony (2015 film): The Testimony is a 2015 American short-documentary film which chronicles the largest rape tribunal in the history of the Democratic Republic of the Congo as a result of M23 rebellion fighting.  It is directed and shot by Vanessa Block and produced by Block, Steve Tisch, Mitchell Block and Michael Sarnoski.  "The Testimony " was shortlisted with ten other documentaries from 74 entries submitted to the 88th Academy Awards in Documentary Short Subject category.  The final five nominations are scheduled to be announced on January 14, 2016.
