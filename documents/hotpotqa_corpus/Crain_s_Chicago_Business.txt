Crain's Chicago Business: Crain's Chicago Business is a weekly business newspaper in Chicago.  It is owned by Detroit-based Crain Communications, a privately held publishing company with more than 30 magazines, including "Advertising Age", "Modern Healthcare", "Crain's New York Business", "Crain's Detroit Business", "Crain's Cleveland Business", and "Automotive News".  It has a print circulation of 53,313 and a readership of 219,693 per week.  ChicagoBusiness.com, the paper's digital equivalent, draws over 1 million unique visitors per month and over 2.2 million page views per month.
