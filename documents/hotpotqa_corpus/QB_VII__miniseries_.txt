QB VII (miniseries): "QB VII" is an American television miniseries produced by Screen Gems; it was also the final program from Columbia Pictures's television division to be made under the Screen Gems banner.  It began airing on ABC on April 29, 1974.  Adapted to the screen by Edward Anhalt from the novel "QB VII", it was produced by Douglas S. Cramer and directed by Tom Gries.  The original music was written by Jerry Goldsmith and the cinematography by Paul Beeson and Robert L. Morrison.
