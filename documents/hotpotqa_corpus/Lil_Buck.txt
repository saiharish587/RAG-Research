Lil Buck: Charles "Lil Buck" Riley (born May 25, 1988) is a Los Angeles-based dancer and occasional model from Memphis, Tennessee who specializes in a style of street dance called jookin.  He gained popularity after director Spike Jonze used his cell phone to record an interpretive performance of "The Dying Swan" by Lil Buck and Yo-Yo Ma.  Jonze uploaded the video to YouTube and as of November 2015, it had amassed over three million views.
