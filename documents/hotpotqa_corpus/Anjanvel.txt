Anjanvel: Anjanwel is a small coastal town in Guhagar taluka, Ratnagiri district, in the Konkan region and administrative division of the Indian state of Maharashtra.  It is located around 80 km north of the district headquarters of Ratnagiri, 10 km north of its taluka, and 200 km south of Mumbai, the state capital.  Marathi is the official language, but Daldi, a dialect of Konkani language, is also spoken by large numbers of inhabitants.  The nearest villages are Veldur, which is home of an Enron plant, Dhopawe, Vanoshi T. Panchanadi, Navse, and Sakhari Trishul.  The Gopalgad Fort, also known as Anjanvel Fort ( ) is a coastial fort, half of the fort is on a hill and the other half is directly adjacent to the Arabian Sea.  Anjanwel has a tropical monsoon climate with 47% of humidity also due to its proximity to the sea.
