None Shall Escape: None Shall Escape is a 1944 war film.  Even though the film was made during the Second World War, the setting is a post-war Nuremberg-style war crimes trial.  Alexander Knox plays Wilhelm Grimm, a Nazi officer who is on trial, and the story unfolds through the eyes of several witnesses, including a Catholic priest, Father Warecki (Henry Travers), Grimm's brother Karl (Erik Rolf) and Marja Pacierkowski (Marsha Hunt), a woman to whom he was once engaged.
