Anything Goes! (C+C Music Factory album): Anything Goes!  is the second album by the US dance group C+C Music Factory.  The album's lead single "Do You Wanna Get Funky"; which featured Martha Wash, Zelma Davis, and Trilogy, reached #1 on the Dance/Club Play charts, #40 on the Hot 100, and #11 on the R&B Singles chart.  The follow-up single, "Take a Toke" reached #23 on the Dance/Club Play and #48 on the R&B Singles chart.
