Paradise Interchange: Paradise Interchange is an interchange of the O-Bahn Busway in Adelaide, South Australia, located in the suburb of Paradise, South Australia.  With services operated by Adelaide Metro, this interchange is situated mid-way along the O-Bahn Busway, between Tea Tree Plaza Interchange and Klemzig Interchange, six kilometres (3.7 mi) from the city centre.  Paradise Interchange has 625 carparks, and is located on the south side of Darley Road, with access roads permitting buses to transfer between local roads and the busway.
