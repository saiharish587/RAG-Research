Chuck Berry's Golden Decade: Chuck Berry's Golden Decade is a compilation of music by Chuck Berry, released in three volumes in 1967, 1973, and 1974.  Covering the decade from 1955 to 1964, each volume consists of a two-LP set of 24 songs recorded by Berry.  The first volume reached number 72 on "Billboard"'s Pop Albums" chart.  The second volume peaked at number 110.  The third volume, which included only two hit singles among its tracks, did not chart.
