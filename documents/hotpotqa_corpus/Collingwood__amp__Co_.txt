Collingwood &amp; Co.: Collingwood & Co. (formerly Collingwood O'Hare) is a TV animation studio based in London headed by writer/director Tony Collingwood.  They were responsible for animated TV series 'Oscar's Orchestra', 'Dennis the Menace', 'Gordon the Garden Gnome', 'Yoko!  Jakamoko!  Toto!'  and 'The Secret Show', among others.
