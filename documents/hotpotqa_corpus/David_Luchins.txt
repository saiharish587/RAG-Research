David Luchins: David Luchins (born 1946) is a professor at Touro College and chair of its political science department.  He is a national vice-president of the Orthodox Union and a national officer of the Jewish Council for Public Affairs (JCPA).  Luchins is a "much-lauded longtime Orthodox Jewish activist" who is active in Jewish communal life and is a frequent speaker on educational, political and Jewish topics.  Luchins served as an aide to then-Vice President Hubert Humphrey and for 20 years on the Senate staff of New York Senator Daniel Patrick Moynihan.
