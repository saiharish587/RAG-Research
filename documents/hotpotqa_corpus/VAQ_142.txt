VAQ-142: Electronic Attack Squadron 142 (VAQ-142), also known as "The Gray Wolves", is an EA-18G Growler squadron of the United States Navy stationed at Naval Air Station Whidbey Island, Oak Harbor, Washington.  They are attached to Carrier Air Wing Eleven (CVW-11), aboard USS "Nimitz" .  Their tailcode is NH and their ATC callsign is ""GRIM"".
