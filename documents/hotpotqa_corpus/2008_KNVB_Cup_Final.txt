2008 KNVB Cup Final: The 2008 KNVB Cup Final was a football match between Feyenoord and Roda JC on 27 April 2008 at De Kuip, Rotterdam.  It was the final match of the 2007–08 KNVB Cup competition.  Feyenoord beat Roda JC 2–0 after goals from Denny Landzaat and Jonathan de Guzmán.  It was their eleventh KNVB Cup triumph.
