Leonotis nepetifolia: Leonotis nepetifolia, also known as klip dagga, Christmas candlestick, or lion's ear, is a species of plant in the genus "Leonotis" and the family Lamiaceae (mint).  It is native to tropical Africa and southern India.  It can also be found growing abundantly in much of Latin America and the West Indies.  It grows to a height of 3 m and has whorls of striking lipped flowers, that are most commonly orange, but can vary to red, white, and purple.  It has very soft serrated leaves that can grow up to 4 in wide.
