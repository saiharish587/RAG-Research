Dost (1944 film): Dost (Hindi: दोस्त ; English: Friend ) is a 1944 Bollywood film produced under the banner Navin Pictures, directed by Shaukat Hussain Rizvi.  Kanhaiyalal and Maya Banerjee play the lead with Agha, Himalaya Dasani, Husn Banu, Mirza Musharraf, Motilal, Noor Jehan, Ram Pyari, Shaukat Hussain Rizvi, Sherali and Varmala Kumthekar in the support cast.  The film song lyrics were written by Shams Lakhnavi with music composed by Sajjad Hussain.
