Nevada State Route 582: State Route 582 (SR 582) is a major 16.688 mi highway in the Las Vegas Valley.  The highway is the former route of U.S. Route 93 (US 93) and US 95 before they were moved to the current freeway alignment shared with Interstate 515 (I-515).  It connects Downtown Las Vegas with Henderson and (indirectly) Boulder City to the southeast.  The highway is primarily known as Boulder Highway, but is named Fremont Street within the Las Vegas city limits.
