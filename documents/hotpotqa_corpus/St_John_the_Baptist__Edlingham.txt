St John the Baptist, Edlingham: St. John the Baptist Church is a Mediaeval (11th century) Church in Edlingham, Alnwick, Northumberland, England.  The church is mostly Norman, from two periods, the late 11th - early 12th Century and late 12th century.  The chancel arch and the south porch, with its rare Norman tunnel vault, are late 11th century, and the north aisle arcade is from the late 12th century.  The columns are circular and the capitals are scalloped with bands of nail-head.  The defensible west tower may also have begun in the late 12th Century, but completed later.
