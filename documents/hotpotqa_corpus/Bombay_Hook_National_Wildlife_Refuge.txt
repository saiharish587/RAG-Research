Bombay Hook National Wildlife Refuge: The Bombay Hook National Wildlife Refuge is a 15,978 acre National Wildlife Refuge located along the eastern coast of Kent County, Delaware, United States, on Delaware Bay.  It was established on March 16, 1937 as a refuge and breeding ground for migratory and wintering waterfowl along the Atlantic Flyway.  The Refuge was purchased from local land owners with federal duck stamp funds.
