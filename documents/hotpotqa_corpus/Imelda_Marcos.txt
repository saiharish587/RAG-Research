Imelda Marcos: Imelda Marcos (née Romuáldez, born 2 July 1929) is the widow of Filipino dictator Ferdinand Marcos, the 10th president of the Philippines.  She served as First Lady from 1965 to 1986 during the presidency of her husband.  She remains one of the richest politicians in the Philippines through her collection of clothing, artwork, and jewelry, along with money in offshore bank accounts under the pseudonym "Jane Ryan".  As a result, she has been called a kleptocrat by her critics who accuse her of plunder.
