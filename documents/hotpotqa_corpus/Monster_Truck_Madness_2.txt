Monster Truck Madness 2: Monster Truck Madness 2 is a monster truck racing video game developed by Terminal Reality and Edge of Reality, and published by Microsoft for Microsoft Windows 95, NT, and Nintendo 64 in 1998 and 1999.  It's a sequel to "Monster Truck Madness".  The Nintendo 64 version of the game was co-published with Rockstar and released as Monster Truck Madness 64.
