David Huckabee: David James Huckabee (born July 22, 1980) is an American mortgage broker and one of two-time presidential candidate and former Arkansas governor, Mike Huckabee's two sons.  David Huckabee's wife Lauren, an attorney, worked on Mike Huckabee's campaign for president, having registered Mike as a candidate and having served as "ballot access and delegate director".  Both David and Lauren Huckabee have been listed as payees for the Mike Huckabee campaign.
