4th AVN Awards: The 4th AVN Awards ceremony, organized by Adult Video News (AVN), took place on January 9, 1987 at the Tropicana Hotel Grand Ballroom in Paradise, Nevada.  During the ceremony, AVN Awards were presented in 32 categories honoring excellence in the world of adult movies released on videocassette between January 1 and December 31, 1986.  The show was hosted by "Adult Video News" co-publishers Paul Fishbein and Barry Rosenblatt.
