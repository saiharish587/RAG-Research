Jennifer Granholm: Jennifer Mulhern Granholm (born February 5, 1959) is a Canadian-American politician, lawyer, educator, author, political commentator and member of the Democratic Party who served as the Attorney General of Michigan from 1999 to 2003 and as the Governor of Michigan from 2003 to 2011.  She is currently an MSNBC political contributor.  In January 2017, she became a CNN political contributor.
