Dodi Papase: Dodi Papase is a town in the Volta Region of Ghana.  With a population of approximately 5,254 ,Dodi Papase is the second-most-populous city in the Volta region of Ghana.  It is located in eastern Ghana on the banks of the Asukawkaw River and is known for the Dodi Papase Community Secondary School, a second cycle institution (senior high school).
