We Will Become Silhouettes: "We Will Become Silhouettes" is a single by US indie band The Postal Service, released February 8, 2005 under the Sub Pop Records label.  The single included a new track "Be Still My Heart" and two remixed tracks by Matthew Dear ("We Will Become Silhouettes") and Styrofoam ("Nothing Better").  The title track has been used in a Honda Civic commercial.  Indie band The Shins recorded an acoustic cover of the title track, which was included as a B-side on The Postal Service's "Such Great Heights".
