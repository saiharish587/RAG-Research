Pilgrim Reformed Church Cemetery: Pilgrim Reformed Church Cemetery is a historic church cemetery located near Lexington, Davidson County, North Carolina.  It is associated with the Pilgrim Reformed Church, founded about 1757 By a man of the name Valentine Leonhardt.  It contains approximately 350 burials, with the earliest gravestone dated to 1781.  It features a unique collection of folk gravestones by local stone cutters erected in Davidson County in the late-18th and first half of the 19th centuries.  The church was the first Pilgrim church in North Carolina.
