Ed Schafer: Edward Thomas "Ed" Schafer (born August 8, 1946) is an American business leader, who was the 30th Governor of North Dakota from 1992 to 2000.  Schafer also served as the 29th United States Secretary of Agriculture from 2008 to 2009, appointed by President George W. Bush.  He was appointed as the Interim President of the University of North Dakota, serving from January through June 2016.  His last name is frequently misspelled "Shafer".
