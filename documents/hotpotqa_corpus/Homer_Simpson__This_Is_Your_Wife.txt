Homer Simpson, This Is Your Wife: "Homer Simpson, This Is Your Wife" is the fifteenth episode of the seventeenth season of the American animated television sitcom "The Simpsons".  It originally aired on the Fox network in the United States on March 26, 2006, and was watched by around ten million people during that broadcast.  In the episode, Homer signs the Simpson family up for a reality show in which the mothers of two families switch places.  Marge gets to live with a friendly man named Charles and his perfect son, while Homer, Bart, and Lisa must spend time with Charles' strict wife Verity.
