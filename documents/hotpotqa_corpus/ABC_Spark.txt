ABC Spark: ABC Spark is a Canadian English-language Category B digital cable and satellite television specialty channel which is owned by Corus Entertainment, and launched on March 23, 2012.  The channel is the replacement and successor to Dusk.  ABC Spark is based on the U.S. cable network of Freeform (previously known as ABC Family).  The channel primarily consists of programming aimed at teenagers and preteens, and has an additional interest in programming aimed towards families.  The channel's name and various programs are licensed from the Disney–ABC Television Group, a subsidiary of The Walt Disney Company.
