The Onion: The Onion is an American digital media company and news satire organization that publishes articles on international, national, and local news.  Based in Chicago, the company originated as a weekly print publication on August 29, 1988 in Madison, Wisconsin.  In the spring of 1996, "The Onion" began publishing online.  In 2007, the organization began publishing satirical news audio and video online, as the Onion News Network.  In 2013, "The Onion" ceased publishing its print edition and launched Onion Labs, an advertising agency.
