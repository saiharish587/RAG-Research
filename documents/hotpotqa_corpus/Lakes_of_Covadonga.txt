Lakes of Covadonga: The Lakes of Covadonga (el.  1134 m.) are composed of two glacial lakes located on the region of Asturias, Spain.  These lakes, often also called Lakes of Enol or simply Los Lagos, are Lake Enol and Lake Ercina located in the Picos de Europa range and they are the original center of the Picos de Europa National Park, created in 1918.
