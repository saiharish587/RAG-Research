Tony Harrison (boxer): On February 25, 2017 he lost to Jarrett Hurd for the International Boxing Federation light middleweight world title.  The fight was originally supposed to be a title eliminator for the right to fight Jermall Charlo, but when Charlo vacated the title, the fight was elevated to be for the vacant title.
