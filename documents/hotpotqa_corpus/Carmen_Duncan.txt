Carmen Duncan: Carmen Joan Duncan (born 7 July 1942) is an Australian actress.  She was nominated for the AFI Award for Best Actress for the 1980 film "Harlequin".  Her other film appearances include "Touch and Go" (1980) "Turkey Shoot" (1982) and "Now and Forever" (1983).  She went on to play Iris Wheeler on the American soap opera "Another World" from 1988 to 1994.
