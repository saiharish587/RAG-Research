Funki Porcini: Funki Porcini is the stage name of English musician and film maker James Braddell.  His music is a combination of downtempo, breakbeat and nu jazz with highlights of samples and time-stretched vocals.  The name "Funki Porcini" is a twist on "Funghi Porcini", the Italian name of the mushroom Boletus edulis, commonly known as "penny bun".
