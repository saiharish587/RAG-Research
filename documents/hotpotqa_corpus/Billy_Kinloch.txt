Billy Kinloch: William Francis Kinloch (March 21, 1874 - February 15 1931), was a Major League Baseball player.  He played one game at third base for the 1895 St. Louis Browns.  He was born on March 21, 1874 in Providence, Rhode Island and he died on February 15, 1931 in New York City.  He is buried at the Calvary Cemetery in Woodside, New York.  He played his first game at the age of 21 years.
