Uzbek language: Uzbek is a Turkic language and the official language of Uzbekistan.  It has 27 million native speakers and is spoken by the Uzbeks in Uzbekistan and elsewhere in Central Asia.  Uzbek belongs to the Eastern Turkic, or Karluk, branch of the Turkic language family.  External influences include Persian, Arabic and Russian.  One of the most noticeable distinctions of Uzbek from other Turkic languages is the rounding of the vowel /a/ to /ɒ/ , a feature that was influenced by Persian.
