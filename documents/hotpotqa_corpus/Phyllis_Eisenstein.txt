Phyllis Eisenstein: Phyllis Eisenstein (born February 2, 1946) is an American author of science fiction and fantasy short stories and novels whose work has been nominated for both the Hugo Award and Nebula Award.  She is an old friend of author George R. R. Martin and convinced him to include dragons in his international best-selling fantasy series "A Song of Ice and Fire".  Martin then dedicated the third novel in the series, "A Storm of Swords", to Eisenstein.
