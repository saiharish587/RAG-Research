Alien Intelligence (Palladium Books): In the various game settings of Palladium Books, Alien Intelligences are fictional, vastly powerful beings of unknown origin that are stated to be a combination of equal parts spirit, magical energy, and physical flesh.  They are said to exist on different planes of existence simultaneously, and in the Palladium hierarchy of powers, most Alien Intelligences, apart from the Vampire Intelligences, are more powerful than the mightiest Gods in Palladium.  In fact, some of the most powerful Gods of the Palladium Megaverse were themselves spawned by the Alien Intelligences.
