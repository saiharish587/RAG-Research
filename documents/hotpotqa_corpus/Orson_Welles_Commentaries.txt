Orson Welles Commentaries: Orson Welles Commentaries (1945–46) is an ABC radio series produced and directed by Orson Welles.  Featuring commentary by Welles, with reminiscences and readings from literature, the 15-minute weekly program aired Sunday afternoons at 1:15 p.m. ET beginning September 16, 1945.  Lear Radio sponsored the program through the end of June 1946 when it failed to find a larger audience.  The series was continued by ABC as a sustaining show through October 6, 1946.  "Orson Welles Commentaries" was the last of Welles's own radio shows.
