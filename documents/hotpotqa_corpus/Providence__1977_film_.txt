Providence (1977 film): Providence is a 1977 French/Swiss film directed by Alain Resnais from a screenplay by David Mercer.  It explores the processes of creativity through a portrayal of an ageing novelist, played by John Gielgud, who imagines scenes for his latest novel which draw upon his past history and his relationships with members of his family.  The film won the 1978 César Award for Best Film.
