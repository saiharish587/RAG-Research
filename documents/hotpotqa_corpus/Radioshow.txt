Radioshow: Radioshow is а cult Lithuanian black comedy radio and TV show hosted by Algis Ramanauskas-Greitai and Rimas Šapauskas.  It is also the name of their humorous rock band.  Radioshow has started at radio station Radiocentras in 1992.  Later it has moved to the radio station Ultra Vires.  In 1995 Radioshow debuted as a comedy puppet show on TV at LNK station.  From 1997 till 1999 Radioshow was running at BTV TV station.  In 2004 it briefly ran on Vilnius radio station Užupio radijas before being shut down, because of swearing on the air.  However the show was immediately picked up by the regional radio station Vox Maris.
