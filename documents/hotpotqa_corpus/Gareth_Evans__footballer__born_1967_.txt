Gareth Evans (footballer, born 1967): Gareth John Evans (born 14 January 1967) is an English football player and coach.  He played for Coventry City, Rotherham United, Hibernian, Stoke City, Northampton Town, Partick Thistle, Airdrie and Alloa Athletic.  Evans has been the caretaker manager of Hibernian and Livingston, and was head coach of the Scotland women's under-19 national team for four years.
