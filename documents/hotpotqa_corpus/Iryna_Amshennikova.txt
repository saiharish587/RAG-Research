Iryna Amshennikova: Iryna Vitaliïvna Amshennikova (Ukrainian: Ірина Віталіївна Амшеннікова , born 19 May 1986) is a Ukrainian backstroke swimmer.  She had her best achievements in short course competitions, where she won bronze at the 2002 FINA World Swimming Championships (25 m) in the 200 m backstroke and six medals, including one gold, at the European Short Course Swimming Championships.  She also competed at the 2004 and 2008 Summer Olympics in several events, but was eliminated in the preliminaries.  During her career she set 29 national records in the 50–200 m backstroke and various relay events.
