Diselma: Diselma archeri (Dwarf Pine) or (Cheshunt pine) is a species of plant of the family Cupressaceae and the sole species in the genus Diselma.  It is endemic to the alpine regions of Tasmania's southwest and Central Highlands, on the western coast ranges and Lake St. Clair.  It is a monotypic genus restricted to high altitude rainforest and moist alpine heathland.  Its distribution mirrors very closely that of other endemic Tasmanian conifers "Microcachrys tetragona" and "Pherosphaera hookeriana".
