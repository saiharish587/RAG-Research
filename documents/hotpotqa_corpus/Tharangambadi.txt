Tharangambadi: Tharangambadi, formerly Tranquebar, is a town in the Nagapattinam district of the Indian state of Tamil Nadu.  It lies 15 km north of Karaikal, near the mouth of a distributary of the Kaveri River.  Tharangambadi is the headquarters of Tharangambadi taluk while its name means "place of the singing waves".  It was a Danish colony from 1620 to 1845, and in Danish it is still known as "Trankebar".
