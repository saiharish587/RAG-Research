Van Diemen's Land Company: The Van Diemen's Land Company (also known as Van Dieman Land Company) is a farming corporation in the Australian state of Tasmania.  It was founded in 1825 and received a royal charter the same year, and was granted 250,000 acres.  (1,000 km) in northwest Van Diemen's Land (now Tasmania) in 1826.  The company was a group of London merchants who planned a wool growing venture to supply the needs of the British textile industry.
