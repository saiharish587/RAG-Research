Quentin C. Aanenson: Quentin C. Aanenson (April 21, 1921 – December 28, 2008) was a World War II veteran fighter pilot and former captain of the 391st Fighter Squadron, 366th Fighter Group, 9th Air Force, U.S. Army Air Corps.  He flew the P-47 Thunderbolt in the Normandy D-Day invasion and subsequent European campaign.
