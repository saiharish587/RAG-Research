Stroud and District Combination Bill Adams Cup: The Stroud and District Combination Bill Adams Cup is an annual rugby union knock-out club competition organized by the Stroud and District Combination - one of the five bodies that make up the Gloucestershire Rugby Football Union.  It was first introduced during the 1983-84 season, with the first ever winners being Cirencester III.  The Bill Adams Cup is the third most important cup competition in Stroud District behind the Senior Cup (1st) and Stroud and District Combination Junior Cup (2nd).  With only 9 member clubs the combination is one of the smallest in Gloucestershire.
