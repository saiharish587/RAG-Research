Axess (CRS): Axess (アクセス国際ネットワーク) is a Computer reservations system based in Japan which provides its services in the Japanese market.  It originated as the IT department of Japan Airlines called Jalinfotech.  In 1991 it was established as an independent company it began to be marketed to travel agencies in Japan.  In 1995 Axess partnered with Sabre Holdings in order to provide travel agencies in Japan with booking and ticketing capabilities for a wider range of international airlines.
