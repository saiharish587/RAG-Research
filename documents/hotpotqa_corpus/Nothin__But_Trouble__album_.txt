Nothin' But Trouble (album): Nothin' But Trouble is the debut studio album by American singer and actress Nia Peeples.  It was released on August 30, 1988 by Mercury Records.  Peeples decided to venture into the music industry after she came to recognition as a regular cast in the musical television series "Fame".  She worked with Steve Harvey, Howard Hewett, Doc Powell and Tony Prendatt on the album.  Musically, "Nothin' But Trouble" is a dance-pop and R&B album with influence of other popular genres at the time, such as freestyle and new jack swing.
