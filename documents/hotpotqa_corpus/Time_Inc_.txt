Time Inc.: Time Inc. is a multinational mass media corporation founded on November 28, 1922 by Henry Luce and Briton Hadden and based in New York City.  It owns and publishes over 100 magazine brands, most notably its flagship "Time".  Other magazines include "Sports Illustrated", "Travel + Leisure", "Food & Wine", "Fortune", "People", "InStyle", "Life", "Golf Magazine", "Southern Living", "Essence", "Real Simple", and "Entertainment Weekly".  It also has subsidiaries which it co-operates with the UK magazine house Time Inc.  UK, whose major titles include "What's on TV", "NME", "Country Life", and "Wallpaper".  Time Inc. also co-operates over 60 websites and digital-only titles including "MyRecipes", "TheSnug", HelloGiggles, and "MIMI".
