BRICS U-17 Football Cup: The BRICS U-17 Football Cup is a U-17 Football tournament to be played among teams of five BRICS countries - economic block formed by Brazil, Russia, India, China and South Africa.  The 1st historic edition was played in Goa, India in 2016 on the occasion of the 8th BRICS summit that was held in India.  The Tournament will take place once every year with 10-day and following the lines of the tournaments under-17 FIFA.  Each edition will be held in a BRICS nation.  On 1 October 2016 The Prime Minister of India Shri Narendra Modi, unveiled the Trophy for the 2016 BRICS U-17 Football Tournament in london
