Rosa Parks Transit Station: Rosa Parks Transit Station is an intermodal transit station in Downtown Jacksonville, Florida.  It is operated by the Jacksonville Transportation Authority and serves as Jacksonville's main city bus station as well as a station for the Jacksonville Skyway elevated people mover and the First Coast Flyer bus rapid transit system.  It is located on Hogan Street between State Street and Union Street, and is the Skyway's northern terminus.  It is across the street from the Downtown campus of Florida State College at Jacksonville.
