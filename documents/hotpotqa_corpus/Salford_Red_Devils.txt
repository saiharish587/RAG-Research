Salford Red Devils: Salford Red Devils R.L.F.C. is a professional rugby league club in Salford, Greater Manchester, England, who play in the Super League.  Formed in 1873, they have won six Championships and one Challenge Cup.  Their home ground since 2012 has been the AJ Bell Stadium in Barton-upon-Irwell, before which they played at the Willows in Weaste.  Before 1995, the club was known simply as Salford, from 1995-98 Salford Reds and from 1999-2013 Salford City Reds.
