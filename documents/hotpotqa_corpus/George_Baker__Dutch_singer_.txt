George Baker (Dutch singer): George Baker (born Johannes (Hans) Bouwens, 8 December 1944) is a Dutch singer and songwriter who, with his band George Baker Selection, scored two international hits in the 1970s, "Paloma Blanca" and "Little Green Bag."  He became a solo artist after 1989.  "Little Green Bag" was used as the opening soundtrack for the movie "Reservoir Dogs."
