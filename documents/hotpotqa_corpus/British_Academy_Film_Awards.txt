British Academy Film Awards: The British Academy Film Awards or BAFTA Film Awards are presented in an annual award show hosted by the British Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA) to honour the best British and international contributions to film.  Between 2008 and 2016, the ceremony was held in central London at the Royal Opera House, having taken over from the flagship Odeon cinema in Leicester Square.  The 70th British Academy Film Awards were held on 12 February 2017 at the Royal Albert Hall in London.
