Det perfekte mord: Det perfekte mord (English: The Perfect Murder ), also known as Det perfekte mord – Homo Falsus, is a 1992 Norwegian thriller film directed by Eva Isaksen, starring Gard B. Eidsvold, Anna-Lena Hemström and Anne Marit Jacobsen.  It is based on the novel "Homo Falsus" by Jan Kjærstad.
