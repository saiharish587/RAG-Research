Narada sting operation: The Narada sting operation was a sting operation carried out by Mathew Samuel in 2011 for the Indian newsmagazine "Tehelka" and published in Naradanews.com just before the 2016 West Bengal Assembly elections.  The sting targeted high-ranking officials and politicians of All India Trinamul Congress (AITC).  As of June 2017, the Central Bureau of Investigation (CBI) and a parliamentary ethics committee are probing the case.  The Trinamool Congress, has claimed that the money was received in way of donations.
