Jacksonville Skyway: The Jacksonville Skyway is a people mover in Jacksonville, Florida, United States.  It is an automated monorail train operated by the Jacksonville Transportation Authority (JTA).  Opening in 1989 with three stations in Downtown Jacksonville, the Skyway was extended in 1996 following a conversion from its original technology to Bombardier Transportation equipment.  It was expanded again in 1998 and 2000.  The system currently comprises two routes across 2.5 mi of track, serving eight stations, and crosses the St. Johns River on the Acosta Bridge.  There is currently no fare to ride the Skyway, which had 1.2 million passengers in 2014.
