Marx in Soho: Marx in Soho is a 1999 one-man play written by American historian Howard Zinn, about the life of 19th-century philosopher Karl Marx.  Zinn wrote the play to "show Marx as few people knew him, as a family man, struggling to support his wife and children."  The play is set in the New York City neighborhood of Soho, a humorous nod to the London neighborhood of the same name in which Marx spent much of his life.
