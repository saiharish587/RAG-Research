Zeke Jones: Larry Lee "Zeke" Jones (born December 2, 1966) is an American wrestler who won a silver medal at the 1992 Summer Olympics in Barcelona, a world championship in Varna, Bulgaria, in 1991, and was the former freestyle head coach at USA Wrestling.  He is currently the head coach of the Arizona State University Wrestling team as of April, 2014.  He was a six-time national freestyle champion, four-time World Cup champion, Pan American Games champion, and received the "World's Most Technical Wrestler Award" awarded by FILA, the international governing body for the sport.  In college, he was a three-time All-American for Arizona State University and competed on the 1988 NCAA Championship team, coached by famed Bobby Douglas.  After college, he wrestled on two world championships teams with the United States wrestling team.  In 2005, he was inducted into the National Wrestling Hall of Fame.
