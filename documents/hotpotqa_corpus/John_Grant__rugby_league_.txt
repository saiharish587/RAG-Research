John Grant (rugby league): John Grant (born 19 March 1950) is an Australian businessman, rugby league football administrator and current chairman of the Australian Rugby League Commission which controls rugby league in Australia.  A former player of the 1970s, he was a Queensland interstate representative three-quarter back and a member of the Australian team which lost the 1972 World Cup to Great Britain in France.  Grant had been playing his club football for the Brisbane Rugby League's Souths club under Wayne Bennett.  Following the World Cup, Grant joined English club Warrington, playing for them during their table-topping 1972–73 season.
