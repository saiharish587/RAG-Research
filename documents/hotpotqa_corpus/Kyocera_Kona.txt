Kyocera Kona: Kyocera Kona is a line of low cost cellular phones manufactured by Kyocera Communications, Inc.  The Kona line up of phones are available on the following US Carriers: Sprint, Cricket, Virgin Mobile, and Boost Mobile.  The Kyocera Kona is respective representation of the current line up of low cost and usually contract free phones available for sale in the US during the year 2013.
