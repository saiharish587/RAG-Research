The Census Taker: The Census Taker is a 1984 black comedy directed by Bruce R. Cook.  It stars Greg Mullavey, Meredith MacRae, Timothy Bottoms, and Garrett Morris.  The film was The Residents' first film soundtrack commission when their participation was suggested by Penn Jillette to Cook.  It was released by Trans World Entertainment on VHS in 1988 under the title Husbands, Wives, Money & Murder.
