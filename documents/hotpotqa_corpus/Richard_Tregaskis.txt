Richard Tregaskis: Richard William Tregaskis (November 28, 1916 – August 15, 1973) was an American journalist and author whose best-known work is "Guadalcanal Diary" (1943), an account of just the first several weeks (in August - September 1942) of the U.S. Marine Corps invasion of Guadalcanal in the Solomon Islands during World War II.  This was actually a six-month-long campaign.  Tregaskis served as a war correspondent during World War II, the Korean War, and the Vietnam War.
