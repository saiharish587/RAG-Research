Berger Blanc Suisse: The Berger Blanc Suisse (English: White Swiss Shepherd , German: "Weisser Schweizer Schäferhund" , Italian: "Pastore Svizzero Bianco" ) is a breed of dog from Switzerland.  It is of the same origins as the White Shepherd and the German Shepherd Dog, and has been recognized as a separate breed by the FCI.
