KXI92: KXI92 (sometimes referred to as Mount Ida All Hazards) is a NOAA Weather Radio station that serves Mount Ida, Arkansas and surrounding cities.  It is programmed from the National Weather Service forecast office in Little Rock, Arkansas with its transmitter located in Mount Ida.  It broadcasts weather and hazard information for the following counties: Clark, Garland, Hot Spring, Perry, Pike, Polk, Yell, and Montgomery.
