Jack Little (songwriter): Jack Little (born John Leonard; May 30, 1899 – April 9, 1956), (Another source gives his birth date as May 28, 1902.)  sometimes credited Little Jack Little, was a British-born American composer, singer, pianist, actor, and songwriter whose songs were featured in several movies.  He is not to be confused with the burlesque comedian also known as "Little" Jack Little, who stood 4'5".
