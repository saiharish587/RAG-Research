Lapeer, Michigan: Lapeer is a city in the U.S. state of Michigan and is the county seat of Lapeer County.  As of the 2010 census, the city population was 8,841.  Most of the city was incorporated from land that was formerly in Lapeer Township, though portions were also annexed from Mayfield Township and Elba Township.  The city government is politically independent of all three townships.  Lapeer is in southern Michigan, east of Flint, on the Flint River.  The name "Lapeer" is a corruption of the French "la pierre", which means "the flint".  (See List of Michigan county name etymologies.)
