Michael Symon: Michael D. Symon (born September 19, 1969) is an American chef, restaurateur, television personality, and author.  He is of Greek and Sicilian descent.  He is seen regularly on Food Network on shows such as "Iron Chef America", "Food Feuds", and "The Best Thing I Ever Ate", as well as "Cook Like an Iron Chef" on the Cooking Channel and "The Chew" on ABC.  He has also made numerous contributions to periodicals such as "Bon Appétit", "Esquire", "Food Arts", "Gourmet", "Saveur" and "O, The Oprah Magazine".
