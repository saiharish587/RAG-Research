Dying to Say This to You: Dying to Say This to You is the second studio album in English by Swedish new wave group The Sounds.  It was released on 15 March 2006 in Sweden and 21 March 2006 in the United States.  The album blends Swedish-influenced new wave music with a sassy and spunky delivery by vocalist Maja Ivarsson, reminiscent of Blondie.  The cover depicts The Misshapes' DJ Leigh Lezark on the left and her friend Alexis Page on the right.
