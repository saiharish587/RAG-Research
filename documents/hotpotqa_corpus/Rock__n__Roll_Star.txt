Rock 'n' Roll Star: "Rock 'n' Roll Star" is a song by English rock band Oasis.  It is the opening track from their record breaking debut album, "Definitely Maybe".  Like the majority of the band's songs from this era, it was written by lead guitarist Noel Gallagher.  Noel said that "Rock 'n' Roll Star" was one of only three songs in which he wanted to say something: "I've pretty much summed up everything I wanted to say in "Rock 'n' Roll Star", "Live Forever" and "Cigarettes & Alcohol", after that I'm repeating myself, but in a different way".  It became a fan favourite and was often played to close the band's gigs.
