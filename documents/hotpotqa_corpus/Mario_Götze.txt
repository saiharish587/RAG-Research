Mario Götze: Mario Götze (] ; born 3 June 1992) is a German professional footballer who plays as an attacking midfielder for German Bundesliga club Borussia Dortmund and the Germany national team.  Although his favoured position is that of a playmaker.  Götze possesses speed, technique, dribbling skills, and playmaking capabilities.  In 2010, German Football Association's then technical director Matthias Sammer described Götze as "one of the best talents Germany has ever had."
