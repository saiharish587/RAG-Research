Lanphier Park: Lanphier Park is a stadium in Springfield, Illinois.  It is primarily used for baseball.  It originally opened in 1928 and was renovated in 1977.  It holds 5,000 people.  The facility's full name is Robin Roberts Stadium at Lanphier Park.  Roberts (1926–2010), a Hall-of-Fame pitcher and a graduate of Lanphier High School, was Springfield's most accomplished ballplayer.
