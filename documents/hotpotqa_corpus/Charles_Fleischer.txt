Charles Fleischer: Charles Fleischer (born August 27, 1950) is an American stand-up comedian, actor, voice actor, writer and musician, best known for appearing in films such as "A Nightmare on Elm Street", "Who Framed Roger Rabbit", "Back to the Future Part II", "The Polar Express", "Rango", and "We're Back!  A Dinosaur's Story".  He also reprised the role of Roger Rabbit in the "Roger Rabbit" theatrical shorts.  After beginning his career on the comedy club circuit, Charles Fleischer's first big break in comedy television came when he made an appearance on "Rowan & Martin's Laugh-In".
