Berberis standleyi: Berberis standleyi is a shrub in the Berberidaceae described as a species in 1952.  It was published with the name "Mahonia glauca," a very different plant from "Berberis glauca."  Thus if one desires to consider "Berberis" and "Mahonia" as one genus instead of two, it is necessary to use a different name, i.e. "Berberis standleyi".
