Emperor of Japan: The Emperor of Japan is the head of the Imperial Family and the traditional head of state of Japan.  According to the 1947 constitution, he is "the symbol of the State and of the unity of the people."  Historically, he is also the highest authority of the Shinto religion as he and his family are said to be the direct descendants of the sun-goddess Amaterasu, and his importance also lies in dealing with heavenly affairs, including Shinto ritual and rites throughout the nation.
