Gérald Genta: Charles Gérald Genta (1931-2011) was a Swiss jewellery designer, noted for his eponymous line of time pieces as well as his design work with other firms, including IWC, Universal Genève, Patek Philippe and Audemars Piguet, among others.  Christie's auction house of New York called Genta's work ""the Fabergé of watches"", while "The Wall Street Journal" has called them the "world's most complicated and pricey watches".
