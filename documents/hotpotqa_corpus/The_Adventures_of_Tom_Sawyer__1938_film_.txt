The Adventures of Tom Sawyer (1938 film): The Adventures of Tom Sawyer is a 1938 American literature adaptation directed by Norman Taurog starring Tommy Kelly in the title role.  The screenplay by John V. A. Weaver was based on the classic 1876 novel by Mark Twain.  The picture was the first film version of the novel to be made in color.  It was remade in 1973 as a musical.
