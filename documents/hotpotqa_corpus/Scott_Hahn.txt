Scott Hahn: Scott W. Hahn (born October 28, 1957) is an American Roman Catholic lay theologian, contemporary author, professor, and Christian apologist.  A former Presbyterian who converted to Catholicism, Hahn's popular works include "Rome Sweet Home" and "The Lamb's Supper: The Mass as Heaven on Earth".  His lectures have been featured in multiple audio distributions through Lighthouse Catholic Media.  Hahn is known for his notable research on early Christianity during the Apostolic Age and various theoretical works concerning the early Church fathers.
