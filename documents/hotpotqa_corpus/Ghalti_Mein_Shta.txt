Ghalti Mein Shta: "Ghalti Mein Shta" ( Pashto: غلطي خه ښټه‎ ; lit.  "The Fault is Mine") is a 2014 Pashtu language single by Pakistani Soft Alternative rock band Naseer & Shahab.  It was released on August 23, 2014 as a digital download.  It is an soulful Pashtu love-inspired ballad that depicts the female protagonist remembers her lover who is lost long, she visits his grave hoping one day she will see him.  The song is written and composed by Naseer Afridi while directed, produced and co-composed by Shahab Qamar.
