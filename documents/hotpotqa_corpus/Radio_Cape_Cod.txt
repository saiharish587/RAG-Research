Radio Cape Cod: Radio Cape Cod is a 2008 film directed and produced by Andrew Silver.  It stars Tamzin Outhwaite as a radio interviewer coming to terms with the loss of her husband and a new love entering her life.  She also must cope with her teenage daughter, played by Tamzin Merchant, who is experiencing her first love.  The film also stars O. T. Fagbenle.  The film won several festival awards.
