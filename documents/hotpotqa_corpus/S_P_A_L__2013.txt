S.P.A.L. 2013: S.P.A.L. 2013, better known as SPAL (] ), an acronym for Società Polisportiva Ars et Labor, is a professional Italian football club, founded in 1907 and based in Ferrara, Emilia-Romagna.  The club plays in Serie A, the highest level of the Italian football league system.  Since 1928 SPAL has played its home matches at the Stadio Paolo Mazza, named after Paolo Mazza (chairman of the club 1946–1977).
