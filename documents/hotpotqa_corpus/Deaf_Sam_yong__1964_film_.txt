Deaf Sam-yong (1964 film): Deaf Sam-yong (Korean: 벙어리 삼룡 ; "Beongeori Samryong") is a 1964 South Korean drama film directed, produced by Shin Sang-ok, based on the 1925 short story of the sama title by Na Do-hyang.  It was chosen as Best Film at the Grand Bell Awards.  The film was also selected as the South Korean entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 37th Academy Awards, but was not accepted as a nominee.
