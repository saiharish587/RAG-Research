Bond Girls Are Forever: Bond Girls Are Forever is a 2002 James Bond documentary film hosted by actress Maryam d'Abo, who had played the role of Kara Milovy in the 15th James Bond film "The Living Daylights".  It was accompanied by a 2003 book written by John Cork and d'Abo.  The book is subtitled The Women of James Bond.  Both the film and the book is a tribute to the elite club of women who have played the role of a Bond girl.
