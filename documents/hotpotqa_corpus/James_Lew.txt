James Lew: James Jene Fae Lew (born September 6, 1952) is an American martial arts actor.  He has made 80 on-screen film and television appearances and 46 more as a stunt coordinator or stunt double.  He has done choreography for movies like "Get Smart", "Killers" and the cult classic "Big Trouble In Little China", as well as television shows such as "National Geographic's Fight Science", "The Crow" and "Entourage".  He does Shaolin Kung-Fu, Tae Kwon Do, Jeet Kune Do, Hapkido, and Boxing.  He also trained Brad Pitt with sword fighting for the movie "Troy".
