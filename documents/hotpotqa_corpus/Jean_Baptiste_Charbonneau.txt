Jean Baptiste Charbonneau: Jean Baptiste Charbonneau (February 11, 1805 – May 16, 1866) was an American Indian explorer, guide, fur trapper trader, military scout during the Mexican-American War, "alcalde" (mayor) of Mission San Luis Rey de Francia and a gold prospector and hotel operator in Northern California.  He spoke French and English, and learned German and Spanish during his six years in Europe from 1823 to 1829.  He spoke Shoshone, his mother tongue, and other western American Indian languages, which he picked up during his years of trapping and guiding.
