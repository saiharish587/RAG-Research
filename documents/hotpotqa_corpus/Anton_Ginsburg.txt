Anton Ginsburg: Anton Ginsburg (18 September 1930 – 19 July 2002) was a Russian pianist.  He was born in Moscow.  A disciple of Heinrich Neuhaus, he graduated from the Moscow Conservatory in 1953.  Four years later he won the Smetana Competition in Prague.  Ginsburg has been active as a concert pianist both in the USSR and abroad, but is best remembered for his work as an accompanist with Daniil Shafran.
