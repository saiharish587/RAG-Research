Tom Brokaw: Thomas John Brokaw ( ; born February 6, 1940) is an American television journalist and author, best known for being the anchor and managing editor of "NBC Nightly News" for 22 years from 1982 to 2004.  He is the only person to have hosted all three major NBC News programs: "The Today Show", "NBC Nightly News", and, briefly, "Meet the Press".  He now serves as a Special Correspondent for NBC News and works on documentaries for other outlets.
