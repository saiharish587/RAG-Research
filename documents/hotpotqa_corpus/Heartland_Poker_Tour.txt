Heartland Poker Tour: Heartland Poker Tour (HPT) is an internationally syndicated television program airing 52 weeks each year and a U.S.-based poker tour.  Created by friends Greg Lang and Todd Anderson in 2005, HPT promotes the tagline "Real People, Unreal Money."  Originally developed to be a regional TV show airing in the Midwest, syndication expanded as the appetite for televised poker grew.  HPT now airs weekly on hundreds of stations across the United States, Europe, the Caribbean, and the Middle East.  DirecTV and DISH Network carry the program on multiple stations in the U.S.
