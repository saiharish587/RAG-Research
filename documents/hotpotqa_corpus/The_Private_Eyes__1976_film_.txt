The Private Eyes (1976 film): The Private Eyes is a 1976 Hong Kong comedy film written, directed by and starring Michael Hui and co-starring his brothers, Samuel Hui and Ricky Hui, and also starring Shih Kien and Richard Ng in his second film role.  John Woo was the production designer and also co-director though he was uncredited.  Sammo Hung served as the film's action director and Jackie Chan was also a stuntman.  This is the third film of the Hui Brothers and it is the first film that established the Hui Brothers' comedies internationally.
