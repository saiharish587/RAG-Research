Parthen Rasithen: Parthen Rasithen English: "Saw Admired" ), is a 2000 Indian Tamil-language romantic thriller written and directed by Saran.  The film features Simran, Prashanth, Laila in the lead roles, while Raghuvaran, Jai Ganesh, Vinu Chakravarthy, Vaiyapuri, Charle, Dhamu, Lawrence Raghavendra, Fathima Babu and Jyothi play supporting roles.  The film music was composed by Bharadwaj.
