Rogue by Rihanna: Rogue is the fourth fragrance released by Barbadian singer Rihanna.  The fragrance was released in two installments; firstly a women's fragrance and finally a men's cologne.  The women's fragrance was officially released on September 4, 2013 under the perfume line, Parlux Fragrances and was made available for purchase on Rihanna's official perfume website and also at Macy's, Dillards and Belk stores.
