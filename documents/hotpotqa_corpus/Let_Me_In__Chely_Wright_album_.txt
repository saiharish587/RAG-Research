Let Me In (Chely Wright album): Let Me In is the third studio album by American country artist Chely Wright.  The album was released on September 9, 1997 on MCA Nashville Records and was produced by Tony Brown.  "Let Me In" was Wright's first album to chart on the "Billboard Magazine" album charts and also spawned her first Top 40 singles.  It was also the first of three albums Wright recorded for the MCA Nashville label.
