Eldar Abdulayev: Eldar Rasimovich Abdulayev (Russian: Эльдар Расимович Абдулаев ; born January 20, 1985) is a Kazakhstani professional ice hockey forward currently playing for HC Astana in the Kazakhstan Hockey Championship.  He was a member of the Kazakhstan men's national junior ice hockey team at the 2005 World Junior Ice Hockey Championships.
