Lap Dance (film): Lap Dance, formally titled Monica, is an American drama produced by Datari Turner Productions & directed by Greg Carter.  The film's ensemble cast includes Briana Evigan, Robert Hoffman, Ali Cobrin, Datari Turner, James Remar, Mariel Hemingway, Omari Hardwick, Lynn Whitfield, Carmen Electra, Nia Peeples, Stacey Dash, Junie Hoang and LisaRaye.
