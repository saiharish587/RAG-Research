Road to Nowhere: "Road to Nowhere" is a rock song written by David Byrne for the 1985 Talking Heads album "Little Creatures."  It also appeared on "Best of Talking Heads", "", the "Once in a Lifetime" box set and the "Brick" box set.  The song was released as a single in 1985 and reached number 25 on the Billboard Mainstream Rock Tracks and number 6 in the British, German and South African singles charts.  It also made number 8 on the Dutch Top 40.
