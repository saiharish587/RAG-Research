Retina Display: Retina Display is a brand name used by Apple for its series of IPS panel displays that have a higher pixel density than traditional displays.  Apple has applied to register the term "Retina" as a trademark in regard to computers and mobile devices with the United States Patent and Trademark Office, Canadian Intellectual Property Office, and in Jamaica.  On November 27, 2012 the US Patent and Trademark office approved Apple's application and "Retina" is now a registered trademark for computer equipment.
