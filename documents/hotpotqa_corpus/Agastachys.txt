Agastachys: Agastachys odorata, commonly known as the white waratah, is the sole member of the genus Agastachys in the protea family.  It is an evergreen shrub to small tree and is endemic to the heaths and button grass sedgelands of western Tasmania.  It occurs most often in moist heath and scrub and occasionally in the alpine regions, but generally prefers well-drained but poor soils.  It can grow in some rainforests where it forms a small tree but is normally a shrub in all other situations.  The heaviest concentrations are along the island's south coast.  Its leaves are dark green, hairless and almost succulent.  Masses of white flowers are produced in erect spikes from the ends of the branches.  Measuring 8 to 12 cm high, they appear in January and February.
