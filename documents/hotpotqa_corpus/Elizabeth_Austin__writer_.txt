Elizabeth Austin (writer): Elizabeth Austin (born 1958) is an American writer living in Oak Park, Illinois.  Austin has lived in the Midwestern United States all her life.  She began her journalism career at the now-defunct City News Bureau of Chicago and has written articles for national news magazines such as "Newsweek" and "Time", in addition to a wide range of other national and regional publications, such as "People", and the "Chicago Tribune".  She has also written for "The Washington Monthly, Ladies Home Journal, Good Housekeeping, Self", and "Shape".  She has often written about medicine and health care.  Austin also has been heard on WBEZ, the National Public Radio station in Chicago, as well as the BBC in the UK and Scotland.  She has appeared on the Today Show and on ITV in Europe.
