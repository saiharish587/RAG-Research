2017–18 FA Cup: The 2017–18 FA Cup (also known as the FA Challenge Cup) is the 137th edition of the oldest recognised football tournament in the world.  It is sponsored by Emirates, and known as the Emirates FA Cup for sponsorship purposes.  737 clubs were accepted into the tournament.  It began with the Extra Preliminary Round on 5 August 2017, and will conclude with the final on 19 May 2018.  The winner will qualify for the 2018–19 UEFA Europa League group stage.
