The Creature That Ate Sheboygan: The Creature That Ate Sheboygan is a science fiction board game released in 1979 by Simulations Publications (SPI).  The game was originally designed by Greg Costikyan.  It won the Charles S. Roberts Award for "Best Fantasy or Science Fiction Game of 1979".  The game is very similar to the Epyx 1981 release, "Crush, Crumble and Chomp! ".
