Des O'Connor: O'Connor was born in Stepney, East London to Maude ("née" Bassett) and Harry O'Connor.  His mother was Jewish and his father was from Ireland.  Des claims to be the only O'Connor who ever had a Bar Mitzvah.  He had a brother, William O'Connor, and has a sister, Patricia, who is one year his junior.  He was evacuated to Northampton during the Second World War and was briefly a professional footballer with Northampton Town.
