Hope Sandoval &amp; the Warm Inventions: Hope Sandoval & The Warm Inventions is an independent alternative/dream pop band composed of Hope Sandoval from the band Mazzy Star and Colm Ó Cíosóig of My Bloody Valentine.  Their first studio album, "Bavarian Fruit Bread", was released on October 23, 2001.  Alan Browne, from Irish band Dirt Blue Gene, played bass and co-wrote several songs on the album. " Through the Devil Softly" was released on September 29, 2009, and was recorded with Dirt Blue Gene.  Their third album, "Until the Hunter", was released on November 4.  Its first single, "Isn't It True", was released on 7" vinyl as part of Record Store Day 2016.  A second single, "Let Me Get There" featuring Kurt Vile, was released on September 23.
