Melt! (Siouxsie and the Banshees song): "Melt!"  is a song by English post-punk band Siouxsie and the Banshees.  It was released in 1982 by record label Polydor as a double A-sided single with the song "Il est né, le divin Enfant" and is the second and final single from the band's fifth studio album, "A Kiss in the Dreamhouse" ("Il est né, le divin Enfant" does not appear on that album).
