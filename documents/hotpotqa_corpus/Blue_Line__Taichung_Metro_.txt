Blue Line (Taichung Metro): The Taichung BRT Blue Line (Chinese: 臺中快捷巴士) was a Bus Rapid Transit (BRT) system located in Taichung which stretched from Taichung Station to Providence University on the main road, Taiwan Boulevard.  The line was 17.1 km in length with 21 stations.  It was the first BRT line in Taiwan.  The line was converted to dedicated bus lane for conventional buses on July 8, 2015 and the BRT is no longer in operation.
