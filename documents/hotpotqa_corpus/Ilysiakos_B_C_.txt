Ilysiakos B.C.: Ilysiakos B.C. (Greek: Ηλυσιακός Κ.Α.Ε.)  is a Greek professional basketball team that is located in the Ilisia neighborhood of Zografou, Athens, Greece.  The club's name is said to be a reference to Elysium, which is why the club's name and logo beings with a Greek alphabet Η, instead of an Ι, even though the name of Ilissia, where the club is based, begins with an Ι in Greek.  However, when the name is translated into the English alphabet, the H becomes an I.
