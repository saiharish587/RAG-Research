Bushwick, Brooklyn: Bushwick is a working-class neighborhood in the northern part of the New York City borough of Brooklyn.  The neighborhood, historically a community of Germanic immigrants and their descendants, has been predominantly Hispanic in the late 20th century.  The neighborhood, formerly Brooklyn's 18th Ward, is now part of Brooklyn Community Board 4.  It is policed by the NYPD's 83rd Precinct and is represented in the New York City Council as part of Districts 34 and 37.
