Hillsville Historic District: Hillsville Historic District is a national historic district located at Hillsville, Carroll County, Virginia.  The district encompasses 14 contributing buildings and 1 contributing object in the core commercial district of Hillsville.  Notable properties include the Carter Building (1857), Carroll County Bank (1907), and the Hillsville Diner (1936).  Also in the district is the former U.S. Post Office (1951) that houses the Carroll County Historical Society.  The remaining buildings are two- and three-story brick commercial buildings from the 1930s and 1940s.  The Carroll County Courthouse is located in the district and separately listed.
