Durham UCCE in 2005: The British cricket team Durham UCCE played three first-class games in 2005.  They started their first-class season on a batting paradise in Taunton, which secured them their first draw of the year.  Thanks to a painfully slow innings against Leicestershire, they drew their second game.  Their third and final first-class match of the year was also a draw, although a close one - they finished with nine wickets down in the second innings.
