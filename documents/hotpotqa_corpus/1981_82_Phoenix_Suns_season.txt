1981–82 Phoenix Suns season: The 1981–82 Phoenix Suns season was the 14th season for the Phoenix Suns of the National Basketball Association.  The Suns were in the playoffs for the fifth consecutive season, extending a then-franchise record.  In the first round, Denver was taken down by the Suns, two games to one.  Phoenix would find a tougher opponent, however, in the Western Conference Semifinals, getting swept four games to zero by the eventual league champions, Los Angeles Lakers.  The Suns were led by head coach John MacLeod and played all home games in Arizona Veterans Memorial Coliseum.
