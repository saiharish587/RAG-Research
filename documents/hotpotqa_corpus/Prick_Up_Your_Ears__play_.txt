Prick Up Your Ears (play): Prick Up Your Ears is a play by Simon Bent, based on the life of playwright Joe Orton.  Produced by Sonia Friedman it opened at the Comedy Theatre in London's West End on 30 September 2009 following previews from 17 September.  It starred Chris New as Joe Orton and Matt Lucas as Orton's lover and murderer, Kenneth Halliwell.
