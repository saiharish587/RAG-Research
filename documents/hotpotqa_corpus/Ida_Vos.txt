Ida Vos: Ida Vos (maiden name Gudema) (Groningen, December 13, 1931 – Amstelveen, April 3, 2006) was a Dutch author.  She wrote books for adults and children.  In most of her books, Vos wrote about her experiences as a Jewish girl during the Second World War.  Her best-known book was "Wie niet weg is wordt gezien" (published in English as "Hide and Seek"), which was awarded with a Dutch literature prize for children's books in 1982.
