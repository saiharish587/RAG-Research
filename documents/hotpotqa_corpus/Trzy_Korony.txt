Trzy Korony: Trzy Korony (English: Three Crowns , Slovak: "Tri koruny" ) is the summit of the Three Crowns Massif, an independent portion of a range called Pieniny Mountains in the south of Poland.  Trzy Korony forms the central part of a compact group of connected mountains known as Pieniny Środkowe, consisting mainly of the limestone and dolomite rock strata.  Trzy Korony is located within the Pieniny National Park in Lesser Poland Voivodeship.
