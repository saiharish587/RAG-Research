The Prisoner of the Caucasus (poem): The Prisoner of the Caucasus (Russian: Кавказский пленник ), also translated as Captive of the Caucasus, is a narrative poem written by Alexander Pushkin in 1820-21 and published in 1822.  Dedicated to his friend General Nikolay Raevsky, it was inspired by the poet's time spent in Pyatigorsk during his southern exile.
