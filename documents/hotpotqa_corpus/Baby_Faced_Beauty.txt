Baby Faced Beauty: Baby Faced Beauty () is a 2011 South Korean television series starring Jang Na-ra, Choi Daniel, Ryu Jin, and Kim Min-seo.  It aired on KBS2 from May 2 to July 5, 2011 on Mondays and Tuesdays at 21:55 for 20 episodes.  The drama is about an aspiring 34-year-old fashion designer who fakes her age to get a job in a fashion company.
