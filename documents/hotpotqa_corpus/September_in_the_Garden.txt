September in the Garden: September in the Garden is Jordyn Jackson's latest album, and re-envisions ten musical standards.  It was recorded by Jacques Delatour and Jack Miele at Better Than Ezra's Fudge Recording Studio in New Orleans in September 2009.  It was mixed by Shawn Grove at Edible Studios, the home studio of Ed Roland, lead singer and songwriter of Collective Soul and mastered by Glenn Schick at his studio in Atlanta.  While this is Jackson's fifth album, it is the first to concentrate on jazz vocals where she truly has created "a sound that is all her own."
