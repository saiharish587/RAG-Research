Mott the Hoople discography: The rock band Mott the Hoople have released seven studio albums, seven live albums, twelve compilation albums and 15 singles.  The discographies of Mott and British Lions are also included because they are a continuation of Mott the Hoople (without founding members Mick Ralphs and Ian Hunter, but featuring members of the original line-up of Mott the Hoople).
