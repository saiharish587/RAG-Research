The Roy Files: The Roy Files is an Irish-based British children's television show, filmed in Dublin, Ireland, which was broadcast by CBBC in the United Kingdom and ABC Me in Australia.  It began airing 7 December 2015.  The show centres on the title character Roy O'Brien, the 11-year-old animated son of a live-action family.  The series is a spin-off from "ROY", an Irish-British television show which ran from 1 July 2009 to 7 April 2015.  It stars Jason Cullen (replacing Scott Graham as Roy), Simon Delaney, Cathy Belton and Martha Byrne.
