Stefen Fangmeier: Stefen Markus Fangmeier (born 9 December 1960 in El Paso, Texas, United States) is an American visual effects supervisor of numerous major feature films, including "Lemony Snicket's A Series of Unfortunate Events", "Saving Private Ryan", "", "Twister", "Perfect Storm" and "".  He also has been a second unit director for two films, "Dreamcatcher" (2003) and "Galaxy Quest" (1999).  After more than 15 years of visual effects work, Fangmeier moved into feature film directing with his debut on "Eragon", which was released in 2006 and was a critical disappointment that upset many fans of the series, but was a modest financial success.
