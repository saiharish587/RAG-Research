Hispar Muztagh: Hispar Muztagh is a sub-range of the Karakoram mountain range.  It is located in the Gojal region of Gilgit-Baltistan, Pakistan, north of Hispar Glacier, south of Shimshal Valley, and east of the Hunza Valley.  It is the second highest sub-range of the Karakoram, the highest being the Baltoro Muztagh.  The highest mountain in the range is Distaghil Sar (7,885m/25,869 ft).
