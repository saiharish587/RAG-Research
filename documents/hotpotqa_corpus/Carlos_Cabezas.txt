Carlos Cabezas: Carlos Eduardo Cabezas Jurado (born November 14, 1980) is a Spanish professional basketball player for Real Betis Energía Plus of the Liga ACB.  He is the nephew of the former Uruguayan professional footballer Hugo Cabezas, who played in Spain in the late 70s.  His father (Hugo's brother), and his grandfather, are also former Uruguayan professional basketball players.  He is a 1.87 m (6 ft 1 ¾ in) tall point guard.
