How Dare You! (album): How Dare You!  is the fourth album by British band 10cc.  Released in 1976, it included UK hit singles "I'm Mandy Fly Me" and "Art for Art's Sake".  It was also the last 10cc album by the original line-up of Eric Stewart, Graham Gouldman, Kevin Godley, and Lol Creme, with the latter two departing to work on their own musical projects, and eventually becoming music video pioneers.  The album was the band's third with cover artwork by the Hipgnosis creative team.
