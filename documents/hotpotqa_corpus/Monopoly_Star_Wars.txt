Monopoly Star Wars: Monopoly Star Wars is a 1997 video game based on the board game of the same name.  It is one of many video game adaptions of the board game Monopoly.  The game was developed by Artech Digital Entertainment and published by Hasbro Interactive.  It was released exclusively for Microsoft Windows.  The game employs the same basic ruleset of traditional Monopoly gameplay, but adds a "Star Wars" theme which includes famous characters and locales in place of the original game pieces and properties.
