List of Red Hot Chili Peppers band members: Red Hot Chili Peppers is an American funk rock band formed in Los Angeles, California, in 1983.  The band was formed by vocalist Anthony Kiedis, guitarist Hillel Slovak, bassist Flea, and drummer Jack Irons after they met while attending Fairfax High School.  Since its inception, Red Hot Chili Peppers has maintained a four-member lineup, with fourteen official members overall.
