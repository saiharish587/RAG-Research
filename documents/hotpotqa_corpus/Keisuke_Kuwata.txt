Keisuke Kuwata: Keisuke Kuwata (桑田 佳祐 , Kuwata Keisuke , born February 26, 1956) is a Japanese multi-instrumentalist, singer-songwriter, and frontman for the Southern All Stars, as well of his own solo band, the Kuwata band.  He has also done significant amount of scoring music for films.  He went to Aoyama Gakuin University.
