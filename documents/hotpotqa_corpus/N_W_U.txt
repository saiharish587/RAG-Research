N.W.U: N.W.U is a 2016 Japanese-language album by South Korean rock band F.T. Island and is the band's seventh Japanese studio album.  N.W.U is an acronym of 'Naite' (泣いて, to cry), 'Waratte' (笑って, to laugh), 'Utatte' (歌って, to sing).  The album was released on 6 April 2016 and became top selling album that week in Japan's Tower Records.
