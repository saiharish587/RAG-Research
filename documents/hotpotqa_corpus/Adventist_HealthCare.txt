Adventist HealthCare: Adventist HealthCare is a not-for-profit health services organization based in Gaithersburg, Maryland that employs more than 6,200 people and provides healthcare for more than 400,000 individuals in the community each year.  The primary service area for Adventist HealthCare is the Washington, D.C. metropolitan area.  Despite similar names, it is not a part of the California-based Adventist Health, or the Florida-based Adventist Health System.
