George Melville Baker: George Melville Baker (1832–1890) was a playwright and publisher in Boston, Massachusetts, in the 19th century.  He worked for Lee & Shepard publishers, then opened his own imprint.  "George M. Baker & Co." issued works by authors such as Henry M. Baker, F.E. Chase, and Herbert Pelham Curtis.  Baker's company ceased in 1885, succeeded by his brother's "Walter H. Baker & Co." George Baker also performed with comedian Henry C. Barnabee, appearing in "lyceum entertainments" in New England.  He belonged to the Mercantile Library Association.  He married Emily Bowles in 1858; children included novelist Emilie Loring, playwright Rachel Baker Gale, and screenwriter Robert Melville Baker.
