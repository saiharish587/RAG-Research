David B. Ingram: David Bronson Ingram is an American heir, businessman and philanthropist.  He serves as the Chairman and President of Ingram Entertainment, the largest distributor of DVDs and video games in the United States.  He is also the Founder and Chairman of DBI Beverage, a distributor of California beers and non-alcoholic drinks in Chico, Napa, Sacramento, San Francisco, San Joaquin County, San Jose, Truckee and Ukiah.
