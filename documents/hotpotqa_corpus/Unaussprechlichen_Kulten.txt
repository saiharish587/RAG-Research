Unaussprechlichen Kulten: Unaussprechlichen Kulten (also known as Nameless Cults or the Black Book) is a fictional book of arcane literature in the Cthulhu Mythos.  The book first appeared in Robert E. Howard's short stories "The Children of the Night" (1931) and "The Black Stone" (1931) as "Nameless Cults".  Like the "Necronomicon", it was later mentioned in several stories by H. P. Lovecraft.
