List of Australian Nobel laureates: Since 1915 there have been sixteen Australian winners of the Nobel Prize.  The majority of these prizes (eight) have been awarded in the field of Physiology or Medicine, and include the second youngest ever laureate (William Lawrence Bragg, who was awarded the prize at 25 years of age).  Bragg and his father (William Henry Bragg) are also the only father-son duo to have won a Nobel Prize in the same year.  Most Australians awarded Nobel prizes before the end of the awarding of British/Imperial honours (in 1992) also received (or were offered) knighthoods.
