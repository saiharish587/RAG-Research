Ultimate Cake Off: Ultimate Cake Off is an American television series that currently airs on TLC.  The show is based on professional cake artists that go "head-to-head" in constructing cakes over five feet tall with the assistance of a team of chefs, designers etc. for a money prize.  Season one of the series, hosted by Michael Schulson, premiered on August 3, 2009.  Season two, hosted by George Duran, premiered on February 1, 2010.  Professional bakers Margaret Braun and Leigh Grode join the hosts as judges.
