Yuzuru Hanyu: Yuzuru Hanyu (羽生結弦 , Hanyū Yuzuru , born 7 December 1994) is a Japanese figure skater who competes in the men's singles discipline.  He is the 2014 Olympic champion, a two-time World champion (2014, 2017), a four-time Grand Prix Final champion (2013–2016), a three-time Four Continents silver medalist (2011, 2013, 2017), the 2010 World Junior champion, the 2009–10 Junior Grand Prix Final champion, and a four-time Japanese national champion (2012–2015).  He has also medaled at three other World Championships, taking bronze in 2012, and silver in 2015 and 2016.
