The Lodger (2009 film): The Lodger is a 2009 mystery/thriller film directed by David Ondaatje and starring Alfred Molina, Hope Davis and Simon Baker.  It is based on the novel "The Lodger" by Marie Belloc Lowndes, filmed previously by Alfred Hitchcock in 1927, by Maurice Elvey in 1932, by John Brahm in 1944, and as "Man in the Attic" (1953) directed by Hugo Fregonese.
