Shuangjing Subdistrict, Beijing: Shuangjing Subdistrict () is a subdistrict of Chaoyang District, Beijing, located in the vicinity of Beijing East Railway Station and the Beijing CBD Its boundaries are the Tonghui River to the North, the East 4th Ring Road to the East, Jinsong High Street to the South, and the boundary between Chaoyang District and Dongcheng District to the West.  , it had 12 residential communities (社区 ) under its administration.
