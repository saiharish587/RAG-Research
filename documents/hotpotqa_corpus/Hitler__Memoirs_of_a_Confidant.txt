Hitler: Memoirs of a Confidant: Hitler: Memoirs of a Confidant are the published memoirs written by Otto Wagener about Adolf Hitler and the Nazi Party's early history.  A German major general by the end for World War II and, for a period, Wagener was Adolf Hitler's party economist, chief of staff of the SA, and confidant, whose career was derailed by rival Hermann Göring.  Wagener wrote his memoirs in 1946 while being held by the British, filling “thirty-six British military exercise notebooks.”  His work was not published until seven years after his death, in 1978 in German.  The English edition was published in 1985 by Yale University Press.  His memoirs are used, to some degree, by Third Reich historians.
