Out of Control (2002 film): Out of Control is a 2002 British television film written and directed for BBC by Dominic Savage.  It stars Tamzin Outhwaite as Shelley Richards, an impoverished single mother whose son, Dean (Danny Young), is involved in a string of crimes that lead to his incarceration in a young offenders institute (YOI).  David Morrissey plays prison warden Mike, who tries to keep Dean out of trouble but has difficultly watching him all the time.  After continuous bullying at the YOI, Dean is no longer able to cope and makes a fatal decision.  It is the third in a loose trilogy of films by Savage about social deprivation, following "Nice Girl" (2000) and "When I Was Twelve" (2001).
