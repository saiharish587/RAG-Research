Multilateral trading facility: A multilateral trading facility (MTF) is a European regulatory term for a self-regulated financial trading venue.  These are alternatives to the traditional stock exchanges where a market is made in securities, typically using electronic systems.  The operation of a MTF is considered an investment service.  The concept was introduced within the Markets in Financial Instruments Directive (MiFID), a European Directive designed to harmonise retail investors protection and allow investment firms to provide services throughout the EU.  Article 4 (15) of MiFID describes MTF as multilateral system, operated by an investment firm or a market operator, which brings together multiple third-party buying and selling interests in financial instruments – in the system and in accordance with non-discretionary rules – in a way that results in a contract.  The term 'non-discretionary rules' means that the investment firm operating an MTF has no discretion as to how interests may interact.  Interests are brought together by forming a contract and the execution takes place under the system's rules or by means of the system's protocols or internal operating procedures.
