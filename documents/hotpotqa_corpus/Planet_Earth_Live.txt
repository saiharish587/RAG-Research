Planet Earth Live: Planet Earth Live is a 2010 BBC nature documentary film that celebrated its premier in the U.S. with a tour featuring narration and live orchestral accompaniment featuring the score by composer George Fenton, who serves as conductor.  Each show runs 90 minutes plus an intermission, featuring the highlights from the television series "Planet Earth" in a full high definition screening with live orchestra and narration.  The tour features some of the leading classical orchestras providing accompaniment in prominent music venues in several large cities.  The tour which was originally scheduled to visit Dallas, Atlanta, Chicago and Los Angeles, has added performances in Philadelphia and the Baltimore-Washington, D.C. area.  In 2011, the documentary played in the United Kingdom.
