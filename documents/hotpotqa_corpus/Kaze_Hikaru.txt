Kaze Hikaru: Kaze Hikaru (Japanese: 風光る , lit.  "Shining Wind") is a Japanese manga series written and illustrated by Taeko Watanabe.  Set in the "bakumatsu" period, the series follows Tominaga Sei, a young girl who poses as a boy named Kamiya Seizaburo so she can join the Mibu-Roshi (Special Police; later known as the Shinsengumi).  She befriends her sensei, Okita Sōji, who discovers her secret.
