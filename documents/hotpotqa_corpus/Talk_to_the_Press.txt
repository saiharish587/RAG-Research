Talk to the Press: Talk to the Press is a press and publicity agency that was founded in 2007 by media expert and former national newspaper journalist Natasha Courtenay-Smith.  The company provides an outlet for individuals who wish to sell their stories and specialises in feature stories of a personal nature that range from dealing with the credit crunch to teen prostitution.  Its articles predominantly appear in tabloid newspapers including "The Daily Mail", "The Sun" and "The Daily Mirror", and in women's magazines such as "Closer", "Reveal", "Bella" and "Take a Break".  In March 2010 Talk to the Press featured in the "Cutting Edge" documentary "My Daughter Grew Another Head" about women's magazines.
