Ain't Worried About Nothin': "Ain't Worried About Nothin'" is a song by American rapper French Montana for his debut studio album "Excuse My French" (2013).  It was released on April 15, 2013 as the third single from the album.  The song was written and produced by Rico Love and Earl & E, with additional songwriting provided by Montana.  "Ain't Worried About Nothin'" peaked at number 63 on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot 100.  An accompanying music video was released on May 7, 2013.
