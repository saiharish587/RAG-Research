2014–15 Seton Hall Pirates men's basketball team: The 2014–15 Seton Hall Pirates men's basketball team represented Seton Hall University during the 2014–15 NCAA Division I men's basketball season.  The Pirates, led by fifth year head coach Kevin Willard, played its home games in Newark, New Jersey at the Prudential Center and were members of the Big East Conference.  They finished the season 16–15, 6–12 in Big East play to finish in a tie for seventh place.  They lost in the first round of the Big East Tournament to Marquette.
