Cardell Butler: Cardell Butler (born April 28, 1981 in San Francisco, California), also known as "Ballaholic," is an American streetball player.  He is known for his appearances on the AND1 Mixtape Tour shows, which aired on ESPN.  In conventional basketball, Butler has also played for the San Diego Wildcats of the American Basketball Association.  He is 6-foot 4-inches tall, wears size 19 shoes, and plays the guard position.  Butler is known for his aggressive scoring ability.  When he was 15, Butler received his nickname, "Ballaholic", because he earned a reputation on the basketball court of always shooting the ball whenever he gained possession of it.  Butler now plays for the San Francisco Rumble of the American Basketball Association.
