Rock 'n' Roll Circus: Rock 'n' Roll Circus is the eleventh studio album by Japanese recording artist Ayumi Hamasaki.  It was released on April 14, 2010, by Avex Trax.  It was also released just a little over a year after her 2009 album, "Next Level".  "Rock 'n' Roll Circus" marks Hamasaki's eleventh consecutive album to be fully produced by Japanese producer and manager Max Matsuura, while she contributes to the album as the lead vocalist, background vocalist, and songwriter to all songs.  Recorded in Japanese with minor phrases in English, "Rock 'n' Roll Circus" is a rock album with numerous musical elements such as electropop, J-pop, rock, pop ballad, and dance music.
