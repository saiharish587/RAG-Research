Wake Forest School of Medicine: Wake Forest School of Medicine is the medical school of Wake Forest University, located in Winston-Salem, North Carolina.  It is affiliated with Wake Forest Baptist Medical Center, the academic medical center whose clinical arm is Wake Forest Baptist Health.  "U.S. News & World Report" in 2015 ranked Wake Forest School of Medicine as 49th best for research in the nation and 71st best for primary care.  The School of Medicine also ranks in the top third of U.S. medical schools in funding from the National Institutes of Health (NIH).
