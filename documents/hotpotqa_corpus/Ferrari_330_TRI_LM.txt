Ferrari 330 TRI/LM: The Ferrari 330 TRI/LM Spyder (chassis number 0808) is a 1962 racing sports car purpose-built by Ferrari to achieve victory at the 24 Hours of Le Mans.  It was the last Ferrari racing sports car with a front-mounted engine and the last of a series of Ferrari race cars known as the Testa Rossas.  The "I" in its designation indicates that the car has an independent rear suspension ("indipendente" in Italian).
