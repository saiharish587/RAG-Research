St. Nicholas Magazine: St. Nicholas Magazine was a popular monthly American children's magazine, founded by Scribner's in 1873.  The first editor was Mary Mapes Dodge, who continued her association with the magazine until her death in 1905.  Dodge published work by the country's best writers, including Louisa May Alcott, Frances Hodgson Burnett, Mark Twain, Laura E. Richards and Joel Chandler Harris.  Many famous writers were first published in "St. Nicholas League", a department that offered awards and cash prizes to the best work submitted by its juvenile readers.  Edna St. Vincent Millay, F. Scott Fitzgerald, E. B. White, and Stephen Vincent Benet were all St. Nicholas League winners.
