Outland (video game): Outland is a platform game developed by Housemarque and published by Ubisoft.  The game combines two-dimensional platforming with a polarity system similar to Treasure's "Ikaruga" and "Silhouette Mirage".  "Outland" was released in April 27, 2011 for Xbox Live Arcade.  The PlayStation 3 version was delayed because of the PlayStation Network outage then later released on June 14, 2011.  A Windows version was released on September 29, 2014.  The Linux version was released on February 25, 2015.
