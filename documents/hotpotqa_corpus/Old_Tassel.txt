Old Tassel: Old Tassel (or sometimes Corntassel) (Cherokee language: "Utsi'dsata"), ( died 1788), was "First Beloved Man" (the equivalent of a regional Cherokee chief) of the Overhill Cherokee after 1783.  He continuously tried to keep the Cherokee people of the Overhill region out of the Cherokee–American wars being fought at the time between the American frontiersmen and the Chickamauga warriors under Dragging Canoe.  He was murdered under a flag of truce while defending his tribe from white settlers.
