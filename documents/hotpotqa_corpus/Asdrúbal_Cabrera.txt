Asdrúbal Cabrera: Asdrúbal José Cabrera (] ; born November 13, 1985) is a Venezuelan professional baseball shortstop for the New York Mets of Major League Baseball (MLB).  He previously played for the Cleveland Indians, Washington Nationals, and Tampa Bay Rays.  Cabrera, a switch hitter, is a two-time All-Star.  Cabrera was primarily a second baseman early in his career before transitioning to shortstop and then in 2017, he transitioned to also play third base for the Mets.
