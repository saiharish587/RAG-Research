Nick Gillespie: Nicholas John Gillespie ( ; born August 7, 1963) is an American libertarian journalist who was former editor-in-chief of "Reason" magazine from 2000 to 2008.  He is currently a contributing editor of Reason.com and the editor-in-chief at Reason.tv.  He has written articles or been a commentator for a variety of media outlets.  Gillespie has edited one anthology, "Choice: The Best of Reason", and co-authored one book, "The Declaration of Independents".
