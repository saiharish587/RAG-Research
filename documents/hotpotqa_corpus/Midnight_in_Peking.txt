Midnight in Peking: Midnight in Peking is the true story of the murder of a young British woman in January 1937 Peking written by Paul French.  It was first published by Penguin Australia in association with Penguin China in 2011 and has since been published by Penguin Books in the UK and by Penguin Group USA.  It has appeared on international best seller lists including the "New York Times" Best Seller List and the "South China Morning Post" Best Seller List.  Additionally the book was adapted for radio by "BBC Radio 4".
