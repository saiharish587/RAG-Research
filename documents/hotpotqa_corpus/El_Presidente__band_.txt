El Presidente (band): El Presidente (also written El Pres!dente) was a pop rock band from Glasgow, Scotland.  Formed in 2002 by Gun member Dante Gizzi, the band gained major exposure with slots at T in the Park 2005, V Festival 2005 and again at T in the Park in 2006.  The band have also supported Oasis, Duran Duran, Simple Minds and Kasabian on major tours of the UK.
