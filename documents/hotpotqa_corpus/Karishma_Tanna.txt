Karishma Tanna: Karishma Tanna (born 21 December 1983) is an Indian film actress, model and anchor who predominantly works in Hindi films and shows.  She is well known for her role in "Kyunki Saas Bhi Kabhi Bahu Thi", "Naagarjuna – Ek Yodha".  She was a contestant in the popular controversial reality show "Bigg Boss" in 2014.  She also appeared in more reality shows like "Zara Nachke Dikha" (2008), "Nach Baliye" (2015) and "Jhalak Dikhhla Jaa" (2016).
