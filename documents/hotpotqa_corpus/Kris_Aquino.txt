Kris Aquino: Kristina Bernadette "Kris" Cojuangco Aquino (born February 14, 1971) is a Filipino talk show host, actress, producer, entrepreneur, product endorser, and writer.  Dubbed the "Queen of All Media," she has hosted talk shows and game shows, and has also starred in movies and selected television series.  She is the youngest daughter of former Philippine senator Benigno S. Aquino, Jr., and Corazon Cojuangco-Aquino, who served as the 11th President of the Philippines.  Her brother is Benigno S. Aquino, III, who served as the 15th President of the Philippines.
