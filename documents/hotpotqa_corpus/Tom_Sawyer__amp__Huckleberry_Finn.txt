Tom Sawyer &amp; Huckleberry Finn: Tom Sawyer & Huckleberry Finn is a 2014 American comedy-drama/adventure film starring Joel Courtney as Tom Sawyer, Jake T. Austin as Huckleberry Finn, Katherine McNamara as Becky Thatcher, Noah Munck as Ben Rogers, and with Val Kilmer as Mark Twain.  It is based on Mark Twain's novels "The Adventures of Tom Sawyer" and "Adventures of Huckleberry Finn".  Directed by Jo Kastner, the film is released by VMI Worldwide.
