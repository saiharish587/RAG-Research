Baron Brownlow: Baron Brownlow, of Belton in the County of Lincoln, is a title in the Peerage of Great Britain.  It was created in 1776 for Sir Brownlow Cust, 4th Baronet.  The Cust family descends from Richard Cust who represented Lincolnshire and Stamford in Parliament.  In 1677 he was created a baronet, of Stamford in the County of Lincoln.  He was succeeded by his grandson, the second Baronet.  He married Anne Brownlow, daughter of Sir William Brownlow, 4th Baronet, of Humby and sister and sole heiress of John Brownlow, 1st Viscount Tyrconnel (and 5th Baronet, of Humby).
