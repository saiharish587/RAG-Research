The Real Housewives of Orange County (season 4): The fourth season of "The Real Housewives of Orange County", an American reality television series, was broadcast on Bravo.  It aired from November 25, 2008 until June 11, 2009, and was primarily filmed in Orange County, California.  Its executive producers are Adam Karpel, Alex Baskin, Douglas Ross, Gregory Stewart, Scott Dunlop, Stephanie Boyriven and Andy Cohen.
