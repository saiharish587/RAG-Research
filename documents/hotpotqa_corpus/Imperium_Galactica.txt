Imperium Galactica: Imperium Galactica is a 4X video game, developed by Digital Reality.  The same company would later make its sequel, "Imperium Galactica II", in 1999.  "Imperium Galactica" was published and distributed by GT Interactive in 1997.  The soundtrack is the work of Tamás Kreiner.
