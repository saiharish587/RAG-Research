Nawab Bai: Rahmat-un-Nissa (Persian: رحمت النساء بیگم‎ ‎ ) ( 1623 – 1691) better known by her title Nawab Bai, was a secondary wife of the Mughal emperor Aurangzeb.  Nawab Bai was born a Rajput princess and was the daughter of Rajah Raju of Rajauri.  She married Aurangzeb in 1638, and bore him three children including, Aurangzeb's eldest son Prince Muhammad Sultan, his second son Prince Muhammad Muazzam, who succeeded his father as Bahadur Shah I.
