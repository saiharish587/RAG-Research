Alex Mendelssohn: Alex Mendelssohn (born 30 May 1935) is an Australian artist and opal miner of Hungarian descent.  popularly known as Alex or his birth name "Sándor Mendelssohn" (variant of the name Alexander in Hungary).  He is the great-great-grandson of Felix Mendelssohn, the Romantic German composer who gave the world the famous "Wedding March" overture.
