Jeff Grimes: Jeff Grimes (born September 23, 1968 in Garland, Texas) is an American college football assistant coach who is currently the offensive line coach and run game coordinator at Louisiana State University (LSU).  Prior to joining the LSU staff, he served as offensive line coach on the Virginia Tech staff in 2013, as well as a similar position at Auburn University from 2009 through the 2012 season.  Before coaching at Auburn, he was the assistant head coach, running game coordinator and offensive line coach for the Colorado Buffaloes.  Grimes also coached offensive line at BYU, Arizona State and Boise State.
