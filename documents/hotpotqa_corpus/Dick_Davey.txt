Dick Davey: Richard James "Dick" Davey (born April 5, 1942) is a retired American college basketball coach.  Davey is best known for being head men's basketball coach at Santa Clara University from 1992 to 2007.  Most recently, Davey was the associate head men's basketball coach at Stanford University under head coach Johnny Dawkins from 2008 to 2012 and helped Stanford win the 2012 National Invitation Tournament.
