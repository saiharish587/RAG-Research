Lorraine Broderick: Lorraine Broderick (born 1948) is an American television soap opera writer who got her start on "All My Children" as a protégée of the show's creator, Agnes Nixon.  She went on to serve four different stints as its Head Writer, ultimately earning her four Daytime Emmy awards in that capacity.  Broderick's work on the show has often been met with critical acclaim, citing her as its finest head writer outside of Nixon.  She was the last head writer of "All My Children's" 40-year broadcast run on ABC, penning the show through its network finale on September 23, 2011.
