Sally Kohn: Sally Rebecca Kohn (born March 27, 1977) is an American liberal political commentator, community organizer, and founder and chief executive officer of the Movement Vision Lab, a grassroots think tank that focuses on far left, liberal, progressive, and radical ideas and positions.  Kohn was a contributor for the Fox News Channel, and now regularly appears as a political commentator on CNN.  Her writing is published in publications like "The Washington Post" and "USA Today".
