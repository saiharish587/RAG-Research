Ella Mary Edghill: Ella Mary Edghill (born 13 November 1881 at Aldershot; date of death unknown) was a British translator known primarily for her translation of "Categories" which appeared in Volume 1 (1928) of the "The Works of Aristotle" series, edited by W. D. Ross and J. A. Smith.  She was the daughter of Rev. John Cox Edghill, DD, Chaplain General to British Armed Forces, and Mary Nesfield.
