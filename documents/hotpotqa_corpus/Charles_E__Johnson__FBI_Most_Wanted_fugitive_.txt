Charles E. Johnson (FBI Most Wanted fugitive): Charles E. Johnson (born February 22, 1907) was a New York burglar who was listed on the "FBI's Ten Most Wanted" during 1953.  He was a professional boxer.  While still a teenager, Johnson was first arrested for burglary in 1921.  He continued committing burglary and armed robbery throughout the 1920s until his eventual arrest in 1934 after a robbery in New York.  Sentenced to serve four to eight years imprisonment, he was transferred to Dannemora Prison after he shot a police officer during a failed jailbreak from Sing Sing Prison.  Although released briefly for six months, he remained imprisoned from 1935 until 1952.
