Mowag T1 4x4: The 1953 MOWAG T 1 4x4 was modular.  It was built: with an open loading ramp, with radio as a closed van, etc.  In order to keep manufacturing, maintenance and repair simple and inexpensive, many parts of the U.S. Dodge car maker are used, especially from the Dodge WC.  The Mowag T1 exist with or without a winch.  Most MOWAG T1 4x4 were built as right-hand drive vehicles.  Over 1600 cars were delivered in 7 different versions to the Swiss Army.  MOWAG 4x4 T 1 were also supplied as emergency vehicles to fire and police departments.
