Æthelbert II of Kent: Æthelbert II (Old English: "Æðelberht" ) (725–762) was king of Kent.  Upon the death of his father Wihtred, the kingdom was ruled by his three sons, Æthelbert II, Eadberht I and Alric.  Æthelbert seems to have outlived both of his brothers and later reigned jointly with his nephew Eardwulf.  He died in 762, according to the "Anglo-Saxon Chronicle" (recorded under 760 due to chronological dislocation).  He seems to have left a son, Eadberht II.
