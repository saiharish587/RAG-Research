1993 San Jose State Spartans football team: The 1993 San Jose State Spartans football team represented San Jose State University during the 1993 NCAA Division I-A football season as a member of the Big West Conference.  The team was led by head coach John Ralston, in his first year as head coach at San Jose State.  They played their home games at Spartan Stadium in San Jose, California.  The Spartans finished the 1993 season with a record of two wins and nine losses (2–9, 2–4 Big West).
