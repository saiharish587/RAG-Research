Gulf Coast State College Commodores: Gulf Coast State College athletic teams are nicknamed the Commodores and participate in men's basketball, men's baseball, women's basketball, women's softball, and women's volleyball.  The school's athletic teams compete in the Panhandle Conference of the Florida State College Activities Association, a body of the National Junior College Athletic Association Region 8.  The current athletic director is Gregg Wolfe who has been serving in this capacity since 1992.
