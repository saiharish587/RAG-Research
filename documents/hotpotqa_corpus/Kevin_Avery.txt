Kevin Avery: Kevin Avery (born June 24, 1957) is an American biographer, short story writer, and editor.  Born and raised in Salt Lake City, Utah, he now lives in Brooklyn, New York.  His first two books, "Everything Is an Afterthought: The Life and Writings of Paul Nelson" and "Conversations with Clint: Paul Nelson's Lost Interviews with Clint Eastwood, 1979 - 1983", were published within weeks of each other by two different publishers, Fantagraphics Books and Continuum Books, respectively, in 2011.
