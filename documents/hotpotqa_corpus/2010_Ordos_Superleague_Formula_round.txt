2010 Ordos Superleague Formula round: The 2010 Ordos Superleague Formula round was a Superleague Formula round held on October 3, 2010, at the newly built Ordos International Circuit, Ordos City, China.  It was Superleague Formula's first visit to China, and is followed the week after by a round through the streets of Beijing.  It was the tenth round of the 2010 Superleague Formula season.
