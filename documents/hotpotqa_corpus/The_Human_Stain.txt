The Human Stain: The Human Stain (2000) is a novel by Philip Roth set in late 1990s rural New England.  Its first person narrator is 65-year-old author Nathan Zuckerman, who appeared in several earlier Roth novels, and who also figures in both "American Pastoral" (1997) and "I Married a Communist" (1998), two books that form a loose trilogy with "The Human Stain".  Zuckerman acts largely as an observer as the complex story of the protagonist, Coleman Silk, a retired professor of classics, is slowly revealed.
