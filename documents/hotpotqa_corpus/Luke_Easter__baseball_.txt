Luke Easter (baseball): Luscious "Luke" Easter (August 4, 1915 – March 29, 1979) was a professional baseball player in Major League Baseball and the Negro leagues.  He batted left-handed, threw right-handed, was 6 ft , and weighed 240 lb.  The birth year listed here is drawn from census data.  Easter himself listed multiple birth years ranging from 1911 to 1921 on different occasions, so some ambiguity as to the correct year exists.
