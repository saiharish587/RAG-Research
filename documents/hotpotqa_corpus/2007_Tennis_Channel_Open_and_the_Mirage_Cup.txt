2007 Tennis Channel Open and the Mirage Cup: The 2007 Tennis Channel Open was a tennis event on the 2007 ATP Tour.  Lleyton Hewitt, who was the 2006 runner-up, was the singles champion, while Bob and Mike Bryan were the doubles champions.  The event was held in Las Vegas, Nevada at the Darling Tennis Center.  It was the twentieth Tennis Channel Open, but just the second to be held in Las Vegas.  The ITF women's competition was won by Caroline Wozniacki in singles and by Victoria Azarenka and Tatiana Poutchek for doubles.
