Shin Hyun-joon (actor): Shin Hyun-joon (Hangul: 신현준 ; born October 28, 1968) is a South Korean actor.  He is best known for his roles in "Barefoot Ki-bong", "Stairway to Heaven" and the "Marrying the Mafia" sequels, and as the photographer in the popular music video "Because I'm A Girl" by KISS.  In the Korean press he is nicknamed as 아랍왕자 ("Prince of Arab") due to his foreign look and long eyelashes.
