Sher Ali Khan Pataudi: Major General Nawabzada Sher Ali Khan of Pataudi (Urdu: ‎ ) HJ (13 May 1913 – 29 May 2002) was the second son of Nawab Ibrahim Ali Khan of Pataudi, in Pataudi.  He was educated at Aitchison College, Lahore, the Prince of Wales Royal Indian Military College (RIMC), Dehradun and the Royal Military College, Sandhurst.
