A Word in Your Ear: A Word in Your Ear is a game show that originally aired BBC1 from 19 April 1993 to 14 October 1994 then on The Family Channel from 1995.  It was hosted by Gordon Burns.  The host presided over male and female pairs of celebrities as they participate in a few rounds of communication games.  Celebrities appearing included Lynsey De Paul, Nick Owen, Philippa Kennedy and Bob Holness.
