Georg Muschner: Georg Muschner (12 June 1885 – 17 May 1971) was a German cinematographer.  He worked on over sixty productions during his career in the Weimar Republic, Austria and Nazi Germany.  Muschner originally worked as a portrait photographer, before entering the film industry during the silent era.  He worked on several Harry Piel films, including "His Greatest Bluff".  During the 1930s he often worked with the director Johann Alexander Hübler-Kahla.
