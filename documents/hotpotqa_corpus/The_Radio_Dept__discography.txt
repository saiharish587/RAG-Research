The Radio Dept. discography: The discography of Swedish indie pop group, The Radio Dept. consists of three studio albums, four extended plays, ten singles and one compilation album.  The Radio Dept. was formed in 1995 by Johan Duncanson, although they did not start recording until 2001.  They were signed to Labrador Records the same year and have stayed with the label since.  The band's lineup has changed over the years until it settled on the trio of Johan Duncanson and Martin Carlberg, with Daniel Tjäder on keyboards.
