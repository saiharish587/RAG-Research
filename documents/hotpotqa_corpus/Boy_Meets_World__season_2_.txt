Boy Meets World (season 2): The second season of the television comedy series "Boy Meets World" aired between September 23, 1994 and May 19, 1995, on ABC in the United States.  The season was produced by Michael Jacobs Productions and Touchstone Television with series creator Michael Jacobs as executive producer.  It was broadcast as part of the ABC comedy block TGIF on Friday evening, airing at 8:30 between "Family Matters" and "Step by Step".  This is the last season to have Lily Nicksay portray Morgan Matthews.  (She would be replaced by Lindsay Ridgeway in season three.)
