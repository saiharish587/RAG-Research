Kangalal Kaidhu Sei: Kangalal Kaidhu Sei (English: Arrest by eyes ) is a 2004 Tamil language film, written and directed by Bharathiraja.  It stars Vaseegaran in the protagonist's role and marks actress Priyamani's debut in the Tamil film industry.  Music was scored by A. R. Rahman, which was the major highlight of this film.  The film received mixed reviews and became a box office failure.
