Zwart-Wit '28: RCVV Zwart-Wit '28 (full name: "Rotterdamse Christelijke Voetbalvereniging Zwart-Wit 1928") was a Dutch Saturday football club from Rotterdam, dubbed the "Feyenoord of Saturday Football".  Its women's team won the 2000 National Women's Cup then played in the professional Eredivisie.  Its men's team won the National Amateur Championship in 1971, then played in the Hoofdklasse.
