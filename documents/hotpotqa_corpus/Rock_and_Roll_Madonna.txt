Rock and Roll Madonna: "Rock and Roll Madonna" is a rock and roll song by Elton John with lyrics written by Bernie Taupin.  The song was released as a single in Britain in 1970, where it never charted.  It appeared on several bootlegs and rarities compilations before it appeared on the 1995 remaster of his "self-titled album".  The song structure is, as the title says, a rock and roll song.  Live-effects has been added, with an audience cheering throughout most of the song, anticipating similar usage in Bennie and the Jets.
