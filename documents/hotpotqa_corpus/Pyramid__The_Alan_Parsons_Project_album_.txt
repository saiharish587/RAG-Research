Pyramid (The Alan Parsons Project album): Pyramid (stylized as Pyr△mid) is the third album by progressive rock band The Alan Parsons Project, released in 1978.  It is a concept album centred on the pyramids of Giza.  At the time the album was conceived, interest in pyramid power and Tutankhamun was widespread in the US and the UK.  "Pyramid" was nominated for the 1978 Grammy Award for Best Engineered Album, Non-Classical.  Liner notes read "From the rise and fall of an ancient dynasty, to the quest for a key to unlock the secrets of the universe, this album seeks to amplify the haunting echoes of the past and explore the unsolved mysteries of the present.  Pyramid...the last remaining wonder of the ancient world."
