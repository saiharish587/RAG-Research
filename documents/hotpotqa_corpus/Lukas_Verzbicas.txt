Lukas Verzbicas: Lukas Verzbicas (born "Lukas Veržbickas" January 6, 1993 in Kaunas, Lithuania) is a Lithuanian-American triathlete.  A prominent high school track and cross country runner, Verzbicas holds the U.S. high school national record in the 2 mile with a time of 8:29.46.  He was also the fifth of only nine American high school boys to run a sub four-minute mile, running 3:59.71 at the end of his senior year of high school.
