I Still Believe (Frank Turner song): "I Still Believe" is the ninth single by UK-based songwriter Frank Turner, and the first of his fourth EP "Rock & Roll".  It was released on 28 October 2010.  "I Still Believe" had a limited release of 500 7" vinyl's for the 2012 Record Store Day on 21 April.  The B-Side to the vinyl is Frank's cover of Queen's "Somebody to Love".  Both of which were released digitally a week later on 30 April.
