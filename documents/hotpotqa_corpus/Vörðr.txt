Vörðr: In Norse mythology, a vǫrðr (pl.  varðir or verðir — "warden," "watcher" or "caretaker") is a warden spirit, believed to follow from birth to death the soul ("hugr") of every person.  In Old Swedish, the corresponding word is "varþer"; in modern Swedish "vård", and the belief in them remained strong in Scandinavian folklore up until the last centuries.  The English word '"wraith" is derived from "vǫrðr", while "" and "warden" are cognates.
