Old Coyote Town: "Old Coyote Town" is a song recorded by American country music artist Don Williams.  It was released in January 1989 as the fourth single from the album "Traces".  The song reached number 5 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks chart.  The song previously appeared on co-writer Larry Boone's 1988 album "Swingin' Doors, Sawdust Floors", and was the B-side to that album's single "Wine Me Up".  Boone wrote the song with Paul Nelson and Gene Nelson.
