Alan Whicker: Alan Donald Whicker {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (2 August 1921 – 12 July 2013) was a British journalist and television presenter and broadcaster.  His career spanned almost 60 years, during which time he presented the documentary television programme "Whicker's World" for over 30 years.  He was made a Commander of the Order of the British Empire (CBE) in 2005 for services to broadcasting.
