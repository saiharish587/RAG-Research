Charles Macklin: Charles Macklin (26 September 1690 – 11 July 1797), [Gaelic: Cathal MacLochlainn] (Charles McLaughlin in English), was an Irish actor and dramatist who performed extensively at the Theatre Royal, Drury Lane.  Macklin revolutionised theatre in the 18th century by introducing a "natural style" of acting.  He is also famous for killing a man in a fight over a wig at the Theatre Royal, Drury Lane.
