Giada in Paradise: Giada in Paradise is a show that debuted on the Food Network on June 16, 2007.  The series features host Giada De Laurentiis exploring food and culture at her favorite vacation destinations.  In 2012, the series switched to the Cooking Channel, where, as of May 25, 2013, five more episodes have aired.  In 2014, the series won a Daytime Creative Arts Emmy Award for Outstanding Special Class Directing (Anne Fox), Single Camera Photography, and Single Camera Editing.
