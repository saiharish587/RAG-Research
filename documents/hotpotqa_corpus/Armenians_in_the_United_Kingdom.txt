Armenians in the United Kingdom: The Armenian community of the United Kingdom consists mainly of British citizens who are fully or partially of Armenian descent.  There has been sporadic emigration from Armenia to the UK since the 18th century, with the biggest influx coming after the Second World War.  The majority are based in the major cities of London and Manchester.  The 2001 UK Census recorded 589 Armenian-born people living in the UK, and in 2013, the Office for National Statistics estimated that there were 1,235 people born in Armenia resident in the UK, with the number of Armenian nationals being 1,720, although it has been estimated by the Armenian Diaspora Conference that there are up to 18,000 ethnic Armenians including those who are British-born, and of part Armenian descent, living in the UK.
