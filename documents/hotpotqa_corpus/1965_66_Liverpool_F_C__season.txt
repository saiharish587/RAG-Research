1965–66 Liverpool F.C. season: Liverpool F.C. won its seventh league title, tying Arsenal's record.  In the competitive Football League First Division, Liverpool breezed to the championship victory with a six-point cushion to Leeds and Burnley.  Roger Hunt scored 30 league goals, which earned him a place in the England squad for the World Cup, where he became the first player to win the World Cup representing Liverpool.  It was not until 44 years later that Fernando Torres played an active part in the Spanish team winning the World Cup as a Liverpool player.
