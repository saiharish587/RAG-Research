Alien autopsy: The alien autopsy is a 17-minute black and white film depicting a medical examination or autopsy.  It was released in 1995 by London-based entrepreneur Ray Santilli.  He presented it as an authentic autopsy on the body of an extraterrestrial being recovered from the 1947 crash of a "flying disc" near Roswell, New Mexico.  The film footage was allegedly supplied to him by a retired military cameraman who wished to remain anonymous.
