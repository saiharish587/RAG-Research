Mobutu Sese Seko: Mobutu Sese Seko Kuku Ngbendu Wa Za Banga ( ; born Joseph-Désiré Mobutu; 14 October 1930 – 7 September 1997) was the military dictator and President of the Democratic Republic of the Congo (which Mobutu renamed Zaire in 1971) from 1965 to 1997.  He also served as Chairperson of the Organisation of African Unity in 1967–1968.
