List of Baa Baa Black Sheep episodes: "Baa Baa Black Sheep" (renamed for Season 2 as "Black Sheep Squadron" and later syndicated under that title) was a television series that premiered on September 21, 1976 with a lead-in movie ("Flying Misfits") and ran from September 23, 1976 to April 6, 1978.  The series consisted of 2 seasons, a 23-episode Season 1, and a 13-episode Season 2, for a total of 36 episodes.
