KBXI: KBXI (92.5 FM, "MoJo 92.5") is a commercial radio station licensed in Park City, Montana, broadcasting to the Billings, Montana, area.  KBXI airs an adult hits music format.  Licensed to Park City, Montana, United States, the station serves the Billings area.  The station is currently owned by local radio personality Kurt Anthony, through licensee Anthony Media Inc.  Anthony also operates Twang 107.5 and Crossroads 105.1.
