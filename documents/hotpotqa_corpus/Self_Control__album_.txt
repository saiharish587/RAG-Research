Self Control (album): Self Control is the third album by American singer Laura Branigan, released in 1984.  Two songs from the album were US Top-20 hits, the title song (#4) and "The Lucky One" (#20).  The album became Branigan's biggest commercial success, earning her a Platinum certification in the United States for over 1 million confirmed sales, and selling several million copies worldwide.  Branigan also enjoyed huge success in some countries with the single "Ti amo" (her second Umberto Tozzi cover, after "Gloria"), which reached #5 in the Canadian singles chart, but most notably reaching #2 in Australia.  The album was a huge success internationally, gaining several sales certifications.
