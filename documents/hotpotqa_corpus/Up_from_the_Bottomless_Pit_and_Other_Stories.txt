Up from the Bottomless Pit and Other Stories: Up from the Bottomless Pit and Other Stories (ISBN  ) is an original collection featuring a novel and other short literary works by American science fiction author Philip José Farmer, edited by Christopher Paul Carey, published in 2007.  All of the works in the book written by Farmer previously appeared in the periodical "" between the years 2005 and 2007.  The collection includes several mainstream stories by Farmer, who is better known for his science fiction work.
