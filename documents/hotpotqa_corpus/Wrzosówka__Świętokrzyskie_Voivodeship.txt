Wrzosówka, Świętokrzyskie Voivodeship: Wrzosówka is a village in the administrative district of Gmina Małogoszcz, within Jędrzejów County, Świętokrzyskie Voivodeship, in south-central Poland.  It lies approximately 5 km north of Małogoszcz, 25 km north of Jędrzejów, and 25 km west of the regional capital Kielce.
