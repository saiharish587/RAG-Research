1910 Detroit Titans football team: The 1910 Detroit Titans football team was an American football team that represented the University of Detroit in the 1910 college football season.  In its third season under head coach George A. Kelly, the team compiled a 3–2 record, but was outscored by its opponents by a combined total of 67 to 28.  The team opened the season with back-to-back losses Michigan Agricultural (35–0) and Olivet College (29–5), and ended the season with three victories over Hillsdale College, Adrian College, and Michigan Normal School.
