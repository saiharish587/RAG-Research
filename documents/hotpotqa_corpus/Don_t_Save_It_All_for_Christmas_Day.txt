Don't Save It All for Christmas Day: "Don't Save It All for Christmas Day" is a song by Canadian recording artist Celine Dion.  It was written by Peter Zizzo, Ric Wake, and Dion for her first English-language holiday album "These Are Special Times" (1998), while Wake also served as its producer.  The pop ballad was issued as a promotional single on 4 December 2000, two years after album's original release.
