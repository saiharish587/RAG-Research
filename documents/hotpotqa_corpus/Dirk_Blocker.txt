Dirk Blocker: Dennis Dirk Blocker (born July 31, 1957), better known as Dirk Blocker, is an American actor.  The son of actor Dan Blocker and Dolphia Lee Blocker ("née" Parker), he currently co-stars as Detective Hitchcock on the Fox comedy series "Brooklyn Nine-Nine".  He was a regular on "Baa Baa Black Sheep" (1976-1978), playing pilot Jerry Bragg.
