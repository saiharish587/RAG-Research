Vanessa Rousso: Vanessa Ashley Rousso (born February 5, 1983) is an American professional poker player.  Born in White Plains, New York, Rousso holds dual citizenship with the United States and France.  Rousso was a member of Team PokerStars from 2006 to 2015, with the online name Lady Maverick.  She is a spokesperson for GoDaddy.com.  She has earned money as a professional poker player since 2005, and has become one of the game's sex symbols.
