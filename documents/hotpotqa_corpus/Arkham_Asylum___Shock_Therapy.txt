Arkham Asylum – Shock Therapy: Arkham Asylum – Shock Therapy is a Vekoma SLC roller coaster located at Warner Bros.  Movie World in Gold Coast, Queensland, Australia.  When the ride was introduced in 1995, it was themed to the Lethal Weapon film series and was named Lethal Weapon – The Ride.  In 2012, the ride was rethemed to and renamed Arkham Asylum – Shock Therapy.  The ride was the first steel inverted roller coaster at an Australian theme park.
