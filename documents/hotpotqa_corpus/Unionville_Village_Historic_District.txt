Unionville Village Historic District: Unionville Village Historic District is a national historic district located at Unionville in East Marlborough Township, Chester County, Pennsylvania.  It encompasses 69 contributing buildings in the village of Unionville.  It includes a variety of brick, stone, and frame residences the earliest of which is dated to about 1750.  Notable buildings include Unionville Academy (1834), country store (c. 1875), Union Hotel (1834), Cross Keys Inn (1751), Unionville Saddle Shop (1887), Unionville Hall (1849–50), Grange Hall (1845; originally the Friends Meetinghouse), and Green Lawn Seminary.
