The Daily Dot: The Daily Dot is a digital media company covering Internet culture and life on the web.  Founded by Nicholas White in 2011, "The Daily Dot" is headquartered in Austin, Texas with offices in New York City and San Francisco.  It had a full-time staff of 76 in addition to 222 freelance contributors in early 2016 before laying off 40% of its total staff in September 2016.
