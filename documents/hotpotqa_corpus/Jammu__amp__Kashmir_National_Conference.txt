Jammu &amp; Kashmir National Conference: The Jammu & Kashmir National Conference (JKNC) is a state political party in the Indian state of Jammu and Kashmir.  Founded as the "All Jammu and Kashmir Muslim Conference" by Sheikh Abdullah and Chaudhry Ghulam Abbas in 1932 in the princely state of Jammu and Kashmir, the organisation renamed itself to "National Conference" in 1939 in order to represent all the people of the state.  It supported the accession of the princely state to India in 1947.  Prior to that, in 1941, a group led by Ghulam Abbas broke off from the National Conference and revived the old Muslim Conference.  The revived Muslim Conference supported the accession of the princely state to Pakistan and led the movement for Azad Kashmir.
