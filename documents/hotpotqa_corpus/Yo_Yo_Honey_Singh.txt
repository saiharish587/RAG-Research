Yo Yo Honey Singh: Hirdesh Singh (born 15 March 1983), better known by his stage name Yo Yo Honey Singh or Honey Singh, is an Indian music producer, Pop singer, film actor.He started as a session and recording artist, and became a Bhangra music producer.  He has also begun to produce music for Bollywood films, and has become one of the highest-paid music producers in Bollywood.
