Lift (Radiohead song): "Lift" is a song by the English rock band Radiohead, recorded during the sessions for their third album, "OK Computer" (1997).  It was unreleased until 2017, when it was included on the "OK Computer" reissue, "OK Computer OKNOTOK 1997 2017", in June 2017.  Radiohead released a music video for the song in September.
