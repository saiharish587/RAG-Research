Dylan Kussman: Dylan Kussman (born January 21, 1971) is an American film and television writer and actor.  He played the part of Richard Cameron in the 1989 film "Dead Poets Society" as well as Dr. Allen Painter in "The Way of the Gun" in 2000.  He has also appeared in such films as "Wild Hearts Can't Be Broken", "Leatherheads", "X2", "Flight" and "Jack Reacher", and is the writer, director, and star of the online noir drama "The Steps".  He also co-wrote the 2017 Tom Cruise film, "The Mummy".
