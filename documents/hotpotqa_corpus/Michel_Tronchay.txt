Michel Tronchay: Michel Tronchay (October 1668 – October 30, 1733) was a French Catholic priest, writer, philosopher, and the secretary of French historian Louis-Sébastien Le Nain de Tillemont.  After Tillemont's death, Tronchay completed and published volumes 6–16 of "Mémoires pour servir à l'histoire ecclésiastique des six premiers siècles", a history of the first six-centuries of the Christian church.  He also completed and published the final volume 6 of "Histoire des empereurs et autres princes qui ont régné pendant les six premiers siècles de l'Église", a history of the Roman Empire.  This work was often cited by Edward Gibbon in his "Decline and Fall of the Roman Empire".
