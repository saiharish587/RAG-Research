Gustav (name): Gustav , also spelled Gustaf, is a male given name of likely Old Swedish origin, used mainly in Sweden, Norway, Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Estonia, France, Germany, Austria, the Netherlands, Belgium, Switzerland, and South Africa, possibly meaning "staff of the Geats or Goths or gods", possibly derived from the Old Norse elements "Gautr" ("Geats"), "Gutar/Gotar" ("Goths"), "goð ōs" ("gods") and the word "stafr" ("staff").  Another etymology speculates that the name may be of Medieval Slavic origin, from the name "Gostislav", a compound word for "glorious guest", from the Medieval Slavic words "Gosti" ("guest") and "slava" ("glory") and was adopted by migrating groups north and west into Germany and Scandinavia.  This name has been borne by eight Kings of Sweden, including the 16th-century Gustav Vasa and the current king, Carl XVI Gustaf.  It is a common name for Swedish monarchs since the reign of Gustav Vasa.
