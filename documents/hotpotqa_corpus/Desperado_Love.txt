Desperado Love: "Desperado Love" is a song written by Michael Garvin and Sammy Johns, and recorded by American country music artist Conway Twitty.  It was released in June 1986 as the first single from his album "Fallin' for You for Years".  "Desperado Love" was Conway Twitty's 35th and final solo number one country hit on the Billboard chart (he also reached number 1 five more times on Billboard in duets with Loretta Lynn during the 1970s, giving him an overall total of 40).  The single went to number one for one week and spent a total of 13 weeks on the country chart.
