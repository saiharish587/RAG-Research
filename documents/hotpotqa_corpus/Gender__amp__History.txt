Gender &amp; History: Gender & History is an international academic journal.  It is an important academic journal for articles relating to the history of femininity, masculinity, and gender relations.  The current editors are Sarah Chambers, Maud Bracke, Tracey Deutsch, Rosemary Elliot, Mary Jo Maynes, and Stuart Airlie.  It was edited by Karen Adler and Ross Balzaretti between 2004 and 2010.
