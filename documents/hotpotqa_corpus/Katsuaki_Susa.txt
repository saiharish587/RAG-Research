Katsuaki Susa: Katsuaki Susa (須佐 勝明 , Susa Katsuaki , born September 13, 1984 in Aizuwakamatsu, Fukushima) is a Japanese boxer who competed at the 2012 Summer Olympics in the flyweight division (– 52 kg).  He is an alumnus of the Toyo University, and is Second lieutenant in the Japan Ground Self-Defense Force.
