Floyd Mayweather Jr. vs. Shane Mosley: Floyd Mayweather vs. Shane Mosley was a boxing welterweight non-title superfight, in which Mayweather won by unanimous decision with two judges scoring it 119–109 and one judge scoring it 118–110.  The bout was held on May 1, 2010, before a "sellout" crowd of 15,117 at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada, United States.  Boxing greats Muhammad Ali and Mike Tyson were among a long list of celebrities in attendance.  The match was put together after Andre Berto pulled out of his scheduled January 30 unification bout against Mosley.
