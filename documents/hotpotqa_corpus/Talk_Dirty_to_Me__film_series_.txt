Talk Dirty to Me (film series): Talk Dirty to Me is a pornographic movie series of 15 films running from 1980 until 2003.  Anthony Spinelli written and the direct the first film.  John Leslie starred in the first five films, playing the character "Jack".  Although, after the fifth film relevance to the original film and Leslie's character disappears.  Talk Dirty to Me Part III is notable for featuring Traci Lords in one her earliest roles.  The original movie Talk Dirty to Me also generated a spin-off called Nothing to Hide (1981), starring John Leslie and Richard Pacheco, playing their characters Jack and Lenny.  "Nothing to Hide" was followed by two sequels Justine (1993) and Nothing to Hide 3 (1999).
