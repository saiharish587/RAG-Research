Leni Junker: Helene "Leni" Junker (later Thymm; 8 December 1905 – 9 February 1997) was a German sprint runner who competed at the 1928 Summer Olympics.  She won a bronze medal in the 4 × 100 m, but failed to reach the final of the individual 100 m event.  Earlier in 1925 she set a world record in the 110 yards at 12.2 seconds.
