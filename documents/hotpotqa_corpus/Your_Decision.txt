Your Decision: "Your Decision" is a song by Alice in Chains, featured on their fourth studio album "Black Gives Way to Blue" (2009).  It was released as the third single and fourth track from the album on November 16, 2009 in the UK, and was released in the US on December 1, 2009.  The track features an acoustic rhythm and breaks from the dark, aggressive leanings of the previous songs in exchange for an emotional tone that, while muted and perhaps tinged with sad resignation, can also be interpreted as calmer and more at peace.  The single reached number one on the "Billboard" Rock Songs and Mainstream Rock charts, and number four on the Alternative Songs chart.
