Korni Grupa: Korni Grupa (Serbian Cyrillic: Корни Група, trans.  "Korni Group") was a Yugoslav rock band from Belgrade, also known as the Kornelyans, the name which they used during a short-lived foray into the international market.  Formed in 1968, Korni Grupa was one of the first Yugoslav rock bands to achieve major mainstream popularity, and the first Yugoslav supergroup.  Korni Grupa is considered to be one of the most prominent and influential bands in the history of rock music in Yugoslavia.
