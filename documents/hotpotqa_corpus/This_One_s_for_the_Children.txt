This One's for the Children: "This One's for the Children" is a 1989 single from New Kids on the Block.  The lead vocals were sung by Jordan Knight and Donnie Wahlberg.  Taken from the group's holiday album, "Merry, Merry Christmas", the single peaked at #7 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 in December 1989, and #9 on the UK singles chart in December 1990.
