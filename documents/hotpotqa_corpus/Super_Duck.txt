Super Duck: Super Duck was a comic book character created in 1943 for what was then MLJ Comics (now Archie Comics) by staff artist Al Fagaly.  As his name implies, Super Duck (nicknamed "Supe") was originally a parody of Superman, even down to a red and blue costume.  But his time as a superhero was short, and by late 1944 his stories became more conventional, in the Disney/Carl Barks mode.
