Colin Hardie: Colin Graham Hardie (16 February 1906 – 17 October 1998) was a British classicist and academic.  From 1933 to 1936, he was Director of the British School at Rome.  From 1936 to 1973, he was a Fellow of Magdalen College, Oxford, and a tutor in classics.  In addition, from 1967 to 1973, he was the Public Orator of the University of Oxford.  He was a member of the Inklings, an informal literary discussion group which included the likes of J. R. R. Tolkien and C. S. Lewis.
