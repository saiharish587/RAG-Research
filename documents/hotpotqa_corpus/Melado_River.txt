Melado River: The Melado is a river of Linares province, Maule Region, of Chile.  It rises in the "Cordillera de los Andes" with the name of river Guaiquivilo where it is formed by two tributaries, the river Cajón Troncoso, born near the Argentine border and the river Palaleo, from the outflow of Dial lake, located some 70 km upstream from the joining of the two rivers.  The Guaiquivilo flows northwardly along a typical interandean longitudinal valley.
