David Rees (cartoonist): David Thomas Rees ( ; born June 22, 1972) is a humorist and cultural critic whose career has moved between various disciplines.  He first became known as a cartoonist whose best-known work combines bland clip art with outrageous "trash talk" to incongruous effect.  He moved on to his Artisanal Pencil Sharpening service and publishing a related book.  More recently he has been a presenter for "Going Deep with David Rees".
