Ragyndrudis Codex: The Ragyndrudis Codex ("Codex Bonifatianus II") is an early medieval codex of religious texts, now in Fulda in Germany, which is closely associated with Saint Boniface, who, according to tradition, used it at the time of his martyrdom to ward off the swords or axes of the Frisians who killed him on 5 June 754 near Dokkum, Friesland.  This long association has given the codex the status of a contact relic.
