The Day Before I Met You: "The Day Before I Met You" is a song by Australian recording artist Jessica Mauboy from the platinum edition of her third studio album, "Beautiful".  It was released on 13 February 2015 as the album's sixth overall single and the second single from the platinum edition.  "The Day Before I Met You" was written by Antonio Dixon, Kenneth "Babyface" Edmonds, Khristopher Riddick-Tynes, Helen Jane Culver and Mauboy.  Dixon and Edmonds also produced the song with The Rascals.  "The Day Before I Met You" is a pop love song which features an acoustic guitar riff and "very low pitched" vocals in its verses.  Lyrically, Mauboy sings about "not wanting to go back to a time before that special someone was in her life".
