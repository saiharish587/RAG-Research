Terri Conn: Terri Conn (born January 28, 1975), formerly known as Terri Colombino, is an American actress.  She is known for her roles as Katie Snyder on "As the World Turns" and as Christine "Aubrey Wentworth" Karr on "One Life to Live".  On July 1, 2011, she married her former "As the World Turns" co-star, Austin Peck.
