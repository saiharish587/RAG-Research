Damian Perkins: Damian Perkins is a singer, actor, dancer, artist and model.  He played Mereb in the original Broadway cast of the Broadway musical Aida and also played the role of Benny in the Canadian tour of the musical Rent.  Aside from his work on Broadway, he has made guest appearances in shows such as "", "Family Matters", "Charmed", "Providence", "The Net", "The Sentinel", and "Then Came You".
