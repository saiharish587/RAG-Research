Prestes Maia (building): The Prestes Maia building, or sometimes simply Prestes Maia (] ), is believed to be the largest squatted highrise building in South America, with an estimated 2000 inhabitants.  The complex is made up of two tower blocks, Bloco A and Bloco B, the latter of which has the address "Avenida Prestes Maia, 911" near Luz Station in downtown São Paulo.  Businessman Jorge Nacle Hamuche purchased the building at auction in 1993 and co-owns it with his business partner, Eduardo Amorim.  The building remains registered to the previous owner, the bankrupt National Cloth Company ("Companhia Nacional de Tecidos" in Portuguese), where Hamuche has been employed.
