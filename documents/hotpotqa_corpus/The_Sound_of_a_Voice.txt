The Sound of a Voice: The Sound of a Voice is a 1983 play by American playwright David Henry Hwang.  Hwang's fifth play, it is an original ghost story inspired by Japanese folk stories, films, and Noh theater.  The play was first produced as part of the production "Sound and Beauty" on November 6, 1983 Off-Broadway at the Joseph Papp Public Theater.  It was directed by and featured John Lone.
