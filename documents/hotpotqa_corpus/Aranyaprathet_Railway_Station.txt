Aranyaprathet Railway Station: Aranyaprathet Railway Station is a railway station located in Aranyaprathet Subdistrict, Aranyaprathet District, Sa Kaeo, Thailand.  The station is a class 1 railway station located 254.5 km from Bangkok Railway Station.  Aranyaprathet Railway Station opened in November 1926 as part of the Eastern Line Kabin Buri-Aranyaprathet section.
