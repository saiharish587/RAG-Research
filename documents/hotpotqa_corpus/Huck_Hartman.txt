Huck Hartman: Pierre "Huck" Hartman (1920 – March 25, 1946) was an American professional basketball player.  Hartman played in the National Basketball League for the Pittsburgh Raiders in 1944–45 and the Youngstown Bears in 1945–46.  He died on March 25, 1946 from pneumonia, just two weeks after competing in a game against the Chicago American Gears.  Hartman was the first active player to die in the NBL.
