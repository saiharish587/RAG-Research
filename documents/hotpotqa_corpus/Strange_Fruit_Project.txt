Strange Fruit Project: Strange Fruit Project is an underground hip hop group from Waco, Texas, which consists of producer and emcee Symbolyc One (also known as S1, born Larry D. Griffin Jr.), his cousin emcee Myth (short for Mythological, born Kevin Gaither) and emcee Myone (pronounced "My Own", born Anthony Ligawa, originally from Indiana).  The trio officially debuted in 2004, with a pair of underground releases, "Soul Travelin" and "From Divine".  Their biggest exposure came with the release of their acclaimed 2006 album "The Healing".  The group's name is derived from the Billie Holiday civil rights song "Strange Fruit".
