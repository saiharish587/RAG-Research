Eagle Street College: The Eagle Street College was an informal literary society established in 1885 at the home of James William Wallace in Eagle Street, Bolton, to read and discuss literary works, particularly the poetry of Walt Whitman, (1819–91).  The group subsequently became known as the Bolton Whitman Fellowship or Whitmanites.  Its founder members were Wallace, Dr John Johnston and Fred Wild.  The group held an annual 'Whitman Day' celebration around the 31 May which was the poet's birthday.
