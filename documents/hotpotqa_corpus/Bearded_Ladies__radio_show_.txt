Bearded Ladies (radio show): Bearded Ladies is a radio programme that was originally aired on BBC Radio 4 between 2003 and 2007.  There are currently 22 half-hour episodes; although the last 6, aired in 2007, do have a loose narrative structure holding the individual sketches together, it is essentially a sketch-driven comedy programme.  It stars Oriane Messina, Fay Rusling, Charlotte MacDougall, and Susie Donkin.
