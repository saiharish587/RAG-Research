Rochester Bridge: Rochester Bridge in Rochester, Medway was for centuries the lowest fixed crossing of the River Medway in South East England.  There have been several generations of bridge at this spot, and the current "bridge" is in fact four separate bridges: the "Old" bridge and "New" bridge carrying the A2 road, "Railway" bridge carrying the railway and the "Service" bridge carrying service pipes and cables.  The bridge links the towns of Strood and Rochester in Medway.  All except the railway bridge are owned and maintained by the Rochester Bridge Trust.
