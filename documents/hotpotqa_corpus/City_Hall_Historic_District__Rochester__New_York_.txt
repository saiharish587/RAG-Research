City Hall Historic District (Rochester, New York): City Hall Historic District is a national historic district located at Rochester in Monroe County, New York.  The district consists of four buildings arranged in a 19th-century civic complex.  The buildings are the Rochester City Hall (1874–1875), Monroe County Courthouse (1894–1896), Rochester Free Academy (1872–1873), and St. Luke's Episcopal Church (1824).  The City Hall and Free Academy buildings were designed by Andrew Jackson Warner.  The Monroe County Courthouse was designed by his son, J. Foster Warner.
