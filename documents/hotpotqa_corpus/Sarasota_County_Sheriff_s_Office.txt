Sarasota County Sheriff's Office: Sarasota County Sheriff's Office (SSO) is the primary law enforcement agency for Sarasota County, Florida.  The agency is responsible for law enforcement services in unincorporated areas of Sarasota County (home to over 60 percent of the county's residents), jail facilities and courthouse security for Florida's 12th Judicial Circuit.  SSO also operates Public Safety Communications (PSC), the county's primary 911 center.
