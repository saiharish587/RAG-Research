Dorian Gray (character): Dorian Gray is the main character in Oscar Wilde's novel The Picture of Dorian Gray.  Dorian Gray is a handsome, susceptible, wealthy young Victorian gentleman.  He falls in love with and devotes himself to Sibyl Vane.  Dorian has two friends, Basil and Henry.  Basil is an artist who draws a portrait of Dorian that emphasizes his youthful, noble, and natural beauty.  Henry is a friend who has a cynical nature and who shares various thoughts with Dorian.
