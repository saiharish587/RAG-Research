Vin Diesel: Mark Sinclair (born July 18, 1967), better known by his stage name Vin Diesel, is an American actor, producer, director and screenwriter.  He is well known for his portrayals of Dominic Toretto in "The Fast and the Furious" film series, Richard B. Riddick in "The Chronicles of Riddick" series and Xander Cage in the "xXx" series.  He is also a producer on sequels in these franchises.
