Flag of Thailand: The flag of the Kingdom of Thailand (Thai: ธงไตรรงค์ , "Thong Trairong", meaning "tricolour flag”) shows five horizontal stripes in the colours red, white, blue, white and red, with the central blue stripe being twice as wide as each of the other four.  The design was adopted on 28 September 1917, according to the royal decree issued by Rama VI that year.  Since 2016, that day is a national holiday in Thailand celebrating the nation's flag.
