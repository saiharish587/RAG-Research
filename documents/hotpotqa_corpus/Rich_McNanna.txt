Rich McNanna: Rich McNanna (born 1977 in Newark, New Jersey) is an American actor.  He attended Seton Hall University in South Orange, New Jersey from 1995-2000 and is best known for his work in several anime productions, most notably portraying Shuichi Shindo in the " Gravitation" series, Hiroyuki Fujita in the "To Heart" series, Jack Walker in the feature "Pokémon Ranger and the Temple of the Sea", and Tonio in "".  He has also appeared several times in non-recurring roles in the "Pokémon" television series on Cartoon Network, and is a regular on several series for Everest Productions on the Turkish American Ebru Television.  McNanna is an eight grade teacher in New Jersey.
