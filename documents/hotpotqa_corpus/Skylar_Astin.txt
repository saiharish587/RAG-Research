Skylar Astin: Skylar Astin Lipstein (born September 23, 1987), known professionally as Skylar Astin, is an American actor and singer.  He became known for portraying Jesse Swanson in the musical films "Pitch Perfect" (2012) and "Pitch Perfect 2" (2015).  He was also in the original cast of the Broadway musical "Spring Awakening", and has since appeared in films such as "Hamlet 2" (2008), "Taking Woodstock" (2009), "Cavemen" (2013), and "21 & Over" (2013).
