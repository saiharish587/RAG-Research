The Day (2011 film): The Day is a 2011 Canadian post-apocalyptic film directed by Douglas Aarniokoski.  The film stars Ashley Bell, Shannyn Sossamon, Dominic Monaghan, Shawn Ashmore and Cory Hardrict.  The film premiered on September 16 , 2011 at the 2011 Toronto International Film Festival.  The film was released in 12 theaters in the United States on August 29 , 2012.  It screened theatrically for 16 days and grossed $20,984.
