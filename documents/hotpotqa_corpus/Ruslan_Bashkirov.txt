Ruslan Bashkirov: Ruslan Bashkirov (born March 7, 1989) is a Russian professional ice hockey player.  Bashkirov is currently a member of Amur Khabarovsk of the Russian KHL.  On June 23, 2007, he was drafted in the second round, 60th overall, by the Ottawa Senators in the 2007 NHL Entry Draft held in Columbus, Ohio.  He is 6'0" tall and weighs 184 pounds.  He plays as a forward, and shoots left.  He is the twin brother of ice hockey player Roman Bashkirov.
