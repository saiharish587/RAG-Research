Ernesta Stern: Ernesta Stern, born Maria Ernesta de Hierschel, also known as Maria Star, (December 8, 1854 – 1926) was an Italian-born French author.  She wrote many Venetian tales and novels.  She held a salon in Paris and she was awarded the knighthood of the Legion of Honour.  Her Villa Torre Clementina in Roquebrune-Cap-Martin is an official historical monument.
