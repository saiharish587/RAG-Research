Clube Atlético Mineiro: Clube Atlético Mineiro (] ), commonly known as Atlético Mineiro or Atlético, and colloquially as Galo (] , "Rooster"), is a professional football club based in Belo Horizonte, Minas Gerais, Brazil.  The team competes in the Campeonato Brasileiro Série A, the first level of Brazilian football, as well as in the Campeonato Mineiro, the top tier state league of Minas Gerais.
