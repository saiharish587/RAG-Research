Gretel Scarlett: Gretel Scarlett (born 9 November 1987) is an Australian actress and performer.  Having appeared in theatre productions including "Wicked" and "Mamma Mia! ", she is best known for starring as Sandy in the 2013–2015 Australian production of "Grease".  In 2016, Scarlett appears as Kathy Selden in the 2016/2017 Australian production of "Singin' in the Rain" which opened at Her Majesty's Theatre, Melbourne.
