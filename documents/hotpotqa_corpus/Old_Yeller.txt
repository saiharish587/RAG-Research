Old Yeller: Old Yeller is a 1956 children's novel written by Fred Gipson and illustrated by Carl Burger.  It was nominated for the Newberry Medal.  The title is taken from the name of the yellow dog who is the center of the book's story.  In 1957 Walt Disney released a film adaptation starring Tommy Kirk, Fess Parker, Dorothy McGuire, Kevin Corcoran, Jeff York, and Beverly Washburn.
