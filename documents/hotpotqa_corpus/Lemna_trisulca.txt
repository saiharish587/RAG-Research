Lemna trisulca: Lemna trisulca L. (syn.  "Staurogeton trisulcus" (L.) Schur; star duckweed; ivy-leaved duckweed) is a species of aquatic plants in the genus "Lemna" (duckweed) with a subcosmopolitan distribution, occurring in quiet freshwater habitats in cool temperate regions.  "L. trisulca" normally doesn't occur in warm temperate regions.  Unlike other duckweeds, it has submersed rather than floating fronds, except when flowering or fruiting.
