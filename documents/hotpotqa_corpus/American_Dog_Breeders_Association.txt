American Dog Breeders Association: The American Dog Breeders Association(also known as ADBA) was started in September 1909 as a multiple breed association.  The residing president, Guy McCord, was an avid fancier and breeder of the American Pit Bull Terrier and was a close friend of John P. Colby.  Colby was the mainstay of the ADBA and therefore it was considered the "home" registration office for the Colby dogs.  All members in good standing could register their dogs and litters for the yearly fee of $2.50.  The exclusive member’s idea gradually was replaced by a registry open to all owners and breeders of purebred dogs.  Over time the association focused on the registration of the American Pit Bull Terrier.
