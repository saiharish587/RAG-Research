Kadhalan (soundtrack): Kadhalan (காதலன்) is the soundtrack to the 1994 Tamil film of the same name, directed by Shankar.  The soundtrack, released as "Kadhalan: The Original Motion Picture Soundtrack", features 9 songs composed by A. R. Rahman and lyrics penned by Vairamuthu, Vaali, Shankar and Thirikudarasappa Kavirayar.  Owing to the immense national popularity of the song Mukkabla, the soundtrack was subsequently dubbed in Hindi as "Humse Hai Muqabala" and in Telugu as "Premikudu".  Lyrics for this versions were written by P. K. Mishra and Rajashri respectively.
