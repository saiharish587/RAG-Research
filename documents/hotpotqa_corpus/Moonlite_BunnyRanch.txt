Moonlite BunnyRanch: The Moonlite BunnyRanch (often misspelled "Moonlight", though that is the correct spelling of the road on which it is addressed) is a legal, licensed brothel in Mound House, Nevada, United States, 6 mi east of Carson City.  The ranch is owned and operated by Dennis Hof.  The Love Ranch (previously known as the BunnyRanch Two), a separately licensed brothel, is located about a mile south of the Moonlite BunnyRanch, near two other brothels (the Kit Kat Guest Ranch and the Sagebrush Ranch).
