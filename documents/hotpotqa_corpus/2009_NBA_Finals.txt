2009 NBA Finals: The 2009 NBA Finals was the National Basketball Association (NBA)'s championship series for the 2008–09 season.  The best-of-seven playoff was contested between the Western Conference champion Los Angeles Lakers (who were also the defending Western Conference champions), and the Eastern Conference champion Orlando Magic.  The Lakers defeated the Magic, four games to one, to win the franchise's 15th NBA championship.  The 63rd edition of the championship series was played between June 4 and June 14 and was broadcast on U.S. television on ABC.
