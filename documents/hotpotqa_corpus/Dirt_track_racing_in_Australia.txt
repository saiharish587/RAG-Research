Dirt track racing in Australia: Dirt track racing is a type of auto racing performed on clay or dirt surfaced oval tracks in Australia.  The seasons tend to be from September to April.  There are a large number of tracks available Australia wide, with some of the most popular ones being Perth Motorplex, Bunbury Speedway, Speedway City, Premier Speedway Warrnambool (Sungold Stadium) and Sydney Speedway (trading as Valvoline Raceway).  The richest and best known Speedway series in Australia is the World Series Sprintcars.  The series was conceived by Adelaide based sedan driver and promoter John Hughes in 1986 as an Australian version of the famous World of Outlaws (WoO) series run in the United States since 1978.  The most recent season was the 2014–15 World Series Sprintcars season.
