Czarface (album): Czarface is the eponymous debut studio album by American hip hop supergroup Czarface.  The album was released on February 19, 2013, by Brick Records.  The album features guest appearances from Roc Marciano, Oh No, Ghostface Killah, Action Bronson, Cappadonna and Vinnie Paz.  The album's production comes from 7L, Spada4 and DJ Premier.
