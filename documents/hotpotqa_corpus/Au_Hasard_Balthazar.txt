Au Hasard Balthazar: Au hasard Balthazar (] ; meaning "Balthazar, at Random"), also known as "Balthazar", is a 1966 French film directed by Robert Bresson.  Believed to be inspired by a passage from Fyodor Dostoyevsky's novel "The Idiot", the film follows a donkey as he is given to various owners, most of whom treat him callously.
