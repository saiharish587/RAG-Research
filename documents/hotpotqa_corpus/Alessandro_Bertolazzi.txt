Alessandro Bertolazzi: Alessandro Bertolazzi is an Italian makeup artist.  His works on films such as "Babel" (2006), "Biutiful" (2010), "J. Edgar" (2011), "To the Wonder" (2012), "Skyfall" (2012), "The Impossible" (2012), "Fury" (2014) and "Suicide Squad" has earned him numerous awards and nominations.  For "Suicide Squad", he won the Academy Award for Best Makeup and Hairstyling at 89th Academy Awards.
