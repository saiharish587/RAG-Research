D.C. Armor: The D.C. Armor was a professional indoor football team that began play in the American Indoor Football Association (AIFA) in the 2009 season.  The team was based in Washington, D.C., with home games at the under-renovation D.C. Armory.  The Armor were the first professional football team to play within the District of Columbia since the Washington Redskins left for FedExField in 1997.  The Armor was also the area's first indoor football team since the Washington Commandos played in the Arena Football League in 1990, and the only arena/indoor football team to play within the district (the Commandos played in the Capital Centre and the Patriot Center).  After one, poorly attended season, the Armor folded.
