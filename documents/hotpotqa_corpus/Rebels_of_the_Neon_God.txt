Rebels of the Neon God: Rebels of the Neon God () is a 1992 Taiwanese film by Tsai Ming-liang.  It is his first full-length film.  It tells two stories of Taipei youth.  One details alienated buxiban student Hsiao Kang (Lee Kang-sheng) and his troubled interactions with his family.  The other shows two petty hoods, Ah Tze and Ah Ping, along with Ah Kuei, Tze's erstwhile girlfriend.  An idle act of violence brings the two groups into collision, and an act of revenge at the end completes the circle.  It is a story of troubled youth, dissatisfaction, and the alienating effect of urban life.
