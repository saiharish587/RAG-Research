The Phone Co-op: The Phone Co-op, is a consumer co-operative in the United Kingdom, which provides fixed, mobile telephone and internet services, including web hosting and broadband.  It is 100% owned by its customer-members who democratically control the business and who share in its profits.  This makes The Phone Co-op the only telephone co-operative in the UK.  The co-op is a Social enterprise and was awarded the title of UK customer-facing social enterprise of the year 2015.  The business is a living wage employer and is accredited to hold the "Fair Tax Mark".
