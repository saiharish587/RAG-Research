D'Urville Martin: D'Urville Martin (February 11, 1939 – May 28, 1984) was an American actor and director in both film and television.  He appeared in numerous 1970s movies in the blaxploitation genre.  He also appeared in two unaired pilots of what would become "All in the Family" as Lionel Jefferson, the role was later played by Mike Evans.  Born in New York City, Martin began his career in the mid-1960s, soon becoming a prominent recurring figure in the genre.  Martin acted in several movies of the time, including "Black Like Me" and "Guess Who's Coming to Dinner".  Martin also directed films in his career, including "Dolemite", starring Rudy Ray Moore.
