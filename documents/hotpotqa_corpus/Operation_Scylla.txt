Operation Scylla: Operation Scylla (Italian: "Operazione Scilla") was a successful Italian Navy attempt to transfer the light cruiser "Scipione Africano" from their bases in the Tyrrhenian Sea to Taranto, in the Ionian Sea, during the Allied invasion of Sicily in July 1943, in the course of World War II.  The operation is notable for the night engagement between the Italian cruiser and four British motor torpedo boats during the passage of the strait of Messina, in the early hours of 17 July 1943.  The action also marked the only time that an Italian warship made an effective combat use of surface radar in World War II.
