Kelly Bryant: Kelly Bryant (August 28, 1908 – October 1975) served as the Democratic secretary of state of the U.S. state of Arkansas from 1963 until his death nearly thirteen years later.  He was one of three statewide politicians born in Hope, the seat of Hempstead County, in southern Arkansas.  The others are former Governor and U.S. President Bill Clinton and former Governor and unsuccessful 2008 presidential candidate Mike Huckabee.
