Cartoon Network: Battle Crashers: Cartoon Network: Battle Crashers is a side-scrolling beat 'em up video game developed by French studio Magic Pockets and published by GameMill Entertainment.  It was released for Nintendo 3DS, PlayStation 4, and Xbox One, on 8 November 2016.  The game received extremely negative reviews from several video game journalists, who panned it as a repetitive and boring beat 'em up with bland representations of otherwise unique characters.  A port for the Nintendo Switch will be released on 14 November 2017.
