2012 WGC-Bridgestone Invitational: The 2012 WGC-Bridgestone Invitational was a professional golf tournament held August 2–5 on the South Course of Firestone Country Club in Akron, Ohio.  It was the 14th WGC-Bridgestone Invitational tournament, and the third of four World Golf Championships events held in 2012.  Keegan Bradley shot a 64 (−6) in the final round to finish with 267 (−13) to win his first WGC event, one stroke ahead of runners-up Jim Furyk and Steve Stricker.
