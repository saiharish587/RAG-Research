Matthew Butturini: Matthew Butturini (born 7 August 1987 in Murwillumbah) is an Australian field hockey player.  He is a member of the Australia men's national field hockey team.  He won a gold medal at the 2010 Men's Hockey World Cup and a gold medal at the 2011 Men's Hockey Champions Trophy.  He was part of the bronze medal winning Australian team at the 2012 Summer Olympics.
