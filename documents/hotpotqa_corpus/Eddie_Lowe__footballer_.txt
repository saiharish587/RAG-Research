Eddie Lowe (footballer): Edward "Eddie" Lowe (11 July 1925 – 9 March 2009) was an English former professional footballer who played for Aston Villa and Fulham, making the second-all-time club record appearances for Fulham of 511, behind Johnny Haynes, between 1950 and 1963.  He was also an England international and later, the manager for Notts County.  Lowe died on 9 March 2009 in Nottingham.
