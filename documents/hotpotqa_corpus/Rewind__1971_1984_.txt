Rewind (1971–1984): Rewind (1971–1984) is a compilation album by The Rolling Stones and was released in 1984.  Coming only three years after "Sucking in the Seventies", the album was primarily compiled to mark the end of the band's alliance with Warner Music (in North America) and EMI (all other territories), both of whom were the distributors of Rolling Stones Records.  It is the second Rolling Stones album to include a lyric sheet (after 1978's "Some Girls".)
