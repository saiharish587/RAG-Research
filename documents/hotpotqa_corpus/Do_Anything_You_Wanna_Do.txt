Do Anything You Wanna Do: "Do Anything You Wanna Do" is a song written by Eddie and the Hot Rods' manager Ed Hollis (the brother of Talk Talk's Mark Hollis) and guitarist Graeme Douglas and recorded by the band, although the actual record label credited The Rods as the artist.  It reached #9 on the UK Singles Chart in 1977.  The song was featured on their 1977 album, "Life on the Line".
