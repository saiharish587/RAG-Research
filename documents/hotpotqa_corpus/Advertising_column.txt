Advertising column: Advertising columns or Morris columns (French: "colonne Morris" , German: "Litfaßsäule" ("Litfasssäule", the spelling used in Switzerland and Liechtenstein)) are cylindrical outdoor sidewalk structures with a characteristic style that are used for advertising and other purposes.  They are common in the city of Berlin, Germany, where the first 100 columns were installed in 1855.  Advertising columns were invented by the German printer Ernst Litfaß in 1854.  Therefore, they are known as "Litfaßsäulen" (Litfaß columns), and can be found all over Germany.
