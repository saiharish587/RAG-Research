Star Wars: Republic: Star Wars: Republic is an American comic book series set in the fictional "Star Wars" universe.  The series was published by Dark Horse Comics from 1998 to February 2006.  The series was originally titled simply "Star Wars", but acquired its "Republic" title at issue 46.  The entire series comprises 83 issues.  The "Star Wars: Republic" series is one of a number of comic book series set in the "Star Wars" expanded universe.
