Blumenau Esporte Clube: Blumenau Esporte Clube is a football club from the city of Blumenau in the south Brazilian state of Santa Catarina.  The club was founded on 19 July 1919 as "Brasil Football Club".  In 1936 the club was renamed to "Recreativo Brasil Esporte Clube", and in 1944, owing to a law prohibiting national denominations in club names, to "Palmeiras Esporte Clube."
