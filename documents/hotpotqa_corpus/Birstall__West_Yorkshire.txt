Birstall, West Yorkshire: Birstall is a village and part of the town of Batley in the metropolitan borough of Kirklees, West Yorkshire, England.  It is part of Birstall and Birkenshaw ward which had a population of 16,298 at the 2011 census.  Historically in the West Riding of Yorkshire, it is situated close to the M62 motorway, approximately 6 mi south-west of Leeds.  The village is situated between Leeds, Bradford, Huddersfield and Wakefield.
