Ice Arena (Adelaide): IceArenA, located in Thebarton, Adelaide, South Australia first opened on 17 September 1981 as the Ice Arena.  In late 1986, the centre closed for the construction of the world's first indoor ski slope and reopened in 1987 as Mt Thebarton Snow and Ice, featuring what was reported to be the world's first indoor ski slope on artificial snow.  The centre underwent a further name change to Snowdome Adelaide.  Most recently, following the closure of the Ski Slope in 2005, a final name change to IceArenA was made.  The IceArenA is South Australia’s premier ice skating facility located just minutes from the Adelaide CBD.
