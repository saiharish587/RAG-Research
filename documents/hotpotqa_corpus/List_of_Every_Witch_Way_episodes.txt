List of Every Witch Way episodes: "Every Witch Way" is an American teen sitcom that premiered on Nickelodeon on January 1, 2014.  It is the United States version of Nickelodeon Latin America series "Grachi", starring Isabella Castillo.  The series tells the story of 14-year-old Emma Alonso that moves to Miami, Florida, with her father, and finds out she's a witch and the most powerful of her generation.
