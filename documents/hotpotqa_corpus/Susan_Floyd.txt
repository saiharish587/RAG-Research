Susan Floyd: Susan Floyd (born May 13, 1968) is an American actress who has appeared in many episodes of "Law & Order", as well as numerous other television series.  She has also had featured roles in several motion pictures, including "Domestic Disturbance" and "Forgiven", and starred opposite Al Pacino and Jerry Orbach in "Chinese Coffee".  Along with mainstream films, she has also appeared in a 2003 indie film "Particles of Truth".
