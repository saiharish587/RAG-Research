Bhaji in Problem: Bhaji in Problem (Punjabi: ਭਾਜੀ ਇਨ ਪ੍ਰਾਬਲਮ ) is a 2013 Indian Punjabi-language comedy film directed by Smeep Kang, who had earlier directed films like "Carry on Jatta" and "Lucky Di Unlucky Story", both of which featured Gippy Grewal in the lead role.  Grewal also appears in this film, along with an ensemble cast including Ragini Khanna, Gurpreet Ghuggi, B.N. Sharma, Karamjit Anmol, Japji Khaira, Khushboo Grewal and Misha Bajwa amongst others.  The film is produced by Ashvini Yardi, and Bollywood actor Akshay Kumar who also appears in the film shortly, enacting as a lookalike of himself.
