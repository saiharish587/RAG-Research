Chuvash language: Chuvash (Чӑвашла, "Čăvašla" ; ] ) is a Turkic language spoken in central Russia, primarily in the Chuvash Republic and adjacent areas.  It is the only surviving member of the Oghur branch of Turkic languages and thus makes up one full half of the Turkic language family.  Because of this, Chuvash has diverged considerably from the other Turkic languages, which typically demonstrate mutual intelligibility among one another to varying degrees.
