Oregon Field Guide: Oregon Field Guide is a weekly television program produced by Oregon Public Broadcasting focusing on recreation, the outdoors, and environmental issues in the state of Oregon.  The show has become part of the Oregon zeitgeist.  Steve Amen is the show's host and executive producer.  Named for the field guides used to identify plants, animals, and natural phenomenon, the wide-ranging series covers Oregon natural history, outdoor recreation, conservation, agriculture, rural life, and other local subjects.  Produced with deep narratives rather than short segments, 13 half-hour and one full-hour episodes are shown per year.
