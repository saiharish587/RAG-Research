Only for Love: "Only For Love" is the debut single by former Kajagoogoo singer Limahl, following being fired from the group by his bandmates in 1983.  The song was featured in the first UK version of "Now That's What I Call Music", and later included on Limahl's 1984 debut solo album, "Don't Suppose".  The song became his first UK Top 20 hit, peaking at No. 16 on the UK Singles Chart.  It remains his second most popular single in the UK as a solo act (the first being "The Neverending Story").
