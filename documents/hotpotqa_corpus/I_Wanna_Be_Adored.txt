I Wanna Be Adored: "I Wanna Be Adored" is a song by the British rock band The Stone Roses.  It was the first track on their debut album, "The Stone Roses", and was released as a single.  The US release charted at number 18 on the "Billboard" Modern Rock chart in 1990.  In 1991, the single was released in the UK, Germany and Japan featuring previously unreleased B-sides.
