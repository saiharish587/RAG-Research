Slimer: Slimer is a character from the "Ghostbusters" franchise.  He appears in the films "Ghostbusters" (1984), "Ghostbusters II" (1989) and "Ghostbusters: Answer the Call" (2016), in the animated television series "The Real Ghostbusters", "Slimer! " and "Extreme Ghostbusters", and in the video games "" and Beeline's "Ghostbusters".  Slimer was voiced by Ivan Reitman and Adam Ray in the films and by Frank Welker in the cartoon series.  In "The Real Ghostbusters", he is the Ghostbusters' mascot.
