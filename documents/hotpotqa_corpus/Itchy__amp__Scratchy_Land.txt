Itchy &amp; Scratchy Land: "Itchy & Scratchy Land" is the fourth episode of "The Simpsons"<nowiki>'</nowiki> sixth season.  It first aired on the Fox network in the United States on October 2, 1994.  Wanting a perfect family vacation, the Simpson family visits Itchy & Scratchy Land.  It was written by John Swartzwelder and directed by Wes Archer.
