Richard Woodbury: Richard G. "Dick" Woodbury (born October 10, 1961) is an American politician and economist from Maine.  Woodbury served as an unenrolled State Senator from Maine's 11th District, representing part of Cumberland County, including the population centers of Falmouth and Cumberland as well as his residence in Yarmouth.  He was first elected to the Maine State Senate in 2010 after defeating incumbent Republican Gerald Davis and Green Independent Chris Miller.  The Democrat in the race, Cynthia Bullens, dropped out of the race and endorsed Woodbury, though her name remained on the ballot.  He served three terms from 2002-2008 in the Maine House of Representatives.  He has also been a visiting scholar with the Federal Reserve Bank of Boston and written extensively on tax reform in Maine.
