Pamela Sneed: Pamela Sneed is an American poet, performance artist, actress, activist, and teacher.  She is the author of two collections of poetry, "Imagine Being More Afraid of Freedom Than Slavery" (Henry Holt, 1998), and "KONG And Other Works" (Vintage Entity Press, 2009) and the chapbook, "Lincoln" (2014).  Her poems and monologues have also been anthologized in "The 100 Best African American Poems"," edited by Nikki Giovanni.  Recent publications include work in "Best Monologues from Best American Short Plays" and "Future Perfect."
