Viva Bianca: Viva Bianca (born Viva Skubiszewski, ; ] ) is an Australian actress best known for her role as Ilithyia on the Starz network series "" and "".  Bianca graduated from the Western Australian Academy of Performing Arts where she received a best actress award.  She is the daughter of Cezary Skubiszewski, a Polish Australian composer for film, television and orchestra.  Bianca cites Cate Blanchett and Heath Ledger as Australian actors who have influenced her.
