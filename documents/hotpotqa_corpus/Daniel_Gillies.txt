Daniel Gillies: Daniel Gillies (born March 14, 1976) is a Canadian-born New Zealand actor.  He is best known for his role as Elijah Mikaelson on the television series "The Vampire Diaries" and its spin-off "The Originals", as well as Dr. Joel Goran on the Canadian series "Saving Hope", a medical themed show with a mystical, paranormal twist.  He wrote and directed the 2012 film "Broken Kingdom".
