Race Wars (The Fast and the Furious): Race Wars is a drag racing event in the movie "The Fast and the Furious", which describes the underground illegal racing scene in Los Angeles, California, that character Dominic Toretto (Vin Diesel) and his team invent.  Race Wars is not mentioned again until they reference it in the seventh installment of the "Fast and Furious" franchise, "Furious 7".  The event consists of drag races where racers compete for large amounts of cash or pink slips, while beautiful women dance around the cars and throughout the event.  There is a real life event based on the movie that occurs in multiple places throughout the world, including Australia and Royal City, Washington.
