Gaddafi Stadium: Gaddafi Stadium (Urdu: ‎ / ALA-LC: "Qaẕẕāfī Isṭeḍiyam" ) is a cricket ground in Lahore, Pakistan.  It was designed by famous architect and engineer Nasreddin Murat-Khan, and constructed by Mian Abdul Khaliq and Company in 1959.  The stadium was renovated for the 1996 Cricket World Cup when it hosted the final.  The headquarters of the Pakistan Cricket Board are situated at Gaddafi Stadium making it the home of Pakistan cricket team.  The stadium has a capacity of 27,000 spectators making it one of the biggest in the country.
