Kevin Nalty: Kevin 'Nalts' Nalty (born May 12, 1969) is a YouTube comedian and partner based in Doylestown, Pennsylvania better known under his YouTube username Nalts.  Nalts began on YouTube as one of the top-20 most-viewed comedy channels, and collaborates with many of today's top YouTube personalities.  He has more than 1,000 videos which, as of April 18, 2016, have been viewed more than 297 million times on YouTube alone.  He has been ranked as one of YouTube's Most Subscribed users.  He is the author of "Beyond Viral: How to Attract Customers, Promote Your Brand, and Make Money with Online Video (Wiley & Sons, 2010).
