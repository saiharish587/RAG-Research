Rob Bottin: Robin R. Bottin (born April 1, 1959) is an American special make-up effects creator.  Known for his collaborations with directors John Carpenter, Paul Verhoeven and David Fincher, Bottin worked with Carpenter on both "The Fog" and "The Thing", with Verhoeven on "RoboCop", "Total Recall" and "Basic Instinct", and with Fincher on "Se7en" and "Fight Club".  His other film credits include "Legend", "Innerspace" and "Fear and Loathing in Las Vegas".
