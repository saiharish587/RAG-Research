Grover Jones: Grover Jones (November 15, 1893 – September 24, 1940) was an American screenwriter - often teamed with William Slavens McNutt - and film director.  He wrote more than 104 films between 1920 and his death.  He also was a film journal publisher and prolific short story writer.  Jones was born in Rosedale, Indiana, grew up in West Terre Haute, Indiana, and died in Hollywood, California.
