Gordon Day: Gordon Raymond Day (born 4 January 1936) is a retired South African sprinter.  He competed at the 1960 Summer Olympics in the 400 metres and 4×400 metres relay events and finished fourth in the relay.  He was part of the South African team that won the 4×440 yards relay at the 1958 British Empire and Commonwealth Games, while finishing third in the individual 220 yards.
