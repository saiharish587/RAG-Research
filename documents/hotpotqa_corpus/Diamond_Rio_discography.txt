Diamond Rio discography: Diamond Rio is an American country music band founded in 1982.  Their discography consists of Ten studio albums, 36 singles, four compilation albums, and 20 music videos. Founded in 1984, Diamond Rio released their self-titled debut album in 1991. " Meet in the Middle", the lead-off single, reached #1 on the "Billboard" country singles chart, making Diamond Rio the first country group in history to have their debut single reach that position.
