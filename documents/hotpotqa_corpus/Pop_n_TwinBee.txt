Pop'n TwinBee: Pop'n Twinbee (Pop'nツインビー ) is a top-view shoot-'em-up game originally released in 1993 by Konami for the Super Famicom in Japan.  The game was also released for the Super NES in the PAL region, but not in North America.  It is the sixth game in the "TwinBee" series and a direct follow-up to the arcade game "Detana!!  TwinBee" ("Bells & Whistles").
