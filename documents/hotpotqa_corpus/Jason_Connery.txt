Jason Connery: Jason Joseph Connery (born 11 January 1963) is a British actor and director.  He is the son of former James Bond actor Sir Sean Connery.  On screen, he is best known for appearing in the third series of the ITV drama programme "Robin of Sherwood" in 1986.  He took over the main role after Michael Praed's character was killed off at the end of the second series.
