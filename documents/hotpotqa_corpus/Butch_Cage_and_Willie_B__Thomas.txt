Butch Cage and Willie B. Thomas: James "Butch" Cage (born March 16, 1894 - 1975) and Willie B. Thomas (born May 25, 1912 - ?)  were an American blues duo active from the 1940s to the early 1970s.  Cage and Thomas were discovered in 1959 by musicologist Harry Oster, who promoted the two at the Newport Music Festival.  Field recording with the duo conducted in the following year are praised for upholding pre-blues string band tradition.
