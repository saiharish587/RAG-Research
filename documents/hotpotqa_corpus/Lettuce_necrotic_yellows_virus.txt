Lettuce necrotic yellows virus: Lettuce necrotic yellows virus (LNYV) is a plant virus belonging to the virus order "Mononegavirales", family "Rhabdoviridae" and genus "Cytorhabdovirus".  It was first identified in Australia in the plant species "Lactuca sativa" in 1963 by Stubbs et al.  Since then it has been identified in many other plant species including "Datura stramonium" and "Nicotiana glutinosa".  The virus is transmitted by the insect vector "Hyperomyzus lactucae" the insect can become infected by feeding on an infected plant.  It then acts as a reservoir for the virus in which it can multiply.  The virus is also transmitted congenitally to its progeny
