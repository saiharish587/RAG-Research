Muehlenbeckia axillaris: Muehlenbeckia axillaris (creeping wire vine, sprawling wirevine, matted lignum) is a low evergreen shrub, forming wiry mats up to about 1 m in diameter, native to New Zealand, and Tasmania, New South Wales and Victoria in Australia.  It has thin, red-brown stems, with glossy squarish to roundish leaves that are less than 1 cm in diameter, and 2 – thick.  Flowers are inconspicuous, yellowish-white, 4 - in diameter, and borne in groups of up to 3 in the axils.  Fruit is black, shiny, and up to 3.5 mm long, produced in late summer to fall.
