Otley (film): Otley is a 1969 British comedy thriller film, starring Tom Courtenay and Romy Schneider.  It was adapted by Dick Clement and Ian La Frenais from a book by Martin Waddell, and released by Colombia Pictures.  Film critic Judith Crist described it as "a bright, breezy, light-handed but never lightheaded spies-and-counterspies story".
