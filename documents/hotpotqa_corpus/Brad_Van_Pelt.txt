Brad Van Pelt: Brad Alan Van Pelt (April 5, 1951 – February 17, 2009) was an American football linebacker who played 14 seasons in the National Football League (NFL).  A two-time All-American (1971, 1972) and the 1972 Maxwell Award winner as college football's best player, he was drafted by the New York Giants, earning five Pro Bowl selections during his ten years with the team.  He rounded out his career with the Los Angeles Raiders from 1984 to 1985 and the Cleveland Browns in 1986.  Van Pelt is the father of former Denver Broncos and Houston Texans quarterback Bradlee Van Pelt.
