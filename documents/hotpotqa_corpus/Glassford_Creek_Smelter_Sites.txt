Glassford Creek Smelter Sites: Glassford Creek Smelter Sites are the heritage-listed remains of a former smelter at Glassford State Forest, off Many Peaks Road, Many Peaks, Gladstone Region, Queensland, Australia.  It was built  1903 .  It is also known as Glassford Creek Copper Smelters.  It was added to the Queensland Heritage Register on 4 July 2006.
