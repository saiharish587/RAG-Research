National Rail: In the United Kingdom, National Rail is the trading name licensed for use by the Rail Delivery Group, an unincorporated association whose membership consists of the passenger train operating companies (TOCs) of the island of Great Britain that run the passenger services previously provided by the British Railways Board, from 1965 using the brand name British Rail.  Northern Ireland, which is linked to the Republic of Ireland, has a different system.  National Rail generally does not include services that do not have a BR history; this distinction is important because National Rail services share a ticketing structure and inter-availability that do not necessarily extend to other services.  The name and the accompanying double arrow symbol are the intellectual property of the Secretary of State for Transport.
