Obi Egekeze: Egekeze was born in Augusta, Georgia in 1985.  He attended Westside High School in Augusta, where he was a three-year starter in football, basketball and soccer.  A placekicker and punter for the school's football team, he was selected as an all-state player as a senior.  Egekze also maintained a high school grade point average of 3.86 and was recruited to play college football by several major universities, including the University of Georgia, Ohio State University, University of Texas, and Penn State.  He accepted a scholarship to attend the University of Maryland.
