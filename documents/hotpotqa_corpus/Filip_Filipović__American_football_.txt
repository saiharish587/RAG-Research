Filip Filipović (American football): Filip Filipovic (Serbian Cyrillic: Филип Филиповић) (born November 5, 1977) is a former American football punter.  Filipovic was signed by the Dallas Cowboys as an undrafted free agent in 2002.  Previously, Filipovic played college football at the University of South Dakota as both their kicker and punter.  Filipovic now owns and operates a full-service franchise training grade school, middle school, high school, collegiate, and NFL kickers and punters.
