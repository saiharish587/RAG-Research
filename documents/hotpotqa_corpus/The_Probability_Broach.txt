The Probability Broach: The Probability Broach is the 1979 first novel by American science fiction writer L. Neil Smith.  It is set in an alternate history, the so-called Gallatin Universe, where a libertarian society has formed on the North American continent, styled the North American Confederacy ("NAC").  This history was created when the Declaration of Independence has the word "unanimous" added to the preamble, to read that governments "derive their just power from the "unanimous" consent of the governed".
