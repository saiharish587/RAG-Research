Mega Man 9: Mega Man 9, known in Japan as Rockman 9: Yabō no Fukkatsu!!  (ロックマン9 野望の復活!!  , Rokkuman Nain Yabō no Fukkatsu!!  , lit.  "Rockman 9: Revival of Ambition!!")  , is an action-platform video game developed by Capcom and Inti Creates.  It is the ninth numbered game in the original "Mega Man" series.  "Mega Man 9" is the first, new home console game in the original "Mega Man" series since "Mega Man 8" and "Mega Man & Bass", which were released at least one decade earlier.  "Mega Man 9" was the first game in the series not to have a physical release, and was initially released only on the downloadable gaming services WiiWare, PlayStation Network (PSN), and Xbox Live Arcade (XBLA).  In June 2017, it was announced that Mega Man 9 and 10 would have a physical release with their inclusion in Mega Man Legacy Collection 2 for PlayStation 4, Xbox One and PC.
