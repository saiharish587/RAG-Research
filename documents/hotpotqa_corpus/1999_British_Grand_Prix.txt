1999 British Grand Prix: The 1999 British Grand Prix was a Formula One motor race held on 11 July 1999 at the Silverstone Circuit near Silverstone, England.  It was the eighth race of the 1999 Formula One season.  The 60-lap race was won by McLaren driver David Coulthard after he started from third position.  Eddie Irvine finished second for the Ferrari team and Williams driver Ralf Schumacher came in third.
