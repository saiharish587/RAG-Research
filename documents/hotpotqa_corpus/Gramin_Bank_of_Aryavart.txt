Gramin Bank of Aryavart: The Gramin Bank of Aryavart (Hindi: ग्रामीण बैंक ऑफ आर्यावर्त ) (Urdu: اریارت گرامین بنک‎ ) ("GBA") is a Regional Rural Bank (RRB) in Uttar Pradesh established in 2013.  The bank was formed by the amalgamation of Aryavart Kshetriya Gramin Bank and Shreyas Gramin Bank.  It currently has 651 branches and 11 regional offices in rural areas of Uttar Pradesh, around Lucknow.  It functions under Regional Rural Banks’ Act 1976 and is sponsored by Bank of India.
