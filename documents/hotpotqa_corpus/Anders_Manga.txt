Anders Manga: Anders Manga is an American recording artist best known for his self named Darkwave work and under the name Bloody Hammers.  He was nominated for "Best Band" in 2006 by Gothic Beauty Magazine and has released albums since 1996.  Anders' earliest work was in the style of Deathrock then moved toward a more synthesizer based dark gothic rock.  In 2012, Anders' song "Glamour" was featured in Season 4 Episode 04 of "The Vampire Diaries"
