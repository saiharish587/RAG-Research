Starry Eyed (Ellie Goulding song): "Starry Eyed" is a song by English singer and songwriter Ellie Goulding, released as the second single from her debut studio album, "Lights" (2010).  It was written by Goulding and Jonny Lattimer, and produced by Starsmith.  Goulding made her debut US television performance on "Jimmy Kimmel Live! " on 7 April 2011 performing "Starry Eyed".
