Love Get Chu: Love Get Chu (ラブゲッCHU , Rabu Gē CHU ) is a Japanese mobile phone visual novel created by ARiKO System about a group of five girls trying to become voice actresses.  An anime adaptation entitled "Love Get Chu: Miracle Seiyū Hakusho" (ラブゲッCHU 〜ミラクル声優白書〜 , Rabu Gē CHU ~Mirakuru Seiyū Hakusho~ ) aired in Japan on TV Tokyo between April 4 and September 27, 2006, containing twenty-five episodes.
