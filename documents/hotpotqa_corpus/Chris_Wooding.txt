Chris Wooding: Chris Wooding (born 28 February 1977) is a British writer born in Leicester, and now living in London.  His first book, "Crashing", which he wrote at the age of nineteen, was published in 1998 when he was twenty-one.  Since then he has written many more, including "The Haunting of Alaizabel Cray", which was silver runner-up for the Nestlé Smarties Book Prize, and "Poison", which won the Lancashire Children's Book of the Year.  He is also the author of three different, completed series; "Broken Sky", an anime-influenced fantasy serial for children, "Braided Path", a fantasy trilogy for adults, and "Malice", a young adult fantasy that mixes graphic novel with the traditional novel; as well as another, four-part series, "Tales of the Ketty Jay", a steampunk sci-fi fantasy for adults.
