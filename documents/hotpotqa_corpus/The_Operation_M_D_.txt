The Operation M.D.: The Operation M.D. (formerly The Operation) is a Canadian garage rock band created by Dr. Dynamite (Cone of Sum 41) and Dr. Rocco (Todd Morse of HO) as a side project to their other bands.  The band's debut album "We Have an Emergency" was released in February 2007 by Aquarius Records.  Their second album "Birds + Bee Stings" was self-released by the band's own label "Mouth To Mouth Music" in June 2010.
