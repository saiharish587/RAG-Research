Kansas Barbed Wire Museum: The Kansas Barbed Wire Museum is a barbed wire museum located in La Crosse, Kansas, United States, known as the “Barbed Wire Capital of the World.”  The museum focuses on barbed wire and its history, displaying over 2,000 different forms of the wire and its history.  Barbed wire played a significant role in the history of the settlement of the United States and forever changed the face of the prairie.
