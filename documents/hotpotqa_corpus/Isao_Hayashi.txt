Isao Hayashi: Isao Hayashi (林伊佐緒 , Hayashi Isao , May 11, 1912—September 29, 1995) was a Japanese popular music and military music singer and composer.  He took part in the Japan's famous year-end show "Kōhaku Uta Gassen" eleven times.  One of well-known songs composed by him is the military song "Shussei Heishi o Okuru Uta" (出征兵士を送る歌 , "Song for Giving Warriors a Send-off") , which propaganda vehicles of "uyoku dantai" have aired in Japan.
