Plymouth Playhouse: Plymouth Playhouse, aka ABC Album, is a half-hour US television anthology series.  Fifteen episodes aired on the American Broadcasting Company (ABC) from April 12, 1953 to June 21, 1953.  Some of the productions were live while others were filmed.  It was hosted by David Cook.  ABC, which had a reputation for producing shows that were not as good as their competitors, CBS and the National Broadcasting Company (NBC), used this program featuring top notch casts to audition possible series in the hopes of gaining sponsorship for the 1953 TV season.
