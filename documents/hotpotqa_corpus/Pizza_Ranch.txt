Pizza Ranch: The Pizza Ranch, Inc., founded in 1981, is a "fast casual" restaurant chain.  Pizza Ranch offers pizza, chicken, a salad bar, and a pizza and chicken buffet.  Pizza Ranch has over 200 locations in Colorado, Illinois, Iowa, Kansas, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Montana, Nebraska, North Dakota, South Dakota, Wisconsin, and Wyoming.  Pizza Ranch is the largest regional pizza franchise in Iowa, Minnesota, North Dakota, and South Dakota.
