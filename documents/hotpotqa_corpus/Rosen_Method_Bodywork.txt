Rosen Method Bodywork: Rosen Method Bodywork (or Rosen Method) is a type of Complementary and alternative medicine.  This bodywork, described as "psycho-somatic", claims to help integrate one's bodily and emotional/mental experience while identifying unconscious patterns of muscular holding, feeling, and behavior.  The main theory underpinning this method is that a person protects themselves from past painful experiences through the body, separating one from one's true self.  This alleged protection is said to be experienced most frequently as chronic musculoskeletal pain and tension, and purportedly can be observed by the bodywork practitioners as restricted patterns of movement and posture, muscular tension, or shortness of breath.  Rosen Method Bodywork purports to integrate the body, mind, emotions and spirit; and unlock the unconscious.
