Halloween with the New Addams Family: Halloween with the New Addams Family is a 1977 American made-for-television comedy horror film starring John Astin, Carolyn Jones, Jackie Coogan, Ted Cassidy, Lisa Loring, Ken Weatherwax and Felix Silla, and was a reunion of sorts with the actors reprising their roles from the original 1964–66 series "The Addams Family".  Blossom Rock who portrayed Grandmama was ill at the time of the production (she would die in early 1978, shortly after this special aired) causing her role of Grandmama to be portrayed by (1913-1979).  Character actors Parley Baer and Vito Scotti, who both had recurring roles in the original series, also appeared in the movie, but as different characters than they had portrayed originally.
