Hao Kuih: Hao Kuih (Teochew dialect pronunciation) is a special snack originating in Shantou, Guangdong Province, China.  It is famous for its unique shape and delicious flavor.  For many years, it has been popular among overseas Chinese and local people.  First appearing in Chaoyang District in Shantou, Hao Kuih is generally not well known beyond the Chaoshan community.  Containing wisdom of the old generation, Hao Kuih is still a precious heritage for Chaoshan natives.
