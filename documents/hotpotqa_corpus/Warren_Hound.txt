Warren Hound: Warren Hound refers to the group of Mediterranean hare-hunting dogs.  In Spanish, this kind of breeds are called "podencos" and in Portuguese, "podengos".  Generally speaking, a warren hound has erect ears, a smooth (or in some cases, wire) coat and a whip-like tail.  However, there are some exceptions: for instance, the Cretan Hound has usually rose-ears and a curly or curved tail, while the Barrocal Algarvio is long-haired.  Unlike hounds or sighthounds which rely on just one sense, warren hounds tend to work with three senses: smell, sight and hearing.
