2013 Australian Manufacturers' Championship: The 2013 Australian Manufacturers' Championship was an Australian motor racing series for modified production touring cars.  It comprised two CAMS sanctioned national championship titles, the Australian Manufacturers’ Championship (for automobile manufacturers) and the Australian Production Car Championship (for drivers).  The 2013 Australian Manufacturers' Championship was the 28th manufacturers title to be awarded by CAMS and the 19th to be contested under the Australian Manufacturers' Championship name.  The 2013 Australian Production Car Championship was the 20th Australian Production Car Championship.  The Manufacturers title was awarded to Mitsubishi and the Australian Production Car Championship to Garry Holt.
