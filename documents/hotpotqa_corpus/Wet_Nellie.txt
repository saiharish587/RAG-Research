Wet Nellie: Wet Nellie is a custom-built submarine, created for the 1977 James Bond film "The Spy Who Loved Me" in the shape of a Lotus Esprit S1 sports car.  The Esprit was chosen to give James Bond a glamorous car to drive.  "Wet Nellie" is named in reference to Little Nellie, an autogyro featured in the James Bond film "You Only Live Twice".  Little Nellie was named after Nellie Wallace.
