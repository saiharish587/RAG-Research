Westchester Avenue (NYW&amp;B station): Westchester Avenue is a former railroad station located in the borough of the Bronx in New York City, partially suspended over Amtrak's busy Northeast Corridor line.  It was built in 1908 with rich terra cotta detailing to a design by Cass Gilbert, who would later employ similar terra cotta detailing in his 1910 design for the Woolworth Building.  Train service to the station ceased in 1937, and as of 2014 the station was a ruin in poor condition.
