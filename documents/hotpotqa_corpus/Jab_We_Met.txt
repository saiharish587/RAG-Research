Jab We Met: Jab We Met (English: "When We Met") is a 2007 Indian romantic drama film directed and written by Imtiaz Ali.  The film, produced by Dhillin Mehta under Shree Ashtavinayak Cinevision Ltd, stars Kareena Kapoor and Shahid Kapoor in their fourth film together with Dara Singh, Pavan Malhotra and Saumya Tandon in supporting roles.
