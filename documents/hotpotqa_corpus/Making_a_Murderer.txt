Making a Murderer: Making a Murderer is an American documentary television series that premiered on Netflix on December 18, 2015.  The ten-episode first season, written and directed by Laura Ricciardi and Moira Demos, explores the story of Steven Avery, a man from Manitowoc County, Wisconsin, who served 18 years in prison for the wrongful conviction of sexual assault and attempted murder of Penny Beerntsen, before being fully exonerated in 2003 by DNA evidence.  He filed a suit against the county on this case.  In 2005, Avery was arrested on charges of murdering Teresa Halbach, a local photographer, and was convicted in 2007.  The series also covers the arrest, prosecution, and conviction of Avery's nephew, Brendan Dassey, who was also charged in the murder, largely based on his confession under interrogation.
