Victorian Football Club (SAFA): The Victorian Football Club, renamed the North Adelaide Football Club in its final year, was an Australian rules football club based in Adelaide, South Australia.  Formed in 1874, the club finished second in the interclub competition in 1875 and won in 1876, becoming a founding member of the South Australian Football Association (SAFA) in 1877, sharing the competition's inaugural premiership with South Adelaide .  Having struggled to compete in its later years following an exodus of players, the club disbanded at the end of the 1884 season, having finished last in each of the preceding two seasons.
