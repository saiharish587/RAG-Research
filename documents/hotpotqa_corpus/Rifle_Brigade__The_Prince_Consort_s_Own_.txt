Rifle Brigade (The Prince Consort's Own): The Rifle Brigade (The Prince Consort's Own) was an infantry rifle regiment of the British Army.  Formed in January 1800 as the "Experimental Corps of Riflemen" to provide sharpshooters, scouts and skirmishers, they were soon renamed the "Rifle Corps".  In January 1803 they became an established regular regiment and were titled the 95th Regiment of Foot (Rifles).  In 1816, at the end of the Napoleonic Wars, they were again renamed, this time as the "Rifle Brigade".
