Kilmory Castle, Bute: Kilmory Castle (Kilmorie Castle, Crowner's Castle) is the remains of a 15th-century castle at Meikle Kilmory, Isle of Bute, Scotland.  The castle was the residence of the Jamiesons of Kilmorie, and was already a ruin in the 18th century.  The Jamiesons of Kilmorie were the hereditary coroners of Bute.  The ruins are a secular listed building.
