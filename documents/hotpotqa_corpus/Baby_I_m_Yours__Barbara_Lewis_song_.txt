Baby I'm Yours (Barbara Lewis song): "Baby I'm Yours" is a song written by Van McCoy, which was a hit in 1965 for Barbara Lewis, the original recording artist.  The song was featured in the 1995 film "The Bridges of Madison County" and was included on the soundtrack album.  It was also featured in the TV movies "The Midnight Hour" (1985) and "An American Crime" (2007).
