Bob Thomas (actor): Bob Thomas (born March 1, 1954 in Appalachia, Virginia ) is a radio personality, actor, and writer.  He was one of the top radio announcers in Knoxville, Tennessee for 25 years.  As an actor, he has appeared in many films and hundreds of commercials.  He wrote two episodes of the popular TV show "Lizzie McGuire".  In 2004, his voice was heard as "Slammin' Sammy" in the film "Friday Night Lights".
