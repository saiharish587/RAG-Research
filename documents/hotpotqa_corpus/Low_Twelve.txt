Low Twelve: Low Twelve (Low 12 or ↓2) is an American Heavy Metal band from Bloomington.  They typically play in a Thrash Metal style, and often take inspiration from historical figures and events for their music.  The Band currently consists of Founder/Lead Singer and Bassist Pete Altieri, Drummer Travis Waterman, Les Aldridge and Jeremy Meister on Guitars.  The band has released 3 full-length CDs("Flesh of the Weak", "Unfit for Human Occupancy", and "This Side Towards Enemy") and 2 demos, as well as being featured on many compilations and soundtracks.  They are currently in the process of recording their highly anticipated fourth CD "Splatter Pattern".
