Like a Baby: "Like a Baby" is a song written by Jesse Stone.  It was recorded by Vikki Nelson for a Vik records single in 1957.  It was also recorded by Elvis Presley for his 1960 album "Elvis Is Back! ".  James Brown and the Famous Flames recorded the song and released it as a single in 1963, which charted #24 R&B.  The single's B-side, an instrumental version of "Every Beat of My Heart", also charted, reaching #99 on the Billboard Hot 100.  Brown and the Flames performed "Like a Baby" on their 1964 live album "Pure Dynamite!  Live at the Royal".
