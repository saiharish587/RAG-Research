Kris Law: Kris Law, born Chin Chiang (or KLCC) is an internationally and critically acclaimed Malaysian film Artiste, first ASEAN commercial Actor to debut in world's biggest market (India), first Malaysian Actor to portray gay person in the media and world's first Internet viral video star celebrity.  Law has appeared in Malaysian films including" Anak Mami Kembali", "1957:Hati Malaya", "Talent House" and over 20 international commercials.  He has also been featured in television and stage productions.  A true polymath, Law's extensive artistic range and intellectual capabilities transcend beyond performance art, design, social media and authorship.  Law is the world's first person to appear in the Internet as a viral video star celebrity who successfully transitioned into an award-winning Actor with a diversified artistic career.
