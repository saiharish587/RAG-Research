Frederick II, Holy Roman Emperor: Frederick II (26 December 1194 – 13 December 1250; Sicilian: "Fidiricu II" , German: "Friedrich II" ) was a Holy Roman Emperor and King of Sicily in the Middle Ages, a member of the House of Hohenstaufen.  His political and cultural ambitions, based in Sicily and stretching through Italy to Germany, and even to Jerusalem, were enormous.  However, his enemies, especially the popes, prevailed, and his dynasty collapsed soon after his death.
