Strathlene Golf Course: Strathlene at Findochty, near Buckie, is one of the oldest golf courses in Scotland.  It was established in 1877 and is home to Strathlene Buckie Golf Club.  The ground was given to the town by Mr Bryson the factor of Seafield Estates at that time.  The original 9 holes were created between Portessie and Strathlene House, where 2 of the holes were situated in the area which is now the Caravan Site.  The original clubhouse was situated at the entrance to Portessie railway Station.  In 1936 the course was extended to 18 holes with the purchase of ground on the headland between Strathlene and the village of Findochty, and was designed by Mr George Smith of Lossiemouth.
