Sörskogen: Sörskogen was a Swedish progressive rock project Mikael Åkerfeldt of Opeth and Dan Swanö started for fun.  Both musicians are known for their wide musical interests.  The project was supposedly named after a small community near the Stockholm suburb Huddinge where Mikael practiced with his first band Eruption in the late 1980s.  At the Opeth concert on December 18, 2008, Mikael Åkerfeldt claimed to have played soccer for the Sörskogen IF soccer club.  Sörskogen is heavily influenced by 1970s prog rock bands, Camel to name but one.  Not much is known about the project, except a few references made by Mikael in interviews.
