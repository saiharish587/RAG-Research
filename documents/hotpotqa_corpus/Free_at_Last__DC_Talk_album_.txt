Free at Last (DC Talk album): Free at Last is the third studio album from DC Talk, which was released in 1992.  Music videos were filmed for "Jesus Is Just Alright", "The Hardway" and "Luv Is A Verb".  In 1994, DC Talk released a long-form video of the "Free at Last World Tour" entitled "Narrow is the Road", in which all three music videos can be seen.
