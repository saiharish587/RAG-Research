Evan Williams (rugby): Evan Williams (18 June 1906 — 18 November 1976 (aged 70)), born in Port Talbot, was a Welsh rugby union and professional rugby league footballer who played in the 1920s, and 1930s.  He played representative level rugby union (RU) for Wales and at club level for Aberavon RFC as a Centre as number 12 or 13 and played club level rugby league (RL) for Leeds, as a centre , or scrum-half/halfback , as number 3 or 4, 6, or 7, He died in Leeds.
