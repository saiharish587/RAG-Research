Vopak: Royal Vopak N.V. (Dutch: "Koninklijke Vopak" ) is a Dutch company that stores and handles various oil,chemicals, edible oils and natural gas-related products.  The company was created by the merger of Van Ommeren and Pakhoed in 1999.  In 2002, the distribution of oil and natural gas related products was split off; a new company Univar was created for that purpose.  The head office is located in Rotterdam.
