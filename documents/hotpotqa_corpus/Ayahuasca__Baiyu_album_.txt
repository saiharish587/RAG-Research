Ayahuasca (Baiyu album): Ayahuasca is a full length studio album by Chinese-American artist Baiyu released on December 1, 2013.  This is the artist's second full length project and is inspired by her psychedelic and soul exploratory experiences in the Amazon regions of Pucallpa, Peru.  This is her first effort to not only take full rein on songwriting for the entire project, but also a first for producing the instrumentals for two of the fourteen tracks.
