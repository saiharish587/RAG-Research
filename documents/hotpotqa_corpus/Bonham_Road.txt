Bonham Road: Bonham Road (, also 般含道) is a road in Mid-Levels, Hong Kong Island in Hong Kong.  The road is a main road connecting Pokfulam Road in the west, near the University of Hong Kong, and Caine Road in the east, at the junction with Hospital Road and Seymour Road.  It was named after Sir George Bonham, the third Governor of Hong Kong.  It was renamed Nishi-Taisho Dori (西大正通) during Japanese occupation of Hong Kong.
