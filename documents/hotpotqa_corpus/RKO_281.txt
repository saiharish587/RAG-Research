RKO 281: RKO 281 is a 1999 American historical drama film directed by Benjamin Ross and starring Liev Schreiber, James Cromwell, Melanie Griffith, John Malkovich, Roy Scheider and Liam Cunningham.  The film depicts the troubled production behind the 1941 film "Citizen Kane".  The film's title is a reference to the original production number of "Citizen Kane".
