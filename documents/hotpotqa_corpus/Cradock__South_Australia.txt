Cradock, South Australia: Cradock is a settlement 320 kilometres north of Adelaide on the Hawker to Orroroo Road in South Australia.  The nearest town with a greater population is Hawker which is approximately 20 km away with a population of around 360.  Cradock is in the Flinders Ranges Council area, the state Electoral district of Stuart and the federal Division of Grey.  The southern part of the locality is in the electoral district of Stuart and District Council of Orroroo Carrieton.
