Exit Tunes, Inc.: Exit Tunes, Inc. (エグジットチューンズ株式会社 ) is a Japanese media company that acts primarily as a music publisher.  The company was founded as a printing company in 2001, however later became a music company known as Quake Records in 2003.  In 2005, the company was renamed to Quake Holdings.  The company became a subsidiary of Pony Canyon in 2009 (later becoming a wholly owned subsidiary in 2014), and was renamed Exit Tunes in 2011.
