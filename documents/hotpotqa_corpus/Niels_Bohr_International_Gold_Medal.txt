Niels Bohr International Gold Medal: The Niels Bohr International Gold Medal is an international engineering award.  It has been awarded since 1955 for "outstanding work by an engineer or physicist for the peaceful utilization of atomic energy".  The medal is administered by the Danish Society of Engineers (Denmark) in collaboration with the Niels Bohr Institute and the Royal Danish Academy of Sciences.  It was awarded 10 times between 1955 and 1982 and again in 2013.  The first recipient was Niels Bohr himself who received the medal in connection with his 70th birthday.
