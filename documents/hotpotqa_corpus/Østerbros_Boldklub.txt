Østerbros Boldklub: Østerbros Boldklub (also known as ØB) was a Danish sports club based in the Østerbro area of Copenhagen, active from 1894 to 1998.  The club is best known for its amateur football team.  It was one of the oldest Danish football clubs, and played 10 seasons in the top-flight Danish football championship from 1928 to 1950.  ØB was a founding member of the Sports Confederation of Denmark (1896) and the Copenhagen Football Association (1903).  The football club predominantly played its games at the Fælledparken, a common shared by the smaller Copenhagen clubs.
