Making Memories of Us: "Making Memories of Us" is a song written by American country music artist Rodney Crowell that has been recorded by several artists.  The first version was recorded by American country music artist Tracy Byrd on his 2003 album "The Truth About Men".  One year later, Crowell and Vince Gill recorded the song as members of The Notorious Cherry Bombs, a side project comprising former members of Crowell's backing band.
