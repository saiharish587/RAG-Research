Happy Campers (2001 film): Happy Campers is a 2001 American dark comedy film written and directed by Daniel Waters, and starring Brad Renfro, Dominique Swain, Jaime King, Emily Bergl, and Justin Long.  The film focuses on a group of college freshmen and their experiences as summer camp counselors at the fictional Camp Bleeding Dove.  The film is collectively narrated by each of the subjective counselors.  It marks Waters' directorial debut, as well as the film debut of Jaime King.
