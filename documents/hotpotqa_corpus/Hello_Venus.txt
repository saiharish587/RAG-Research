Hello Venus: Hello Venus (; often stylized as HELLOVENUS) is a South Korean girl group formed by Tricell Media, a joint venture between Pledis Entertainment and Fantagio, in 2012.  The group originally consisted of six members: Alice, Nara, Lime, Yooara, Yoonjo, and Yooyoung.  They debuted with lead single "Venus" from their debut extended play, "Venus", on May 9, 2012.
