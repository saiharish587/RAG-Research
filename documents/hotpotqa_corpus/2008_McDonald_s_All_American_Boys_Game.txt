2008 McDonald's All-American Boys Game: The 2008 McDonald's All-American Boys Game was an All-star basketball game played on Wednesday, March 26, 2008 at the Bradley Center in Milwaukee, Wisconsin, home of the NBA's Milwaukee Bucks.  The game's rosters featured the best and most highly recruited high school boys graduating in 2008.  The game was the 31st annual version of the McDonald's All-American Game first played in 1978.
