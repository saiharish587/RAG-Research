Honor Society (band): Honor Society was a pop rock band that formed in New York, New York in 2006.  The band originally consisted of Michael Bruno (vocals/guitar), Jason Rosen (guitar/keyboard), Andrew Lee (bass), and Alexander Noyes (drums); however as of late 2012 Rosen is no longer a part of the band.  They were previously signed to Jonas Records in affiliation with Hollywood Records but parted ways in 2011.  The band released its debut album "Fashionably Late" on September 15, 2009.  The album debuted at No. 18 on the "Billboard" 200.  In October 2011, their second album, "A Tale of Risky Business: Part II", was released.
