American Idol: Greatest Moments: American Idol: Greatest Moments is the first "American Idol" soundtrack, with music from the first season of "American Idol".  It contains two cover songs from both Kelly Clarkson and Justin Guarini, the winner and runner-up of the original season, one song from the remaining eight finalists, one song by all the finalists, and Clarkson's two songs from her debut single as bonus tracks.  It is the only "American Idol" album that endorses the winner's single.  The album was released on October 1, 2002.
