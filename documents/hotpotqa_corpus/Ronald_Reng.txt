Ronald Reng: Ronald Reng is a German sports journalist and author.  Of his books, two have been translated to English and both of them have been honored with book awards in the UK.  "The Keeper of Dreams", the story of the German goalkeeper Lars Leese who ended up playing for Barnsley Football Club in the Premier League, won the "Sports Book of the Year Award" in 2004.  It was the first foreign book to win the award.  Reng's biography of the late German national goalkeeper Robert Enke, "" was voted William Hill Sports Book of the Year in 2011.  Reng was the first non-English speaking author in 23 years to win the award.
