Scott Gregory: Scott Chase Gregory (born July 31, 1959 in Auburn, New York) is an American former competitive ice dancer.  He competed at the 1984 Winter Olympics with Elisa Spitz.  He then paired with Suzanne Semanick, with whom he won the gold medal at the U.S. Figure Skating Championships twice and competed at the Olympics in 1988.  He retired from skating that year due to back injuries.
