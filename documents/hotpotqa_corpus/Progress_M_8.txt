Progress M-8: Progress M-8 was a Soviet unmanned cargo spacecraft which was launched in 1991 to resupply the Mir space station.  The twenty-sixth of sixty four Progress spacecraft to visit Mir, it used the Progress-M 11F615A55 configuration, and had the serial number 207.  It carried supplies including food, water and oxygen for the EO-9 crew aboard Mir, as well as equipment for conducting scientific research, and fuel for adjusting the station's orbit and performing manoeuvres.  It also carried the Naduvaniy Hazovoy Ballon satellite, which was subsequently deployed from Mir.
