Istanbul Şehir University: Istanbul Şehir University (Turkish: "İstanbul Şehir Üniversitesi" , literally City University of Istanbul) is a private, non-profit university located in Istanbul, Turkey.  It was established in 2008 by the Bilim ve Sanat Vakfı (BiSaV or BSV, English: Foundation for Science and Arts ).  The university started its education in the academic year of 2010-11 at its campus in Altunizade, Üsküdar, following a ceremony held on October 5, 2010 that was attended by then State President Abdullah Gül, Prime Minister Recep Tayyip Erdoğan and Minister of Foreign Affairs Ahmet Davutoğlu.
