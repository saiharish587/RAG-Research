Wallace Wilkerson: Wallace Wilkerson (c. 1834 – May 16, 1879) was an American stockman who was sentenced to death by the Territory of Utah for the murder of William Baxter.  Wilkerson professed his innocence, but chose to die by firing squad over hanging or decapitation.  The execution was botched; Wilkerson took up to 27 minutes to die because the firing squad missed his heart.
