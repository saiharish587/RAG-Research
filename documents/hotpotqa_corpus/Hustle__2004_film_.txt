Hustle (2004 film): Hustle is a television film about baseball player Pete Rose, created by ESPN Films.  It was first broadcast on September 25, 2004.  The movie follows Rose as he gambled on Major League Baseball while managing the Cincinnati Reds, then was caught and banned from baseball for life.  The title, "Hustle", is a reference to both Rose's gambling problem and his nickname, "Charlie Hustle."
