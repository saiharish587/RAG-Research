Frank Ocean: Christopher Francis "Frank" Ocean (born Christopher Edwin Breaux; October 28, 1987) is an American singer and songwriter.  Known for his idiosyncratic musical style, Ocean first embarked on a career as a ghostwriter, and in 2010 he became a member of hip hop collective Odd Future.  He released his breakout mixtape, "Nostalgia, Ultra", to critical acclaim in 2011.  It generated his first charting single "Novacane".  In 2012, Ocean finished in second place in BBC's Sound of 2012 poll.
