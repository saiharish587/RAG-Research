WYSE International: WYSE International is a worldwide educational charity specialising in the education and development of emerging leaders.  Established in 1989, it is a non-governmental organization associated with the United Nations Department of Public Information.  It is based in London and has branches in Italy, Brazil, Japan, the Netherlands and a worldwide network of alumni in more than 115 countries.  The organization is run on a volunteer basis and its trainers are professional psychologists, leadership development trainers, educators, coaches and business people.
