Judicature Act 1908: The Judicature Act is an Act of the New Zealand Parliament passed in 1908, to provide for a system of appellate courts.  It was largely repealed as of 1 March 2017 by the Senior Courts Act 2016 and other Acts as part of a Judicature modernisation package.  It will be repealed as a whole on 1 January 2018 when remaining provisions of the new Acts come into force.
