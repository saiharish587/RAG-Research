Celeste Aida: "Celeste Aida" ("Heavenly Aida") is a romanza from the first act of the opera "Aida," by Giuseppe Verdi.  It is preceded by the recitative Se quel guerrier io fossi! .  The aria is sung by Radamès, a young Egyptian warrior who wishes to be chosen as a Commander of the Egyptian army.  He dreams of gaining victory on the battlefield and also of the Ethiopian slave girl, Aida, with whom he is secretly in love.
