Rhinoceros Party of Canada (1963–93): The Rhinoceros Party (French: "Parti Rhinocéros") was a registered political party in Canada from the 1960s to the 1990s.  Operating within the tradition of political satire, the Rhinoceros Party's basic credo, their so-called primal promise, was "a promise to keep none of our promises".  They then promised outlandishly impossible schemes designed to amuse and entertain the voting public.
