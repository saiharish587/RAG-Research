Put Me Off at Buffalo: Put Me Off at Buffalo is a song by the vaudeville team of the Dillon Brothers, with lyrics by Harry Dillon and music by John Dillon.  It was first published in 1895, and also appeared in the play "A Trip to Chinatown".  After an initial period of popularity, the tune was revived in 1901 in connection with the Pan-American Exposition held in Buffalo.  The song's lyrics were planted in the lawn of Buffalo's city hall during the event.
