Water supply and sanitation in Burkina Faso: Water supply and sanitation in Burkina Faso are characterized by high access to water supply in urban areas, while access to an at least basic water sources in rural areas - where three quarters of the population live - remains relatively low.  An estimated one third of water facilities in rural areas are out of service because of a lack of maintenance.  Access to at least basic sanitation lags significantly behind access to water supply.
