Aamir Khan: Aamir Khan (] ; born Mohammed Aamir Hussain Khan on 14 March 1965) is an Indian film actor, director, and producer.  Through his career in Hindi films, Khan has established himself as one of the most popular and influential actors of Indian cinema.  He is the recipient of numerous awards, including four National Film Awards and eight Filmfare Awards, as well as an Academy Award nomination.  He was honoured by the Government of India with the Padma Shri in 2003 and the Padma Bhushan in 2010.
