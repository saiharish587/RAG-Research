Christopher Gibbs: Christopher Henry Gibbs (born 29 July 1938) is a British antiques dealer and collector who was also an influential figure in men's fashion and interior design in 1960s London.  He has been credited with inventing Swinging London, and has been called the "King of Chelsea" and "London's most famous antiques dealer".  The "New York Times" described him as a "man of infinite taste, judgment and experience, the one who introduced a whole generation to the distressed bohemian style of interior design."
