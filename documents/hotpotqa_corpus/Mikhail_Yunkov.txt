Mikhail Yunkov: Mikhail Yunkov (Russian: Михаил Юньков ; born February 16, 1986 in Voskresensk, USSR) is a Russian professional ice hockey forward who is currently playing for Avangard Omsk in the Kontinental Hockey League (KHL).  In the 2004 NHL Entry Draft, Mikhail was drafted 62nd overall in the 2nd round by the Washington Capitals.  He is the brother of Alexander Yunkov, who is also a professional ice hockey player.
