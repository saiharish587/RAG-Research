Bukit Kayu Hitam: Bukit Kayu Hitam is Kedah's main border town on the Malaysia–Thailand border.  It marks the end of the North–South Expressway and Malaysia Federal Route 1, the longest Malaysian road which runs from Johor Bahru, Johor in the south till the border at Bukit Kayu Hitam pass.  The checkpoint and duty-free shopping complex is located near the border.  Across the border to the north is the town of Ban Dan Nok in the district of Sadao, Songkhla province in Southern Thailand.  To its south in Malaysia is the town of Changlun.
