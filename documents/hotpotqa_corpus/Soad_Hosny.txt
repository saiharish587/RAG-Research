Soad Hosny: Soad Hosny (Arabic: سعاد حسنى‎ ‎  ] : January 26, 1943 – June 21, 2001) was an Egyptian actress born in Cairo.  She was known as the "Cinderella of Egyptian cinema" and one of the most influential actresses in the Middle East and the Arab world.  She ascended to stardom at the end of the 1950s, performing in more than 83 films between 1959 and 1991.  A majority of her films were shot in the 1960s and 1970s.  Her final screen appearance was in the 1991 film, "The Shepherd and the Women", directed by her ex-husband, Ali Badrakhan.
