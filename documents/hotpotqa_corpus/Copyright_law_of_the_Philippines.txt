Copyright law of the Philippines: A copyright is the legal protection extended to the owner of the rights in an original work.  Original work refers to every production in the literary, scientific, and artistic domains.  The Intellectual Property Office (IPOPHL), the leading agency responsible for handling the registration and conflict resolution of intellectual property rights enforce the copyright law.  IPOPHL was created by virtue of Republic Act No. 8293 or the Intellectual Property Code of the Philippines which took effect on January 1, 1998, under the presidency of Fidel V. Ramos.
