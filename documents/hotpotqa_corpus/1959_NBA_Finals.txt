1959 NBA Finals: The 1959 NBA World Championship Series was the championship series of the 1958–59 National Basketball Association season, and was the conclusion of the 1959 NBA Playoffs.  The best-of-seven series was played between the Western Division champion Minneapolis Lakers and the Eastern Division champion Boston Celtics.  It was Boston's third trip to the NBA Finals and Minneapolis's sixth.  The Boston Celtics swept the Lakers 4–0.  That was the start of the Celtics' 8 consecutive championships, from 1959–1966.  To date, this is the most recent time that an NBA team from Minnesota appeared in an NBA Finals, as well as the first of two times in NBA history that a team with a losing record made the NBA Finals (the other was in 1981).
