Newbold Verdon: Newbold Verdon is a village and civil parish in the county of Leicestershire, England.  The parish includes Newbold Heath to the north and Brascote to the south.  Originally an agricultural centre Newbold Verdon grew in size during the 1850s with the expansion of coal mining in the area.  That industry has now ceased leaving Newbold Verdon as primarily a commuter village.  The 2001 census recorded a population of 3,193, reducing to 3,012 at the 2011 census.
