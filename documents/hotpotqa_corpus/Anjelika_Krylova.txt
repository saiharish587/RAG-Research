Anjelika Krylova: Anjelika Alexeyevna Krylova (Russian: Анжелика Алексеевна Крылова ; born 4 July 1973) is a Russian retired ice dancer.  With partner Oleg Ovsyannikov, she is the 1998 Olympic silver medalist and two-time (1998, 1999) World champion.  She currently works as a coach and choreographer in Bloomfield Hills, Michigan.
