Forman Mills: Forman Mills, Inc. is a Pennsauken, New Jersey-based retail chain and department store with 35 stores, located in Philadelphia, Baltimore, Delaware, New Jersey, Washington DC, Chicago, Cleveland, Detroit, New York City and their suburbs.  They also operate a store at the Iverson Mall in Hillcrest Heights, Maryland.  It was begun by Richard Forman when he started selling items at the Columbus Farmers Market.  The chain is known for their low-priced designer clothing such as shirts, pants, shorts, capri pants, and hats.
