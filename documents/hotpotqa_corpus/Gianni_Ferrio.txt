Gianni Ferrio: Born in Vicenza, Ferrio studied at conservatories of Vicenza and Venice.  He started working at the end of the fifties, and was pretty active as a composer of film scores, signing about 120 sound-tracks especially for spaghetti westerns and commedie sexy all'italiana films.  His piece "One Silver Dollar", the main theme to Giorgio Ferroni's "Blood for a Silver Dollar" (1965), was later included in Quentin Tarantino's "Inglourious Basterds".
