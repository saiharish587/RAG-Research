Imran Khan (Indian actor): Imran Khan (] ; born Imran Pal 13 January 1983) is an American-born film actor, who appears in Hindi films.  He is the nephew of actor Aamir Khan and director-producer Mansoor Khan, and the grandson of director-producer Nasir Hussain.  He appeared as a child artist in the films "Qayamat Se Qayamat Tak" (1988) and "Jo Jeeta Wohi Sikander" (1992).
