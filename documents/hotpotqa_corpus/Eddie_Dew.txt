Eddie Dew: Eddie Dew (29 January 1909, Sumner, Washington- 6 April 1972, Burbank, California) was an American actor, film director, and television director.  As an actor, he is best remembered for his starring roles in B movie western films during the 1940s.  In the 1950s he became active in directing both for film and television, most notably for the television series "Sergeant Preston of the Yukon".
