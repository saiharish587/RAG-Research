The Infestation Hypothesis: "The Infestation Hypothesis" is the second episode of the fifth season of "The Big Bang Theory" that first aired on CBS on September 22, 2011.  It is the 89th episode overall.  In it, Sheldon (Jim Parsons) becomes worried when Penny acquires a new chair, while Leonard (Johnny Galecki) tries to make his long distance relationship with Priya work.  The episode was watched by nearly 12 million viewers in the U.S. and received mixed reviews.
