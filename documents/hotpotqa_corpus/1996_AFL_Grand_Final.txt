1996 AFL Grand Final: The 1996 AFL Grand Final was an Australian rules football game contested between the North Melbourne Football Club and the Sydney Swans, held at the Melbourne Cricket Ground in Melbourne on 28 September 1996.  It was the 100th annual Grand Final of the Australian Football League (formerly the Victorian Football League), staged to determine the premiers for the 1996 AFL season.  The match, attended by 93,102 people, was won by North Melbourne by a margin of 43 points, marking that club's third premiership victory.  North Melbourne were awarded a gold premiership cup instead of the usual silver in honor of the centenary grand final.
