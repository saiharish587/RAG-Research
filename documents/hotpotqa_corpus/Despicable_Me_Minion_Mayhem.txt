Despicable Me Minion Mayhem: Despicable Me Minion Mayhem is a 4-D computer-animated simulator ride attraction located at Universal Studios Florida, Universal Studios Hollywood, and Universal Studios Japan.  The attraction is based on Universal Studios and Illumination Entertainment's 2010 animated film "Despicable Me" and its franchise and employs the use of 3-D HD digital animation.  The attraction replaced Jimmy Neutron's Nicktoon Blast (Before Jimmy Neutron's Nicktoon Blast, that space was The Funtastic World of Hanna-Barbera) in Florida, in Hollywood, and in Japan.
