City of the Caesars: The City of the Caesars (Spanish Ciudad de los Césares), also variously known as "City of Patagonia", "the Wandering City", "Trapalanda" or "Trapananda", "Lin Lin" or "Elelín", is a mythical city of South America.  It was supposedly located somewhere in Patagonia, in a valley of the Andes between Chile and Argentina.  Despite being searched for during the colonization of South America, no evidence proves that it ever existed, although reports of it circulated for two hundred years.  In 1766 a Jesuit, Father José García Alsue, explored the area now part of Queulat National Park in Aysén Region, Chile, searching unsuccessfully for the City of the Caesars.
