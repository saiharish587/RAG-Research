Agnes Bugge: Agnes Bugge (born before 1417) was an English brewer.  Bugge is notable because she was a women who was running a brewery in the 15th/16th century.  Usually wives assisted their husbands and their contribution is never identified in the records.  In Agnes' case her husband was a draper and therefore it becomes clear that it was Agnes who brewed.  This is noted in 1419/20 when the brewers were in dispute with the City of London.  The brewers agreed to create a fighting fund and Stephen paid the largest contribution for his wife's brewery.  Idonea Hatton's husband also made a contribution but in her case they had the brewery jointly.  19 of the 24 brewery's involved included women but the Bugge's brewery appeared to the only one operated by a woman, although it was noted that 80% of the breweries were run in part by a married woman.  When Stephen died it becomes clear how the law saw the situation as Stephen had to leave Agnes' brewery to her.  Coincidentally it is interesting to note that the funds raised won the case against the city of London.  At that time the Lord Mayor of London was Dick Whittington.
