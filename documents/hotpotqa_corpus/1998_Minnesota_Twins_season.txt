1998 Minnesota Twins season: Like many Twins teams of its half-decade, the 1998 Minnesota Twins neither impressed nor contended.  The team finished with a 70-92 record, with subpar batting and pitching.  The season was not without its bright spots, as individual players had solid seasons and Hall of Fame designated hitter Paul Molitor announced his retirement at the end of the season.  Tom Kelly's team had plenty of lowlights, most notably David Wells' perfect game against the team on May 17 at Yankee Stadium.
