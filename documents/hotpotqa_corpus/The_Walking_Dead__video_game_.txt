The Walking Dead (video game): The Walking Dead (also known as The Walking Dead: The Game and The Walking Dead: Season One) is an episodic interactive drama graphic adventure survival horror video game developed and published by Telltale Games.  Based on "The Walking Dead" comic book series, the game consists of five episodes, released between April and November 2012.  It is available for Android, iOS, Kindle Fire HDX, Microsoft Windows, Mac OS X, PlayStation 3, PlayStation Vita, Xbox 360, PlayStation 4 and Xbox One.  The game is the first of "The Walking Dead" video game series published by Telltale.
