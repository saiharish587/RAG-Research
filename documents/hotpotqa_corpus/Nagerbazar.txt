Nagerbazar: Nagerbazar is a neighbourhood in north Kolkata, in Indian state of West Bengal.  It is flanked by Netaji Subhash Chandra Bose International Airport in the north, Dum Dum railway station which is a juncture of both Kolkata Circular Railway as well as the Kolkata suburban railway and Dum Dum metro station in the west, area of Baguiati and adjoining areas of VIP Road in east and the posh locality of Bangur Avenue and Laketown in south.  The area comes under the jurisdiction of South Dum Dum Municipality.  In 2012, a flyover was opened at Nagerbazar to decongest the heavy traffic on Jessore Road for airport bound traffic.
