Nadia Ali (singer): Nadia Ali (Urdu: ‎ , born 3 August 1980) is a Pakistani American singer-songwriter.  Ali gained prominence in 2001 as the frontwoman and songwriter of the band iiO after their first debut single "Rapture" reached No. 2 on the UK Singles Chart.  The song also charted across several countries in Europe.  Their 2006 single, "Is It Love? ", reached the top of the "Billboard" Hot Dance Club Play Chart.
