Vikrant Rai: Vikrant Rai (born January 1, 1986) is an Indian Actor known for his works in Indian TV Serials and Movies.  He starred in the Indian TV serials "Kkavyanjali" (2005 -2006) which aired on Star Plus and "Maayka"( 2007 -2009) and "Ghar Ki Lakshmi Betiyaan" (2006 - 2009) which both aired on Zee TV & later made his Bollywood debut in a leading role with film UVAA (2015).
