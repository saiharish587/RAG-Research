World Wrestling Alliance (Massachusetts): The World Wrestling Alliance (WWA, formerly known as World Wrestling Stars and WWA New England) was a New England-based American independent professional wrestling promotion located in Massachusetts, founded by former WWF referee Fred Sparta and his brother Mike in 1996.  Fred retired in the year 2000 and the company was taken over by Focal Point Consulting.  In October 2010, Mike Sparta inherited the company due to his longtime "partner" Bob Ambrose's death.  The company briefly changed its name to World Wrestling Stars in 2009, and then WWA New England a year later.  The promotion has been a part of the New England "indy scene" for over 15 years.  It has regularly featured "Attitude Era" stars from World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE) as well as the local talents of the New England wrestling scene.
