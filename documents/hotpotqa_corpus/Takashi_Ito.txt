Takashi Ito: Takashi Itō (born September 22, 1970) is a Japanese former welterweight kickboxer fighting out of Yamaki Gym in Setagaya, Tokyo.  He is the owner of kickboxing gym "TARGET", and a manager of promotion company "R.I.S.E." of kickboxing events.  He won the world title of WMAF at Junior middleweight.  He is Christian.
