Lathyrus aphaca: Lathyrus aphaca is a legume known as the yellow pea or yellow vetchling.  It is native to southern Europe, parts of Asia, and North Africa.  Some consider it to be a weed, particularly when in areas where it is an introduced species, including northern Europe and North America.  It acclimates best to dry places, such as sand, gravel, and chalk, and requires a well-drained habitat.  It is an annual herb producing yellow pea flowers just over a centimeter wide.
