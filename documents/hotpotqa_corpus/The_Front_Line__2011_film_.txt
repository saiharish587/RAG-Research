The Front Line (2011 film): The Front Line (; also known as "Battle of Highlands") is a 2011 South Korean war film directed by Jang Hoon, set during the 1953 ceasefire of the Korean War.  This is the third film by director Jang Hoon, after completing "Secret Reunion" and "Rough Cut".  It was selected as South Korea's submission to the 84th Academy Awards for Best Foreign Language Film, but did not make the final shortlist.  It also won four Grand Bell Awards, including Best Film.
