Chris Pine: Christopher Whitelaw Pine (born August 26, 1980) is an American actor.  He is known for playing James T. Kirk in the "Star Trek" reboot film series (2009–2016), Will Colson in "Unstoppable" (2010), Cinderella's Prince in "Into the Woods" (2014), Toby Howard in "Hell or High Water" (2016) and Steve Trevor in "Wonder Woman" (2017).
