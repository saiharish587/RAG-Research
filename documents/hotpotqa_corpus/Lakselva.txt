Lakselva: Lakselva (; Kven: "Lemmijoki" ) is a river in Finnmark county in northern Norway.  It begins in Karasjok Municipality runs north through Porsanger Municipality into the Porsangerfjorden, a fjord off of the Barents Sea.  The 103 km long river runs past the village of Lakselv.  The European route E06 highway runs along the river for much of its course.
