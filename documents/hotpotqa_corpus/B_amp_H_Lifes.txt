B&amp;H Lifes: B&H Lifes is a Japanese pet product supplier, best known for its non-alcoholic drinks for cats and dogs.  The late 2013 launch of their "wine" for cats became an international story and was carried by cat and wine columns and magazines.  Only one thousand of the 180mL cat wine bottles were put on sale for the 2013 Christmas season, at 399 yen (US$4) each.
