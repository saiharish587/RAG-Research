A Tribute to Bad Santa Starring Mike Epps: Jim Jones & Skull Gang Present A Tribute to Bad Santa Starring Mike Epps is a Christmas-themed hip hop collaborative studio album by American hip hop recording artist Jim Jones and American hip hop group Skull Gang.  The album was released on November 25, 2008, by Koch Records and Splash.  The album features guest appearances from members of Dipset and ByrdGang, respectively.
