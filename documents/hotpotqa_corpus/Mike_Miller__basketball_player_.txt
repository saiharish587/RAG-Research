Mike Miller (basketball player): Michael Lloyd Miller (born February 19, 1980) is an American professional basketball player who last played for the Denver Nuggets of the National Basketball Association (NBA).  He played college basketball for the University of Florida, and was selected by the Orlando Magic in the first round of the 2000 NBA draft.  He has also played for the Memphis Grizzlies, Minnesota Timberwolves, Washington Wizards and Miami Heat.  He was named the NBA Rookie of the Year in 2001, and the NBA Sixth Man of the Year in 2006.  Miller won back-to-back NBA championships with the Miami Heat in 2012 and 2013.  He is a swingman who is primarily a three-point specialist.
