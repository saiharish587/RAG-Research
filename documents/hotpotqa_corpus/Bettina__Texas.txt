Bettina, Texas: Bettina is a vanished community founded in 1847 by German immigrants as part of the Adelsverein colonization of the Fisher-Miller Land Grant in the U.S. state of Texas.  It was located on the banks of the Llano River in Llano County, and no trace of the settlement remains today.  The community was named after German artist and social activist Bettina von Arnim and was one of five attempted by the Darmstadt Forty.  It was also known as the "Darmstaedter Kolonie".  The community was sponsored by the Adelsverein, and founded on idealistic philosophies of European freethinkers of the day.  It is notable for the community's camaraderie and mutually respectful relations with local indigenous tribes.  Lack of a formal community framework caused Bettina to fail within a year of its founding.
