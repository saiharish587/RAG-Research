World Match Racing Tour: The World Match Racing Tour is an annual match racing tour and World Championship in the match racing event of sailing.  The World Match Racing Tour has "special event"-status from the International Sailing Federation.  The Tour awards over US$1.5 million in prize money, with points awarded at each event culminating in the crowning of the ISAF Match Racing World Champion.  A US$500,000 overall prize pool for the Championship is now on offer.
