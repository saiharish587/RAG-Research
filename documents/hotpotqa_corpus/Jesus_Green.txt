Jesus Green: Jesus Green is a park in the north of central Cambridge, England.  It is located north of Jesus College, hence the name.  Jesus Ditch runs along the southern edge Jesus Green.  On the northern edge of Jesus Green is the River Cam, with Chesterton Road (the A1303) on the opposite side.  To the east is Victoria Avenue and beyond that Midsummer Common, common land that is still used for grazing.  Victoria Avenue crosses the Cam at Victoria Bridge, connecting to Chesterton Road, at the northeastern corner of Jesus Green.
