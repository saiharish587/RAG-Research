Desperado (song): "Desperado" is a song by the American rock band Eagles.  It was written by Glenn Frey and Don Henley and appeared on the 1973 album "Desperado" as well as numerous compilation albums. Although the song was never released as a single, it is one of the group's best known songs and ranked No. 494 on "Rolling Stone"' s 2004 list of "The 500 Greatest Songs of All Time".
