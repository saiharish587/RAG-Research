Rajaraja Cholan: Rajaraja Cholan is a 1973 Tamil film about the life of the Chola king Rajaraja Chola.  The film has Sivaji Ganesan playing the title role.  This high-budget production was the first CinemaScope film to be released in Tamil. Famous violin artist Kunnakudi Vaidhyanathan composer of music for the movie.  Sivaji Ganesan's portrayal as Rajaraja Chola was critically acclaimed.
