Philippe Bouvard: Philippe Bouvard (born 6 December 1929 in Coulommiers) is a French television and radio presenter.  He was (from 1977 to 2014) the well-known host of the French radio program "Les Grosses Têtes" on Radio Luxemburg RTL, the TV program "Le Petit Théâtre de Bouvard" broadcast from 1982 to 1986 on Antenne 2 and the radio program Allo Bouvard, since 2014 on RTL.
