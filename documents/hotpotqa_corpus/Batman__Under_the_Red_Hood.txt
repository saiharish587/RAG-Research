Batman: Under the Red Hood: Batman: Under the Red Hood is a 2010 American animated superhero direct-to-video film produced by Warner Bros.  Animation and released by Warner Home Video.  It is the eighth feature in the DC Universe Animated Original Movies series.  It was released on July 27, 2010.  The film stars Bruce Greenwood as Bruce Wayne/Batman, Jensen Ackles as the Red Hood/Jason Todd, John DiMaggio as the Joker, Neil Patrick Harris as Nightwing/Dick Grayson, Jason Isaacs as Ra's al Ghul, and Wade Williams as Black Mask.  The screenplay was written by Judd Winick, who also wrote the "" run in the monthly "Batman" comic.As in the comic, it focuses on Batman dealing with the return of his former apprentice, the second Robin, Jason Todd , who now goes by the moniker of Red Hood, a murderous vigilante and the former alias of Batman's archenemy , The Joker , on whom Jason is trying to exact revenge.
