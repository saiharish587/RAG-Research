Liquid Shard: Liquid Shard is a 2016 art installation by Patrick Shearn of Poetic Kinetics.  The installation was on display in Pershing Square in Los Angeles, California from July 28 through August 11, 2016.  Shearn came up with the idea after being approached by the Los Angeles Recreation and Parks Department and Now Art LA with the proposal to create an art installation for Pershing Square.
