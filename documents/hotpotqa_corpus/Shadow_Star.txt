Shadow Star: Shadow Star, known in Japan as Narutaru (Japanese: なるたる ) , is a Japanese manga series created by Mohiro Kitoh, originally serialized in Kodansha's seinen magazine "Afternoon".  The Japanese name is an abbreviation of "Mukuro Naru Hoshi, Tama Taru Ko" (骸なる星 珠たる子 ) , which roughly translates to "Corpse of a Star; A Precious Child".  In the United States, it was licensed by Dark Horse and serialized in "Super Manga Blast! ".
