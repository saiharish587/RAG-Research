Jenn Brown: Jennifer Lynne "Jenn" Brown (born March 23, 1981) is an American sports broadcaster and television host.  A former Division I Collegiate Athlete for the University of Florida, she is often on the sidelines of the biggest college football and baseball games for ESPN.  She was the first female correspondent for Inside the NFL and can be regularly seen covering both boxing and MMA.  She spent eight years as a reporter and host for ESPN and recently sidelined the show "American Ninja Warrior".
