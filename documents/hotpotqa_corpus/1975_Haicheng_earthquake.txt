1975 Haicheng earthquake: The 1975 Haicheng earthquake hit Haicheng, Liaoning in China at 19:36 CST on February 4.  The earthquake registered at 7.3 on the Richter Scale, which is associated with total destruction of infrastructure and property.  Haicheng had approximately 1 million residents at the time of the earthquake, which is known for being one of the few earthquakes to be successfully predicted throughout history.
