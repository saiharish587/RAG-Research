Blancpain GT Series Sprint Cup: The Blancpain GT Series Sprint Cup, formerly the FIA GT Series in 2013 and the Blancpain Sprint Series in 2014 and 2015, is a sports car racing series organized by the Stéphane Ratel Organisation (SRO) with the approval of the Fédération Internationale de l'Automobile (FIA).  The championship is mostly concentrated in Europe, but will visit other continents including Asia.  The series continues the sprint format for GT-cars carried out by the defunct FIA GT1 World Championship.
