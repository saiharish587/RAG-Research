Shards of Honor: Shards of Honor is an English language science fiction novel by Lois McMaster Bujold, first published in June 1986.  It is a part of the Vorkosigan Saga, and is the first full-length novel in publication order.  "Shards of Honor" is paired with Bujold's 1991 "Barrayar" in the omnibus "Cordelia's Honor" (1996).
