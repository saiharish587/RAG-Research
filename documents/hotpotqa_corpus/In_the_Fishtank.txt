In the Fishtank: In the Fishtank is an ongoing project of Konkurrent, an independent music distributor in the Netherlands.  In this project, Konkurrent invites one or two bands to record and gives them two days studio time.  The first four albums were recorded by individual bands, but eight of the last ten releases were the result of two bands (three in one case) teaming up to record.  The Ex is so far the only band to appear on more than one album in the series.
