Provincetown Playhouse: The Provincetown Playhouse is a historic theatre at 133 MacDougal Street between West 3rd and West 4th Streets in the Greenwich Village neighborhood of Manhattan, New York City.  It is named for the Provincetown Players, who converted the former bottling plant into a theater in 1918.  The original players were Eugene O’Neill, Edna St. Vincent Millay and Djuna Barnes.  Paul Robeson performed at the theatre, and E. E. Cummings had his plays performed in the building.  Bette Davis and Claudette Colbert made their New York stage debuts in the facility.
