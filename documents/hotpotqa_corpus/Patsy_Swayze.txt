Patsy Swayze: Yvonne Helen "Patsy" Swayze (nee Karnes; February 7, 1927 – September 16, 2013) was an American film choreographer, dancer, and dance instructor.  Her credits included the choreography for "Urban Cowboy", "Liar's Moon" and "Hope Floats".  Her five children included the actors Patrick Swayze and Don Swayze.  She has been credited with having trained Patrick in dance, leading to his starring role in "Dirty Dancing" in 1987.
