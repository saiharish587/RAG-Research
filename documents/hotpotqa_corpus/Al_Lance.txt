Al Lance: Al Lance (born Alan George Lance; April 27, 1949) is a retired Judge of the United States Court of Appeals for Veterans Claims.  Upon his retirement, Judge Lance assumed senior status as a recall-eligible retired judge.  He joined the Court of Appeals on December 16, 2004, after being nominated by President George W. Bush.  Prior to that, Lance served as the National Commander of The American Legion, from 1999 to 2000.  He served as the 31st Attorney General of Idaho from 1995 to 2003 and was a member of the Idaho House of Representatives from 1990 to 1994.
