Black and Greene Records: Black and Greene Records is an independent record label that was founded in 2004 by David Greene and Jeremy Black (Apollo Sunshine).  Black & Greene Records is located, run and operated out of Los Angeles, California and San Francisco.  The label works exclusively with Coyote Hearing Studios co-run by Jeremy Black.  The label's bands have been showcased on HBO's Eastbound & Down and 90210 as well as various other television, film and commercial projects.  In 2010, the label has begun recording the 3rd Drug Rug album as well as adding additional acts to its roster.
