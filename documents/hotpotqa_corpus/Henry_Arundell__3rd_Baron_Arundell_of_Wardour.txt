Henry Arundell, 3rd Baron Arundell of Wardour: Henry Arundell, 3rd Baron Arundell of Wardour, PC (bef.  23 February 1607/828 December 1694) was a Peer of England during the 17th century, and the most famous of the Lords Arundell of Wardour.  He served as Lord Privy Seal and Lord High Steward, and was appointed to the Privy Council.  During the Popish Plot he suffered a long period of imprisonment, although he was never brought to trial.
