GoldenEye: GoldenEye (1995) is the seventeenth spy film in the "James Bond" series, and the first to star Pierce Brosnan as the fictional MI6 officer James Bond.  The film was directed by Martin Campbell and is the first in the series not to take story elements from the works of novelist Ian Fleming.  The story was conceived and written by Michael France, with later collaboration by other writers.  In the film, Bond fights to prevent an ex-MI6 agent, gone rogue, from using a satellite against London to cause a global financial meltdown.
