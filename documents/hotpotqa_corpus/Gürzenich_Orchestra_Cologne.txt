Gürzenich Orchestra Cologne: The Gürzenich Orchestra Cologne (Gürzenich Orchester Köln) is a German symphony orchestra based in Cologne ("Köln").  On some recordings, the orchestra goes under the name "Gürzenich-Orchester Kölner Philharmoniker".  Its name comes from its past principal concert venue, the Gürzenich concert hall in Cologne.  Currently, its primary concert venue is the "Kölner Philharmonie" (Cologne Philharmonic Hall).
