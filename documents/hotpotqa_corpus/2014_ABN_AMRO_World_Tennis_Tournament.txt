2014 ABN AMRO World Tennis Tournament: The 2014 ABN AMRO World Tennis Tournament (or Rotterdam Open) was a men's tennis tournament played on indoor hard courts.  It took place at the Ahoy Rotterdam arena in the Dutch city of Rotterdam, between 10–16 February 2014.  It was the 41st edition of the Rotterdam Open, whose official name is the ABN AMRO World Tennis Tournament.  The competition was part of the ATP World Tour 500 series of the 2014 ATP World Tour.  Third-seeded Tomáš Berdych won the singles title.
