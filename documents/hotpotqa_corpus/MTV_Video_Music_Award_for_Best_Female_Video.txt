MTV Video Music Award for Best Female Video: The MTV Video Music Award for Best Female Video is one of the original general awards that has been handed out every year since the first annual MTV Video Music Awards in 1984.  In 2007, however, the award was briefly renamed Female Artist of the Year, and it awarded the artist's whole body of work for that year rather than a specific video.  In 2008, though, the award returned to its original name.  The category would become defunct beginning with the 2017 ceremony after the gender specific categories would be merged into the Artist of the Year category.
