Rejseholdet: Rejseholdet (English: "Mobile Unit" [lit.  "The Travel Team"] ; international title: Unit One) is a Danish television crime series starring Charlotte Fich, Mads Mikkelsen and Lars Brygmann.  Produced by Danmarks Radio (DR), the program aired 32 episodes spanning four seasons from 2000 to 2004.  Each episode revolved around an elite mobile police task force called "Unit One" that travels around Denmark helping local police solve crimes.  Cases portrayed in the show were loosely based upon actual incidents of sensational crimes such as murders, kidnappings, cross-border sex traffic and child pornography.  "Rejseholdet" won the 2002 International Emmy Award for best drama series.
