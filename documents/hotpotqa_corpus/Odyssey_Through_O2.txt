Odyssey Through O2: Odyssey Through O is an album by Jean-Michel Jarre, released in 1998.  It contains remixes of tracks from Jean Michel Jarre's "Oxygène 7–13" album, as well as the "Rendez-Vous 98" single.  It also contains a computer program, JArKaos, a scaled down version of the ArKaos software used by Jarre to produce visual accompaniment to his music at concerts.  JArKaos allows users to manipulate visuals using their computer keyboard while listening to the album.
