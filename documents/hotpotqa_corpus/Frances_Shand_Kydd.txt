Frances Shand Kydd: Frances Ruth Roche Shand Kydd (20 January 1936 – 3 June 2004) was the mother of Diana, Princess of Wales.  Her biographer, Max Riddington, who was the writer of "Frances: The Remarkable Story of Princess Diana's Mother", described Shand Kydd as a woman who was "certainly complicated" and also "funny, warm, intelligent, and energetic."  Following her divorce from Viscount Althorp in 1969, and Diana's death in 1997, Shand Kydd devoted the final years of her life to Roman Catholic charity work.
