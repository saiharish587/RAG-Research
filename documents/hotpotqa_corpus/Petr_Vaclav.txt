Petr Vaclav: Vaclav was born in Prague and graduated from .  His short documentary "Paní Le Murie" ("Madame Le Murie") of 1993 was nominated for FAMU's Student Academy Award and won a prize for best documentary at the Internationales Festival der Filmhochschulen (Filmschoolfest) in Munich.  Vaclav´s first feature film, "Marian" (1996) won the Silver Leopard and FIPRESCI Award at the Locarno International Film Festival and other prizes at film festivals in Angers, Thessaloniki, Belfort, Cottbus, Bratislava and Tehran.  His second feature, "Paralelní světy" ("Parallel Worlds") of 2001 was written in collaboration with the French screenwriter Marie Desplechin and selected for presentation at the San Sebastián International Film Festival.
