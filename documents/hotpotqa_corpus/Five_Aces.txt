Five Aces: Five Aces is a 1999 black comedy/drama film written, produced and directed by David Michael O'Neill.  It stars Charlie Sheen, Christopher McDonald, Aimée Leigh, Jeff Cesario, Michael McGrady, Tia Carrere, Geoffrey Lewis, Virginia Hamilton, Matt Clark, Hannes Jaenicke with John LaMotta and Elizabeth Morehead.  It is one of the films where Charlie Sheen is credited as Charles Sheen.
