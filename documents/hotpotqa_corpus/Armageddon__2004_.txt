Armageddon (2004): Armageddon (2004) was the fifth annual Armageddon professional wrestling pay-per-view (PPV) event produced by World Wrestling Entertainment (WWE).  It was presented by PlayStation, and took place on December 12, 2004, at the Gwinnett Center in the Atlanta suburb of Duluth, Georgia.  Nine professional wrestling matches were scheduled on the event's card.  The event featured wrestlers and other talent from WWE's SmackDown!  brand.  It was the fifth annual Armageddon event.  Professional wrestling is a type of sports entertainment in which theatrical events are combined with a competitive sport.  The buildup to the matches and the scenarios that took place before, during, and after the event were planned by WWE's script writers.  The event starred wrestlers from the SmackDown brand: storyline expansions of the promotion where employees are assigned to wrestling brands under the WWE banner.
