Go dynasty: Go Royal Family (Reigned from 37 BC to 668 AD) was the dynasty that founded and ruled over the ancient Korean kingdom of Goguryeo.  Its founder, Jumong (Hangul: 주몽 ; Hanja: 朱蒙 ), broke away from another ancient Korean kingdom called Dongbuyeo to start his own kingdom.  The Taewangs were all members of the Go Royal Family.
