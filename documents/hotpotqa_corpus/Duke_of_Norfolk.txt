Duke of Norfolk: The Duke of Norfolk is the premier Duke in the peerage of England, and also, as Earl of Arundel, the premier Earl.  The Duke of Norfolk is, moreover, the Earl Marshal and hereditary Marshal of England.  The seat of the Duke of Norfolk is Arundel Castle in Sussex, although the title refers to the county of Norfolk.  The current Duke of Norfolk is Edward Fitzalan-Howard, 18th Duke of Norfolk.  The dukes have historically been Catholic, a state of affairs known as recusancy in England.
