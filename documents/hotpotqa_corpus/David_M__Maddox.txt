David M. Maddox: David M. Maddox (born April 5, 1938) is a retired United States Army four-star general who served as Commander in Chief, United States Army Europe/Commander, Central Army Group (CINCUSAREUR/COMCENTAG) from 1992 to 1993; Commander in Chief, U.S. Army Europe (CINCUSAREUR) from 1993 to 1994.  He commanded the 2nd Armored Cavalry Regiment from 1981 to 1983.  He is a 1960 graduate of Virginia Military Institute.  He received his MS in Applied Science (Operations Research) from Southern Illinois University in 1969.
