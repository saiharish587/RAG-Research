Dan Flannery: Arthur Daniel Flannery (born March 10, 1944) is an American actor from Evanston, Illinois.  He has played roles in several movies, short films, and T.V. shows from a variety of genres including drama, action, and thriller.  His filmography includes The Straight Story (1999), and Contagion (2011) and T.V. series such as Empire (2015 TV series) and Boss (2011-2012).
