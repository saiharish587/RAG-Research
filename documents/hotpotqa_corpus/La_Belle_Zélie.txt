La Belle Zélie: Portrait of Madame Aymon, La Belle Zélie is an 1806 oil on canvas painting by the French artist Jean-Auguste-Dominique Ingres.  The painting is one of Ingres' early painted portraits, completed just before his first stay in Rome.  It first came to public notice during a 1867 Ingres exhibition in Paris, and was acquired by the Musée des Beaux-Arts de Rouen in 1870.
