Kato (producer): Kato (born Christopher Ju, December 25, 1986) is an American record producer.  Originally hailing from Fairfax, Virginia, in 2006, he moved to Atlanta where he currently resides.  Notable artists he has worked with includes Dizzy Wright, Hopsin, Jarren Benton, Roscoe Dash, Rittz, Lil' Scrappy, Kalenna (Diddy Dirty Money), as well as numerous upcoming independent artists like Dumbfoundead, and Jarren Benton.  His name 'Kato' comes from the 1960s television show The Green Hornet, where Bruce Lee plays the role of Kato.  In September 2013, he signed with American rapper Hopsin's record label Funk Volume as an in-house producer.
