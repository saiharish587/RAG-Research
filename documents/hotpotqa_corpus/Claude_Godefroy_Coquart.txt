Claude-Godefroy Coquart: Claude-Godefroy Coquart (February 2, 1706 – July 4, 1765) was a Jesuit priest who probably arrived in Quebec in 1739.  He was almost immediately assigned to accompany La Vérendrye to the western forts.  He was to replace Father Jean-Pierre Aulneau who had lost his life in the massacre on Lake of the Woods in 1736.
