Manhattan (soundtrack): Manhattan is the original motion picture soundtrack to Woody Allen's 1979 American romantic comedy-drama film, "Manhattan", composed by George Gershwin.  It was performed by the New York Philharmonic under Zubin Mehta and the Buffalo Philharmonic Orchestra under Michael Tilson Thomas.  The soundtrack works supremely well with the film and is equally effective without the film.  Sony BMG Music Entertainment released the soundtrack in 1979.  It was nominated for Best Soundtrack in the 33rd British Academy Film Awards.
