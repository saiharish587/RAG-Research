Stand (Poison song): "Stand" is a song recorded by American band Poison, written by Richie Kotzen prior to joining the band.  It was the 1st single from their 1993 "Native Tongue" album.  The song reached number 15 on the Mainstream rock chart, #35 on the top 40 mainstream chart and #50 on the "Billboard" Hot 100.  The song also charted at number 25 on the UK Singles chart, it was also the first Poison single and music video to feature lead guitarist Richie Kotzen.  The song fuses elements of country rock and gospel music; the album version includes the Los Angeles First A.M.E. Church choir on backing vocals.
