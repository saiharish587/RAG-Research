Mikhail Krichman: Mikhail Krichman (Михаил Владимирович Кричман; born 1967) is a Russian cinematographer who received a Golden Osella award at the 67th Venice Film Festival for "Silent Souls".  He photographed all of Andrey Zvyagintsev's films, including "The Return" (2003), "The Banishment" (2007), "Elena" (2010) and "Leviathan" (2014).  Zvyagintsev claims that Krichman (an engineer by profession) learned his craft by reading "American Cinematographer".  Krichman also shot "Miss Julie" for Norwegian director Liv Ullmann.
