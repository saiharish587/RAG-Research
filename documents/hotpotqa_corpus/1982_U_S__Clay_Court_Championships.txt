1982 U.S. Clay Court Championships: The 1982 U.S. Clay Court Championships was a men's Grand Prix and women's Toyota Series tennis tournament held in Indianapolis in the United States and played on outdoor clay courts.  It was the 14th edition of the tournament and was held from August 2 through August 8, 1982.  Fifth-seeded José Higueras and top-seeded Virginia Ruzici won the singles titles.
