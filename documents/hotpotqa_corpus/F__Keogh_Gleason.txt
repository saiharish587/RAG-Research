F. Keogh Gleason: Francis Keogh Gleason (April 14, 1906 – December 18, 1982) was a resident set decorator at MGM studios for over 40 years.  In that time he won 4 Academy Awards (for "An American in Paris" in 1951, "The Bad and the Beautiful" in 1952, "Somebody Up There Likes Me" in 1956 and "Gigi" in 1958) and was nominated an additional 3 times.  Gleason graduated from the Minnesota School of Fine Arts (now known as the Minneapolis College of Art and Design) in 1927.
