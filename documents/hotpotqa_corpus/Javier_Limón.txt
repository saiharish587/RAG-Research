Javier Limón: Javier Limón (born 1973) is a record producer, singer and songwriter born and raised in Madrid, Spain.  Limón has worked with several artists, mainly from Spain, since he incorporates elements of flamenco rhythm to his work.  Besides his work as producer has also dabbled as an interpreter and has released three studio albums, "Limón", "Son de Limón" and "Mujeres de Agua".  Since 2003, Limón has done recordings in Israel, Bogotá, Bristol, Paris, Bahía Blanca, Buenos Aires, New York City and Morocco.  In 2004, Limón was awarded the Latin Grammy Award for Producer of the Year for his work on "Lágrimas Negras" by Diego El Cigala and Bebo Valdés, "Cositas Buenas" by Paco de Lucía, "El Cantante" by Andrés Calamaro, "El Pequeño Reloj" by Enrique Morente, " Niño Josele" by Niño Josele and "Tributo Flamenco A Don Juan Valderrama" by Various Artists.  Javier Limón serves as the Artistic Director of the Berklee College of Music's Mediterranean Music Institute, which operates in Valencia, Spain as well as Boston, Massachusetts.
