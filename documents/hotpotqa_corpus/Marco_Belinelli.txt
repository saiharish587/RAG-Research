Marco Belinelli: Marco Stefano Belinelli (] ; born March 25, 1986) is an Italian professional basketball player for the Atlanta Hawks of the National Basketball Association (NBA).  He was selected 18th overall in the 2007 NBA draft by the Golden State Warriors.  In 2014, he won the NBA championship with the San Antonio Spurs, becoming the first Italian player to achieve such a feat.  He also won the NBA Three-Point Shootout during the 2014 NBA All-Star Weekend.  He also played the 2007, 2011, 2013, 2015 and 2017 editions of the FIBA EuroBasket and the 2006 FIBA World Championship with the Italian national basketball team.
