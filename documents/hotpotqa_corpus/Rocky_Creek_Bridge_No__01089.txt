Rocky Creek Bridge No. 01089: Rocky Creek Bridge No. 01089, also known as Ben Jones Bridge, is a concrete highway bridge spanning Rocky Creek along the Pacific Ocean coast of the U.S. state of Oregon.  The bridge crosses a gorge near the creek's mouth, about 2 mi south of Depoe Bay in Lincoln County.  Built in 1927, the bridge originally carried U.S. Route 101 (the Oregon Coast Highway) over the creek.  After a stretch of the highway was relocated to make it straighter, the abandoned piece of the old highway became Otter Crest Loop Road, a bypass west of the new highway.
