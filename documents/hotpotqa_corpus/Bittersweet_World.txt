Bittersweet World: Bittersweet World is the third studio album by American recording artist Ashlee Simpson.  It was first released in Australia on April 19, 2008, with a release in the United States following on April 22.  The project serves as her third and final release through Geffen Records.  Simpson first spoke of a third album following her run as Roxie Hart in the musical "Chicago" in 2006.  Work on the project officially began in 2007, with Geffen announcing an October release date.  The album was initially titled "Color Outside the Lines", taken from a line in the song "Rule Breaker".  Simpson claimed in November 2007 that she had finished the album, though she continued to write new material.
