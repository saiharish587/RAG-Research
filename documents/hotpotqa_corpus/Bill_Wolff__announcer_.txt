Bill Wolff (announcer): Bill Wolff is a staff announcer for WNBC and the NBC network.  He is best known for announcing the soap opera "Another World" from 1964 until 1987.  He announced the "NBC Nightly News" in 2015, when Brian Williams was suspended.  In June 2015, Wolff became permanent announcer for "NBC Nightly News", replacing Michael Douglas.
