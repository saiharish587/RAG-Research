Larry Gatlin: Larry Wayne Gatlin (born May 2, 1948) is an American country and Southern gospel singer and songwriter.  As part of a trio with his younger brothers Steve and Rudy, he achieved considerable success within the country music genre, performing on thirty-three Top 40 singles (combining his solo recordings and those with his brothers).  As their fame grew, the band became known as Larry Gatlin & the Gatlin Brothers.
