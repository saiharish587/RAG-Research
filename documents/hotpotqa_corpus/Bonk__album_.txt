Bonk (album): Bonk is the debut studio album by Australian rock band Big Pig.  It was released in March 1988 on White Label Records.  The album went gold, then platinum in Australia with three top-twenty singles ("Hungry Town", "Breakaway" and "Big Hotel").  The album was released in America by A&M Records in 1988, and the music video for "Breakaway" was played on MTV.  "Breakaway" was featured on the "Bill & Ted's Excellent Adventure" soundtrack and "Hungry Town" on the "Young Einstein" soundtrack.  "Breakaway" and "Money God" were used in the "Miami Vice" TV series in the 1980s.  "Breakaway" was also featured in Season 1, Episode 4 of "Glitch (TV series)".
