Sprite (drink): Sprite is a colorless, caffeine-free, lemon and lime-flavoured soft drink created by The Coca-Cola Company.  It was first developed in West Germany in 1959 as Fanta Klare Zitrone (“Clear Lemon Fanta”) and was introduced in the United States under the current brand name Sprite in 1961 as a competitor to 7 Up.  Bottles of Sprite are usually a transparent green colour with a green and yellow label whereas cans are coloured silver, green, blue and aluminum bottles are coloured a solid lime green.  Though often confused with Lemonade, Sprite stands in separate class of carbonated soft drink.
