Hugpatch Records: Hugpatch Records is a record label based out of Brooklyn, New York.  It was founded in 2006 by Maxwell Williams.  It was originally a 7" single only record label, which gives it the reputation as "the world's tiniest record label."  Its releases are limited to 500 copies of each record.  Releases include singles by Brooklyn-based indie-pop band The Besties (HP01), Barcelona-based pop group Nosoträsh (HP02) and Austin, Texas-based minimal pop trio Yellow Fever (HP03).  In 2007, Hugpatch was one of the organizers of the pop music festival, NYC Popfest.  Later on, the label began releasing full-length albums, beginning with The Besties' Home Free (HP08), originally released on CD, and later as a vinyl LP.  The label also produces an internet radio show for the American Apparel radio station, Viva-Radio. com.
