Roderich von Erckert: Roderich von Erckert (15 December 1821 – 12 December 1900) was a German ethnographer and officer.  His work on the Caucasian languages includes "Der Kaukasus und seine Völker" (The Caucasus and Its Peoples; 1887); "Die Sprachen des kaukasischen Stammes" (The Languages of the Caucasian Tribes; 1895); and "Wanderungen und Siedelungen der germanischen Stämme in Mitteleuropa" (Migration and Settlement of the Germanic Tribes in Central Europe; 1901).
