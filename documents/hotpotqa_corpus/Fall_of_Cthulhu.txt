Fall of Cthulhu: Fall of Cthulhu is a 2007 American comic book series, written by Michael Alan Nelson and published in 2007 by Boom!  Studios.  Creatively influenced by H.P. Lovecraft's Cthulhu Mythos, particularly "The Call of Cthulhu", the main characters of the series find themselves swept up in an ancient and elaborate plot, designed by the dark god Nyarlathotep to provoke an apocalyptic war between powerful beings known as the Great Old Ones.
