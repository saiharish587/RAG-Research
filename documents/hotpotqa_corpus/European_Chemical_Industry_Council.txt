European Chemical Industry Council: The European Chemical Industry Council or Cefic (from its former French name "Conseil Européen des Fédérations de l'Industrie Chimique") is the main European trade association for the chemical industry.  It was founded in 1959, and its history follows through the creation of the European Union.  Its headquarters are located in Brussels.  Cefic represents 29,000 large, medium and small chemical companies in Europe, interacting on behalf of their members with international and EU institutions, non-governmental organisations, the international media, and other stakeholders.  Chemicals companies in the European Union employ a total staff of about 1.2 million.
