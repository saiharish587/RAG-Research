Gessekai: "Gessekai" (月世界) (English: "Moon World") is the fifteenth single released by the Japanese rock band Buck Tick, released on May 13, 1998.  The song "Gessekai" was used as opening theme of the anime "".  Track three is a remix of "Muchi no Namida" from "Sexy Stream Liner" done by Tomoyasu Hotei.
