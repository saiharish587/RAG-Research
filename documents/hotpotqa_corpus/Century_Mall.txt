Century Mall: Century Mall was an indoor shopping mall located on the southeast corner of Broadway (Indiana 53) and US 30 in Merrillville, Indiana.  The mall opened in 1979 with anchor stores Goldblatt's and Montgomery Ward.  Competitive and economic factors sent the mall into steady decline and national retail chains left throughout the 1990s until the mall was ultimately sold for redevelopment in 2001 then closed and largely demolished in 2002.  The mall's interior stores also suffered following Montgomery Ward's remodeling.  Montgomery Ward removed their mall entrance and told customers that no stores remained, when in reality close to a dozen stores were still open.  A strip mall development named Century Plaza now occupies the land and some of the former anchor store buildings.
