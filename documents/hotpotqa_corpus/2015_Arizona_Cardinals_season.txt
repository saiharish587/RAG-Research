2015 Arizona Cardinals season: The Arizona Cardinals season was the franchise's 117th season, 96th season in the National Football League and the 28th in Arizona.  It was also the team's 10th season playing their home games at University of Phoenix Stadium and the third under head coach Bruce Arians.  The Cardinals clinched their first NFC West title since 2009, in addition to the first 13-win season in franchise history.  They also clinched a first round bye for the first time in franchise history.
