Batthyány: Batthyány (] ) is the name of an old distinguished Hungarian Magnate family.  The members of this family bear the title count or countess (Graf/Gräfin) respectively prince or princess (Fürst/Fürstin) Batthyány von Német-Ujvár.  A branch of the family (Croatian: "Baćan" ) was notable in Croatia as well, producing several Bans (viceroys) of Croatia in the 16th, 17th and 18th century.
