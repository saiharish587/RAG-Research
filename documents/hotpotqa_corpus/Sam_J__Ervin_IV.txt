Sam J. Ervin IV: Samuel James "Jimmy" Ervin IV (born November 18, 1955) is a North Carolina lawyer and jurist who has served on the North Carolina Supreme Court since January 1, 2015.  He previously served as a state Utilities Commissioner and as a judge of the North Carolina Court of Appeals.  He is the grandson of U.S. Sen. Sam Ervin and the son of Judge Sam Ervin, III.
