Willsmere railway station: Willsmere was a railway station on the Outer Circle, located in the suburb of Kew, Melbourne, Australia.  The station was named after the estate of early Kew settlers H.S. and Thomas Wills.  Willsmere was opened with the line in 1891, and closed with it in 1893, with 2 side platforms and a loop off the main line to the south.  The station platforms and buildings were believed to have been removed around 1930.
