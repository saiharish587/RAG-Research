Tiger Please: Tiger Please is an Indie / Alternative five-piece band from Cardiff, Wales.  The band formed in August 2008.  The band's influences are U2, Sigur Rós, Kings of Leon, John Mayer and Counting Crows.  They signed with Walnut Tree Records in 2009 and released their debut EP "They Don't Change Under Moonlight". " Kerrang! " magazine, "Rock Sound" magazine, and "Classic Rock" magazine praised the EP and featured the band on the "Rock Sound" and "Classic Rock" cover-mount albums.  The band toured with Kids In Glass Houses, InMe, Twin Atlantic and Funeral For A Friend.
