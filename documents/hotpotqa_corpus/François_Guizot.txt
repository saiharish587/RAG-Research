François Guizot: François Pierre Guillaume Guizot (] ; 4 October 1787 – 12 September 1874) was a French historian, orator, and statesman.  Guizot was a dominant figure in French politics prior to the Revolution of 1848.  A conservative liberal who opposed the attempt by King Charles X to usurp legislative power, he worked to sustain a constitutional monarchy following the July Revolution of 1830.
