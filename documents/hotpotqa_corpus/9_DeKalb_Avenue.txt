9 DeKalb Avenue: 9 DeKalb Avenue, alternatively referred to as 340 Flatbush Avenue Extension, is a supertall residential skyscraper under construction for Brooklyn, New York being developed by Michael Stern's JDS Development Group and Joseph Chetrit's Chetrit Group.  When completed it will become the tallest structure in New York City outside Manhattan, as well as the first supertall building in Brooklyn.
