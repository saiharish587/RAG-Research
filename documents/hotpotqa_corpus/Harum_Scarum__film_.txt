Harum Scarum (film): Harum Scarum is a 1965 American musical comedy film starring Elvis Presley, which was shot on the original Cecil B. DeMille set from the film "The King of Kings" with additional footage shot on location at the Iverson Movie Ranch in Chatsworth, Calif. Some of the film was based on Rudolph Valentino's "The Sheik" released in 1921.  The film reached #11 on the "Variety" national weekly box office chart, earned $2 million at the box office, and finished #40 on the year end list of the top-grossing films of 1965.  The film is listed in Golden Raspberry Award founder John Wilson's book "The Official Razzie Movie Guide" as one of The 100 Most Enjoyably Bad Movies Ever Made.  The film was released in the United Kingdom as "Harem Holiday".
