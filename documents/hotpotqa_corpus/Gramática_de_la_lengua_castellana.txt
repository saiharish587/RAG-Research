Gramática de la lengua castellana: Gramática de la lengua castellana ("Grammar of the Castilian Language", originally titled in Latin: Grammatica Antonii Nebrissensis) is a book written by Antonio de Nebrija and published in 1492.  It was the first work dedicated to the Spanish language and its rules, and the first grammar of a modern European language to be published.
