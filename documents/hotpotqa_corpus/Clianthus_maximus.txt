Clianthus maximus: Clianthus maximus, commonly known as kaka beak ("kōwhai ngutu-kākā" in Māori), is a woody legume shrub native to New Zealand's North Island.  It is one of two species of "Clianthus" (kaka beak) and both have striking clusters of red flowers which resemble the beak of the kākā, a New Zealand parrot.
