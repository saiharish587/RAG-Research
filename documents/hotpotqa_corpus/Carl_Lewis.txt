Carl Lewis: Frederick Carlton "Carl" Lewis (born July 1, 1961) is an American former track and field athlete who won nine Olympic gold medals, one Olympic silver medal, and 10 World Championships medals, including eight gold.  His career spanned from 1979 to 1996 when he last won an Olympic event and subsequently retired.  He is one of only three Olympic athletes who won a gold medal in the same event in four consecutive Olympic Games.
