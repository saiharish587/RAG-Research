Badger (band): Badger were a British rock band from the early 1970s.  They were co-founded by keyboardist Tony Kaye after he left Yes, with bassist and vocalist David Foster.  Foster had been in the Warriors with Jon Anderson before Anderson co-founded Yes.  Foster later worked with the band on "Time and a Word" (1970).  Kaye had worked on a solo project by Foster that was never released.
