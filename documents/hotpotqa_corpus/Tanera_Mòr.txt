Tanera Mòr: Tanera Mòr (Scottish Gaelic: Tannara Mòr) is an uninhabited (previously inhabited) island in Loch Broom in the Inner Hebrides of Scotland.  It is the largest of the Summer Isles and was the last inhabited island in that group.  Tanera Mòr has issued its own postage stamps and was the location of Frank Fraser Darling's book "Island Years".  In 2014 it was reported that the island's permanent residents had left and that it is for sale for £1.95 million.
