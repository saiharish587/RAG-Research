Jazz Jackrabbit 2: Jazz Jackrabbit 2 is a platform game produced by Epic MegaGames, now known as Epic Games.  It was accidentally confirmed by Arjan Brussee in 1994 and released in 1998 for PCs running Windows, and later for Macintosh computers.  Like the prequel, "Jazz Jackrabbit", "Jazz Jackrabbit 2" is a side-scrolling platform game but features additional multiplayer options, including the ability to play over a LAN or the Internet.
