Progress M-17: Progress M-17 was a Russian unmanned cargo spacecraft which was launched in 1993 to resupply the Mir space station.  The thirty-fifth of sixty four Progress spacecraft to visit Mir, it used the Progress-M 11F615A55 configuration, and had the serial number 217.  In addition to delivering cargo, Progress M-17 was also used to demonstrate extended duration Progress missions; remaining in orbit for almost a year with a docked phase lasting 132 days.
