Baihu Prison: Baihu Prison is a prison in Lujiang County, Anhui, China.  It was established in 1953.  Originally the Anhui Prov.  No. 1 LGB.  The prison is a particularly large-sized agriculture prison built on land reclaimed from a lake.  The total area the prison covers is 162 km2 , 130,000 mu of cultivated land and 8,000 mu of water area.  It holds roughly 18,000 prisons yearly making it China's second largest prison and Anhui Province's largest prison.
