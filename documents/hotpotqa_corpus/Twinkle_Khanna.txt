Twinkle Khanna: Twinkle Khanna (also known as Tina Jatin Khanna; born on 29 December 1973) is an Indian interior designer, newspaper columnist, film producer, author, and former film actress.  Her first book "Mrs Funnybones" sold over one hundred thousand copies making her India's highest-selling female writer of 2015.  She repeated the success with her second book the legend of Lakshmi Prasad which also went on to sell over a 100,000 copies by august 2017. She won the Crossword Book Award 2016 for Mrs Funnybones She won India Today Woman Writer of the year 2017 for Her second book, The Legend Of Lakshmi Prasad, published by Juggernaut Books that has been at No 2 on Amazon since its release and has received rave reviews : 'Twinkle Khanna's first collection of short fiction, features sensitive, humorous and eminently readable stories' says The Hindustan Times 'The Legend of Lakshmi Prasad, a collection of superbly crafted short stories by Twinkle Khanna, is as delightful as its author.
