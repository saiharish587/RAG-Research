Marc Webb: Marc Preston Webb (born August 31, 1974) is an American music video, short film, and film director.  He made his feature film directorial debut with the 2009 romantic comedy-drama "(500) Days of Summer", and went on to direct the 2012 "Spider-Man" reboot "The Amazing Spider-Man", its 2014 sequel, "The Amazing Spider-Man 2", and the 2017 comedy-drama films "Gifted" and "The Only Living Boy in New York".
