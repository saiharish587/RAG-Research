Tawhid Boys School: Tawhid Boys School located in Stoke Newington, in the London Borough of Hackney. It is the first Islamic boys school in the Stoke Newington area.  The school was founded in June 2000 (Rabi-al Awwal 1421).  Hadhrat Moulana Yusuf Motala, head and founder of Darul-Uloom, Bury, Greater Manchester, inaugurated the school in September 2000.
