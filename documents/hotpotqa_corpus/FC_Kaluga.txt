FC Kaluga: FC Kaluga (Russian: ФК Калуга ) is an association football club from Kaluga, Russia, founded in 2009.  The Club is playing in the Russian Professional Football League.  The team appeared after uniting two football Clubs from Kaluga: FC MiK Kaluga and FC Lokomotiv Kaluga.  The two teams finished 1st and 2d in Russian Amateur Football League Chernozemic Zone championship in 2009 and Kaluga faced the opportunity to promote a football club that could represent the City to Russian Professional Football League so the two teams were united.
