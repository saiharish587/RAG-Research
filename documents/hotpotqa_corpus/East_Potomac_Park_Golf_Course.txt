East Potomac Park Golf Course: East Potomac Park Golf Course (also known as East Potomac Golf Course) is a golf course located in East Potomac Park in Washington, D.C., in the United States.  The course includes an 18-hole course, two 9-hole courses, and a miniature golf course.  It is the busiest of the city's three golf courses (all of which are publicly owned).  The original nine-hole course opened in 1921, and the miniature golf course in 1930 (making it one of the oldest miniature golf courses in the nation).  Additional holes opened in stages between 1921 and 1925, leaving the course with 36 holes in all.
