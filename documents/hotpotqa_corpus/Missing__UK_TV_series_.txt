Missing (UK TV series): Missing is a British daytime television crime drama series starring Pauline Quirke and Mark Wingett.  The series is set in a busy, under-resourced missing persons unit, and follows the team led by DS Mary Jane "MJ" Croft (Quirke).  The first series of five episodes aired on BBC One in 2009, with an extended second series of ten episodes airing in 2010.  It was filmed in and around Dover, Kent and Tonbridge, Kent.  The series also starred Felix Scott and Pooja Shah as Croft's sidekicks, Jason Doyle and Amy Garnett.  Guest stars who appeared throughout the series run include Paul Nicholas, Brooke Kinsella, Gary Lucy and Sylvia Syms.
