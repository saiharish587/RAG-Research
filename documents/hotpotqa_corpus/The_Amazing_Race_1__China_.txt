The Amazing Race 1 (China): The Amazing Race 1 () is a Chinese reality television series loosely based on the American reality TV series, "The Amazing Race".  It is the first season of The Amazing Race ().  It features eight teams of two in a race around the world.  This is the second version of "The Amazing Race" to be produced in China, following "".  This new version is broadcast on Shenzhen TV, and had its premiere on October 17, 2014.  The hosts for the show are Hong Kong actor Andy On and Singapore based Chinese-American actor Allan Wu, who was also the host of "The Amazing Race Asia" and "The Amazing Race: China Rush".
