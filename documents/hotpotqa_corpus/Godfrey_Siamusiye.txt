Godfrey Siamusiye: Godfrey Malumo Siamusiye (born September 23, 1972) is a cross country and track and field runner from Choma, Zambia.  He has competed in the Olympics in 1992 and 1996.  He was also NCAA Division I Cross Country National Champion in 1995 and 1996.  He was a member of the 1995 Arkansas Razorback cross country National Champion team.  He coached from 2006 to 2012 as the head cross country coach at Shiloh Christian School in Springdale, Arkansas.  In 2012, Siamusiye left Shiloh for the position of cross country coach at Springdale High School.
