Yanzhou Village: Yanzhou Village (Chinese: 燕州城; pinyin: "Yānzhōuchéng") is a small settlement in Dengta Prefecture of Liaoyang Prefecture in Liaoning Province in China.  The village is the site of the ancient Goguryeo city of Baegam (Hangul :백암성, Hanja :白巖城) in Korea or Baiyan City (Chinese: 白岩城) in Chinese.  The ancient city was the scene a major battle between the Tang dynasty Chinese emperor Taizong and the Goguryeo in 645 AD.
