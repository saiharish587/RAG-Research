George Lazenby: George Robert Lazenby (born 5 September 1939) is an Australian actor and former model best known for his portrayal of James Bond in the Eon series in the 1969 film "On Her Majesty's Secret Service".  He was 29 years old, making him the youngest actor to date to have portrayed the character.  Lazenby is also the only Bond actor to receive a nomination for the Golden Globe Award for New Star of the Year – Actor.
