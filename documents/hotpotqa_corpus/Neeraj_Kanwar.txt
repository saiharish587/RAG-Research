Neeraj Kanwar: Neeraj Kanwar (born 6 September 1971) is the vice chairman and managing director of Apollo Tyres, India's second largest tyre manufacturer with annual revenues of over $2 billion.  He is credited with turning Apollo Tyres from a commercial vehicle-focussed tyre manufacturer in India when he joined in 1995 into a multinational company that now manufactures tyres for commercial and passenger vehicles as well as two wheeler tyres.
