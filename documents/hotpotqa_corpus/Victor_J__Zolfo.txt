Victor J. Zolfo: Victor J. Zolfo is a set decorator who has worked in the film industry since the late 1980s.  Zolfo won the Academy Award for Best Art Direction and the BAFTA Award for Best Production Design for the 2008 film "The Curious Case of Benjamin Button", sharing the awards for the film with art director and production designer Donald Graham Burt.  He was also part of the 20-person team who won the Art Directors Guild's Excellence in Production Design Award for "The Curious Case of Benjamin Button".
