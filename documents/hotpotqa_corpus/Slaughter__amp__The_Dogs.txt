Slaughter &amp; The Dogs: Slaughter & the Dogs are an English punk rock band that formed in 1975 in Wythenshawe, Manchester, England.  They were one of the first UK punk bands to sign with a major label, Decca Records.  Their original line-up consisted of Wayne Barrett (vocals), Mick Rossi (guitar), Brian "Mad Muffet" Grantham (drums) and Howard "Zip" Bates (bass).
