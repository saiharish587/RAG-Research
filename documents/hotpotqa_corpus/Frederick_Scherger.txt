Frederick Scherger: Air Chief Marshal Sir Frederick Rudolph William Scherger, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (18 May 190416 January 1984) was a senior commander in the Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF).  He served as Chief of the Air Staff, the RAAF's highest-ranking position, from 1957 until 1961, and as Chairman of the Chiefs of Staff Committee, forerunner of the role of Australia's Chief of the Defence Force, from 1961 until 1966.  He was the first RAAF officer to hold the rank of air chief marshal.
