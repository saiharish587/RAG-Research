Nanyue: Nanyue () or Zhuang: Namzyied , or Nam Viet (Vietnamese: "Nam Việt" ) was an ancient kingdom that covered parts of northern Vietnam and the modern Chinese provinces of Guangdong, Guangxi, and Yunnan.  Nanyue was established in 204 BC at the collapse of the Qin dynasty by Zhao Tuo, then Commander of Nanhai.  At first, it consisted of the commanderies Nanhai, Guilin, and Xiang.
