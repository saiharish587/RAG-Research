Equality Matters: Equality Matters is a media and communications initiative in support of LGBT rights in the United States.  According to its website, Equality Matters is a partner organization with Media Matters for America, a progressive media watchdog group.  Richard Socarides, longtime LGBT rights activist and former senior advisor to President Bill Clinton on gay civil rights issues, served as president of Equality Matters from its founding, and Kerry Eleveld, journalist for "The Advocate", served as editor beginning in 2011.  Both announced their departure from the organization in November 2011.
