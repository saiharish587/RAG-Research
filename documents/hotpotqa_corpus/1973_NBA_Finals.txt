1973 NBA Finals: The 1973 NBA World Championship Series was the championship series of the 1972–73 National Basketball Association (NBA) season, and the culmination of that season's playoffs.  The Eastern Conference champion New York Knicks defeated the Western Conference champion Los Angeles Lakers 4 games to 1.  The series was an exact reversal of the prior year, with the Lakers winning Game 1 and the Knicks taking the next four games.  Knicks center Willis Reed was named as the NBA Finals MVP.
