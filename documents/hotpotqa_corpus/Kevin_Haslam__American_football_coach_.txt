Kevin Haslam (American football coach): Kevin Haslam is a college sports administrator and former college football coach.  He is an assistant athletic director at Arizona State University, and used to be athletic director at MacMurray College in Jacksonville, Illinois, the second school in National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) history to have imposed the death penalty on one of its sports programs.  Haslam took over as the athletic director after the resignation of the athletic director that oversaw the severe penalty.
