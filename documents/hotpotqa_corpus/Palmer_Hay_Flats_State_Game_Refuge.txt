Palmer Hay Flats State Game Refuge: The Palmer Hayflats State Game Refuge is located in Alaska, south of Wasilla and north of Anchorage.  It is composed of 28800 acre of coastal marshy areas adjacent to Knik Arm that support populations of moose, muskrat, foxes, coyotes, eagles, and migratory waterfowl.  The Knik River, the Matanuska River, Rabbit Slough, Wasilla Creek, Cottonwood Creek, and Spring Creek flow through it.
