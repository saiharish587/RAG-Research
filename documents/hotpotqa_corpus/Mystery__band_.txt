Mystery (band): Mystery (often stylized as MYSTERY) is a Canadian rock band formed in 1986 by multi-instrumentalist Michel St-Père.  The band released their eponymous debut EP in 1992 with Raymond and Gary Savoie on lead vocals, followed by their first album "Theatre of the Mind" in 1996 and "Destiny? " in 1998, both with Gary on lead vocals.  In 2007 the band released their third album "Beneath the Veil of Winter's Face" with singer Benoît David who would record two more studio albums with the band: "One Among the Living" in 2010 and "The World is a Game" in 2012.  On November 1, 2015 the band released their sixth album "Delusion Rain" with current singer Jean Pageau.
