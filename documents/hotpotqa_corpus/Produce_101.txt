Produce 101: Produce 101 () is a 2016 reality girl group survival show on Mnet.  It is a large-scale project in which the public "produces" a unit girl group by choosing members from a pool of 101 trainees from 46 entertainment companies as well as the group's concept, debut song, and group name.  The show has the second-largest budget of all shows produced by Mnet at about four billion won (approximately $3.4 million).
