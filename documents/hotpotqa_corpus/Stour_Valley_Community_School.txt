Stour Valley Community School: Stour Valley Community School is a coeducational free school for pupils aged 11 to 16 in Clare in the English county of Suffolk.  The school opened in September 2011 as one of the first 24 free schools in England.  The school opened with around 170 pupils and a total staff of 29, including non-teaching support staff.  The headteacher is Christine Inchley.
