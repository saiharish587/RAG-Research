Strange Euphoria: Strange Euphoria is a career-spanning box set album by Heart.  It features several of their hit songs as well as songs by The Lovemongers, solo songs by Ann Wilson and Nancy Wilson, a recording by the pre-Heart group Ann Wilson & The Daybreaks, and previously unreleased demos and live tracks.  A DVD is also included featuring a 1976 concert at Washington State University for the Pullman, Washington-based KWSU-TV concert series "The Second Ending" in promotion for their debut album "Dreamboat Annie".
