Cats Under the Stars: Cats Under the Stars is an album by the Jerry Garcia Band.  Released in 1978, it is the only studio album credited to the band.  It was Jerry Garcia's first album on the Arista label and his first LP using the band name Jerry Garcia Band.  It includes only one song that was performed by the Grateful Dead.  Keith and Donna Godchaux, who were at the time members of the Garcia Band, contributed to the music.
