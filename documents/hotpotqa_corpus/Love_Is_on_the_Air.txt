Love Is on the Air: Love is on the Air is a 1937 American film directed by Nick Grinde.  The film stars Ronald Reagan and June Travis, supported by Eddie Acuff, Robert Barrat, Raymond Hatton, and Willard Parker.  It was Reagan's screen debut.  The movie was the first of three remakes of the 1933 Paul Muni picture "Hi, Nellie".  (The later ones were "You Can't Escape Forever" (1942), with George Brent, and "House Across the Street" (1949), with Wayne Morris.)
