Bob Eggleton: Bobera Eggleton (born September 13, 1960) is an American science fiction, fantasy, and horror artist.  Eggleton has been honored with the Hugo Award for Best Professional Artist eight times, first winning in 1994.  He also won the Hugo Award for Best Related Book in 2001 for his art book "Greetings From Earth".  He has also won the Chesley Award for Artistic Achievement in 1999 and was the guest of honor at Chicon 2000.
