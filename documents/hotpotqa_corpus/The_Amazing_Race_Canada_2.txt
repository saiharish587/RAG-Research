The Amazing Race Canada 2: The second season of The Amazing Race Canada was a reality game show based on the American series "The Amazing Race".  It featured eleven teams of two, each with a pre-existing relationship in a race across Canada and around the world.  The winning team was awarded CA$ , two Chevrolet Silverado "High Country Edition" trucks, free gasoline for life from Petro-Canada and the ability to fly for free anywhere for a year with Air Canada in business class.  Jon Montgomery returned as the host.
