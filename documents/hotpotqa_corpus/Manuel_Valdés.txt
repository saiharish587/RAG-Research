Manuel Valdés: Manuel "El Loco" Valdés (born 29 January 1931) is a Mexican actor and comedian, member of the Valdés family.  He is the brother of Ramón Valdés (a.k.a. "Don Ramón"), from the sitcom "El Chavo", and Germán Valdés (a.k.a. "Tin Tan").  He is also the father of singer Cristian Castro and Marcos Valdés.
