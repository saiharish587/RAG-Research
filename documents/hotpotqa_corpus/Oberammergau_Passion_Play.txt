Oberammergau Passion Play: Oberammergau Passion Play is a passion play performed since 1634 as a tradition by the inhabitants of the village of Oberammergau, Bavaria, Germany.  It was written by Othmar Weis, J A Daisenberger, Otto Huber, Christian Stuckl, Rochus Dedler, Eugen Papst, Marcus Zwink, Ingrid H Shafer, and the inhabitants of Oberammergau, with music by Dedler.  Since its first production it has been performed on open-air stages in the village.  The text of the play is a composite of four distinct manuscripts dating from the 15th and 16th centuries.
