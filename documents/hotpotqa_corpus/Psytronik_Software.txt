Psytronik Software: Psytronik Software is a British software publisher started in 1993.  The company publish new games for retro gaming platforms; Commodore 64, Amstrad CPC, Commodore 16, Commodore Plus/4, VIC 20.  But also some titles for Windows.  Among the games are: Mayhem in Monsterland, Knight 'n' Grail and Maze of the Mummy.
