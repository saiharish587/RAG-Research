The Biggest Loser UK (series 3): The Biggest Loser UK 2009 was the third season of the reality television series "The Biggest Loser".  The season first aired on 27 April 2009 with the final episode on 19 June 2009 with eight overweight couples (sixteen individuals) competing for a cash prize of £10,000.  Kate Garraway was featured as the host, with trainers Angie Dowds and Richard Callender.  Kevin Sage was named as the winner after losing 9 st 6 lb .
