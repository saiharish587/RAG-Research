Wu Jing (actor): Wu Jing (born 3 April 1974), sometimes credited as Jacky Wu or Jing Wu, is a Chinese martial artist, actor and director.  Wu is best known for his roles in various martial arts films such as "Tai Chi Boxer", "Fatal Contact" and the "SPL" films, and as Leng Feng in 2017 mega-hit Chinese action film "Wolf Warriors 2".
