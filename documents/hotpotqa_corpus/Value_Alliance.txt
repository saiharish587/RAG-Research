Value Alliance: Value Alliance is an airline alliance formed in May 2016.  It is the world's second alliance (after the U-FLY Alliance) to consist only of low-cost carriers (LCCs).  However, it is the first pan-regional LCC alliance.  It comprises seven Asia-Pacific airlines: Cebu Pacific, Jeju Air, Nok Air, NokScoot, Scoot, Tigerair Australia and Vanilla Air, along with Tigerair who merged with Scoot under the Scoot brand.
