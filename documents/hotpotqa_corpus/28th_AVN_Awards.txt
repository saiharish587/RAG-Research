28th AVN Awards: The 28th AVN Awards ceremony in Las Vegas, presented by "Adult Video News" ("AVN"), honored the best pornographic movies and adult entertainment products of 2010.  The ceremony was held on January 8, 2011 in the Pearl Concert Theater inside the Palms Casino Resort in Paradise, Nevada.  During the ceremony, AVN Media Network presented awards in 155 categories of movies or products released between October 1, 2009 and September 30, 2010.  The ceremony was televised in the United States by Showtime.  Comedian Lisa Lampanelli hosted the show with co-hosts Tori Black and Riley Steele.
