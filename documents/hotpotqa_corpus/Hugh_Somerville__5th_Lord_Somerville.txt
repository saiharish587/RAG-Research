Hugh Somerville, 5th Lord Somerville: Hugh Somerville, 5th Lord Somerville (c. 1484 – 1549) was a lord of the Parliament of Scotland.  He is sometimes reckoned to be the 4th Lord Somerville.  He succeeded his brother, John Somerville, 4th Lord Somerville.  Hugh and John were sons of William Somerville, Master of Somerville, and Marjory Montgomerie.
