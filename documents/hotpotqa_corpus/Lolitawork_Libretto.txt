Lolitawork Libretto: "Lolitawork Libretto" (少女仕掛けのリブレット , Shoujo Jikake no Riburetto ) is the second studio album by singer and cellist Kanon Wakeshima.  The song "Toumei no Kagi" was released as a digital download prior to the release of the album on September 16, 2009.  The song was used as the theme song of the online game "Avalon no Kagi".  A promotional music video for the song "Lolitawork Libretto ~Storytelling by Solita~" was also released prior to the album.
