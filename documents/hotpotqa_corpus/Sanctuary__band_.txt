Sanctuary (band): Sanctuary is an American heavy metal band founded in Seattle in 1985.  They split up in 1992, but reformed 18 years later.  The band consists of Warrel Dane (vocals), Lenny Rutledge (guitar), Nick Cordle (guitar), George Hernandez (bass), and Dave Budbill (drums).  To date, they have released four studio albums and one live EP.
