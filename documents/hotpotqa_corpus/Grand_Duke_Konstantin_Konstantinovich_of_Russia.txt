Grand Duke Konstantin Konstantinovich of Russia: Grand Duke Konstantin Konstantinovich of Russia (Russian: Константи́н Константи́нович ; ] ; 22 August 1858 in Strelna – 15 June 1915 in Pavlovsk) was a grandson of Emperor Nicholas I of Russia, and a poet and playwright of some renown.  He wrote under the pen name "K.R.", initials of his given name and family name, Konstantin Romanov.
