Bonnie (TV series): Bonnie (originally titled The Bonnie Hunt Show) is an American CBS television comedy that aired from 1995 to 1996.  Bonnie Hunt played Bonnie Kelly, a television reporter who moves from Wisconsin to take a job in Chicago.  There she encounters an eclectic group of coworkers.  Each episode had parts where Bonnie would interview real people, asking ridiculous questions.  These scenes were improvised.  The show bore similarities to another series produced by David Letterman, "Welcome to New York".
