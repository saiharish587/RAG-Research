Steve Albert: Steve Albert (born Stephen Aufrichtig in Brooklyn, New York) was an American sportscaster.  He has served as a play-by-play announcer for the New Jersey Nets, New Orleans Hornets, Golden State Warriors, New York Mets, and Phoenix Suns.  He last was the television play-by-play announcer for the Phoenix Suns.  He retired after the Phoenix Suns 2016-2017 season.
