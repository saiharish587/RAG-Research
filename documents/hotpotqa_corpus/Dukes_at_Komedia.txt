Dukes at Komedia: The Dukes at Komedia (sometimes styled as Dukes @ Komedia) is a two-screen cinema in Brighton, England, which was opened in December 2012 by Picturehouse.  Situated predominantly in the upstairs portion of the North Laine based Komedia comedy club, the main screen has 142 seats, whilst a smaller second screen can seat 96 people.  There are two bars, serving snacks and a variety of alcoholic and non-alcoholic drinks, as well as hot food which can be taken into the theatre.  Until February 2014, the cinema had a kitchen which specialised in vegetarian and vegan food.
