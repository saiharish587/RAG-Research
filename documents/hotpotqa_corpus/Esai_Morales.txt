Esai Morales: Esai Manuel Morales, Jr. (born October 1, 1962) is an American actor.  He played Bob Morales in the 1987 biopic "La Bamba".  He also appeared in the PBS drama "American Family" and in the Showtime series "Resurrection Blvd." He is perhaps best known for his roles as Lt. Tony Rodriguez on "NYPD Blue" and Joseph Adama in the science fiction television series "Caprica".
