Moulton Co-operative Society: The Moulton Co-operative Society Limited, or simply Moulton Co-op, was a small regional consumer co-operative in the United Kingdom.  The society was formed in 1861 and operated a single supermarket in Moulton, Northamptonshire.  In January 2009, members voted overwhelmingly to transfer arrangements to Midlands Co-operative Society, which took effect on 8 February 2009.
