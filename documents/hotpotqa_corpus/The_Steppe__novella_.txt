The Steppe (novella): The Steppe (Russian: Степь , "Step' " ), subtitled "The Story of a Journey", is a novella by Russian playwright Anton Chekhov.  In a narrative that drifts with the thought processes of the characters, Chekhov evokes a chaise journey across the steppe through the eyes of a young boy sent to live away from home, and his companions, a priest and a merchant.
