Tufail Niazi: Tufail Niazi (Urdu: طفیل نیازی‎ ) (1916 – 21 September 1990) was a Pakistani folk singer whose songs include "Saada Chirryan Da Chamba Ae," "Akhiyaan Lagiyaan Jawaab Na Daindian," "Layee Beqadran Naal Yaari, Tay Tut Gai Tarak Karkey" and "Mein Nai Jana Kheriyan De Naal."  He used to perform regularly on Pakistan Television (PTV) and Radio Pakistan.
