Spider Clark: Owen F. "Spider" Clark (September 16, 1867 – February 8, 1892) was a professional baseball player.  He played two seasons in Major League Baseball, 1889 for the Washington Nationals, and 1890 for the Buffalo Bisons of the Players' League.  While he was primarily a right fielder, he played all over the diamond on defense, playing every position at least once, including one game as a pitcher for the Bisons.
