GB Theatre Company: The GB Theatre Company is a British touring company that specialises in outdoor productions of Shakespeare.  Founded in 2010 by Gillian Roca and Barrie Palmer.  The first productions in 2010 were "As You Like It" directed by Neil Sheppeck and "Merry Wives of Windsor" directed by Jenny Stephens.  The productions were played in rep and starred Gabriel Thomson, Matt Milburn, Stacey Roca and Alexander Delamere.  The tour took in Dublin's Trinity College, Ramme Gaard in Norway, Prague Castle, Norwich Cathedral, Kentwell Hall and Arundel Castle.
