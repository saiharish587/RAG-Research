St Martin's Church, Brighton: St Martin's Church (in full, St Martin with St Wilfrid, St Alban and St Richard Hollingdean) is an Anglican church in Brighton, England, dating from the mid-Victorian era.  It is located on the Lewes Road (the present A270) in the Round Hill area of the city, northeast of the city centre and approximately 1.1 mi north of the seafront.  It is the largest church in Brighton by capacity and is noted for its ornate interior.
