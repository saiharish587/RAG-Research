Revolutions – The Very Best of Steve Winwood: Revolutions – The Very Best of Steve Winwood is the sixth compilation album by Steve Winwood.  The album includes music from Winwood's solo career, as well as groups with which he has performed, including The Spencer Davis Group, Traffic, and Blind Faith.  The CD was released as a box set and a single disc.  As of October 2014, the box set is out of print (but can be purchased digitally), while the single disc is still available.  The songs "The Finer Things" and "Roll With It" are exclusive to the single disc version and cannot be found on the box set.
