José Ramón Bauzà: José Ramón Bauzà Díaz (born November 16, 1970) is a Spanish politician and the president of the Autonomous Community of the Balearic Islands from 2011 to 2015.  Born to a Majorcan father and a mother native to Madrid, he is licensed as a pharmacist by the Complutense University of Madrid.  He owns his own pharmacy in his hometown of Marratxí.
