Bipasha Basu filmography: Bipasha Basu is an Indian actress who has featured in over 50 films, predominantly in Bollywood.  After a successful career as a model, she made her film debut with a supporting role in Abbas–Mustan's thriller "Ajnabee" (2001), which won her the Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut.  Basu followed this with a role in her first Telugu cinemathe action film "Takkari Donga" (2002).  She had her first major success with the supernatural thriller "Raaz" (2002), which earned Basu her first Filmfare Award for Best Actress nomination.  The following year, she starred opposite John Abraham in the erotic thriller "Jism", in which she played a seductive wife.  She received a Filmfare Award for Best Performance in a Negative Role nomination for the film.  Her roles in these films established her as a sex symbol.
