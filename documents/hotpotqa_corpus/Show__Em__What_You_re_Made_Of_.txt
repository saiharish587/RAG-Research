Show 'Em (What You're Made Of): "Show 'Em (What You're Made Of)" is a song by American pop group Backstreet Boys from their eighth studio album "In a World Like This".  It was released as the second single from the album on November 18, 2013.  The song was written by Morgan Taylor Reid, Mika Guillory, and Backstreet Boys members AJ McLean and Kevin Richardson.
