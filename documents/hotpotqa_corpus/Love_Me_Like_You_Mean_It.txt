Love Me Like You Mean It: "Love Me Like You Mean It" is a song co-written and recorded by American country pop singer Kelsea Ballerini.  Ballerini co-wrote the song with Josh Kerr, Forest Glen Whitehead and Lance Carpenter.  It was released on July 8, 2014 as Ballerini's debut single and the lead single from her debut studio album, "The First Time", released on May 19, 2015, by Black River Entertainment.  The song is about a young woman showing interest in a man.
