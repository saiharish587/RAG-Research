Rourke Chartier: Rourke Chartier (born April 3, 1996) is a Canadian professional ice hockey center.  He is currently playing with the San Jose Barracuda in the American Hockey League (AHL) as a prospect of the San Jose Sharks of the National Hockey League (NHL).  Chartier was selected by the Sharks in the 5th round (149th overall) of the 2014 NHL Entry Draft.
