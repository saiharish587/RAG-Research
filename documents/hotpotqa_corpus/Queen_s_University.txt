Queen's University: Queen's University at Kingston (commonly shortened to Queen's University or Queen's) is a public research university in Kingston, Ontario, Canada.  Founded on 16 October 1841 via a royal charter issued by Queen Victoria, the university predates Canada's founding by 26 years.  Queen's holds more than 1400 ha of land throughout Ontario and owns Herstmonceux Castle in East Sussex, England.  Queen's is organized into ten undergraduate, graduate and professional faculties and schools.
