Alex Magala: Alexandr Magala (born 24 June 1989 in Orhei) is a Moldovan entertainer and sword swallower who won "Russia's Got Talent" in 2014.  He was also a finalist on "Britain's Got Talent" and "Italia's Got Talent", a semi-finalist on "Ukraine's Got Talent", and a quarter-finalist on "America's Got Talent."
