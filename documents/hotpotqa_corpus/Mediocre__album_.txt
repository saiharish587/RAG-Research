Mediocre (album): Mediocre (] ) is the Grammy Award and Latin Grammy nominated debut album of Mexican singer and actress Ximena Sariñana, released in the United States on  15, 2008 (2008--) .  "Mediocre" reached #10 on Billboard's Latin Pop Albums, and spawned the hit single "Vidas Paralelas" ("Parallel Lives").  The album gained additional attention when the iTunes Store featured Sariñana's song "Normal" as the "canción de la semana" — iTunes' free Latino song of the week — during the week of  08, 2008 (2008--) .
