Rail replacement bus service: A rail replacement bus service uses buses to replace a passenger train service either on a temporary or permanent basis.  The train service that is replaced may be of any type such as light rail, tram, streetcar, commuter rail, regional rail or heavy rail, intercity passenger service.  The rail service may be replaced if the line is closed due to rail maintenance, a breakdown of a train, a rail accident, strike action, or if the rail service is not economically viable.
