The Black Stallion Returns: The Black Stallion Returns is a 1983 film adaptation of the book of the same name by Walter Farley, and is a sequel to "The Black Stallion".  It is directed by Robert Dalva and produced by Francis Ford Coppola for MGM/UA Entertainment Company.  The movie stars Kelly Reno, Vincent Spano and Teri Garr.  The portrayal of The Black was shared between Cass Ole, the horse from "The Black Stallion", and El Mokhtar.
