Shahid U. H. Qureshi: Shahid U H Qureshi, an IEEE Fellow, was born in Peshawar, Pakistan on September 22, 1945.  He received the B.Sc.  degree from University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore, Pakistan in 1967.  In 1968 he got the President of Pakistan's award for outstanding student at the graduate level.  In 1967 and 1968 he remained as Lecturer in the Electrical Engineering Department of the University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore.  In 1970 he got his M.Sc.  degree in electrical engineering from University of Alberta, Edmonton, Canada.  Subsequently he completed his PhD Electrical Engineering in 1973 from University of Toronto, Toronto, Ont., Canada.
