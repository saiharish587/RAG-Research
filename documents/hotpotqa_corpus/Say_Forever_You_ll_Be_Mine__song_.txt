Say Forever You'll Be Mine (song): "Say Forever You'll Be Mine" is a song written and recorded by American country music artist Dolly Parton as a duet with American country music artist Porter Wagoner.  It was released in August 1975 as the first single from their album "Say Forever You'll Be Mine".  The song peaked at number 5 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles chart.  It also reached number 1 on the "RPM" Country Tracks chart in Canada.
