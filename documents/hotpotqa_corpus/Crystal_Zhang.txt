Crystal Zhang: Crystal "CC" Zhang (Chinese: 张熙媛, born 12 November 1989), is a Chinese actress and model.  Born in Shenyang, Zhang made her professional acting debut with the role of Liu Xinmei in the 2011 Chinese television drama ""Before the Dawn"" (Chinese: 黎明绝杀) alongside Chen Zihan.  She has since had roles across film and television, appearing alongside such Asian stars as Zhang Fengyi, Bosco Wong, and Tiffany Tang.
