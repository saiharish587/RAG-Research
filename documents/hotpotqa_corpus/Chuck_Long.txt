Chuck Long: Charles Franklin Long Jr. (born February 18, 1963) is an American football coach.  He played quarterback in college at Iowa for Hayden Fry and professionally with the Detroit Lions and the Los Angeles Rams.  He is an inductee of the College Football Hall of Fame.  After his professional career, Chuck became the head football coach of San Diego State University, but was fired on November 23, 2008.  Long also held a position as the offensive coordinator for the Kansas Jayhawks under head coach Turner Gill before Gill was fired in 2011.  Later, Chuck became an analyst for the Big Ten Network.  On September 11, 2014 Chuck was named the CEO and Executive Director of the Iowa Sports Foundation, the organization that runs the Iowa Games, the Senior Games and the Live Healthy Iowa challenge.
