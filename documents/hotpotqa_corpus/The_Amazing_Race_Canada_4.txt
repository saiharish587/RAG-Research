The Amazing Race Canada 4: The fourth season of The Amazing Race Canada is a reality game show based on the American series "The Amazing Race".  It features 10 teams of two, each with a pre-existing relationship.  The grand prize includes: a CA$ cash payout, a "once-in-a-lifetime" trip for two around the world with hotel accommodations provided by new sponsor Hotels.com, and the winning team's choice of any Chevrolet vehicle driven during the race.  For the fourth year in a row, Jon Montgomery returned as the host.
