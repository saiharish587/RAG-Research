Robin Bhatt: Robin Bhatt (Hindi: रोबिन भट्ट ) is known as one of the most successful writers in Bollywood.  He is well known for his skills in penning screenplay.  He is the brother of Mahesh Bhatt.  He has written many films and was nominated 3 times and won award for Baazigar.  His debut film as writer was Aashiqui, which proved to be a hit film.  He has written many films for Bhatt Productions.
