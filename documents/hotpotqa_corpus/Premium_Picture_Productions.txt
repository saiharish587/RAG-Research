Premium Picture Productions: Premium Picture Productions is a former movie studio located in Beaverton, Oregon, which was active in the early 1920s.  It was founded in 1921, with J.J. Fleming as president and Dr. G.E. Watts and three others as directors of the corporation, and it opened its production lot in 1922.  The company went out of business in late 1925.
