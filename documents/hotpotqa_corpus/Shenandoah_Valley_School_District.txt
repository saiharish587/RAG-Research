Shenandoah Valley School District: The Shenandoah Valley School District is a diminutive, rural public school district in Schuylkill County, Pennsylvania.  It serves the municipalities of Shenandoah, and West Mahanoy Township.  The district encompasses approximately 11 sqmi in two noncontiguous areas.  According to 2000 federal census data, it served a resident population of 11,790.  By 2010, the district's population declined to 7,940 people.  The educational attainment levels for the Shenandoah Valley School District population (25 years old and over) were 79.4% high school graduates and 10.3% college graduates.  The district is one of the 500 public school districts of Pennsylvania.
