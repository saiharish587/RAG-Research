2008–09 DFB-Pokal (women): The DFB-Pokal 2008–09 was the 29th season of the competition.  The first round began on 30 August 2008.  In the final, held on 30 May 2009 in Berlin FCR 2001 Duisburg defeated Turbine Potsdam 7–0, marking the highest margin by which a Frauen DFB-Pokal final was ever decided.
