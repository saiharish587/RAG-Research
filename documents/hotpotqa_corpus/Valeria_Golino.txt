Valeria Golino: Valeria Golino (born 22 October 1965) is an Italian actress and director.  She is best known to English-language audiences for her roles in "Rain Man", "Big Top Pee-wee" and the "Hot Shots! " films.  In addition to David di Donatello, Silver Ribbon, Golden Ciak and Italian Golden Globe awards, she is also one of the three actresses who has won the Best Actress award at the Venice Film Festival twice.
