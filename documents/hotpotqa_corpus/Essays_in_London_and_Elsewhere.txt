Essays in London and Elsewhere: Essays in London and Elsewhere is a book of literary criticism by Henry James published in 1893.  The book collected essays that James had written over the preceding several years on a wide range of writers including James Russell Lowell, Gustave Flaubert, Robert Browning and Henrik Ibsen.  The book also included an interesting general essay on the role of the critic in literature and a piece of travel writing about London.
