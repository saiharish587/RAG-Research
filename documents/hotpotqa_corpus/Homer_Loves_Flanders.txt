Homer Loves Flanders: "Homer Loves Flanders" is the sixteenth episode of "The Simpsons"<nowiki>'</nowiki> fifth season.  It originally aired on the Fox network in the United States on March 17, 1994.  In the episode, Ned Flanders invites Homer to a football game and the two become good friends.  However, Ned soon grows weary of Homer's overbearing friendship and stupid antics, and begins to hate him.
