Sault Ste. Marie language resolution: The Sault Ste. Marie language resolution was a government motion passed on January 29, 1990 by Sault Ste. Marie City Council, the governing body of the city of Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario, Canada, which resolved that English was the sole working language of city government.  The resolution ignited a national controversy which made the city a flashpoint in the Meech Lake Accord debate.
