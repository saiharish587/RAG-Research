Candycoatedwaterdrops: candycoatedwaterdrops is the second album of Christian singer Plumb which features the hit songs "Stranded", "God-Shaped Hole", "Late Great Planet Earth", "Here With Me" and "Damaged".  The album has been critically acclaimed, and singles have appeared on major motion picture soundtracks such as "Bruce Almighty" and "Drive Me Crazy".  The songs "Stranded" and "Here With Me" were covered by Jennifer Paige on her second album Positively Somewhere, with the former track becoming a single and an airplay hit in mainland Europe.
