Steven Swanson: Steven Ray Swanson (born December 3, 1960 in Syracuse, New York) is an American engineer and a retired NASA astronaut.  He is married and has three children.  He has received numerous awards and honors.  These include the NASA Exceptional Achievement Medal and the JSC Certificate of Accommodation and many others.  Prior to becoming a NASA astronaut, Swanson worked for GTE in Phoenix, Arizona, as a software engineer.  Swanson has flown 2 shuttle flights, STS-117 and STS-119.  He has logged over 643 hours in space and completed 4 spacewalks totaling 26 hours and 14 minutes.  Swanson has also served in other roles at NASA, such as a CAPCOM for both International Space Station and Space Shuttle missions.
