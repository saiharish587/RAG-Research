Star Model S: The Star Model S was a Spanish semi-automatic pistol manufactured by Star Bonifacio Echeverria, S.A. and designed to use the .380 ACP cartridge.  Like many semi-automatic handguns, the basic design of the Star Model S is based on the .45 calibre Colt 1911.  But it is much smaller, lighter, and lacks some of the safety features of the Colt pistol.  It is this lightness and lack of safety features that make this a fast gun.
