Pulse (Greg Phillinganes album): Pulse is the title of the second solo album from session keyboardist Greg Phillinganes.  Released on July 17, 1984, the album included what is perhaps Phillinganes' best-known solo "hit," a cover of Japanese synthpop band Yellow Magic Orchestra's song, "Behind the Mask," with additional lyrics by Michael Jackson.  The track "Countdown to Love" was also featured in the 1984 film, "Streets of Fire", while both "Playin' with Fire" and "Signals" would later appear in the 1986 film, "Touch and Go".
