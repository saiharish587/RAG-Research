United States elections, 1860: The 1860 United States elections elected the members of the 37th United States Congress.  The election took place during the Third Party System, shortly before the start of the Civil War.  The Republican Party won control of the Presidency and both houses of Congress, making it the fifth party (following the Federalist Party, Democratic-Republican Party, Democratic Party, and Whig Party) to accomplish that feat.  The election is widely considered to be a realigning election.
