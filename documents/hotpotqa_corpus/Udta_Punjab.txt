Udta Punjab: Udta Punjab (English: "Punjab On A High" ) is a 2016 Indian black comedy crime film co-written and directed by Abhishek Chaubey.  It is loosely based on and revolves around the drug abuse by the youth population in the Indian state of Punjab and the various conspiracies surrounding it.  Produced by Shobha Kapoor and Ekta Kapoor under their banner Balaji Motion Pictures, in association with Anurag Kashyap's production house Phantom Films, it features an ensemble cast consisting of Shahid Kapoor, Kareena Kapoor, Alia Bhatt and, Diljit Dosanjh.
