Bill Caswell: Bill Caswell is a country music singer-songwriter and musician active since the early 1980s.  Together with his wife Rosi Caswell, the duo from Bartlesville, Oklahoma perform on rare old style instruments including the ukelin, mandolin-guitar, bell-harp, and tremeloa.  In the 1980s he performed his music on television in "Hee Haw" and on live radio in "A Prairie Home Companion".  The two together performed at Dollywood in Gatlinburg, Tennessee for five seasons.
