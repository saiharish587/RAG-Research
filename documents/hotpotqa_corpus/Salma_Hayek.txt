Salma Hayek: Salma Hayek Pinault ( Hayek Jiménez) (born September 2, 1966), known professionally as Salma Hayek, is a Mexican and American film actress, producer, and former model.  She began her career in Mexico starring in the telenovela "Teresa" and starred in the film "El Callejón de los Milagros" ("Miracle Alley") for which she was nominated for an Ariel Award.  In 1991 Hayek moved to Hollywood and came to prominence with roles in movies such as "Desperado" (1995), "From Dusk Till Dawn" (1996), "Dogma" (1999), and "Wild Wild West" (1999).
