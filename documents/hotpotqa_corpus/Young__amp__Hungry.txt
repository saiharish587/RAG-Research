Young &amp; Hungry: Young & Hungry is an American sitcom created by David Holden, and executive produced by Ashley Tisdale.  The multi-camera series stars Emily Osment, Jonathan Sadowski, Aimee Carrero, Kym Whitley and Rex Lee, and premiered on ABC Family (now Freeform) on June 25, 2014.  On March 7, 2016, Freeform renewed the series for a fourth season, which premiered on June 1, 2016, and concluded on August 3, 2016.  On October 24, 2016, Osment announced via Twitter that "Young & Hungry" had been renewed for a fifth season.  Aimee Carrero confirmed via Twitter that the last 10 episodes of season 5 will not air until 2018.  It is confirmed that the second half of the fifth season of the sitcom will air in January 2018.
