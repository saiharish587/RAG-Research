Chocolate (2005 film): Chocolate is a 2005 Indian Hindi crime thriller film starring Anil Kapoor, Sushma Reddy, Irrfan Khan, Tanushree Dutta, Sunil Shetty, Arshad Warsi, Emraan Hashmi and the British recording artist Emma Bunton in the lead roles.  Most of it was shot in London.  The film is severely criticized to be a brazen copy of 1995 Hollywood film "The Usual Suspects".
