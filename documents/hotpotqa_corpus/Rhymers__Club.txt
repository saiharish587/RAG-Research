Rhymers' Club: The Rhymers' Club was a group of London-based male poets, founded in 1890 by W. B. Yeats and Ernest Rhys.  Originally not much more than a dining club, it produced anthologies of poetry in 1892 and 1894.  They met at the London pub ‘Ye Olde Cheshire Cheese’ in Fleet Street and in the 'Domino Room' of the "Café Royal".
