Brandan Schieppati: Brandan Schieppati (born August 3, 1980, Newport Beach, California) is the singer of metalcore band Bleeding Through and a former guitarist/songwriter of the fellow Orange County metalcore band Eighteen Visions, for which he played from 1997 to 2002.  He is also a bodybuilder, personal trainer and "Rise Above Fitness" gym owner.  He was strictly Straight Edge from an early age until his late twenties.
