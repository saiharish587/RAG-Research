Morocco: Morocco ( ; Arabic: المَغرِب‎ , "al-maġrib ", 'place the sun sets; the west' ‎ ; Standard Moroccan Tamazight: ⵍⵎⵖⵔⵉⴱ‎ , "Lmeɣrib " ‎ ; French: "Maroc" ), officially known as the Kingdom of Morocco (Arabic: المملكة المغربية‎ , "al-Mamlakah al-Maghribiyah " ‎ , lit.  "The Western Kingdom"; Standard Moroccan Tamazight: ⵜⴰⴳⵍⴷⵉⵜ ⵏ ⵍⵎⵖⵔⵉⴱ‎ , "Tageldit n Lmaɣrib " ‎ ), is a sovereign country located in the Maghreb region of North Africa.  Geographically, Morocco is characterised by a rugged mountainous interior, large tracts of desert and a lengthy coastline along the Atlantic Ocean and Mediterranean Sea.
