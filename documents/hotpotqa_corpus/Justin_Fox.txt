Justin Fox: Justin Fox (born January 28, 1964) is an American financial journalist, commentator, and writer born in Morristown, New Jersey.  He is the editorial director of the Harvard Business Review Group and business and economics columnist for Time magazine.  He graduated from Princeton University and has been published by Fortune magazine, The Birmingham News, and American Banker.  His book, "The Myth of the Rational Market," traces the rise of the efficient-market hypothesis.  It was a New York Times Notable Book of 2009 and was named the best business book of the year by Amazon.com.
