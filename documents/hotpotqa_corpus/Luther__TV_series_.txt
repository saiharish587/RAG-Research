Luther (TV series): Luther is a British crime drama programme starring Idris Elba as the title character, DCI John Luther.  Written by Neil Cross, the first season, comprising six episodes, ran in May and June 2010.  A second season of four episodes aired on BBC One in June and July 2011.  A third series was commissioned in 2012, comprising four episodes aired in July 2013.  A two-episode fourth series was broadcast in December 2015.  A fifth series has been announced with filming due to start in early 2018.
