Yellow Machinegun: Yellow Machinegun was a hardcore punk/heavy metal band formed in 1993 in Osaka, Japan.  They produced a demo tape in December 1995.  The band released their debut album called "Father's Golden Fish" on October 21, 1996 for Bandai Music Entertainment.  This band has shared the same stage with bands such as Slayer, Motörhead, and Stormtroopers of Death.  The band became dormant in June 2006.  They did, however, reunite in Tokyo to play a show with the vocalist's new band, SuziSuzi, in April of 2017.
