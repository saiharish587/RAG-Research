Edward St John: Edward Henry St John QC (pr: Sinj'n) (15 August 191624 October 1994) was a prominent Australian barrister, anti-nuclear activist and Liberal politician in the 1960s.  His political career came to a controversial end after he criticised the Prime Minister John Gorton.  His book "A Time to Speak" was an account of his eventful three years in politics from 1966 to 1969.  Justice Michael Kirby described St John as a "contradictory, restless, reforming spirit".
