Nathaniel Popkin: Nathaniel Popkin (born August 23, 1969) is a Philadelphia-based writer, editor, and historian.  He is the author of "Song of the City" (2002, Basic Books), "The Possible City" (2008, Camino Books), Lion and Leopard (2013, The Head and The Hand Press), and "Philadelphia: Finding the Hidden City" (2017, Temple Press).  He co-founded the Hidden City Daily in 2011.
