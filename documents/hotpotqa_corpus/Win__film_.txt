Win (film): Win is a romance thriller trilingual film directed in three languages Hindi, Telugu & Tamil and written by Vinod Kumar assisisted by Sudarshanan.  Director Vinod Kumar is making his first directorial debut.  The film will be released under the banner of Rahmath Productions in Telugu & Jai Balaji Movie Makers in Tamil.  The film will feature Jai Akash alongside Angel Jitendra, Kavya, Nikita, Kousalya, Dinesh Nair, S. Ve.  Sheker, Ganja Karuppu, and various others.  Background score and soundtrack are composed by U. K. Murali audio is released in Telugu on 28 March 2013.  For the first time ever we have three music directors Shankar Ganesh, Deva, A. R. Reihana singing a song together for another music composer for this film.  Shooting for the film will be finished October 2013, and post-production works are also currently going on at Chennai & Hyderabad.
