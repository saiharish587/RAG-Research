Platylesches moritili: Platylesches moritili, the honey hopper or common hopper , is a butterfly of the Hesperiidae family.  It is found in Africa.  In South Africa, it is found from KwaZulu-Natal, north along the coast and hinterland to Maputaland and from Mpumalanga to northern Gauteng and from the central Limpopo Province to Pafuri.  The habitat consists of savanna and riverine forest.
