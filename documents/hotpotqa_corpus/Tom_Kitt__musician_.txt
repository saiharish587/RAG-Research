Tom Kitt (musician): Thomas Robert "Tom" Kitt (born February 28, 1974) is an American composer, conductor, orchestrator and musician.  For his score for the musical "Next to Normal", he shared the 2010 Pulitzer Prize for Drama with Brian Yorkey.  He also won the Tony Award and 2008 Outer Critics Circle Award, and was nominated for a Drama Desk Award for "American Idiot" and "Everyday Rapture".
