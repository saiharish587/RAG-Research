Registered Offender: Registered Offender is the debut comedy album by actor and stand-up comedian Rob Schneider.  Released in July 2010 by Oglio Records, it contains a mixture of sketches and songs, with all voices performed by Schneider.  The content is adult in nature and features various scatological references.  The album was recorded at various times and locations during 2007-2009.  A 7" vinyl single of the musical track "She's Gonna Come" b/w "Swain the Legend" was released simultaneously with the CD version of the album.
