Qualstar: Qualstar Corporation (NASDAQ: QBAK ) is an American manufacturer of magnetic tape data storage products, based in Simi Valley, California.  It was founded in 1984 as a 9 track tape drive manufacturer, and now makes tape library products.  The company sold its last 9 track tape drive in 2006 and as of March 2006 has sold all remaining parts inventory to Vinastar, an aftermarket vendor.
