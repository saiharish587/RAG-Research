The Rhythm and the Blues: The Rhythm and the Blues is the fifteenth solo studio album by Australian rock musician Jimmy Barnes, released through Liberation Music on 28 August 2009.  The album was produced by Don Gehman in Los Angeles and peaked at number one on the Australian Albums Chart for two weeks.  "The Rhythm and the Blues" was Barnes' ninth solo album (thirteenth including his Cold Chisel records) to reach number one on the ARIA Charts, an all-time record for an Australian artist.
