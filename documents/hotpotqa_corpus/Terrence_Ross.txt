Terrence Ross: Terrence James Elijah Ross (born February 5, 1991) is an American professional basketball player for the Orlando Magic of the National Basketball Association (NBA).  He played college basketball for the University of Washington, where he was a first-team All-Pac-12 selection before being drafted eighth overall in the 2012 NBA draft by the Toronto Raptors.  As a rookie, he was crowned champion of the 2013 Sprite Slam Dunk Contest.  In January 2014, he became the first player in NBA history to score 50 or more points in a game while averaging fewer than 10 points per game.
