Sjung: Sjung (] , "Sing") is the fourth studio album by the Swedish singer-songwriter Laleh, released on January 25, 2012.  Produced by Laleh herself, the album was released on Warner Music Sweden and Lost Army.  "Sjung" is her first long play for four years since the January 2009 album "Me and Simon".  The album is produced, written, recorded, engineered and performed by Laleh.  The release follows her appearance on popular Swedish television show "Så mycket bättre" towards the end of 2011.
