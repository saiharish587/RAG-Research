Kaltinėnai Aleksandras Stulginskis Gymnasium: Šilalė district Kaltinėnai Aleksandras Stulginskis Gymnasium is a day, public, co-educational school.  It is located at 22 Varnių Str., Kaltinėnai, Šilalė district.  It offers primary, basic, secondary and optional education programmes.  Institution code 190329256.
