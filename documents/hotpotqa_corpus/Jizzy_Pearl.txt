Jizzy Pearl: Jizzy Pearl (born James Wilkinson) is an American vocalist that was born on March 17, 1958, in Chicago, Illinois.  He's had notable performances in several heavy metal and hard rock groups.  He first fronted the band Data Clan, which eventually became Love/Hate.  Pearl has also sung for L.A. Guns, Ratt, Adler's Appetite, and other, lesser known acts.
