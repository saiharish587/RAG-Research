Yahudi Ki Ladki (1933 film): Yahudi Ki Ladki (The Jew's Daughter) is a 1933 Urdu/Hindi costume drama film directed by Premankur Atorthy.  Produced by New Theatres Ltd.  Calcutta, the cast included K. L. Saigal, Rattan Bai, Pahari Sanyal, Gul Hamid, Nawab and Kumar.  The film was adapted from Agha Hashar Kashmiri’s play of the same name "Yahudi Ki Larki" which had been written in Bengali as "Misar Kumari".  He also wrote the screenplay and lyrics.  The film saw Pankaj Mullick's debut as a Hindi music director.  The dialogues were by Wajahat Mirza.  The story revolves around the rivalry and revenge between the Jewish merchant Prince Ezra and the Roman priest Brutus.
