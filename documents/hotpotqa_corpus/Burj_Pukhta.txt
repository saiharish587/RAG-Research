Burj Pukhta: Burj Pukhta or Burj Pukta (Punjabi: ਬੁਰ੍ਜ ਪੁਖਤਾ ) is a village in Phillaur tehsil of Jalandhar District of Punjab State, India.  It is located 3.3 km away from Nagar, 47.7 km from Jalandhar and 115 km from state capital Chandigarh.  Burj Pukhta has postal head office in Phillaur which is 4.5 km away from the village.  The village is administrated by a sarpanch who is an elected representative of village as per Panchayati raj (India).
