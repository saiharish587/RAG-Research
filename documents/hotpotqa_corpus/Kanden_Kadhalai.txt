Kanden Kadhalai: Kanden Kadhalai (English: "I Saw My Love") is a 2009 Indian Tamil romantic comedy film directed by R. Kannan of "Jayamkondaan" fame.  The film, a remake of the 2007 Hindi blockbuster "Jab We Met" by Imtiaz Ali, stars Bharath and Tamannaah in the lead roles, originally played by Shahid Kapoor and Kareena Kapoor.  The film was released on 30 October 2009 to mainly positive reviews.
