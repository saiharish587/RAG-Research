Karl-Birger Blomdahl: Karl-Birger Blomdahl (19 October 1916 – 14 June 1968) was a Swedish composer and conductor born in Växjö.  He was educated in biochemistry, but was primarily active in music and by his experimental compositions he became one of the big names in Swedish modernism.  His teachers included Hilding Rosenberg.  He died in Kungsängen, Stockholm.
