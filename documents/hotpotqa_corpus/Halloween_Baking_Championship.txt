Halloween Baking Championship: Halloween Baking Championship is an American cooking competition television series that premiered on Food Network on October 5, 2015.  The first season of the series was presented by chef Richard Blais; with fellow chefs Carla Hall, Ron Ben-Israel and Sherry Yard serving as judges.  The second season of the series premiered on October 3, 2016; with Hall being the only chef returning.  She was joined by chefs Sandra Lee and Damiano Carrara as judges; and the season was presented by comedian Jeff Dunham.
