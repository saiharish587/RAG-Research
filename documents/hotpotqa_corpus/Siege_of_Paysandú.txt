Siege of Paysandú: The Siege of Paysandú began 3 December 1864, during the Uruguayan War, when Brazilian forces (under Marquis of Tamandaré) and Colorado forces (under Venancio Flores) attempted to capture the city of Paysandú in Uruguay from its Uruguayan Army defenders.  The siege ended 2 January 1865, when the Brazilian and Colorado forces conquered the town.
