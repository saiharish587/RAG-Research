Suicidal Tendencies discography: The discography of Suicidal Tendencies, a Los Angeles-based crossover thrash band formed in 1980 by vocalist Mike Muir, consists of twelve studio albums, four compilation albums, one extended play, eighteen singles and twenty-two music videos.  Their first studio album, "Suicidal Tendencies", was released in 1983 but failed to chart.  Their second album, "Join the Army", was released four years later and peaked at number 100 in the United States and number 81 in the UK.  The album caught the attention of Epic Records, who signed Suicidal Tendencies in 1988.  The band released their first album for the label, "How Will I Laugh Tomorrow When I Can't Even Smile Today", in September 1988, and in the following year they released "Controlled by Hatred/Feel Like Shit...Déjà Vu", which was their first album to be certified gold by the Recording Industry Association of America (RIAA).
