2016 McDonald's All-American Boys Game: The 2016 McDonald's All-American Boys Game was an All-star basketball game played on Wednesday, March 30, 2016 at the United Center in Chicago, Illinois, home of the Chicago Bulls.  The game's rosters features the best and most highly recruited high school boys graduating in 2016.  The game is the 39th annual version of the McDonald's All-American Game first played in 1977.
