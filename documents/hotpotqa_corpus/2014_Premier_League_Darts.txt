2014 Premier League Darts: The 2014 Betway Premier League Darts was a darts tournament organised by the Professional Darts Corporation; the tenth edition of the tournament.  The event began at the Echo Arena in Liverpool on Thursday 6 February, and ended at The O Arena, London on Thursday 22 May.  The tournament was shown live on Sky Sports in the UK and Ireland.  The new title sponsor was Betway.
