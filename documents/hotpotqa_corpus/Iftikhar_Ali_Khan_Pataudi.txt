Iftikhar Ali Khan Pataudi: Iftikhar Ali Khan, sometimes I. A. K. Pataudi (16 March 1910 – 5 January 1952) was the 8th Nawab of Pataudi and the captain of the India national cricket team for the tour to England in 1946.  His son Mansoor, known as the Nawab of Pataudi Jr., also later served as captain of the India cricket team.
