Into the Light (Phil Stacey album): Into the Light is the second studio album by American singer Phil Stacey.  It is the followup to his 2008 self-titled debut, which was released to country music.  "Into the Light" is a CCM album, released on Reunion Records.  The album's final track, "Old Glory," was previously released as a promotional single during Stacey's 2008 tenure on Lyric Street Records.  "You're Not Shaken" is the first official single from the album.
