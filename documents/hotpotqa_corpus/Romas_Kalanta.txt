Romas Kalanta: Romas Kalanta (February 22, 1953 – May 15, 1972) was a 19-year-old Lithuanian high school student known for his public self-immolation protesting Soviet regime in Lithuania.  Kalanta's death provoked the largest post-war riots in Lithuania and inspired similar self-immolations.  In 1972 alone, 13 more people committed suicide by self-immolation.
