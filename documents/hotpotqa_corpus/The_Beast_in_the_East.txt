The Beast in the East: The Beast in the East was a professional wrestling event produced by WWE.  It took place on July 4, 2015, at the Ryōgoku Kokugikan in Sumida, Tokyo, Japan.  It aired live internationally on the WWE Network and live on J Sports in Japan.  The replays of the event aired on J Sports 4 in Japan.  Additionally, it would be available on demand on both the WWE Network and J Sports.  It was also the first WWE event to be broadcast live from Japan.  The Beast in the East was available to subscribers of the WWE Network, in over 140 countries.
