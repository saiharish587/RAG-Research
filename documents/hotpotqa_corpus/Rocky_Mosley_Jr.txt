Rocky Mosley Jr: Rocky Mosley Jr. (born Roxell Mosley Jr., March 3, 1956 in Riverside, California) is a retired American professional boxer who fought out of Las Vegas, Nevada.  Mosley was the NABF and USBA Junior Middleweight Champion.  At his peak Mosley was ranked as the No. 4 Junior Middleweight in the world by the Ring magazine in 1981, until he lost his North American Championship to Rocky Fratto.  Mosley's biggest wins were a knockout over former Olympic Bronze medalist Johnny Baldwin and a split-decision over Larry Bonds.  Baldwin's only loss at the time was a 10 round decision loss to Marvelous Marvin Hagler.
