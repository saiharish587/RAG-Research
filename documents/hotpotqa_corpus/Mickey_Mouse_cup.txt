Mickey Mouse cup: "Mickey Mouse Cup" is a subjective term used in football, particularly in English football, to negatively describe a cup, league, or other competition as being of a lower standard, importance or significance to a football club or manager relative to other competitions.  The term was first coined by fans of Manchester United as a way of disparaging their rivals Liverpool's achievements and success in the League Cup.  The term has been applied to lesser Confederation competitions such as the UEFA Super Cup.  "Mickey Mouser" itself originated as rhyming slang for Scouser, a person from Liverpool.
