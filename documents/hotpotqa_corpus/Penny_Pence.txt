Penny Pence: Carol Jane "Penny" Pence Taylor (born May 11, 1929), also known by her married name Penny Taylor, is an American former competition swimmer who represented the United States at the 1948 Summer Olympics in London.  She competed in the preliminary heats of the women's 200-meter breaststroke, and finished with a time of 3:28.1.  Pence swam for the Lafayette Swim Club in Indiana and attended Purdue University.  In 1951, when Pence was a finalist for the James E. Sullivan Award, she was part of the US Team for the first Pan American Games in 1951, winning a gold medal in the 3×100 meter medley relay and a bronze medal in the 200 meter breaststroke.
