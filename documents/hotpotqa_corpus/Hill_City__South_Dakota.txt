Hill City, South Dakota: Hill City is the oldest existing city in Pennington County, South Dakota, United States.  The population was 948 at the 2010 census.  Hill City is located 26 mi southwest of Rapid City on State Highway 16 and on U.S. Route 385 that connects Deadwood to Hot Springs.  Hill City is known as the "Heart of the Hills" which is derived from its close proximity to both the geographical center of the Black Hills, and the local tourist destinations.
