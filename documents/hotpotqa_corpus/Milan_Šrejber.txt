Milan Šrejber: Milan Šrejber (] ) (born 30 December 1963) is a former tennis player from Czechoslovakia, who represented his native country at the 1988 Summer Olympics in Seoul.  There he reached the semi finals of the men's doubles competition, partnering Miloslav Mečíř.  The pair was defeated by America's eventual winners Ken Flach and Robert Seguso, but still won the bronze medal.  The right-hander won one career singles title (Rye Brook, 1988), and reached his highest ATP singles ranking of World No. 23 in October 1986.
