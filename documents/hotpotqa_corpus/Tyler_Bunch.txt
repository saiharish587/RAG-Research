Tyler Bunch: Tyler T. Bunch (born February 10, 1970) is an American puppeteer, puppet designer, director, and actor.  In addition to his work for the Jim Henson Company, Bunch has created and performed puppets for PBS, Nickelodeon and Disney's The Muppets Studio.  He has acted in a few Off Broadway productions and has made appearances on American television shows such as "", "Person of Interest" and "" and video games such as "Grand Theft Auto V", "Red Dead Redemption", "" and "Red Dead Revolver".  Currently, he is directing the PBS children's television series "SeeMore's Playhouse".  He is also currently scheduled to be one of the puppeteers for the musical stage adaptation of "Emmet Otter's Jug-Band Christmas".  He resides in Stratford, Connecticut.
