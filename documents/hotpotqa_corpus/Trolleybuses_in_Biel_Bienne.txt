Trolleybuses in Biel/Bienne: The Biel/Bienne trolleybus system (German: "Trolleybus System Biel" ; French: "Réseau trolleybus de Bienne" ) is part of the public transport network of the bilingual city of Biel/Bienne, in the canton of Bern, Switzerland.  The system also serves the neighbouring municipality of Nidau.
