Dead Grandma: Dead Grandma is a 10-episode comedy Web series created by Will Kindrick.  It is written by Kindrick, Courtney Branning, Matt Heder and is produced by Mummy Space Island Productions.  The series follows the life of a hapless young college student Andy (Matt Heder), whose grandma (Beverly Welsh) comes back from the dead to help him find love.  The series is hosted by Jon Heder who begins each episode with a campy intro reminiscent of the openings of nostalgic shows such as Masterpiece Theater and The Wonderful World of Disney.
