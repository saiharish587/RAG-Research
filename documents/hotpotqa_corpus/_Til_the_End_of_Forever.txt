'Til the End of Forever: 'Til the End of Forever is an album by Michael Bolton, released in 2005.  The live cuts on this album were recorded during a DVD taping over two nights of concerts (August 24 and 25, 2004) at the Casino Rama, outside Toronto, Canada.  The recording has been shown on the HDNet show "HDNet Concerts".  A DVD of the concerts was released for sale in Europe in late 2005 and was released for sale in the U.S. in March, 2006, and was titled "The Best of Michael Bolton Live."
