Pasupatheeswarar Temple, Aavoor: Pasupatheeswarar Temple, Aavoor (பசுபதீசுவரர் கோயில், ஆவூர்) or Aavoor Pasupatheeswaram is a Hindu temple dedicated to Hindu god Shiva, located in the village Aavoor, located 12 km south of South Indian town, Kumbakonam, Tamil Nadu.  The temple is one of the 70 "madakoil" built by 2nd century Chola king, Kochengat Chola.  The temple is known for the Panchabairavar, the five images of Bhairavar.  The temple is reverred in the verses of "Tevaram", the 7th century Tamil Saiva canon by Tirugnana Sambandar.
