List of hospitals in North Carolina: This is a list of hospitals in North Carolina.  There are 126 hospitals in 83 counties.  Seventeen counties currently do not have a hospital.  Duke University Hospital is currently the largest hospital, with 943 hospital beds.  Vidant Bertie Hospital is currently the smallest, with six hospital beds.  Charlotte has the highest concentration of hospitals, with seven.  Four hospitals serve as university-affiliated academic medical centers: Duke University Hospital, Vidant Medical Center, UNC Health Care and Wake Forest Baptist Medical Center, while Carolinas Medical Center is unaffiliated.
