DeKalb County Fire and Rescue: DeKalb County Fire and Rescue is the exclusive provider of Fire services for the unincorporated areas of DeKalb County, Georgia.  The agency also serves all incorporated cities in the county with the exception of Decatur and the small portion of Atlanta that extends into DeKalb County from the city's eastern fringes.  Stone Mountain Park which is located within the county is served by its own separate department.  It still relies on the county for assistance with regards to fire and some medical related incidents.  The department serves a population in excess of 700,000.  DeKalb County, Georgia is the state's largest in terms of population density.  In 2015, the agency responded to over 110,000 calls for fire and EMS emergencies; which is more than any other Fire Department in Georgia, including Atlanta.
