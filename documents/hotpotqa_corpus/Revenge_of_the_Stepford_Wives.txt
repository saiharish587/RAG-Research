Revenge of the Stepford Wives: Revenge of the Stepford Wives is a 1980 American made-for-television science fiction-thriller film inspired by the Ira Levin novel "The Stepford Wives".  It was directed by Robert Fuest with a screenplay by David Wiltse and starring Sharon Gless, Julie Kavner, Don Johnson, Arthur Hill, and Audra Lindley.  It is the first in a series of sequels inspired by the 1972 novel and the original 1975 film "The Stepford Wives".
