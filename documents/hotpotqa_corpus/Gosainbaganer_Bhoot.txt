Gosainbaganer Bhoot: Gosaibaganer Bhoot (English: The Phantom Of Gosaibagan Grove , Bengali: গোঁসাইবাগানের ভূত ) is a Bengali comedy-fantasy film directed by art director, production designer, and costume designer Nitish Roy, based on a novel by Bengali writer Shirshendu Mukhopadhyay.  The music/lyrics was composed by Bengali brand Chandrabindoo.
