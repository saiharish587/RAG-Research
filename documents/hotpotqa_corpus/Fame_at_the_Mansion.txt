Fame at the Mansion: Fame at the Mansion was an exclusive black tie event held February 12, 2012, at the Los Angeles Playboy Mansion on the evening of the 54th Grammy Awards.  The event, hosted by Grammy Award winner Sean Combs, featured an honorary ceremony for Playboy's founding father Hugh Hefner and was a media heavy fundraiser for the Angelwish Foundation, which supports children and families with chronic illness.  Hefner was honored as a Humanitarian of the Year by the Angelwish Foundation.  Following the event, Diddy was featured on "Jimmy Kimmel Live! ", and was quoted as saying that it was the "best party he has ever thrown."
