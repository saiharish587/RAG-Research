Atherton Bridge: The Atherton Bridge is a historic iron truss bridge in Lancaster, Massachusetts, spanning the South Branch of the Nashua River.  It is a rare example of a hybrid pony truss that is similar to the 19th century truss design of Simeon S. Post.  It was built by J.H. Cofrode & Co. of Philadelphia in 1870.  It was the first iron bridge to be constructed in the town. <ref name="memory.loc.gov/cgi-bin/query/D?hh:1:./temp/~ammem_Pr5I::">Historic American Engineering Record</ref> The bridge was added to the National Register of Historic Places in 1979.
