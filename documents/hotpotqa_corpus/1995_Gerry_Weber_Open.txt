1995 Gerry Weber Open: The 1995 Gerry Weber Open was a men's tennis tournament played on outdoor grass courts.  It was the 3rd edition of the Gerry Weber Open, and was part of the World Series of the 1995 ATP Tour.  It took place at the Gerry Weber Stadion in Halle, North Rhine-Westphalia, Germany, from 19 June through 26 June 1995.  Marc Rosset won the singles title.
