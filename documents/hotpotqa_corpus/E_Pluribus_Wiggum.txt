E Pluribus Wiggum: "E Pluribus Wiggum" is the tenth episode of "The Simpsons"<nowiki>'</nowiki> nineteenth season.  It first aired on the Fox network in the United States on January 6, 2008.  It was written by Michael Price and directed by Michael Polcino, and it guest starred Jon Stewart and Dan Rather as themselves.  This episode became controversial in Argentina for a joke made about the government of Juan Perón.
