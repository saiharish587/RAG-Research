Volume 6: Black Anvil Ego: Volume 6: Black Anvil Ego is the sixth extended play (EP) by American desert rock collective The Desert Sessions.  Recorded in March 1999 at Rancho De La Luna, it was released by Man's Ruin Records on July 21, 1999.  The album features eleven credited musicians, including Josh Homme, Dave Catching and Gene Trautmann.  It was later re-released with "" as "Volumes 5 & 6".
