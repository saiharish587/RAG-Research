1991 San Jose State Spartans football team: The 1991 San Jose State Spartans football team represented San Jose State University during the 1991 NCAA Division I-A football season as a member of the Big West Conference.  The team was led by head coach Terry Shea, in his second year as head coach at San Jose State.  They played their home games at Spartan Stadium in San Jose, California.  The Spartans finished the 1991 season as co-champions of the Big West conference, with a record of six wins, four losses and one tie (6–4–1, 6–1 Big West).  To date, this remains their last conference title.
