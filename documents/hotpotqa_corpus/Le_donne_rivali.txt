Le donne rivali: Le donne rivali is an intermezzo in two acts by composer Domenico Cimarosa with an Italian libretto by a now unknown poet.  It is speculated that Giuseppe Petrosellini may have been the author of the libretto.  The opera premiered at the Teatro Valle in Rome during Carnival in 1780.  The original choreography was created by Alberto Cavos, the original costumes by Antonio Dian, and the original scenery by Domenico Fossati.  Music critics particularly admire the final quintet that closes the opera.  In recent years, "Le donne rivali" has been occasionally revived and recorded.  In 1991, the Juilliard Opera Center intertwined the work with Mozart's unfinished opera "Lo sposo deluso", which shares the same libretto, to make one large work.
