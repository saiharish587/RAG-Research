Japanese Culture Channel Sakura: Japanese Culture Channel Sakura (日本文化チャンネル桜 , Nihon Bunka Channeru Sakura ) is a Japanese television channel and video-sharing website.  It is known for its support for uyoku dantai and Japanese right-wing, nationalist causes.  It is also called Channel Sakura.  It was founded in 2004 and the main spokesperson is Satoru Mizushima.
