Gallifrey: Series 4: Gallifrey: Series 4 is a set of Big Finish Productions audio dramas based on the long-running British science fiction television series "Doctor Who".  The series is set on the Doctor's home planet of Gallifrey.  The four dramas were released in a box set in 2011, and were directed by Gary Russell and produced by Jason Haigh-Ellery and Nicholas Briggs.
