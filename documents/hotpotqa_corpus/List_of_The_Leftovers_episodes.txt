List of The Leftovers episodes: "The Leftovers" is an American television drama series created by Damon Lindelof and Tom Perrotta airing on HBO.  It is based on Perrotta's novel of the same name.  The series features an ensemble cast that includes Justin Theroux, Amy Brenneman, Christopher Eccleston, Liv Tyler, Chris Zylka, Margaret Qualley, Carrie Coon, Ann Dowd, and Regina King.
