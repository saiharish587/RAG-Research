Electoral district of Ramsay: Ramsay is an electoral district of the House of Assembly in the Australian state of South Australia.  It is named after Alexander Ramsay, who was General Manager of the South Australian Housing Trust for 25 years.  It is a 24.7 km² suburban electorate north of Adelaide—based on the angle between Main North Road and the Port Wakefield Road, Ramsay covers the outer northern Adelaide suburbs of Brahma Lodge, Parafield, Salisbury, Salisbury Downs, Salisbury Plain and Salisbury South as well as parts of Parafield Gardens, Paralowie and Salisbury North.
