The Woman in You: "The Woman in You" is one of five songs the Bee Gees contributed to the film, "Staying Alive", the sequel to "Saturday Night Fever".  Director Sylvester Stallone used the Bee Gees songs in the movie more as background music rather than the prominent way "Saturday Night Fever" had featured them.  The single received more airplay than the Bee Gees previous two singles, though not enough to reach a top 10 position.  In interviews following the release of the film, the brothers expressed their displeasure at the way their songs were edited and revealed that their hearts were not in the music.  By 1983, the Bee Gees were focusing their talents on solo projects and production of other artists, so it is not surprising that they were not all enthusiastic about the "Staying Alive" movie.
