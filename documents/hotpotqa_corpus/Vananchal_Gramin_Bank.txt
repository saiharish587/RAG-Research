Vananchal Gramin Bank: Vananchal Gramin Bank (VGB) (Hindi: ) is a Regional Rural Bank (RRB_).  The bank was established on 30 June 2006 with the amalgamation of the erstwhile "Santhal Parganas Gramin Bank" and erstwhile "Palamau Kshetriya Gramin Bank" under the provisions of RRB Act 1976.  This bank is sponsored by State Bank of India & is jointly Owned by the Government of India, Government of Jharkhand and State Bank of India.
