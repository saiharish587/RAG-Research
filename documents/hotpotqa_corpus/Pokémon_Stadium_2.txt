Pokémon Stadium 2: Pokémon Stadium 2, known as Pocket Monsters' Stadium Kin Gin (ポケモンスタジアム金銀 , Pokemon Sutajiamu Kin Gin , lit.: Pokémon Stadium Gold Silver) in Japan, is a strategy video game developed by Nintendo EAD and published by Nintendo for the Nintendo 64.  It features all 251 Pokémon from the first and second generations of the franchise.  It was released on December 14, 2000, in Japan, March 26, 2001, in North America, and October 10, 2001, in Europe.  In Western regions it was titled "Pokémon Stadium 2", as it was the second "Stadium" game to be released outside Japan, in which it was the third game in the series.  It supports Dolby Surround sound.
