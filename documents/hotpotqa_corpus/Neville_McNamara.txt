Neville McNamara: Air Chief Marshal Sir Neville Patrick McNamara, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (17 April 1923 – 7 May 2014) was a senior commander of the Royal Australian Air Force (RAAF).  He served as Chief of the Air Staff (CAS), the RAAF's highest-ranking position, from 1979 until 1982, and as Chief of the Defence Force Staff (CDFS), Australia's top military role at the time, from 1982 until 1984.  He was the second RAAF officer to hold the rank of air chief marshal.
