Panzer Dragoon: Panzer Dragoon is a series of video games by Sega, created first by its internal Team Andromeda and later, the Smilebit development team.  Aside from the role-playing video game "Panzer Dragoon Saga", the games are of the rail shooter genre.  All games follow the story of a lone hero or heroine fighting an evil empire in a post apocalyptic world, while riding a Dragon.
