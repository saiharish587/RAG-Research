Half an Hour: Half an Hour is a 1920 American silent drama film directed by Harley Knoles and written by Clara Beranger.  The film stars Dorothy Dalton, Charles Richman, Albert L. Barrett, Frank Losee, and H. Cooper Cliffe.  It is based on the play "Half an Hour" by J. M. Barrie.  The film was released on September 19, 1920, by Paramount Pictures.
