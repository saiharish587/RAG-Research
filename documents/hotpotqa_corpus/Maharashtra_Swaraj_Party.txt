Maharashtra Swaraj Party: Maharashtra Swaraj Party (MSP) is a regional political party formed by the East Indian community in Mumbai, India.  The party's name draws inspiration from the phrase "Swaraj is my Birthright", coined by East Indian freedom fighter and Mumbai's first mayor of Indian origin, Joseph 'Kaka' Baptista.  Kaka Baptista was a close associate of Lokmanya Tilak who made the phrase popular.
