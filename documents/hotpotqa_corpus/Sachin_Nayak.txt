Sachin Nayak: Sachin Nayak(Hindi: सचिन नायक ), is an Indian actor and model, known primarily for his roles in Bollywood films.  He was born in the village of Tada, in the Sagar, Madhya Pradesh district.  He graduated from Dr. Hari Singh Gour University with a degree in the Performing Arts.  Nayak began acting at the age of 8.  He appeared in Rajat Kapoor's 2009 film .  He has appeared in over 75 Hindi films and TV commercials.
