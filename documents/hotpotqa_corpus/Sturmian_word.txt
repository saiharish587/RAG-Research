Sturmian word: In mathematics, a Sturmian word (Sturmian sequence or billiard sequence), named after Jacques Charles François Sturm, is a certain kind of infinitely long sequence of characters.  Such a sequence can be generated by considering a game of English billiards on a square table.  The struck ball will successively hit the vertical and horizontal edges labelled 0 and 1 generating a sequence of letters.  This sequence is a Sturmian word.
