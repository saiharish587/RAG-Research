Jeremiah Porter: Porter was born in manhattan.  He grew up in New York City and Lorton, Virginia.  He is a current member of Texas State University but his home resides in Brooklyn.  He is the only child of Corinne Douglas and his father Jerry Porter has another daughter named Nakayla Porter.  Porter attended the Professional Performing Arts School in Manhattan for drama in the fall of 2013.  In 2015 Porter was adopted by Glenn Cook, the father of Ben Cook (actor) and attended the Metropolitan School of the Arts.  He was among the first class to have graduated high school from The Metropolitan School of the Arts in 2016.  While attending school he also attended French Woods Festival of the Performing Arts annually in the summer as part of his Musical theatre training.
