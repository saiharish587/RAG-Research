James Briggs (musician): Randall James Briggs, Jr. (born February 27, 1978), credited professionally as James R. Briggs, Jr., is an American musician, best known as the keyboardist for the Orange County rock band The Aquabats, in which he has served as a member since 1997 under the stage name and persona of Jimmy the Robot.  Briggs also portrayed this role on The Aquabats' television series "The Aquabats!  Super Show! " during the show's run from 2012 to 2014.
