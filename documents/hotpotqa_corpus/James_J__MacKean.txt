James J. MacKean: James J. MacKean was an Irish politician.  He was a Cumann na nGaedheal member of the Free State Seanad Éireann from 1922 to 1936.  He was first elected to the Seanad in 1922 for 9 years and was re-elected in 1931 for 9 years.  He served until the Free State Seanad was abolished in 1936.
