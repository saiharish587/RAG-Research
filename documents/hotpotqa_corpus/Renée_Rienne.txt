Renée Rienne: Renée Rienne (real name Renée Goursaud, alias "Le Corbeau", "The Raven") is a fictional character in the spy-fi television series "Alias".  Played by Élodie Bouchez, she was introduced as a new character for the fifth season.  Bouchez appears in the opening credit sequence during the first half of the fifth season; beginning with "S.O.S." Bouchez and her character were removed from the opening and listed as a special guest star.
