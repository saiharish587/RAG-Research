Torchy Runs for Mayor: Torchy Runs for Mayor is a 1939 American drama-comedy film directed by Ray McCarey.  The film stars Glenda Farrell and Barton MacLane.  This is the eighth film in the Torchy Blane movie series by Warner Bros. It was released on May 13, 1939.  The film is followed by "Torchy Blane... Playing with Dynamite".
