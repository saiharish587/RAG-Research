West Low German: West Low German, also known as Low Saxon (German: "Niedersächsisch" or German: "Westniederdeutsch" ; literally: "Nether-saxon"; Low German: "Nedersassisch, Nedersaksies, Platduuts, Plat(t)" ; Dutch: "Nedersaksisch" ; ) is a group of Low German (also Low Saxon; German: "Niederdeutsch" or "Plattdeutsch", Dutch: "Nederduits") dialects spoken in parts of the Netherlands, northwestern Germany and southern Denmark (in North Schleswig by the German minority).  It is one of two groups of mutually intelligible dialects, the other being East Low German dialects.
