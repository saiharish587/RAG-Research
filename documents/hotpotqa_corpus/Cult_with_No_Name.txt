Cult with No Name: Cult With No Name (often abbreviated to CWNN) is a musical duo from London, UK, comprising Erik Stein and Jon Boux.  Influenced largely by electronic music, post-punk and modern classical music, they refer to themselves 'post-punk electronic balladeers'.  Formed in 2004, the band were signed to Los Angeles label Trakwerx in 2007, founded by Jackson Del Rey of Californian post-punk band Savage Republic and 17 Pygmies.  Now on their own 'CWNN Music' label, they are distributed worldwide through Darla Records, home of Harold Budd and My Morning Jacket.
