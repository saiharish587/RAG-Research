Quincy, Illinois: Quincy ( ), known as Illinois's ""Gem City"," is a city in and the county seat of Adams County, Illinois, United States, located on the Mississippi River.  The 2010 census counted a population of 40,633 in the city itself, up from 40,366 in 2000.  As of July 1, 2015, the Quincy Micro Area had an estimated population of 77,220.
