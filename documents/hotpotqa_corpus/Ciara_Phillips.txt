Ciara Phillips: Ciara Phillips is a Canadian Artist of Irish Ancestry based primarily in Glasgow, United Kingdom.  Phillips was born in Ottawa, Canada.  Her higher education was completed, first, at Queen’s University in Kingston, Ontario, Canada (Bachelor of Fine Art — 1996/2000).  Subsequently, she studied at the Glasgow School of Art in Glasgow, United Kingdom, obtaining a Master in Fine Art (2002/2004).  Her work exploits the traditional use of Printmaking practices, taking much influence from collaboration and, more personally, the philosophies of artist and teacher "Corita Kent".  On 7 May 2014, she was nominated for the "Turner Prize" for her work at "The Showroom Gallery" in London, England.
