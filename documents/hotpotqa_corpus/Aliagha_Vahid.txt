Aliagha Vahid: Aliagha Vahid (Azerbaijani: "Əliağa Vahid" ), born Aliagha Mammadqulu oglu Isgandarov (17 February 1895, Baku – 1 October 1965, Baku), was an Azerbaijani poet and Honoured Artist of the Azerbaijan SSR (1943).  He was known for reintroducing medieval ghazel style in Soviet Azerbaijani poetry.
