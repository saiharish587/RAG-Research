The Rolling Stones US Tour 1978: The Rolling Stones' US Tour 1978 was a concert tour of the United States that took place during June and July 1978, immediately following the release of the group's 1978 album "Some Girls".  Like the 1972 and 1975 U.S. tours, Bill Graham was the tour promoter.  One opening act was Peter Tosh, who was sometimes joined by Mick Jagger for their duet "Don't Look Back".
