Jeanne Daman: Jeanne Daman (1918-86) is one of the Righteous Among the Nations.  She helped rescue two thousand Jewish children from the Nazis by taking them to shelters.  After the war she helped to find the children so they could be brought back to their families, and helped care for children who had survived the concentration camps.  Daman also took Jewish women to be maids in Belgian households, giving them false identity papers and ration cards, and attempting to keep them informed of where their children were hiding.
