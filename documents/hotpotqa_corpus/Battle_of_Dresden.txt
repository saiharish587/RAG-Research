Battle of Dresden: The Battle of Dresden (26–27 August 1813) was a major engagement of the Napoleonic Wars.  The battle took place around the city of Dresden in modern-day Germany.  With the recent addition of Austria, the Sixth Coalition felt emboldened in their quest to kick the French out of Central Europe.  Despite being heavily outnumbered, French forces under Napoleon scored a modest victory against the Allied army led by Field Marshal Schwarzenberg.  However, Napoleon's victory did not lead to the collapse of the coalition, and the lack of effective French cavalry units precluded a major pursuit.  A few days after the battle, the Allies surrounded and captured a French corps at the Battle of Kulm.
