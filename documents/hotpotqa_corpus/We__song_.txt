We (song): On March 23, 2016, Pledis Entertainment announced they were to debut a new girl group, temporarily called Pledis Girlz.  7 of the 10 members, Eunwoo, Kyulkyung, Nayoung, Rena, Roa, Xiyeon and Yuha, previously competed during the first season of Mnet's survival show Produce 101, where Kyulkyung and Nayoung became finalists and debuted as part of the group I.O.I.
