Hillside Terrace, New Jersey: Hillside Terrace is an unincorporated community located within Robbinsville Township in Mercer County, New Jersey, United States.  The community consists of single-family houses on both sides of Robbinsville-Allentown Road (County Route 526) between U.S. Route 130 and the New Jersey Turnpike.  The original section of the neighborhood near the intersection of CR 526 and Spring Garden Road was constructed between the late 1950s and the early 1960s.  A more modern subdivision was constructed on the southwestern side of CR 526 by 1995 consisting of larger homes.
