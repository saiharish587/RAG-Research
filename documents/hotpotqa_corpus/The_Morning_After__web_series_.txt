The Morning After (web series): The Morning After is a Hulu original web series that premiered on January 17, 2011 and ended April 24, 2014.  It was produced by Hulu and Jace Hall's HDFilms, streaming Monday through Friday.  The show originally featured Brian Kimmet and Ginger Gonzaga as hosts.  Later shows used a rotation of hosts including Alison Haislip, Dave Holmes, Damien Fahey, Bradley Hasemeyer, Haley Mancini, Paul Nyhart, and Rachel Perry.
