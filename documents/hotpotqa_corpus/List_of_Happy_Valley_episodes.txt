List of Happy Valley episodes: "Happy Valley" is a British crime drama television series created by Sally Wainwright and produced by Red Production Company.  The first series of six episodes started airing on BBC One in the United Kingdom on 29 April 2014.  It was released on Netflix in the United States and Canada on 20 August 2014.  A second series of six episodes started airing on BBC One on 9 February 2016 and was made available on Netflix in the US later that year.
