Final Curtain (film): Final Curtain was a television pilot written and directed by Ed Wood in 1957.  It starred Duke Moore as an actor wandering an empty theater after dark, where he is haunted by creepy sounds and eerie sights.  The film is narrated by Dudley Manlove.  Both Moore and Manlove also appear in Wood's cult film "Plan 9 from Outer Space".  Wood had hoped to use the film as the pilot for a television series he intended to produce called "Portraits of Terror."
