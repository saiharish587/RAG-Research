Weston by Welland: Weston by Welland is a village and civil parish in the north of the English county of Northamptonshire administered as part of the borough of Kettering.  As its name suggests, it is near to the River Welland that, thereabouts, forms the boundary with the county of Leicestershire.  The Wheel & Compass pub stands on the village's outskirts.  At the time of the 2001 census, the parish's population was 141 people, including Sutton Bassett and increasing to 246 at the 2011 Census.
