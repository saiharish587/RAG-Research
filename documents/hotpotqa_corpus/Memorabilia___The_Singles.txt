Memorabilia – The Singles: Memorabilia – The Singles is a compilation album featuring songs by the British singer/songwriter Marc Almond as a solo artist and with his partner Dave Ball as the synthpop duo Soft Cell.  The album is a compilation for both Almond and Soft Cell, it was released in 1991 and reached number eight on the UK Albums Chart.  "Memorabilia – The Singles" was promoted by the singles "Say Hello, Wave Goodbye '91" and "Tainted Love '91.
