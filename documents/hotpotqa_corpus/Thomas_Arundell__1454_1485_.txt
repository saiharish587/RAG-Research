Thomas Arundell (1454–1485): Sir Thomas Arundell (1454–1485) was an English nobleman.  He was made a Knight of the Bath at the Coronation of Richard III in 1483.  Two years later, when Richard III was defeated at the Battle of Bosworth (1485), he was attainted for rebelling against the King.  Arundell then gave his support to Henry Tudor in his claim to the throne.  His marriage to the heiress, Katherine Dynham, brought great wealth to the Arundell family.  She was one of the four sisters and coheirs of John Dynham, 1st Baron Dynham.
