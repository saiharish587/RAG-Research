Revolution (2017 TV series): Revolution () is an upcoming South Korean television series starring Choi Si-won, Kang So-ra and Gong Myung.  The series marks Choi Si-won's first acting project after his military service.  It is set to air on tvN starting October 14, 2017 at 21:00 KST, replacing "Live Up to Your Name, Dr. Heo".
