3 Arts Entertainment: 3 Arts Entertainment is a Beverly Hills–based talent management and television/film production company founded by Erwin Stoff in 1991 in preparation for producing the television show "Down the Shore".  The company is best known for producing comedic TV shows "Parks and Recreation", "The Mindy Project", "Brooklyn Nine-Nine", "It's Always Sunny in Philadelphia", "Unbreakable Kimmy Schmidt" but also for blockbuster and dramatic films "Edge of Tomorrow", "Unbroken" and "".
