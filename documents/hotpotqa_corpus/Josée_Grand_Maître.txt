Josée Grand'Maître: Josée Grand'Maître (born July 28, 1961) is a Canadian racquetball player from Hull, Quebec.  Grand'Maître is the current Canadian Women's Doubles Champion, winning her 14th doubles title in Langley, British Columbia with Jennifer Saunders in May 2013.  Grand'Maître's also been the Canadian Women's Champion in singles three times, and her 18 titles overall tie her for most by a Canadian with Jennifer Saunders.
