Peabody High School (Tennessee): Peabody High School is a public high school in Trenton, Tennessee, operated by the Trenton Special School District for grades 9-12.  The school mascot is The Golden Tide and school colors are black and gold.  The Trenton campus of Dyersburg State Community College is adjacent to the Peabody campus.  This allows Peabody students an opportunity to obtain college credit by taking courses at Dyersburg State while attending high school.
