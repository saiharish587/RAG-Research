Andrew Gillum: Andrew D. Gillum (born July 26, 1979) is an American politician who serves as the mayor and formerly served as a city commissioner of Tallahassee, Florida.  At the age of 23, Gillum became the youngest person ever elected to the Tallahassee City Commission in February 2003.  Gillum has declared his candidacy for Governor of Florida in the 2018 election.
