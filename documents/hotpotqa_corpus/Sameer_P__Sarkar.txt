Sameer P. Sarkar: Sameer P. Sarkar is a consultant in psychiatry and forensic psychiatry, almost entirely in private practice.  He trained in forensic psychiatry at the Maudsley Hospital, London and the University of Massachusetts Amherst.  In addition, he studied Law at Harvard University and at Northumbria University.  He teaches psychiatric ethics at two London medical schools.  He sits on the Ethics committee of the Royal College of Psychiatrists and previously also sat on the College's Law committee.
