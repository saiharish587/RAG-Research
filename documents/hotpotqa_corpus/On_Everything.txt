On Everything: "On Everything" is a song by American musician DJ Khaled featuring American rappers Travis Scott, Rick Ross and Big Sean.  It appeared on Khaled's tenth studio album, "Grateful" (2017).  The song was written by DJ Khaled, Travis Scott, Rick Ross, Big Sean and Danja, the latter also co-produced it with Khaled.  The song contains a recurring sample of "Under the Sun" by Mark Pritchard.
