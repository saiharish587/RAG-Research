Charlie McCoy: Charles Ray "Charlie" McCoy (born March 28, 1941 in Oak Hill, West Virginia) is a Grammy-winning American session musician noted mainly for his harmonica performance, but also for his skill on a wide variety of instruments.  In 2009 he was inducted into the Country Music Hall of Fame.  Based in Nashville, McCoy has performed with musicians including Elvis Presley, Bob Dylan, Johnny Cash, Chet Atkins, Waylon Jennings and Loretta Lynn.  He has recorded thirty-seven studio albums, including fourteen for Monument Records.  Thirteen of his singles have entered the "Billboard" country charts.  He was a member of Area Code 615 and Barefoot Jerry.  In 2007 McCoy was inducted into the International Musicians Hall of Fame as a part a group of session musicians dubbed " The A-Team".
