Død Kalm: "Død Kalm" is the nineteenth episode of the second season of the American science fiction television series "The X-Files".  It premiered on the Fox network on March 10, 1995.  The story was written by Howard Gordon, the teleplay was written by Gordon and Alex Gansa, and the episode was directed by Rob Bowman.  The episode is a "Monster-of-the-Week" story, unconnected to the series' wider mythology.  "Død Kalm" earned a Nielsen household rating of 10.7, being watched by 10.2 million households in its initial broadcast.  The episode received mostly mixed-to-positive reviews.
