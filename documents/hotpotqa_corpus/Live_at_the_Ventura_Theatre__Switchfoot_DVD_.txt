Live at the Ventura Theatre (Switchfoot DVD): Live at Ventura Theatre is a live performance DVD featuring the rock band, Switchfoot.  It was recorded live March 29, 2007 on one of the stops during the band's spring leg of the Oh!  Gravity.  Tour.  It is the first DVD to be released under the band's own label, lowercase people records.  The DVD features the performance, as well as a short mini-documentary about the event.
