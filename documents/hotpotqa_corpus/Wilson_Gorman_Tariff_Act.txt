Wilson–Gorman Tariff Act: The Revenue Act or Wilson-Gorman Tariff of 1894 (ch.  349, §73, 28 Stat.  570 , August 27, 1894) slightly reduced the United States tariff rates from the numbers set in the 1890 McKinley tariff and imposed a 2% income tax.  It is named for William L. Wilson, Representative from West Virginia, chair of the U.S. House Ways and Means Committee, and Senator Arthur P. Gorman of Maryland, both Democrats.
