Vilnius Cathedral: The Cathedral of Vilnius (Lithuanian: "Vilniaus Šv.  Stanislovo ir Šv.  Vladislovo arkikatedra bazilika" , Polish: "Bazylika archikatedralna św.  Stanisława Biskupa i św.  Władysława" ) is the main Roman Catholic Cathedral of Lithuania.  It is situated in Vilnius Old Town, just off of Cathedral Square.  Dedicated to Saints Stanislaus and Ladislaus, the church is the heart of Catholic spiritual life in Lithuania.
