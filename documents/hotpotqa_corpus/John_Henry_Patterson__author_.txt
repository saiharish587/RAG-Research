John Henry Patterson (author): Lieutenant-Colonel John Henry Patterson, DSO (10 November 1867 – 18 June 1947), known as J. H. Patterson, was an Irish born British soldier, hunter, author and Christian Zionist, best known for his book "The Man-Eaters of Tsavo" (1907), which details his experiences while building a railway bridge over the Tsavo river in British East Africa (now Kenya) in 1898–99.  The book has inspired three Hollywood films - "Bwana Devil" (1952), "Killers of Kilimanjaro" (1959) and "The Ghost and the Darkness" (1996) in which he was portrayed by Val Kilmer.
