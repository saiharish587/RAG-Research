Káťa Kabanová: Káťa Kabanová (also known in various spellings including "Katia", "Katja", "Katya", and "Kabanowa") is an opera in three acts, with music by Leoš Janáček to a libretto by , based on "The Storm", a play by Alexander Ostrovsky.  The opera was also largely inspired by Janáček's love for Kamila Stösslová.  This is often considered his first "mature" opera, despite the fact that he was 67 when it was premiered.  "Káťa Kabanová" is a clear response to Janáček's feelings for Kamila, and the work is dedicated to her.  The first performance was at the National Theatre (Národní divadlo v Brně ) in Brno on 23 November 1921.
