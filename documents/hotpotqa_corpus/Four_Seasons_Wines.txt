Four Seasons Wines: Four Seasons Wines Limited is an Indian winery which was established in 2006,based in Bangalore, India.  It produces wines from grapes grown around Sahyadri valley in Maharashtra. It is a subsidiary of United Spirits Limited(USL) of the UB Group.  Four Seasons Wines Limited manufactures and markets wines in India.  It provides red, white, and rose wines.  Four Seasons Wines markets its wines under the two brand names Zinzi and Four Seasons.
