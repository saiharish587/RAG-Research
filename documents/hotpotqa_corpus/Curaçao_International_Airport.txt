Curaçao International Airport: Hato International Airport or Curaçao International Airport (formerly "Dr. Albert Plesman International Airport") (IATA: CUR, ICAO: TNCC) is the airport of Willemstad, Curaçao.  It has services to the Caribbean region, South America, North America and Europe.  Hato Airport is a fairly large facility, with the third longest commercial runway in the Caribbean region after Rafael Hernández Airport in Puerto Rico and Pointe-à-Pitre International Airport in Guadeloupe.  The airport serves as a main base for Insel Air.
