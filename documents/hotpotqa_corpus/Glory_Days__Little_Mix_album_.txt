Glory Days (Little Mix album): Glory Days is the fourth studio album by British girl group Little Mix.  It was released on 18 November 2016, by Syco Music and Columbia Records.  The album was preceded by the UK number-one single "Shout Out to My Ex", released a month before.  The single later won the Best British Single award at 2017 BRIT Awards.  The album debuted at number one on the UK Albums Chart; it later became their first album to yield more than two top ten singles when the fourth single, "Power", reached number six on the UK Singles Chart.  The album has sold over 600,000 copies in the U.K and over 1 million copies worlwide.
