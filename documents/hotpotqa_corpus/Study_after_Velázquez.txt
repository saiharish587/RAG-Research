Study after Velázquez: Study after Velázquez is a large 1950 panel painting by the Irish born, English artist Francis Bacon.  After "Head VI", it is the second of Bacon's long series of paintings influenced by Diego Velázquez's 1650 "Portrait of Innocent X".  The panel shows a full length view of the pope, engulfed in vertical folds that may be either the linings of a curtain or the bars of a cage.
