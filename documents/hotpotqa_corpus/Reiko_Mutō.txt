Reiko Mutō: Reiko Mutō (武藤 礼子 or 武藤 禮子 , Mutō Reiko , March 1, 1935 – October 29, 2006, born in Tokyo, Japan) was a Japanese voice actress.  She is best known for playing the voice of Elizabeth Taylor in Japanese dubbed television and films, and playing main characters in "Marvelous Melmo" (as Melmo), "Astro Boy" as Uran (Astro Girl in the English version), and "Moomin" (as Non Non).  As a child, she found she enjoyed acting after joining the child acting troupe Gekidan Komadori.
