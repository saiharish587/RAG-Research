Hagibis (band): Hagibis is a Filipino singing group from the Philippines which was formed in Manila in 1979.  They were composed of Bernie Fineza (died January 15, 2015), Mike Respall, Sonny Parsons, Joji Garcia and Mon Picazzo.  Mike Hanopol was the group's producer and main songwriter.  Best known for their macho image and their equally macho songs like "Katawan", Hagibis has been coined as "Village People of the Philippines", a title which they still hold today.
