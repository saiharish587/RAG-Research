Ursula St Barbe: Ursula St Barbe (died 18 June 1602) (aka Ursula, Lady Worsley and Ursula, Lady Walsingham) was a lady at the court of Queen Elizabeth I of England.  She was the daughter of Henry St Barbe, of Ashington, Somerset, by his wife, Eleanor Lewknor.  She first married Sir Richard Worsley, who was the captain of the Isle of Wight.  After his death, she married Sir Francis Walsingham in 1566.  The following year, her two sons by Worsley, John and George, were killed along with others in an accidental gunpowder explosion at the Worsley estate on the Isle of Wight, Appuldurcombe.  Gunpowder had been laid out to dry in the gatehouse, where the boys had lessons, when a stray spark ignited it.  With Walsingham, Ursula had two daughters: Frances, who was born in about October 1567 and married Sir Philip Sidney, and Mary, who was born in early January 1573 and died in 1580.
