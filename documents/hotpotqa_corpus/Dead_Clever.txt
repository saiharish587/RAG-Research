Dead Clever: Dead Clever is a British black comedy film, first screened on ITV on New Year's Day, 2007.  Written by Sally Wainwright, it stars Suranne Jones, Helen Baxendale and Dean Lennox Kelly.  Although officially titled "Dead Clever" it was subtitled "The Life and Crimes of Julie Bottomley".  The music was written by BAFTA nominated TV & film music composer Sheridan Tongue.
