Ted Kooshian: Ted Kooshian (born October 8, 1961) is an American jazz pianist, keyboardist, and composer, who has performed with Aretha Franklin, Chuck Berry, Marvin Hamlisch, Sarah Brightman, Il Divo, and Blood, Sweat, and Tears.  Kooshian has played in many Broadway pit orchestras, and has been a member of the Ed Palermo Big Band since 1994.  Originally from San Jose, California, Kooshian has been performing since the 1980s.
