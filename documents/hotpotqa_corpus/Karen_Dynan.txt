Karen Dynan: Karen Dynan is an American economist.  She previously served as the Assistant Secretary of the Treasury for Economic Policy and Chief Economist of the United States Department of the Treasury.  From 2009 to 2013, Dr. Dynan was the vice president and co-director of the Economic Studies program at the Brookings Institution.  Prior to joining Brookings, she served on the staff of the Federal Reserve Board for 17 years.  Dr. Dynan is an expert on macroeconomic policy.
