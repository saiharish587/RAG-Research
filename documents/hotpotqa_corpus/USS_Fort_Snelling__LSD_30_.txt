USS Fort Snelling (LSD-30): USS "Fort Snelling" (LSD-30) was a "Thomaston"-class dock landing ship of the United States Navy.  She was named for Fort Snelling at the confluence of the Minnesota and Mississippi Rivers, for many years the northernmost military post in the land of the Sioux and Chippewa.  She was the second ship assigned that name, but the construction of "Fort Snelling" (LSD-23) was canceled on 17 August 1945.
