Nearer the Moon: Nearer the Moon: From a Journal of Love (full title "Nearer the Moon: From a Journal of Love, the Unexpurgated Diary of Anaïs Nin (1937–1939)") is a 1996 book based upon material excerpted from the unpublished diaries of Anaïs Nin.  It corresponds temporally to part of Nin's published diaries.  It consists mainly of material that was left out of the published version because it would have hurt people involved or their relationships with Anaïs Nin had it been published at the time.
