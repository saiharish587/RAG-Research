KLPac Sinfonietta: The Kuala Lumpur Performing Arts Centre Orchestra (klpac Orchestra) (formerly known as klpac Sinfonietta until 2008) was an amateur symphony orchestra located in Kuala Lumpur, Malaysia.  It presented performances at the Kuala Lumpur Performing Arts Centre (klpac), which fostered creative excellence and develops and nurtures a performing arts culture in Malaysia.  At the time that the Centre was proposed a music conservatory was part of the proposal, but the cost proved to be too high at that time.
