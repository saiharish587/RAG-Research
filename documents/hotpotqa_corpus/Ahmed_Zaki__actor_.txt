Ahmed Zaki (actor): Ahmed Zaki Metwally Badawi (Arabic:احمد زكي متولى بدوى‎ ) (November 18, 1949 – March 27, 2005) was a leading Egyptian film star.  He was characterized by his talent, skill and ability in impersonating.  He was also famous for his on-screen intensity, often genuinely hitting co-stars during scenes of violence.  He is widely regarded as the greatest and most talented male star in the history of Arabian cinema.
