PEN/Nabokov Award: The PEN/Nabokov Award for Achievement in International Literature is awarded biannually by the PEN American Center to writers, principally novelists, "whose works evoke to some measure Nabokov's brilliant versatility and commitment to literature as a search for the deepest truth and the highest pleasure— what Nabokov called the 'indescribable tingle of the spine'."  The winner is awarded $50,000 as of 2016.  The award is financed by the Vladimir Nabokov Foundation, founded by Dmitri Nabokov.  It has been called one of the most prestigious PEN prizes.
