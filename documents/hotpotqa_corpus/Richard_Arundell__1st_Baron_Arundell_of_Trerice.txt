Richard Arundell, 1st Baron Arundell of Trerice: Richard Arundell, 1st Baron Arundell of Trerice (1616 – 7 September 1687) of Trerice in Cornwall, was an English politician who sat in the House of Commons at various times between 1640 and 1664 when he was raised to the peerage.  He fought in the Royalist army in the English Civil War.
