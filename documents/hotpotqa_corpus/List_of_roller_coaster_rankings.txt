List of roller coaster rankings: Roller coasters are amusement rides developed for amusement parks and modern theme parks.  During the 16th and 17th centuries, rides consisting of wooden sleds that took riders down large slides made from ice were popular in Russia.  The first roller coasters, where the train was attached to a wooden track, first appeared in France in the early 1800s.  Although wooden roller coasters are still being produced, steel roller coasters are more common and can be found on every continent except Antarctica.
