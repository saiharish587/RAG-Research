Fallen Angel (The X-Files): "Fallen Angel" is the tenth episode of the first season of the American science fiction television series "The X-Files".  It premiered on the Fox network on November 19, 1993.  It was written by Howard Gordon and Alex Gansa, and directed by Larry Shaw.  The episode featured the first of three guest appearances by Scott Bellis as Max Fenig, and saw Jerry Hardin reprise his role as Deep Throat.  The episode helped explore the series' overarching mythology.  The episode was mostly well received.
