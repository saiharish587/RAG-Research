Søren Lindsted: Søren Lindsted (born February 2, 1957) is a Danish former football player in the striker position.  He started his career as a semi-professional with Holbæk Boldklub, reaching the 1976 Danish Cup final and was the team top goalscorer in 1977 and 1978.  He then signed a professional contract with FC Twente in the Netherlands, playing there from 1979 to 1982.  He also played for Belgian teams KFC Winterslag and RFC de Liège, before returning to Holbæk in 1985.  He played a single season with KB, before ending his career with Holbæk in 1990.
