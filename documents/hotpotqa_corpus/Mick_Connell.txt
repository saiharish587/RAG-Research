Mick Connell: Mick "Michael" Connell (born 13 November 1961) is an Australian wheelchair tennis player.  He won a silver medal in the Men's Singles event at the 1988 Seoul Paralympics.  He participated without winning any medals at the 1992 Barcelona Paralympics.  In 1996, he won the men's doubles at the Australian Open with his partner, David Hall.  At the 1996 Atlanta Paralympics, he won a silver medal in the Men's Doubles event with Hall.
