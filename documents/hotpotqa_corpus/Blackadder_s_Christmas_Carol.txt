Blackadder's Christmas Carol: Blackadder's Christmas Carol is a one-off episode of "Blackadder", a parody of Charles Dickens' "A Christmas Carol".  It is set between "Blackadder the Third" (1987) and "Blackadder Goes Forth" (1989), and is narrated by Hugh Laurie.  Produced by the BBC, it was first broadcast on BBC1 on 23 December 1988.
