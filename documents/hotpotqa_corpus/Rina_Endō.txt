Rina Endō: Rina Endō (遠藤 璃菜 , Endō Rina , born October 4, 2005) is a Japanese child actress.  She is represented by Theatre Academy.  Besides acting in television dramas and movies, she also acts on stage and is active in voice acting.  She has also appeared in several commercials.  In 2012, she became one of the Ueno Zoo's Panda Ambassadors and served as a captain.
