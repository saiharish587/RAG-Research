Dortmund Airport: Dortmund Airport (IATA: DTM, ICAO: EDLW) , is a minor international airport located 10 km east of Dortmund, North Rhine-Westphalia.  It serves the eastern Rhine-Ruhr area, the largest urban agglomeration in Germany, and is mainly used for low-cost and leisure charter flights.  Dortmund Airport served approximately 1.9 million passengers in 2013, the nearest major international airport is Düsseldorf Airport approx. 70 km to the southwest.  In 2016, the airport served 1,918,843 passengers.
