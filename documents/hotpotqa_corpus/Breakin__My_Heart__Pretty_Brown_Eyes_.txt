Breakin' My Heart (Pretty Brown Eyes): "Breakin' My Heart (Pretty Brown Eyes)" is a song performed by Mint Condition, issued as the second single from their debut album "Meant to Be Mint".  The song was written by band members Jeffrey Allen, Lawrence Waddell and Stokley Williams.  Released in 1991, the song is their biggest hit to date, peaking at #6 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 in 1992.  The single spent 34 weeks total on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Singles & Tracks and 20 weeks in the top 40 of the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Singles & Tracks.
