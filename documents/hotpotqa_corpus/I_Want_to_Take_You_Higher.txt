I Want to Take You Higher: "I Want to Take You Higher" is a song by the soul/rock/funk band Sly and the Family Stone, the B-side to their Top 30 hit "Stand! ".  Unlike most of the other tracks on the "Stand! " album, "I Want to Take You Higher" is not a message song; instead, it is simply dedicated to music and the feeling one gets from music.  Like nearly all of Sly & the Family Stone's songs, Sylvester "Sly Stone" Stewart was credited as the sole songwriter.
