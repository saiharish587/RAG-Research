Electoral district of Florey: Florey is an electoral district of the House of Assembly in the Australian state of South Australia.  It is named after scientist Howard Florey, who was responsible for the development of penicillin.  It is a 16.1 km² urban electorate in Adelaide's north-east, taking in the suburbs of Modbury, Modbury Heights and Modbury North, as well as parts of Gilles Plains, Hope Valley, Para Hills, Para Vista, Redwood Park, Ridgehaven, Valley View and Wynn Vale.
