Rent (albums): Rent (Original Broadway Cast Recording) is an album of music from the Tony Award- and Pulitzer Prize-winning musical "Rent".  It is produced by DreamWorks with music and lyrics by Jonathan Larson.  The album is a 2-disc (in its CD format) collection of every song from the musical; some small segments of narration and spoken dialogue from the play are not included in the recording.  The collection ends with a studio-recorded rearrangement of the song "Seasons of Love" featuring Stevie Wonder.  The album was recorded by the original Broadway cast of "RENT" and was released on August 27, 1996.  A second one-disc album was released in 1999 containing highlights from the original cast album.
