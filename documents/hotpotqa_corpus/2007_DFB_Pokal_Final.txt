2007 DFB-Pokal Final: The 2007 DFB-Pokal Final decided the winner of the 2006–07 DFB-Pokal, the 64th running of Germany's premier football cup competition.  In the final, 1.  FC Nürnberg defeated VfB Stuttgart 3–2 after extra time, thereby claiming their fourth title and denying Bundesliga champions Stuttgart a double.  A 109th-minute strike from Danish midfielder Jan Kristiansen won the game for Nürnberg.
