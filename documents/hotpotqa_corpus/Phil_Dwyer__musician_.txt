Phil Dwyer (musician): Phil Dwyer is a jazz saxophonist, pianist, composer, producer and educator.  In 2017 he graduated from the University of New Brunswick (UNB) Faculty of Law in Fredericton, New Brunswick.  Dwyer is Member of the Order of Canada, having been invested in 2013 "For his contributions to jazz as a performer, composer and producer, and for increasing access to music education in his community."  Dwyer has been nominated for Juno Awards six times and won Best Mainstream Jazz Album in 1994 with Dave Young for "Fables and Dreams" and Contemporary Jazz Album of the Year in 2012 for the recording "Changing Seasons".  Dwyer has also appeared on Juno Award winning recordings with Hugh Fraser (1988), Joe Sealy (1997), Guido Basso (2004), Don Thompson (2006), Molly Johnson (2009), Terry Clarke (2010), and Diana Panton (2015).  He is an alumnus and Honorary Fellow of The Royal Conservatory of Music.
