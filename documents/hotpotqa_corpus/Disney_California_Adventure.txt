Disney California Adventure: Disney California Adventure Park, commonly referred to as Disney California Adventure, California Adventure, or DCA, is a theme park located in Anaheim, California.  It is owned and operated by The Walt Disney Company through its Parks and Resorts division.  The 72 acre park is themed after the history and culture of California.  It also celebrates the fun and adventure of California.  The park opened in 2001 as Disney's California Adventure Park, and it is the second of two theme parks built at the Disneyland Resort complex, after Disneyland Park.
