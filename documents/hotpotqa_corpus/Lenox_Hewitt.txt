Lenox Hewitt: Sir Lenox Hewitt {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born 7 May 1917) is a retired senior Australian public servant.  He served the Commonwealth in various capacities for over 40 years (1939–80), principally as Secretary of the Prime Minister's Department under John Gorton (Liberal) 1968-71, and Secretary of the Department of Minerals and Energy under Rex Connor (Labor) 1972-75.  He later also served the governments of New South Wales and Western Australia.  He remains active in public policy debate.
