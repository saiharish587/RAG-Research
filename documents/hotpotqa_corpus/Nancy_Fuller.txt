Nancy Fuller: Nancy Fuller (also known as Nancy Fuller Ginsberg or Nancy Ginsberg; born March 27, 1949) is an American chef and businesswoman from Claverack, New York.  She is the co-owner of Ginsberg's Foods, and she is the host of the Food Network television series "Farmhouse Rules ."  She also serves as a judge on the Food Network cooking competition series "Clash of the Grandmas", "Holiday Baking Championship" and "Spring Baking Championship".
