Rawhide Mine: The Rawhide Mine is a coal mine located 10 miles (16.1km) north of Gillette, Wyoming in the United States in the coal-rich Powder River Basin.  The mine is an open pit mine that utilizes a combination of cast blast/dozer push and truck/shovel mining methods to strip an average of 165 feet (50.3m) of overburden off of approximately 105 (32.0m) feet of coal.  Rawhide produces a low-sulfur, sub-bituminous coal from the Roland and Smith seams.  This coal is used for domestic energy generation and shipped to customers via railroad.  The mine is currently owned and operated by Peabody Energy.
