Jamill Kelly: Larry Jamill Kelly (born October 25, 1977) is an American freestyle wrestler.  He represented the United States at the 2004 Summer Olympic Games where he earned a silver medal.  Up until August 2010 he was the head coach of Dallas Dynamite Wrestling Club in Dallas, Texas.  In 2015, he became a guest wrestling coach for Daniel Cormier, Luke Rockhold and Cain Velasquez, all from the American Kickboxing Academy.
