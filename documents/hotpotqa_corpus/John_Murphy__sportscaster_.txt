John Murphy (sportscaster): John Murphy is an American sportscaster from Buffalo, New York.  He is best known as the voice of the Buffalo Bills Radio Network and host of "The John Murphy Show" on WGR and MSG Western New York.  In addition to the Bills, he also served as commentator for the Buffalo Bisons, Canisius College Golden Griffins, Buffalo Bulls and Niagara University Purple Eagles in the 1980s.
