Yōko Maki (artist): Yōko Maki (槙ようこ , Maki Yōko , born July 11, 1981) is a Japanese manga artist best known for "Aishiteruze Baby".  Maki debuted in 1999 with "Love Service!"  in "Ribon Original" magazine.  She also has a pet dog named Leo and her profile in the "Aishiteruze Baby" comic says her hobby is "...blowing soap bubbles" and that one of her skills is "...passing quickly through a crowd of people".  She has a sister, Mochida Aki, which she is currently making manga with such as, Zen Zen.
