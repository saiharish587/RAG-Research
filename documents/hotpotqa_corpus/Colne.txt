Colne: Colne is a town and civil parish in Lancashire, England, six miles north-east of Burnley, 25 miles east of Preston, 25 miles north of Manchester and 30 miles west of Leeds.  It is a market town and the cross allowing a market to be held there dates to the 15th century.  The cross was originally in the Parish Church yard, but has been relocated in Market St, the main road through the town centre.
