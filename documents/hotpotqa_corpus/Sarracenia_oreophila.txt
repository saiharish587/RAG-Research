Sarracenia oreophila: Sarracenia oreophila, also known as the green pitcherplant, is a carnivorous plant in the genus "Sarracenia".  It has highly modified leaves in the form of pitchers that act as pitfall traps for prey.  The narrow pitcher leaves are tapered tubes that rise up to 75 centimetres from the ground, with a mouth 6 to 10 centimetres in circumference Like all the "Sarracenia", it is native to the New World.  "Sarracenia oreophila" is the most endangered of all "Sarracenia" species, its range limited to a handful of sites in northern Alabama, North Carolina, Georgia, and—historically--Tennessee.
