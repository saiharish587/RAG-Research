Jane Stevenson: Jane Stevenson (born 1959) is a British author who was born in London and brought up in London, Beijing and Bonn.  She has lectured in history at Sheffield University, and teaches literature and history at the University of Aberdeen, where she is the Regius Professor of Humanity.  Her fiction books include "Several Deceptions" and "Good Women", collections of novellas; a novel, "London Bridges"; and the historical trilogy made up of the novels "The Winter Queen", "The Shadow King", and "The Empress of the Last Days".  Stevenson lives in Aberdeenshire, Scotland.
