Taken by Force: Taken By Force is the fifth studio album by German hard rock band Scorpions, released by RCA Records in 1977.  This was the first Scorpions album to feature drummer Herman Rarebell and the final studio album to feature guitarist Uli Jon Roth.  Roth left the band in 1978 following the end of the album's tour, and was eventually replaced by Matthias Jabs.
