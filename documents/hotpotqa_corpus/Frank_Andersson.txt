Frank Andersson: Frank Öivind Stefan Andersson, generally referred to as Frank Andersson (born 9 May 1956 in Trollhättan) is a Swedish semi-retired professional wrestler, entertainer and retired amateur wrestler.  He started in amateur wrestling, winning several world championship gold medals as well as a bronze medal at the 1984 Summer Olympics in Los Angeles.  He later became a professional wrestler with mixed success.  He was also a participant on "Let's Dance 2011", the Swedish version of "Strictly Come Dancing" / "Dancing with the Stars" where he finished second.
