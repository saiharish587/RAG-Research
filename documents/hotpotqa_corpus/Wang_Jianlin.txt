Wang Jianlin: Wang Jianlin (; born October 24, 1954) is a Chinese business magnate, investor, and philanthropist.  He is the founder of the conglomerate company Dalian Wanda Group, China's largest real estate development company, as well as the world's largest movie theater operator.  He owns 20% of the Spanish football club Atlético Madrid.  In 2016, Wang agreed a deal with FIFA to launch the China Cup, in which national football teams compete in Asia each year.
