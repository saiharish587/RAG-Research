The Nun (2018 film): The Nun is an upcoming American horror film directed by Corin Hardy.  The screenplay by Gary Dauberman is from a story by Dauberman and James Wan.  It is a spin-off of 2016's "The Conjuring 2" and is set to be the fifth installment in "The Conjuring" series.  It stars Taissa Farmiga, Demián Bichir, Charlotte Hope and Bonnie Aarons.  The film is scheduled for release on July 13, 2018 by Warner Bros.  Pictures.
