Cotam 001: Cotam Unité is the official air traffic control call sign of any French Air Force aircraft carrying the President of France.  From the 1960s to 1994, COTAM was the acronym for the French Air Force's Military Air Transport Command.  In November 2010, the Governmental transport squadron (ETEC 65) received a newly refurbished Airbus A330 ordered by the government to be used as the presidential (or prime ministerial) aircraft replacing 2 Airbus 319.
