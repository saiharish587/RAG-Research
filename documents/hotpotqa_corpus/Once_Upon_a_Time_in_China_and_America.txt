Once Upon a Time in China and America: Once Upon a Time in China and America, also known as Once Upon a Time in China VI, is a 1997 Hong Kong martial arts film directed by Lau Kar-wing and Sammo Hung in his last directorial effort until "The Bodyguard", who also worked on the film's fight choreography.  The film is the sixth and final installment in the "Once Upon a Time in China" film series.  It also saw the return of Jet Li as Chinese folk hero Wong Fei-hung, who was replaced by Vincent Zhao in the fourth and fifth films.  The film was released in the Hong Kong on 1 February 1997.
