Sun Records (TV series): Sun Records is an American drama television series based on the musical "Million Dollar Quartet" written by Colin Escott and Floyd Mutrux.  It takes place at the Sun Studio in Memphis.  The series stars Drake Milligan, Trevor Donovan, Kevin Fonteyne, Christian Lees, Dustin Ingram, Billy Gardell, Jonah Lees, Chad Michael Murray and Jennifer Holland.  The series premiered on CMT on February 23, 2017 and concluded on April 13, 2017.  While originally intended to be a three-night event, CMT extended the show to an eight episode miniseries and hinted that future episodes could be a possibility due to the show's success.
