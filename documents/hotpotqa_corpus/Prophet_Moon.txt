Prophet Moon: Prophet Moon is an album by American jazz drummer Whit Dickey recorded live in 2002 at the New York club Roulette and released on the Riti label, operated by free jazz musician Joe Morris.  Dickey leads Trio Ahxoloxha, which includes Morris on guitar and Rob Brown on alto sax.  The same lineup was originally an ensemble organized by Brown to record the CD "Youniverse" a decade before and has at various times been led by Morris as well.
