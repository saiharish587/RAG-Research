Chesya Burke: Chesya Burke is an editor, educator and author of comic books and speculative fiction, most notably horror and dark fantasy.  She has published over a hundred short stories, essays, and articles in magazines and anthologies such as "Clarkesworld", "Nightmare Magazine", and "Stories for Chip: A Tribute to Samuel R. Delany".  Her short story collection "Let's Play White" was published in 2011 while her debut novel, "The Strange Crimes of Little Africa", was released in late 2015.  Nikki Giovanni has compared Burke's fiction to that of Octavia Butler and Toni Morrison.
