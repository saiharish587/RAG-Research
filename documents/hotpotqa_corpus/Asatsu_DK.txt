Asatsu-DK: Asatsu-DK Inc. (株式会社アサツー ディ・ケイ , Kabushiki-gaisha Asatsū Di Kei , Trading name: ADK) is a Japanese advertising agency.  Headquartered in the Toranomon Hills building complex in Minato, Tokyo since June 2014 (previously in Tsukiji, Chūō, Tokyo), the firm is Japan's third largest advertising agency after Dentsu and Hakuhodo.  The agency has 80 offices in over 20 countries.  One, Asatsu-DK Europe, was established in 1993 in Amsterdam, Netherlands.  The WPP Group holds a 20% share in Asatsu-DK.
