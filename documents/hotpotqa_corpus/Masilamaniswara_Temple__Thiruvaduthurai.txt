Masilamaniswara Temple, Thiruvaduthurai: Masilamaniswara Temple (மாசிலமணீஸ்வரர் கோயில், திருவாவடுதுறை) is a Hindu temple dedicated to Hindu god Shiva, located in the village Thiruvaduthurai, located 22 km from the South Indian town, Kumbakonam and 14 km from Mayiladuthurai, Tamil Nadu.  The temple is reverred in the verses of "Tevaram", the 7th century Tamil Saiva canon by Tirugnana Sambandar, Appar and Sundarar.
