Pauline Henriques: Pauline Clothilde Henriques (1 April 1914 – 1 November 1998) was a Jamaican-born English actress.  In 1946, she became the first black female actress for British television.  She was also the first black female Justice of the Peace, and was awarded the Order of the British Empire in 1969.  She worked extensively with unmarried mothers and helped found the Brook Counselling and Advisory Clinic.
