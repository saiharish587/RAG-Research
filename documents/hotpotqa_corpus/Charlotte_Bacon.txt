Charlotte Bacon: Lady Charlotte Mary Bacon, née Harley (12 December 1801 - 9 May 1880), was the second daughter of Edward Harley, 5th Earl of Oxford and Earl Mortimer.  Her beauty as a child prompted Lord Byron to dedicate the first two cantos of "Childe Harold's Pilgrimage" to her, under the name "Ianthe".  Lord Byron had been one of the many lovers of her mother, Jane Elizabeth Scott.  Lady Charlotte was also the subject of the painting "Lady Charlotte Harley as Hebe" by Richard Westall.
