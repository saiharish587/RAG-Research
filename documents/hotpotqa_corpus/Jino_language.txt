Jino language: Jino Language (Jinuo; also known as Buyuan, Jinuo, Buyuan Jinuo 基諾語補遠方言.)  autonyms: "tɕy˦no˦", "ki˦nʲo˦") Jino language is a pair of Loloish languages spoken by the Jino people of Yunnan.  In total, there are about 28,320 Jinuo people living in China.  70%-80% of jinuo people can speak Jino fluently in the world.  The Jino languages has two subdialects of Youle Jino and Buyuan Jinuo, and they are not mutually intelligible.  Buyuan Jinuo is spoken by 21,000 people Most of the speakers are monolingual, which means they only speak Jino language.  There is no official written form.  Most Jino also speak one of the Tai languages or Chinese.  The iso 639-3 code for jino language is "jiu" for Youle Jino, or "jiy" for Buyuan Jino.  The Glottocode for Jino language is "youl1235" for Youle Jino, or "buyu1238" for Buyuan Jino.
