Mollie King: Mollie Elizabeth King (born 4 June 1987) is an English singer-songwriter.  She is best known for being a member of girl group The Saturdays.  In the Saturdays, King has had thirteen top-ten and eight top-five singles, including the UK no.1 hit What About Us on the UK Singles Chart and five top-ten albums on the UK Albums Chart.  Before The Saturdays, King was a member of another girl group, Fallen Angelz, with whom she appeared on "The X Factor".  In 2015, King signed a solo deal with Island Records.
