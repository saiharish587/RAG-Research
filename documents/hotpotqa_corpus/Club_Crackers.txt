Club Crackers: Club Crackers are a brand of crackers made by the American Keebler Company.  They are somewhat similar in resemblance to saltines, but are rectangular and have 18 holes in a 3x6 pattern instead of the 13 holes in a 3-2-3-2-3 pattern that are on a saltine.  Also, their edges are even, not perforated.  They have a buttery flavor and a large amount of fat, 0.5g per serving, not found in regular saltines.  The crackers contain 70 calories per serving with four total crackers in one serving.
