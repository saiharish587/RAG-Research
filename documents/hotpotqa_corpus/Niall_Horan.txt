Niall Horan: Niall James Horan (born 13 September 1993) is an Irish singer and songwriter.  He is best known as a member of the boy band One Direction.  In 2010, Horan auditioned as a solo artist for British televised singing competition "The X Factor".  After being eliminated as a solo performer, Horan joined four other contestants to form One Direction.  The band has released five commercially successful albums, performed on four worldwide tours, and won a number of awards.
