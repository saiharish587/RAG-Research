Shortest common supersequence problem: In computer science, the shortest common supersequence of two sequences X and Y is the shortest sequence which has X and Y as subsequences.  This is a problem closely related to the longest common subsequence problem.  Given two sequences X = < x.. . ,x > and Y = < y.. . ,y >, a sequence U = < u.. . ,u > is a common supersequence of X and Y if items can be removed from U to produce X or Y.
