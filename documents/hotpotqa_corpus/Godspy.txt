Godspy: Godspy is a dormant English-language online magazine "for Catholics and other seekers" launched in 2003, dealing with subjects from "politics to the arts, science to the economy, sexuality to ecology," and exploring the "ideas and experiences that reveal God’s presence in the world."  The magazines name was inspired by the line in King Lear ""And take upon's the mystery of things, As if we were God's spies"."
