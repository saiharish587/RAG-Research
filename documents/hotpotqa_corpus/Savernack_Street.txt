Savernack Street: Savernack Street is a small art gallery in the Mission District of San Francisco founded in 2013 by artist Carrie Sinclair Katz.  The gallery interior is inaccessible and visitors can only view artwork by looking through a reverse peephole located on the storefront.  The monthly exhibitions at Savernack Street usually feature a single piece of artwork that appears larger or life size when viewed through the peephole.  The name Savernack comes from a road in London and is not an actual street in San Francisco.
