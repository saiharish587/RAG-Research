I'm Glad I Can Make You Cry: I'm Glad I Can Make You Cry is a World War I song written and composed by Chas.  R. McCarron & Carey Morgan.  The song was published in 1918 by Jos. W. Stern & Co. in New York, NY.  The sheet music cover, illustrated by Starmer, depicts photos of Alice Joyce & Evart Overton, as well as Bessie Hamilton and Gus Hall's Minstrels.
