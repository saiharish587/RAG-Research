Peter Gifford: Peter Gifford (born 5 April 1955), sometimes known as "Giffo," is an Australian musician.  From 1980 until 1987 he played bass guitar, Chapman Stick and sang backing vocals for Australian rock band Midnight Oil.  His last recorded work with the oils was diesel and dust.  he did not tour this album and Bones Hillman was recruited to play bass and fill the hole in what was a raucous rhythm section (Bones remains the oils base man still).
