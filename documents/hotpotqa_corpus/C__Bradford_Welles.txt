C. Bradford Welles: C. Bradford Welles (August 9, 1901 – October 8, 1969) was an American Classicist and ancient historian, born in Old Saybrook, Connecticut.  His academic career was at Yale University.  He received a B.A. in 1924, a Ph.D. in 1928, became an Instructor in 1927, an Assistant Professor in 1931, an Associate Professor in 1939 and Professor in 1940.  At his death he was Professor of Ancient History and Curator of the Yale Collection of Papyri.  He was profoundly influenced by the great ancient historian Michael I. Rostovtzeff, who arrived at Yale in 1925.
