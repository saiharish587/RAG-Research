Omar Rezaq: Omar Mohammed Ali Rezaq (born 1963) is the only surviving hijacker of EgyptAir Flight 648.  He was a member of Abu Nidal.  The plane was hijacked by a group of three people.  The remaining two hijackers were killed, either during in-flight shooting with the plane's sky-marshal, Methad Mustafa Kamal, or after Egyptian commandos stormed the hijacked plane.
