Maninder Singh (actor): Maninder Singh is an Indian actor.  He played the role of Indian independence activist Ananta Singh in the film "Khelein Hum Jee Jaan Sey" (2010).  He played the lead role in the suspense thriller television show "2612" (2012) as Randeep Rathore and reprised the same role in the next season titled as "2613".  In 2014, he was also seen in the popular television show "CID" portraying senior CID cop.  He was also seen in Zee tv's Sanyukt.  He is currently seen in Star Bharat's Kya Hal Mister Panchal (TV Series).
