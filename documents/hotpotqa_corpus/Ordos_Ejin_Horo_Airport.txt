Ordos Ejin Horo Airport: Ordos Ejin Horo Airport (IATA: DSN, ICAO: ZBDS) is an airport serving Ordos City in China's Inner Mongolia Autonomous Region.  It is located in Ejin Horo Banner.  First built in 1959 and called Dongsheng Airport, the airport ceased operation in 1983.  In 2005 the airport was rebuilt at the current site with an investment of 350 million yuan, and re-opened in July 2007.
