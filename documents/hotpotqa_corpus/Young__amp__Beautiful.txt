Young &amp; Beautiful: Young & Beautiful (French: Jeune & Jolie ) is a 2013 French drama film of adolescent sexuality directed by François Ozon and produced by Eric and Nicolas Altmayer.  The film stars Marine Vacth in the leading role of Isabelle, a teenage prostitute, and features supporting performances by Johan Leysen, Géraldine Pailhas, Frédéric Pierrot, and Charlotte Rampling.  The film was nominated for the Palme d'Or at the 2013 Cannes Film Festival, and received praise from the film critics.  It was shown at the 2013 Toronto International Film Festival.
