Paper Gods: Paper Gods is the fourteenth studio album from British new wave band Duran Duran on Warner Bros.  Records.  The record was announced on 15 June 2015 via an official press release posted on their website, confirming a scheduled worldwide release date of 11 September 2015.  The record was produced by Mr Hudson and Joshua Blair, who has worked with the band on "All You Need Is Now" (2010) and "A Diamond in the Mind"; Nile Rodgers, who had previously first worked on the band's "The Reflex", his remixed version reaching number 1, "The Wild Boys" single and "Notorious" album, and Mark Ronson, who produced "All You Need Is Now".  The first single, "Pressure Off", features vocals from American singer Janelle Monáe.  Duran Duran promoted the album through the Paper Gods on Tour.
