Mappowder: Mappowder is a village and civil parish in the county of Dorset in southern England.  It lies within the North Dorset administrative district, approximately 9 mi south-east of the town of Sherborne.  The parish covers about 1900 acre at an altitude of 75 to .  It is sited on Corallian limestone soil at the southern edge of the Blackmore Vale, close to the northern scarp face of the Dorset Downs.  In the 2011 census the parish had 71 dwellings, 69 households and a population of 166.
