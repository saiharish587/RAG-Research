Theo Fabergé: Theo Fabergé (London, 26 September 1922 - 20 August 2007) was the grandson of Peter Carl Fabergé.  His father Nicholas Fabergé, Carl’s youngest son, arrived in London in 1906 to help run the only branch of ‘The House of Fabergé’ outside Russia, in Dover Street, London.  After 1917 Nicholas remained in London and in 1922 his son, Theo Fabergé was born.
