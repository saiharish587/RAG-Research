Alkmund of Derby: Alkmund of Derby (or of Lilleshall), also spelt Ealhmund, Alhmund, Alcmund, or Alchmund (d. c. 800) was a son of Alhred of Northumbria.  After more than twenty years in exile as a result of Northumbrian dynastic struggles, he returned with an army.  He was killed in about 800, for which King Eardwulf of Northumbria was held responsible.  Whatever the exact circumstances, his death was regarded as a martyrdom, and Alkmund as a saint.
