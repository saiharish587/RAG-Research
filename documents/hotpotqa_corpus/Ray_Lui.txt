Ray Lui: Ray Lui Leung-wai (born 22 December 1956) is a Vietnam-born Hong Kong actor, with family roots in Lianjiang, Zhanjiang, Guangdong.  He is best known for his role as "Ting Lik" in the 1980 Hong Kong television series "The Bund" produced by TVB, which propelled him to fame.  Since then, Lui has acted in several films and other television series also produced by TVB.
