Edward N. Zalta: Edward N. Zalta (born 1952) is a Senior research scholar at the Center for the Study of Language and Information.  He received his Ph.D. in philosophy from the University of Massachusetts Amherst in 1980.  Zalta has taught courses at Stanford University, Rice University, the University of Salzburg, and the University of Auckland.  Zalta is also the Principal Editor of the "Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy".
