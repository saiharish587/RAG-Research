Despair (novel): Despair (Russian: "Отчаяние" , or "Otchayanie ") is the seventh novel by Vladimir Nabokov, originally published in Russian, serially in the politicized literary journal "Sovremennye zapiski" during 1934.  It was then published as a book in 1936, and translated to English by the author in 1937.  Most copies of the 1937 English edition were destroyed by German bombs during World War II; only a few copies remain.  Nabokov published a second English translation in 1965; this is now the only English translation in print.
