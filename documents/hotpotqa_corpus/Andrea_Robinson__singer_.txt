Andrea Robinson (singer): Andrea Robinson is an American singer and voice actress.  She has been a chorus member and singing voice for other actresses in many films (animated and live action).  She also was the opening act for Burt Bacharach.  Her most prominent job as a singing voice of another actress is Sister Mary Robert (Wendy Makkena) in "Sister Act".
