The Tra-La Days Are Over: The Tra-La Days Are Over is a 1973 album by American singer-songwriter Neil Sedaka.  The album was the second to be produced at Strawberry Studios in England in collaboration with Graham Gouldman, Lol Creme, Kevin Godley and Eric Stewart, who had formed the band 10cc since their first joint venture with Sedaka on "Solitaire" (1972).
