Le Conquet radio: Le Conquet radio or Call sign FFU (station "Française Fixe de Ushant") was a French maritime radio station located on of City Le Conquet (La Pointe du Renard 4°43'58"W 48°20'24"N).  The station was established in 1952, by the French Administration of France Télécom, as a result of the work by Call sign Ushant TSF, Call sign FFU.
