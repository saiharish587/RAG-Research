The Panther (novel): The Panther is a 2012 novel by American author Nelson DeMille.  It is the sixth of DeMille's novels to feature Detective John Corey, now working as a contractor for the fictional FBI Anti-Terrorist Task Force in New York City.  The novel is the sequel to "The Lion".  The Panther is followed by DeMille’s 2015 novel, "Radiant Angel".  Also featured in this novel is DeMille’s other fictional character, Paul Brenner, who appears in The General's Daughter and Up Country.
