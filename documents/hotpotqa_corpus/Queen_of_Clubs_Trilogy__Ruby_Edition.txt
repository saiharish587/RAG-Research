Queen of Clubs Trilogy: Ruby Edition: 'Queens of Clubs Trilogy: The Best of Nadia Ali Remixed' is a three-part compilation of remixed tracks by singer-songwriter Nadia Ali.  The albums feature songs from her time as one-half of iiO and her subsequent solo career.  The compilation celebrates Ali's decade-long career as a musician.  The title of the trilogy was inspired by Ali's 'un-rivalled contributions to dance music and club culture'.  The first installment, "Ruby Edition", was released on August 31, 2010 by Smile in Bed Records.
