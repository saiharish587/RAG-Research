The Best of 1990–2000: The Best of 1990–2000 is the second greatest hits compilation album by Irish rock band U2.  The album was released on 5 November 2002 through Island Records, except in the United States where the album was released on the Interscope label as a single-disc CD compilation.  The Best of 1990–2000 & B-Sides was released on the same day with a second disc featuring 14 of the b-side singles released from 1990 to 2000 and a bonus DVD with a trailer for the album and three other segments.  A video album of the same name was later released in December 2002 (see 2002 in music).
