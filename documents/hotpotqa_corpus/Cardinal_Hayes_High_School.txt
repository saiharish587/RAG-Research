Cardinal Hayes High School: Cardinal Hayes High School is a Catholic high school for boys in the Concourse Village neighborhood of the Bronx, New York City.  The school serves the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of New York.  It is a member of the CHSAA.  It was constructed in the Art Deco style.  It is named after Cardinal Patrick Joseph Hayes, a previous archbishop of (Catholic Archdiocese of) New York.
