St. Columba Church (Saint Paul, Minnesota): The Church of St. Columba is located in Saint Paul, Minnesota.  The parish was formed in the Hamline-Midway neighborhood in 1915.  After the Baby Boom from World War II the church was expanding and needed a new building.  The then pastor Michael Casey contracted with Barry Byrne to design and construct the building.  Byrne was based in Chicago and his formal schooling ended in the Ninth Grade.  Byrne worked under Frank Lloyd Wright and was involved with the Prairie School of architecture before later turned towards Expressionist architecture.  Byrne designed the building later in his career.
