Caesar (dog): Caesar (1898–1914) was a Wire Fox Terrier owned by King Edward VII.  He was bred in the kennels of Kathleen, Duchess of Newcastle, and became the constant companion of the King.  After the King's death in 1910, the dog attended the funeral and walked in the procession in prominence ahead of nine kings and other heads of state.  Caesar has been the subject of paintings, and a hand crafted hardstone model created by the House of Fabergé.
