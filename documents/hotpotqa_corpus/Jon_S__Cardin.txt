Jon S. Cardin: Jon S. Cardin (born January 12, 1970) is an American politician from Maryland and a member of the Democratic Party.  Previously he served in the Maryland House of Delegates, representing Maryland's District 11 in Baltimore County, and was a member of the Ways and Means Committee.  Cardin chaired the Election Law Subcommittee.  Cardin is the nephew of Ben Cardin, a United States Senator from Maryland.
