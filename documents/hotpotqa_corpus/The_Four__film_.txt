The Four (film): The Four is a 2012 Chinese-Hong Kong "wuxia" film directed by Gordon Chan and Janet Chun.  It is the first film adaptation of Woon Swee Oan's novel series "Si Da Ming Bu" (四大名捕; "The Four Great Constables"), which has previously been adapted to a television series.  In all adaptations and interpretations, the nicknames of the Four remained the same — Emotionless, Iron Hands, Life Snatcher and Cold Blood.  They dedicated their special skills to the service of their chief, Master Zhuge, in solving crimes and apprehending powerful criminals.
