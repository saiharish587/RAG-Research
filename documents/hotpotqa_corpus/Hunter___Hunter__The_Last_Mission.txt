Hunter × Hunter: The Last Mission: Hunter × Hunter: The Last Mission (劇場版 HUNTER×HUNTER -The LAST MISSION- , Gekijō-ban HUNTER×HUNTER -The LAST MISSION- ) is a 2013 Japanese animated feature film directed by Keiichiro Kawaguchi.  Released on December 27, 2013, it is the second film based on the "Hunter × Hunter" manga series created by Yoshihiro Togashi.
