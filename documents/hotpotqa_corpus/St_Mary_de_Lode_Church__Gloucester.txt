St Mary de Lode Church, Gloucester: St Mary de Lode Church, Archdeacon Street, Gloucester GL1 2QT, is a Church of England church immediately outside the grounds of Gloucester Cathedral.  It is believed by some to be on the site of the first Christian church in Britain.  The church is in the Diocese of Gloucester and Grade I listed by English Heritage.  It has also been known as "St. Mary Before the Gate of St. Peter", "St. Mary Broad Gate" and "St. Mary De Port".
