Roger Garis: Roger (Carroll) Garis, ((1901--) 10, 1901 – (1967--) 30, 1967 ) was an American author known as a writer for magazines and the author of "The Outboard Boys" series of books.  Roger also wrote several books under pseudonyms for the Stratemeyer Syndicate, as his parents did (see My Father Was Uncle Wiggily for further details).
