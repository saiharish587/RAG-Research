Ego Trippin' (Part Two): "Ego Trippin' (Part Two)" is a 1994 single by the group, De La Soul, and the second single to be released from the group's 1993 album, "Buhloone Mindstate".  The song (and its music video) were a scathing parody on gangsta rap complete with "hardcore" screaming.  The video caught the attention of rappers such as Ice Cube and Tupac Shakur, the latter taking exception to the video showing a rapper splashing around in a pool similar to Shakur's own video for his song entitled "I Get Around".  The song features vocal contributions from Philadelphia rapper Shorty No Mas who also appeared in the video.
