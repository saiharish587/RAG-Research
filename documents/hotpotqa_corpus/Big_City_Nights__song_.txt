Big City Nights (song): "Big City Nights" is a song by German hard rock band Scorpions.  The song was released as the sixth track of their 1984 album "Love at First Sting".  Like many Scorpions songs, "Big City Nights" was composed by band members Rudolf Schenker and Klaus Meine.  The song was also released as the third single from the album in 1984, with the B-side being "Bad Boys Running Wild".  The guitar solo is performed by Rudolf Schenker.
