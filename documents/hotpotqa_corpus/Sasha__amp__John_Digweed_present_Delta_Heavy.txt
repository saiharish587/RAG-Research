Sasha &amp; John Digweed present Delta Heavy: Sasha & John Digweed present Delta Heavy is a DVD release from System Recordings which chronicles Sasha & John Digweed's "Delta Heavy" tour of the United States.  It was released in 2005, three years after the original tour.  "Delta Heavy" is known for being one of the first DJ tours to incorporate a rock concert atmosphere into traditional DJ venues.  The DVD package includes guest commentary from Jimmy Van M and Nick Warren.
