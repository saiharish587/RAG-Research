Hollywood, Florida: Hollywood is a city in Broward County, Florida, located between Fort Lauderdale and Miami.  The average temperature is between 68 and 83°F.  As of July 1, 2015 Hollywood has a population of 149,728.  Founded in 1925, the city grew rapidly in the 1950s and 1960s, and is now the twelfth-largest city in Florida.  Hollywood is a principal city of the Miami metropolitan area, which was home to an estimated 6,012,331 people at the 2015 census.
