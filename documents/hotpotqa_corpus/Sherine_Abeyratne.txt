Sherine Abeyratne: Sherine Yvonne Abeyratne is an Australian singer.  She and her twin sister, Suzanne, are London-born Australian-raised vocalists who often sang together as backing singers of a number of groups including Models, INXS and U2.  They also pursued their own separate careers; for example, Sherine provided lead vocals for Big Pig from 1985 to 1991.
