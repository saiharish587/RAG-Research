Enkarterri: Enkarterri (Spanish: "Las Encartaciones") is a comarca of the province of Biscay, in the Basque Country, Spain.  It is one of the seven "comarcas" or districts that make up the province of Biscay.  Its administrative centre is Balmaseda.  Enkarterri stretches from the river Nervion and the Estuary of Bilbao in the east to the mountains that form its southern border with Cantabria and Castile-Leon in the west and south.
