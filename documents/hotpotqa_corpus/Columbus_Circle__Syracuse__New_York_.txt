Columbus Circle (Syracuse, New York): Columbus Circle is a neighborhood and plaza in the downtown section of Syracuse, New York.  Columbus Monument was designed by the Syracuse-born architect, Dwight James Baum in 1934.  Columbus Circle is home to Syracuse's two cathedrals, the Episcopalian St. Paul's Cathedral and the Roman Catholic Cathedral of the Immaculate Conception.  As well as County Court House and the County's John H. Mulroy Civic Center, home of the Onondaga County Government.
