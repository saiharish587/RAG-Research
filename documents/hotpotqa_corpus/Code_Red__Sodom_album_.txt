Code Red (Sodom album): Code Red is the ninth studio album by German thrash metal band Sodom, released on 31 May 1999 by Drakkar Entertainment.  On this album, Sodom returned to classic thrash metal which pleased many fans.  It was also released as a two-disc limited edition with a Sodom tribute album called "Homage to the Gods" and as a two-disc edition with an Onkel Tom Angelripper's album called "Ich glaub´nicht an den Weihnachtsmann".
