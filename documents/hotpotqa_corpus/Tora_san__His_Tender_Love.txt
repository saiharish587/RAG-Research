Tora-san, His Tender Love: Tora-san, His Tender Love (男はつらいよ フーテンの寅 , Otoko wa Tsurai yo: Fūten no Tora ) is a 1970 Japanese comedy film directed by Azuma Morisaki.  It stars Kiyoshi Atsumi as Kuruma Torajirō (Tora-san), and Michiyo Aratama as his love interest or "Madonna".  "Tora-san, His Tender Love" is the third entry in the popular, long-running "Otoko wa Tsurai yo" series, and the first of only two in the series not directed by Yoji Yamada.
