Have You Ever (S Club 7 song): "Have You Ever" was a single released by UK pop group S Club 7 on 19 November 2001.  Following the success of the 2000 "Children in Need" track, "Never Had a Dream Come True", the BBC asked S Club 7 to perform the 2001 track for the charity too.  "Have You Ever" was co-written by Cathy Dennis, A. Frampton and Chris Braide.  The track is the penultimate ballad S Club 7 released as a single with lead vocals coming from singer Jo.  "Have You Ever" acted as an introduction to S Club 7's third album "Sunshine".  The single entered the UK Singles Chart at number one on 25 November 2001, and remained on the chart for 14 weeks (seven of which were in the top 10).  The Children in Need version of "Have You Ever" is listed in the "Guinness Book of World Records" as having the highest number of people's voices recorded in a single song; as recordings from children in schools across the UK were used in the chorus.  The song has sold 380,000 copies in the UK according to the Official UK Charts Company.  The photo for the cover of the single was shot in another major architectural landmark of the Los Angeles area - the Sheats Goldstein Residence near Beverly Hills.
