Greatest Hits (Monster Magnet album): Greatest Hits is the title of New Jersey stoner rock band Monster Magnet's 2003 greatest hits album released by A&M Records.  The first disc contains Monster Magnet's best known material, and contains tracks from 1991's "Spine of God" (albeit re-recorded) up until their 2000 effort, "God Says No", while the second disc contains the band's music videos and a few b-sides and rarities.
