Ocean County Mall: The Ocean County Mall is a super-regional mall, opened in 1976 by Edward J. DeBartolo Corporation, located in Toms River, New Jersey on Hooper Avenue (County Route 549).  The mall is accessible from Exit 82 of the Garden State Parkway via Route 37 East.  The mall is managed by Simon Property Group.  The mall has a gross leasable area of 890000 sqft .
