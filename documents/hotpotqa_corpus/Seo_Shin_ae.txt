Seo Shin-ae: Seo Shin-ae (born October 20, 1998) is a South Korean actress.  She made her entertainment debut in 2004 in a commercial for Seoul Milk.  Seo then became known as a child actress, notably in the film "Meet Mr. Daddy" (2007), and the television dramas "Thank You" (2007), "" (2010), and "The Queen's Classroom" (2013).
