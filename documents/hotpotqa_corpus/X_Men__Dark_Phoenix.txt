X-Men: Dark Phoenix: X-Men: Dark Phoenix is an upcoming American superhero film based on Marvel Comics' X-Men characters, distributed by 20th Century Fox.  It is intended to be the thirteenth installment in the "X-Men" film series, and the beginning of a new "X-Men" trilogy.  The film is being written and directed by Simon Kinberg, and is set to star James McAvoy, Michael Fassbender, Jennifer Lawrence, Nicholas Hoult, Tye Sheridan, Sophie Turner, Alexandra Shipp, Kodi Smit-McPhee, Evan Peters, and Jessica Chastain.  In "X-Men: Dark Phoenix", the X-Men must face the full power of the Dark Phoenix as well as the alien Shi'ar empire.
