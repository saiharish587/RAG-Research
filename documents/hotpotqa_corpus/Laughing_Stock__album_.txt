Laughing Stock (album): Laughing Stock is the fifth and final studio album by British post-rock band Talk Talk.  Following on from their previous release "Spirit of Eden" (1988), and the departure of bassist Paul Webb, which reduced the band to the duo of Mark Hollis and Lee Harris, Talk Talk acrimoniously left EMI and signed to the jazz-based Verve Records, and recorded "Laughing Stock" at Wessex Sound Studios, London, with producer Tim Friese-Greene and engineer Phill Brown from September 1990 to April 1991.
