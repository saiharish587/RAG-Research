Jingdezhen: Jingdezhen (or the Town of Jingde) is a prefecture-level city, previously a town, in northeastern Jiangxi province, China, with a total population of 1,554,000 (2007), bordering Anhui to the north.  It is known as the "Porcelain Capital" because it has been producing pottery for 1,700 years.  The city has a well-documented history that stretches back over 2,000 years.
