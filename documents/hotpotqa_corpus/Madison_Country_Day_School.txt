Madison Country Day School: Madison Country Day School is a nonsectarian, private day school in Dane County, Wisconsin for grades PreK through 12.  The school has an enrollment of about 400 students.  It is accredited by the Independent Schools Association of the Central States (ISACS), and is a member of the National Association of Independent Schools (NAIS).  It is an International Baccalaureate (IB) World School that offers the IB Diploma Program to high school students.  Madison Country Day School is informally known as "MCDS", and "Country Day".
