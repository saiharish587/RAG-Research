Wake Forest Demon Deacons women's soccer: The Wake Forest University Demon Deacons women's soccer team is an amateur, NCAA Division I college soccer team composed of students attending Wake Forest University in Winston-Salem, North Carolina.  They achieved their greatest result in 2011, when they reached the College Cup.  Like all sports teams from Wake Forest, women's soccer competes in the Atlantic Coast Conference.  The Deacons play their home matches at Spry Stadium on the campus of Wake Forest.
