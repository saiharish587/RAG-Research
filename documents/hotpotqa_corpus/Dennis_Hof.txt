Dennis Hof: Dennis Hof (born October 14, 1946) is an American brothel owner, entrepreneur, restaurateur and star of the HBO series .  He is best known as the owner of seven legal brothels in Nevada.  In the state of Nevada, prostitution's legality is determined on a county-by-county basis.  Several of his brothels are in Moundhouse, Nevada, a few minutes outside Carson City.  His best-known brothel is the Moonlite BunnyRanch.
