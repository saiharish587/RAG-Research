Chaste Susanne (film): Chaste Susanne (German:Die keusche Susanne) is a 1926 German silent comedy film directed by Richard Eichberg and starring Lilian Harvey, Willy Fritsch and Ruth Weyher.  It is based on the 1910 operetta "Die keusche Susanne" composed by Jean Gilbert with a libretto by Georg Okonkowski.  In Britain it was released under the alternative title The Girl in the Taxi in reference to "The Girl in the Taxi" in the English version of the operetta.  The film's art direction is by Jacek Rotmil.
