Madhuri Banerjee: Madhuri Banerjee (born 9 August 1975) is an Indian author, columnist and screenwriter.  Her debut novel "Losing My Virginity And Other Dumb Ideas" sold over 40,000 copies.  She is also the writer of the successful Bollywood film, "Hate Story 2".  She has also worked with actress Karishma Kapoor on a non-fiction book called "The Yummy Mummy Guide".
