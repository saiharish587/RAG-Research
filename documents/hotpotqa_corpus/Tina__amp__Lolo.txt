Tina &amp; Lolo: Tina & Lolo is an upcoming Bollywood action erotic  thriller film directed by Devang Dholakia.  It features Sunny Leone and Karishma Tanna in the lead roles.  Filming began in 2013, and Leone bruised her ribs during a stunt performance.  The film was scheduled for a worldwide release in 2015 but has been delayed for some unknown reasons.
