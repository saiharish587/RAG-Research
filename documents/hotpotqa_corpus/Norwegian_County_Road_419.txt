Norwegian County Road 419: Norwegian County Road 419 (Fv419) is a Norwegian county road which runs between the villages of Hannåsmoen and Klepp in Evje og Hornnes municipality in Aust-Agder county, Norway.  The 1.8 km long road includes a bridge over the river Otra.  It is one of five bridges over the river Otra in the municipality.  The western end of the road connects with the Norwegian National Road 9 which runs north-south into the Setesdalen valley.
