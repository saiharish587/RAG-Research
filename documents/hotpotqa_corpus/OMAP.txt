OMAP: OMAP (Open Multimedia Applications Platform) is a series of image/video processors developed by Texas Instruments.  They are a category of proprietary system on chips (SoCs) for portable and mobile multimedia applications.  OMAP devices generally include a general-purpose ARM architecture processor core plus one or more specialized co-processors.  Earlier OMAP variants commonly featured a variant of the Texas Instruments TMS320 series digital signal processor.
