Jennifer Branstetter: Jennifer Branstetter served as the Secretary of Planning and Policy for the administration of former Pennsylvania Governor Tom Corbett.  In that position, she was part of the Governor's "senior staff."  As Secretary of Planning and Policy, she manages policy development for the Governor's Office, including energy policy.  She also served as the policy director for Governor-elect Corbett's transition team.  During Corbett's term as Pennsylvania Attorney General, she was the Director of Education and Outreach.
