Two Kinds: "Two Kinds" is a short story from the book "The Joy Luck Club" by Amy Tan.  It was first published in 1989.  The short story outlines the main character Jing-mei Woo’s childhood and the effects of her mother’s high expectations for her life.  It is clear that some of the events in the short story reflect events that happened in the author’s life.  For example, the main character's mother left China, leaving behind her family and children.  The same is true with Amy Tan's mother.  In 1993, a movie based on the book was made.
