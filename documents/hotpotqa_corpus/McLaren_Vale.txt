McLaren Vale: McLaren Vale is a wine region approximately 35 km south of Adelaide in South Australia.  It is internationally renowned for the wines it produces.  The region was named after either David McLaren, the Colonial Manager of the South Australia Company or John McLaren (unrelated) who surveyed the area in 1839.  Among the first settlers to the region in late 1839, were two English farmers from Devon, William Colton and Charles Thomas Hewett.  William Colton established the Daringa Farm and Charles Thomas Hewett established Oxenberry Farm.  Both men would be prominent in the early days of McLaren Vale.
