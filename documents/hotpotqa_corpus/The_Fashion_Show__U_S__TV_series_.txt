The Fashion Show (U.S. TV series): The Fashion Show: Ultimate Collection (originally styled as The Fashion Show) is an American reality television series which premiered on May 7, 2009, on the Bravo cable network.  The show focuses on fashion design and featured hosts fashion designer Isaac Mizrahi and supermodel Iman.  The contestants competed against each other to create the best clothes and were restricted in time, materials, and theme, with the resulting designs judged and one or more designers eliminated each week.  The show was originally co-hosted by singer Kelly Rowland, however, she was replaced by Iman in Season 2.
