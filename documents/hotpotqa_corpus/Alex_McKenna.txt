Alex McKenna: Alex McKenna (born October 15, 1984) is an American television and film actress.  She gained fame by playing Petunia Stupid in "The Stupids" (1996) and Mickey Apple in "You Wish" (1997).  She resumed her acting career with guest appearances in CW hit teen drama series "90210" in 2010.  In 2012, she had recurring appearances in the TV series, including Dallas, "Guys with Kids" and "Two and a Half Men".  She served as a voice actress in the "The Legend of Korra" (2014), appeared in the horror film "Haunted" (2014) and Boston Police officer Sara in the American drama film "Patriots Day".
