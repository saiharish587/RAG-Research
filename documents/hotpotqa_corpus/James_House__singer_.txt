James House (singer): James Andrew House (born March 22, 1955) is an American country music artist.  Originally a member of a group called the House Band, he recorded a solo rock album in 1983 on Atlantic Records before he began his country music career in 1989 on MCA Records, recording two albums for that label.  He later penned singles for Diamond Rio and Dwight Yoakam, before finding another record deal on Epic Records in 1994.  That year, he charted two Top 40 singles on the "Billboard" country chart, including the Top ten hit "This Is Me Missing You".  He has also written singles for Diamond Rio, Dwight Yoakam, and Martina McBride.
