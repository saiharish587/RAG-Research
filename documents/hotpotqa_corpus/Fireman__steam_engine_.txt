Fireman (steam engine): Fireman or stoker is the job title for someone whose job is to tend the fire for the running of a steam engine.  On steam locomotives the term "fireman" is usually used, while on steamships and stationary steam engines, such as those driving saw mills, the term is usually "stoker" (although the British Merchant Navy did use "fireman").  The German word "Heizer" is equivalent and in Dutch the word "stoker" is mostly used too.  The United States Navy referred to them as watertenders. Much of the job is hard physical labor, such as shoveling fuel, typically coal, into the engine's firebox.
