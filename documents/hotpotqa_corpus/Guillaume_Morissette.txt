Guillaume Morissette: Guillaume Morissette (born 1984) is a Canadian fiction writer and poet based in Montreal, Quebec.  His work has frequently been associated with the Alt Lit movement, with Dazed & Confused magazine describing him as "Canada's Alt Lit poster boy."  He has published stories, poems and essays online and in print, in venues such as "Maisonneuve", "Little Brother", "Broken Pencil", "Shabby Doll House" and "Thought Catalog", and was listed as one of CBC Books' "Writers to Watch" for 2014.
