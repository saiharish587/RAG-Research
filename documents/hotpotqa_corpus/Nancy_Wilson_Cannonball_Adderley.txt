Nancy Wilson/Cannonball Adderley: Nancy Wilson/Cannonball Adderley is a 1961 studio album by Nancy Wilson with Cannonball Adderley and his quintet.  Wilson considered her vocals on the album "as a sort of easy-going third horn" (Wilson quoted in the liner notes).  All tracks were recorded in New York City, those with Wilson on June 27 and 29, 1961, and the instrumental tracks on August 23 and 24, 1961.
