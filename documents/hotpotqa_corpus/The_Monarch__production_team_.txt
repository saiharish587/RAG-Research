The Monarch (production team): The Monarch is a grammy nominated music production duo originally from Orlando, Florida, consisting of Andre "Dre" Davidson and Sean "Sean D" Davidson.  Currently residing in Los Angeles, they are best known for producing "All Eyes On You" by Meek Mill and Nicki Minaj, "Take It to the Head" by DJ Khaled, as well as How We Do (Party) by Rita Ora, and Cher Lloyd's debut single Swagger Jagger which both debuted at #1 on the UK Singles Chart The Monarch has also produced several other songs including "Kiss and Tell" by Justin Bieber, "What I Do" by Chris Brown featuring Plies, "Thinkin' About You" by Mario, "Out The Mud" by Kevin Gates, "We On" by Ace Hood, "Biggest Fan" by Chris Brown, "Him" by Tamia, "Take It to the Head" by DJ Khaled featuring Chris Brown, Rick Ross, Nicki Minaj & Lil Wayne, "Piss Em Off" by Ace Hood and "Lay Back" by Rick Ross featuring Robin Thicke
