Park Hyung-sik: Park Hyung-Sik (born November 16, 1991), known mononymously as Hyungsik, is a South Korean singer and actor.  He is a member of the South Korean boy group, and its sub-group ZE:A Five.  Apart from his singing career, he is most well known for his work in "The Heirs" (2013), "High Society" (2015), "" (2016) and "Strong Woman Do Bong-soon" (2017).
