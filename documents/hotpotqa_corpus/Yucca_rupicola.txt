Yucca rupicola: Yucca rupicola is a plant in the family Asparagaceae, known as the twistleaf yucca, twisted-leaf yucca, Texas yucca or twisted-leaf Spanish-dagger.  The species was described by George Heinrich Adolf Scheele in 1850.  This is a small, acaulescent plant with distinctive twisted leaves.  It is native to the Edwards Plateau region of Texas and also to northeastern Mexico (Coahuila, Nuevo León).
