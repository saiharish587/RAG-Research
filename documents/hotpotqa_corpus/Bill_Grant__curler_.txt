Bill Grant (curler): William Alexander Grant (June 16, 1882 – April 16, 1942) was a Canadian curler.  He was the lead of the 1928 and 1929 Brier Champion teams (skipped by Gordon Hudson), representing Manitoba.  Grant was a 1975 inductee to the Canadian Curling Hall of Fame.  He died suddenly in 1942 while attending a curling meeting at the Fort Rouge Curling Club.
