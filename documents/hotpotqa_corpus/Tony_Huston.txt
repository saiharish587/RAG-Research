Tony Huston: Walter "Tony" Antony Huston (born April 16, 1950) is an American actor, writer, and assistant director.  He is known for his work on "The List of Adrian Messenger" (1963), "The Dead" (1987), "Wise Blood" (1979), "" (2010), "" (2008), "Look Up and Wave Your Glove" (2005), "Great Performances" (2002), "" (2001), and "John Huston and the Dubliners" (1987).  He is the son of John Huston and Enrica Soma, and sibling of Anjelica Huston, Danny Huston, and Allegra Huston.  His son is actor Jack Huston.
