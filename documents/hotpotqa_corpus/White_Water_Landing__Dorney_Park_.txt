White Water Landing (Dorney Park): White Water Landing is a Shoot-the-Chutes ride at Dorney Park & Wildwater Kingdom amusement park in Allentown, Pennsylvania.  It was built in 1992, 10 years after a sister park named Cedar Point Built a defunct flume using the same name: White Water Landing (Cedar Point).  This ride is identical to Snake River Falls at Cedar Point in Sandusky, Ohio.  It is located in the upper side near Hydra the Revenge.
