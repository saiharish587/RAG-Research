Montel Williams: Montel Brian Anthony Williams (born July 3, 1956) is an American television personality, radio talk show host, and actor.  He is best known as host of the long-running "The Montel Williams Show", and more recently as a spokesman for the Partnership for Prescription Assistance (PPA), Williams is also active with the nonprofit MS Foundation, which he founded after being diagnosed with multiple sclerosis in 1999.  Williams is also noted for his service in both the Marine Corps and the Navy, from which he was honorably discharged after 15 years of service.
