Connecticut Route 118: Route 118 is a state highway in northwestern Connecticut, running from the borough of Litchfield via the village of East Litchfield to the town center of Harwinton.  Route 118 is a scenic road at both ends.  The 2.8 mi section from the western terminus at the Litchfield green to the village of East Litchfield, and the 0.1 mi section within the town center of Harwinton have both been designated by the state as scenic roads.
