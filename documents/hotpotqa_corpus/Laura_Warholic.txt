Laura Warholic: Laura Warholic; or, The Sexual Intellectual is a 2007 novel by Alexander Theroux.  The plot concerns the relationship between Eugene Eyestones, the writer of an advice column called "The Sexual Intellectual", and his editor's ex-wife, Laura Warholic, whom Eyestones pities more than likes.  This basic story provides the jumping off point for Theroux's satire of American culture.
