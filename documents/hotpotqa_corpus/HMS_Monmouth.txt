HMS Monmouth: Seven ships of the Royal Navy have been named HMS "Monmouth".  Monmouth was the name of a castle and is now the name of a town in Wales; the name also recognises James Scott, 1st Duke of Monmouth, the "Black Duke".  The first HMS "Monmouth" was named after the town but whilst the second one was being used the Monmouth Rebellion took place and this caused some concern to the crew.  Since that time, all of "Monmouth"s have had their names painted in black and the name and the crest are covered because of the shame of the Duke of Monmouth's execution.  Today the officers of the latest HMS "Monmouth" carry black handkerchiefs and the ship is known as the Black Duke.  However the town of Monmouth still gives the freedom of the town to the ship's crew and talks of the "enduring link" between the town and the ship.
