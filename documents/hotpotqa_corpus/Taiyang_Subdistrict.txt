Taiyang Subdistrict: Taiyang Subdistrict () is a subdistrict of Wafangdian, Dalian District, Liaoning, China.  It is based around the small town of Taiyang, Taiyangsheng or Taiyangshengxiang, which lies 19 kilometres east by road of Fuzhou, 64 kilometres north by road from Pulandian.  To the east is the DFM reservoir.
