Bylakuppe: Bylakuppe is an area in Karnataka which is home to the Indian town Bylakuppe and several Tibetan settlements (there are several Tibetan settlements in India), established by Lugsum Samdupling (in 1961) and Dickyi Larsoe (in 1969).  It is located to the west of Mysore district in the Indian state of Karnataka which is roughly 80 km from Mysore city.  Twin (Indian) town Kushalanagar is about 6 km from Bylakuppe.  It also shares the border with Eastern part of Coorg district.
