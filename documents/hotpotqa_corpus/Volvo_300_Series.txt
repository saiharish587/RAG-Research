Volvo 300 Series: The Volvo 300 Series is a rear wheel drive small family car sold as both a hatchback and (later) a conventional saloon from 1976 to 1991.  It was launched in the Netherlands shortly after Volvo acquired a major stake in the passenger car division of DAF in 1973.  The series consisted of the Volvo 340 (previously 343/345) and the later Volvo 360.
