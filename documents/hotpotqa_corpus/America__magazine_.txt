America (magazine): America is a national weekly magazine published by the Jesuits of the United States and headquartered in midtown Manhattan.  It contains news and opinion about Roman Catholicism and how it relates to American politics and cultural life.  Published continuously since 1909, it is one of the oldest periodicals in the U.S., the only Catholic weekly magazine in North America, and is considered the leading Catholic journal of opinion in the country.  The magazine is also available online.
