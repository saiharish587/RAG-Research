Source UK Services: Source UK Services Ltd., or simply Source, is a specialist British-based provider of exchange-traded funds (ETFs) and exchange-traded commodities (ETCs).  The first products of Source, 22 T-ETCs and 13 ETFs, became available in April 2009 on Deutsche Börse.  Source was started by five of the world's largest equity trading houses – BofA Merrill Lynch, Goldman Sachs, J.P. Morgan, Morgan Stanley and Nomura.  In 2014, Warburg Pincus, the large global Private Equity firm, purchased 51% and is now the sixth owner.  As part of that deal, Lee Kranefuss, the innovator behind the iShares ETF line at Barclays and BlackRock, became Executive Chairman of Source.
