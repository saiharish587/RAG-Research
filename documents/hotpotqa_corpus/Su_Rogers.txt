Su Rogers: Susan Jane Rogers, known as Su Rogers ("née" Brumwell, born 22 February 1939) is a British architect and educator.  She was a co-founder and partner in Team 4, and a partner in Colquhoun, Miller and Partners, which became John Miller and Partners in 1990.  Rogers is perhaps best known for her work on the Pompidou Centre in the 1970's, and the concept Zip-Up House in the 1960's.  She was also the architect in two commissions from her parents, namely Creek Vean (while partner at Team 4) and Pillwood House (while partner at Colquhoun, Miller and Partners), which are now both grade II* listed buildings.
