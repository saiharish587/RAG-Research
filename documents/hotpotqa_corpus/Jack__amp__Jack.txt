Jack &amp; Jack: Jack & Jack is an American pop-rap duo from Omaha, Nebraska, consisting of Jack Johnson and Jack Gilinsky, who are now based in Los Angeles, California.  After success via the social media app Vine, the duo turned to a career as musicians.  They are best known for their 2014 single "Wild Life" which has peaked at number 87 on the US "Billboard" Hot 100.  Both attended Omaha Westside High School.
