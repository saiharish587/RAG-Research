The Videos 1992–2003: The Videos 1992–2003 is a DVD featuring all of the music videos released by the American third wave ska band No Doubt, between 1992 and 2003.  It was released first in 2003 as the second disc of the "Boom Box" box set, and was the companion to the first disc in the set, "The Singles 1992–2003".  It was later released as a separate DVD on May 4, 2004 (see 2004 in music).  The video has been certified gold in the United States.
