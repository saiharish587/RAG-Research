Gary Barlow: Gary Barlow OBE (born 20 January 1971) is an English singer, songwriter and record producer.  He is frontman and lead vocalist of British pop-group Take That.  Barlow also served as head judge of "The X Factor UK" from 2011 to 2013 and "Let It Shine" in 2017.  Barlow is one of Britain's most successful songwriters, having written fourteen number one singles and twenty-four top 10 hits.  He has had three number one singles, six top 10 singles and two number one albums as a solo artist, and has had seventeen top 5 hits, twelve number one singles and seven number one albums with Take That.  He is also a six-time recipient of the Ivor Novello Award and has sold over 50 million records worldwide.
