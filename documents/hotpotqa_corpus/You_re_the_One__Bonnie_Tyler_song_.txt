You're the One (Bonnie Tyler song): "You're the One" is a song recorded by Welsh singer Bonnie Tyler for her eleventh studio album, "Free Spirit" (1995).  It was written by German songwriters Rudolf Schenker and Klaus Meine, both of whom are members of hard rock band Scorpions.  The song was produced by Humberto Gatica and Simon Franglen.  "You're the One" was released as a single in 1995.  It spent one week in the German Top 100, reaching number ninety-nine.
