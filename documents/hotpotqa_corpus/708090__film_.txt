708090 (film): 708090 is a 2016 Chinese romantic drama film directed by Lin Yiqi, Deng Jianquan and Chen Muchuan.  It features Kenji Wu, Song Ji-hyo, Zhao Yihuan, Ray Lui, Irene Wan, Duo Liang, Li Fengming, Chen Rui and Lau Shek-yin.  Production started in September 14, 2014 in Shenzhen and ended on October 20, 2014 in Phnom Penh.  The film was released in China by Beijing Huaxinbo Media on May 20, 2016.
