Mark S. Golub: Mark S. Golub (born May 10, 1945) is an American rabbi, media entrepreneur, personality and educator.  He created the first Russian language television channel produced in America, RTN (The Russian Television Network of America) and the first "PBS-Style" Jewish Television Channel, Shalom TV.  Golub is the rabbi of two small congregations in Connecticut, Chavurah Aytz Chayim (Stamford, CT) and Chavurah Deevray Torah (Greenwich, CT), but is most well known as the host of L'Chayim, an interview talk show he created in 1979 in which he discusses "issues of importance to the Jewish community" with prominent Jewish figures.
