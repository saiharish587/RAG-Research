List of Amar Chitra Katha comics: This is a list of titles in the Indian Amar Chitra Katha comic book series.  The table below shows the numbering as part of the old series as well as that of the new series.Titles which were published in only one of the series have been indicated with a "NA" against the series in which they did not appear.The old series runs from #11 to #436 and the new series starts from #501. New series issues typically appear in a deluxe format and are usually reprints of titles in old series.However some issues such as Kalpana Chawla, JRD Tata etc. have appeared in the new series alone.Similarly,although most of the old series have reappeared in the new series, certain issues such as Napoleon Bonaparte, Louis Pasteur etc. are present in old series alone.
