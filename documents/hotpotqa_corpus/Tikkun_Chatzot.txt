Tikkun Chatzot: Tikkun Chatzot (Hebrew: תקון חצות‎ ‎ , lit.  "Midnight Rectification"), also spelled Tikkun Chatzos, is a Jewish ritual prayer recited each night after midnight as an expression of mourning and lamentation over the destruction of the Temple in Jerusalem.  It is not universally observed, although it is popular among Sephardi and Hasidic Jews.
