Fred Mace (footballer): Fred Mace (October quarter 1895 – 5 November 1962) was an English professional footballer who played as a goalkeeper.  Born in Hayfield, Derbyshire, he began his playing career in local-league football with Godley Athletic and Copley Celtic.  In 1919, he joined Lancashire Combination side Stalybridge Celtic.  The club was one of the founder members of the Football League Third Division North two years later, and Mace made one league appearance for them.  Stalybridge left the Football League in 1923 to play in the Cheshire County League, where Mace was described as one of the best goalkeepers in the competition.
