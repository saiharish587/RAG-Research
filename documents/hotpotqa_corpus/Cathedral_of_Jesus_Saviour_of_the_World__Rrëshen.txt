Cathedral of Jesus Saviour of the World, Rrëshen: The Cathedral of Jesus Saviour of the World (Albanian: "Katedralja e Jezusi i Vetmi Shpëtimtar i Botës" , also Catholic Cathedral of Rrëshen) is a religious building affiliated with the Catholic church that functions as the cathedral of Rrëshen.  It is located in Rrëshen, in the municipality of Mirditë, Albania.
