List of acquisitions by Hewlett-Packard: Hewlett-Packard, commonly referred to as HP, was an information technology corporation based in Palo Alto, California, which was split into two companies: Hewlett Packard Enterprise and HP Inc.  The company was founded by Bill Hewlett and Dave Packard in a small garage on January 1, 1939.  As of 2012, HP is the largest technology company in the world in terms of revenue, ranking 10th in the Fortune Global 500.
