1967 Pan American Games medal table: The 1967 Pan American Games, officially known as the V Pan American Games, were a continental multi-sport event held in Winnipeg, Canada, from July 22 to August 7, 1967.  At the Games, 2,361 athletes selected from 29 National Olympic Committees (NOCs) participated in events in 19 sports.  Twenty-one nations earned medals during the competition, and eleven won at least one gold medal.
