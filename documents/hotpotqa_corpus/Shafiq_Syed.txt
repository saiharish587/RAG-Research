Shafiq Syed: Shafiq Syed (born 1976) is a former Indian child actor turned auto rickshaw driver.  At the age of twelve, he starred in a lead role in the Mira Nair's Academy Award-nominated film "Salaam Bombay! " (1988), for which he also received the 1989 National Film Award for Best Child Artist at the 36th National Film Awards.  He drives an auto rickshaw in Bangalore and also works as an assistant in television production units.
