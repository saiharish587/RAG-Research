Maléku language: The Maléku Jaíka language, also called Guatuso, Watuso-Wétar, and Guetar, is an indigenous language of north central Costa Rica.  It is a Chibchan language and Votic language spoken by around 300 to 750 indigenous Maléku people.  This language is considered to be endangered according to The Endangered Languages Project.  Corobicí is possibly a dialect.
