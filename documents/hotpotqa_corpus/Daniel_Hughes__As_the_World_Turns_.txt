Daniel Hughes (As the World Turns): Daniel Hughes is a fictional character on the daytime soap opera "As the World Turns".  Jared and Lindsey Baskin originated the role from 1999–2001.  Dylan Bluestone took over from 2001–06, and Kevin Csolak also played the role in 2006.  Sam Stone last played the role from January 19, 2007 to November 24, 2008
