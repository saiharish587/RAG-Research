Grendon, Northamptonshire: Grendon is a small village and civil parish in rural Northamptonshire, England on the borders of Bedfordshire and Buckinghamshire.  Many houses are made of the local limestone and various older thatched houses still survive.  The name of the village means "green hill" and today the village remains centred on the hill.  As with Earls Barton, the village was owned by Judith, the niece of William the Conqueror.
