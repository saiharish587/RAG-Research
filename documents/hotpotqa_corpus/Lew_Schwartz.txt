Lew Schwartz: Lewis Sayre Schwartz (July 24, 1926 – June 18, 2011) was an American comic book artist, advertising creator and filmmaker, credited as a ghost artist for Bob Kane on DC Comics "Batman" from 1946-47 through 1953, and with writer David Vern Reed, as co-creator of the villain Deadshot.  Alongside Pablo Ferro and Fred Mogubgub, he was cofounder of Ferro, Mogubgub and Schwartz in 1961, a film company whose work includes the credits to Stanley Kubrick's "Dr. Strangelove".  Schwartz was a teacher at the School of Visual Arts during the early 1960s.  He produced a film about Milton Caniff in 1981.
