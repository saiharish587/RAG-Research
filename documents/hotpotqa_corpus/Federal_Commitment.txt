Federal Commitment: Federal Commitment is an Argentinian political coalition proposed by Alberto Rodríguez Saá for the 2011 Argentinian general election.  Adolfo Rodríguez Saá promoted the Commitment for 2015.  The Commitment was composed of the Federal Peronism, Partido Nacionalista Constitucional(National Constitution Party) and Movimiento Independiente de Justicia y Dignidad(Independent Movement for Justice and Dignity) parties.
