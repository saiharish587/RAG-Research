Wild Horse Island: Wild Horse Island is the largest island on Flathead Lake, the largest freshwater lake in Montana.  For centuries, the Salish-Kootenai used the island, approximately 2164 acre in size, in order to pasture horses and keep them from being stolen by other tribes.  The island was part of the Flathead Indian Reservation from the time of its creation in 1872 up until its dismantlement in 1904, when the island was divided into individual plots of land.  A number of attempts were made on the island towards agricultural development, but none succeeded.
