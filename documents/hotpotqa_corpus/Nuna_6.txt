Nuna 6: The Nuna 6 is the 2011 model solar-powered racing car in the Nuna series built by the Dutch Nuon Solar Team.  Nuna 6 has been built by students who are part of the Nuon Solar Team at the Delft University of Technology in the Netherlands.  Nuna 6 weighs 145 kg, and is therefore lighter than the previous 5 cars.
