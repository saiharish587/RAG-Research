Girish Chandra Ghosh: Girish Chandra Ghosh (28 February 1844 – 8 February 1912) was a Bengali musician, poet, playwright, novelist, theatre director and actor.  He was largely responsible for the golden age of Bengali theatre.  He can be referred to as the Father of Bengali Theatre .  He was a versatile genius, a scholar without having any formal educational background, an actor of repute and a mentor who brought up many actors and actresses, including Binodini Dasi.
