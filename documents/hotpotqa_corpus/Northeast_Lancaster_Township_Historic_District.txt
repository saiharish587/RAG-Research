Northeast Lancaster Township Historic District: Northeast Lancaster Township Historic District, locally known as School Lane Hills, is a national historic district located at Lancaster Township, Lancaster County, Pennsylvania.  The district includes 183 contributing buildings and is almost exclusively residential.  The oldest buildings date to about 1820 and include Wheatland and the Herr House.  The majority of the residences were built between 1920 and 1939, and include notable examples of the Colonial Revival, Tudor Revival, and American Foursquare architectural styles.
