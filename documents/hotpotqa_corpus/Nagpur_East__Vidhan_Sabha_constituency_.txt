Nagpur East (Vidhan Sabha constituency): Nagpur East (Vidhan Sabha constituency) (Marathi: नागपूर पूर्व विधानसभा मतदारसंघ ) is one of the 288 Vidhan Sabha (legislative assembly) constituencies of Maharashtra state, western India.  The Constituency Number is 54.  This constituency is located in the Nagpur district.  The delimitation of the constituency happened in 2008.  It comprises part of Nagpur Taluka and Ward No. 6 to 8, 28 to 36, and 67 to 72 of Nagpur Municipal Corporation.
