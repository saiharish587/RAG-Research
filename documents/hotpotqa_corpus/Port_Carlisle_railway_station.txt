Port Carlisle railway station: Port Carlisle railway station was a railway station in Port Carlisle, Cumbria; the terminus on the Port Carlisle Railway, serving the village and old port and the steamer service to Liverpool that ran from here until 1856, when it was transferred to Silloth.  Port Carlisle was two and a half miles away by train from Drumburgh and Glasson was one and a quarter miles away.  The journey time to Drumburgh was nine minutes, although Glasson was a request stop.
