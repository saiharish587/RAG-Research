Jonas Max Ferris: Jonas Max Ferris (born September 13, 1971, Southfield, Massachusetts) is an economist, investment advisor, and Fox News economic analyst who regularly appears as a panelist on "Fox Business' Channel" stocks and investment news program "Cashin' In".  He joined Fox Business Network (FBN) as a panelist on the stocks investment/news program "Cashin' In" in 2002.  He is also the founder and editor of "MAXFunds.com", a website offering financial advice and services to consumers.
