Alois Brunner: Alois Brunner (8 April 1912 – 2001 or 2010) was an Austrian "Schutzstaffel" (SS) officer who worked as Adolf Eichmann's assistant.  Brunner is held responsible for sending over 100,000 European Jews to ghettos and internment camps in eastern Europe.  He was commander of the Drancy internment camp outside Paris from June 1943 to August 1944, from which nearly 24,000 people were deported.
