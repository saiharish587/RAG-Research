Hornblower and the Crisis: Hornblower and the Crisis is a 1967 historical novel by C. S. Forester.  It forms part of the Horatio Hornblower series, and as a result of Forester's death in 1966, it was left unfinished.  There is a one-page summary of the last several chapters of the book found on the final page, taken from notes left behind from the author.  It was the eleventh and last book of the series to be published, but it is fourth in chronological sequence.
