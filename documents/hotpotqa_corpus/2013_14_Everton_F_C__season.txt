2013–14 Everton F.C. season: The 2013–14 season was Everton's 22nd season in the Premier League and 60th consecutive season in the top division of English football.  It was also Everton's 115th season of league football and 117th season in all competitions.  It was the first season without David Moyes as manager since 2002, with Moyes leaving Everton to succeed Alex Ferguson as manager of Manchester United.  In addition, the club crest was redesigned ahead of this season for the first time since 2000.  On 5 June 2013, Roberto Martínez was announced as the new Everton manager, having left his post at relegated FA Cup holders Wigan Athletic.  Everton finished in sixth position in the domestic league in the previous season, missing out on qualification for the UEFA Europa League, meaning it failed to qualify for any European competitions for the fourth season running.
