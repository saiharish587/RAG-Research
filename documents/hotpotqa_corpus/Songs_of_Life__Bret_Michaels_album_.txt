Songs of Life (Bret Michaels album): Songs of Life is the second solo studio album by Bret Michaels, lead singer of the rock band Poison.  The album was released April 22, 2003 and coincides with the same date of his two-year-old daughter Raine's birthday.  The album is written and produced by Bret Michaels, Cliff Calabro and co-produced by Jeremy Rubolino.  The music is inspired by events from Bret Michael's life.
