Halloween Baking Championship (season 2): The second season of the American cooking competition television series Halloween Baking Championship premiered October 3, 2016 on Food Network.  Carla Hall was the only chef to return from the first season; she was joined by fellow chefs Damiano Carrara and Sandra Lee as judges.  The season was presented by comedian Jeff Dunham; and it consisted of five episodes, instead of four episodes as in the previous season.
