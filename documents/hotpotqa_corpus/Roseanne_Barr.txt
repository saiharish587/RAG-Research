Roseanne Barr: Roseanne Cherrie Barr (born November 3, 1952) is an American actress, comedian, writer, and television producer.  She was also the 2012 presidential nominee of the California-based Peace and Freedom Party.  Barr began her career in stand-up comedy at clubs before gaining fame for her role in the hit television sitcom "Roseanne".  The show ran for nine seasons, from 1988 to 1997.  She won both an Emmy and a Golden Globe Award for Best Actress for her work on the show.  It was announced in 2017 that an eight episode revival of the show will air in 2018.  Barr had crafted a "fierce working-class domestic goddess" persona in the eight years preceding her sitcom and wanted to do a realistic show about a strong mother who was not a victim of patriarchal consumerism.
