Oslo Tramway Museum: Oslo Tramway Museum (Norwegian: "Sporveismuseet" is a railway museum located at Majorstuen in Oslo, Norway.  It is dedicated to the preservation of the Oslo Tramway, Oslo T-bane, Oslo trolleybus and buses used in Oslo.  The museum is run by the non-profit organization Lokaltrafikkhistorisk Forening (LTF) with 450 members.  The museum also operates a heritage tramway in Vinterbro outside Oslo.
