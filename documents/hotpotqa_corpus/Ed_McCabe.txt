Ed McCabe: Ed McCabe was born in Chicago, Illinois in 1938.  He is a founder of Scali, McCabe, Sloves, an American advertising agency of the 1960s and 1970s.  In 1974, he was elected to the One Club Hall of Fame at the age of 34.  He remains the youngest person ever elected to the One Club Hall of Fame.  He wrote several of the iconic ad campaigns of his era, including the Perdue Chicken, Volvo and Maxell advertising efforts.
