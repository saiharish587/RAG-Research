Fame Kills: Starring Kanye West and Lady Gaga: Fame Kills: Starring Kanye West and Lady Gaga was a planned co-headlining concert tour by American rapper Kanye West and singer Lady Gaga.  For the tour, which would have supported West's fourth album "808s & Heartbreak" (2008) and Gaga's "The Fame Monster" EP (2009), the pair conceived a production that would unite their different musical audiences.  The tour was scheduled to run from November 2009 to January 2010, but was canceled after public controversy regarding West's interruption of Taylor Swift's Best Female Video speech at the 2009 MTV Video Music Awards.  Shortly after the cancellation, Gaga embarked on her own tour, The Monster Ball Tour, while West took a break from his career.
