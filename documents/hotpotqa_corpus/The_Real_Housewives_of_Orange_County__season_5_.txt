The Real Housewives of Orange County (season 5): The fifth season of The Real Housewives of Orange County, an American reality television series, was broadcast on Bravo.  It aired from November 5, 2009 until March 11, 2010, and was primarily filmed in Orange County, California.  Its executive producers are Adam Karpel, Alex Baskin, Douglas Ross, Gregory Stewart, Scott Dunlop, Stephanie Boyriven and Andy Cohen.
