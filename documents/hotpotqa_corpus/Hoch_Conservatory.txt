Hoch Conservatory: Dr. Hoch’s Konservatorium - Musikakademie was founded in Frankfurt am Main on 22 September 1878.  Through the generosity of Frankfurter Joseph Hoch, who bequeathed the Conservatory one million German gold marks in his testament, a school for music and the arts was established for all age groups.  Instrumental to the foundation, prosperity and success of the Conservatory was its director Joachim Raff who did most of the work including setting the entire curriculum and hiring all its faculty.  It has played an important role in the history of music in Frankfurt.  Many distinguished have taught there: in the late 19th century, with such teachers as Clara Schumann on the faculty, the Conservatory achieved international renown.  In the 1890s about 25% of the students were from other countries: 46 were from England and 23 from America.
