Metamorphosis (Mercenary album): Metamorphosis is the sixth studio album by the Danish melodic death metal band Mercenary.  This is the first album, except for their debut, First Breath, not to include the Sandager brothers, Mikkel on clean vocals and Morten on keyboards and Mike Park on drums since 11 Dreams.  This is also the first album to feature Morten Løwe on drums.  It was released on February 25, 2011, under the label Napalm Records.  The US version was released on March 29 under the label Prosthetic Records, and will include one bonus track.  The song "The Follower" was released on the band's YouTube channel on December 23, 2010, and the song "In a River of Madness" was released for streaming on the band's Facebook page on February 8, 2011.  On February 14, the whole album was released for streaming on the Metal Hammer Germany website.
