Andrew Davies (cricketer, born 1976): Davies was born in Neath and attended Dwyr-Y-Felin high school from 1988-1993.  Andrew joined Christ College Brecon from 1993-1995 and boarded at School House Red.  Davies played first team football, rugby and rugby sevens playing in the championship winning 7's team of 1994/95 under coach Jon Williams.  Andrew captained the cricket team coached by Colin Kleiser.
