1980 World Series: The 1980 World Series was the 77th edition of Major League Baseball's championship series and the conclusion of the 1980 Major League Baseball season.  A best-of-seven playoff, it matched the National League (NL) champion Philadelphia Phillies against the American League (AL) champion Kansas City Royals.  The Phillies defeated the Royals four games to two to capture the club's first World Series championship in franchise history.  Phillies third baseman Mike Schmidt was named as the World Series MVP.  The series concluded after Game 6, which ended with Tug McGraw striking out Willie Wilson at 11:29 pm on October 21, 1980.  Wilson set a World Series record by striking out twelve times (after getting 230 hits in the regular season) in the six-game set.
