Herbert West–Reanimator: "Herbert West–Reanimator" is a horror short story by American writer H. P. Lovecraft.  It was written between October 1921 and June 1922.  It was first serialized in February through July 1922 in the amateur publication "Home Brew".  The story was the basis of the 1985 horror film "Re-Animator" and its sequels, in addition to numerous other adaptations in various media.
