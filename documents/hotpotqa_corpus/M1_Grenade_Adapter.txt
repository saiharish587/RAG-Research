M1 Grenade Adapter: The Grenade Projection Adapter was an expedient rifle grenade used by the American Military in World War II, Korea, and Vietnam.  It consisted of an add-on 22mm stabilizer tube and fins that converted a hand-grenade into a rifle grenade.  It was supplemented by the M17 rifle grenade, and eventually made obsolete by the 40 mm M79 grenade launcher.
