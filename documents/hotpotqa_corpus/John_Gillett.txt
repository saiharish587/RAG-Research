John Gillett: John Gillett (1925–1995) was a British film critic and researcher whose career at the British Film Institute spanned over 44 years.  He was also a programmer for the National Film Theatre on the works of Buster Keaton, early Russian cinema and Japanese cinema, to name a few.  He wrote film reviews for "The Monthly Film Bulletin".  With Ian Christie, he edited "Futurism/Formalism/FEKS: 'Eccentrism' and Soviet Cinema 1918-1936."  He was also an editor of "Yasujiro Ozu: A Critical Anthology", with David Wilson.
