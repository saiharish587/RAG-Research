Symphony No. 1 (Bruckner): Anton Bruckner's Symphony No. 1 in C minor (WAB 101) was the first symphony the composer thought worthy of performing, and bequeathing to the Vienna national library.  Chronologically, it comes after the Study Symphony in F minor and "before" Symphony in D minor ("No. 0").  The first version of the Symphony No. 2 in C minor was completed after the Symphony in D minor.
