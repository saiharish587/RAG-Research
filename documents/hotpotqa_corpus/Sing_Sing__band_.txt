Sing-Sing (band): Sing-Sing were an English indie pop/dream pop band formed in 1997 in London, comprising vocalist Lisa O'Neill (who had previously worked with Locust, Mad Professor, and Kid Loco) and guitarist/vocalist Emma Anderson (formerly of Lush).  They worked with a variety of musicians to create a sound which nodded to 1960s girl groups, electronica and folk.  They disbanded in 2007.
