Angels &amp; Devils (Fuel album): Angels & Devils is the fourth studio album by American rock band Fuel.  Released on August 7, 2007, it was their first studio effort since 2003's "Natural Selection" and was the last Fuel album to feature original songwriter/guitarist, Carl Bell and bassist Jeff Abercrombie.  No musician on this album is still with the band.  It was also Fuel's only studio album to feature new vocalist Toryn Green, and their final album for Epic Records.  With a new singer, the album also introduced a new Fuel logo.
