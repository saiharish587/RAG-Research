1959 Pan American Games medal table: The 1959 Pan American Games, officially known as the III Pan American Games, were a continental multi-sport event held in Chicago, United States, from August 27 to September 7, 1959.  At the Games, 2,263 athletes selected from 25 National Olympic Committees (NOCs) participated in events in 18 sports.  Nineteen nations earned medals during the competition, and eleven won at least one gold medal.
