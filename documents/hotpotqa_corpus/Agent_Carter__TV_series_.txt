Agent Carter (TV series): Marvel's Agent Carter, or simply Agent Carter, is an American television series created for ABC by Christopher Markus & Stephen McFeely, inspired by the 2011 film "", and the 2013 Marvel One-Shot short film of the same name.  It is set in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), sharing continuity with the films and other television series of the franchise.  The series was produced by ABC Studios, Marvel Television, and F&B Fazekas & Butters, with Tara Butters, Michele Fazekas, and Chris Dingess serving as showrunners.
