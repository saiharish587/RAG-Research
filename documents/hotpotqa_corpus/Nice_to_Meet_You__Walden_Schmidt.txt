Nice to Meet You, Walden Schmidt: "Nice to Meet You, Walden Schmidt" is the ninth season premiere of the comedy series "Two and a Half Men" and the first appearance of Ashton Kutcher as Walden Schmidt, "an internet billionaire with a broken heart".  It is the 178th episode of the show, and the first episode without former lead Charlie Sheen as Charlie Harper.  The episode was watched by 28.74 million people on its original air date, making it the most watched episode of "Two and a Half Men".
