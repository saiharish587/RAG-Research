Iris domestica: Iris domestica, commonly known as leopard lily, blackberry lily, and leopard flower, is an ornamental plant in the Iridaceae family.  In 2005, based on molecular DNA sequence evidence, "Belamcanda chinensis", the sole species in the genus "Belamcanda", was transferred to the genus "Iris" and renamed "Iris domestica".  Other synonyms are "Epidendrum domesticum" L., "Vanilla domestica" (L.) Druce, "Belamcanda punctata" Moench, "Gemmingia chinensis" (L.) Kuntze, "Ixia chinensis" L., "Morea chinensis", and "Pardanthus chinensis" Ker Gawl. )
