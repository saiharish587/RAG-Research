Zelo: Choi Jun-hong (Hangul: 최준홍; born October 15, 1996) better known by his stage name Zelo, is a South Korean rapper, dancer, singer and beatboxer.  He is best known as a member of the South Korean boy group B.A.P.  He made his recording debut with the single "Never Give Up" under B.A.P's sub-unit Bang&Zelo in November 2011.  He is currently signed under TS Entertainment.
