Julia Fletcher: Julia Fletcher (born 1958), sometimes credited as Julia DeMita, is an American voice actress whose throaty, articulate vocals have graced many popular animated works and video games.  Among her best-known roles are the Instructor (narrator) in "The Animatrix: The Second Renaissance", Elma and Yunalesca in "Final Fantasy X", Carmila in "", Scaphandra and Judy in "Aeon Flux", and Olmpias in the first four episodes of "Reign: The Conqueror".  She is married to voice actor John DeMita.  They have two sons, Connor (born 1995) and Elliot Fletcher (born 1996).  She also was voice cast for Orphan in "Final Fantasy XIII".
