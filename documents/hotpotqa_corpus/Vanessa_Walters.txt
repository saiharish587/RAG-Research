Vanessa Walters: Vanessa Walters, (born 1978, in London, United Kingdom) is an English novelist and playwright.  She is also a commentator and critic.  She is best known as the teenage novelist discovered to be writing a novel as a hobby to share with her school friends.  Educated at Queen's College, London, when discovered by teachers, the journal was passed over to an agent who quickly had her signed to a publishing company with a five-figure book deal even before she'd left.
