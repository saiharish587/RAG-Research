Sky Airline: Sky Airline is an airline based at Comodoro Arturo Merino Benítez International Airport in Santiago, Chile.  It is the second largest airline in the country behind rival LATAM Airlines.  It serves international routes to Argentina, Brazil, Peru and Uruguay.  It operates under a semi-low cost model.  Compared to other European or US low cost carriers, it has a smaller business model, lower wages for its employees, and lucrative regulatory requirements .  It also operates charter flights in Chile and South America.
