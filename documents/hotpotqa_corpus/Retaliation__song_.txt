Retaliation (song): "Retaliation" is a single by Hip Hop group Jedi Mind Tricks, the third released from their second album "Violent by Design", following "Heavenly Divine" and "Genghis Khan".  Released in 2001, the single was the group's last release on self-run Superegular Recordings.  The following year, the group migrated to Babygrande Records.  "Retaliation" contains a musical sample from "El Rio Y Las Rosas" by Rosita Peru, a vocal sample from "Cross My Heart" by Killah Priest, and a vocal sample from "Money in the bank" by Kool G. Rap.  The single also contains the edited "Retaliation (Remix)", and the B-Side track "Blood Runs Cold", featuring Heltah Skeltah's Sean Price, both originally only available on this single.  Babygrande Records re-released "Violent by Design" in 2004, including both "Retaliation (Remix)" and "Blood Runs Cold" as bonus tracks.
