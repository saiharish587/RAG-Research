H. E. Kirkemo: Henry Elmer "Kirk" Kirkemo (July 26, 1894 - March 22, 1987) was an American architect principally known for his work in western Montana and, in particular, in Missoula, Montana.  His son, James W. Kirkemo, later took over his architecture practice.  His papers are maintained at the University of Montana - Missoula.  At the time of the 1930 and 1940 United States Censuses, Kirkemo was living in Missoula with his wife Lillian and son James Wallace Kirkemo.
