Shearwater, The Mullumbimby Steiner School: "Shearwater, The Mullumbimby Steiner School", also known as "Shearwater Steiner School", is a private co-ed school that caters for primary and secondary education; years K-12.  There is also a pre-school day care on campus.  It is so named after the Shearwater bird; its local township, "Biggest Little Town in Australia", Mullumbimby, in New South Wales; and because it is based on the philosophy of Rudolf Steiner.
