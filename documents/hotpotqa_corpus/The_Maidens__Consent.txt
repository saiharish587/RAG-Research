The Maidens' Consent: The Maidens' Consent (Spanish: "El sí de las niñas" ] ) is a play by the Spanish playwright Leandro Fernández de Moratín.  It was written in 1801 and first performed in 1806.  The play is a satirical commentary on Spanish social norms of the time and has since become part of the repertoire.  37,000 people saw the play performed during the 26 days it ran at Madrid's Teatro de la Cruz, a figure equivalent to around 25% of the population of the Spanish capital during the period.
