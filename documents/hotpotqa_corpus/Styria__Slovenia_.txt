Styria (Slovenia): Styria (Slovene: "Štajerska" ), also Slovenian Styria ("Slovenska Štajerska") or Lower Styria ("Spodnja Štajerska"; German: "Untersteiermark" ), is a traditional region in northeastern Slovenia, comprising the southern third of the former Duchy of Styria.  The population of Styria in its historical boundaries amounts to around 705,000 inhabitants, or 34.5% of the population of Slovenia.  The largest city is Maribor.
