Aphra Behn: Aphra Behn ( ; 14 December 1640?  (baptismal date)–16 April 1689) was a British playwright, poet, translator and fiction writer from the Restoration era.  As one of the first English women to earn her living by her writing, she broke cultural barriers and served as a literary role model for later generations of women authors.
