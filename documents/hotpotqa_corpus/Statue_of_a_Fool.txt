Statue of a Fool: "Statue of a Fool" is a song written by David Ruffin and recorded by many country artists.  It was first recorded in 1969 by country music artist Jack Greene where it was released as a single and became a number 1 hit.  Brian Collins recorded and released it in 1974 from his second album, "This Is Brian Collins".  It peaked at number 10 on the country charts.  David Ruffin, formerly of The Temptations, also recorded a version of the song in 1975.  Bill Medley, formerly of The Righteous Brothers, also released a rendition in 1979 that went to number 91 on the same chart.  In 1989, it was recorded by country music artist Ricky Van Shelton, who released it as a single from the album, "RVS III".  It peaked at number 2 on the "Billboard" Hot Country Songs chart and hit #1 on the Canadian "RPM" country singles chart.
