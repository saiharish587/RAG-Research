1964 New York Jets season: The 1964 New York Jets season was the fifth season for the team in the American Football League (AFL).  The season marked their first in Shea Stadium, after four seasons in the Polo Grounds.  The season began with the team trying to improve on their 5–8–1 record from 1963 under head coach Weeb Ewbank.  The Jets finished the season 5–8–1.
