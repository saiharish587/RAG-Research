Bunnyranch: Bunnyranch is a Portuguese rock'n'roll band originally from Coimbra formed by Kaló, Filipe Costa, Pedro Calhau and André Ferrão.  After departure of Filipe Costa and André Ferrão, they were replaced by João Cardoso and Augusto Cardoso.  The band was named after a famous American brothel called Moonlite BunnyRanch in Mound House, Nevada in the United States.
