Elaine Walker (composer): Elaine Walker is a composer, electronic musician, and theorist.  She specializes in microtonal music, including founding ZIA, an all electronic band, and performing with D.D.T.  She has performed with and mixed the bands Number Sine, Vitruvian, and Alcoholiday.  She describes: "I compose microtonal music strictly by ear and leave it to others to analyze, so you won't find ratios or mathematics here."
