1977 Florida Federal Open: The 1977 Florida Federal Open was a women's singles tennis tournament played on outdoor hard courts in Palm Harbor, Florida in the United States.  The event was part of the A category of the 1977 Colgate Series.  It was the fifth edition of the tournament and was held from September 26 through October 2, 1977.  Unseeded Virginia Ruzici won the title and earned $6,000 first-prize money.
