A.C. Milan in European football: Associazione Calcio Milan is an Italian football club based in Milan, Lombardy.  The club was founded in 1899 as Milan Foot-Ball and Cricket Club, and has competed in the Italian football league system since 1900.  They were the first Italian club to qualify for the European Cup in 1955.  Since then, the club has competed in every UEFA-organised competition, with the exception of the Intertoto Cup.
