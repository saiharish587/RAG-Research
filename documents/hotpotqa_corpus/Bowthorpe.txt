Bowthorpe: Bowthorpe is a suburban village to the west of Norwich, in the county of Norfolk, England.  It is primarily a residential area, but includes a large industrial estate (Bowthorpe Industrial Estate; occupied by mix-use commercial business, including the technology sector) and one small out-of-town shopping centre, containing a supermarket and various smaller retail outlets.  A police station and community hall are situated close to Bowthorpe village centre.  Most of present-day Bowthorpe has been developed from the 1970s onward.
