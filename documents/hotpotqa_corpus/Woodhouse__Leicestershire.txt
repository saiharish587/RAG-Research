Woodhouse, Leicestershire: Woodhouse, often known to locals as Old Woodhouse, is a small village in the heart of Charnwood, England.  The population of the civil parish at the 2011 census was 2,319, including around 300 term-time boarders at the Defence College.  Located between the larger Woodhouse Eaves and Quorn villages, the village contains a mixture of small cottages and large modern houses.  It is a commuter village for both Leicester and Loughborough, as well as further afield.
