CloudVisit Telemedicine: CloudVisit Telemedicine is a software platform designed for physician use.  It allows healthcare providers the ability to schedule and conduct online appointments with patients using a webcam and microphone for live video chat sessions.  The telemedicine platform is available in two formats: CloudVisit Connect and CloudVisit Private Practice.  Both formats are HIPAA-compliant making them viable for physician-patient connectivity under guidelines as set for by the Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA).  The software emphasizes patient and provider security.
