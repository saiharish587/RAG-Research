Bob Gallagher (baseball): Robert Collins Gallagher (born July 7, 1948 in Newton, Massachusetts) is a former outfielder in Major League Baseball who played from through for the Boston Red Sox (1972), Houston Astros (1973–74) and New York Mets (1975). Listed at 6' 3", 185 lb., he batted and threw left-handed.  His grandfather, Shano Collins, was a Major League outfielder/manager and a player in the 1917 and 1919 World Series.
