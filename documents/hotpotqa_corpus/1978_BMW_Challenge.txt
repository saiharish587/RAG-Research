1978 BMW Challenge: The 1978 BMW Challenge was a women's singles tennis tournament played on indoor carpet courts at the Brighton Centre in Brighton in England.  The event was part of the AA category of the 1978 Colgate Series.  It was the inaugural edition of the tournament and was held from 16 October through 22 October 1978.  Fifth-seeded Virginia Ruzici won the singles title and earned $14,000 first-prize money.
