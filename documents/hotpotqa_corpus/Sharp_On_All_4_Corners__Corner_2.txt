Sharp On All 4 Corners: Corner 2: Sharp On All 4 Corners: Corner 2 is the twenty second studio album by American rapper E-40.  The album was released on December 9, 2014, by Heavy on the Grind Entertainment.  The album features guest appearances from B-Legit, Mack 10, Turf Talk, Dej Loaf, Ludacris, Kirko Bangz, Plies, Ty Dolla Sign and others.
