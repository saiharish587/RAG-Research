Western River Railroad: The Western River Railroad (reporting mark WRR) is a narrow gauge rail transport attraction in Tokyo Disneyland.  Its route is 5,283 ft long and does not circle the whole park; it instead passes through Adventureland, Westernland, and Critter Country.  Additionally, this railroad differs from other Disney railroads because its track gauge is narrow gauge as compared with other Disney railroad track gauges of narrow gauge.  At the time that the Western River Railroad was opened, Japanese rail regulations required that any railway line with more than one stop be subject to the same rules as any other conventional rail line.  As such, there is only one stop on the Western River Railroad in order to avoid having to charge fares and to allow the use of passenger cars that are not fully enclosed, which would not be allowed otherwise.  It was sponsored by Takara Tomy.
