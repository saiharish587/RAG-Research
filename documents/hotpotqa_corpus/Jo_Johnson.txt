Jo Johnson: Joseph Edmund Johnson (born 23 December 1971) is a British Conservative Party politician.  He was elected the Member of Parliament (MP) for Orpington in the General Election in May 2010, and re-elected in 2015 and 2017.  From April 2013 to May 2015, he was the Head of the Number 10 Policy Unit, and became Minister of State for the Cabinet Office in July 2014.  Following the May 2015 general election, he became the Minister for Universities and Science.
