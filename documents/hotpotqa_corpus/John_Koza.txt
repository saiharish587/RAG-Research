John Koza: John R. Koza is a computer scientist and a former consulting professor at Stanford University, most notable for his work in pioneering the use of genetic programming for the optimization of complex problems.  He was a cofounder of Scientific Games Corporation, a company which built computer systems to run state lotteries in the United States.  John Koza is also credited with being the creator of the 'scratch card' with the help of retail promotions specialist Daniel Bower.
