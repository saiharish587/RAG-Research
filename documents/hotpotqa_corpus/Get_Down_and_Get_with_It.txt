Get Down and Get with It: "Get Down With It" is a song by American R&B singer-songwriter Bobby Marchan, first released as the B-Side to his 1964 single "Half a Mind".  In 1967, American singer Little Richard would record his own version, which was released as a single.  In 1971, the British rock band Slade recorded a version of the song, "Get Down and Get with It", based on Little Richard's version, which gave the band their first UK chart hit.
