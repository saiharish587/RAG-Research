Spialia dromus: Spialia dromus, the forest sandman, dromus grizzled skipper or large grizzled skipper, is a butterfly of the Hesperiidae family.  It is found in tropical Africa.  In South Africa it is found along the eastern Cape coast to KwaZulu-Natal into Swaziland.  It is also present in Mpumalanga and the Limpopo Province into northern Gauteng and the extreme north-west of the North West Province.
