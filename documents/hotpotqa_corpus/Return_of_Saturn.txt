Return of Saturn: Return of Saturn is the fourth studio album by American rock band No Doubt, released on April 11, 2000 by Trauma Records and Interscope Records.  It marked the band's first album as a quartet, following the departure of original keyboardist Eric Stefani in 1994.  After touring for two and a half years to promote their breakthrough third studio album, "Tragic Kingdom", No Doubt wrote several dozen songs for its follow-up and eventually settled on working with producer Glen Ballard.  Creating the album became a tumultuous process lasting two years, during which there was dissension among band members and between the band and its label.  The album was completed after the band returned to the studio and recorded what became two of the album's singles.
