George Nicholls (rugby league): George Nicholls was born on 14 May 1944 in Widnes, Lancashire.  He played for Rugby Football League club Widnes at prop forward in their 8-15 loss to Wigan in the 1971 Lancashire County Cup Final during the 1970–71 season at Knowsley Road, St. Helens on Saturday 28 August 1971.  While playing club football for Widnes, Nicholls played at Loose forward for the Great Britain Lions who retained the 1972 World Cup.
