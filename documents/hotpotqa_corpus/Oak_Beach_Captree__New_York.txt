Oak Beach–Captree, New York: Oak Beach–Captree is a census-designated place (CDP) in the town of Babylon in Suffolk County, New York, United States.  The population was 286 at the 2010 census.  Prior to the 2010 census the area was part of a larger CDP called Gilgo-Oak Beach-Captree, New York.  The Oak Beach–Captree CDP consists of some small beach communities on the barrier island along the southern edge of Long Island, including Oak Beach, Oak Island, and Captree Island.
