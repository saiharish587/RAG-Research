Deepwater Horizon oil spill: The "Deepwater Horizon" oil spill (also referred to as the BP oil spill, the BP oil disaster, the Gulf of Mexico oil spill, and the Macondo blowout) began on April 20, 2010, in the Gulf of Mexico on the BP-operated Macondo Prospect.  Killing eleven people, it is considered the largest marine oil spill in the history of the petroleum industry and estimated to be 8% to 31% larger in volume than the previous largest, the Ixtoc I oil spill.  The US Government estimated the total discharge at 4.9 Moilbbl .  After several failed efforts to contain the flow, the well was declared sealed on September 19, 2010.  Reports in early 2012 indicated that the well site was still leaking.
