Mary Barton (obstetrician): Mary Barton founded a fertility clinic in Portland Place, London and later in Wimpole Street London with her husband Bertold Wiesner in the late 1930s and it did not close until 1967.  It is assumed that all records were destroyed when the practice closed.  The clinic pioneered artificial insemination using donor sperm for women whose husbands may have been infertile.  The clinic helped women conceive 1,500 babies, nicknamed the 'Barton Brood', although the exact records appear to have been destroyed in 1963 owing to the social taboos and lack of regulation surrounding the subject.
