List of newspapers in the Cayman Islands: The Cayman Islands is a group of three islands in the Caribbean Sea.  The first monthly publication on the islands was "The Gospel of the Kingdom", a religious themed newspaper founded in 1945.  In 1964, the newspaper "Tradewinds" began publication.  This was joined by the rival "Caymanian Weekly" in 1965.  This was followed by a second weekly publication, the "Cayman Compass", which started in 1972.  In 1974, the two weeklies merged to form the "Caymanian Compass".  This became a bi-weekly publication in 1976, appearing on Tuesdays and Fridays.
