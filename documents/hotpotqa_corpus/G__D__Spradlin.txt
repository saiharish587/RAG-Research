G. D. Spradlin: Gervase Duan "G.D." Spradlin (August 31, 1920 – July 24, 2011) was an American actor.  Known for his distinctive accent and voice, he often played devious authority figures.  He is credited in over 70 television and film productions, and performed alongside actors including Marlon Brando, Al Pacino, Charlton Heston, George C. Scott, and Johnny Depp.
