Mae Ngat Somboon Chon Dam: The Mae Ngat Somboon Chon Dam (Thai: เขื่อนแม่งัดสมบูรณ์ชล ; rtgs: "Khuean Mae Ngat Sombunchon" ), is a multi-purpose hydroelectric dam in the Mae Taeng District of Chiang Mai Province, Thailand.  It impounds the Mae Ngat River, a tributary of the Ping River.  The dam is located at the western side of Si Lanna National Park.
