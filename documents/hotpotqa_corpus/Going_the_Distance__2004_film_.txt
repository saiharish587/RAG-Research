Going the Distance (2004 film): Going the Distance is a 2004 Canadian teen/comedy film directed by Mark Griffiths, and written by Eric Goodman and Kelly Senecal.  A road movie set across Canada, its tagline was "They came.  They saw.  They came. " The film was released in Canada as "Going the Distance", but for American release the film's title was expanded to "National Lampoon's Going the Distance".  The Canadian DVD release retains its original release title.
