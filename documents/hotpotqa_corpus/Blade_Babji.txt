Blade Babji: Blade Babji is a Telugu comedy movie released on 24 October 2008 under the banner of Satya Movies.  Its star cast included Allari Naresh, Sayali Bhagat, Venu Madhav, Srinivasa Reddy, Krishna Bhagavan, Dharmavarapu, Kondavalasa, Jaya Prakash Reddy, Melkote, Brahmanandam, Khuyyum, Ruthika, Kausha, Hema & Apoorva.  It was produced by Muthyala Satya Kumar and directed by Devi Prasad.  It fared reasonably well, with people considering it to be a hit movie.  This is based on Hollywood movie "Blue Streak".
