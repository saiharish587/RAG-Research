In God's Hands (film): In God's Hands (1998) is a film by Zalman King released through "Sheen Michaels Entertainment" a production company created by actor Charlie Sheen and Bret Michaels.  The basic story is of three young surfers on a roller coaster action tour of the globe's most exotic and dangerous surfing spots.  They travel to Madagascar, Mexico, Bali and Hawaii seeking the ultimate wave, a 40-foot force of nature that travels at speeds up to 35 miles per hour.
