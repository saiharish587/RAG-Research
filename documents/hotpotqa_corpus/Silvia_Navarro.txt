Silvia Navarro: Silvia Navarro (born as Silvia Angélica Navarro Barba on September 14, 1978 in Irapuato, Guanajuato, Mexico) is a Mexican actress of film, television, and theatre, primarily known for starring in several telenovelas.  In May 2012, People en Español magazine named her one of the "50 Most Beautiful".
