Peanut punch: Peanut punch is a beverage popular in the Caribbean and it is made with peanut butter, milk, sugar and sometimes spices.  It is also available commercially in supermarkets and grocery stores as well to cater those markets.  In Trinidad and Tobago, peanut punch is a popular drink that is often sold on sidewalks or in established food stores.  The drink is traditionally marketed as an energy drink and made with a variety of ingredients according to the vendor.
