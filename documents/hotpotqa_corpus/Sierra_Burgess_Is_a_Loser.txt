Sierra Burgess Is a Loser: Sierra Burgess Is a Loser is an upcoming American teen comedy film directed by Ian Samuels from a screenplay by Lindsey Beer.  The film is a modern retelling of the "Cyrano de Bergerac" story, and stars Shannon Purser, RJ Cyler, Noah Centineo, Will Peltz, Kristine Froseth, Lea Thompson, and Alan Ruck.
