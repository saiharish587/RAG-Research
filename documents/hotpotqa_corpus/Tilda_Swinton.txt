Tilda Swinton: Katherine Matilda Swinton (born 5 November 1960) is a British actress, model, and artist.  She is known for her roles in both arthouse and independent films to large-scale Hollywood productions.  She won the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actress and the BAFTA Award for Best Actress in a Supporting Role for her performance as Karen Crowder in the 2007 film "Michael Clayton".  She also won the BAFTA Scotland Award as Best Actress for the 2003 film "Young Adam", and has received three nominations for a Golden Globe Award.
