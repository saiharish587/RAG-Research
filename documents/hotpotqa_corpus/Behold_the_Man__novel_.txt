Behold the Man (novel): Behold the Man (1969) is a science fiction novel by British writer Michael Moorcock.  It originally appeared as a novella in a 1966 issue of "New Worlds"; later, Moorcock produced an expanded version which was first published in 1969 by Allison & Busby.  The title derives from the Gospel of John, Chapter 19, Verse 5: "Then Jesus came out, wearing the crown of thorns and the purple robe.  And Pilate said to them Behold the Man."
