The Raid: Redemption (soundtrack): The Raid: Redemption is a soundtrack/score album composed by Joseph Trapanese and Linkin Park's co-vocalist Mike Shinoda, who also serves as producer for the album, which was originally inspired from the 2012 live-action film "".  The first official single from the album is "Razors Out" by Chino Moreno and Shinoda.  The second single released for the film is "Suicide Music" by Get Busy Committee and Shinoda.  The two singles were released as a double single on March 16, 2012.
