Robert Cantley: Robert Cantley is a conservationist and Managing Director of Borneo Exotics, a Sri Lankan-based plant nursery specialising in tissue-cultured and seed-grown "Nepenthes" species and hybrids.  Cantley has contributed to a number of papers on "Nepenthes".  The species "Nepenthes robcantleyi", the natural hybrid "Nepenthes × cantleyi" and the cultivar "Nepenthes" 'Cantley's Red' are named after him.
