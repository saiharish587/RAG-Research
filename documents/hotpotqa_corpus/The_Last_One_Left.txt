The Last One Left: The Last One Left (1966) is a mystery novel by John D. MacDonald.  The story largely takes place in southern Florida and the Bahamas, and is similar to many of the author's Travis McGee stories.  The book is in fact dedicated to McGee "who lent invaluable support and encouragement," and a named runabout motorboat later appears in the McGee novel "Pale Gray for Guilt".  The book's subtitle is "A story about money and dying", and it is written on several different levels.  Throughout the plot are subtle discourses on what it means to have a "good" life, how people deal with stress and uncertainty, and at what point will someone reach out for healthy human contact, or else take self-interest as their highest goal.
