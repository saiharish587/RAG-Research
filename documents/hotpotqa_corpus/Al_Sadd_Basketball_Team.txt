Al Sadd Basketball Team: Al Sadd Basketball Team (Arabic: فريق السد لكرة السلة‎ ‎ ) is a Qatari professional basketball team based in Doha, Qatar.  Al Sadd Basketball Team is a branch of Sadd Sports Club, which is the most successful team in all of Qatar, with many domestic and international titles, including 2 AFC Champions League victories for the football team, and 5 AFC Champions League titles for its handball team.
