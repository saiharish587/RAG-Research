Battle of Hama: The Battle of Hama was fought some 24 km from the city of Hama in Syria on 29–30 November 903 between the forces of the Abbasid Caliphate and the Qarmatians.  The Abbasids were victorious, resulting in the capture and execution of the Qarmatian leadership.  This weakened the Qarmatian presence in northern Syria, which was finally eradicated after the suppression of another revolt in 906.  More importantly, it paved the way for the Abbasid attack on the autonomous Tulunid dynasty and the reincorporation of the Tulunid domains in southern Syria and Egypt into the Abbasid Caliphate.
