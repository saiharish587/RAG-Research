Al-Masjid an-Nabawi: Al-Masjid an-Nabawī (Arabic: ٱلْمَسْجِدُ ٱلنَّبَويّ‎ , 'Prophet's Mosque' ‎ ) is a mosque established and originally built by the Islamic prophet Muhammad, situated in the city of Medina in Saudi Arabia.  Al-Masjid an-Nabawi was the third mosque built in the history of Islam and is now one of the largest mosques in the world.  It is the second-holiest site in Islam, after Masjid al-Haram in Makkah.  It is always open, regardless of date or time.
