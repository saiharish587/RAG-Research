Charles Purdy: Writer and etiquette expert Charles Purdy is the author of the book "Urban Etiquette: Marvelous Manners for the Modern Metropolis" (Wildcat Canyon Press, 2004; ISBN  ).  He has written modern-etiquette columns for the "San Francisco Weekly", Gay.com, and "Genre" magazine; his advice has appeared in publications such as "Real Simple", the "Wall Street Journal", and "Men's Health"; and he has appeared on numerous television shows as a guest expert.  As "Charles in Charge," he has also been a regular guest of KFOG Radio's "Morning Show", in San Francisco, California.
