The Real Housewives of Orange County (season 9): The ninth season of "The Real Housewives of Orange County", an American reality television series, was broadcast on Bravo.  It aired from April 14, 2014 until September 8, 2014, and was primarily filmed in Orange County, California.  Its executive producers were Adam Karpel, Alex Baskin, Douglas Ross, Gregory Stewart, Scott Dunlop, Stephanie Boyriven and Andy Cohen.
