Sippin' on Some Syrup: "Sippin' on Some Syrup," sometimes known as "Sippin' on Some Sizzurp," is the second single from Three 6 Mafia's fourth studio album "".  It features UGK and Project Pat.  The song samples the synthesizer intro and keyboard instrumental from Marvin Gaye's 1978 song "Is That Enough" from his album "Here, My Dear".  The song peaked at #30 on the Hot R&B/Hip-Hop Songs chart.
