Alison Aprhys: Alison Aprhys is an Australian journalist with extensive print, online and broadcast experience.  Currently she presents two weekly sports programs on 94,7 The Pulse.  She was formerly with the "Geelong Advertiser", a regional daily newspaper.  Previously, she was a freelance journalist and photographer whose focuses included surfing and firefighting.  Her work has appeared in many newspapers, magazines and online publications including "Sydney Morning Herald", "Griffith Review", "Eureka Street" and cfaconnect.net.au.
