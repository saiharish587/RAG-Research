Wittelsbach-Graff Diamond: The Wittelsbach-Graff Diamond is a 31.06 carat deep-blue diamond with internally flawless clarity.  Laurence Graff purchased the Wittelsbach Diamond in 2008 for £16.4 million.  In 2010, Graff revealed he had had the diamond cut by three diamond cutters to remove flaws.  The diamond was now more than 4 carat lighter and was renamed the Wittelsbach-Graff Diamond.  There is controversy, as critics claim the recutting has so altered the diamond as to make it unrecognisable, compromising its historical integrity.
