Pismo Beach, California: Pismo Beach is a city in San Luis Obispo County, in the Central Coast area of California, United States.  The estimated population was 7,931 in 2014, up from 7,655 in the 2010 census.  It is part of the Five Cities Area, a cluster of cities in that area of San Luis Obispo County.  The "5 Cities" is actually only 3 cities; Grover Beach, Pismo Beach, and Arroyo Grande.  Oceano is a Community Service District and Shell Beach is part of Pismo Beach.  Its motto is "Clam Capital of the World".
