2015–16 Football League: The 2015–16 Football League (known as the Sky Bet Football League for sponsorship reasons) was the 117th season of The Football League.  It began on 7 August 2015 and concluded on 30 May 2016, with the League Two play-off final at Wembley Stadium.  The Football League was contested through three Divisions; Championship, League One and League Two.  The winners of the Championship, Burnley and runner-up Middlesbrough were automatically promoted to the Premier League and on 28 May 2016 were joined by the winner of the Championship play-off, Hull City.  The bottom two teams in League Two, Dagenham & Redbridge and York City, were relegated to the National League.
