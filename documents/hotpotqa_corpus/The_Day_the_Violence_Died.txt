The Day the Violence Died: "The Day the Violence Died" is the eighteenth episode of "The Simpsons"' seventh season.  It originally aired on the Fox network in the United States on March 17, 1996.  It was written by John Swartzwelder and directed by Wes Archer.  Kirk Douglas guest stars as Chester J. Lampwick, Alex Rocco as Roger Meyers Jr., Jack Sheldon as an anthropomorphic constitutional amendment, Suzanne Somers as herself, and Phil Hartman as Lionel Hutz.  The end of the episode features Lester and Eliza, versions of Bart and Lisa Simpson that appeared in "The Tracey Ullman Show" in the 1980s.
