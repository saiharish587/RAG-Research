United States Senate election in Iowa, 2004: The 2004 United States Senate election in Iowa was held on November 2, 2004.  Incumbent Republican United States Senator Chuck Grassley ran for re-election to a fifth term in the United States Senate.  Grassley and former State Senator Arthur A. Small won the Republican and Democratic primaries, respectively, unopposed, and faced each other in the general election.  Though this election coincided with the highly competitive presidential election, Grassley was in little danger of losing his seat and defeated Small handily.
