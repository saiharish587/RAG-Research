Jolanda di Savoia: Jolanda di Savoia is a "comune" (municipality) in the Province of Ferrara in the Italian region Emilia-Romagna, located about 70 km northeast of Bologna and about 30 km east of Ferrara.  Founded as Le Venezie in 1903, it took its current name (from that of Princess Yolanda of Savoy) in 1911.
