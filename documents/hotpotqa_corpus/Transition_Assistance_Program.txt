Transition Assistance Program: The Transition Assistance Program (TAP) is a U.S. Department of Defense-led program that provides service members with information and resources to prepare them for their civilian life.  TAP is an interagency program between the Department of Defense (DoD), U.S. Department of Labor, Department of Veterans Affairs, Small Business Administration, U.S. Department of Education, and Office of Personnel Management.  Service member participation in TAP is mandatory.
