Angie Cheong: Angie Cheong Wai-yee is a Hong Kong-based Malaysian Chinese actress.  She won the Miss Chinese Malaysia in 1992 and went to Hong Kong to compete for the Miss Chinese International in 1993.  After that, she signed a contract with the Hong Kong television TVB and became an actress.  In 2003, she was beaten by her boyfriend and was seriously injured.  Cheong recently returned to Malaysia to film an episode of "Super Trio Series".  In 2013, Cheong returned to TVB to film "Never Dance Alone" after being persuaded by her good friend and executive producer of the serial drama, Eric Tsang.
