Manitoba: Manitoba ( ) is a province at the longitudinal centre of Canada.  It is one of the three prairie provinces (with Alberta and Saskatchewan) and Canada's fifth-most populous province with its estimated 1.3 million people.  Manitoba covers 649950 km2 with a widely varied landscape.  The province is bordered by the provinces of Ontario to the east and Saskatchewan to the west, the territories of Nunavut to the north, and Northwest Territories to the northwest, and the US states of North Dakota and Minnesota to the south.
