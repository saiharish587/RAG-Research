Luke Lee: Luke Lee Wen Loong (born December 16, 1991) is a Singaporean actor, best known for portraying characters in both film and television.  His best known role was Sergeant Heng in Jack Neo's army movies "Ah Boys to Men" and "Ah Boys to Men 2".  He has also appeared in Hong Kong-Singapore horror film "A Fantastic Ghost Wedding".  Notable roles include appearing in MediaCorp Channel 5's "Tanglin (TV series)", "Point of Entry (TV series)", HBO (Asia) 's "Serangoon Road (TV series)" as well as BBC's "Insatiable Teens".
