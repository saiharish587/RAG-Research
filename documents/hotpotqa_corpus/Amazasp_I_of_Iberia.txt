Amazasp I of Iberia: Amazasp I (Georgian: ამაზასპი ) was a king of Iberia (Kartli, modern eastern Georgia) whose reign is placed by the early medieval Georgian historical compendia in the 2nd century.  Professor Cyril Toumanoff suggests 106–116 as the years of his reign, and considers him to be the son and successor of Mithridates I of Iberia who is known from epigraphic material as a Roman ally.  Toumanoff also identifies him with the Amazaspus of the Armazi stele of Vespasian and Xepharnuges of the Armazi stele of Serapit.
