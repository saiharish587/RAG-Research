International Chopin Piano Competition: The International Chopin Piano Competition (Polish: "Międzynarodowy Konkurs Pianistyczny im.  Fryderyka Chopina"), often referred to as the Chopin Competition, is a piano competition held in Warsaw, Poland.  It was initiated in 1927 and has been held every five years since 1955.  It is one of few competitions devoted entirely to the works of a single composer, in this case, Frédéric Chopin.
