These Are Special Times (TV special): These Are Special Times is a one-off American television special by Canadian singer Celine Dion that was broadcast by CBS on 25 November 1998.  The special was a promotion for her first English Holiday album of the same name, "These Are Special Times".  The special was filmed in front of a live studio audience.  It featured Dion (backed by her touring band and a full orchestra) performing holiday music from the album as well as some of her hits.  She was also joined by special guests comedic actress and singer Rosie O'Donnell and Italian Tenor Andrea Bocelli.  The special also included footage of Dion in her hometown of Charlegmagne, Quebec.
