Late Night Tales: Franz Ferdinand: Late Night Tales: Franz Ferdinand is a mix album compiled by Scottish band Franz Ferdinand, released on 15 September 2014 as part of the "Late Night Tales" series.  The mix includes tracks from artists such as R. Stevie Moore, Lee "Scratch" Perry, James Brown, Paul McCartney & Wings and Boards of Canada.  It also features an exclusive Franz Ferdinand cover version of Jonathan Halper’s "Leaving My Old Life Behind".
