GC Biaschesi: GC Biaschesi is a Swiss football club from the town of Biasca in Canton Ticino, the Italian-speaking region of Switzerland.  The team currently plays in Liga 1. , the third highest tier in the Swiss football pyramid.  The club narrowly missed out on promotion to the Challenge League after losing a play off match in May 2008, against FC Stade Nyonnais.  Finished the 2008/2009 season in 13th position.
