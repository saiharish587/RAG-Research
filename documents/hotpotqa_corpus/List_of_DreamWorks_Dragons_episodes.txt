List of DreamWorks Dragons episodes: "DreamWorks Dragons" is an American television series airing on Cartoon Network (for the first two seasons) and Netflix (after the second season) based on the 2010 film "How to Train Your Dragon".  The series serves as a bridge between the first film and its 2014 sequel.
