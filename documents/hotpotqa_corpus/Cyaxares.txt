Cyaxares: Cyaxares (Ancient Greek: Κυαξάρης ; Old Persian: 𐎢𐎺𐎧𐏁𐎫𐎼 "Uvaxštra" ; <a href="Persian%20language">Persian</a>: هووخشتره‎ , "Hovakhshatra " ‎ ; r. 625–585 BC) was the third and most capable king of <a href="Medes">Media</a>, according to <a href="Herodotus">Herodotus</a>, with a far greater military reputation than his father <a href="Phraortes">Phraortes</a> or grandfather <a href="Deioces">Deioces</a>.  He was the first to divide his troops into separate sections of spearmen, archers, and horsemen.
