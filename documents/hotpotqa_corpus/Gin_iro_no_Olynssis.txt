Gin'iro no Olynssis: Giniro no Olynssis (銀色のオリンシス , Giniro no Orinshisu ) is a Japanese light novel created by Hitomi Amamiya, with illustrations by Hisashi Hirai.  It was first serialized in the seinen magazine "Dengeki Maoh" in November 2006.  An anime adaptation directed by Katsumi Tokoro and produced by Toei Animation aired in Japan from October 5, 2006 to December 21, 2006.
