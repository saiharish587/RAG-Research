Mira Nair: Mira Nair (born 15 October 1957) is an Indian American filmmaker based in New York City.  Her production company, Mirabai Films, specializes in films for international audiences on Indian society, whether in the economic, social or cultural spheres.  Among her best known films are "Mississippi Masala", "The Namesake", the Golden Lion-winning "Monsoon Wedding" and "Salaam Bombay! ", which was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Foreign Language Film.
