Boy Hits Car: The band was formed in 1993.  After releasing their first album, "My Animal", independently in 1998, they went on the Sno-Core Tour and recorded their next album, "Boy Hits Car", on Wind-up Records.  Their song "LoveFuryPassionEnergy" was used as a theme song for WWE Hall of Famer Lita and was distributed on the "WWF Forceable Entry" album in 2002.  A third album was self released in 2005 called "The Passage".  In 2006 the band re-released the album in July after signing with Rock Ridge Music as their distributor.  It was announced on their homepage that they will have a new album with a planned release on March 15, 2011.  Boy Hits Car released their 5th studio album "All That Led Us Here" in May 2014 and are following it up with a European tour.
