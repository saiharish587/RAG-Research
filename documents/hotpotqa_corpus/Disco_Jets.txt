Disco Jets: Disco Jets is a "Tongue in Cheek" project organized and recorded by Todd Rundgren and Utopia shortly after recording Todd's "Faithful" LP and including most of the Musicians from those sessions.  It's an instrumental recording humorously parroting 1976's "US Bi-Centennial celebrations, Disco music, science fiction movies such as "Star Wars" and the CB Radio" fads.  It was never officially released until 2001, 25 years after its recording was finished.  It's first official release was as part of the Todd Archive Series Vol.  4 - "Todd Rundgren Demos and Lost Albums" (Rhino CRCL-7707/08) 2-CD set on Rhino Entertainment/Crown Japan, and then more recently in 2012 as a standalone CD Import on Esoteric Recordings and in 2015 on Cherry Red.  It was also released as a limited edition vinyl that was manufactured exclusively by Cherry Red for Record Store Day, only appearing in record shops from Saturday 16 April.
