Starrcade (1997): Starrcade (1997) was the fifteenth annual Starrcade professional wrestling pay-per-view (PPV) event.  It was the tenth Starrcade event produced by World Championship Wrestling (WCW), and it took place on December 28, 1997 from the MCI Center in Washington, D.C.  It featured the ongoing storyline between WCW and the New World Order (nWo) organization.
