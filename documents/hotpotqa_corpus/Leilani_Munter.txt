Leilani Munter: Leilani Maaja Münter (born February 18, 1974) is an American race car driver and environmental activist.  She drives in the ARCA Racing Series, and previously drove in the Firestone Indy Lights, the development league of IndyCar.  She was born and raised in Rochester, Minnesota, earned a degree in biology from the University of California, San Diego and currently lives in Cornelius, North Carolina.
