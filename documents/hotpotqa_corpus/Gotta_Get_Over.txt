Gotta Get Over: "Gotta Get Over" is a pop rock song written by Doyle Bramhall II, Nikka Costa and Justin Stanley.  It was recorded by the British rock musician Eric Clapton for his 2013 studio album "Old Sock".  On February 14, 2013, the song was released as a digital download and CD single for Bushbranch and Surfdog Records.  It features backing vocals by Chaka Khan.
