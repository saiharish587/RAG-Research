Associated Press of Pakistan: Associated Press of Pakistan (APP) is a government-operated national news agency of Pakistan.  The name APP should not to be confused with Associated Press Service (APS) is a private independent Pakistani news agency and other the much larger Associated Press news agency (AP), based in New York, though it is a subscriber to both AP and Reuters, based in London.  APP has News Exchange Agreements with 37 Foreign News Agencies and more than 500 correspondents.
