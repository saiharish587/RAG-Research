Rathsoon: Rathsoon or Rathsun is a village in the Beerwah tehsil of the Budgam district in Jammu and Kashmir, India.  The village population is over 7,500, being one of the largest in tehsil Beerwah.  Rathsoon has a shrine of one Sofi Saint Baab Sahab, located on the hill top.  The neighboring village is Aripanthan, these two villages are separated by River Stream( Nallah) Sukhnag.  The village is 27 km away from Srinagar.  The nearest town is Magam, 4.5 km away.
