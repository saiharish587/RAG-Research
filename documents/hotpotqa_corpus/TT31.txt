TT31: The Theban Tomb TT31 is located in Sheikh Abd el-Qurna, part of the Theban Necropolis, on the west bank of the Nile, opposite to Luxor.  It is the burial place of the Ancient Egyptian official, Khonsu who was First Prophet of Menkheperre (Thutmose III), during the 19th Dynasty or 20th Dynasty.
