Madison Gesiotto: Madison Gesiotto (born Madison Mari Gesiotto on March 20, 1992) is an American conservative commentator, columnist, figure skater, model, and beauty queen who won Miss Ohio USA 2014.  She represented the state of Ohio on NBC and Univision at Miss USA 2014 in Baton Rouge, Louisiana on June 8, 2014.  She is currently the author of a weekly column at "The Washington Times" titled "Millennial Mindset" and previously anchored daily news briefings before they were discontinued.
