Houston Beauty: Houston Beauty is an American reality television series that airs on the Oprah Winfrey Network and premiered on November 2, 2013.  It chronicles the lives of Glenda "Ms. J" Jemison, the owner and director of Franklin Beauty School, and also encompasses some of the hardships the students face outside of the classroom plus the drama that occurs between them.  Franklin Beauty School is the oldest continuously operated licensed beauty school in Texas.
