Sudan (beverage): Sudan (수단 ; 水團/水𩜵 ) is a traditional Korean <a href="Punch%20%28drink%29">punch</a> made with boiled grain cake balls and <a href="Honey">honied</a> water.  It is usually served during the summer for quenching thirst.  Traditionally Sudan was always served during a village rite in 6th month in lunar calendar.  Korean farmers prayed for a bountiful harvest and god’s blessing for their life in the future by making food offering including foods and Sudan drink.  It is sometimes considered a type of "hwachae".
