Dave White (writer born 1979): Dave White (born 1979) is a Derringer Award-winning mystery author and educator.  White, an eighth grade teacher for the Clifton, NJ Public School district, has written two novels featuring former New Brunswick, New Jersey police detective turned private investigator Jackson Donne.  The novels take place in locations around northern New Jersey.  White grew up in Clifton, New Jersey.  He attended Rutgers University and received his MAT from Montclair State University
