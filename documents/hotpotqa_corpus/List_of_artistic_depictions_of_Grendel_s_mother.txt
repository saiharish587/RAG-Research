List of artistic depictions of Grendel's mother: This list of artistic depictions of Grendel's mother (Old English: Grendles modor) refers to the figure of Grendel's mother.  She is one of three antagonists (along with Grendel and the dragon) in the Anglo-Saxon epic poem "Beowulf" (c. 700 – 1000 AD); she is never given a name in the text.
