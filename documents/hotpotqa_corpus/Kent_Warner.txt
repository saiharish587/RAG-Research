He is known to have found four pairs used in the film, (of five pairs that have surfaced).  One pair he sold to memorabilia collector Michael Shaw in 1970 for $2,500.  These ruby slippers were later stolen from the Judy Garland Museum near Garland's birthplace in Grand Rapids, Minnesota sometime between August 27–28, 2005.
