Douche and Turd: "Douche and Turd" is the eighth episode of the eighth season of the animated television series "South Park", and the 119th episode overall.  Written by series co-creator Trey Parker, it first aired on Comedy Central in the United States on October 27, 2004, just before the 2004 presidential election.  In the episode, PETA forces South Park Elementary to change their school mascot, so an election is held to determine a new mascot.  Stan ends up getting exiled from South Park after refusing to vote.
