Museum Ovartaci: Museum Ovartaci in Aarhus, Denmark is a combined art and historical museum dedicated to the history of psychiatric treatment and art produced by patients at the Risskov Psychiatric Hospital.  It is a part of Aarhus University Hospital and resides in the same buildings as the Psychiatric Hospital in Risskov.  The museum also offers social programmes directed at psychiatric patients, including an open atelier, creative workshops and a café.
