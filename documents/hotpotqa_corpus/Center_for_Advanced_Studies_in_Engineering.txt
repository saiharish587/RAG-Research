Center for Advanced Studies in Engineering: The Center for Advanced Studies In Engineering (CASE), is a private research college located in the suburb of Islamabad, Pakistan.  CASE is affiliated with the University of Engineering and Technology, Taxila.  It offers post-graduate and doctorate programs in Computer Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Telecommunication, Software Engineering, and Engineering Management.  The B.Sc program in Electrical Engineering is accredited by the Pakistan Engineering Council and has two variants: Telecommunication Engineering and Computer Engineering.  CASE offers a BBA program and PDP programs under advance Engineering Management Department.
