Buddy Holly (song): "Buddy Holly" is a song by the American rock band Weezer, written by Rivers Cuomo.  It was released as the second single from the band's debut album "Weezer" ("The Blue Album") in 1994.  The single was released on what would have been Buddy Holly's 58th birthday.  The lyrics reference the song's 1950s namesake and actress Mary Tyler Moore.  It reached #2 and #34 on the US Modern Rock Tracks chart and the US Mainstream Rock Tracks chart, respectively.
