Lake Margaret Power Station: The Lake Margaret Power Stations comprise two hydroelectric power stations located in Western Tasmania, Australia.  The power stations are part of the KingYolande Power Scheme and are owned and operated by Hydro Tasmania.  Officially the Upper Lake Margaret Power Station, a conventional hydroelectric power station, and the Lower Lake Margaret Power Station, a mini-hydroelectric power station, the stations are generally collectively referred to in the singular format as the Lake Margaret Power Station.  The stations are located approximately 2.5 km apart.
