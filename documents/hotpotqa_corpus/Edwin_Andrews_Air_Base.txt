Edwin Andrews Air Base: Edwin Andrews Air Base was built by the Spanish Alegre C. Ledesma as San Roque Airfield.  The base was later renamed as Moret Field after Paul Moret, a US Marine Colonel who died in 1943 because of a plane crash.  On March 15, 1945, American forces re-established their units and consecutively improved the base with the help of the U.S. Army airfield construction unit together with Filipino forces.  The single runway was completed to be used to fight against the remaining Japanese forces in the Philippines and was about 4,500 feet long aligned with the prevalent winds in the area.
