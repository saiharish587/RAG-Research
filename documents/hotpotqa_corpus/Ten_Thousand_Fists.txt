Ten Thousand Fists: Ten Thousand Fists is the third studio album by American rock band Disturbed.  It was released on September 20, 2005 and became Disturbed's second consecutive number 1 debut on the "Billboard" 200 in the United States, shipping around 239,000 copies in its opening week.  It has been certified Platinum by the RIAA and was also the band's second number 1 release in New Zealand.  It is also the first Disturbed album to not have the Parental Advisory label.
