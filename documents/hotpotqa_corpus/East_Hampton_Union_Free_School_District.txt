East Hampton Union Free School District: East Hampton Union Free School District is a public school district located in the Town of East Hampton on Long Island, in Suffolk County, New York, United States.  It includes the village of East Hampton in addition to the surrounding area located north of the village.  The district is made up of three schools: the John.  M. Marshall Elementary School, the East Hampton Middle School, and East Hampton High School.  The superintendent is Richard J. Burns.
