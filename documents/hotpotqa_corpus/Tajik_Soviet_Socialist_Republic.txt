Tajik Soviet Socialist Republic: The Tajik Soviet Socialist Republic (Tajik: Ҷумҳурии Шӯравии Сотсиалистии Тоҷикистон , "Çumhurii Şūravii Sotsialistii Toçikiston"; Russian: Таджикская Советская Социалистическая Республика , "Tadzhikskaya Sovetskaya Sotsialisticheskaya Respublika") also commonly known as Soviet Tajikistan and Tajik SSR was one of the constituent republics of the Soviet Union existed from 1929 to 1991 located in Central Asia as a landlocked state.
