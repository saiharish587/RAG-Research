Mark Bickley: Mark Alan Bickley (born 4 August 1969)is a former professional Australian rules footballer who played for the Adelaide Football Club in the Australian Football League (AFL).  Bickley was a player for the Crows from 1991 until 2003, captaining the team to both the 1997 and 1998 AFL premierships.  He was a media commentator, most notably with Channel 9 in Adelaide as their sports presenter.  In 2011 he had a brief coaching career as caretaker coach of the Crows after the retirement of Neil Craig.
