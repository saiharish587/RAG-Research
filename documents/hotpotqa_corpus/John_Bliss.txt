John Bliss: John Bliss (October 8, 1930 – February 28, 2008) was an American actor known for playing the role of the 8th grade Social Studies teacher and former Principal Irving Pal on "Ned's Declassified School Survival Guide".  He was also seen in the first episode of "Out of Jimmy's Head".  He fondly remembered George Clooney, and how George was so polite and called him "Mr. Bliss" and offered him food on the set of "Intolerable Cruelty", in which they both appeared.
