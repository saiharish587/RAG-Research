The Mummy: The Animated Series: The Mummy: The Animated Series is an animated series produced by Universal Cartoon Studios based on the 1999 film "The Mummy".  It premiered on Kids' WB!  On The WB network on September 29, 2001.  It is set sometime between 1920 and 1934.  It was retooled and rechristened The Mummy: Secrets of the Medjai for its second season, which began on February 15, 2003.  The show was cancelled on June 7, 2003.  Reruns of the show aired on Toon Disney.
