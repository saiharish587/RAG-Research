The Spice-Box of Earth: The Spice-Box of Earth is Canadian poet and songwriter Leonard Cohen's second collection of poetry.  It was first published in 1961 by McClelland and Stewart, when Cohen was 27 years old.  The book brought the poet a measure of early literary acclaim.  One of Cohen's biographers, Ira Nadel, stated that "reaction to the finished book was enthusiastic and admiring. . .[noting that] the critic Robert Weaver found it powerful and declared that Cohen was 'probably the best young poet in English Canada right now.'"
