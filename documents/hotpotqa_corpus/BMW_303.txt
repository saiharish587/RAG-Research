BMW 303: The BMW 303 was a small family saloon produced by BMW in 1933 and 1934.  It was the first BMW motor car with a six-cylinder engine and the first BMW motor car with the "kidney grille" associated with the brand.  The platform developed for the 303 was used for several other BMW cars, including the BMW 309, a four-cylinder version of the 303, the BMW 315, a 1.5-litre version of the 303 which replaced it in 1934 and was built until 1937, the BMW 319, a 1.9-litre version of the 303 produced alongside the 315 from 1935 to 1937, and the BMW 329, a development of the 319 with styling based on the newer, larger BMW 326, that briefly replaced the 319 in 1937.
