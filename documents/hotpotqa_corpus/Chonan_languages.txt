Chonan languages: The Chonan languages were spoken in Tierra del Fuego and Patagonia.  Two Chon languages are well attested: Selk'nam, spoken by the people of the same name who occupied territory in the northeast of Tierra del Fuego; and Tehuelche spoken by the people of the same name who occupied territory north of Tierra del Fuego.  The name 'Chon', or "Tshon", is a blend of 'Tehuelche' and 'Ona'.
