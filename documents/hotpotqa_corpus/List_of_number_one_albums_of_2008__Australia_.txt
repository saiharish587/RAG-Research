List of number-one albums of 2008 (Australia): The highest-selling albums in Australia are ranked in the Australian Recording Industry Association albums chart, also known as the ARIA Charts, published by the Australian Recording Industry Association (ARIA).  The data are compiled from a sample that includes music stores, music departments at electronics and department stores and Internet sales, in other words, both digital as well as CD sales.  ARIA also issues a weekly singles chart and an end of year albums and singles chart, among other charts.
