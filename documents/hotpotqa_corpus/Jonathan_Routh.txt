Jonathan Routh: Jonathan Routh, born John Reginald Surdeval Routh, (1927–2008) co-starred in the British version of the television show "Candid Camera" (1960–67) and co-starred with Germaine Greer and Kenny Everett in a later attempt at a revival, "Nice Time" (1968).  He published a number of humorous books, and also painted for many years.
