Da'Rel Scott: Da'Rel Scott (born May 26, 1988) is an American football running back who is currently a free agent.  He was selected in the seventh round of the 2011 NFL Draft by the New York Giants.  He played college football for the University of Maryland, where he was a starting running back.  During the 2008 season, he was the second-leading rusher in the Atlantic Coast Conference, behind Jonathan Dwyer of Georgia Tech.
