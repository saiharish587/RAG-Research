Walney Wind Farm: Walney Wind Farm is an offshore wind farm 14 km west of Walney Island off the coast of Cumbria, in the Irish Sea, England.  It has a capacity of 367 MW, which makes it one of the world's largest offshore wind farms.  The wind farm was developed by Walney (UK) Offshore Windfarms Limited, a partnership between DONG Energy and Scottish and Southern Energy.  The farm is immediately north west of the West of Duddon Sands Wind Farm and also to the west of Ormonde Wind Farm.  The farm is in water depths ranging from 19m to 23m and covers an area of approximately 73 km.
