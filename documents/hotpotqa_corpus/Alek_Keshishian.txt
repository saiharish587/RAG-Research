Alek Keshishian: Alek Keshishian (Armenian: Ալեք Գևորգի Քեշիշյան , born 30 July 1964 in Beirut, Lebanon) is an Armenian-American film and commercial director, writer, producer and music video director.  He is best known for his 1991 film "" which was, at the time, the highest-grossing documentary of all time.
