Alston Moor: Alston Moor is a civil parish, also electoral ward in Cumbria, England, based around the small town of Alston.  It is set in the moorlands of the North Pennines, mostly at an altitude of over 1000 feet.  The parish/ward had a population of 2,088 at the 2011 census.  As well as the town of Alston, the parish includes the villages of Garrigill and Nenthead, along with the hamlets of Nenthall, Nentsberry, Galligill, Blagill, Ashgill, Leadgate, Bayles and Raise.  Alston Moor is part of the North Pennines Area of Outstanding Natural Beauty (AONB), the second largest of the 40 AONBs in England and Wales.
