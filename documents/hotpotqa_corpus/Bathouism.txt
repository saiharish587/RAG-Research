Bathouism: Bathouism (बाथौ) is the traditional religion of the Bodo people or Kachari people.  The name "Bathou" in Bodo means "five principles".  The five principles are: "bar" (air), "san" (sun), "ha" (earth), "dwi" (water) and "okhrang" (sky).  The chief deity, called "Bathoubwrai" ("bwarai": "the Elder")—omnipresent, omniscient and omnipotent—is said to have created the five principles.  Though there are other minor gods and goddesses, Bathoubwrai is considered the Supreme God.  Bathoubwrai is unseen.  The second most important deity is "Mainao", the consort of Bathoubwrai, who is considered as the "protector of the rice fields".
