Duke Cunningham: Randall Harold Cunningham (born December 8, 1941), usually known as Randy or Duke, is a United States Navy 20-year career pilot and officer, retiring as a commander; and a Republican politician.  He served as member of the United States House of Representatives from California's 50th Congressional District from 1991 to 2005.  He resigned in 2005, after having pleaded guilty to bribery, fraud, and tax evasion.
