1982 CART PPG Indy Car World Series: The 1982 CART PPG Indy Car World Series season, the fourth in the CART era of U.S. open-wheel racing, consisted of 11 races, beginning in Avondale, Arizona on March 28 and concluding at the same location on November 6.  The PPG Indy Car World Series Drivers' Champion was Rick Mears.  Rookie of the Year was Bobby Rahal.
