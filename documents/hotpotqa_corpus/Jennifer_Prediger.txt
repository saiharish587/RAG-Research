Jennifer Prediger: Jennifer Prediger is New York City–based actress, writer, and director known for the films "Uncle Kent" (2011), "Red Flag" (2012), and "Apartment Troubles" (2014)—her directorial debut with collaborator Jess Weixler.  Prediger also played and wrote "Ask Umbra", the "world's most trusted eco advice columnist," on Grist.org.
