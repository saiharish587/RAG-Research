2011 Hampton Pirates football team: The 2011 Hampton Pirates football team represented Hampton University in the 2011 NCAA Division I FCS football season.  The Pirates were led by third year head coach Donovan Rose and played their home games at Armstrong Stadium.  They are a member of the Mid-Eastern Athletic Conference.  They finished the season 7–4, 5–3 in MEAC play to finish in a tie for fourth place.
