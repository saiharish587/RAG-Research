Armaan (1966 film): Armaan (Urdu: ارمان) is a Pakistani black-and-white film produced by Waheed Murad and directed by Pervaiz Malik.  It was the first Pakistani film to complete 75 weeks in cinemas and, thus, became the first Pakistani "Platinum Jubilee" film.  The film was picturized in black-and-white prints in Karachi.
