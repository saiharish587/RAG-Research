Shotgun start: A Shotgun start is a golf tournament format in which all groups of players tee off simultaneously from different holes.  Each hole on a course will be the tee off hole for each foursome.  Group 1 would start from hole 1, Group 2 from hole 2, etc.  Each group starts play at the same time.  A shotgun start will allow a tournament to end at the same time it takes the slowest foursome to finish a full round or 18 holes of golf.  If there are more than 18 teams, some double up at the 4 and 5 par holes as A & B teams.
