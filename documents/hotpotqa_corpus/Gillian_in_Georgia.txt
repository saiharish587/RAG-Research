Gillian in Georgia: Gillian in Georgia is a U.S. television sitcom micro-series created and produced by Jenna Milly and Angela Barnes Gomes.  The show is about Gillian, a hip New Yorker, who comes to the South to live with her sister's family.  The show premiered on April 28, 2010 on TBS.  The show stars Jill Marie Jones, Shondrella Avery, Darrin Dewitt Henson and James Mathis III.
