Tabernaemontana: Tabernaemontana is a genus of flowering plants in the family Apocynaceae.  It has a pan-tropical distribution, found in Asia, Africa, Australia, North America, South America, and a wide assortment of oceanic islands.  These plants are shrubs and small trees growing to 1–15 m tall.  The leaves are evergreen, opposite, 3–25 cm long, with milky sap; hence it is one of the diverse plant genera commonly called "milkwood".  The flowers are fragrant, white, 1–5 cm in diameter.
