Nadine (album): Nadine is an album by George Thorogood and the Destroyers.  Although it was marketed as a new release in 1986, "Nadine" is actually a repackaged version of the 1979 vinyl album "Better Than the Rest" that was reissued on compact disc.  The songs on "Nadine" were Thorogood's first recordings, which were made in 1974.
