Surada: Surada (also known as Sorada or Suruda or Soroda) is a town which is a Notified Area Council and it is the headquarter of Surada Block which area is about 2663.89 km under the Bhanjanagar Sub-division in Ganjam district in the Indian state of Odisha.  Surada Block consists of 364 villages, 25 Grama Panchayats and Surada (N.A.C.).
