Honky Tonk Attitude (song): "Honky Tonk Attitude" is a song co-written and recorded by American country music artist Joe Diffie.  It was released in March 1993 as the lead single and title track from his album "Honky Tonk Attitude".  The song reached the top five of the "Billboard" Hot Country Singles & Tracks (now Hot Country Songs) chart and peaked at number 11 on the Canadian "RPM" Country Tracks chart.  The song was written by Diffie and Lee Bogan.
