Interstate (Mike Burns &amp; Mark Lewis): Interstate is an LA-based electronic music production duo made up of Mike Burns (born 1972 in Miami Beach) and Mark Lewis (born in South London).  The production pair became known to fans and the industry back in 2005 when Armin Van Buuren's Netherlands based record label Armada Music signed them to a 3 track deal.  Their breakthrough single "I Found U" was later featured on Paul Oakenfold's Grammy nominated Creamfields compilation.  They have had releases on Paul Van Dyk's Vandit Records as well as System Recordings in the US.
