Eli Gold: Eli Gold (born December 15, 1953) is an American sportscaster.  Gold is best known as the radio voice for the Alabama Crimson Tide football team, along with Tom Roberts, as part of the Crimson Tide Sports Network since 1988.  He has also been the host of "NASCAR Live" on the Motor Racing Network since 1982.  He formerly called play-by-play for Arena Football League's coverage on TNN and NBC and currently calls college football and NFL games for Sports USA Radio Network.
