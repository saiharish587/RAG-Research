Cottonwood-Oak Creek School District: In 1877 the district's first school opened in Upper Verde.  In 1878 Yavapai County School DIstrict #6, the first school in the Verde Valley, opened in 1878.  In 1890 the Jerome School District #9 opened.  In 1896 the Oak Creek District #16 in Cornville opened.  In 1917 Clemenceau (bungalow) School opened.  In 1917 Jerome High School opened.  Clemenceau Public School opened.  In 1954 Sedona residents within Yavapai County officially joined the COCSD school district.  In 1960 Mingus Union High School opened.
