Rabbi Jonathan: Rabbi Jonathan (Hebrew: רבי יונתן, "Rabi Yonatan") was a "tanna" of the 2nd century and schoolfellow of R. Josiah, apart from whom he is rarely quoted.  Jonathan is generally so cited without further designation; but there is ample reason for identifying him with the less frequently occurring Jonathan (or Nathan) b. Joseph (or "Jose"; comp.  Mek.  , Yitro, Baḥodesh, 10, with Sifre, Deut.  32; Mek., Ki Tissa, 1, with Yoma 85b; Tosef. , Niddah, ii.  2, Ket.  60b, and Yer.  Soṭah vii.  19c).
