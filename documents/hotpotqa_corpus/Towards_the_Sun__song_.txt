Towards the Sun (song): "Towards the Sun" is a song recorded by Barbadian singer Rihanna for the soundtrack to the 2015 film "Home".  The song premiered on BBC Radio 1 on February 24, 2015 and it was released for digital download as the soundtrack's lead single the same day though Westbury Road.  The song was set to impact mainstream radio on March 17, 2015 however the radio release was cancelled.  The song was written and produced by Tiago Carvalho and Gary Go, with an additional writing done by Rihanna.
