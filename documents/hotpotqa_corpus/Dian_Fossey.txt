Dian Fossey: Dian Fossey ( ; January 16, 1932 – c. December 26, 1985) was an American primatologist and conservationist known for undertaking an extensive study of mountain gorilla groups over a period of 18 years.  She studied them daily in the mountain forests of Rwanda, initially encouraged to work there by anthropologist Louis Leakey.  Her 1983 book, "Gorillas in the Mist", combines her scientific study of the gorillas at Karisoke Research Center with her own personal story.  It was adapted into a 1988 film of the same name.  Fossey was brutally murdered in her cabin at a remote camp in Rwanda in December 1985.
