It Was Raining That Night: It Was Raining That Night is a film simultaneously made in English-Bengali bilingual film.  Written and directed by Indian actor-director Mahesh Manjrekar, it is a relationship drama.  The film was notable for being a joint venture production of India,USA and Bangladesh.  Stars Riaz, Riya Sen, Sushmita Sen, Victor Banerjee, Mahesh Manjrekar, Moon Moon Sen, Dawn Moeller, Stefanie Siegel.
