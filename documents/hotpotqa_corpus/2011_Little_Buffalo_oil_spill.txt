2011 Little Buffalo oil spill: The Little Buffalo oil spill on April 29, 2011, resulted in the discharge of 28,000 barrels of oil in an isolated stretch of boreal forest in northern Alberta, about ten kilometres from Little Buffalo, Alberta.  The spill was caused by rain then damaging the pipes in the Rainbow Pipeline system, owned by Plains Midstream Canada, a unit of Plains All American Pipeline.  It was the largest oil spill in Alberta in 36 years.  The local school was closed following the oil spill due to concerns about the effects of fumes.
