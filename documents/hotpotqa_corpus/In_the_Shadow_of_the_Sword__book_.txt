In the Shadow of the Sword (book): In the Shadow of the Sword is a history book charting the origins of Islam.  The author, Tom Holland, had previously written two works on ancient history: "", which charted the fall of the Roman Republic, and "Persian Fire", which is an account of the Greco-Persian Wars during the 5th century BC.  According to Holland, "To understand the origins of Islam, and why it evolved in the way that it did, we must explore the empires and religions of late antiquity".
