2017 ABN AMRO World Tennis Tournament: The 2017 ABN AMRO World Tennis Tournament (or Rotterdam Open) was a men's tennis tournament played on indoor hard courts.  It took place at the Ahoy Rotterdam arena in the Dutch city of Rotterdam, between 13–19 February 2017.  It was the 44th edition of the tournament, and part of the ATP World Tour 500 series of the 2017 ATP World Tour.
