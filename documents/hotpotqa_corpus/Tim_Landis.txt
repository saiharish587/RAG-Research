Tim Landis: Timothy Joseph "Tim" Landis (born July 13, 1964) is an American football coach who is currently quarterbacks coach and special teams coordinator at Lycoming College.  Previously, Landis was the head coach for the Rensselaer Polytechnic Institute football team.  He was also formerly the offensive coordinator for the San Jose State Spartans football team and the head football coach for Bucknell University.  He compiled a 23–33 record at Bucknell since 2003 and a 76–85–1 record overall.  Prior to arriving at Bucknell, Landis served as head football coach at Davidson and St. Mary's.
