Birthday (Katy Perry song): "Birthday" is a disco song recorded by American singer Katy Perry for her fourth studio album, "Prism" (2013).  She co-wrote the song with Bonnie McKee and its producers Dr. Luke, Max Martin, and Cirkut.  Critics, as well as Perry herself, have compared the track to the music of Prince and Mariah Carey.  Through double entendres in the lyrics of "Birthday", Perry makes sexual references while celebrating a partner's birthday.  Capitol Records sent the track to mainstream and rhythmic radio on April 21, 2014 as the album's fourth single.
