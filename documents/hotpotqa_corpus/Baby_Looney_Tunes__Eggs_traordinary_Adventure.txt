Baby Looney Tunes' Eggs-traordinary Adventure: Baby Looney Tunes' Eggs-traordinary Adventure is a 2003 American direct-to-video animated comedy film from Warner Bros.  Animation.  It is the first, and only, movie based on the TV series "Baby Looney Tunes".  The film follows the main characters from "Baby Looney Tunes" as they go on a search for the true meaning of Easter.
