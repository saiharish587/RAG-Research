Václav Havel Tribute Concert: Tribute to Václav Havel (Czech: Pocta Václavu Havlovi) was an event held in memory of Václav Havel, the last Czechoslovak and the first Czech President, writer, playwright and human rights activist.  The concert took place in Lucerna Music Bar in Prague on 23 December 2011.  Václav Havel died on Sunday 18 December 2011 at 10:15 a.m.
