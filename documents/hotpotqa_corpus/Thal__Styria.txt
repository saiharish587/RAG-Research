Thal, Styria: Thal (] ) is a small village in Austria about 3.2 km west from the edge of Graz, Austria's second largest city.  Its population in the 2014 census was 2,240.  It is the birthplace of actor, bodybuilder, and the 38th Governor of California Arnold Schwarzenegger, who lived in Thal until 1966 at age 19.  On July 2011, Schwarzenegger opened his childhood house as the Arnold Schwarzenegger Museum.  It also boasts a modern church.
