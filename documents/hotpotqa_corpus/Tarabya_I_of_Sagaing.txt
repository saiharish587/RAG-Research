Tarabya I of Sagaing: Tarabya I (Burmese: တရဖျားကြီး , ] ;1297–1339) was king of Sagaing from 1327 to 1335/36.  He succeeded King Saw Yun, his maternal half-brother.  In 1335/36, he was brought put under arrest by his own son Shwetaungtet.  The deposed king managed to have Shwetaungtet killed in 1339 but he himself was killed by Chief Minister Nanda Pakyan.
