Wim de Bois: Cornelis Hendrik Willem de Bois (19 June 1896 – 1 August 1975) is a former Dutch football manager and player who played for AFC Ajax in the Netherlands.  During his managerial career he managed the Dutch Guyana national team ("now known as Suriname"), DFC, DOS, RBC, Go Ahead, SC Veendam, VV Oldenzaal, Quick '20 and Tubantia.
