2017–18 VfL Wolfsburg season: The 2017–18 VfL Wolfsburg season is the 73rd season in the football club's history and 21st consecutive and overall season in the top flight of German football, the Bundesliga, having been promoted from the 2.  Bundesliga in 1997.  In addition to the domestic league, VfL Wolfsburg also are participating in this season's edition of the domestic cup, the DFB-Pokal.  This is the 16th season for Wolfsburg in the VOLKSWAGEN ARENA, located in Wolfsburg, Lower Saxony, Germany.  The season covers a period from 1 July 2017 to 30 June 2018.
