DJ Klever: Josh Winkler (born July 4, 1977), better known as DJ Klever is a prominent American turntablism and 2 time US Disco Mix Club champion.  He has toured across the world and resides in Atlanta, Georgia.  Since early 2014 he has been rapper Yelawolf's touring DJ, he later started working with the rapper and signed to his record label Slumerican.  He is involved in the rapper's second studio album "Love Story", and has appeared to most of the single's music videos.
