Fredric Warburg: Fredric John Warburg (27 November 1898 – 25 May 1981) was a British publisher best known for his association with the author George Orwell.  During a career spanning a large part of the 20th century and ending in 1971 Warburg published Orwell's "Animal Farm" (1945) as well as "Nineteen Eighty-Four" (1949), and works by other leading figures such as Thomas Mann and Franz Kafka.  Other notable publications included "The Third Eye" by Lobsang Rampa, Pierre Boulle's "The Bridge over the River Kwai", Adolf Hitler's "Mein Kampf" and William Shirer's "The Rise and Fall of the Third Reich".
