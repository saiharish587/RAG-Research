Say Hello to Soft Cell: Say Hello to Soft Cell is a budget-priced compilation album by Soft Cell.  The album was released in 1996 by Spectrum and collects singles (minus "Tainted Love", their most popular hit), album tracks and b-sides.  It is also notable for the inclusion of "A Man Can Get Lost" (incorrectly titled as "A Man Could Get Lost" in the artwork), formerly previously available only on 7" vinyl single and unavailable on CD, it remains the only CD version of this song.  The four page booklet contains a brief biography by Marc Almond.
