Return of the Sentimental Swordsman: Return of the Sentimental Swordsman, also known as The Flying Blade, is a 1981 Hong Kong "wuxia" film written and directed by Chor Yuen and produced by the Shaw Brothers Studio, based on Gu Long's "Xiaoli Feidao" series of novels.  It stars Ti Lung, Alexander Fu Sheng and Derek Yee.
