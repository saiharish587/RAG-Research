Laramie High School (Wyoming): Laramie High School (LHS) is a high school (grades 9-12) in Laramie, Albany County, Wyoming, United States.  In the Albany County School District, high school begins in the 9th grade (freshman year); 9th grade students are now able to attend high school in Laramie due to the building of a new high school.  Many LHS students concurrently attend classes at Laramie County Community College (Albany County Campus), or the University of Wyoming.
