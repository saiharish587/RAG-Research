Lynx Software Technologies: Lynx Software Technologies, Inc. (formerly LynuxWorks) is a San Jose, California software company founded in 1988.  Lynx Software Technologies specializes in secure virtualization and open and reliable real-time operating system (RTOS).  Originally known as Lynx Real-Time Systems, the company changed its name to LynuxWorks in 2000 after acquiring, and merging with, ISDCorp (Integrated Software & Devices Corporation) a nine-year-old embedded systems company with a strong Linux background.  In May 2014, the company changed its name to Lynx Software Technologies as a representation of the company’s forward direction as the LynxOS RTOS family of products and the LynxSecure hypervisor continued to gain increased traction both with current customers and markets, and with the new Internet connected embedded world.
