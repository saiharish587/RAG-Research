Viacheslav Zhigalin: Viacheslav Vladimirovich Zhigalin (Russian: Вячеслав Владимирович Жигалин ; born 27 February 1950) is a former ice dancer who competed for the Soviet Union.  With partner Tatiana Voitiuk, he is the 1970 European bronze medalist and 1972 Soviet national champion.  With partner Lidia Karavaeva he won the bronze medal at the 1975 Prize of "Moscow News".
