BB Talk: "BB Talk" is a song by American singer Miley Cyrus for her fifth studio album "Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz" (2015).  It was premiered through SoundCloud on August 30, 2015 alongside the parent record.  Its lyrics discuss Cyrus' frustration with an overbearing romantic interest over primarily spoken verses.  "BB Talk" was supplemented with an accompanying music video on December 11, 2015, featuring Cyrus as an adult baby.  Cyrus performed the track during her Milky Milky Milk Tour in November and December.
