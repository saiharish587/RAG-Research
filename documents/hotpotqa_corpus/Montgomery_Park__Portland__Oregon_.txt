Montgomery Park (Portland, Oregon): Montgomery Park is an office building and former Montgomery Ward mail-order catalog warehouse and department store located in Portland, Oregon, United States, built in 1920.  It is listed on the National Register of Historic Places under its historic name Montgomery Ward & Company Building.  The building is located on property once used for the Lewis and Clark Centennial Exposition, of 1905.  It was occupied by Montgomery Ward from 1920 until 1985, although the majority of the company's operations at this location ended in 1982.  The building is the second largest office building in Portland with 577339 ft2 .
