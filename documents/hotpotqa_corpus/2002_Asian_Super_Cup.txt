2002 Asian Super Cup: The 2002 Asian Super Cup was the 8th Asian Super Cup, a football match played between the winners of the previous season's Asian Club Championship and Asian Cup Winners Cup competitions.  The 2002 competition was contested by Suwon Samsung Bluewings of South Korea, who won the Asian Club Championship 2001-02, and Al Hilal of Saudi Arabia, the winners of the Asian Cup Winners Cup 2002.
