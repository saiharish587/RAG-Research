Windgap GAA: Windgap GAA is a Gaelic Athletic Association club located in Windgap, County Kilkenny, Ireland.  The club was founded in 1954 and is almost exclusively concerned with the game of hurling. Windgap are a Junior club located in South Kilkenny on the Tipperary border.  Senior County hurlers from the club are Kieran Purcell and Paddy Walsh.  Due to low numbers the underage team amalgamated with Galmoy in 2006 and won the "B" league the same year,they are still joined together in underage levels and have had some good successes.  The Juniors have not had any major successes in recent years and are still slugging away in Junior.
