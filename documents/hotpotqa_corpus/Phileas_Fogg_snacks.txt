Phileas Fogg snacks: Phileas Fogg is a range of branded snack products in the United Kingdom that was created in 1982.  The product is named after Phileas Fogg, the protagonist of Jules Verne's "Around the World in Eighty Days", and is made in Consett, County Durham.  When the Phileas Fogg company was sold in the 1990s, its products were rebranded but poorly received, leading to a decline in popularity.  The brand was relaunched in 2009.
