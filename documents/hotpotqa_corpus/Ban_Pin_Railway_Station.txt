Ban Pin Railway Station: Ban Pin Railway Station is a railway station located in Ban Pin Subdistrict, Long District, Phrae.  It is located 563.865 km from Bangkok Railway Station and is a class 2 railway station.  It is on the Northern Line of the State Railway of Thailand.  The station opened in June 1914, following the Northern Line extension from Huai Mae Ta to Ban Pin.  The line continued to Pha Khan in 1915.
