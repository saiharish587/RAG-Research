Joe Casely-Hayford: Joe Casely-Hayford, OBE (born in Kent, England, 24 May 1956), is a British fashion designer.  Since the mid 1980s he has established an international reputation as one of the UK's most respected and consistently relevant designers of men's and womenswear clothing.  He was appointed an Officer of the Order of the British Empire, for services to the fashion industry, in the Queen's Birthday Honours List, published on 16 June 2007.
