Hendrick van Cleve III: Hendrick or Hendrik van Cleve III (c. 1525 in Antwerp - between 1590 and 1595) was a Flemish painter and engraver.  He was the son and pupil of Willem van Cleve the Elder, and the elder brother of Marten van Cleve the Elder and of Willem Van Cleve the Younger.  He is called "the third" to differentiate him from Hendrik van Cleve I (registered as a master of the Guild of St. Luke 1489/90) and Hendrick II (Guild of St. Luke, 1534), about whom little else is known.
