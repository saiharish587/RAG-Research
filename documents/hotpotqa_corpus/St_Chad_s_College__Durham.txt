St Chad's College, Durham: St Chad's College is a recognised (independent) college of Durham University in England, founded in 1904 as an Anglican hall for the training of Church of England clergy.  The main part of the college is located on the Bailey, occupying nine historic buildings at the east end of Durham Cathedral.  It neighbours Hatfield College to its north, while St John's College and St Cuthbert's Society are to its south.  The college is named after St Chad of Mercia, a 7th-century bishop.
