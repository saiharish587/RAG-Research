Bobby Moore: Robert Frederick Chelsea Moore OBE (12 April 1941 – 24 February 1993) was an English professional footballer.  He captained West Ham United for more than ten years and was captain of the England team that won the 1966 World Cup.  He is widely regarded as one of the greatest defenders of all time, and was cited by Pelé as the greatest defender that he had ever played against.  Moore is a member of the World Team of the 20th Century.
