Dana (surname): Dana ( or ) as a surname may have several origins.  In England, it came from "dann", the valley of a meadow, and it may mean the dweller of that valley.  In Continental Europe, it probably came from "Dane" (or Danish, from Denmark).  This surname is related to Danese in Italy and it can be found mostly in the Piedmont region.  It may also be a modification of Huguenot French origin, probably a variant of "d'Aunay", of geographical origin.  It may be also a Gaelic patronymic, since it is a common forename in Ireland.  Dana is a relatively common surname in the US, ranking 7161 out of 88,799 in the 1990 U.S. Census.  Dana is also the Persian word for wisdom.
