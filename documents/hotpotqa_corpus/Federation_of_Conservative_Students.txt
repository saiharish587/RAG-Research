Federation of Conservative Students: The Federation of Conservative Students (FCS) was the student organisation of the British Conservative Party from the late 1940s to 1986.  It was created to act as a bridge between the student movement and the Conservative Party.  In its final years it became known colloquially as "Maggie's Militant Tendency", in reference to then Prime Minister Margaret Thatcher and to Militant, an entryist group active in the Labour Party at the time.  The FCS was then broken up by the Chairman of the Conservative Party, Norman Tebbit, after one of its members had accused previous former Prime Minister Harold Macmillan of war crimes in extraditing Cossacks to the Soviet Union.  The FCS was replaced by the Conservative Collegiate Forum.
