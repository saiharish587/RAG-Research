Philip Holiday: Phillip James Holiday (born 23 May 1970 in Benoni, South Africa) is a professional boxer in the junior middleweight (160 lb) division.  Holiday turned pro in 1991 and captured the Vacant IBF Lightweight Title in 1995 with a win over Miguel Julio.  He defended the title six times before losing the belt to Shane Mosley by decision in 1997, in a fight dominated by Mosley.
