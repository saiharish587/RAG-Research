Stieff Silver Company Factory: Stieff Silver Company Factory is a historic silver factory located at Baltimore, Maryland, United States.  It is a two-story nine-bay rectangular brick factory building, designed by Theodore Wells Pietsch and built in two stages in 1925 and 1929.  The exterior features a lighted sign flanking a central clock that rises above a parapeted roof.  The Stieff Silver Company was the last of the Baltimore silverware manufacturers, operating between 1892 and 1999.
