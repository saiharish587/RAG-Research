Terry Carisse: Terrance Victor Carisse (July 11, 1942–May 22, 2005) known as Terry Carisse, was one of Canadian Country Music's most awarded, decorated and popular singer-songwriters.  His awards include the Canadian Country Music Association's Male Vocalist of the Year Award which he has won six times, and still holds this record.  He was nominated four times for a Juno Award.  In 1989 he was inducted into the Ottawa Valley Country Music Hall of Fame.  In 2006, Terry Carisse was inducted into the Canadian Country Music Hall of Fame.
