Heineken Premium Light: Heineken Light (labelled as "Heineken 3" in Australia ) is a light beer brewed by Heineken for the United States market.  It was introduced in 2005.  Heineken light reportedly has: 99 calories per 12 oz.  bottle and 6.8 grams of carbohydrates.  The beer has fewer calories, less carbohydrate, and less alcohol than lager beers such as the Heineken Pilsener.  The beer has 3.3% alcohol by volume.
