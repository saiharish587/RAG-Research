Vois comme c'est beau: "Vois comme c'est beau" (meaning "Look How Beautiful it Is") is a duet between Claudette Dion and her sister, Celine Dion, released as a single from Claudette Dion's album "Hymnes à l'amour: Volume 2".  It was issued in 1985 in Quebec, Canada.  "Vois comme c'est beau" has never appeared on any of Celine Dion's albums.
