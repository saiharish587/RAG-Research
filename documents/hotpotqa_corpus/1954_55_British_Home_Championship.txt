1954–55 British Home Championship: The 1954-55 British Home Championship was a football tournament played between the British Home Nations during the 1954–55 football season.  It was won by a strong England side which included players such as Johnny Haynes and Nat Lofthouse as well as future manager Don Revie.  England and Scotland, had competed at the 1954 FIFA World Cup in the summer before the tournament began and both teams had struggled, eventually being knocked out by Uruguay, Scotland by a 7–0 margin.
