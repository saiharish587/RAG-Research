AT&amp;T Park: AT&T Park is a baseball park located in the South Beach neighborhood of San Francisco, California.  Since 2000, it has served as the home of the San Francisco Giants, the city's Major League Baseball (MLB) franchise.  Originally named Pacific Bell Park, then SBC Park in 2003 after SBC Communications acquired Pacific Bell, the stadium was ultimately christened AT&T Park in 2006, following SBC's buyout of AT&T.  The park stands along the San Francisco Bay, a segment of which is named McCovey Cove in honor of former Giants player Willie McCovey.
