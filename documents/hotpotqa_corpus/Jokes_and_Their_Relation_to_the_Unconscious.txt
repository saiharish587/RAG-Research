Jokes and Their Relation to the Unconscious: Jokes and Their Relation to the Unconscious (German: "Der Witz und seine Beziehung zum Unbewußten" ) is a book on the psychoanalysis of jokes and humour by Sigmund Freud (1856-1939), first published in 1905 (translated into English in 1960).  In this work, Freud described the psychological processes and techniques of jokes, which he likened as similar to the processes and techniques of dreamwork and the Unconscious.
