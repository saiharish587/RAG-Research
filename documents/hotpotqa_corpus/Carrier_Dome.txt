Carrier Dome: Carrier Dome is a 49,250-seat domed sports stadium located on the campus of Syracuse University in the University Hill neighborhood of Syracuse, New York.  It is home to the Syracuse Orange football, basketball, and lacrosse teams.  The Syracuse Orange men's basketball team drew the highest average home attendance in college basketball in 2015-16, with an average of 26,253.  In 2006–07, the women's basketball team began playing home games in the Dome.  New York high school football state championships as well as the annual New York State Field Band Conference championships are held in the stadium, as are occasional concerts.
