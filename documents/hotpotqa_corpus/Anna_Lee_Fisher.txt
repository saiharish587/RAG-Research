Anna Lee Fisher: Anna Lee Fisher (née Tingle) (born August 24, 1949) is an American chemist, emergency physician, and a former NASA astronaut.  Formerly married to fellow astronaut Bill Fisher, and the mother of two children, in 1984 she became the first mother in space.  Fisher was formerly the oldest active American astronaut.  During her career at NASA, she has been involved with three major programs: the Space Shuttle, the International Space Station and the Orion project.
