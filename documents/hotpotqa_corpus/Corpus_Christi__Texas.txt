Corpus Christi, Texas: The city's population was estimated to be 320,434 in 2014, making it the eighth-most populous city in Texas.  The Corpus Christi metropolitan area had an estimated population of 442,600.  It is also the hub of the six-county Corpus Christi-Kingsville-Alice Combined Statistical Area, with a 2013 estimated population of 516,793.  The Port of Corpus Christi is the fifth-largest in the United States.  The region is served by the Corpus Christi International Airport.
