Nagpur South (Vidhan Sabha constituency): Nagpur South Vidhan Sabha constituency (Marathi: नागपूर दक्षिण विधानसभा मतदारसंघ ) is one of the 288 Vidhan Sabha (legislative assembly) constituencies of Maharashtra state, western India.  The Constituency Number is 53.  This constituency is located in the Nagpur district.  The delimitation of the constituency happened in 2008.  It comprises parts of Nagpur Taluka, and Ward No. 9 to 11, 37 to 42, 73 to 78, 99 to 102 and 120 of Nagpur Municipal Corporation.
