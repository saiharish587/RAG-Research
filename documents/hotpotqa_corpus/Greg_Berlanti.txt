Greg Berlanti: Greg Berlanti (born May 24, 1972) is an American film and television writer and producer.  He is known for his work on the television series "Dawson's Creek", "Everwood", "Political Animals"; and for his contributions to DC Comics on film and television, including The CW's "Arrowverse".  In 2017 Berlanti set the record in having 10 different scripted television series planned to air in the 2017–18 television season on various networks and digital platforms.
