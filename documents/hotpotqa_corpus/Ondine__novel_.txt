Ondine (novel): Ondine is a romance novel first published in 1988 under Heather Graham Pozzessere’s pen name, Shannon Drake.  It is the story of a lady of 17th century England who finds herself cast from nobility when her father is accused of treason and murdered.  As a noose rests at her neck, her one regret is that she did not have a chance to find his killer.  She is once again given that chance when Lord Chatham, hoping to use the lady to solve the murder of his wife, takes her hand in marriage to save her from the hangman’s rope.  The novel consists of multiple plot lines making it more than just an average romance novel.  It may also be classified under the genres of historical romance, mystery, and gothic.
