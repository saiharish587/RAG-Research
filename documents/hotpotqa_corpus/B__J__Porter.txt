B. J. Porter: B. J. Porter (born May 12, 1970, in Dallas, Texas) is an actor, writer and comedian.  Porter began his career writing and performing on the HBO sketch comedy program, "Mr. Show."  Porter, along with the rest of the "Mr. Show" writing staff, was nominated for an Emmy Award in 1999.  Porter won an Emmy in 2014 and 2015 for the Funny or Die series "Between Two Ferns with Zach Galifianakis", which he co-created.  "Between Two Ferns" was also nominated for an Emmy in 2013.
