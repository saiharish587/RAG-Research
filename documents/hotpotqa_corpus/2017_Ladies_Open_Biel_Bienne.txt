2017 Ladies Open Biel Bienne: The 2017 Ladies Open Biel Bienne was a women's tennis tournament played on indoor hard courts.  It was the first edition of the Ladies Open Biel Bienne and part of the International category of the 2017 WTA Tour.  It took place at the Swiss Tennis Center in Roger Federer Allee, Biel, Switzerland, from 10 April through 16 April 2017.
