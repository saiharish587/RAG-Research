Typhoon Dinah (1987): Typhoon Dinah, known as Typhoon Luding in the Philippines, was the fourth typhoon to form during August 1987.  An area of low pressure developed near Guam on August 19, and two days later, the low reached tropical storm intensity as it moved generally west.  Intensification was initially gradual, with Dinah becoming a typhoon early on August 24 before it subsequently intensified at a faster pace.  Dinah reached its highest strength on August 26 before turning northward on August 28 and into a less favorable conditions aloft, which prompted weakening.  Dinah entered the Sea of Japan after passing near Okinawa on August 29, where Dinah leveled off in intensity.  The system then began to recurve towards southwestern Japan, and after tracking through the area, Dinah transitioned into an extratropical cyclone on August 31, although the remnants could be traced for four more days as it approached the International Date Line.
