Law Uk Folk Museum: Law Uk (; meaning "Law's House") is a former Hakka village house located in Chai Wan, Hong Kong.  Named after the surname of the family who had previously lived in the house, it was built in the mid-18th century during the Qing Dynasty, about ninety years before the British took possession of Hong Kong Island.  It was rediscovered in the 1970s and is a declared monument of Hong Kong.  After being restored, the house was turned into the Law Uk Folk Museum, which serves as a branch of the Hong Kong Museum of History.  It is the only example of Hakka architecture left in the area.
