Barry Keoghan: Barry Keoghan (born 18 October 1992) is an Irish actor.  He has appeared in the films "Dunkirk" along with Mark Rylance, Tom Hardy, Cillian Murphy, Kenneth Branagh, Tom Glynn-Carney; "The Killing of a Sacred Deer" with Nicole Kidman, Colin Farrell and Alicia Silverstone; and "Trespass Against Us" with Michael Fassbender and Brendan Gleeson.  He has also played the "heartless cat killer" Wayne in the RTÉ drama "Love/Hate".
