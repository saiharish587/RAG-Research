Jarvis Williams (American football): Jarvis Eric Williams, Sr. (May 16, 1965 – May 25, 2010) was an American college and professional football player who was a defensive back in the National Football League (NFL) for seven seasons during the 1980s and 1990s.  Williams played college football for the University of Florida, and was recognized as an All-American.  Thereafter, he played professionally for the Miami Dolphins and the New York Giants of the NFL.  Williams died unexpectedly at the age of 45.
