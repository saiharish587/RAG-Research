On Parole: On Parole is the first recording made by the band Motörhead.  It was left unreleased at the time of its completion in 1976, and it was not released until over three years later, on 8 December 1979, after the commercial success of "Overkill" and "Bomber" that same year.  It was released without the band's permission, and they consequently distanced themselves from it upon its release.
