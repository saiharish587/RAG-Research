List of NASCAR race wins by Kevin Harvick: Kevin Harvick is an American racing driver who won a Monster Energy NASCAR Cup Series championship.  Over the course of his racing career, Harvick has won multiple races, 36 of which were in Monster Energy NASCAR Cup Series as well as 46 in Xfinity Series and 14 in Camping World Truck Series for a total of 96 wins across NASCAR's top 3 Series.
