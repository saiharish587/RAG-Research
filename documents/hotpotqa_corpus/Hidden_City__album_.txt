Hidden City (album): Hidden City is the tenth studio album by the British rock band The Cult, released on 5 February 2016 through Cooking Vinyl and Dine Alone Records.  It is the final part of a trilogy that began with "Born into This" (2007), and The Cult's first album since their 1994 self-titled album not to feature bassist Chris Wyse; the role was filled by producer Bob Rock and Jane's Addiction bassist Chris Chaney.  It also marks the fifth time Rock had produced a Cult album.
