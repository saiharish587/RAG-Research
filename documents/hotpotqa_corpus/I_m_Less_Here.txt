I'm Less Here: "I'm Less Here" is a stand-alone single released by alternative rock band Mazzy Star for Record Store Day 2014, and was the band's first release of new material since their previous album, "Seasons of Your Day".  The track had previously been performed live under the name "It Speaks of Distance," with its first known performance dating back to March 1994.  The A-side was backed by another previously unreleased song, titled "Things".  The day before the single's release, the band posted a music video for the track on their official Vevo account.
