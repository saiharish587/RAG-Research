L'Opoponax: L’Opoponax is a 1964 novel by French writer Monique Wittig.  It was translated into English in 1966 by Helen Weaver, and published in the US by Simon & Schuster.  The title comes from the plant Opopanax, aka sweet myrrh, which appears as the cover illustration on the 1976 reprint by Daughters, Inc..
