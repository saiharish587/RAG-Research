Fra Lippo Lippi (poem): Fra Lippo Lippi is an 1855 dramatic monologue written by the Victorian poet Robert Browning which first appeared in his collection "Men and Women".  Throughout this poem, Browning depicts a 15th-century real-life painter, Filippo Lippi.  The poem asks the question whether art should be true to life or an idealized image of life.  The poem is written in blank verse, non-rhyming iambic pentameter.
