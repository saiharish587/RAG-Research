Freckleton: Freckleton is a village and civil parish on the Fylde coast in Lancashire, England, to the south of Kirkham and east of the seaside resort of Lytham St. Annes.  In 2001 it had a population of 6,045, reducing to 6,019 at the 2011 Census.  The village is near Warton, with its links to BAE Systems.  Warton Aerodrome's 1.5 miles runway is partly within Freckleton's boundary.  Freckleton has a parish council, and is part of Fylde Borough, and Fylde constituency.
