2016 San Jose State Spartans football team: The 2016 San Jose State Spartans football team represented San Jose State University in the 2016 NCAA Division I FBS football season.  The Spartans were led by fourth-year head coach Ron Caragher and played their home games at Spartan Stadium.  They were members of the Mountain West Conference in the West Division.  They finished the season 4–8, 3–5 in Mountain West play to finish in a three-way tie for third place in the West Division.
