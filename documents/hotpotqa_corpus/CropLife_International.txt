CropLife International: CropLife International is an international trade association of agrobusiness companies founded in 2001.  It was previously known as "Global Crop Protection Federation" and started out as "International Group of National Associations of Manufacturers of Agrochemical Products" in 1967.  Its members include the world's largest agricultural biotechnology and agricultural pesticide businesses namely BASF, Bayer CropScience, Dow AgroSciences, DuPont, FMC Corp., Monsanto, Sumitomo and Syngenta.
