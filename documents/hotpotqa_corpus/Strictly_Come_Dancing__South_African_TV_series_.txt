Strictly Come Dancing (South African TV series): Strictly Come Dancing is a South African reality dance competition television series produced for SABC2 by Rapid Blue, based on the British show of the same name.  It is broadcast live from the Carlton Centre Ballroom, Johannesburg, and it is hosted by Siphiwe Nhlapo.  The show premiered in South Africa on SABC2 on Saturday 4 February 2006, at 20:00.
