Anton Media Group: Anton Media Group, formerly Anton Community Newspapers, and also known by its legal name Long Island Community Newspapers, Inc. is a print media company based in Mineola, NY on Long Island in Nassau County and produces 17 weekly newspapers in Nassau County as well as "Long Island Weekly" and special sections such as Healthy Living, Camp & School, and Dining Guides.  Anton Community Newspapers is one of the largest privately owned newspaper companies in New York State.
