VAQ-140: Electronic Attack Squadron 140 (VAQ-140) is a US Navy electronic attack squadron.  Known as the "Patriots", the squadron operates the EA-18G Growler.  The squadron is home ported at NAS Whidbey Island, Washington.  They are attached to Carrier Air Wing Seven, and deploy aboard USS "Harry S. Truman" .  The squadron's radio callsign is "Talon".
