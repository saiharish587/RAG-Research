Rasel: Rafael Abad Anselmo (born in Seville, Spain on December 23, 1981) better known by his stage name Rasel, is a Spanish singer, with various influences including rap, reggae, R&B and dance music.  His debut was with "Publicidad engañosa" in 2007.  His biggest commercial success is the 2012 hit "Me pones tierno" featuring Carlos Baute reaching #6 on the Spanish Singles Chart.  He is signed to Warner Music.
