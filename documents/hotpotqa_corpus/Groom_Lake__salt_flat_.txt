Groom Lake (salt flat): Groom Lake is a salt flat in Nevada used for runways of the Nellis Bombing Range Test Site airport (KXTA).  It is located immediately north of the Area 51 USAF installation.  The lake at 4409 ft elevation is approximately 3.7 mi from north to south and 3 mi from east to west at its widest point, and is approximately 11.3 miles in circumference.  Located within the namesake Groom Lake Valley portion of the Tonopah Basin, the lake is 25 mi south of Rachel, Nevada.
