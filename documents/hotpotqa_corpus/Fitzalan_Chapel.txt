Fitzalan Chapel: The Fitzalan Chapel is located within the eastern end of the church building constructed on the western grounds of Arundel Castle.  This church building is one of the very few church buildings that is currently divided into two worship areas, one Catholic and one Anglican, with the western side of the church building occupied by the Anglican church of St Nicholas.  The Catholic chapel, now the private mausoleum of the Dukes of Norfolk, is a Grade I listed building.
