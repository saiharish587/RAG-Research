InStyle UK: InStyle, published by IPC Media, was a monthly glossy magazine focusing on celebrities and their style.  The British edition launched in 2001, following the success of the magazine in the US.  The current editor is Trish Halpin, who commenced the position in September 2006.  The circulation of "In Style" was currently more than 145,000 in 2013.  In October 2016 the print version was cancelled and it became a web-only publication.
