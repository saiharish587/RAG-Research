Eakins Press: The Eakins Press Foundation is an American publishing house based in New York established by Leslie George Katz in 1966 and named after the painter Thomas Eakins.  Since its founding in 1966, the Eakins Press Foundation has published some of the classic volumes on American art and photography, including Lee Friedlander’s "The American Monument", Walker Evans’s "Message from the Interior", and Lincoln Kirstein’s definitive monograph on Elie Nadelman.  The Eakins Press Foundation was recognized as a 501(c)(3) charitable foundation in 1974.
