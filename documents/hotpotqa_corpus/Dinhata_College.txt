Dinhata College: Dinhata College, (Bengali: দিনহাটা মহাবিদ্যালয়) established in 1956, is one of the oldest college in Dinhata.  It offers undergraduate courses in arts, commerce and sciences.  The campus is in the Cooch Behar district.  It is affiliated to Cooch Behar Panchanan Barma University.  Formerly affiliated to University of North Bengal.
