Far West (New South Wales): The Far West region of New South Wales, Australia refers generally to a fairly flat and low-lying area in the western part of the state, which is too dry to support wheat or other crops or intensive pastoral endeavours.  It is west of the North West Slopes, Central West and the Riverina.  It is an area with limited rainfall, and the only major rivers found in it are the Darling River and the Murray River (on its southern edge), which originate in the Great Dividing Range to the east.  The region corresponds to the combination of the Australian Bureau of Meteorology's forecast areas of "Upper Western" and "Lower Western".  It also corresponds to the Western Division established under the New South Wales "Western Lands Act 1901".
