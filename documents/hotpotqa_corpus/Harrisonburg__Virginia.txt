Harrisonburg, Virginia: Harrisonburg is an independent city in the Shenandoah Valley region of the U.S. state of Virginia.  As of the 2010 census, the population was 48,914, with a census-estimated 2016 population of 53,078.  Harrisonburg is the county seat of the surrounding Rockingham County, although the two are separate jurisdictions.  The Bureau of Economic Analysis combines the city of Harrisonburg with Rockingham County for statistical purposes into the Harrisonburg, Virginia Metropolitan Statistical Area, which has a 2011 estimated population of 126,562.
