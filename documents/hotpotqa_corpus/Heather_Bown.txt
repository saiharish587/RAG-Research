Heather Bown: Heather Erin Bown (born November 29, 1978) is a retired volleyball player from the United States, who played as a middle-blocker.  She represented her native country at the 2004 Summer Olympics in Athens, Greece.  There she finished in fifth place with the USA national team.  She also competed at the 2000 Summer Olympics.  Bown made her third straight Olympic appearance in Beijing, helping Team USA to a silver medal.
