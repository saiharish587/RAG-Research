History of Statoil (1972–2007): Statoil ASA is a Norwegian petroleum company established in 1972.  It merged with the oil and gas division of Norsk Hydro in 2007 and was known as StatoilHydro until 2009, when the name was changed back to Statoil ASA.  The brand Statoil was retained as a chain of fuel stations owned by StatoilHydro.  Statoil is the largest petroleum company in the Nordic countries and Norway's largest company, employing over 25,000 people.  While Statoil is listed on both the Oslo Stock Exchange and the New York Stock Exchange, the Norwegian state still holds majority ownership, with 64%.  The company's headquarters are located in Norway's oil capital Stavanger.  The name Statoil is a truncated form of "the State's oil (company)".
