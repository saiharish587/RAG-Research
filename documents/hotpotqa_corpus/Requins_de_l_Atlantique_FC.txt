Requins de l'Atlantique FC: Requins de l'Atlantique Football Club is a football club of Benin, playing in the town of Cotonou.  They play in the Beninese first division, the Benin Premier League. Some notable players would include: Jude Axelsson, Francis Abalo, Mohamed kabiru, Ebun akinbote, Dosugbete expetit, Sacramento, Gariga Abou Maïga, Stéphane Sessègnon
