1992 DFB-Pokal Final: The 1991–92 DFB-Pokal competition came to a close on 23 May 1992 when 1.  Bundesliga club Borussia Mönchengladbach played 2.  Bundesliga team Hannover 96 at the Olympiastadion in Berlin.  Hannover 96 made history as the first, and to date only, club from outside the top division to win the cup when they won 4–3 on penalties.  The game had finished goalless after 120 minutes.
