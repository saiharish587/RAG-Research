Joan Sanderson: Joan Sanderson (24 November 1912 – 24 May 1992) was an English television and stage actress.  During a long career, the tall and commanding actress played dowagers, spinsters and matrons, as well as intense Shakespearean roles.  Her television work included the sitcoms, "Please Sir! " (1968–72) and "Me and My Girl" (1984–88).
