ITV Nightly News: The ITV Nightly News was the late evening news programme on British television network ITV, produced by ITN and broadcast Monday to Friday at 11pm.  The 20-minute bulletin, originally presented by newscaster Dermot Murnaghan, was introduced as part of a major overhaul of news on ITV that saw its 5:40pm "" and prestigious "News at Ten" programmes axed.
