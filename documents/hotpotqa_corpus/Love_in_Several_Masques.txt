Love in Several Masques: Love in Several Masques is a play by Henry Fielding that was first performed on 16 February 1728 at the Theatre Royal, Drury Lane.  The moderately received play comically depicts three lovers trying to pursue their individual beloveds.  The beloveds require their lovers to meet their various demands, which serves as a means for Fielding to introduce his personal feelings on morality and virtue.  In addition, Fielding introduces criticism of women and society in general.
