Little Bay, Newfoundland and Labrador: Little Bay is a town located in the Canadian province of Newfoundland and Labrador.  Its current population is about 166 people.  It is located in Green Bay, which is part of Notre Dame Bay, which is located in the central part of Newfoundland and Labrador.  Some towns near Little Bay include Beachside, St Patrick's, Little Bay Islands, and the main center for Green Bay, Springdale.
