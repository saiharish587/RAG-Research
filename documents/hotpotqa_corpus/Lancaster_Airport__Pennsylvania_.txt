Lancaster Airport (Pennsylvania): Lancaster Airport (IATA: LNS, FAA LID: KLNS, TC LID: LNS) is a public use airport four nautical miles (5 mi, 7 km) north of the central business district of Lancaster, in Lancaster County, Pennsylvania, United States.  It is owned by the Lancaster Airport Authority.  It is served by one commercial airline (subsidized by the Essential Air Service program) and one charter airline.
