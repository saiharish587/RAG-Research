Doug Parker (voice actor): Doug Parker is a Canadian voice actor and animation director.  He has been active in the industry since 1988.  He has cast, and directed many animated shows and films.  He also has voiced characters in several cartoons and anime; he is probably best known for his work in "ToddWorld", which was nominated as an outstanding children's animated program.  His character Terrorsaur in "" is also well-known, as well as Starscream.  Doug also provided the voice of Prince Adam in "The New Adventures of He-Man" (1990).
