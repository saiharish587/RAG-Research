Vaněk plays: The Vaněk plays are a set of plays to which the character Ferdinand Vaněk is central.  Vaněk first appeared in the play "Audience" by Václav Havel.  He subsequently appeared in three other plays by Havel ("Protest", "Unveiling", and "Dozens of Cousins"), as well as plays by his friends and colleagues, including Pavel Landovský and Tom Stoppard.
