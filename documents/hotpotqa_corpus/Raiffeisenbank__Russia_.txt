Raiffeisenbank (Russia): ZAO Raiffeisenbank (Russian: ЗАО «Райффайзенбанк» ) is a bank in Russia.  It is a subsidiary of Raiffeisen Bank International, which in turn is a fully controlled subsidiary of Raiffeisen Zentralbank.  It was founded in 1996 as "Raiffeisenbank Austria" and has expanded considerably following the 2006 takeover of Russia's Impexbank.
