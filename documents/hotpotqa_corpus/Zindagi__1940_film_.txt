Zindagi (1940 film): Zindagi (Life) is a 1940 Indian Bollywood film directed by P.C. Barua.  It was the highest grossing Indian film of 1940.  The music, by Pankaj Mullick, features songs such as "So Ja Rajkumari" and "Jeevan Asha Hai".  It starred K. L. Saigal, Jamuna, Pahari Sanyal, Shyam Laha, Sitara Devi, and Nemo.  The film has been described as one of Barua's "most beautiful films, and his last for New Theatres".
