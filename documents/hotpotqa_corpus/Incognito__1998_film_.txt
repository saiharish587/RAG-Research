Incognito (1998 film): Incognito is a 1997 American crime thriller film directed by John Badham and starring Jason Patric and Irene Jacob.  Written by Jordan Katz, the film is about a talented art forger who paints a fake Rembrandt despite pressure from his dying father who urges him to use his talent on his own original paintings.  The film is notable for a sequence that reveals the specific details involved in forgery, including canvas aging, precise paints, and other deceptions.
