Coquese Washington: Coquese Makebra Washington (born January 17, 1971) is a former collegiate and professional basketball player who currently serves as the head coach of Penn State's women's basketball team, the Lady Lions.  Washington holds a law degree and was the first president of the WNBA Players Association, holding that position from 1999 to 2001.  She played her high school basketball at Flint Central High School and her collegiate basketball at the University of Notre Dame.
