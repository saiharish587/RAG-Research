1988 Football League Cup Final: The 1988 Football League Cup Final (also known as the Littlewoods Challenge Cup Final for sponsorship reasons) was an association football match between Luton Town and Arsenal on 24 April 1988 at Wembley Stadium, London.  It was the final match of the 1987–88 staging of the Football League Cup.  Luton were making their first League Cup Final appearance, while the competition holders Arsenal were appearing in their fourth final.
