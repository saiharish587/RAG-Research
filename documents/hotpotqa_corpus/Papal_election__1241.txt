Papal election, 1241: The papal election, following the death of Pope Gregory IX, from September 21 to October 25, 1241 elected Cardinal Goffredo da Castiglione as Pope Celestine IV.  The election took place during the first of many protracted "sede vacantes" of the Middle Ages, and like many of them was characterized by disputes between popes and the Holy Roman Emperor.  Specifically, the election took place during the war between Frederick II, Holy Roman Emperor against the Lombard League and the deceased pontiff, Pope Gregory IX, with Italy divided between pro-Papal and pro-Imperial factions known as the Guelphs and Ghibellines.
