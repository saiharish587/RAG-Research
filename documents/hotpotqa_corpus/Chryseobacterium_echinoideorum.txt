Chryseobacterium echinoideorum: Chryseobacterium echinoideorum is a Gram-negative, rod-shaped, aerobic and non-motile bacteria from the genus of Chryseobacterium which has been isolated from a sea urchin (Tripneustes gratilla) on the Penghu Island on Taiwan.  "Chryseobacterium echinoideorum" produces flexirubin.
