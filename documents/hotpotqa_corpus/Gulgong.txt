Gulgong: Gulgong is a 19th-century gold rush town in the Central Tablelands and the wider Central West regions of the Australian state of New South Wales.  The town is situated within the Mid-Western Regional Council local government area.  It is located about 300 km north west of Sydney, and about 30 km north of Mudgee along the Castlereagh Highway.  At the 2016 Census, Gulgong had a population of 2,521.
