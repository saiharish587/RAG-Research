Oath Keepers: Oath Keepers is a far-right, anti-government American organization associated with the patriot and militia movements.  The group describes itself as a non-partisan association of current and former military, police, and first responders, who pledge to fulfill the oath that all military and police take in order to "defend the Constitution against all enemies, foreign and domestic."  It encourages members – some of whom are current and former U.S. military and law enforcement officers – not to obey orders which they believe would violate the United States Constitution.  The organization claims a membership of 35,000.
