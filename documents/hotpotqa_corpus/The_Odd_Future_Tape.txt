The Odd Future Tape: The Odd Future Tape is the debut mixtape by Odd Future.  In 2007, Odd Future consisted of Tyler, The Creator, Hodgy Beats, Left Brain, Jasper Dolphin, The Super 3 (Matt Martians and Hal Williams) and Casey Veggies, the latter of which left after the mixtape's release.  The songs were recorded on a computer camera microphone.  The mixtape was made available for free download in 2008 – LA Weekly dates the release as November 2008.
