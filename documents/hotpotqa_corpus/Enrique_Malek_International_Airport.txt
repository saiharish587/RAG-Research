Enrique Malek International Airport: Enrique Malek International Airport (IATA: DAV, ICAO: MPDA) ("Spanish: Aeropuerto Internacional Enrique Malek") is an international airport located in the city of David, Chiriquí in the Republic of Panama.  The former terminal was demolished for a new one to be built.  When construction had finished, daily flights from Air Panama started arriving.  Now the international flight terminal receives numerous daily flights from Copa Airlines and Air Panama.  Also car rentals commenced operations in the new terminal.
