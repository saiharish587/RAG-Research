Vágar Airport: Vágar Airport (Faroese: "Vága Floghavn" ) (IATA: FAE, ICAO: EKVG) is the only airport in the Faroe Islands, and is located 1 NM east of Sørvágur.  Due to the Faroe Islands' status as a self-governing territory, the airport is not subject to the rules of the European Union.  It is the main operating base for Faroese national airline Atlantic Airways and, for a brief period during 2006, was also the base for the low cost airline FaroeJet.
