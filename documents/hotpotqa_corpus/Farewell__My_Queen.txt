Farewell, My Queen: Farewell, My Queen (French: Les Adieux à la reine ) is a 2012 French drama film directed by Benoît Jacquot and based on the novel of the same name by Chantal Thomas, who won the "Prix Femina" in 2002.  It gives a fictional account of the last days of Marie Antoinette in power seen through the eyes of Sidonie Laborde, a young servant who reads aloud to the queen.  The film stars Diane Kruger as the Queen, Léa Seydoux, and Virginie Ledoyen.
