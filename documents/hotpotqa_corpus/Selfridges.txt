Selfridges: Selfridges, also known as Selfridges & Co., is a chain of high end department stores in the United Kingdom, operated by Selfridges Retail Limited.  It was founded by Harry Gordon Selfridge.  The flagship store on London's Oxford Street is the second largest shop in the UK (after Harrods) and opened 15 March 1909.  Other Selfridges stores opened in the Trafford Centre (1998) and Exchange Square (2002) in Manchester, and in the Bullring in Birmingham (2003).
