Asno de las Encartaciones: The Asno de las Encartaciones , Basque: "" , is a breed of small domestic donkey from the western part of the autonomous community of the Basque Country, in north-east Spain.  It is named for the comarca of Las Encartaciones (Enkarterri), in the province of Biscay (Bizkaia).  It is the only small donkey breed of Spain, and resembles the Gascon donkey, now a sub-type of the Pyrenean donkey.  The Asno de las Encartaciones is critically endangered, and is protected by conservation measures.
