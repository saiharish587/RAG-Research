Toyota Mark II Blit: The Toyota Mark II Blit was the replacement of the Toyota Mark II Qualis, and shared a platform with the Mark II rear wheel drive sedan.  The Mark II Qualis was a rebadged (SXV20) Camry Gracia wagon, with front wheel drive.  The Blit was introduced in January 2002.  Production ended in June 2007 due to consolidation efforts.  Toyota's official Blit successor was a front wheel drive minivan, the Mark X ZiO, from September, 2007.  The Blit was a return to the Mark II platform with rear wheel drive with optional AWD and not a wagon version of the front wheel drive Camry.  The Blit used straight-6 engines with an optional turbo that was discontinued May 2006.  The engines used were the 2.0 L 1G-FE, 2.5 L 1JZ-FSE, 2.5 L 1JZ-GE, and 2.5 L turbocharged 1JZ-GTE.
