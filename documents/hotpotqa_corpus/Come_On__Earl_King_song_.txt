Come On (Earl King song): "Come On" (often called "Let the Good Times Roll") is a song written by New Orleans rhythm and blues artist Earl King.  He first recorded the song as "Darling Honey Angel Child" in 1960 for the Ace Records subsidiary Rex.  Later that year, he recorded it as a two-part song for Imperial Records using some new lyrics.  Retitled "Come On", it was released in 1960 with "Come On - Part I” as the A-side backed with “Come On - Part II” (Imperial 5713).
