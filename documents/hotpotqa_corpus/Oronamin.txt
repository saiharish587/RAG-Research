Oronamin: Oronamin C Drink (オロナミンCドリンク , Oronamin Shī Dorinku ) , produced by Otsuka Chemical Holdings Co., Ltd., (distributed and sold by Otsuka Pharmaceutical Co.) is a carbonated beverage available in Japan.  It is commonly called Oronamin C or Oronamin.  Its name is similar to the Takeda Pharmaceutical Co. product "Arinamin" and its name comes from Otsuka's own Oronine H Ointment (オロナインH軟膏 ) and its main ingredient, vitamin C.  Oronamin C was named after the Oronine H Ointment in hopes that it would prove to be equally successful.
