Para Hills Knights SC: Para Hills Knights SC are a soccer club based in Para Hills, South Australia.  The club competes in the FFSA National Premier League.  The Para Hills Knights home ground is at The Paddocks in Para Hills, north of Adelaide.  They currently have some of the strongest sides in the Junior Premier League and senior league.  2012 was a very successful year for the club gaining promotion to the top league in South Australia by winning the premier league competition.  The club also gained a place in the cup after beating some strong super league sides.  The under 19's team also finished top of their league and the reserves had a successful season, finishing the 2006 Premier League season on top earning promotion to the 2007 South Australian Super League.  The Knights recently recently regained Premier League status following promotion from the State League in 2016.
