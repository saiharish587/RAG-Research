Scryptic Studios: Scryptic Studios (or simply, "Scryptic") is a website created by a group of comic book writers as a massive resource for comic book writers to network, research stories, read news and columns, and find script samples.  The etymology of the title, Scryptic (pronounced skrip-tik), is the combination of the words "SCRIPT" (for writing) and "CRYPTIC" (for secret); so the title literally translates to: "SECRET WRITING".
