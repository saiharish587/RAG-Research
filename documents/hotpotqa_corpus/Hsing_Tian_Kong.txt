Hsing Tian Kong: Hsing Tian Kong (; also Xingtian Temple or Xingtian Gong) is a popular temple in Zhongshan District, Taipei, Taiwan.  This temple is devoted to Guan Yu, the patron god of businessmen, and is relatively recent in origin.  This temple is situated on a street corner near the center of the city.  Sculptures of dragons feature prominently in this temple's design.  It covers over 7,000 square meters.
