Take Me Home (TV series): Take Me Home was a British television drama series that originally aired from 2–16 May 1989.  The show starred Keith Barron, Maggie O'Neill, Reece Dinsdale, and Annette Crosbie.  It was created and written by Tony Marchant and was shown in three episodes on BBC One.  The lead character, Kathy, was one of the first television roles for actress Maggie O'Neill.  Reece Dinsdale was also known for his role opposite John Thaw in the comedy "Home to Roost".
