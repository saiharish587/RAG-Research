Midwives (film): Midwives is a 2001 American television film, starring Sissy Spacek, Peter Coyote, Terry Kinney, Alison Pill and Piper Laurie.  It was directed by Glenn Jordan.  The film is based in the 1997 novel Midwives written by Chris Bohjalian.  It was Lifetime's 100th Original Movie and had the highest rating in the network's history.
