Wonderful (Gary Go song): "Wonderful" is a song by British singer Gary Go, released on 16 February 2009 as the first and debut single from his debut album "Gary Go".  It reached #25 on the UK singles chart and was a Top 40 on the Hot AC chart in the US.  The song was selected as Pop Song Of The Year by iTunes US in their Rewind recap of 2009 releases.  A version of the song became the title song for the German film "Men in the City" (German: 'Männerherzen' ) for which a new music video was filmed on a Berlin rooftop including scenes from the film.  It is also used in the trailer for the Disneynature film "Oceans", in the TVI Portuguese novella Mar de Paixão, at the conclusion of an "American Idol" episode, and in the Eurovision Song Contest 2011 during the interval, in a mini movie involving clips from the shorts that were broadcast before every song from the contest.  "Wonderful" was also used in Sky Sports' closing montage of the 2011–12 Premier League season.
