Eight Little Piggies: Eight Little Piggies (1993) is the sixth volume of collected essays by the Harvard paleontologist Stephen Jay Gould.  The essays were selected from his monthly column "The View of Life" in "Natural History" magazine, to which Gould contributed for 27 years.  The book deals, in typically discursive fashion, with themes familiar to Gould's writing: evolution and its teaching, science biography, probabilities and common sense.
