1965 Connecticut Huskies baseball team: The 1965 Connecticut Huskies baseball team represented the University of Connecticut in the 1965 NCAA University Division baseball season.  The Huskies were led by Larry Panciera in his 4th year as head coach, and played as part of the Yankee Conference.
