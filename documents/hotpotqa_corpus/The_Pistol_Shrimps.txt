The Pistol Shrimps: The Pistol Shrimps is a 2016 Canadian-American documentary film about the all-female recreational basketball team of the same name, written and directed by Brent Hodge.  The film stars Aubrey Plaza, Molly Hawkey, Angela Trimbur, Melissa Stetten, Maria Blasucci, and Jesse Thomas.  The film had its world premiere at the Tribeca Film Festival on April 14, 2016.  The film was released on June 16, 2016 by Seeso.
