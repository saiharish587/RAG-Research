OLG Casino Sault Ste. Marie: Gateway Casinos Sault Ste. Marie (formerly OLG Casino Sault Ste. Marie) is a casino in Sault Ste. Marie, Ontario.  Owned an Operated by Burnaby based Gateway Casinos & Entertainment Ltd., it was Northern Ontario's first full-time casino when it opened in 1999.  The casino is located near the International Bridge which links the city to Sault Ste. Marie, Michigan.
