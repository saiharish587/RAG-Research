Ford Romeo Engine Plant: The Ford Romeo Engine Plant is a Ford Motor Company automobile plant is located on 701 East Saint Clair Street in Romeo, Michigan.  The Romeo Engine Plant was formerly a tractor facility acquired in 1973.  From 1973 to 1988 implements for tractors were made there such as the 4.6 liter V-8 engine 2-valve for a variety of the ford tractors.  Shortly after 1990, the first 4.6 2-valve per cylinder V-8 engine was produced for the Lincoln town car.
