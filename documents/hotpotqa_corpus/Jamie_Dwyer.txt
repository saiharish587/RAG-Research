Jamie Dwyer: Jamie Dwyer (born 12 March 1979) is an Australian field hockey player.  He currently plays for YMCA Coastal City Hockey Club in the Melville Toyota League in Perth, Western Australia.  He plays for the Queensland Blades in the Australian Hockey League.  He debuted for Australia as a junior player in 1995, and for the senior side in 2001.  He has played over 350 matches for Australia and scored over 220 goals.  He has represented Australia at the 2004 Summer Olympics where he won a gold medal and the 2008 Summer Olympics and 2012 Summer Olympics where Australia won a bronze medals.  He has also represented Australia at the 2006 Commonwealth Games where he won a gold medal and the 2010 Commonwealth Games where he also won gold.  He has won silver medals at the 2002 Men's Hockey World Cup and the 2006 Men's Hockey World Cup.  He won a gold medal at the 2010 Men's Hockey World Cup.
