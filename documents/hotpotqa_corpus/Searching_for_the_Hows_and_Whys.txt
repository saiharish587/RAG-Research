Searching for the Hows and Whys: Searching for the Hows and Whys is the second album by Get Cape.  Wear Cape.  Fly.  It was released on 10 March 2008.  The record was co-produced by Sam Duckworth and Nitin Sawhney.  "Waiting for the Monster to Drown" was released as a free download via Get Cape's official website and Myspace on 7 December 2007.
