Presidency of George H. W. Bush: The presidency of George H. W. Bush began at noon EST on January 20, 1989, when George H. W. Bush was inaugurated as 41st President of the United States, and ended on January 20, 1993.  Bush, a Republican, took office after a landslide victory over Democrat nominee Michael Dukakis in the 1988 presidential election.  He was the first sitting vice president to be elected president since Martin Van Buren in 1836.  Later, Bush, the 41st president, and his oldest son, George W. Bush, the country's 43rd (2001–2009), would become only the second father and son pair to become president.  (John Adams and John Quincy Adams were the first.)  Bush was denied a second term in the 1992 presidential election, which was won by Democrat Bill Clinton.
