Queen Anne's War: Queen Anne's War (1702–1713), as the North American theater of the War of the Spanish Succession was known in the British colonies, was the second in a series of French and Indian Wars fought between France and England, later Great Britain, in North America for control of the continent.  The War of the Spanish Succession was primarily fought in Europe.  In addition to the two main combatants, the war also involved numerous American Indian tribes allied with each nation, and Spain, which was allied with France.  It was also known as the Third Indian War or in French as the Second Intercolonial War.
