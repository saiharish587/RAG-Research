Pizza Pizza: Pizza Pizza Ltd. is a franchised Canadian pizza fast-food restaurant, with its headquarters in Etobicoke, Toronto.  Its restaurants are mainly in the province of Ontario.  Other locations operate in Quebec, Nova Scotia, and in western Canada.  Franchises in western Canada are mostly run through Alberta-based subsidiary Pizza 73, and in non-traditional locations such as university campuses and movie theatres throughout Canada.  It has over 500 locations, including over 150 non-traditional locations.
