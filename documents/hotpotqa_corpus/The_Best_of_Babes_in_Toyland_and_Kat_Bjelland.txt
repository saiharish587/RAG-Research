The Best of Babes in Toyland and Kat Bjelland: The Best of Babes In Toyland and Kat Bjelland is a CD/DVD compilation featuring songs by Babes in Toyland and Kat Bjelland's other projects, including her work in Crunt, Katastrophy Wife, and Pagan Babies.  It was released in 2004 by WEA International in the United Kingdom.
