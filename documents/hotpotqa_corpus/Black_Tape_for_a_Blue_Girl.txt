Black Tape for a Blue Girl: Black Tape for a Blue Girl (often stylized as black tape for a blue girl) is an American dark wave band formed in 1986 by Projekt Records' founder Sam Rosenthal.  Their music takes on elements of dark wave, ethereal, ambient, neoclassical, and dark cabaret music.  Director David Lynch is one of their more well-known fans.  Their 11th album, "These Fleeting Moments", was released on August 12, 2016.  on Metropolis Records.
