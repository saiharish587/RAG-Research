Dungeons &amp; Dragons (film): Dungeons & Dragons is a 2000 American-Czech fantasy film directed by Courtney Solomon, written by Carroll Cartwright and Topper Lilien, and based on the "Dungeons & Dragons" role-playing game.  Among the more notable features of the otherwise poorly received film are cameo appearances by Richard O'Brien (in a parody of his TV program "The Crystal Maze") and Tom Baker.  Parts of the film were made on location at Sedlec Ossuary.
