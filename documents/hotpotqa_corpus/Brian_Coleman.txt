Brian Coleman: Brian Coleman FRSA (born 25 June 1961) is an English Independent Conservative politician and a former councillor in the London Borough of Barnet.  He was a Conservative Party member of the London Assembly for Barnet and Camden between 2000 and 2012, and in Barnet was Mayor for 2009–2010.  He was also Chairman and Leader of the London Fire and Emergency Planning Authority (LFEPA) from May 2008 to 5 May 2012.
