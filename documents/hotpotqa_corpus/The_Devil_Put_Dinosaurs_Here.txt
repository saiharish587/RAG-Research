The Devil Put Dinosaurs Here: The Devil Put Dinosaurs Here is the fifth studio album by the American rock band Alice in Chains, released on May 28, 2013.  It is the band's second reunion album.  Following a worldwide tour in support of its previous album, "Black Gives Way to Blue" (2009), Alice in Chains began work on a new album.  The making of "The Devil Put Dinosaurs Here" lasted for more than a year and the release of the album was delayed numerous times.  The band entered the studio in July 2011 to start work on their fifth album.  During the writing and recording sessions, guitarist Jerry Cantrell underwent shoulder surgery, which resulted in the delay of the album.  The recording sessions of "The Devil Put Dinosaurs Here" were completed in December 2012.
