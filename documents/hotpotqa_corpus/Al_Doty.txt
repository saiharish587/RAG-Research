Al Doty: Al Doty (born October 19, 1945) is a Minnesota politician and a former member of the Minnesota House of Representatives who represented District 12B, which includes portions of Crow Wing and Morrison counties.  A Democrat, he was first elected in 2006 when he defeated three-term Republican incumbent Greg Blaine.  He was re-elected in 2008, but was unseated by Republican Mike LeMieur in the 2010 general election.
