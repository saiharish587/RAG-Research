Levriero Italiano: The Levriero Italiano or Levriero Meridionale is a very ancient breed of dog which has survived through the centuries in southern Italy due to its excellent reputation as a hunter.  This is the dog that medieval feudal lords used for their hunts.  The breed declined heavily in numbers because firearms made other hunting styles more popular and hunting with sight hounds became rare.  The breed is outstanding as a sight-hound and possesses not only great speed but also great endurance.
