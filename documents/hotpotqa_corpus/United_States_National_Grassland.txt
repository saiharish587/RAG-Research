United States National Grassland: National Grassland is a classification of protected and managed federal lands in the United States authorized by Title III of the Bankhead–Jones Farm Tenant Act of 1937.  For administrative purposes, they are essentially identical to United States National Forests, except that grasslands are areas primarily consisting of prairie.  Like National Forests, National Grasslands may be open for hunting, grazing, mineral extraction, recreation and other uses.  Various National Grasslands are typically administered in conjunction with nearby National Forests.
