Neetu Singh (born 1990): Neetu Singh (born 25 November 1990) is a model and Punjabi actress.  She came to the showbiz spotlight when she won Miss PTC Punjabi in 2008.  Soon after she came in a famous music video "Call Jalandhar Ton" by Harbhajan Maan.  She debuted in Dil Tainu Karda Ae Pyaar with Gulzar Inder Chahal in 2012.  Her latest movie Saadi Love Story was released in January 2013.  In addition to that she also appeared in the Bollywood heist film Special 26 released in February 2013.
