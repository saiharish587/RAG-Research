2016 SprintX GT Championship Series: The 2016 SprintX GT Championship Series was the inaugural season of the SprintX GT Championship Series.  The series was managed by WC Vision and sanctioned by the Sports Car Club of America (SCCA).  On May 28, 2015 WC Vision announced it would be launching the SprintX GT Championship Series as a support series of the Pirelli World Challenge.  Similar to GT races in the PWC, SprintX races had a sprint format as races were 60 minutes in length.  The difference between PWC GT races and SprintX races was that SprintX races featured mandatory driver and tire changes.
