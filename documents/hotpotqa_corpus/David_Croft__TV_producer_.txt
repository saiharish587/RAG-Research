David Croft (TV producer): Major David John Croft OBE (born David John Andrew Sharland; 7 September 1922 – 27 September 2011) was an English writer, producer and director.  David Croft is particularly noted for producing and co-writing a string of popular BBC sitcoms with Jimmy Perry and Jeremy Lloyd including "Dad's Army", "Are You Being Served? ", "It Ain't Half Hot Mum", "Hi-de-Hi! " and "'Allo 'Allo! ".
