Purple Naked Ladies: Purple Naked Ladies is the debut studio album by American soul band The Internet, a duo consisting of Syd tha Kyd and Matt Martians of Odd Future.  The digital version of the album was released on December 20, 2011, with a physical copy, with bonus tracks released on January 31, 2012.  The album is the first physically released album on Odd Future's own record label Odd Future Records.
