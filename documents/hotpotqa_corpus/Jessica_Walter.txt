Jessica Walter: Jessica Walter (born January 31, 1941) is an American actress.  She is known for appearing in the films "Play Misty for Me" and "Grand Prix", her role as Lucille Bluth on the sitcom "Arrested Development", and providing the voice of Malory Archer in the FX animated series "Archer".  Jessica Walter also studied acting at The Neighborhood Playhouse School of the Theatre in New York City.
