Tom Holland (actor): Thomas Stanley Holland (born 1 June 1996) is an English actor and dancer.  His breakthrough role was Spider-Man in "" (2016), followed by "" (2017), as part of the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU).  He previously appeared on stage in the title role of "Billy Elliot the Musical" in London's West End, and in the films "The Impossible" (2012) and "In the Heart of the Sea" (2015).  Holland received the BAFTA Rising Star Award in 2017.
