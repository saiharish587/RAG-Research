Tourism in Benin: Tourism in Benin is a small industry.  In 1996, Benin had approximately 150,000 tourists.  A small country with a high concentration of tourist attractions, Benin's national parks and culture are among its main tourist attractions.  Abomey is one of Benin's main tourist attractions, with palaces that became a World Heritage Site in 1982.  The capital city Porto Novo's attractions include its museums and architecture.
