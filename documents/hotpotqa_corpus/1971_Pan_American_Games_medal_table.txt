1971 Pan American Games medal table: The 1971 Pan American Games, officially known as the VI Pan American Games, were a continental multi-sport event held in Cali, Colombia, from July 30 to August 13, 1971.  At the Games, 2,935 athletes selected from 32 National Olympic Committees (NOCs) participated in events in 17 sports.  Twenty nations earned medals during the competition, and fifteen won at least one gold medal.
