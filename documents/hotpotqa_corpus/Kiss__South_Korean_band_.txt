Kiss (South Korean band): Kiss (Korea International Super Star, stylized as KISS) was a South Korean female pop trio.  Their debut single "Because I'm a Girl" () was a 2001 hit.  The music video stars actress Goo Hye-Joo, and actor Shin Hyun-joon, which increased initial public interest in the song.  The song remained a karaoke staple in Korea for many years.
