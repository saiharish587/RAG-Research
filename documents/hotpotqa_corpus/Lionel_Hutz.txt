Lionel Hutz: Lionel Hutz is a fictional character in the animated television series "The Simpsons".  He was voiced by Phil Hartman, and his first appearance was in the season two episode "Bart Gets Hit by a Car".  Hutz is a stereotypical ambulance chasing lawyer in Springfield with questionable competence and ethics.  He is nevertheless (or thus) often hired by the Simpsons.  Following Hartman's murder in 1998, Hutz was retired out of respect; and his final speaking role was in the season nine episode "Realty Bites" five months earlier.
