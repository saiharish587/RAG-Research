Ryukyukoku Matsuri Daiko Hawaii: Ryukyukoku Matsuri Daiko Hawaii (琉球國祭り太鼓ハワイ支部) is chapter of the Okinawan Eisā taiko ensemble Ryukyukoku Matsuri Daiko.  It is one of many chapters around the world.  Other chapters are found in Okinawa Prefecture, mainland Japan, South America, and the U.S. mainland.
