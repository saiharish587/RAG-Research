Shehab El-Din Ahmed: Shehab El-Din Ahmed (Arabic: شهاب الدين أحمد‎ ‎ ) (born on 22 August 1990) is an Egyptian footballer who plays as a Midfielder for El-Gaish.  He made his debut with Al-Ahly in a Premier League match on 20 May 2009 against Tersana.  He scored 3 premier league goals and a famous winning goal against Ettihad Libya in the quarter final of the Champions league 2010 from a long distance shot.
