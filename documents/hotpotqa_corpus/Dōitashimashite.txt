Dōitashimashite: Dōitashimashite (どういたしまして , "You're Welcome") is a live album released by artist Omar Rodríguez-López which was recorded in USA over four nights in September 2010.  It is the second live album to be released by the Omar Rodriguez Lopez Group, with the first being the live BBC studio session "Los Sueños de un Hígado".  Notably, during the week of these four concerts vocalist Ximena Sariñana was said to be suffering from laryngitis, a cold, and the flu.
