Volume 1: Instrumental Driving Music for Felons: Volume 1: Instrumental Driving Music for Felons is the debut extended play (EP) by American desert rock collective The Desert Sessions.  Recorded in August 1997 at Rancho De La Luna, it was released by Man's Ruin Records on November 18, 1997.  The album features eight credited musicians, including Josh Homme, John McBain and Ben Shepherd.  It was later re-released with "" in 1998 as "Volumes 1 & 2".
