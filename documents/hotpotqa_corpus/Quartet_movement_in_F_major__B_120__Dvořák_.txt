Quartet movement in F major, B.120 (Dvořák): The Quartet Movement in F major B. 120, is the first movement of a projected string quartet in F major by Antonín Dvořák composed in early October 1881 to fulfill a commission from the Hellmesberger Quartet and abandoned in favour of the String Quartet No. 11 in C major, Op. 61, B. 121.
