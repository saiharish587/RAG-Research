Érik Morales vs. Marco Antonio Barrera III: Érik Morales vs Marco Antonio Barrera III, billed as Once and For All, was a professional boxing match between the three-division world champion Marco Antonio Barrera and reigning WBC Super Featherweight world champion Erik Morales.  It took place on November 27, 2004, at the MGM Grand Garden Arena in Las Vegas, Nevada.  Barrera won the contest by majority decision, with two judges scoring it 115-113 and 115-114 for Barrera and the other 114-114.  The fight was named the 2004 Ring Magazine Fight of the Year.
