The Strap-Ons: The Strap-Ons are a punk rock band formed by vocalist Brian "Idle" Diederich, guitarist Billy England, bassist Emmit Joe George and drummer Ruben Remulla.  A few months later, guitarist Kate Grottenhaler joined the group.  The band formed in late 1996 originally as The Pimps in Norfolk, Virginia, but due to legal disputes, were forced to change their name to The Strap-Ons.  The band's first CD was put out by a cooperative of six labels around the world called The World Label Cooperative.
