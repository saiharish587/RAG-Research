Love Is a Drug (Markus Feehily song): "Love Is a Drug" is a song by Irish singer and songwriter and former member of Westlife Markus Feehily.  The song was released in the United Kingdom as a digital download On 19 April 2015 through Harmoney Entertainment.  It was released as the lead single from his debut studio album "Fire" (2015).  The song was written by Markus Feehily, Steve Anderson and Tinashé Fazakerley; and produced by Mojam.  The song has peaked at number 65 on the Irish Singles Chart and number 55 on the UK Singles Chart.
