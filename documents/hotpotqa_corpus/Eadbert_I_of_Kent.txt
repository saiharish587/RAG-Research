Eadbert I of Kent: Eadberht I was king of Kent from 725 to 748.  After his father, Wihtred of Kent died, he inherited the kingdom of Kent along with his two brothers Æðelberht II and Alric.  Æðelberht II seems to have been the eldest and more dominant brother.  Eadberht I died in 748, according to the "Anglo-Saxon Chronicle".  He left a son, Eardwulf, who succeeded as king jointly with his uncle.
