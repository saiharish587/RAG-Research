Swear It Again: "Swear It Again" is a song by Irish boy band Westlife.  It was released on April 12, 1999 in the United Kingdom and on February 25, 2000 in the United States as the first single from their self-titled debut album Westlife (1999).  It peaked at number one in the UK Singles Chart for two weeks in May 1999.  It moved to 182,000 units in the first two weeks of its release and spent 13 weeks on the charts.  This made it the first of fourteen UK number-one singles.  To date, "Swear It Again" is Westlife's only single to have charted in the U.S., peaking at number 20 on the Billboard Hot 100 and ranking number 75 on the "Billboard" Hot 100 Year End Charts, in 2000.  The song was performed live on "Miss Teen USA 2000".
