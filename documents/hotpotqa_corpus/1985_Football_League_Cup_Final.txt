1985 Football League Cup Final: The 1985 Football League Cup Final was won by Norwich City.  The Canaries defeated Sunderland 1–0 at Wembley Stadium on 24 March 1985 with an own goal scored by Gordon Chisholm, who deflected Asa Hartford's shot past goalkeeper Chris Turner.  Later in the second half, Clive Walker missed a penalty awarded for a handball by Norwich defender Dennis van Wijk.
