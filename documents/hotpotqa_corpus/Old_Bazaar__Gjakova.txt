Old Bazaar, Gjakova: The Old Bazaar (Albanian: "Pazari i vjetër" ; Serbian: Стари базар / "Stari bazar" ) in Gjakova is the oldest bazaar in Kosovo (also known as "Çarshia e Madhe" (Grand Bazaar) or "Dakovica".  Mëhalla e Hadumit, the historical neighborhood where it is located also houses the city's oldest mosque, the Hadum Mosque ("), which dates from the 15th century.
