Suicide Squad: The Suicide Squad is the name of two fictional antihero teams appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics.  The first version of the Suicide Squad debuted in "The Brave and the Bold" #25 (Sept.  1959), and the second and modern version, created by John Ostrander, debuted in "Legends" #3 (Jan.  1987).  One of the two teams saves the world from an threatening race of savages.
