XIII: The Series: XIII: The Series is an English-language Franco-Canadian TV series that premiered in April 2011 in France and Canada.  Loosely based on the Belgian graphic novel series created by Jean Van Hamme and William Vance debuting in 1984, about an amnesiac protagonist who seeks to discover his concealed past.  The TV series follows the events of the 2008 TV film "", which was also produced by Prodigy Pictures and Cipango.  The first season follows the plot in parallel with the existing volumes in the comic series, while the second season diverts into an all-new original story arc.
