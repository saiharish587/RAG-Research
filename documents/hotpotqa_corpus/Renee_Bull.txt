Renee Bull: Renee Bull (born February 24, 1993 in Dover) is an American beauty pageant titleholder from Middletown, Delaware.  She was 1st Runner-Up at Miss Delaware 2012 and a Top 11 Finalist at Miss Delaware 2013.  She was crowned Miss Delaware USA 2015 and represented her state at Miss USA 2015 where she became the first delegate from Her state to place in the semifinals, finishing in the Top 11.
