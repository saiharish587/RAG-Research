Fakta Ladh Mhana: Fakta Ladh Mhana is a 2011 multistarrer Marathi action film with lead roles by Aniket Vishwasrao, Siddharth Jadhav, Sanjay Narvekar, Santosh Juvekar, Bharat Jadhav, Mahesh Manjrekar, Sachin Khedekar and Vaibhav Mangle.  The film is written and produced by Mahesh Manjrekar and is directed by Sanjay Jadhav.  It is notable for being one of the costliest Marathi film.
