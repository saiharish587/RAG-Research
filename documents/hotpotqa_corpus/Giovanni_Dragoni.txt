Giovanni Dragoni: Giovanni Andrea Dragoni (or Draconi, c. 1540 – December 1598) was an Italian composer of the Roman School of the late Renaissance, a student of Giovanni Pierluigi da Palestrina, and a prominent composer and "maestro di cappella" in Rome in the late 16th century.  He left numerous sacred and secular works, almost all vocal, and was especially noted for his often-reprinted books of madrigals.
