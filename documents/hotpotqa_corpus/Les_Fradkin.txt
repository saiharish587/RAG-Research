Les Fradkin: Les Fradkin (born 1951) is an American MIDI guitarist, keyboardist, songwriter, composer, and record producer.  He is best known for being a member of the original cast of the hit Broadway show "Beatlemania".  In addition to playing MIDI guitar, he plays 12 string guitar, the Starr Labs Ztar, guitar synthesizer, SynthAxe, Hammond organ, Mellotron, piano, bass guitar, and Moog synthesizer.
