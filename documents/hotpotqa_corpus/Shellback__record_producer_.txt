Shellback (record producer): Karl Johan Schuster (] ; born 1 February 1985), known professionally as Shellback, is a Swedish songwriter, record producer, and musician.  Shellback was listed as the No. 1 producer of 2012 on "Billboard" magazine's year end chart, and he also topped the list of their "Top 10 Songwriters Airplay Chart" the same year; he has won four Grammy Awards.
