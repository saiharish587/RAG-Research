News Review: News Review was a British news magazine, first published by Cosmopolitan Press in 1936.  Its publishers, who also launched "Cavalcade" around the same time, envisaged "News Review" as a competitor to the U.S. "Time" magazine.  It was later sold to Odhams Press.  The headquarters was in London.  The magazine ended its run by eventually being amalgamated into Odhams' "Illustrated" magazine in 1950.
