2009 China Open – Women's Doubles: Anabel Medina Garrigues and Caroline Wozniacki were the defending champion, but Wozniacki chose not to participate this year.Medina Garrigues partnered with Virginia Ruano Pascual, but they lost in the second round against Anastasia Pavlyuchenkova and Yanina Wickmayer. Hsieh Su-Wei and Peng Shuai won in the final 6-3, 6-1 against Alla Kudryavtseva and Ekaterina Makarova.
