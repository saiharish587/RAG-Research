Army of the Holy Roman Empire: The Army of the Holy Roman Empire (German "Reichsarmee", "Reichsheer" or "Reichsarmatur"; Latin "exercitus imperii") was created in 1422, and came to an end when the Holy Roman Empire was wound up in 1806, as the result of the Napoleonic Wars.  It must not be confused with the Imperial Army ("Kaiserliche Armee") of the Emperor.
