One Day at a Time (album): One Day at a Time is a 1970 album by Joan Baez.  Recorded in Nashville, the album was a continuation of Baez' experimentation with country music, begun with the previous year's "David's Album".  It is significant in that it was the first to include Baez' own compositions, "Sweet Sir Galahad" and "A Song for David", the former song a ballad for her younger sister Mimi Fariña, and the latter song being for her then husband, David Harris, at the time in prison as a conscientious objector.  "One Day at a Time" also included work by The Rolling Stones, Willie Nelson and Pete Seeger.
