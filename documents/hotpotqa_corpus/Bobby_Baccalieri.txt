Bobby Baccalieri: Robert "Bobby Bacala" Baccalieri, Jr., played by Steve Schirripa, is a fictional character on the HBO series "The Sopranos".  A mobster, he is first shown as one of the soldiers and then later top aides to Corrado "Junior" Soprano.  Throughout the series, he rises through the ranks of the organized crime organization, becoming a capo, as well as Tony Soprano's brother-in-law.
