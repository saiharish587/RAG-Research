Kubelie Mosque: Kubelie Mosque is a mosque in Kavajë, Albania.  A mosque was originally built here under the Ottomans by Kapllan Beu, the Kapllan Pasha Mosque or "Bachelors' Mosque".  The Old Mosque (Xhamija e Vjetër), stood on the main street of the city, about 70m to the east of the current one.  Kubelie mosque is described as a "grand, beautiful building, with a dome and a peristyle.  Marble facades rise under the cypress trees with their Byzantine columns and their Arabian arches."
