Western Bahr el Ghazal: Wau State was one of the 28 states of South Sudan.  It had an area of 93900 km2 and was the least populous state in South Sudan, according to the controversial Sudanese census conducted in 2008.  It was part of the Bahr el Ghazal region.  Its capital was Wau.  The state shared international borders with Sudan to the north and the Central African Republic to the west.  The portion now occupied by Raga County (pronounced 'Raja') is the southern part of the historical region known as "Dar Fertit".
