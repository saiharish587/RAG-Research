2006 Football League Championship play-off Final: The 2006 Football League Championship play-off Final was an association football match played at the Millennium Stadium, Cardiff on 21 May 2006.  It determined the third and final team in the 2005–06 football season to be promoted to the Premier League, the highest division in the English football league system.  It was the last play-off final to be held at the Millennium Stadium, as the new Wembley Stadium was completed in time for the 2007 final.
