Libertarian Party of Maryland: The Libertarian Party of Maryland is the Maryland affiliate of the Libertarian Party.  The state chair is Robert S. Johnston III.  The party, also known as "LPMaryland," is Maryland’s third-largest political party, with 13,549 registered voters across the state as of March 31, 2014.  According to its website, the party advocates "a smaller government that costs less and leaves individuals with more economic opportunity and more personal freedom," and "work[s] to advance that view by supporting Libertarian candidates for local, state, and federal office."  LPMaryland also forms coalitions with other civic organizations who share at least some common ground with libertarians, including groups that concern themselves primarily with civil liberties, world peace, fiscal restraint, and government reform.  The official views of the party on state-level policy issues are set forth in the Libertarian Party of Maryland Program.
