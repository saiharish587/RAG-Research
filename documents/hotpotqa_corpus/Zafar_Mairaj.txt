Zafar Mairaj: Zafar Mairaj (Urdu: ‎ ) born on 11 October 1968, at Quetta, Balochistan, is a Pakistani drama writer, lyricist, and short story writer.  He primarily writes in Urdu, but has also written in Brahui, and Balochi.  He has written more than 50 drama serials, drama series, short plays and telefilms, for National television PTV (Pakistan Television) as well as for most of the private channels in Pakistan like Geo TV, AAJ TV, ARY Digital, Indus Vision, TVOne Global, Hum TV etc.  He has won National Drama awards.
