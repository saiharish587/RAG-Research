Cajun Moon: "Cajun Moon' is a song written by Jim Rushing, and recorded by American country artist Ricky Skaggs.  It was released in January 1986 as the second single from the album "Live in London".  "Cajun Moon" was Ricky Skaggs' tenth number one on the country chart.  The single went to number one for one week and spent thirteen weeks on the country chart.
