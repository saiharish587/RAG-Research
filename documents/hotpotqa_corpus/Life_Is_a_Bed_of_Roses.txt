Life Is a Bed of Roses: Life Is a Bed of Roses (French: La vie est un roman) is a 1983 French film directed by Alain Resnais from a screenplay by Jean Gruault.  The English-language distribution title of the film is "Life Is a Bed of Roses", though it has also been known as "Forbek's Castle" and "Life Is a Fairy Tale".  A literal translation of the original title is "Life is a novel ["or" story, romance]"; in the film the French quotation (or misquotation) is attributed to Napoleon.
