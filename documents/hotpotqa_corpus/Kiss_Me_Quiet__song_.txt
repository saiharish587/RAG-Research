Kiss Me Quiet (song): "Kiss Me Quiet" is a song recorded by Canadian country singer Jess Moskaluke for her second extended play of the same name (2015).  It was written by Kelly Archer, Corey Crowder, and Emily Shackelton and was produced by Crowder.  "Kiss Me Quiet" was released August 28, 2015 as the EP's lead single.  The song was Moskaluke's first solo top 10 on the Canada Country chart.
