Samsung Galaxy S7: Samsung Galaxy S7 and Samsung Galaxy S7 Edge are Android smartphones manufactured and marketed by Samsung Electronics.  The S7 series serves as the successor to the Galaxy S6, S6 Edge and S6 Edge+ released in 2015.  The phones were officially unveiled on 21 February 2016, during a Samsung press conference at Mobile World Congress, with a European and North American release on 11 March 2016.
