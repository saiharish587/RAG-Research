York Cliffs Historic District: The York Cliffs Historic District is located on Agamenticus Avenue in York, Maine.  The district was added to the National Register of Historic Places on July 26, 1984.  It encompasses a collection of eight late Victorian summer mansions built as part of an exclusive development by the York Cliffs Company between 1890 and 1902, representing one of the finest such collections remaining on the coast of Maine.  The district was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1984.
