Maine School Administrative District 9: Maine School Administrative District 9 (MSAD 9) is a regional school district in Franklin County, Maine.  It serves children in grades K-12 with one high school (Mount Blue High School), one middle school and four elementary schools from Wilton, Maine; Farmington, Maine; Weld, Maine; Temple, Maine; Chesterville, Maine; New Vineyard, Maine; Industry, Maine; New Sharon, Maine; Starks, Maine; and Vienna, Maine.  MSAD 9 recently become RSU 9.
