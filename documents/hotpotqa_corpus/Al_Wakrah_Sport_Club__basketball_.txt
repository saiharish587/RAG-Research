Al-Wakrah Sport Club (basketball): Al Wakrah Basketball Team (Arabic: فريق كرة السلة الوكرة‎ ‎ ) is a Qatari professional basketball team based in the city of Al Wakrah, in southern Qatar.  Al Wakrah's basketball team currently competes in the top tier of basketball, the Qatari Basketball League.  It is part of the Al-Wakrah Sport Club multisport club.
