George Gould (businessman): George Gould (6 April 1865 – 26 May 1941) was a New Zealand farmer, businessman, stock breeder, racehorse owner and breeder, and racing administrator.  He was born in Christchurch, New Zealand on 6 April 1865.  His father (1823–1889), a prominent businessman, had the same name.  He was the uncle of George Macdonald.  He was the grandfather of British politician, Bryan Gould.
