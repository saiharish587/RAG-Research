Mukti (organisation): MUKTI (Bengali: মুক্তি "Liberation"), is a registered not-for-profit and socio-economic development trust working in Sunderban and other part of West Bengal, India for making uplift of the grassroots are in penury, social exclusion and myriad discriminations.  Mukti is registered under the Government of India Trust Act Section 64.  Mukti, which means "liberation", was founded in 2003.  More than 500 volunteers work in the Sunderban region of India in the areas of health, education, the economy, ecology and culture.
