Greenport Union Free School District: Greenport Union Free School District is a public school district located on the North Fork of Long Island, in the Town of Southold, Suffolk County, New York, United States.  It includes the village of Greenport, as well as the census-designated place(CDP) of Greenport West.  To the west, the district is bordered by the Southold Union Free School District; and on the east, the Oysterponds Union Free School District.
