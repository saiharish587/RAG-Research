Spiceberg Slim: Spiceberg Slim is the eighth studio album by American rapper Spice 1.  The album was released in 2002 for Hard Tyme Records, & Riviera Records and made it to #79 on the Top R&B/Hip-Hop album charts and #39 on the Top Independent album charts.  The album also featured guest appearances from Kokane, Tray Dee, Outlawz and Jayo Felony.
