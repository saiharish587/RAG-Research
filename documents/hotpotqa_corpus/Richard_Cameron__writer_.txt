Richard Cameron (writer): Richard Cameron is an English playwright from Doncaster (born (1948--)16 1948 ).  His themes are Northern post-industrial society, working class life, tough women and violent men.  Cameron's plays include "Pond Life" (1992), "Not Fade Away" (1993), "The Mortal Ash" (1994), "All of You Mine" (1996), "The Glee Club" (2002), "Gong Donkeys" (2004), "Flower Girls" (2007), and "Can't Stand Up For Falling Down".
