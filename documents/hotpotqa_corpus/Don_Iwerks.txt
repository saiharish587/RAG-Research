Don Iwerks: Donald Warren Iwerks ( ; born July 24, 1929) is a former Disney executive, an Oscar winner, and co-founder of Iwerks Entertainment along with former Disney executive Stan Kinsey.  He is the son of the animator Ub Iwerks (Walt Disney's original business partner and co-creator of Mickey Mouse and Oswald the Lucky Rabbit) and father of Oscar nominated documentary film producer Leslie Iwerks.
