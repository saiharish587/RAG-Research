Central Asian Review: Central Asian Review was a journal of Central Asian Studies published from 1953 to 1968.  The journal’s full title was Central Asian Review: A Quarterly Review of Current Developments in Soviet Central Asia and Kazakhstan and was published quarterly by the Central Asian Research Centre in association with St. Antony's College, Oxford University.  Founder and director of the center, Geoffrey Wheeler was the editor-in-chief and frequent contributor to the journal.
