2012 Delaware Fightin' Blue Hens football team: The 2012 Delaware Fightin' Blue Hens football team represented the University of Delaware in the 2012 NCAA Division I FCS football season.  They were led by 11th-year head coach K. C. Keeler and played their home games at Delaware Stadium in Newark, Delaware.  They were a member of the Colonial Athletic Association (CAA).  They finished the season 5–6, 2–6 in CAA play to finish in eighth place.
