Matti Hoikka: Matts (Matti) Wiktor Hoikka (25 February 1859 in Rovaniemi – 15 July 1939) was a Finnish blacksmith and politician.  He was a member of the Parliament of Finland from 1907 to 1914 and again from 1917 to 1918, representing the Social Democratic Party of Finland (SDP).  He was imprisoned in 1918 for having sided with the Reds during the Finnish Civil War.
