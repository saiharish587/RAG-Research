Winter Palace of Prince Eugene: The Winter Palace of Prince Eugene (German: "Winterpalais Prinz Eugen" ), also known as the City Palace (German: "Stadtpalais" ), is a high-Baroque palace in the Innere Stadt district of Vienna, Austria.  Located on a narrow street at Himmelpfortgasse 8, the palace was used as the winter residence of Prince Eugene of Savoy, who spent his summers at the Belvedere.  The Winter Palace was designed and constructed by Johann Bernhard Fischer von Erlach from 1695 to 1700, and by Johann Lukas von Hildebrandt from 1702 to 1724 following his predecessor's plans.
