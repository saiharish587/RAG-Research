Hasso von Wedel (general): Hasso von Wedel (20 November 1898 – 3 January 1961) was a German general who commanded the Wehrmacht Propaganda Troops during World War II.  He was directly subordinate to the head of OKW Operations Staff ("Wehrmachtführungsstab, WFSt.") , General Alfred Jodl.  Wedel's Propaganda Department had control over the propaganda units and served to mediate between them and the Reich Propaganda Ministry of Joseph Goebbels.
