The Black Crowes: The Black Crowes were an American rock band formed in 1989.  Their discography includes eight studio albums, four live albums and several charting singles.  The band was signed to Def American Recordings in 1989 by producer George Drakoulias and released their debut album, "Shake Your Money Maker", the following year.  The follow-up, "The Southern Harmony and Musical Companion", reached the top of the "Billboard" 200 in 1992.
