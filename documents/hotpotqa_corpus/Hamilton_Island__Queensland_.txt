Hamilton Island (Queensland): Hamilton Island is the largest inhabited island of the Whitsunday Islands in Queensland, Australia.  It is positioned approximately 887 km north of Brisbane and 512 km south of Cairns.  It is also the only island in the Great Barrier Reef with its own commercial airport, with short direct flights from Sydney, Melbourne, Brisbane and Cairns.  Like most in the Whitsunday group, Hamilton Island was formed as sea levels rose which created numerous drowned mountains that are situated close to the east coast of Queensland.
