List of Franklin &amp; Bash episodes: "Franklin & Bash" is an American legal comedy-drama series created by Kevin Falls and Bill Chais that airs on TNT.  It stars Breckin Meyer as Jared Franklin, and Mark-Paul Gosselaar as Peter Bash, two unconventional lawyers and longtime friends that are recruited by the head of a firm to bring new life and perspective to the work place.  "Franklin & Bash" premiered on June 1, 2011.
