1969–70 New York Knicks season: The 1969–70 New York Knicks season was the 24th season of NBA basketball in New York City.  The Knicks had a then single-season NBA record 18 straight victories en route to 60–22 record, which was the best regular season record in the team's history.  They set the record for the best start for the first 24 games at 23-1 before the Golden State Warriors surpassed it in 2015.  After defeating the Bullets in the Eastern Division semifinals and the Milwaukee Bucks in the Eastern Division finals, the Knicks defeated the Los Angeles Lakers in seven games to capture their first NBA title.
