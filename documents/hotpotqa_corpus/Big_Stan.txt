Big Stan: Big Stan is a 2007 American prison comedy directed and produced by Rob Schneider, who also starred in the film.  The film co-stars Jennifer Morrison, Scott Wilson and David Carradine.  The film also features Henry Gibson in his final role.  Although released in some overseas markets during the fall of 2008, it was released straight to DVD in the U.S. on March 24, 2009.  It debuted at number 17 on the DVD rental charts of March 23–30, 2009.  On the radio show "Loveline", Schneider stated that this film will be an "anti-man-raping" film — referring to prison rape.
