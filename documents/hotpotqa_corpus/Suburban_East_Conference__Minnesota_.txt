Suburban East Conference (Minnesota): The Suburban East Conference is a Minnesota State High School League conference in the Twin Cities, Minnesota.  Its members are Cretin-Derham Hall High School, East Ridge High School, Forest Lake Area High School, Hastings High School (Minnesota), Mounds View High School, Park High School, Roseville Area High School, Stillwater Area High School, White Bear Lake Area High School and Woodbury High School.  Member schools field a full complement of 30 interscholastic sports and 14 Fine Arts activities.  Cretin-Derham Hall High School is the newest and single private school in the conference.
