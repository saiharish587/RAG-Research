Mayré Martínez: Mayré Andrea de los Ángeles Martínez Blanco (Born November 28 in Caracas, Venezuela), is a Latin pop singer, songwriter.  She rose to popularity in Latin America after winning the first season of the reality show "Latin American Idol".  In her native country, Venezuela, she was a contestant in Radio Caracas Television's reality show "Fama, Sudor y Lágrimas" 2006, where she came in fifth place, because she decided to back out in order to join the cast of "Latin American Idol".  She's the oldest contestant ever to win "Latin American Idol" prior to being the first winner.
