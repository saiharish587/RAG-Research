Jamie Henderson: Jamie Henderson (born January 1, 1979) is a former American Football player (NFL).  He played cornerback at the University of Georgia and was drafted in the 4th round of the 2001 draft by The New York Jets.  He played for the New York Jets from 2001–2003.  He is the cousin of American football player, Reggie Brown (wide receiver) for the Tampa Bay Buccaneers of the NFL.  He has two sons, Jalen Lyght Henderson (born 4-2-02) and Jamie Isaiah Henderson (born 10-2-04) .  Henderson attended Carrollton High School, as well as Reggie Brown, and played for the Carrollton Trojan football team.
