Lyle Moevao: Lyle Moevao (born January 17, 1987) is a former college football quarterback for the Oregon State Beavers football team.  Moevao was the starting quarterback for the Beavers for the 2008 season, throwing for 2,534 yards and 19 touchdowns with a quarterback rating of 128.41.  He also helped the Beavers to a 3-0 victory in the 2008 Sun Bowl against the Pittsburgh Panthers.
