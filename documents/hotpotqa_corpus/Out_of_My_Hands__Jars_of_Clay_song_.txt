Out of My Hands (Jars of Clay song): "Out of My Hands" is a song by Christian rock act Jars of Clay that appears on their 2010-released album, "The Shelter".  The song, which was the first radio single released in support of the album, features vocal contributions from Mike Donehey of Tenth Avenue North and Leigh Nash of Sixpence None the Richer.  The single peaked at No. 27 on Billboard's Christian Songs chart on September 25, 2010.  "Out of My Hands" was co-written by two members of the band's touring ensemble, Gabe Ruschival and Jeremy Lutito.
