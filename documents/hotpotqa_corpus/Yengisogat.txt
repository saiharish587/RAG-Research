Yengisogat: The Yengisogat range (音苏盖提 ), also known as the Wesm Mountains, is a Chinese subrange of the Karakoram mountain range.  It lies north of the Baltoro Muztagh, home of the eight-thousanders of the Karakoram.  The highest peak is Huangguan Shan, or Crown Peak, 7,265 m (23,835 ft) (also sometimes given as 7,295 m/23,934 ft).
