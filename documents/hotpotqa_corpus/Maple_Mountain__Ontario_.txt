Maple Mountain (Ontario): Maple Mountain is a mountain, located within Lady Evelyn-Smoothwater Provincial Park, Northeastern Ontario, Canada, estimated 642 m above mean sea level.  It has a higher vertical rise over the surrounding landscape, 37 m higher than the Ishpatina Ridge, which is the highest point of land in Ontario rising over Scarecrow Lake.  Maple Mountain rises over Tupper Lake and is considerably better known than Ishpatina Ridge.  It ranks thirteenth, but when ranked by vertical rise it is the highest of the top 25 peaks in Ontario.  The 100-ft fire tower is also still intact and attracts many hikers.  A 3.2 kilometre long hiking trail leads to the abandoned fire tower.
