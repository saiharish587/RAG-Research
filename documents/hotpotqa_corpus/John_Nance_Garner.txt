John Nance Garner: John Nance Garner IV (November 22, 1868 – November 7, 1967), known among his contemporaries as "Cactus Jack", was an American Democratic politician and lawyer from Texas.  He was the 39th Speaker of the United States House of Representatives from 1931 to 1933.  He was also the 32nd Vice President of the United States, serving from 1933 to 1941.  Along with Schuyler Colfax, Garner is one of two individuals to serve as Vice President of the United States and Speaker of the United States House of Representatives.
