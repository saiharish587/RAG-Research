2010 Russian Figure Skating Championships: The 2010 Russian Figure Skating Championships (Russian: Чемпионат России по фигурному катанию на коньках 2010 ) was the Russian Figure Skating Championships of the 2009–10 figure skating season.  They were the national championship to determine the national champions of Russia.  They were organized by the Figure Skating Federation of Russia.  In addition to determining the national champions, the event was also used to help determine the teams for the 2010 World Figure Skating Championships, the 2010 European Figure Skating Championships, and the 2010 Winter Olympics.
