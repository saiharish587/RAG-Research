Alexei Yagudin: Alexei Konstantinovich Yagudin (Russian:    ; 18 March 1980) is a Russian former competitive figure skater.  He is the 2002 Olympic champion, a four-time World champion (1998, 1999, 2000, 2002), a three-time European champion (1998, 1999, 2002), a two-time Grand Prix Final champion (1998–1999, 2001–2002), the 1996 World Junior champion, and a two-time World Professional champion (1998, 2002).
