Jurassic Park Builder: Jurassic Park Builder is an Apple and later Android and Facebook application produced by Ludia in 2012.  It is a construction and management simulation game in which the player builds a theme park featuring extinct animals.  Three kinds of parks can be made: "Jurassic Park", located on Isla Nublar, featuring dinosaurs and other Mesozoic animals; "Aquatic Park", located on a seabed featuring extinct aquatic animals; and "Glacier Park", located in Patagonia, featuring extinct animals from the Cenozoic era along with some Mesozoic crocodilians.
