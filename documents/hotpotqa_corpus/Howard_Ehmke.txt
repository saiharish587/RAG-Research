Howard Ehmke: Howard Jonathan Ehmke (April 24, 1894 – March 17, 1959) was a right-handed American baseball pitcher.  He played professional baseball for 16 years from 1914 to 1930, including 15 seasons in Major League Baseball pitcher for the Buffalo Blues (1915), Detroit Tigers (1916–1917, 1919–1922), Boston Red Sox (1923–1926), and Philadelphia Athletics (1926–1930).
