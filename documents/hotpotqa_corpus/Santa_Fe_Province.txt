Santa Fe Province: The Province of Santa Fe (Spanish: "Provincia de Santa Fe" , ] ) is a province of Argentina, located in the center-east of the country.  Neighboring provinces are from the north clockwise Chaco (divided by the 28th parallel south), Corrientes, Entre Ríos, Buenos Aires, Córdoba, and Santiago del Estero.  Together with Córdoba and Entre Ríos, the province is part of the economico-political association known as the Center Region.
