Metamorfosi Sotiros, Patras: Metamorfosi Sotiros (Greek: Μεταμόρφωση Σωτήρος ) is a neighbourhood in the southern part of the city of Patras.  The area is next to the municipality of Messatida, the subdivision is divided between the two municipalities and then, the communes of Ovrya and Saravali in which are now municipal districts.  The subdivision was built in 1970 and many inhabitants came from Tritaia.  It also features the central vegetable shop in Patras.  The area is the northern part of Demenika.  Farmlands covers the outer areas as well as a small forest.
