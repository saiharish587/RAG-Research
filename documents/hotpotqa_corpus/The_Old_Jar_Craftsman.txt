The Old Jar Craftsman: The Old Jar Craftsman (독짓는 늙은이 - "Dokjinneun neulgeuni") "aka" Old Man Making a Jar is a 1969 South Korean film directed by Choi Ha-won.  It was awarded Best Film at the Blue Dragon Film Awards ceremony.  The film was selected as the South Korean entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 42nd Academy Awards, but was not accepted as a nominee.
