Fantasy (game show): Fantasy is a combination American game show and human interest show co-hosted by Peter Marshall and Leslie Uggams, with Chris Lemmon and Meredith MacRae as remote correspondents.  Bill Armstrong announced the show, with substitutes from Johnny Gilbert and Charlie Tuna.  Tuna replaced Armstrong about midway through the series' run.  It aired on NBC from September 13, 1982 to October 28, 1983, and was videotaped at the network's studios in Burbank, California.
