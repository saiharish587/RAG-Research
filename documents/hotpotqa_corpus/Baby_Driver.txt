Baby Driver: Baby Driver is a 2017 action crime comedy film written and directed by Edgar Wright.  It stars Ansel Elgort, Kevin Spacey, Lily James, Eiza González, Jon Hamm, Jamie Foxx, and Jon Bernthal.  The plot follows Baby, a young getaway driver and music lover who must work for a kingpin.  The film is best known for its choreography, in which the actors' timing and movements are synced with the soundtrack.
