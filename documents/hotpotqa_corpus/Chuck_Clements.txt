Chuck Clements: Chad "Chuck" Clements (born September 29, 1973) is a former American football quarterback who played one season with the New York Jets of the National Football League (NFL).  He was drafted by the New York Jets in the sixth round of the 1997 NFL Draft.  He played college football at the University of Houston and attended Huntsville High School in Huntsville, Texas.  He was also a member of the Philadelphia Eagles, Denver Broncos, Berlin Thunder, Las Vegas Outlaws and Ottawa Renegades.  Clements was drafted fifth overall by the Las Vegas Outlaws in the 2001 XFL Draft but, because of a preseason injury, never played for them.
