Meurimueng-rimueng-do: Meurimueng-rimueng-do is a two-player abstract strategy board game from Sumatra, Indonesia.  It is played by the Acehnese.  The game was published in the book entitled "The Achehnese" by Hurgronje, O'Sullivan, and Wilkinson in 1906 and described on page 204.  The game is a hunt game similar to Pulijudam and Demala diviyan keliya.  They use the same triangular board.  Therefore, meurimueng-rimueng-do is specifically a leopard hunt game (or leopard game).  In this game, 5 tigers (or leopards) are going up against 15 sheep.  The sheep attempt to surround and trap the 5 tigers while the tigers attempt to avoid this fate by capturing enough of the sheep.
