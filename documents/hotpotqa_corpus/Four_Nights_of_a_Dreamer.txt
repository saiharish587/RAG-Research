Four Nights of a Dreamer: Four Nights of a Dreamer (French: Quatre nuits d'un rêveur ) is a 1971 French drama film directed by Robert Bresson and starring Isabelle Weingarten.  The film was entered into the 21st Berlin International Film Festival.  The film is loosely based on the story "White Nights" written by Fyodor Dostoyevsky.
