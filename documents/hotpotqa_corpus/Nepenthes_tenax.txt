Nepenthes tenax: Nepenthes tenax ( ; from Latin: "tenax" "tenacious") is a lowland species of tropical pitcher plant native to northern Queensland, Australia.  It is the third "Nepenthes" species recorded from the continent and its second endemic species.  "Nepenthes tenax" is closely related to the two other Australian "Nepenthes" species: "N. mirabilis" and "N. rowaniae".
