URO VAMTAC: The URO VAMTAC (Vehículo de Alta Movilidad Táctico, "High Mobility Tactical Vehicle" ) is a Spanish four-wheel drive military vehicle manufactured by the UROVESA.  It is similar in appearance and design to the Humvee of the United States Military.  More than 2,000 of the vehicles have been delivered to the Spanish Armed Forces.  Several other countries operate the VAMTAC as well, and it has seen service most recently in Afghanistan and Syria.  The vehicle comes in two models, named I3 and S3, and has several configurations.
