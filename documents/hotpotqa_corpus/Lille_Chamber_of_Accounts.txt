Lille Chamber of Accounts: The Lille Chamber of Accounts (French: "Chambre des comptes de Lille" ) was founded by Philip the Bold on 15 February 1386 to audit the accounts of his functionaries in the county of Flanders.  As Burgundian rule expanded in the Low Countries, similar chambers were founded in Brussels and The Hague.  The Lille chamber ultimately oversaw government expenditure not only in the county of Flanders, but also in the county of Artois, county of Hainaut, marquisate of Namur, the Franche Comté, and the lordship of Mechelen in the Burgundian Netherlands, and later among the Seventeen Provinces of the Habsburg Netherlands.
