Bert Andrews (photographer): Bert Andrews (March 21, 1929–January 25, 1993) was an American photographer, who chronicled black theatre in New York City.  In a career that spanned over three decades he photographed many of the leading African American actors of the stage and screen including James Earl Jones, Cicely Tyson, Diana Sands, Louis Gossett, Jr., Billy Dee Williams, Morgan Freeman, Alfre Woodard, Denzel Washington and Samuel L. Jackson.
