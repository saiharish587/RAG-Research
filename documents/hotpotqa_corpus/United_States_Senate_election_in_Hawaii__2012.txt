United States Senate election in Hawaii, 2012: The 2012 United States Senate election in Hawaii took place on November 6, 2012, concurrently with the 2012 U.S. presidential election as well as other elections to the United States Senate and House of Representatives and various state and local elections.  Incumbent Democratic U.S. Senator Daniel Akaka decided to retire instead of running for re-election to a fourth term.  Democratic Congresswoman Mazie Hirono defeated former Hawaii Governor Linda Lingle in a rematch of the 2002 Hawaii gubernatorial election.
