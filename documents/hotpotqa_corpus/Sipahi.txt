Sipahi: Sipahi (Ottoman Turkish: سپاهی "sipâhi"‎ , ] ) were two types of Ottoman cavalry corps, including the fief-holding provincial "timarli sipahi", which constituted most of the army, and the regular "kapikulu sipahi", palace troops.  Other types of cavalry which were not regarded "sipahi" were the irregular "akıncı" ("raiders").  The "sipahi" formed their own distinctive social classes, and were notably in rivalry with the Janissaries, the elite corps of the Sultan.
