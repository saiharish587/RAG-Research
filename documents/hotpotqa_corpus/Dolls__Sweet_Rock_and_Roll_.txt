Dolls (Sweet Rock and Roll): "Dolls (Sweet Rock and Roll)" (sometimes referred to as "Dolls" or "Dolls (Sweet Rock n Roll)") is a song by Scottish band Primal Scream.  It was released as the second single from the band's eighth album, "Riot City Blues", on 7 August 2006, and reached number forty on the UK Singles Chart.  It also features the vocals of Alison Mosshart (VV) from the British/American rock band, The Kills.
