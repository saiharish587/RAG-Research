Serge Hélan: Serge Hélan (born 24 February 1964 in Pointe-à-Pitre, Guadeloupe) is a retired French triple jumper, best known for his bronze medal at the 1995 World Indoor Championships.  His personal best was 17.55 metres, achieved at the 1994 European Championships in Helsinki.  This was a French record as well.  He also competed in the long jump from time to time, his personal best was 8.12 metres.
