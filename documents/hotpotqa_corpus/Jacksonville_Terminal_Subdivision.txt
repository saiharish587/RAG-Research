Jacksonville Terminal Subdivision: The Jacksonville Terminal Subdivision is a railroad line owned by CSX Transportation in the U.S. State of Florida.  The S-Line runs from Jacksonville, Florida to Baldwin, Florida for a total of 17.8 miles.  At its north end it continues south from the Nahunta Subdivision and at its south end it continues south as the Tallahassee Subdivision.  The A-Line runs from Jacksonville, Florida to St. Johns, Florida for a total of 13.0 miles.  At its north end it continues south from the Nahunta Subdivision and at its south end it continues south as the Sanford Subdivision.
