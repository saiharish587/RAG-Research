Neil Campbell (scientist): Neil Allison Campbell (April 17, 1946 – October 21, 2004) was an American scientist known best for his textbook "Biology".  First published in 1987, the text is currently in its 11th edition (ISBN  , published 19 October 2016 by Lisa A. Urry, "et al".)  The title is popular worldwide and is used by over 500,000 students in both high school and college-level classes.
