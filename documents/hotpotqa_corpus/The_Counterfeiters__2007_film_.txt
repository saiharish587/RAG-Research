The Counterfeiters (2007 film): The Counterfeiters (German: "Die Fälscher" ) is a 2007 Austrian-German drama film written and directed by Stefan Ruzowitzky.  It fictionalizes Operation Bernhard, a secret plan by Nazi Germany during World War II to destabilize the United Kingdom by flooding its economy with forged Bank of England pound notes.  The film centres on a Jewish counterfeiter, Salomon 'Sally' Sorowitsch, who is coerced into assisting the operation at the Sachsenhausen concentration camp.
