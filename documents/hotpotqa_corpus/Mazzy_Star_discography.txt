Mazzy Star discography: The discography of American alternative rock band Mazzy Star consists of four studio albums and ten singles.  The group formed in 1989 from Opal, with guitarist David Roback and bassist Kendra Smith.  When Smith left the band, she was replaced by vocalist Hope Sandoval.  Mazzy Star's first studio album was "She Hangs Brightly", released in 1990 on Capitol Records.  It was certified gold in the United States and spawned two singles, "Halah" and "Blue Flower."
