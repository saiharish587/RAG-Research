Nara Lokesh: Indian politician and businessman.  He is the Cabinet Minister for Information Technology, Panchayati Raj and Rural Development in Andhra Pradesh.  He is the son of Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh and Telugu Desam Party Chief Nara Chandrababu Naidu, and grandson of former Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh Sri N. T. Rama Rao.  He is famously known as Andhra pappu.
