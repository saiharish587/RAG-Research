Don't Laugh Now: Don't Laugh Now was Ray Stevens' nineteenth studio album and his third and final for RCA Records, released in 1982.  The front of the album cover shows Stevens looking pensive and holding a comedy mask, while the back cover shows Stevens smiling and holding a tragedy mask.  Two singles were lifted from the album: "Written Down in My Heart" and "Where the Sun Don't Shine," both of which became minor country hits.
