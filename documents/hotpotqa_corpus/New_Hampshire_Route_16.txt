New Hampshire Route 16: New Hampshire Route 16 is a 149.75 mi long north–south highway in New Hampshire.  Much of its length is close to the border with Maine.  The section from Portsmouth to Milton is a controlled-access toll highway known as the Spaulding Turnpike.  Between the Portsmouth Circle and Dover Point, US 4 is also routed along the Turnpike.  Its southern terminus of NH 16 is at the Portsmouth Traffic Circle, intersecting with Interstate 95 and the US 1 Bypass.  The northern terminus is at the Maine state line near Wentworth's Location, where it continues as Maine State Route 16.
