John Gioia: John Gioia (pronounced: Joy-a ) is an American politician.  He has served on the Contra Costa County Board of Supervisors in Contra Costa County, California since 1998 and was overwhelmingly re-elected three times.  He served as chair in 2002, 2006 and 2010.  John Gioia is a Democrat.  Contra Costa Supervisory seats are non-partisan.
