Peter Sloly: Peter John M. Sloly, {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born 5 August 1966) is a former Deputy Chief of the Toronto Police Service (Divisional Policing Command and Operational Support Command 2009-2016) and was a member of the Toronto Police for 27 years.  He is also a former soccer player who earned 1 cap for the Canadian national side in 1985.  He attended McMaster University, and after retiring as a player became a police officer eventually rising to the position of deputy chief on 22 September 2009.  In 2015, he was considered a serious candidate to succeed Bill Blair as Chief of Police but was passed over in favour of Mark Saunders.
