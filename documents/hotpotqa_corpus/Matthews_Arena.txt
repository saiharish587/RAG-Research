Matthews Arena: Matthews Arena, located in Boston, Massachusetts, is a basketball and ice hockey arena.  Renovated several times, it is the oldest indoor ice hockey arena still being used for hockey — and is the oldest multi-purpose athletic building still in use in the world.  It opened in 1910 on what is now the east end of Northeastern University's campus, and is currently owned by the university.  It is the original home of the National Hockey League (NHL) Boston Bruins — the only team of the NHL's Original Six whose original home arena still exists for the sport of ice hockey at any level of competition — and the WHA New England Whalers (now the NHL Carolina Hurricanes), as well as the secondary home of the NBA Boston Celtics.
