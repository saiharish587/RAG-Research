Chellamae: Chellamae (Tamil: செல்லமே ; English: Darling ) is a 2004 Tamil film directed by Gandhi Krishna, who directed the critically acclaimed "Nila Kaalam".  The film stars Vishal, Reema Sen, Bharath, Bhanupriya and Vivek. Jyothika was initially approached for the Lead role.  The film's score and soundtrack are composed by Harris Jayaraj.  This film marks the debut of Vishal Krishna as an actor.  It was dubbed into Telugu as "Prema Chadaragam".
