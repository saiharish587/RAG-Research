Orange Is the New Black (season 3): The third season of the American comedy-drama television series "Orange Is the New Black" premiered on Netflix on June 11, 2015, at 12:00 am PST in multiple countries.  It consists of thirteen episodes, each between 53–60 minutes, with a 90-minute finale.  The series is based on Piper Kerman's memoir, "" (2010), about her experiences at FCI Danbury, a minimum-security federal prison.  The series is created and adapted for television by Jenji Kohan.
