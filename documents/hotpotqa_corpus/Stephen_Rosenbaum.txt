Stephen Rosenbaum: Stephen Rosenbaum is an American visual effects artist and supervisor, and has worked for more than 25 years on numerous movie and commercial productions, including six that have won Academy Awards.  He received two Academy Awards and two BAFTA Awards for his contributions on "Forrest Gump" and "Avatar", and has played an integral role on such pioneering films as "Jurassic Park", "", "The Abyss", "X2: X-Men United", "Death Becomes Her", "Contact" and "The Perfect Storm".
