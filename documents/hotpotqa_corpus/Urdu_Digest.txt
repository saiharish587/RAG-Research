Urdu Digest: Urdu Digest (Urdu: ‎ ) is a monthly Urdu magazine in Pakistan.  It is first digest in Pakistan and was first published in November 1960 in Lahore.  Its appearance is similar to the famous American monthly, "Reader's Digest".  Its writings present a traditional way of religious Pakistani life and covers a large variety of topics.  "Urdu Digest" is constantly performing the duty of character building and consciousness of Pakistanis for the last 53 years.  The magazine also has been serving to strengthen the ideology of Pakistan and promoting national integration.  "Urdu Digest" publishes translations of international literary stories, adventure stories, hunting stories and articles on science, technology, history, animals, education, health, positive thinking and business role models.
