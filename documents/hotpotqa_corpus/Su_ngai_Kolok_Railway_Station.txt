Su-ngai Kolok Railway Station: Su-ngai Kolok Railway Station is a railway station located in Su-ngai Kolok Subdistrict, Su-ngai Kolok District, Narathiwat.  It is a class 1 railway station located 1142.993 km from Thon Buri Railway Station.  Su-ngai Kolok Station is the furthest railway station from Bangkok, and the terminus of the Southern Line.
