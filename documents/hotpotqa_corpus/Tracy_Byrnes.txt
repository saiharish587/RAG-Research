Tracy Byrnes: Tracy Byrnes (born 1970) is an American television business news anchor, journalist, and accountant who worked for the Fox Business Network.  Byrnes appeared as a recurring panelist on Fox Business Channel stocks and investment news programs "Cashin' In", "Bulls & Bears" and "Your World with Neil Cavuto".  She formerly hosted the 1 P.M. ET weekday "FBN Live" on FoxNews.com Live.  She joined Fox Business Network as a reporter in October 2007 after being a recurring guest since 2005.  She left Fox Business Network in March 2015.
