1927 St. Louis – East St. Louis tornado: The 1927 St. Louis – East St. Louis tornado was a powerful and devastating tornado that struck St. Louis, Missouri on September 29, 1927, at 1:00pm.  The tornado is estimated to be at least a F3 or F4 on the Fujita scale.  The 2nd deadliest tornado to occur in the St. Louis metropolitan area, it caused at minimum 72–79 deaths and injured more than 550 people all within a seven-to-twelve-mile long, 100–600 yard wide path.  At one time it was the 2nd costliest tornado in US history.  More than 200 city blocks were destroyed.  It is one of four tornadoes (1871, 1896, 1927, 1959) that have torn through downtown St. Louis.
