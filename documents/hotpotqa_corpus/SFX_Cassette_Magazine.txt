SFX Cassette Magazine: SFX Cassette Magazine was a short-lived British music magazine published in the very early 1980s (not to be confused with SFX magazine, a best-selling science fiction magazine published continuously since 1995).  The distinguishing feature of SFX was its format: rather than traditional print media, the magazine was distributed in the form of a one-hour cassette.  Magazines were sold as cassettes twist-tied to an 8-1/4" x 11-3/4" cardboard backing.  The tag line of each issue: "The Only Music Magazine on C-60."
