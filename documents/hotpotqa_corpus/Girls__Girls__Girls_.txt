Girls! Girls! Girls!: Girls!  Girls!  Girls!  is a 1962 Golden Globe-nominated American musical comedy film starring Elvis Presley as a penniless Hawaiian fisherman who loves his life on the sea and dreams of owning his own boat. " Return to Sender", which reached #2 on the "Billboard" pop singles chart, is featured in the movie.  The movie opened at #6 on the "Variety" box office chart and finished the year at #31 on the year-end list of the top-grossing movies of 1962.  The movie earned $2.6 million at the box office.
