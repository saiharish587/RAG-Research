Bob Maddox: Robert "Bob" Earl Maddox (born May 2, 1949 in Frederick, Maryland) is a former professional American football defensive lineman in the National Football League.  He played college football at Frostburg State College, where he played defensive tackle and defensive end.  He then was drafted in the 7th round by the Cincinnati Bengals in the 1973 NFL Draft.  He went on to play for the Kansas City Chiefs for two years afterwards.  He was also illegally drafted by the San Francisco 49ers in the 15th round of the 1972 NFL Draft.
