Yvan Rodic: Yvan Rodic also known as "FaceHunter", is a Swiss street style-photographer who travels the world photographing people at cultural events and fashion events.  In February 2006, he began posting the results online and since then he's contributed to brands like Armani, Esprit and Volvo.  He published two books "FaceHunter" (2010)and "A Year in The Life of FaceHunter" (2013) (published by Thames & Hudson).
