The World (film): The World () is a 2004 Chinese film written and directed by Jia Zhangke.  Starring Jia's muse, Zhao Tao, as well as Chen Taisheng, "The World" was filmed on and around an actual theme park located in Beijing, Beijing World Park, which recreates world landmarks at reduced scales for Chinese tourists.  "The World" was Jia's first to gain official approval from the Chinese government.  Additionally, it was the first of his films to take place outside of his home province of Shanxi.
