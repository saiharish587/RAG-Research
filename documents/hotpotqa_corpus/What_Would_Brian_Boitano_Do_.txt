What Would Brian Boitano Do?: "What Would Brian Boitano Do?"  is a song from the 1999 film "" written by "South Park" co-creator Trey Parker and composer Marc Shaiman.  In the song Olympic and professional figure skater Brian Boitano is treated lyrically as a superhero in a series of increasingly ludicrous situations.  The title of the absurdist song is a parody of the evangelical Christian motto "What Would Jesus Do? "
