Highway 40 Blues: "Highway 40 Blues" is a song written by Larry Cordle, and recorded by American country music artist Ricky Skaggs.  It was released in March 1983 as the third single from the album "Highways & Heartaches".  "Highway 40 Blues" was Ricky Skaggs' fifth number one on the country chart and his fifth consecutive number one.  The single stayed at number one for one week and spent a total of twelve weeks on the chart.
