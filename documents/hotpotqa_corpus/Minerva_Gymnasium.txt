Minerva Gymnasium: Minerva Gymnasium (or Minervagymnasium) is an independent upper secondary school in Umeå, Sweden.  It was established in 2002 and is located in the vicinity of the University area, IKSU Sport as well as the Uminova Science Park.  The facilities used by the school was formerly office spaces for Ericsson, and is currently shared by IT consulting firm Tieto.  Admission requirements for Minerva Gymnasium involves adequate final grades (E) in Swedish, Mathematics, English as well as 9 other subjects.  The school is also approved for nationwide admission of students.
