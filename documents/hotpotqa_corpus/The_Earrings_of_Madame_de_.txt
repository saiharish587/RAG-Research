The Earrings of Madame de…: The Earrings of Madame de… (French: Madame de… ] ) is a 1953 drama film directed by Max Ophüls, adapted from Louise Leveque de Vilmorin's period novel by Ophüls, Marcel Archard and Annette Wadement.  The film is considered a masterpiece of the 1950s French cinema.  Andrew Sarris called it "the most perfect film ever made".  Ophüls said the story's construction attracted him, stating "there is always the same axis around which the action continually turns like a carousel.  A tiny, scarcely visible axis: a pair of earrings."
