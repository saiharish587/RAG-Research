Ballybeg, County Waterford: Ballybeg (Irish: "Baile Beag" ) is a largely working class district in Waterford, Ireland.  The area consists of four council housing estate - Ballybeg, Priory Lawn, Clonard Park and Ardmore Park, and a private housing estate, Glencarra, and is the largest single housing area in Waterford city, with a population of nearly 4,000 .
