Caproni Campini N.1: The Caproni Campini N.1, also known as the C.C.2, was an experimental jet aircraft built in the 1930s by Italian aircraft manufacturer Caproni.  The N.1 was powered by a motorjet, a type of jet engine in which the compressor is driven by a conventional reciprocating engine.  The N.1 first flew in 1940 and was briefly regarded as the first successful jet-powered aircraft in history, before news emerged of the German Heinkel He 178's first flight a year earlier.
