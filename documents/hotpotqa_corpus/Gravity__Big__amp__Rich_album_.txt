Gravity (Big &amp; Rich album): Gravity is the fifth studio album by American country music duo Big & Rich, and was released on September 23, 2014.  The duo announced that they had begun work on their next album in summer 2013 before releasing the album's lead off single, "Look at You," in January 2014.  In addition to releasing the single, the duo announced that they had started their own record label, Big & Rich Records, which will handle the release of this album.
