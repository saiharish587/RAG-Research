Pahiram ng Isang Ina: Pahiram ng Isang Ina (English: "Borrow A Mother") is a 2011 Philippine drama series created and developed by GMA Network, starring Bea Binene and Jake Vargas with Maxene Magalona and Carmina Villaroel (her last show before returning to ABS-CBN).  It premiered on August 15, 2011 replacing "Blusang Itim" on GMA's Dramarama Sa Hapon blocked (now Afternoon Prime) and on August 17, 2011 on GMA Pinoy TV, two days after its original premiere.
