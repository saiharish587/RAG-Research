The Voyage (roller coaster): The Voyage is a wooden roller coaster located at Holiday World & Splashin' Safari in Santa Claus, Indiana.  It opened to the public on May 6, 2006.  Designed and built by The Gravity Group with the help of designers Mike Graham, Korey Kiepert, Larry Bill, Chad Miller, and former park President Will Koch, the roller coaster is themed to the famous voyage of the "Mayflower" by Pilgrims to North America in 1620.
