Moore-Mann House: Moore-Mann House is a historic home located at Columbia, South Carolina.  It built about 1903, and is a 2 1/2-story, irregular plan, Queen Anne style frame dwelling.  It features a one-story verandah, bay windows, decorative shingles and an arched entrance.  It was designed by W. B. Smith Whaley, Co., a prominent Columbia architectural and engineering firm, whose owner also built the W. B. Smith Whaley House.
