Alien Autopsy (film): Alien Autopsy is a 2006 British comedy film with elements of science fiction, directed by Jonny Campbell.  Written by William Davies, it relates the events surrounding the famous "alien autopsy" film promoted by Ray Santilli and stars Ant McPartlin and Declan Donnelly, also known as Ant & Dec.  The film was a moderate commercial success domestically, making no. 3 on the British box office chart.
