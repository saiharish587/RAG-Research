Lawn Dogs: Lawn Dogs is a 1997 British-American fantasy-drama film directed by John Duigan and starring Mischa Barton and Sam Rockwell.  The film tells the story of a precocious young girl (Barton) from a gated community who befriends a landscape worker (Rockwell), and examines the societal repercussions of their friendship.  Written by Naomi Wallace, the film was released by Rank Organisation, and was the company's last production.
