Ran Off on da Plug Twice: "Ran Off On Da Plug Twice" (originally titled "Ritz Carlton") is a song by American hip hop recording artist Plies.  It was released on January 29, 2016 by Big Gates Records, Slip-n-Slide Records and Atlantic Records, as a single from his mixtape "Ain't No Mixtape Bih 2".  This song was produced by DTSpacely.
