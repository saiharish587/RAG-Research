6th Air Intelligence Squadron: The 6th Air Intelligence is an inactive unit of the United States Air Force.  Its last assignment was with Seventh Air Force, stationed at Osan Air Base, Korea.  It was inactivated in 1993.  The unit was originally established at Army Air Base, Colorado Springs, Colorado (later Peterson Air Force Base) as the 6th Photographic Group on 5 February 1943, under the command of Lt Waymond Davis.  Later that year, the unit was redesignated the 6th Reconnaissance Group and deployed to the southwest Pacific as a component of the Fifth Air Force.
