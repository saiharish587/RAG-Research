Tommy Noonan: Tommy Noonan (April 29, 1921 – April 24, 1968) was a comedy genre film performer, screenwriter and producer.  He acted in a number of 'A' and 'B' pictures from the 1940s through the 1960s, and he is best known for his supporting performances as Gus Esmond, wealthy fiancé of Lorelei Lee (Marilyn Monroe) in "Gentlemen Prefer Blondes" (1953), and as the musician Danny McGuire in "A Star Is Born" (1954).  He played a stock room worker in the film "Bundle of Joy" (1956) with Eddie Fisher and Debbie Reynolds.
