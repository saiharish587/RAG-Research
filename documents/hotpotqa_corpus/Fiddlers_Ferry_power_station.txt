Fiddlers Ferry power station: Fiddlers Ferry Power Station is a coal fired power station located in Warrington, Cheshire, in North West England, which is capable of co-firing biomass.  It is situated on the north bank of the River Mersey between the towns of Widnes and Warrington.  Opened in 1971, the station has a generating capacity of 1,989 megawatts (MW).  In a bid to combine efforts at the design and construction stages the Boiler and Turbo-generator plant were replicated at West Burton power station located between Retford and Gainsborough in North Nottinghamshire.
