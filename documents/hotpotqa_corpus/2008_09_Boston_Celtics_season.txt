2008–09 Boston Celtics season: The 2008–09 Boston Celtics season was the 63rd season of the Boston Celtics in the National Basketball Association (NBA), starting out as the defending NBA champions.  The Celtics started the season 27–2, which surpassed a mark set by the 1966–67 Philadelphia 76ers for the best two-loss start in NBA history.  This run also included a 19-game winning streak, which improved a Celtics franchise record set in 1981–82.  However, the Celtics lost seven out of the following nine games, and eventually finished with a 62–20 record.
