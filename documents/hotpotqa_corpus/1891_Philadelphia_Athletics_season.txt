1891 Philadelphia Athletics season: The 1891 Philadelphia Athletics season was a season in American baseball.  The team, which had played the 1890 season in the defunct Players' League, joined the American Association as a replacement for the previous version of the Philadelphia Athletics, who were expelled after the 1890 season.  They finished with a 73–66 record and fifth place in the AA.  This league folded after the 1891 season and the team disbanded with it.
