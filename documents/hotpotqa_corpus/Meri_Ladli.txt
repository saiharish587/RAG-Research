Meri Ladli: Meri Ladli (Urdu: میری لاڈلی‎ ) was 2012 Pakistani romantic drama serial, directed by Nadeem Siddique, written by Maha Malik and produced by A & B Entertainment.  The drama aired on ARY Digital and has gained a large following globally and is critically acclaimed.  It was received as one of the most watched TV serial with the highest TRP at that time.
