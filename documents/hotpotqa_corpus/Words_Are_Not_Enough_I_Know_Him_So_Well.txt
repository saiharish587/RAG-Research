Words Are Not Enough/I Know Him So Well: "Words Are Not Enough"/"I Know Him So Well" is the fifteenth single from the British group Steps, and their fifth double A-side single.  The former is a new song taken from the "" compilation, while "I Know Him So Well" appeared on the "Abbamania" compilation and later Steps' own "The Last Dance" collection.  The song "Words Are Not Enough" was slightly edited for a more radio friendly sound for the single release.  This was their last single to be released before their Boxing Day split later that same month.  The B-side was Bittersweet, which member Lisa and then-fiancé Johnny wrote together.
