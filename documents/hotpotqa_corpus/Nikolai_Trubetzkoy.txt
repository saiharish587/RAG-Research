Nikolai Trubetzkoy: Prince Nikolai Sergeyevich Trubetzkoy (also Troubetskoy; Russian: Никола́й Серге́евич Трубецко́й; Moscow, April 16, 1890 – Vienna, June 25, 1938) was a Russian linguist and historian whose teachings formed a nucleus of the Prague School of structural linguistics.  He is widely considered to be the founder of morphophonology.  He was also associated with the Russian Eurasianists.
