2000 Australian Open – Men's Singles: Yevgeny Kafelnikov was the defending champion, but he lost to Andre Agassi in the final, 3–6, 6–3, 6–2, 6–4.  This was the first time Agassi made 4 consecutive grand slam finals, if not for his loss to Pete Sampras in the 1999 Wimbledon final, Agassi would have won a non-calendar grand slam.
