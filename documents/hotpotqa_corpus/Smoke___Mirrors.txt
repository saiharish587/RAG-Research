Smoke + Mirrors: Smoke + Mirrors is the second studio album by American rock band Imagine Dragons.  The album was recorded during 2014 at the band's home studio in Las Vegas, Nevada.  Self-produced by members of the band along with English hip-hop producer Alexander Grant, known by his moniker Alex da Kid, the album was released by Interscope Records and Grant's KIDinaKORNER label on February 17, 2015, in the United States.
