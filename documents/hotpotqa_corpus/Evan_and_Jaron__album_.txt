Evan and Jaron (album): Evan and Jaron is Evan and Jaron's second album, released on September 5, 2000.  It contained the hit singles "Crazy for This Girl", which peaked at #15 in the U.S. charts, "From My Head to My Heart" (which was previously on the soundtrack to "Runaway Bride"), and "The Distance" (which would later be on the "Serendipity" soundtrack.)
