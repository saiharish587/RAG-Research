Barkley Inc.: Barkley (formerly Barkley Evergreen & Partners) is a Kansas City, Missouri, United States, based full-service advertising agency known for their work on Sonic Drive-In Restaurants.  Founded in 1964, they employ 400+ people; 2008 capitalized billings were $485 million.  Barkley is the largest independent advertising agency in Kansas City, and the seventh largest independent advertising agency in the U.S.
