Type 5 Chi-Ri: The Type 5 medium tank Chi-Ri (五式中戦車 チリ , Go-shiki chusensha Chi-ri ) ("Imperial Year 2605 Medium Tank Model 9") was the ultimate medium tank developed by the Imperial Japanese Army in World War II.  Intended to be a heavier, more powerful version of Japan's sophisticated Type 4 Chi-To medium tank, in performance it was designed to surpass the US M4 Sherman medium tanks being fielded by the Allied forces.  A single prototype was incomplete when the war ended.
