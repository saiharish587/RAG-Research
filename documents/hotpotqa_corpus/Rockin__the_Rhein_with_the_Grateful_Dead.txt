Rockin' the Rhein with the Grateful Dead: Rockin' the Rhein with the Grateful Dead is a Grateful Dead triple live album released in 2004.  It was recorded April 24, 1972, at "Rheinhallen" (Halle Sechs der Messe Düsseldorf), in the German town of Düsseldorf, during the band's famous European tour of 1972.  The complete concert is included, but the order of the tracks on the CD was altered to fit the show on three discs, while preserving groups of segued tracks.
