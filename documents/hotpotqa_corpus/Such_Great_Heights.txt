Such Great Heights: "Such Great Heights" is a song by American indie pop band The Postal Service.  It was released as the lead single from their debut studio album, "Give Up", on January 21, 2003 through Sub Pop Records.  The single includes a previously unreleased track, "There's Never Enough Time", and two cover tracks by The Shins and Iron & Wine of "We Will Become Silhouettes" and "Such Great Heights", respectively.
