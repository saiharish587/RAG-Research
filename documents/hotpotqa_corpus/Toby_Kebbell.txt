Toby Kebbell: Tobias Alistair Patrick "Toby" Kebbell (born 9 July 1982) is an English stage and film actor.  He is known for his roles in films such as "Dead Man's Shoes" (2004), "RocknRolla" (2008), "" (2010), "War Horse" (2011), "Wrath of the Titans" (2012), "Dawn of the Planet of the Apes" (2014), "Fantastic Four" (2015), "Warcraft" (2016), "A Monster Calls" (2016), and "Gold" (2016).  He is also known for his work in the "Black Mirror" episode, "The Entire History of You".
