On Purpose (song): "On Purpose" is a song by American singer Sabrina Carpenter.  It was released as the lead single from her second studio album, "Evolution" (2016), on July 29, 2016 by Hollywood Records.  The song was produced by Ido Zmishlany and written by Zmishlany and Carpenter.  It is a pop and tropical house song and lyrically talks about a relationship that wasn't supposed to happen.
