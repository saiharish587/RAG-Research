Slapsgiving 3: Slappointment in Slapmarra: "Slapsgiving 3: Slappointment in Slapmarra " is the fourteenth episode of the ninth season of the CBS sitcom "How I Met Your Mother", and the 198th episode overall.  The episode's name is a reference to "The Appointment in Samarra," a fable by W. Somerset Maugham based on an ancient Mesopotamian story about the inevitability of death.
