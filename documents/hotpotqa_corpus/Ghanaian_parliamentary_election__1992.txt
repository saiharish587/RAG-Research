Ghanaian parliamentary election, 1992: The Ghanaian parliamentary election was held on 29 December 1992.  This were the first parliamentary elections since the 1979 election, 13 years earlier.  Presidential elections were held earlier on 3 November 1992.  Only 28.1% of the registered voters turned out for the parliamentary elections.  The preceding presidential election was considered to have been conducted in a free and fair manner by international observers.  The opposition parties however claimed the election was fraudulent and boycotted this parliamentary election.
