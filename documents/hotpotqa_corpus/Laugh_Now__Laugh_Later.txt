Laugh Now, Laugh Later: Laugh Now, Laugh Later is the seventh studio album by American punk rock band Face to Face, which was released on May 17, 2011 through Antagonist Records, a label run by the band's lead singer and guitarist Trever Keith.  It marks 9 years since the release of Face to Face's previous studio album "How to Ruin Everything" in 2002.  It is their first release with second guitarist Chad Yaro in 11 years, since 2000's "Reactionary".
