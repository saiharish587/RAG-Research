Svein Erik Brodal: Svein Erik Brodal (born 21 February 1939 in Østre Toten, Oppland) is a Norwegian actor, theatre director, poet, novelist and politician.  He made his stage debut at Det Norske Teatret in 1960, and served as theatre director from 1979 to 1990.  He was a deputy representative to the Storting from 1997 to 2001.
