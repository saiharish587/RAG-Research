A God in Ruins (novel): A God in Ruins the ninth novel by Kate Atkinson was published in 2015.  The main character, Teddy Todd is the younger brother of Ursula Todd, the protagonist in Atkinson's 2013 novel, Life After Life.  Atkinson calls it the "companion piece" rather than a sequel to the earlier novel.  Both books are about World War II.  It won the Costa Book Award for Novel in 2015.
