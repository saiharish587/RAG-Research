Post-80s: The Post-'80 (also the Post-1980, ) is a colloquial term which refers to the generation, especially in urban cities, whose members were born between 1980 and 1989 in Mainland China after the introduction of the One-child policy.  This generation, the first to grow up entirely within the reformist era, currently ranges in age from 27 to 37 , making up a major portion of China's young adult demographic.
