Zencap: Zencap is a German company, operating a peer-to-peer lending platform, which allows private savers to lend money directly to small and medium sized businesses in Germany, Spain, and the Netherlands.  Using the crowdfunding model, the company based in Berlin, Germany aims to create a direct link between private lenders and small businesses seeking loans.  As of December 2014, Zencap had facilitated 5 million Euro in loans to small and medium sized firms.
