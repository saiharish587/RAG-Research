Night of the Living Dead 3D: Night of the Living Dead 3D or Night of the Living DE3D is a 2006 horror film made in 3D.  It is the second remake of the 1968 horror classic "Night of the Living Dead".  The first remake was released in 1990 and was directed by Tom Savini from a revised screenplay by George A. Romero.  Unlike the first remake, no one involved with the original is involved with this version.  The original film was never properly copyrighted, and so it has fallen into the public domain, making this remake possible with no permission from the original's creators (the original movie can actually be seen playing on TV in this version).
