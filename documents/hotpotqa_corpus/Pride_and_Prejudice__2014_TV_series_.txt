Pride and Prejudice (2014 TV series): Pride and Prejudice (Hangul: 오만과 편견 ; Hanja: 傲慢과 偏見 ; RR: "Omangwa Pyeongyeon " ) is a 2014 South Korean television series starring Choi Jin-hyuk, Baek Jin-hee, Choi Min-soo, Lee Tae-hwan and Son Chang-min.  It aired on MBC from October 27, 2014 to January 13, 2015 on Mondays and Tuesdays at 22:00 for 21 episodes.
