Church Minshull: Church Minshull is a village and civil parish in the unitary authority of Cheshire East and the ceremonial county of Cheshire, England.  The village is located approximately 5 mi north west of Crewe and to the west of the River Weaver and the Shropshire Union Canal.  The principal road through Church Minshull is the B5074 between Nantwich (6 miles to the south) and Winsford (4 miles to the north).  The modern village centre is a designated conservation area which contains many houses of Tudor style architecture.  A large area in the east of the parish falls within the Weaver Valley Area of Special County Value.
