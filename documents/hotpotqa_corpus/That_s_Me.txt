That's Me: "That's Me", originally "Coachman's Farm", is a song recorded by the Swedish pop group ABBA in 1976.  It was released as a single in Japan the following year (with "Money, Money, Money" as the B-side) to promote the band's "Arrival" album, and reached number 75 on the official Japanese charts.  Elsewhere, "That's Me" was used as the B-side to ABBA's hit single, "Dancing Queen".
