Hitler's Table Talk: "Hitler's Table Talk" (German: "Tischgespräche im Führerhauptquartier") is the title given to a series of World War II monologues delivered by Adolf Hitler, which were transcribed from 1941 to 1944.  Hitler's remarks were recorded by Heinrich Heim, Henry Picker, and Martin Bormann, and later published by different editors, under different titles, in three different languages.
