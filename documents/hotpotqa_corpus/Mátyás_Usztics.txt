Mátyás Usztics: Mátyás Usztics (9 April 1949 – 29 April 2017) was a Hungarian stage and film actor, most notable for his role of Sgt. János Karádi in the Hungarian television show "Angyalbőrben".  As a regular voice actor too, he was the Hungarian dubbing voice of Roger Rabbit in "Who Framed Roger Rabbit" and Gopher in "The Many Adventures of Winnie the Pooh".  He served as founding director of the National Chamber Theatre from 2003 until his death.
