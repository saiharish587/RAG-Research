Lovisa Simson: Lovisa Concordia Simson, née "Kliecnik" or "Lindström" (1746 – 26 February 1808, Gothenburg), was a Swedish theater director.  She was the managing director of the theater "Comediehuset" in Gothenburg between 1787 and 1792.  She was the first female theater director over a permanent theater (rather than a travelling theater company) in her country.
