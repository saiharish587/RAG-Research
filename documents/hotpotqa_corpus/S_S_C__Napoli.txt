S.S.C. Napoli: Società Sportiva Calcio Napoli, commonly referred to as Napoli (] ), is a professional Italian football club based in Naples, Campania.  Formed in 1926, the club plays in Serie A, the top flight of Italian football.  The club has won Serie A twice, and been runners-up six times, the Coppa Italia five times, the Supercoppa Italiana twice, and the 1988–89 UEFA Cup.
