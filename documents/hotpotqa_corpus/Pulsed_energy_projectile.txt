Pulsed energy projectile: Pulsed energy projectile or PEP is a technology of non-lethal weaponry currently under development by the U.S. military.  It involves the emission of an invisible laser pulse which, upon contact with the target, ablates the surface and creates a small amount of exploding plasma.  This produces a pressure wave that stuns the target and knocks him off his feet, and electromagnetic radiation that affects nerve cells causing a painful sensation.  The technology can also be used as a lethal weapon, and indeed an early name was "pulsed impulsive kill laser".
