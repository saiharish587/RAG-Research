Bridgeport, Connecticut: Bridgeport is a seaport city in the U.S. state of Connecticut.  It is the largest city in the state and is located in Fairfield County at the mouth of the Pequonnock River on Long Island Sound.  Bridgeport had a population of 144,229 during the 2010 Census, making it also the 5th-most populous in New England.  It is bordered by the towns of Trumbull to the north, Fairfield to the west, and Stratford to the east.  The Greater Bridgeport area is the 48th-largest urban area in the United States.  Located 60 miles from Manhattan and 40 miles from the Bronx, Bridgeport is also part of the New York metropolitan area.
