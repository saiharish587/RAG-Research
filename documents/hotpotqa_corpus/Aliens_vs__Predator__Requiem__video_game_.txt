Aliens vs. Predator: Requiem (video game): Aliens vs. Predator: Requiem is an action game exclusive to the PlayStation Portable, developed by Rebellion Developments and published by Sierra Entertainment.  It was released in November 2007 in North America and Europe, and December 2007 in Australia.  It is part of the "Alien vs. Predator" franchise, an amalgamation of the "Alien" and "Predator" franchises.
