Horpa language: Horpa (Chinese: 道孚语 "Daofu", 爾龔語 "Ergong"), also named Stau, Daofuhua, Bawang, Bopa, Danba, Dawu, Geshitsa, Geshiza, Geshizahua, Hor, Huo’er, Hórsók, Nyagrong-Minyag, Pawang, Rgu, Western Gyarong, Western Jiarong, Xinlong-Muya, rTau"," is one of several closely related Rgyalrongic languages of China.  Horpa is better understood as a cluster of closely related yet unintelligible dialect groups/languages closely related to Horpa Shangzhai or Stodsde skad.  The term "Stodsde skad" is a Tibetan name meaning "language of the people of the far Northwest".
