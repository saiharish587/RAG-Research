Stacy Edwards: Stacy Edwards (born March 4, 1965) is an American actress.  She appeared in a number of B movies before her breakthrough role in the 1997 black comedy film "In the Company of Men", for which she received Independent Spirit Award for Best Female Lead nomination.  Edwards later had roles in films such as "Primary Colors" (1998), "Black and White" (1999) and "Driven" (2001), and was regular cast member in the CBS medical drama series, "Chicago Hope" (1997–1999).
