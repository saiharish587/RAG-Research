Joe Cohn: Joseph Mark "Joe" Cohn (born December 28, 1956, Flushing, New York) is an American jazz guitarist.  His father is Al Cohn.  Joe's daughter Shaye Cohn, is a musician who plays cornet with her band Tuba Skinny in New Orleans and at jazz festivals in Italy and Australia and elsewhere.  Shaye also plays accordion, violin and piano.  Joe studied at Berklee College of Music, and has played with Bob Mover, Nick Brignola, Freddy Cole, Buddy DeFranco, Dick Johnson, Al Grey, Carmen Leggio, and Duffy Jackson.
