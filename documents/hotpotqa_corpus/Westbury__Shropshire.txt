Westbury, Shropshire: Westbury is a village and parish in Shropshire, England.  It includes the settlements of Caus Forest, Lake, Marche, Newtown, Stoney Stretton, Vennington, Wallop, Westbury, Whitton, Winsley and Yockleton.  It lies 8 miles west of the town of Shrewsbury, very close to the Wales-England border.  It is located at 135m altitude.  It had a population of 1,352 according to the 2011 census.  In 2005, Westbury parish expanded with the annexation of half of the former Wollaston parish.
