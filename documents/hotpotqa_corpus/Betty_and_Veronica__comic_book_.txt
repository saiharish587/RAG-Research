Betty and Veronica (comic book): Betty and Veronica (also known as Archie's Girls Betty and Veronica) is an ongoing comic book series published by Archie Comics focusing on "best friends and worst enemies" Betty Cooper and Veronica Lodge.  Betty first appeared in "Pep Comics" #22 while Veronica made her debut a few months later, in "Pep" #26, as an immediate rival to Betty for Archie's affections.  Together the pair form the female part of the classic love triangle which has become a staple of the comic series since 1942.
