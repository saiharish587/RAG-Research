Riccardo Tolentino: Riccardo Tolentino was an Italian actor and film director of the silent era.  He directed the 1917 Pushkin adaptation "Wanda Warenine".  In 1918, the same year of J. Searle Dawley's "Uncle Tom's Cabin", he directed the Italian version "La capanna dello zio Tom", with Paola Pezzaglia.
