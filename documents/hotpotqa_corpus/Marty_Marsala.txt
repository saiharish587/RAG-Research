Marty Marsala: Marty Marsala (2 April 1909 – 27 April 1975) was an American jazz trumpeter born in Chicago, perhaps best known for working from 1926-1946 with his brother Joe Marsala in a big band in New York City and Chicago.  He had also toured with various artists, such as Chico Marx and Miff Mole, to name a few.  During the 1940s Marsala was a celebrated West Coast jazz trumpeter, commuting back and forth from Chicago to San Francisco frequently.  In various club settings Marsala shared stages with Earl Hines and Sidney Bechet.
