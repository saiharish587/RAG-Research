Joseph Pulitzer Jr.: Joseph Pulitzer III (May 13, 1913 – May 26, 1993) grandson of the famous newsman Joseph Pulitzer, was himself publisher of the "St. Louis Post-Dispatch" for 38 years and one of the most famous newsmen of the day.  For 31 years he chaired the board which was responsible for awarding the Pulitzer Prize, and from 1955 -1993 he was chairman of the Pulitzer Publishing Company.
