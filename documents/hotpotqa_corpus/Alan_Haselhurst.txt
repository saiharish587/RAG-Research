Alan Haselhurst: Sir Alan Gordon Barraclough Haselhurst {'1': ", '2': ", '3': ", '4': "} (born 23 June 1937) is a British Conservative politician who served as Member of Parliament (MP) for Saffron Walden from 1977 to 2017, having represented Middleton and Prestwich as MP from 1970 to 1974.  He was Chairman of Ways and Means from 14 May 1997 to 8 June 2010, and later Chairman of the Commonwealth Parliamentary Association between 2011 and 2014.  He was the oldest Conservative MP during his last Parliament, and stood down at the 2017 general election.
