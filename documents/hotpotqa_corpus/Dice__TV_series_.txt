Dice (TV series): Dice is an American comedy television series created by Scot Armstrong.  The series stars Andrew Dice Clay as himself.  On March 20, 2015, Showtime ordered a six episode first season.  The series premiered on April 10, 2016, on Showtime.  The pilot was made available on April 1, 2016, through Amazon, Apple, YouTube, Hulu, Roku, PlayStation Vue and other streaming platforms and all six episodes were made available on April 10, 2016, via its streaming services and on-demand.  On September 22, 2016, Showtime renewed "Dice" for a 7-episode second season, which premiered on August 20, 2017.
