Milton (opera): Milton is an opéra comique in one act by Gaspare Spontini.  The French libretto, by Victor-Joseph Étienne de Jouy and Armand-Michel Dieulafoy, is based on the life of the English poet John Milton.  "Milton" was first performed on 27 November 1804 by the Opéra-Comique at the Salle Feydeau in Paris .  It was Spontini's first major success in France.  The composer planned a reworked version for performances in Germany, under the title "Das verlorene Paradies" ("Paradise Lost"), but in the event it was never staged.
