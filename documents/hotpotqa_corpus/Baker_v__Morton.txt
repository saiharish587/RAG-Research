Baker v. Morton: Baker v. Morton, 79 U.S. 150 (1870) , was the first "serious" court case to come out of Omaha, Nebraska Territory, prior to statehood.  In the trial a claim jumper fought against local land barons to stake out a homestead in the area that was to become the city of Omaha.  The case was important for establishing homesteaders rights and ensuring the future growth of Omaha would benefit everyone, not only wealthy landowners.
