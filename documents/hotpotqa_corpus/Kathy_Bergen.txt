Kathy Bergen: Kathy Bergen (born December 24, 1939) is an American Masters athletics track and field athlete.  She is the current world record holder in the W70 100 meters and the high jump.  She also holds the Indoor World records for the W65 high jump, the W70 60 meters, 200 meters and high jump.  And she holds the American record for the W70 200 meters and the W65 high jump.  She is the oldest woman to break the 15 second barrier in the 100 meters and to break 32 seconds in the 200 meters.
