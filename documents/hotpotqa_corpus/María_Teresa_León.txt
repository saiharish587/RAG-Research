María Teresa León: María Teresa León (31 October 1903 – 13 December 1988) was a Spanish writer, activist and cultural ambassador.  Born in Logroño, she was the niece of the Spanish feminist and writer María Goyri (the wife of Ramón Menéndez Pidal).  She herself was married to the Spanish poet Rafael Alberti.  She contributed numerous articles to the periodical "Diario de Burgos" and published the children's books "Cuentos para soñar" and "La bella del mal amor".
