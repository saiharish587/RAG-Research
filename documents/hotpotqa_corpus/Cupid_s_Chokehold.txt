Cupid's Chokehold: "Cupid's Chokehold" is a single performed by Gym Class Heroes, featuring Patrick Stump of Fall Out Boy.  The song relies heavily on the music and chorus from Supertramp's hit song "Breakfast in America" written by Roger Hodgson.  It peaked at #4 on the "Billboard" Hot 100, #1 on the "Billboard" Mainstream Top 40 radio chart, #3 on the UK Singles Chart, and #3 on the Canadian Hot 100.
