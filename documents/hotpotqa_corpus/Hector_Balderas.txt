Hector Balderas: Hector Hugo Balderas Jr. (born August 16, 1973) is an American attorney, former prosecutor, and politician who is currently serving as the New Mexico Attorney General.  In 2006 Balderas became the youngest statewide Hispanic elected official in the nation when he won his first race for State Auditor at the age of 33.  Before that Balderas served as a State Representative in the New Mexico Legislature from 2004 to 2006.  Balderas also serves as the elected treasurer of the National Association of Latino Elected Officials.
