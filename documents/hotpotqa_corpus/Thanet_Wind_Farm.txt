Thanet Wind Farm: The Thanet Wind Farm (also sometimes called Thanet Offshore Wind Farm) is an offshore wind farm 7 mi off the coast of Thanet district in Kent, England.  On commisioning it was the world's largest offshore wind farm.  It has a nameplate capacity (maximum output) of 300 MW and it cost £780–900 million (US$1.2–1.4 billion).  Thanet is one of fifteen Round 2 wind projects announced by the Crown Estate in January 2004 but the first to be developed.  It was officially opened on 23 September 2010, when it overtook Horns Rev 2 as the biggest offshore wind farm in the world.  It has since been overtaken by many others (medio 2017 it ranks 14th).
