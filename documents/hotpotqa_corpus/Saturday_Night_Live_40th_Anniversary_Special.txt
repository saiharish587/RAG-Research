Saturday Night Live 40th Anniversary Special: The Saturday Night Live 40th Anniversary Special is a three-and-a-half-hour prime-time special that aired on Sunday, February 15, 2015, on NBC, celebrating "Saturday Night Live"' s 40th year on the air, having premiered on October 11, 1975, under the original title "NBC's Saturday Night".  This special generated 23.1 million viewers, becoming NBC's most-watched prime-time, non-sports, entertainment telecast (excluding Super Bowl lead-outs) since the "Friends" series finale in 2004.  It is the third such anniversary special to be broadcast, with celebratory episodes also held during the 15th and 25th seasons.
