Tyler Asemota: Asemota currently attends Notre Dame High School and has been a student there for all four years.  Living in Reseda, California most of his life, Asemota looked up to many high profile athletes from Notre Dame High School, such as Koa Farmer, Khalfani Muhammed, and notable alumni Giancarlo Stanton.  Asemota found late interest in football during his sophomore year when a football coach approached him during a track meet because of his outstanding blend of height and speed.  The same coach, Calvin Taylor, then had Asemota playing wide receiver and free safety for him that season in 2014.
