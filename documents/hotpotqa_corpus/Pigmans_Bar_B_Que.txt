Pigmans Bar-B-Que: Pigman's Bar-B-Que is an Outer Banks landmark as well as a premiere BBQ restaurant on the Outer Banks of North Carolina.  It started as a small ham shop on the Outer Banks in the late 1980s, and has since gone on to win multiple awards like "Best on the Beach."  It has been featured in such publications as "Southern Living" magazine and "The Coast".  According to the jingle it is on the "9 & 1/2 mile post."
