Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park: Glenwood Caverns Adventure Park is an adventure park located above Glenwood Springs, Colorado, about 160 miles west of Denver.  Prior to 2003, only cave tours were available until a major expansion took place.  The park is unique because it sits at an altitude of 7,100 feet on a mountain above Glenwood.  Today, the park features numerous attractions in addition to the cave tours.
