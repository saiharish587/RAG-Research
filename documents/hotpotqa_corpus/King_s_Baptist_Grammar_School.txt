King's Baptist Grammar School: King's Baptist Grammar School is in Wynn Vale, a north eastern suburb of Adelaide, South Australia, having moved from nearby Tea Tree Gully where it was known as "Tea Tree Gully Christian School".  The school is known throughout the region for numerous outstanding accolades.  The school is also recognized for its annual musical production, held at the Shedley Theater in Elizabeth.  In 2013, 950 students were enrolled at the school.
