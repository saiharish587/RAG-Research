John Choma (American football): John Gregory Choma (born February 9, 1955) is a former American football offensive linemen who played three seasons with the San Francisco 49ers of the National Football League.  He was drafted by the San Diego Chargers in the fifth round of the 1978 NFL Draft but later released.  He played college football at the University of Virginia and attended Normandy High School in Parma, Ohio.  He was a member of the San Francisco 49ers team that won Super Bowl XVI.
