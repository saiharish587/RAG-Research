Garden of Cultivation: The Garden of Cultivation (; Suzhou Wu: Nyi pu, ] ) located at No.5 Wenya Nong (文衙弄5号) in Suzhou city, of Jiangsu Province, China.  It is one of the best preserved examples of a Ming Dynasty classical garden in Suzhou.  It is part of the UNESCO World Heritage Site "Classical Gardens of Suzhou" on the World Heritage List.  "Due to its special history, this Garden was virtually unknown before it was listed as a UN World Cultural Heritage site."
