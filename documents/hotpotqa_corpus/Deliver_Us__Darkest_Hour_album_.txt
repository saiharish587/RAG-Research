Deliver Us (Darkest Hour album): Deliver Us is the fifth studio album by American melodic death metal band Darkest Hour.  It was released on July 10, 2007 through Victory Records.  The album debuted at number 110 on the Billboard album charts, with sales of 6,600.  "Sanctuary" is featured in the video game, "Tony Hawk's Proving Ground", while "Demon(s)" is featured in "Guitar Hero 5" and "Doomsayer" was made available in "" through the "Darkest Hour Track Pack", the last to be released by Activision.  The artwork was created by John Baizley who has also done art for bands such as Pig Destroyer, Daughters, and his own band Baroness.
