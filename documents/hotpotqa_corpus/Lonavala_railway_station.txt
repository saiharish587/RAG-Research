Lonavala railway station: Lonavala station (Marathi: लोणावळा रेल्वे स्थानक ) is a train station in Lonavla town, a hill station in the state of Maharashtra in India.  Lonavala station is the origin of Lonavala – Pune Suburban Trains.  17 suburban trains operate on the Pune – Lonavala route.  Lonavla is also a halt for Mumbai – Pune Express and Mail trains.  The Karjat – Pune passenger train also has a halt at Lonavala.  Trains traveling on the Kalyan – Pune route also halt at Lonavala.  This station leads access to Lonavala town and nearby areas like Karla Caves, Bhaja Caves, Lohagad, Visapur Fort, Bhushi Dam and Bor Ghat (Khandala Ghat).  Khandala hill station is just 8 km from Lonavla.
