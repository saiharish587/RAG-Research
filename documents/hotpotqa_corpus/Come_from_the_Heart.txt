Come from the Heart: "Come from the Heart" is a country music song written by Richard Leigh and Susanna Clark and published in 1987.  It is most known through the 1989 single by Kathy Mattea, released in conjunction with her album "Willow in the Wind", though the song was first recorded and released on the 1987 Don Williams album "Traces" and also released in 1988 by Clark's husband on his album "Old Friends".
