Uttarakhand Gramin Bank: Uttarakhand Gramin Bank is a regional rural bank in India. It is sponsored by State Bank of India established under RRB Act 1976 came into existence on 1st Nov 2012 after amalgamation of erstwhile RRB viz.  Uttaranachal Gramin Bank and Nainital Almora Kshetriya Gramin Bank in Uttarakhand State.The Bank is headquartered at Dehradun and presently its area of operation in all 13 Districts in Uttarakhand having 286 branches, 11 satellite offices ,2 extension counters, 4 regional offices.
