Colpothrinax: Colpothrinax is a genus of palms native to Central America and the Caribbean.  "Colpothrinax aphanopetala" is found in southern Central America (from southeast Nicaragua to Panama), while "Colpothrinax cookii" is found in northern Central America (from Belize to Honduras).  The third species, "Colpothrinax wrightii", is endemic to southwest Cuba including the Isle of Youth.
