Jonathan Tisch: Jonathan Tisch is Chairman Emeritus of the "United States Travel Association" (U.S. Travel), a travel industry lobbying group, a trustee of Tufts University, and a board member of the Tribeca Film Institute.  He is a co-owner, a member of the board of directors, and treasurer of the New York Football Giants.  He served as co-chairman of the 2014 NY/NJ Super Bowl Host Committee.
