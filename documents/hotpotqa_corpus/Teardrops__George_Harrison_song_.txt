Teardrops (George Harrison song): "Teardrops" is a single by George Harrison, released on 20 July 1981 in the United States and 31 July in the UK.  It was the second single from the album "Somewhere in England" and reached number 51 on the US "Billboard" Top Tracks chart and number 102 on the "Billboard" Bubbling Under the Hot 100 chart.  In Japan, it was released on 25 September.
