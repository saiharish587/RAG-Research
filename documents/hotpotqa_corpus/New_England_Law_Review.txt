New England Law Review: The New England Law Review ("Bluebook" abbreviation: "New Eng.  L. Rev.") is a law review that was established in 1965 as the Portia Law Journal.  It obtained its current name when Portia Law School changed its name to New England School of Law in 1969.  It is run by students and currently publishes four issues annually.  The review also conducts Fall and Spring symposiums.  The current editor-in-chief is Michael Martucci.
