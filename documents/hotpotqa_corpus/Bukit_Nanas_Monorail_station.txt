Bukit Nanas Monorail station: Bukit Nanas Monorail station is a Malaysian elevated monorail train station that serves as a part of the Kuala Lumpur Monorail (KL Monorail), located in Kuala Lumpur and opened alongside the rest of the train service on August 31, 2003.  This station was formerly called P. Ramlee Monorail station, which was named after the late P. Ramlee, who was a Malay celebrity in Malaysia.
