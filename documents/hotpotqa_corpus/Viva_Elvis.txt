Viva Elvis: Viva Elvis was the seventh resident Cirque du Soleil show on the Las Vegas Strip.  It resided at the Aria Resort & Casino and premiered on February 19, 2010.  The show closed on August 31, 2012.  Cirque du Soleil partnered with Elvis Presley Enterprises to produce this show, similar to how they partnered with The Beatles' Apple Corps Ltd to produce the resident show "Love" at the Mirage.
