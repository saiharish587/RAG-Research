Temugin: Temugin is a fictional character from Marvel Comics.  He is the estranged son of Iron Man's archenemy, the Mandarin.  He first appeared in "Iron Man (volume 3)" #53 (2002).  Temugin is named after his ancestor Genghis Khan, whose real name was Temujin (also spelled Temuchin and Temudjin; u also variates to ü).
