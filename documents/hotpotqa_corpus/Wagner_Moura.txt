Wagner Moura: Wagner Maniçoba de Moura (] ; born 27 June 1976) is a Brazilian film, television and stage actor.  Besides being a well-established and acclaimed actor in Brazil, he has achieved widespread international fame for his portrayal of Captain (later Colonel) Roberto Nascimento, protagonist of the highly successful film "Elite Squad" (2007) and its sequel, "" (2010).  He also gained recognition globally playing "Spider" in the sci-fi film "Elysium" (2013).  He played the lead role of Pablo Escobar in the Netflix series "Narcos" from 2015 to 2016, for which he received a Golden Globe nomination.
