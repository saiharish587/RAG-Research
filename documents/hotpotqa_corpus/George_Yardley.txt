George Yardley: George Harry Yardley III (November 3, 1928 – August 13, 2004) was an American basketball player.  He was the first player in NBA history to score 2,000 points in one season, breaking the 1,932-point record held by George Mikan.  Yardley was enshrined in the Naismith Memorial Basketball Hall of Fame in 1996.
