O'Chi Brown: O'Chi Brown (born Doris Gubbins) is an English singer and songwriter born in Tottenham, London, England.  She scored two hits on the Dance Club Songs, the most successful being "Whenever You Need Somebody," which hit number one in 1986.  The song's producers (Stock Aitken Waterman) would recycle the song for English singer Rick Astley a year later, and it would be the title of his debut studio album on PWL.
