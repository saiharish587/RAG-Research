Freedom Ring: Freedom Ring (real name Curtis Doyle) is a fictional superhero appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics.  The character was created by writer Robert Kirkman.  Curtis first appeared in "Marvel Team-Up" vol.  3 #20 (July 2006), becoming Freedom Ring in the next issue.  He appeared across the series' storyline "Freedom Ring" for five issues.  The character is depicted as a normal civilian who comes across a ring that grants him the ability to alter reality.
