Sten Forshufvud: Sten Gabriel Bernhard Forshufvud (9 February 1903 – 25 June 1985) was a Swedish dentist and physician, and amateur toxicologist (expert on poisons) who formulated and supported the controversial theory that Napoleon was assassinated by a member of his entourage while in exile.  He wrote a book, in Swedish, about this in 1961, which was translated the following year as "Who Killed Napoleon?"  He later published his ideas in English, in the 1983 book "Assassination At St. Helena: The Poisoning Of Napoleon Bonaparte," a book on whose authorship Ben Weider, co-author (with David Hapgood) of the book "The Murder Of Napoleon," published the year earlier, which also advanced Forshufvud's ideas, collaborated.
