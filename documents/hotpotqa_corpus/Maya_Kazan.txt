Maya Kazan: Maya Kazan (born November 24, 1986) is an American stage, TV and film actress and director.  She is known for playing Caroline in "Frances Ha", Eleanor Gallinger on "The Knick" and Mabel Thompson on "Boardwalk Empire".  She is the daughter of screenwriters Nicholas Kazan and Robin Swicord.  She is the granddaughter of film director Elia Kazan and playwright Molly Kazan.  Maya is the younger sister of actress Zoe Kazan.
