Hip Hop Weekly: Hip Hop Weekly Magazine is an American hip hop news and entertainment magazine founded in 2006.  The magazine covers celebrity news, music, film, fashion, sports and features exclusive interviews with many notable figures within popular culture.  Cynthia Horner (former Editor-in-Chief of Right On!  Magazine) serves as Editor-in-Chief.  Published every two weeks, the magazine offers the latest news to millions of young Americans who identify with the hip hop culture.
