Graham Lakes (Minnesota): The Graham Lakes are a pair of lakes located in the northeastern corner of Nobles County, Minnesota.  The lakes are known as West Graham Lake and East Graham Lake.  West Graham Lake is an oval shaped body of water that extends east-to-west slightly more than one mile (1.6 km).  The north-to-south width of the lake is approximately 3/4 of a mile.  The area of West Graham Lake is 519.28 acre , the average depth is 5 ft , and the maximum depth is 8 ft .  The elevation of West Graham Lake is 1438.7 ft , or 438.52 meters.  East Graham Lake is a longer, narrower lake that extends from northeast-to-southwest for 1 miles.  Its width is less than 1/2 mile.  The area of East Graham Lake is 511.32 acre , the average depth is 5 ft , and the maximum depth is 8 ft .  The elevation of East Graham Lake is 1435.08 ft , or 437.41 meters.  A small creek runs from West Graham Lake and empties into East Graham Lake.  Another creek runs from East Graham Lake, and eventually flows into the Des Moines River.
