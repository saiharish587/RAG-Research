Benny Lautrup: Benny Lautrup (born June 25, 1939) is a professor in theoretical physics at the Niels Bohr Institute at the University of Copenhagen.  He has during his career worked at Nordic Institute for Theoretical Physics (Denmark), Brookhaven National Laboratory (USA), CERN (Switzerland), og Institut des Hautes Études Scientifiques (France).  He is known for his part in the Nakanishi-Lautrup formalism, a concept in relativistic quantum field theory.
