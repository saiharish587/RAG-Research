Todd Lowber: Todd Lowber (born January 26, 1982) is a former gridiron football wide receiver.  He most recently played for the Toronto Argonauts of the Canadian Football League (CFL).  He was signed by the Minnesota Vikings as a undrafted free agent in 2007.  He has also been a member of the New York Giants, Dallas Cowboys, and Miami Dolphins.  He earned a Super Bowl ring as a member of the Giants' practice squad in Super Bowl XLII.  He did not play college football but is a former college basketball player and high jump champion.
