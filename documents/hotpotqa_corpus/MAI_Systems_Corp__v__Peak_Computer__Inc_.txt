MAI Systems Corp. v. Peak Computer, Inc.: MAI Systems Corp. v. Peak Computer, Inc., 991 F.2d 511 (9th Cir.  1993), was a case heard by the United States Court of Appeals for the Ninth Circuit which addressed the issue of whether the loading of software programs into Random-Access Memory (RAM) by a computer repair technician during maintenance constituted an unauthorized software copy and therefore a copyright violation.  The court held that it did, although the United States Congress subsequently enacted an amendment to to specifically overrule this holding in the circumstances of computer repair.
