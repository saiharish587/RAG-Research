Grandmasters (album): Grandmasters is a collaborative album by DJ Muggs and GZA.  The album was released on October 25, 2005 on Angeles Records.  The album is the first in the "DJ Muggs vs. " series, followed by his 2007 collaboration with Sick Jacken, "Legend of the Mask and the Assassin", 2008s "Pain Language" with Planet Asia and 2010s "Kill Devil Hills" together with Ill Bill.  "Grandmasters" serves as GZA's fifth studio album.
