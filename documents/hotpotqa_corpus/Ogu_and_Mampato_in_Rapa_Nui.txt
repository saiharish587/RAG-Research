Ogu and Mampato in Rapa Nui: Ogu and Mampato in Rapa Nui (Spanish: "Ogú y Mampato en Rapa Nui" ), also known as Mampato: The Movie (Spanish: "Mampato: La Película") is a feature-length Chilean animated film, created by Cine Animadores and executive produced by Elastic Studios, released June 27, 2002.  Although the film isn't the first animated feature made in Chile, being the second after Alfredo Serey's 1921 film "La Trasmisión del Mando Presidencial" ("The Transmission of Presidential Control"), it is considered the country's first "modern" animated film.  The movie is based on the Chilean comics character Mampato created in 1971 for the magazine of the same name by Themo Lobos and Eduardo Armstrong, and later reprinted as the comic-book Cucalón, the story for the film being adapted from the seventh adventure in the series: "Mata-ki-te-rangui".
