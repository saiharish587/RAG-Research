A Tale of Two Toads: A Tale of Two Toads is a 1989 hour-long feature from the 1980s stop motion animation series "The Wind in the Willows".  The film was animated by Cosgrove Hall and broadcast on CITV.  A further season of 13 episodes was shown under the title, "Oh, Mr. Toad" in some countries, whilst retaining the title "The Wind in the Willows" in others.
