Cuarteto d'Aida: The Cuarteto d'Aida was a famous Cuban female singing group.  It was founded and directed by the pianist Aida Diestro (1924–1973) in 1952.  Diestro picked four brilliant young singers to form the group: Elena Burke, Moraima Secada and the sisters Omara and Haydée Portuondo.  One of the group, Omara, is still alive and performing today; she was brought back into prominence as part of the Buena Vista Social Club phenomenon.
