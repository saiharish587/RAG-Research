The Human Centipede 2 (Full Sequence): The Human Centipede 2 (Full Sequence) is a 2011 Dutch black and white exploitation-horror film written, directed, and co-produced by Dutch filmmaker Tom Six.  The sequel to Six's 2009 film "The Human Centipede (First Sequence)", the film stars Laurence R. Harvey as a mentally impaired English man who watches and becomes obsessed with the first "Human Centipede" film, and decides to make his own "centipede" consisting of twelve people, including Ashlynn Yennie, an actress from the first film.
