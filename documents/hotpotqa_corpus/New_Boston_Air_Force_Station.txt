New Boston Air Force Station: New Boston Air Force Station is a United States Air Force facility located in Hillsborough County in south central New Hampshire.  It occupies more than 2800 acre in three towns: New Boston, Amherst, and Mont Vernon.  It was established in 1942 as a practice area for bombers and fighter planes from nearby Grenier Army Air Field (now Manchester–Boston Regional Airport).  Starting in 1959, it was turned into a satellite-tracking station.  During the late 1970s and early 1980s it was known as Detachment 1 of the 2014th Communications Squadron located at Hanscom Air Force Base in Bedford, Massachusetts.
