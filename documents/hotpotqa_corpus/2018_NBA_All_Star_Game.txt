2018 NBA All-Star Game: The 2018 NBA All-Star Game will be the 67th edition and is an exhibition basketball game that will be played on February 18, 2018.  It will be held at Staples Center in Los Angeles, California, home of the Los Angeles Lakers and Los Angeles Clippers.  In an announcement on March 22, 2016, it will be the sixth time that Los Angeles will host the All-Star Game and the first time since 2011.
