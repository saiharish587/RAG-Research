Gamaliel Churata: Arturo Peralta Miranda (born June 10, 1897; died Lima, November 9, 1969)) was a Peruvian writer, he had an active literary and political life in his country, mostly in his native city: Puno.  Some say that at his time, he was one of the four major representatives of the Peruvian indigenous movement.  He was known in the world of literature and journalism both in Peru and Bolivia under the pseudonyms "John Cajal", "P", "Gonzalez Saavedra," "The man in the street" and / or "Gamaliel CHURATA.
