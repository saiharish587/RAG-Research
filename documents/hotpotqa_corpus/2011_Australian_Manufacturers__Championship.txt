2011 Australian Manufacturers' Championship: The 2011 Australian Manufacturers' Championship was a CAMS sanctioned national motor racing championship for car manufacturers.  It was the 26th manufacturers title to be awarded by CAMS and the 17th to be contested under the Australian Manufacturers' Championship name.  The championship, which was open to modified production touring cars, also incorporated three drivers titles, the 2011 Australian Production Car Championship, the 2011 Australian Production Car Endurance Championship and the 2011 Australian Endurance Championship.
