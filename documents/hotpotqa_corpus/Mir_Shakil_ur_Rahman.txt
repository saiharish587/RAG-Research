Mir Shakil-ur-Rahman: Mir Shakil-ur-Rahman is a Pakistani media mogul and philanthropist.  As a businessman, he is known as the founder of 24-hour news cycle network, "Geo TV", as well as its executive.  In addition, he is the owner of the Jang Group of Newspapers, that was started by his father Mir Khalil ur Rehman and part owner of the Independent Media Corporation.  This media group publishes a number of newspapers and magazines in Urdu and English.  IMC also owns the Geo TV network.
