Islesboro, Maine: Islesboro is a town in Waldo County, Maine, United States.  The population was 566 at the 2010 census.  It has a summer colony accessible by ferry from Lincolnville Beach 3 miles to the west, or by air taxi service.  Home to Warren Island State Park, Islesboro includes the villages of Dark Harbor, Pripet and North Islesboro otherwise known as Guinea.
