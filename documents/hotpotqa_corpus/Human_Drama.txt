Human Drama: Human Drama is an American alternative rock band led by singer/songwriter Johnny Indovina formed in 1985.  With Indovina the only constant member, the band released six studio albums before splitting in 2005.  They reformed for concerts in 2011 and 2012, and again in 2015.  In 2017 they released their 1st studio album in 15 years "Broken Songs for Broken People".
