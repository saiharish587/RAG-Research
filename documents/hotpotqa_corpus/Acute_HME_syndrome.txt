Acute HME syndrome: In many districts of western Uttar Pradesh, Uttarakhand, Odisha and Haryana, India, outbreaks of an acute “encephalopathy” syndrome dubbed as a “mysterious disease” have been an annual feature for many years.  The disease affects rural young children during the winter months of September to December, and carries case fatality rates of around 75-80%.  Most investigators assume it to be viral “encephalitis”.  According to a rough estimate, at least 500-700 young previously healthy children have been losing their lives every year for many years in this region.  Many national investigating agencies failed to diagnose the entity despite investigating for many consecutive years.
