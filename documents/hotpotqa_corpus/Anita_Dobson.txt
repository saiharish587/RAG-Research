Anita Dobson: Anita Dobson (born 29 April 1949) is an English stage, film and television actress, and singer.  She is known for her role from 1985 to 1988 as Angie Watts in the BBC soap opera "EastEnders".  In 1986, she reached number four in the UK Singles Chart with "Anyone Can Fall in Love", a song based on the theme music of "EastEnders".
