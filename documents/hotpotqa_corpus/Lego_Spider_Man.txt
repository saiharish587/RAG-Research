Lego Spider-Man: Lego Spider-Man is a product range of the Lego construction toy, based on the first two Spider-Man movies.  The theme was first introduced in 2002 as a sub-brand of the Lego Studios theme, to tie in with the release of the first "Spider-Man" film.  Three new sets were released in 2003, this time without the Lego Studios branding.  In 2004, 7 new sets were introduced, to tie-in with the "Spider-Man 2" movie.  After this, the line was discontinued, and the rights to the third film in the series "Spider-Man 3" were sold instead to rival company MEGA Brands, resulting in a "Spider-Man 3" range of the Mega Bloks toy.  However, in 2012 LEGO released a set based on the "Ultimate Spider-Man" animated series in their Marvel Superheroes line.
