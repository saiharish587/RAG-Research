Time Sharing (novel): Time Sharing is a 1986 novel by Richard Krawiec, published by Viking Press.  Taking place in Philadelphia, its main characters are Artie, a purse-snatcher, and Jolene, a single mother.  Artie hopes that by pretending to befriend Jolene he can profit from her.  Jolene tries to see good in Artie and hopes he can act as a father for her son, Dandy.  In time, Artie genuinely cares for Jolene.  The novel ends moments after Artie accidentally shoots a cashier in a holdup.  As he waits to be apprehended, Artie feels no remorse for shooting the cashier, but he grieves over the impending loss of his relationship with Jolene.
