QF 14 pounder Maxim-Nordenfelt naval gun: The QF 14 pounder was a 3-inch medium-velocity naval gun used to equip warships for defence against torpedo boats.  It was produced for export by Maxim-Nordenfelt (later Vickers, Sons and Maxim) in competition with the Elswick QF 12-pounder 12 cwt and QF 12-pounder 18 cwt guns.
