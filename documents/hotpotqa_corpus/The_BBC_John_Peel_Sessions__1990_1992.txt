The BBC John Peel Sessions, 1990–1992: The BBC John Peel Sessions, 1990–1992 is an expanded version of the earlier release, "The Peel Sessions" by Babes in Toyland.  It was produced by James Birt Whistle and released on 27 November 2001 by Cherry Red Records, which was also responsible for releasing Babes In Toyland's first live album, "Minneapolism".
