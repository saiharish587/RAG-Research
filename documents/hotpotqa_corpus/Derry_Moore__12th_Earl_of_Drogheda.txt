Derry Moore, 12th Earl of Drogheda: Henry Dermot Ponsonby Moore, 12th Earl of Drogheda (born 1937) is a British photographer known professionally as Derry Moore.  He inherited the title of Earl of Drogheda from his father, Charles Moore, 11th Earl of Drogheda (1910-1989).  His mother was the late Joan Eleanor Carr (died 1989).
