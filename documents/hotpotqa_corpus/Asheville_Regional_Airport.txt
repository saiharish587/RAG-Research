Asheville Regional Airport: Asheville Regional Airport (IATA: AVL, ICAO: KAVL, FAA LID: AVL) is a Class C airport near Interstate 26 near the town of Fletcher, 9 mi south of downtown Asheville, in the U.S. state of North Carolina, United States.  It is owned by the Greater Asheville Regional Airport Authority.  It is included in the Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) National Plan of Integrated Airport Systems for 2017–2021, in which it is categorized as a non-hub primary commercial service facility.  In 2016 it served an all-time record number of passengers for the airport, 826,648, an increase of 5% over 2015 and the third consecutive year of record traffic.
