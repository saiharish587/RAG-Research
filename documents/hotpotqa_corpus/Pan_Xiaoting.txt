Pan Xiaoting: Pan Xiaoting (; born 25 February 1982 in Yanzhou District, Jining, Shandong; sometimes referred to in the Western media as Xiaoting Pan and nicknamed "Queen of Nine-Ball") is the first-ever female professional pool player from China to play full-time on the Women's Professional Billiard Association (WPBA) tour.  Pan's growing list of achievements in billiards, as well as her beauty, have made her one of the most famous female athletes in her country.
