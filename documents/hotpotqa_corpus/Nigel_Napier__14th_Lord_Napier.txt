Nigel Napier, 14th Lord Napier: Major (Francis) Nigel Napier, 14th Lord Napier, 5th Baron Ettrick (5 December 1930 – 15 March 2012) was a Scottish soldier and courtier.  He was the son of Lt.-Colonel William Francis Cyril James Hamilton Napier, 13th Lord Napier, 4th Baron Ettrick and Violet Muir Newson, daughter of Sir Percy Wilson Newson, 1st Bt.
