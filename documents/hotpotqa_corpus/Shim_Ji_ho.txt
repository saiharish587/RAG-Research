Shim Ji-ho: Shim Ji-ho (born May 2, 1981) is a South Korean actor.  He began his entertainment career as a model, then turned to acting.  He has starred in television dramas such as "School 2" (1999), "My Lovely Family" (2004), "Our Stance on How to Treat a Break-up" (2005) and "Color of Women" (2011), as well as Park Chul-soo's erotic film "Green Chair" which premiered at the 2005 Sundance Film Festival.
