White Noise (Disclosure song): "White Noise" is a song by British electronic music duo Disclosure, featuring vocals from electronic music duo AlunaGeorge.  It was released as a digital download in the United Kingdom on 1 February 2013.  The song peaked at number two on the UK Singles Chart.  The track is the second single from the duo's debut studio album, "Settle" (2013).  The song was written by Howard Lawrence, Guy Lawrence, Aluna Francis, George Reid and James Napier.
