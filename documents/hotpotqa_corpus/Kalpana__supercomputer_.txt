Kalpana (supercomputer): Kalpana was a supercomputer at NASA Ames Research Center operated by the NASA Advanced Supercomputing (NAS) Division and named in honor of astronaut Kalpana Chawla, who was killed in the Space Shuttle "Columbia" disaster and had worked as an engineer at Ames Research Center prior to joining the Space Shuttle program.  It was built in late 2003 and dedicated on May 12, 2004.
