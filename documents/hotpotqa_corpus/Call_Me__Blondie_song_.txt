Call Me (Blondie song): "Call Me" is a song by the American new wave band Blondie and the theme to the 1980 film "American Gigolo".  Released in the US in early 1980 as a single, "Call Me" was number one for six consecutive weeks on the "Billboard" Hot 100 chart, where it became the band's biggest single and second #1.  It also hit #1 in the UK and Canada, where it became their fourth and second chart-topper respectively.  In the year-end charts of 1980 it was Billboard's #1 hit, and according to Billboard magazine, was the top-selling single of the year in the United States in 1980 and RPM's #3.
