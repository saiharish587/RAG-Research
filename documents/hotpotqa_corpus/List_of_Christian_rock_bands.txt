List of Christian rock bands: In the 1980s Christian rock started to become a staple in the music industry, fueled by bands such as Stryper. " Billboard" magazine started to post the top 10 Christian rock songs, and some radio stations started to play Christian rock.  Bands such as DC Talk, Newsboys, Jars of Clay, Audio Adrenaline and many others achieved commercial success.  This list excludes bands that are primarily heavy metal or hardcore punk; those bands appear in the list of Christian metal artists and list of Christian hardcore bands, respectively.
