Origanum rotundifolium: Origanum rotundifolium, the round-leaved oregano, is a species of flowering plant in the family Lamiaceae, native to Turkey, Armenia and Georgia.  It is a small woody-based perennial or subshrub growing to 10 - tall by 30 cm wide, with strongly aromatic leaves, and loose clusters of pink flowers with hop-like pale green bracts, throughout the summer.
