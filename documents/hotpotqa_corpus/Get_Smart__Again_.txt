Get Smart, Again!: Get Smart, Again!  is a 1989 American made-for-television comedy film based on the 1965–1970 NBC/CBS sitcom "Get Smart!"  starring Don Adams and Barbara Feldon reprising their characters of Maxwell Smart and Agent 99.  It originally aired February 26, 1989 on ABC (the network that rejected the original pilot for "Get Smart!")  and has subsequently been released twice on DVD by different publishers.  In the video release of the movie, the laugh track is absent.
