Falconeria: Falconeria is a monotypic plant genus in the family Euphorbiaceae, first described as a genus in 1839.  The genus is sometimes included within the genus "Sapium".  The sole species is Falconeria insignis.  The plant is found from India, Nepal, Bangladesh, and Sri Lanka to Indochina, China (Hainan, Sichuan, Yunnan), Thailand and Peninsular Malaysia.
