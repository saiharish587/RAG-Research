Willie Morris: William Weaks "Willie" Morris (November 29, 1934 – August 2, 1999), was an American writer and editor born in Jackson, Mississippi, though his family later moved to Yazoo City, Mississippi, which he immortalized in his works of prose.  Morris' trademark was his lyrical prose style and reflections on the American South, particularly the Mississippi Delta.  In 1967 he became the youngest editor of "Harper's Magazine".  He wrote several works of fiction and non-fiction, including his seminal book "North Toward Home", as well as "My Dog Skip".
