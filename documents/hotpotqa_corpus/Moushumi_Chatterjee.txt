Moushumi Chatterjee: Moushumi Chatterjee (born 26 April 1948 as Moushumi Chattopadhyaya) is an Indian actress who has acted in Hindi and Bengali cinema.  Her on-screen pairings with actors like Rajesh Khanna, Shashi Kapoor, Jeetendra, Sanjeev Kumar and Vinod Mehra were popular.  According to a source she wasn't the highest paid actress in Hindi films but in Bengali films she was the sixth highest paid.
