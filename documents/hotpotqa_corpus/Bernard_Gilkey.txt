Bernard Gilkey: Otis Bernard Gilkey (born September 24, 1966 in St. Louis, Missouri) is a former Major League Baseball (MLB) player for the St. Louis Cardinals, New York Mets, Arizona Diamondbacks, Boston Red Sox, and Atlanta Braves.  Primarily a left fielder, Gilkey occasionally played right field as well.  He also played a small number of games as a center fielder, first baseman, and designated hitter.  Gilkey was a right-handed batter.
