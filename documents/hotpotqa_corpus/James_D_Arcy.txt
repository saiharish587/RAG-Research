James D'Arcy: James D'Arcy (born Simon Richard D'Arcy; 24 August 1975) is an English actor.  He is best known for his portrayals of Howard Stark's butler, Edwin Jarvis, in the Marvel Entertainment and ABC series, "Agent Carter", and murder suspect Lee Ashworth in the second series of the ITV series "Broadchurch".  D'Arcy also co-starred as a British Army Colonel in Christopher Nolan's action-thriller "Dunkirk" (2017).
