Birgit Kober: Birgit Kober (10 July 1971) is a German Paralympic athlete.  She initially competed in F34 seated throwing events, and from 2011-2013 she became the reigning champion at European, World and Paralympic level in both shot put and javelin in her class.  At the 2012 Summer Paralympic Games in London, she broke the world record in both her events.  Following changes to the IPC rules for seated throws in 2014, she chose to compete in a standing position as an F36 athlete.  She won the F36 shot put at the 2016 Summer Paralympic Games with a Paralympic record throw.  s of April 2017 she is World Record holder in the Women's F36 shot put, a distance of 11.52 m set in July 2016.
