Rupertswood: Rupertswood is a mansion and country estate located in Sunbury on the outskirts of Melbourne in Victoria, Australia.  It is well known as the birthplace of The Ashes urn which was humorously presented to English cricket captain Ivo Bligh to mark his team's victory in an 1882-83 Test match series between Australia and England.  Rupertswood is one of the largest houses constructed in Victoria and, although now subdivided, has significant farm land.  The estate also had its own private railway station (until closure in 2004), and artillery battery.  It is listed on the Victorian Heritage Register.
