Anholt Offshore Wind Farm: Anholt Offshore Wind Farm is a Danish offshore wind power wind farm in the Kattegat, between Djursland and Anholt island.  With a nameplate capacity of 400 megawatts (MW), it is the third largest offshore wind farm in the world (along with BARD Offshore 1) and the largest in Denmark.  A cable from the wind farm to Anholt replaces most of the diesel-powered electricity on the island.
