Kate Voegele: Kate Elizabeth Voegele ( ; born December 8, 1986) is an American singer-songwriter and actress.  She made her musical debut in 2003, with the release of her "The Other Side" extended play.  She performed numerous local live shows to promote the album, and toured with artists such as John Mayer.  In 2005, she released her second extended play, "Louder Than Words".  Both had local success, and helped raise awareness of Voegele as an artist.  During this time period, Voegele performed at events such as Farm Aid to promote her music, and began posting her music on popular social media network MySpace.  She also won numerous awards and honors for her songwriting abilities.
