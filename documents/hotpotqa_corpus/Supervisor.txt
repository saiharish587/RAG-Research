Supervisor: Supervisor, when the meaning sought is similar to foreman, foreperson, overseer, cell coach, manager, facilitator, monitor, or area coordinator, is the job title of a low level management position that is primarily based on authority over a worker or charge of a workplace.  A Supervisor can also be one of the most senior in the staff at the place of work, such as a Professor who oversees a PhD dissertation.  Supervision, on the other hand, can be performed by people without this formal title, for example by parents.  The term Supervisor itself can be used to refer to any personnel who have this task as part of their job description.
