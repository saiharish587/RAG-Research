Kecharis Monastery: Kecharis (Armenian: Կեչառիս ), is a medieval Armenian monastic complex dating back to the 11th to 13th centuries, located 60 km from Yerevan, in the ski resort town of Tsakhkadzor in Armenia.  Nestled in the Bambak mountains, Kecharis was founded by a Pahlavuni prince in the 11th century, and construction continued until the middle of the 13th century.  In the 12th and 13th centuries, Kecharis was a major religious center of Armenia and a place of higher education.  Today, the monastery has been fully restored and is clearly visible from the ski slopes.
