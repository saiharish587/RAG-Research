1998–99 CF Extremadura season: Extremadura finished the season in 17th place.  Although this was the club's highest-ever finish in the Spanish football pyramid (the season was only the second Extremadura had played in the top flight), they still finished one point adrift of safety.  Extremadura thus qualified for the relegation play-off, where they faced the fifth-placed team of the Segunda División, Rayo Vallecano.  The Madrid-based side easily defeated Extremadura 2-0 both home and away for a 4-0 aggregate win which saw Extremadura relegated after a single season in the top flight of Spanish football.  Manager Rafael Benítez resigned after only two seasons in charge, deciding to instead study in Italy and England.
