WAMC: WAMC is a public radio network headquartered in Albany, New York.  The network has 12 broadcast radio stations (transmitters) and 16 broadcast relay stations (translators,repeaters).  One of the stations is an AM station: WAMC (AM) 1400 in Albany.  The organization's legal name is "WAMC" and it is also known as "WAMC Public Radio" or "WAMC Northeast Public Radio."
