Stray Cat Blues: "Stray Cat Blues" is the eighth song on the Rolling Stones' album "Beggars Banquet".  It was written by Mick Jagger and Keith Richards and produced by Jimmy Miller.  Miller's production of the song is very representative of his style, featuring a very prominent hi hat beat, droning piano performed by Nicky Hopkins, a mellotron performed by Brian Jones, all electric guitars (including slide) performed by Richards and vocals from Jagger kept even in the mix.  According to Mick Jagger, the song was inspired by "Heroin" by the Velvet Underground.  The intro of Stray Cat and Heroin are similar.
