Shambala Animal Kingdom: Shambala Animal Kingdom was a 53 ha zoological park outside Kuranda, Queensland, Australia.  The park was privately owned.  It was formerly known as "Cairns Wildlife Safari Reserve", "Mareeba Wild Animal Park" and "Out of Africa Reserve".  After being purchased by a Sydney lawyer, the zoo was renamed "Shambala Animal Kingdom".  The zoo was closed in April 2013 citing financial reasons.
