Moving On (Asking Alexandria song): Moving On is a power ballad by English Metalcore band Asking Alexandria.  It is the tenth track on their third album "From Death to Destiny" and was released as the sixth and final single from the album on July 29, 2014.  It was released on July 29, 2014.  It is the band's last single before the departure of vocalist Danny Worsnop in January 2015.
