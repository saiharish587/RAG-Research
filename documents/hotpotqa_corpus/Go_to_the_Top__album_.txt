Go to the Top (album): Go to the Top is the debut album by Japanese singer-songwriter Hitomi, released on September 27, 1995 by Avex Trax.  The first press edition of the album came with the CD case housed inside a hardback case, similar to a book.  The inside of the back of the case contains a mini-photobook.  The RIAJ has certified it 2x Platinum, recognizing over 500,000+ shipments throughout Japan.  On the Oricon charts, the album's peak position was #3, and it stayed on the charts for eight weeks.
