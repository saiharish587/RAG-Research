St John the Baptist's Church, Tunstall: St John the Baptist Church is located to the northeast of the village of Tunstall, Lancashire, England.  It is an active Anglican parish church in the united benefice of East Lonsdale, in the deanery of Tunstall, the archdeaconry of Lancaster and the diocese of Blackburn.  The benefice of East Lonsdale combines this church with St Peter, Leck, St Wilfrid, Melling, St James the Less, Tatham, The Good Shepherd, Lowgill, and Holy Trinity, Wray.  The church is recorded in the National Heritage List for England as a designated Grade I listed building.
