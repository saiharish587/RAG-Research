The Carpenters: The Carpenters were an American vocal and instrumental duo consisting of siblings Karen (1950-1983) and Richard Carpenter (b. 1946).  Producing a distinctively soft musical style, which combined Karen's contralto vocals with Richard's arranging and composition skills, they became one of the best-selling music artists of all time.  During their 14-year career, the Carpenters recorded 11 albums, 31 singles, five television specials, and a short-lived television series.
