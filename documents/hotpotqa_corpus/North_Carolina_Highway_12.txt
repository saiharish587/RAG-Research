North Carolina Highway 12: North Carolina Highway 12 (NC 12) is a state highway that traverses the northeastern coastline of North Carolina, linking the peninsulas and islands of the northern Outer Banks.  Most sections of NC 12 are two lanes wide, and there are also two ferry routes which maintain continuity of the route as it traverses the Outer Banks region.  NC 12 is part of the Outer Banks Scenic Byway, a National Scenic Byway.  The first NC 12 appeared on the 1924 North Carolina Official Map and at its height ran from NC 30 in Pollocksville to NC 48 near Murfreesboro.  Over time it was replaced by both US 258 and NC 58 and ceased to exist in 1958.  The current NC 12 first appeared on the 1964 state highway map running from US 158 in Nags Head to Ocracoke.  In 1976 NC 12 was extended to US 70 on the mainland and in 1987 was extended north to Corolla.
