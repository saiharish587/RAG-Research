The Camel (Parks and Recreation): "The Camel" is the ninth episode of the second season of "Parks and Recreation", and the fifteenth overall episode of the series.  It originally aired on NBC in the United States on November 12, 2009.  In the episode, Leslie and the parks department bicker as they work on a proposal for a new town hall mural, while Ron and Andy share an awkward moment at Andy's new shoe-shine job.
