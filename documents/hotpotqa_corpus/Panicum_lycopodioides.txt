Panicum lycopodioides: Panicum lycopodioides, common name false club-moss panic grass or (in French) panic faux-lycopode, is endemic to the French island of Réunion in the Indian Ocean.  It is found at high altitudes on the island, at elevations over 2000 m (6700 feet).  The highest point on the island is Piton des Neiges, 3069 m (10,230 feet).  The plant requires full sun-light and a cold climate.
