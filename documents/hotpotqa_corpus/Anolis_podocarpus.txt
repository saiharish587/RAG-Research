Anolis podocarpus: Anolis podocarpus is a species of lizard in the family Polychrotidae.  It was first described by Fernando P. Ayala-Varela and Omar Torres-Carvajal in 2010, the type locality being the Podocarpus National Park at Romerillos Alto in Zamora-Chinchipe Province, Ecuador on the southeastern slopes of the Andes.  The specific name refers to the "Podocarpus" trees which are found in the Park.
