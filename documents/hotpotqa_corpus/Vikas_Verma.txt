Vikas Verma: Vikas Verma (born 24 September 1987) is an Indian actor and model.  Vikas began his career as a model where he won the title of India's Fashion Star 2009, Zoom TV and has walked for almost top notch designers in the industry at various fashion weeks.  He made his acting debut with a negative role in the romance adventure film "Yaariyan".  He went on to star in the romantic-comedy film Shaandaar where he played the role of Robin Fundwani.  Before his debut in Bollywood he was seen in TV serial "Jhansi Ki Rani" where he played the negative role as "Marshall and Captain Robert Hamilton".  For this role he has won the nomination for "Best Male Negative Lead" at the Indian Telly Awards.
