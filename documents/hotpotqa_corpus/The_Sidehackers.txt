The Sidehackers: The Sidehackers (also known as Five the Hard Way) is a 1969 American action film about motorcycle racing with a twist.  Each motorcycle has a sidehack (or "sidecar"), in which a passenger rides and tilts to one side or another when going around curves.  The credits thank the "Southern California Sidehack Association"; sidehacking is also known as sidecarcross or "sidecar motocross racing".
