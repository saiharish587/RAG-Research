Dinner Party Wars: Dinner Party Wars is a series that airs on Food Network Canada that is produced by Cineflix.  Dinner Party Wars is a three-night, high-stakes dining challenge that dares couples to be the best by any means possible.  From setting the menu and the table, to the cooking, conversation, and all the kitchen crises, hidden cameras capture every detail for viewers.  The show is narrated by Garnet Williams, while two expert judges, Chef Corbin Tomaszeski and Anthea Turner, watch every move from a distance and determine the winner from the comfort of their curbside studio.
