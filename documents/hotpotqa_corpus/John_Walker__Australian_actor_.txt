John Walker (Australian actor): John Walker is an Australian actor noted for his role in the sketch comedy show "Full Frontal" (1994–97) and its successor "Totally Full Frontal" (1998–99).  His most notable characters in the series was sleazy news presenter Ian Goodings, as well as impersonating John Howard, the Prime Minister of Australia at the time.  Walker also made a brief appearance in the 1999 comedy "The Craic" alongside comedian Jimeoin.  He has also appeared in the Australian drama "Blue Heelers" several times and a politically incorrect Australian show "Pizza".
