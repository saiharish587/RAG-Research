Food Lion: Food Lion LLC is a grocery store company headquartered in Salisbury, North Carolina, that operates more than 1,100 supermarkets in 10 states of the Southeastern United States under the Food Lion banner.  With about 63,000 employees, Food Lion, LLC.  is currently owned by Ahold Delhaize after it was acquired by the Delhaize Group in 1974.  One of the founders was philanthropist Ralph Ketner.
