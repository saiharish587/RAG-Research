North Point State Park: North Point State Park is a public recreation area located on Chesapeake Bay in Edgemere, Baltimore County, Maryland.  The state park includes the site of the former Bay Shore Park, which was one of the state's premiere amusement parks during the first half of the 20th century.  The park features restored remnants of the old amusement park as well as facilities for swimming, picnicking, bicycling, and hiking.  Black Marsh, a 667 acre state wildlands area, makes up half the park's area.  The park is administered by the Maryland Department of Natural Resources.
