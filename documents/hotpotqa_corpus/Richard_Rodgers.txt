Richard Rodgers: Richard Charles Rodgers (June 28, 1902 – December 30, 1979) was an American composer of music for more than 900 songs and for 43 Broadway musicals.  He also composed music for films and television.  He is best known for his songwriting partnerships with the lyricists Lorenz Hart and Oscar Hammerstein II.  His compositions have had a significant impact on popular music up to the present day, and have an enduring broad appeal.
