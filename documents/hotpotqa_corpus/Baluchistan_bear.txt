Baluchistan bear: The Baluchistan bear, also called the Pakistan black bear ("Ursus thibetanus gedrosianus") is a subspecies of the Asian black bear found in the Baluchistan Mountains of southern Pakistan and Iran.  It has an unusually thin coat for an Asian black bear, but this is because it is found in a warmer climate than most of the other subspecies, which are found in the much colder Himalayan Mountains.  It is also more frugivorous than the other subspecies, and it loves to eat figs and bananas.
