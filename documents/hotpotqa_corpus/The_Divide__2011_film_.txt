The Divide (2011 film): The Divide is a post-apocalyptic horror film directed by Xavier Gens and written by Karl Mueller and Eron Sheean.  It stars Michael Biehn, Lauren German, Milo Ventimiglia and Rosanna Arquette.  "The Divide" was first screened at the SXSW festival in March 2011, then released in theaters in the United States on January 13, 2012 to negative reviews.  It was released on DVD in the United States on April 17, 2012.
