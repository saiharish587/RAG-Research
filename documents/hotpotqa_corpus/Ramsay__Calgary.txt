Ramsay, Calgary: Ramsay is a residential neighbourhood in the south-east quadrant of Calgary, Alberta.  It is an inner city community, located east of the Elbow River, Macleod Trail, Stampede Grounds and the Scotiabank Saddledome arena and south of Inglewood.  To the south-east, it borders the Alyth-Bonny Brook industrial area.  The eastern half of the community consists primarily of older homes and there is an industrial area in the most eastern corner of the community.
