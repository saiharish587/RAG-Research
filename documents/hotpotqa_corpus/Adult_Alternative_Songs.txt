Adult Alternative Songs: Adult Alternative Songs, also known as Triple A, is a record chart currently published by "Billboard" that ranks the most popular songs on adult album alternative radio stations.  The 30-position chart is formulated based on each song's weekly radio spins, as measured by Nielsen Broadcast Data Systems.  The earliest incarnation of the chart was first published on January 20, 1996 as a feature in "Billboard" sister publication "Airplay Monitor".  In 2006, "Airplay Monitor" ceased publication after "Billboard" parent company VNU Media's acquisition of rival radio trade magazine "Radio & Records", which then subsequently incorporated "Airplay Monitor"' s Nielsen-based Triple A chart.
