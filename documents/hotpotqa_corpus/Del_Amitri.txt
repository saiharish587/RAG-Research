Del Amitri: Del Amitri is a Scottish alternative rock band, formed in Glasgow, Scotland in 1983.  The band grew out of Justin Currie's Jordanhill College School band and came together after a teenaged Currie placed an advertisement in the window of a music store asking for people who could play to contact him.  The band was formed with the original line-up of Currie (bass and vocals), Iain Harvie (lead guitar), Bryan Tolland (guitar) and Paul Tyagi (drums).  Currie and Harvie were the only members of the band to remain present throughout its history.  They were also the main songwriters of the group.
