My Dog Skip (film): My Dog Skip is a 2000 family drama film, directed by Jay Russell and starring Frankie Muniz, Diane Lane, Luke Wilson, and Kevin Bacon with narration by Harry Connick, Jr. Based on the autobiographical novel of the same name, the film stars Muniz as 9-year-old Willie Morris as he is given a Jack Russell terrier for his birthday, and how the dog fundamentally changes several aspects of his life.  "My Dog Skip" was released on March 3, 2000, by Warner Bros., receiving generally positive reviews from critics.  The film earned $35,547,761 on a $4.5 million budget.
