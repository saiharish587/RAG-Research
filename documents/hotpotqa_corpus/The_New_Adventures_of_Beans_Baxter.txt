The New Adventures of Beans Baxter: The New Adventures of Beans Baxter is an adventure/comedy television series that aired in 1987 and 1988 on the Fox television network.  It was created by Savage Steve Holland, who also wrote and directed most of the 17 episodes that were actually produced and aired.  The title character was acted out by Jonathan Ward, who won a "Best Young Actor Starring in a New Television Comedy Series" Young Artist Award for the role.  The lead role of Beans Baxter was originally offered to David Spade, and he regretted turning it down, per advice of his agents.
