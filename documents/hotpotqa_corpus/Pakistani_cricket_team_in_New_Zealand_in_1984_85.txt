Pakistani cricket team in New Zealand in 1984–85: The Pakistan national cricket team toured New Zealand in January and February 1985 and played a three-match Test series and four One Day International (ODI) matches against the New Zealand national cricket team.  New Zealand won the series 2–0 and Pakistan won the ODI series 3–1.  New Zealand were captained by Geoff Howarth and Pakistan by Javed Miandad.  This was New Zealand's last Test series win against Pakistan until November 2016.
