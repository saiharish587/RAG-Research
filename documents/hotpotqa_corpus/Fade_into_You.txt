Fade into You: "Fade into You" is a song by rock group Mazzy Star from their album "So Tonight That I Might See".  The song was written by lyricist Hope Sandoval and composer David Roback, who also served as producer.  It reached number three on the Billboard Modern Rock chart in 1994, and is Mazzy Star's only single to make the "Billboard" Hot 100, peaking at number 44.  The song also charted at number 48 on the UK Singles Chart.
