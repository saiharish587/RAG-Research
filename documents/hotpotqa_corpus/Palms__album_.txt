Palms (album): Palms is the first studio album by the rock band Palms.  It was released in 2013 in CD digipack, limited edition vinyl, limited edition cassette and digital download formats by Ipecac Records.  The album was made available for streaming by "Spin" on June 18, 2013, one week before its release.  Upon its release, "Palms" debuted at No. 55 on the "Billboard" 200 and received positive reviews.  The album has been described as post-metal and alternative rock, much like singer Chino Moreno's main band, Deftones.
