Disenchanted: "Disenchanted" is a song co-written and recorded by American country music artist Michael Martin Murphey.  It was released in April 1984 as the third single from the album "The Heart Never Lies".  The song peaked at number 12 on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot Country Singles and at number 11 on the Canadian "RPM" Country Tracks chart.  It was written by Murphey, Jim Ed Norman and Chick Rains.
