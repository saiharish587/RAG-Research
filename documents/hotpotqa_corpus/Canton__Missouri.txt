Canton, Missouri: Canton is a city in Lewis County, Missouri, United States.  The population was 2,377 at the 2010 census.  Canton is the home of Culver-Stockton College, a small liberal arts college affiliated with the Christian Church (Disciples of Christ).  It also had the oldest continuously operating ferry across the Mississippi River, which closed in April 2014.  Four members of the United States House of Representatives have come from Canton, and are buried in the city's Forest Grove Cemetery.  Canton is part of the Quincy, IL–MO Micropolitan Statistical Area.
