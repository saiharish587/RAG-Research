Imran Mahmudul: Imran Mahmudul, also known simply as Imran, is a Bangladeshi music composer and singer whose songs has been featured in different albums and movies.  He was the first runner-up of "Channel i Sera Kontho - 2008".  He also won Best Male Singer at the Meril Prothom Alo Awards for the song "Dil Dil Dil" from the 2016 film "Bossgiri".
