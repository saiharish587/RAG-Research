The Carp (opera): The Carp is a one-act comic opera (styled "a whimsicality") with a libretto by Frank Desprez and music by Alfred Cellier.  It was first produced at the Savoy Theatre from 13 February 1886 to 19 January 1887, as a companion piece to "The Mikado".  It was then revived as companion to "Ruddigore" from 21 February 1887 to 5 November 1887.  The piece also toured throughout 1888.
