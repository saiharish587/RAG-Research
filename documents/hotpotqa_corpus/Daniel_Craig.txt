Daniel Craig: Daniel Wroughton Craig (born 2 March 1968) is an English actor.  He trained at the National Youth Theatre and graduated from the Guildhall School of Music and Drama in 1991, before beginning his career on stage.  His film debut was in the drama "The Power of One" (1992).  Other early appearances were in the Disney family film "A Kid in King Arthur's Court" (1995) and the biographical film "Elizabeth" (1998), as well as in the historical war drama television series "Sharpe's Eagle" (1993) and the action-adventure drama series "Zorro".  Cast as the British secret agent James Bond in October 2005, his first film in the role, "Casino Royale", was released internationally in November 2006.
