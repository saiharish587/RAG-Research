In the Air Tonite (Lil' Kim song): "In the Air Tonite" is a remix of "In the Air Tonight" released in 2001 to promote the R&B/hip hop Phil Collins tribute album "Urban Renewal"; the song is a duet between Collins and American rapper Lil' Kim.  They did not work together for the video; scenes from the original video and new scenes featuring Lil' Kim were used to create a video.  The single has been certified Platinum in Germany by the IFPI.
