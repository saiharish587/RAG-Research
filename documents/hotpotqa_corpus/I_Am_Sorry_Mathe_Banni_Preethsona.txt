I Am Sorry Mathe Banni Preethsona: I Am Sorry Mathe Banni Preethsona is a 2011 Kannada film in the romance genre starring Prem Kumar and Karishma Tanna in the lead roles.  The film is directed by Ravindra H P Das.  Anoop Seelin is the music director of the film.  Vinay Narkar has jointly produced the film with Ravindra.  The film released on 17 June 2011.
