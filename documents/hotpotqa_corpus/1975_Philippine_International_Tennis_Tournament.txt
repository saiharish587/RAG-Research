1975 Philippine International Tennis Tournament: The 1975 Philippine International Tennis Tournament, also known as the Philta International Championships was a men's tennis tournament played an outdoor hard courts in Manila, Philippines.  It was the third edition of the tournament and was held from 27 October through 2 November 1975.  The tournament was part of the Grand Prix tennis circuit and categorized in Group A. Ross Case won the singles title and the $12,000 first prize money.
