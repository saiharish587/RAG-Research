Bui Dam: The Bui Dam is a 400 MW hydroelectric project in Ghana.  It is built on the Black Volta river at the Bui Gorge, at the southern end of Bui National Park.  The project is a collaboration between the government of Ghana and Sino Hydro, a Chinese construction company.  Construction on the main dam began in December 2009.  Its first generator was commissioned on 3 May 2013, and the dam was inaugurated in December of the same year.
