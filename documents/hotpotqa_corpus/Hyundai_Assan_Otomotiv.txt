Hyundai Assan Otomotiv: The Hyundai Assan Otomotiv San ve Tic.  A.Ş.  is an automotive company based in Kozyatagi, Istanbul, Turkey, established at the end of 1994, as a joint venture between the Hyundai Motor Company of South Korea and the Kibar Holding of Turkey.
