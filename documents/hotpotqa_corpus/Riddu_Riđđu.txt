Riddu Riđđu: Riddu Riđđu is an annual Sami music and culture festival held in Olmmáivággi (Manndalen) in the Gáivuotna (Kåfjord) municipality in Norway.  The goal of the festival is to bring forward both Sami culture and that of other indigenous peoples.  Translated to English, the name of the festival is "small storm at the coast".  The festival has permanent support from the Ministry of Culture and Church Affairs, the Sami parliament, Troms county and Kåfjord municipality.
