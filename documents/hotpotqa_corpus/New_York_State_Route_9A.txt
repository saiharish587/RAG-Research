New York State Route 9A: New York State Route 9A (NY 9A) is a state highway in the vicinity of New York City in the United States.  Its southern terminus is at the northern end of the Brooklyn–Battery Tunnel in New York City, where it intersects with both the unsigned Interstate 478 (I-478) and FDR Drive.  The northern terminus of NY 9A is at U.S. Route 9 (US 9) in Peekskill.  It is predominantly an alternate route of US 9 between New York City and Peekskill; however, in New York City, it is a major route of its own as it runs along the West Side Highway and Henry Hudson Parkway.  In Westchester County, NY 9A follows the Briarcliff–Peekskill Parkway.
