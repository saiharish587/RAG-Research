Pompano Beach, Florida: Pompano Beach is a city in Broward County, Florida, United States, along the coast of the Atlantic Ocean just to the north of Fort Lauderdale.  The nearby Hillsboro Inlet forms part of the Atlantic Intracoastal Waterway.  As of the 2010 census the city's population was 99,845, with an estimated population of 102,984 as of 2012.  It is a principal city of the Miami metropolitan area, which was home to an estimated 6,012,331 people at the 2015 census.
