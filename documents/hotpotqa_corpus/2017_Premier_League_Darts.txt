2017 Premier League Darts: The 2017 Betway Premier League Darts was a darts tournament organised by the Professional Darts Corporation – the thirteenth edition of the tournament.  The event began on Thursday 2 February at the Metro Radio Arena in Newcastle and ended with the Play-Offs at The O2 Arena in London on Thursday 18 May.  This is the fourth year that the tournament is sponsored by Betway.
