2011 Ghana Movie Awards: The 2011 Ghana Movie Awards was the second edition of the ceremony to reward film practitioners in the Ghana Film Industry.  The event was held at Accra International Conference center on 25 December 2011.  Winners included Kimberly Elise, Majid Michel, "Somewhere in Africa". " Ties That Bind" had 21 nominations and eventually won 9 awards to top the winners list.  Olu Jacobs received the Lifetime Achievement Award.
