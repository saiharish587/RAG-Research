Run for the Sun: Run for the Sun is a 1956 Technicolor thriller adventure film released by United Artists, the third film to officially be based on Richard Connell's classic suspense story, "The Most Dangerous Game", after both RKO's "The Most Dangerous Game" (1932), and their remake, "A Game of Death" (1945).  This version stars Richard Widmark, Trevor Howard, and Jane Greer, and was directed by Ray Boulting from a script written by Boulting and Dudley Nichols.  Connell was credited for his short story.
