Australian cricket team in Australia in 1950–51: The 1950-51 Australians defeated the touring England team 4-1 in the 1950-51 Ashes series, Australia's last Ashes success until 1958-59.  The series was tilted the balance from the powerful Australian teams of the 1940s to the strong England teams of the 1950s.  While in the end they won easily the team made heavy weather of defeating a weak touring team and would lose the next three hard-fought Ashes series.  The newly knighted Sir Donald Bradman had retired from cricket, but most of his great 1948 Australian team still played and Australia had not lost a Test series since 1932-33.
