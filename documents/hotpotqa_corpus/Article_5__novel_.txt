Article 5 (novel): Article 5 is a 2012 young adult dystopian novel by Kristen Simmons.  The book was published in January 2012 by Tor Teen and is the first installment in a trilogy.  The novel tells the story of Ember Miller and Chase Jennings, two teenagers who are on the run from the government in a post-War dystopian America.  It was followed by two sequels titled Breaking Point and Three.
