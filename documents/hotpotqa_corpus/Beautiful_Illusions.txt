Beautiful Illusions: Beautiful Illusions (镜中人) is a Singaporean TV series which aired in 2005.  It stars Fann Wong in two diverse roles - a quiet and nondescript cartoon illustrator with a quirky sense of style (Yixin), and a stylish, vampy air stewardess who lives the high life on her motorbike (Joe Ann).  The series title literally means "person in the mirror" and alludes to the main character
