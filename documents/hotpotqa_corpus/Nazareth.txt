Nazareth: Nazareth ( ; Hebrew: נָצְרַת‎ , "Natzrat"; Arabic: النَّاصِرَة‎ ‎ , "an-Nāṣira"; Aramaic: ܢܨܪܬ‎ ‎ , "Naṣrath") is the capital and the largest city in the Northern District of Israel.  Nazareth is known as "the Arab capital of Israel".  In 2016 its population was 75,922 .  The inhabitants are predominantly Arab citizens of Israel, of whom 69% are Muslim and 30.9% Christian.  Nazareth Illit (lit.  "Upper Nazareth") is built alongside old Nazareth, and had a Jewish population of 40,312 in 2014.  The Jewish sector was declared a separate city in June 1974.
