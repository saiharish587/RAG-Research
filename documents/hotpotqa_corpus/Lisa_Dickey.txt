Lisa Dickey: Lisa Dickey, a native of Pensacola, Florida, is an American author and book collaborator.  Since 1997, she has helped write and/or edit 17 nonfiction books, in fields ranging from technology to politics to Hollywood memoirs.  Eight books she worked on have been on the New York Times best seller or extended best seller lists.  In January 2017, St. Martin's Press published her first non-collaborative book, "Bears in the Streets: Three Journeys Across a Changing Russia".
