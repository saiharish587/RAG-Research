Ceephax Acid Crew (album): Ceephax Acid Crew is the name of two 12" vinyl EPs and a double-CD album of music by Ceephax Acid Crew.  All tracks except "Milk Tray" from "Part 1" and "Acid Vaccination" from "Part Two" were released on the double-CD.  Additional songs on the double-CD are from "Radiotin EP", "Bainted Smile EP" and "Acid Legacy EP".
