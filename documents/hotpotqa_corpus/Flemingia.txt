Flemingia: Flemingia is a genus of flowering plants in the legume family Fabaceae.  It is native to Asia and the species are distributed in Bhutan, Burma, China, India; Indonesia, Laos, Malaysia, Nepal, Pakistan, Papua New Guinea, Philippines, Sri Lanka, Taiwan, Thailand and Vietnam.  The genus was founded in 1812.  The number of known species is ambiguous due to taxonomic problems; and is usually enumerated as more than 30.  Burma and China have the highest record of "Flemingia" species with 16 each, followed by India (with 15 species), Thailand (11 species), Laos (10 species), Vietnam (8 species), Bhutan (1 species) and Nepal (5 species).
