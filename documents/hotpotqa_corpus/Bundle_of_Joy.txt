Bundle of Joy: Bundle of Joy (1956) is a Technicolor musical remake of the comedy film "Bachelor Mother" (1939), which starred Ginger Rogers.  It stars Eddie Fisher and Debbie Reynolds (who were married in real-life at the time), and Adolphe Menjou.  An unmarried salesgirl at a department store finds and takes care of an abandoned baby.  Much confusion results when her co-workers assume the child is hers and that the father is the son of the store owner.
