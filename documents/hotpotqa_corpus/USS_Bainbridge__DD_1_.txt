USS Bainbridge (DD-1): The second USS "Bainbridge" (Destroyer No. 1/DD-1) was the first destroyer, also called "Torpedo-boat destroyers", in the United States Navy and the lead ship of the "Bainbridge"-class .  She was named for William Bainbridge.  "Bainbridge" was commissioned 12 February 1903.  She served in the Asiatic Fleet before World War I and served in patrol and convoy duty during the war.  She was decommissioned 3 July 1919.
