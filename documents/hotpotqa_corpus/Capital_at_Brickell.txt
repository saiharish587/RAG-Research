Capital at Brickell: CCCC Miami Towers is the preliminary name for a mixed-use project being planned in the Brickell neighborhood of Miami, Florida, United States.  The site is bounded by South Miami Avenue to the east, SW 14th Terrace to the south, SW 1st Avenue to the west, and SW 14th Street to the north.  The site broke ground in 2006 and was excavated for a previous project that stalled known as Capital at Brickell.  It was revived in 2014 by China City Construction Corp, an affiliate of China Communications Construction Company.
