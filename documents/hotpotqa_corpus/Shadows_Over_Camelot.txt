Shadows Over Camelot: Shadows Over Camelot is an Arthurian-themed board game designed by Serge Laget and Bruno Cathala, illustrated by Julien Delval and Cyrille Daujean.  The game was unveiled by the publishers Days of Wonder at the 2005 American International Toy Fair and was more widely released in May and June 2005.  The game was also published in French as "Les Chevaliers de la Table Ronde" and in German as "Schatten über Camelot".  In 2008, an expansion for Shadows over Camelot was released titled "Merlin's Company".
