Sox Appeal: Sox Appeal was a reality television series that premiered August 1, 2007 on NESN and ended in 2008.  It is a Boston Red Sox themed dating game show that follows a man or woman during three, two-inning long blind dates that take place over the course of a Red Sox game.  During the seventh inning stretch, the fan chooses the date he/she wants to continue dating.  The date, however, can choose not to continue the date.
