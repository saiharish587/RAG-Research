Mortadelo and Filemon: Mission Implausible: Mortadelo and Filemon: Mission Implausible (Spanish: "Mortadelo y Filemón contra Jimmy el Cachondo" , "Mortadelo and Filemon versus Jimmy the Joker") is a 2014 Spanish animated comedy film based on the characters from the "Mort & Phil" comic book series.  It achieved six nominations for the 29th Goya Awards, winning in the Best Adapted Screenplay and Best Animated Film categories, and two for the second edition of Premios Feroz.
