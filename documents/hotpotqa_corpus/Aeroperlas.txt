Aeroperlas: Aeroperlas (acronym for Aerolíneas Islas de Las Perlas) was a regional airline based in Panama City, Panama.  It was the third largest airline of the country, only surpassed by Air Panama and Copa Airlines.  From its hub at Marcos A. Gelabert and Enrique Malek International airports, Aeroperlas operated over 50 daily scheduled flights to 15 domestic destinations, as well as charter and courier flights.  It operated services as part of the Grupo TACA Regional Airlines system.
