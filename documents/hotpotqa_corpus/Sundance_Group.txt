Sundance Group: In 1969, Robert Redford purchased 6000 acre at the base of 12000 ft Mount Timpanogos in Utah's Wasatch Mountains.  He named this land Sundance (after his character in the 1969 film "Butch Cassidy and the Sundance Kid").  The Sundance Group is a listing of all the businesses that run under the Sundance corporate umbrella started by and run by actor Robert Redford.
