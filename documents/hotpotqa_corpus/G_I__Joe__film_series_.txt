G.I. Joe (film series): G.I. Joe is a military science fiction action film series, based on Hasbro's G.I. Joe toy, comic and media franchises.  Development for the first film began in 2003, but when the United States launched the invasion of Iraq in March 2003, Hasbro suggested adapting the "Transformers" instead.  In 2009, the first film was released under the title, "".  A second film, "" was released in 2013.  A third film in the series has been confirmed to be in early development, while a possible crossover with the "Transformers" franchise is being considered.
