Police Story 2013: Police Story 2013 (also known as Police Story: Lockdown) is a 2013 Chinese-Hong Kong action crime thriller film directed and written by Ding Sheng, and starring Jackie Chan in another reboot of the "Police Story" film series.  The film is directed by Ding Sheng, who previously helmed Chan's "Little Big Soldier".  Unlike the previous "Police Story" films where he portrays a Hong Kong cop, Chan instead portrays a mainland Chinese officer.
