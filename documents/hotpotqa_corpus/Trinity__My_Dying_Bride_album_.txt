Trinity (My Dying Bride album): Trinity is a compilation album by death/doom metal band My Dying Bride.  The album combines tracks from previously released EPs.  This compilation CD also has two different versions of the front cover.  The American release version has a different cover to the version released in the rest of the world.  In 2004 the CD was re-released and given an extra 2 tracks taken from the band's first demo, "Towards the Sinister".
