Molodtsov alphabet: The Cyrillic Molodtsov alphabet (Komi: Молодцов анбур , "Molodcov anbur") is an alphabet derived from Cyrillic that was used in the 1920s and 1930s to write two versions of the Komi language; Komi-Zyrian and Komi-Permyak.  It was replaced by the Latin Molodtsov alphabet in 1931 and later by the Cyrillic alphabet in the Komi Autonomous Soviet Socialist Republic.
