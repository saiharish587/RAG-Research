Melanie Owen: Melanie Jane "Mel" Owen (also Healy and Beale) is a fictional character from the BBC soap opera "EastEnders", played by Tamzin Outhwaite.  She appeared from 19 October 1998 to 12 April 2002 when Outhwaite opted to leave the role.  Mel was introduced by executive producer Matthew Robinson and became a prominent character during her tenure in the soap.
