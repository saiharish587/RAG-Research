Tomingley: Tomingley is a town in the Central West region of New South Wales, Australia.  The town is in the Narromine Shire local government area and on the Newell Highway, 425 km west of the state capital, Sydney and 54 km south west of the regional centre of Dubbo.  At the 2011 census , Tomingley had a population of 330.  Tomingley is famous for gold mining and owes its origin to that.  It is also known for the story that after WWII the military disposed of a large quantity of guns and other war materials down the biggest mine in the area.
