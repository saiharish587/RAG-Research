Jurassic Park: The Game: Jurassic Park: The Game is an episodic graphic adventure video game based on the "Jurassic Park" film franchise.  The game was developed by Telltale Games as part of a licensing deal with Universal Pictures.  The plot of the game takes place during and after the plot of the first film.  The game was originally announced in June 2010 before being revealed in the February issue of "Game Informer".  The first episode was expected for release in April 2011, but was pushed back to November 15 along with the PlayStation 3 and Xbox 360 release, resulting in a multi-platform release with all four episodes at once.
