18th Infantry Division (United Kingdom): The 18th Infantry Division was an infantry division of the British Army and briefly fought in the Malayan Campaign of the Second World War.  In March 1939, following the re-emergence of Germany and the German occupation of Czechoslovakia, the British army increased the number of divisions within the Territorial Army by duplicating existing units.  The 18th Infantry Division was formed in September 1939 as a 2nd Line duplicate of the 54th (East Anglian) Infantry Division.  Through 1941, the division remained based in Britain undergoing training and being moved to various parts of the country.  Towards the end of the year, the decision was made to deploy the division to the Middle East as reinforcements for the upcoming Allied offensive codenamed Operation Crusader.  It left Britain during October, bound for Nova Scotia to be transported to Egypt via American ships.
