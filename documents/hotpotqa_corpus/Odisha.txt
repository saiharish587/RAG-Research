Odisha: Odisha ( ; formerly Orissa, ) is one of the 29 states of India, located in eastern India.  It is surrounded by the states of West Bengal to the north-east, Jharkhand to the north, Chhattisgarh to the west and north-west, and Andhra Pradesh to the south.  Odisha has 485 km of coastline along the Bay of Bengal on its east, from Balasore to Ganjam.  It is the 9th largest state by area, and the 11th largest by population.  It is also the 3rd most populous state of India in terms of tribal population.
