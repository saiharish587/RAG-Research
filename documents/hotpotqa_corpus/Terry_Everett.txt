Terry Everett: Robert Terry Everett (born February 15, 1937) is an American politician and a Republican former member of the United States House of Representatives from Alabama's 2nd congressional district.  He served from 1993 to his retirement in 2009.  Everett was born on February 15, 1937, in Dothan, Alabama, the son of Bob and Thelma Everett.  He lived and attended school in Midland City, Alabama.
