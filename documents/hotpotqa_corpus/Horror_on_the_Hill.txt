Horror on the Hill: Horror on the Hill is an adventure module published by TSR, Inc. in 1983, for the Basic Rules of the "Dungeons & Dragons" fantasy role-playing game.  Its product designation was TSR 9078.  This 32-page book was designed by Douglas Niles, and features cover artwork by Jim Roslof.  It is intended for beginning gamemasters and 5-10 player characters of level 1-3.  The module contains around 20 encounters on the surface, a monastery, three dungeon levels and three new monsters.
