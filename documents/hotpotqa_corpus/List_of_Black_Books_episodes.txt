List of Black Books episodes: "Black Books", an English sitcom television series created by Dylan Moran and Graham Linehan, premiered on 29 September 2000 on Channel 4 in the United Kingdom and Ireland, and ended on 15 April 2004.  The show spans 18 episodes over three series.  The original 45 minute pilot, recorded in 1998, has not been broadcast and all other episodes were approximately 25 minutes long.  Moran and Linehan decided to end the show in its third series.  Moran has stated that there will be no more episodes and "The Times" reported the series has been "killed off".
