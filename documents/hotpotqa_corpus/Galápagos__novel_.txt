Galápagos (novel): Galápagos is the eleventh novel written by American author Kurt Vonnegut.  The novel questions the merit of the human brain from an evolutionary perspective.  The title is both a reference to the islands on which part of the story plays out, and a tribute to Charles Darwin on whose theory Vonnegut relies to reach his own conclusions.  It was first published in 1985 by Delacorte Press.
