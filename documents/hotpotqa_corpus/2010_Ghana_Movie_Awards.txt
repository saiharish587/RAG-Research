2010 Ghana Movie Awards: The 2010 Ghana Movie Awards was the maiden edition of the ceremony to reward cinematic achievement in Ghana Film Industry.  The event was held at the Golden Tulip Hotel, Accra on 25th December 2010. " Sinking Sands", Juliet Ibrahim, Nadia Buari, John Dumelo, Yvonne Okoro, Majid Michel, John Dumelo & Genevieve Nnaji were among the winners.
