Sri Lankan Tamil nationalism: Sri Lankan Tamil nationalism is the conviction of the Sri Lankan Tamil people, a minority ethnic group in the South Asian island country of Sri Lanka (formerly known as Ceylon), that they have the right to constitute an independent or autonomous political community.  This idea has not always existed.  Sri Lankan Tamil national awareness began during the era of British rule during the nineteenth century, as Tamil Hindu revivalists tried to counter Protestant missionary activity.  The revivalists, led by Arumuga Navalar, used literacy as a tool to spread Hinduism and its principles.
