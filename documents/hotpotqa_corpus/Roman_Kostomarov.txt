Roman Kostomarov: Roman Sergeyevich Kostomarov (Russian: Роман Серге́евич Костомаров , born 8 February 1977) is a Russian ice dancer.  With partner Tatiana Navka, he is the 2006 Olympic champion, two-time World champion (2004–05), three-time Grand Prix Final champion (2003–05), and three-time European champion (2004–06).
