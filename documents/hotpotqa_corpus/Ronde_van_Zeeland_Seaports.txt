Ronde van Zeeland Seaports: Ronde van Zeeland Seaports is a single-day road bicycle race held annually in Zeeland, Netherlands.  The race was created in 2012, as a 1.1 race on the UCI Europe Tour, after financial issues curtailed the Delta Tour Zeeland stage-race, which had been held since 2008.  The first race was won by South African rider Reinardt Janse van Rensburg of the team.
