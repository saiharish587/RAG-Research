Jamie Hince: James "Jamie" William Hince (born 19 December 1968) is an English guitarist, singer, songwriter, best known as the guitarist for the indie rock duo The Kills.  He started his musical career in bands called Fiji, Scarfo, and Blyth Power.  He co-founded The Kills with American singer Alison Mosshart in 2000.  In The Kills, Hince is known as "Hotel" and Mosshart is known as "VV."
