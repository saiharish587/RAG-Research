2009 DFB-Pokal Final: The final of the 2008–09 DFB-Pokal season was held on 30 May 2009 at the Olympiastadion, Berlin.  Werder Bremen won with a 58th-minute goal from midfielder Mesut Özil.  This was the club's sixth DFB-Pokal in its history, after victories in 1961, 1991, 1994, 1999 and 2004.  This was Bayer Leverkusen's DFB-Pokal final loss of the decade, the other occurring in 2002.  Werder Bremen lost the 2009 UEFA Cup final ten days prior to the DFB-Pokal final, losing to Ukrainian side Shakhtar Donetsk.
