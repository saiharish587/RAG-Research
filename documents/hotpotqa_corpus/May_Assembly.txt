May Assembly: May Assembly (Serbian: Мајска скупштина / Majska skupština ) was the national assembly of the Serbs in Austrian Empire, held on 1 and 3 May 1848 in Sremski Karlovci, during which the Serbs proclaimed autonomous Serbian Vojvodina.  This action was later recognized by the supreme Austrian authority in Vienna.  May Assembly was part of the European Revolutions of 1848.
