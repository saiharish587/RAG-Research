Raffi Freedman-Gurspan: Raffi Freedman-Gurspan (born May 3, 1987 in Intibucá, Honduras) is an American transgender rights activist and the first openly transgender person to work as a White House staffer.  She was also the first openly transgender legislative staffer to work in the Massachusetts House of Representatives.  She currently serves as director of external relations at the National Center for Transgender Equality, based in Washington, DC.  She is a longtime advocate and public policy specialist on matters concerning human rights, gender, and LGBT people.
