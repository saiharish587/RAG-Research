The Big O (album): The Big O is the fifteenth music album recorded by Roy Orbison, his first for London Records in the United Kingdom, with the music and backing vocals provided by English group, the Art Movement on all tracks except for "Penny Arcade", which was a studio recording and released as a single in the UK in 1969, where it peaked at #27 and would be Orbison's last UK chart success during his lifetime.  "Penny Arcade" was also his biggest hit in Australia, spending four weeks at Number One around Christmas, 1969.  The second single, "Break My Mind", was Orbison's last Australian chart success during his lifetime, reaching #24 in March 1970.  The album was released in Europe in early 1970.
