2013–14 Pacific Tigers women's basketball team: The 2013–14 Pacific Tigers women's basketball team represented the University of the Pacific during the 2013–14 NCAA Division I women's basketball season.  The Tigers had a season of new beginnings as they joined a new conference- the West Coast Conference.  Pacific was one of the founders of what became the WCC.  After four decades the Tigers returned, allowing the WCC to return to a travel partner scenario.  Pacific and Saint Mary's became travel partners.  The other 4 sets of travel partners were San Francsico and Santa Clara, Loyola Marymount and Pepperdine, Gonzaga and Portland, and BYU and San Diego.  The Tigers were led by eighth year head coach Lynne Roberts and played their home games in the Alex G. Spanos Center.  The Tigers would finish the season 18–13, placing third in the WCC, and participate in their third consecutive WNIT Tournament.
