Streets of Gold: Streets of Gold is the third studio album by American electronic music duo 3OH! 3.  It was released on June 29, 2010 in the United States and July 19, 2010 in the United Kingdom.  The album debuted at number seven on the US "Billboard" 200, selling 41,000 copies in its first week.  Upon its release, "Streets of Gold" received generally mixed reviews from most music critics.
