Cole Konrad: Cole Konrad (born April 2, 1984) is a retired American mixed martial arts fighter.  He is currently a student at the University of Minnesota and former wrestler for the Golden Gophers.  Konrad won a gold medal at the 2005 Pan-American Championships.  He won the NCAA wrestling championships in 2006 and 2007 at 285 pounds and finished third at the 2006 World University Championships at 120 kilograms.  He was the first Bellator Heavyweight Champion.
