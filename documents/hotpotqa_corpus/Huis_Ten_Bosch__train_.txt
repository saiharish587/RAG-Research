Huis Ten Bosch (train): The Huis Ten Bosch (ハウステンボス , Hausutenbosu ) is a limited express train service operated by Kyushu Railway Company (JR Kyushu) in Japan.  It runs between Hakata Station in Fukuoka, Fukuoka, and Huis Ten Bosch Station, the station for the Huis Ten Bosch theme park in Sasebo, Nagasaki.
