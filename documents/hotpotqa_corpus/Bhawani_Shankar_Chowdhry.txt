Bhawani Shankar Chowdhry: Bhawani Shankar Chowdhry (Urdu: بھوانی شنکر چوڈري‎ ) (born 1 January 1959), is a Pakistani ICT Professional and an electronics engineer.  He is the Meritorious Professor and Dean Faculty of Electrical, Electronics and Computer Engineering at the Mehran University of Engineering and Technology Jamashoro, Sindh, Pakistan.
