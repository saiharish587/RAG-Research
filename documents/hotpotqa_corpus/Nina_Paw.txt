Nina Paw: Nina Paw Hee-ching (born 20 July 1949) is a Hong Kong-based actress.  Her parents are actors Pau Fong (13 November 1922 – 22 September 2006) and Liu Su.  Her younger brother Peter Pau is Academy Awards-winning and five-times Hong Kong Film Awards-winning cinematographer.  She was married to Henry Fong, who is also an actor.
