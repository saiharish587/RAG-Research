Scarsdale diet: The Scarsdale diet is a fad diet designed for weight loss created in the 1970s by Herman Tarnower, named for the town in New York where he practiced cardiology, described in the book "The Complete Scarsdale Medical Diet plus Dr. Tarnower's Lifetime Keep-Slim Program", which Tarnower wrote with an author of self-help books, Sam Sinclair Baker.  The diet carries potential health risks and does not instill the kind of healthy eating habits required for sustainable weight loss.
