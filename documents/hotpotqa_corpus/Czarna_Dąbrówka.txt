Czarna Dąbrówka: Czarna Dąbrówka (German: "Schwarz Damerkow" ; Kashubian: "Czôrnô Dąbrówka" ) is a village in Bytów County, Pomeranian Voivodeship, in northern Poland.  Until 1945 it was part of Farther Pomerania in Prussia.  It is now the seat of the gmina (administrative district) called Gmina Czarna Dąbrówka.  It lies approximately 25 km north of Bytów and 71 km west of the regional capital Gdańsk.  The village has a population of 1,112.
