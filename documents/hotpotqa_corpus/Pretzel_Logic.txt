Pretzel Logic: Pretzel Logic is the third studio album by the American rock band Steely Dan, released on February 20, 1974 by ABC Records.  It was written by principal band members Walter Becker and Donald Fagen.  They recorded the album at The Village Recorder in West Los Angeles with producer Gary Katz.  It was the last album to feature the full five-member band of Becker, Fagen, Denny Dias, Skunk Baxter, and Jim Hodder, although on this recording, drummer Hodder appeared on vocals only.  It also featured significant contributions from many prominent Los Angeles–based studio musicians.
