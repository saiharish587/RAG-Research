Leskovec Castle: Leskovec Castle or Turn Castle (Slovene: "Grad Turn" , "Turn na Vrhu", "Turnska graščina", "Šrajbarski turn", "Grad Leskovec"; German: "Thurn am Hart" ) is a 15th-century castle north of the village of Leskovec pri Krškem (Municipality of Krško), southeastern Slovenia.  It has been redesigned in the 16th and the 18th centuries.
