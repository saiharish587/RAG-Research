Batley: Batley is a town in the Metropolitan Borough of Kirklees, West Yorkshire, England.  It lies 7 mi south-east of Bradford, 7 mi south-west of Leeds and 1 mi north of Dewsbury, near the M62 motorway.  Historically in the West Riding of Yorkshire, in 2011 its two wards (Batley East and Batley West) had a combined population of 38,573.  Other nearby towns include: Morley to the north-east, Ossett to the south-east and Brighouse west-south-west.  Batley is part of a special EU transformation zone.
