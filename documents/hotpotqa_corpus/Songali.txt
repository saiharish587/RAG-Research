Songali: Songali (English: The Spy) is a 2002 Indian Bodo Horror film directed by Khanindra Bodosa and produced by Gaurang Film Production Co-operative Society from Kokrajhar.  It stars Umesh Basumatary, Dwimalu, Somaina and Ringkumoni Basumatary in leading roles.  The film story was written by Nilkamal Brahma.  The film was released in 2002.
