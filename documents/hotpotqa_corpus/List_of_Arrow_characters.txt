List of Arrow characters: Arrow is an American television series developed by Greg Berlanti, Marc Guggenheim, and Andrew Kreisberg, based on the DC Comics character Green Arrow.  Many of the other characters appearing in the series are also based on DC Comics characters.  The series premiered on October 10, 2012 in the United States on The CW television network, and is currently in its fifth season.  On January 8, 2017, The CW announced that "Arrow" would be renewed for a sixth season.
