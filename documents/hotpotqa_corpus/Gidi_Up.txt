Gidi Up: Gidi Up is a Nigerian television and web drama series starring OC Ukeje, Deyemi Okanlawon, Somkele Iyamah, and Titilope Sonuga in lead roles.  The series is created by Jadesola Osiberu and executively produced by Lola Odedina.  It is produced by Ndani TV and sponsored by GTBank.  Episodes are first aired online through "Ndani.tv" web channel, and the first episode was aired on 20 February 2013.
