1915 Alabama Crimson Tide football team: The 1915 Alabama Crimson Tide football team (variously "Alabama", "UA" or "Bama") represented the University of Alabama in the 1915 Southern Intercollegiate Athletic Association football season.  It was the Crimson Tide's 23rd overall and 20th season as a member of the Southern Intercollegiate Athletic Association (SIAA).  The team was led by head coach Thomas Kelley, in his first year.  It was in 1915 Alabama moved its on campus home games from The Quad, where all on-campus home games had been played since 1893, and to a new location, University Field (later renamed Denny Field in honor of school president George Denny in 1920).  Home games were also played at Rickwood Field in Birmingham, Alabama.  They finished the season with a 6-2 record, 5-0 in the SIAA.
