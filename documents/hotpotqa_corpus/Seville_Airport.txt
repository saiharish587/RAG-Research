Seville Airport: Seville Airport (IATA: SVQ, ICAO: LEZL) (Spanish: "Aeropuerto de Sevilla" ) is the sixth busiest inland airport in Spain.  It is the main international airport serving Western Andalusia in southern Spain, and neighbouring provinces.  The airport has flight connections to 42 destinations around Europe and Northern Africa, and handled 4,308,852 passengers in 2015.  It serves as base for the low cost carriers Vueling and Ryanair.  It is 10 km east of downtown Seville, and some 110 km north-east of Costa de la Luz.
