Barrayar: Barrayar is a science fiction novel by Lois McMaster Bujold.  It was first published as four installments in "Analog" in July–October 1991, and then published in book form by Baen Books in October 1991.  "Barrayar" won both the Hugo Award for Best Novel and the Locus Award for Best Science Fiction Novel in 1992.  It is a part of the Vorkosigan Saga, and is the seventh full-length novel of the series, in publication order.  "Barrayar" is a direct sequel to Bujold's first novel, "Shards of Honor" (1986), and the two are paired in the 1996 omnibus "Cordelia's Honor".
