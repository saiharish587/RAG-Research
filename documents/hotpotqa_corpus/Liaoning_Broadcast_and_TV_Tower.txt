Liaoning Broadcast and TV Tower: The Liaoning Broadcast and Television Tower (Chinese: 辽宁广播电视塔, Pinyin: liáoníng guǎngbō diànshì tǎ) is a tall free-standing structure used for communication.  It was built in 1989 in Shenyang, China and is 305.5 m tall.  Within the "disk" of the tower, accessible through an elevator, there is an in-door observation deck, rotating restaurant, and a small bar.  On the top of the disk is an outdoor observation deck.  This tower is in World Federation of Great Towers.
