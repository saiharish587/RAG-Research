Laughing Sinners: Laughing Sinners is a 1931 American pre-Code Metro-Goldwyn-Mayer feature film starring Joan Crawford and Clark Gable in a story about a cafe entertainer who experiences spiritual redemption.  The dialogue by Martin Flavin was based upon the play "Torch Song" by Kenyon Nicholson.  The film was directed by Harry Beaumont.  "Laughing Sinners" was the second of eight cinematic collaborations between Crawford and Gable.
