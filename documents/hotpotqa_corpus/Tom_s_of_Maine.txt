Tom's of Maine: Tom's of Maine is a brandname and manufacturer of natural-ingredients-only personal care products, a majority-owned subsidiary of Colgate-Palmolive since 2006.  The company's products are intentionally mostly made without ingredients that are: chemically derived, have a negative environmental impact, or are tested on animals.  While most of its products are vegan, some contain propolis and/or beeswax sourced from bees.
