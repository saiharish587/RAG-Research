Starchildren: Starchildren was a side project of The Smashing Pumpkins frontman Billy Corgan.  From 1990 to 1994 the band played a few scattered live shows, each usually featuring a different lineup of band members with only Corgan having constant involvement.  The band officially released two songs, "Delusions of Candor", an original composition by Corgan, and a cover of "Isolation" by Joy Division.
