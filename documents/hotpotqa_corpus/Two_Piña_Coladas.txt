Two Piña Coladas: "Two Piña Coladas" is a song recorded by American country pop artist Garth Brooks.  It was released in March 1998 as the third single from his album "Sevens".  It hit No. 1 on the Billboard Country Charts in 1998.  A concert version is available on "Double Live."  The song was written by Shawn Camp, Benita Hill and Sandy Manson.  Shawn Camp provides an additional acoustic guitar.
