Qionglai City: Qionglai () is a county-level city under the jurisdiction of Chengdu, the capital of Sichuan province, People's Republic of China.  It is located around 60 km from downtown Chengdu.  The city is located on the western edge of the Sichuan Basin and in the foothills of the Qionglai Mountains that bound the basin from the west, and is bordered by the prefecture-level city of Ya'an to the west.
