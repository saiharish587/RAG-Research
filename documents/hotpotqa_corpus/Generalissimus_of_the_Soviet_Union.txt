Generalissimus of the Soviet Union: Generalissimo of the Soviet Union (Russian: Генералиссимус Советского Союза ; "Generalissimus Sovétskogo Soyuza") was a proposed military rank created on 27 June 1945, following the tradition of the Imperial Russian Army.  It was granted to Joseph Stalin following World War II; however, Stalin refused to officially approve the rank.  It would have been the highest military rank in the Soviet Union.
