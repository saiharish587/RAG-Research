Fallen In Love: Fallen In Love was published on January 24, 2012.  The author of this novel is Lauren Kate.  This book is a young adult fiction novel based on the characters from that author's most notable series, Fallen.  The author takes these characters and writes more in depth about them, especially Lucinda and Daniel, and was inspired by her fans to write this novel.  It is set during the most romantic holiday, Valentine's Day, and tells the stories of each couple in the book and how their dates play out.  Each story in the book is narrated by that particular person.
