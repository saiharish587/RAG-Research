Wendell Phillips Academy High School: Wendell Phillips Academy High School is a public 4–year high school located in the Bronzeville neighborhood on the south side of Chicago, Illinois, United States.  Phillips is part of the Chicago Public Schools district and is managed by the Academy for Urban School Leadership.  It is named for the noted American abolitionist Wendell Phillips.  It was the first predominantly African-American high school in Chicago.  The school opened in 1904.  In 2010, Phillips became a "turnaround" school in an effort to lower the school's one–year dropout rate of 66.8 percent.  The school received the Spotlight on Technology award from the Chicago Public Schools leadership technology summit in 2013.  The school's attendance boundary includes areas of the South Side, Chinatown, and portions of the Chicago Loop.
