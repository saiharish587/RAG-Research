Marek Langhamer: Marek Langhamer (born 22 July 1994) is a Czech professional ice hockey goaltender.  He is currently playing with the Tucson Roadrunners in the American Hockey League (AHL) as a prospect under contract with the Arizona Coyotes of the National Hockey League (NHL).  Langhamer was selected by the Phoenix Coyotes in the 7th round (184th overall) of the 2012 NHL Entry Draft.
