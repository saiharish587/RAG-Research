Cornell Big Red men's ice hockey: The Cornell Big Red men's ice hockey team is a National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I college ice hockey program that represents Cornell University.  Cornell competes in the ECAC Hockey conference and plays its home games at Lynah Rink in Ithaca, New York.  Six of the eight Ivy League schools sponsor men's hockey and all six teams play in the twelve-team ECAC.  The Ivy League crowns a champion based on the results of the games played between its members during the ECAC season.
