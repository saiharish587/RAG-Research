Yokohama Dreamland: Yokohama Dreamland was an amusement park that operated in Totsuka, Yokohama, Japan from 1964 to 2002.  When opened on August 1, 1964, it was Japan's first major amusement park, with a total area of 1320000 m2 .  The management company, , was acquired by the supermarket chain Daiei in 1993, and the amusement park closed permanently on February 17, 2002, due to financial issues.  At the time of its closing, the amusement park was downsized to 145776 m2 .
