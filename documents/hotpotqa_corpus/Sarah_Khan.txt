Sarah Khan: Sarah Khan (Urdu: ‎ ) is a Pakistani actress who appears in Urdu television series.  Khan made her screen debut with a supporting role in the 2012 Hum TV's television serial "Badi Aapa", and followed it with supporting roles in several successful television series.  She rose to prominence with the role of a selfish girl who aspires to be rich in the romantic drama "Alvida" (2015).  Khan then played a headstrong housewife in the mystry drama "Mohabbat Aag Si" (2015), which earned her a Best Supporting Actress award at Hum.  Subsequently, she garnered wide recognition and public appreciation for portraying leading roles the romantic drama "Tumhare Hain" and the black magic-based romance "Nazr-e-Bad" (both 2017).
