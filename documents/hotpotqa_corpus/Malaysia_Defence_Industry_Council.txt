Malaysia Defence Industry Council: The Malaysia Defence Industry Council (MDIC), formed in August, 1999, is tasked with coordinating the orderly development of the Malaysia Defence Industry Sector.  It is chaired by the Malaysian Minister of Defence, and involves 56 members from the private sector as well as from the Government.  It functions to not only support the local development sector, but to market it to overseas buyers and help companies access foreign markets.  It also aims to build relationships with other defence industries.
