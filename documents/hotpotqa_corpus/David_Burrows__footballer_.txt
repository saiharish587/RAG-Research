David Burrows (footballer): David Burrows (born Dudley, 25 October 1968) is an English former footballer.  During his career he played for West Bromwich Albion, Liverpool, West Ham United, Everton, Coventry City, Birmingham City and Sheffield Wednesday as well as the England Under-21 and B teams.  As a Liverpool player he won the Football League, the FA Cup and twice won the Charity Shield.
