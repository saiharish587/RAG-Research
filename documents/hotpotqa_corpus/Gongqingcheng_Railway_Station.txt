Gongqingcheng Railway Station: Gongqingcheng Railway Station is a railway station located in Gongqingcheng city of the Jiujiang city prefecture, in Jiangxi province, eastern China.  It serves the Changjiu Intercity Railway and Nanchang–Jiujiang Intercity Railway.  The station is accessed by Jiuxian Avenue and close to Route G70 Highway.
