So Alive (Skepta and N-Dubz song): "So Alive" is a song by British MC Skepta and British trio, N-Dubz.  It was released as an official single on 6 February 2011.  It is the fourth single released Skepta's third album "Doin' It Again" and N-Dubz's third album "Love.Live.Life".  The single peaked at #99 on the UK Singles Chart.
