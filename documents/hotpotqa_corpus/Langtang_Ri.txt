Langtang Ri: Langtang Ri is a mountain in the Langtang Himal of the Himalayas.  At an elevation of 7205 m it is the 106th highest mountain in the world.  Located on the border between the Bagmati Zone of Nepal and Tibet, China, it is part of a group of high peaks that include Shishapangma (8,013 m) and Porong Ri (7,292 m).
