Anais Zanotti: Anais Zanotti (born January 8, 1986 in Saint-Tropez) is a French model and stunt woman of French and Italian ancestry who lives in Miami, Florida.  She has been featured in magazines such as "Playboy", "FHM", "Maxim", "GQ", "Cosmopolitan", "Vogue", and "People", Men's Fitness magazine, Muscle and Fitness, Women Fitness magazine and more.  She has over 40 magazine covers.  Zanotti is also a certified skydiver with over 1250 jumps.GS .
