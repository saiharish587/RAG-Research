Dryve: Dryve was an alternative/roots/pop/rock band from San Diego, California.  The band's unusual instrumentation - including a Hammond organ, accordions, and a harmonica - gave them a unique sound.  The San Diego music scene is well known for producing Christian rock bands such as Switchfoot and P.O.D., and despite its brief national life Dryve produced what the "Encyclopedia of Contemporary Christian Music" calls "stellar examples of Christian rock at its best."
