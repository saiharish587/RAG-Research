A Touch of Class (album): A Touch of Class is a big band jazz album recorded by the Thad Jones/Mel Lewis Jazz Orchestra in Warsaw, Poland in November 1978.  The first three tracks, together with a 14-minute version of "Fingers," were previously released in Poland on a Poljazz LP and all tracks were also included on the 2007 compilation album, "Thad Jones Mel Lewis Orchestra In Europe" and on the 2009 compilation CD, "The Complete Poland Concerts 1976 & 1978".
