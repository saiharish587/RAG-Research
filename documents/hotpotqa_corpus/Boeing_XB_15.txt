Boeing XB-15: The Boeing XB-15 (Boeing 294) was a United States bomber aircraft designed in 1934 as a test for the United States Army Air Corps (USAAC) to see if it would be possible to build a heavy bomber with a 5000 mi range.  For a year beginning in mid-1935 it was designated the XBLR-1.  When it first flew in 1937, it was the most massive and voluminous aircraft ever built in the US.  It set a number of load-to-altitude records for land-based aircraft, including carrying a 31205 lb payload to 8200 ft on 30 July 1939.
