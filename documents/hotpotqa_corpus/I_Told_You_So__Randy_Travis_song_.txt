I Told You So (Randy Travis song): "I Told You So" is a song written and recorded by American country music singer Randy Travis from his 1988 album, "Always & Forever".  It reached number one on the U.S. "Billboard" and Canadian "RPM" country singles charts in June 1988.  Travis had first recorded it on his 1983 album "Live at the Nashville Palace" under his stage name "Randy Ray".  It became a local hit and one of his most requested songs at the club.  In 2007, the song was covered by Carrie Underwood on her album "Carnival Ride".  Her version was released in February 2009 and was re-recorded and re-released in March as a duet with Travis.  Underwood's and Travis's duet peaked at number two on the U.S. country charts in 2009.
