Ottawa Fury (2005–13): The Ottawa Fury were a Canadian soccer team based in Ottawa, Ontario.  The team played in the USL Premier Development League (PDL) from 2005 to 2013.  The organization joined the North American Soccer League (NASL) as Ottawa Fury FC beginning in the 2014 season.  The team's home stadium was the Algonquin College soccer complex; its colours were white, red and blue.
