Roman Catholic Diocese of Kottapuram: The Roman Catholic Diocese of Kottapuram (Latin: "Kottapuramen(sis)" ) is a diocese located in the city of Kottapuram in the Ecclesiastical province of Verapoly in India.  On Saturday, December 18, 2010, Pope Benedict XVI named Joseph Karikkassery, Auxiliary Bishop of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Verapoly, as Bishop of Kottapuram.  The diocese had until then been a vacant see (sede vacante), as Bishop Francis Kallarakal had been appointed by Pope Benedict XVI in February 2010 as Metropolitan Archbishop of the Roman Catholic Archdiocese of Verapoly, India (the Diocese of Kottapuram is in its Ecclesiastical Province).  The previous Archbishop of Verapoly had died.
