Stanley, Falkland Islands: Stanley ( ; also known as Port Stanley) is the capital of the Falkland Islands.  It is located on the island of East Falkland, on a north-facing slope in one of the wettest parts of the islands.  At the 2012 census, the town had a population of 2,121; the entire population of the Falkland Islands was 2,841 on Census Day on 15 April 2012.
