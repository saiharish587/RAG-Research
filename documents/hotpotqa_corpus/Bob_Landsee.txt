Bob Landsee: Robert John Landsee (born March 21, 1963) is a former American football player and coach.  He was also the head coach and owner of the Milwaukee Mustangs of the Arena Football League (AFL) till the team folded.  He is a former American football center and guard who played for two seasons in the National Football League (NFL).  He played for the Philadelphia Eagles from 1986–1987.  He was drafted by the Eagles in the sixth round of the 1986 NFL Draft.  He played college football at Wisconsin.
