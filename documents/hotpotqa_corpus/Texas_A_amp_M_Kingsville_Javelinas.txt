Texas A&amp;M–Kingsville Javelinas: The Texas A&M–Kingsville Javelinas ( ) are the athletic teams that represent Texas A&M University–Kingsville (TAMUK).  Some of the women's athletic teams use the name "Lady Javelinas;" however, the school's other teams use the "Javelina" name.  The school's athletic program fields teams in 11 varsity sports and numerous club and intramural sports.  The Javelina serves as the mascot representing the teams, and the school colors are blue and gold.
