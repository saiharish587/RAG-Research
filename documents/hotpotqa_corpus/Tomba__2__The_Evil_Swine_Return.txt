Tomba! 2: The Evil Swine Return: Tomba!  2: The Evil Swine Return in North America, Tombi!  2 in Europe and Tomba!  The Wild Adventures (トンバ！ ザ・ワイルドアドベンチャー , Tonba!  Za Wairudo Adobenchā ) in Japan, is a platform video game developed by Whoopee Camp and published by Sony Computer Entertainment for the PlayStation and is the sequel to the 1998 game "Tomba! ".  The game was released in Japan on October 28, 1999, in North America on December 31, 1999 and in Europe on June 16, 2000.  The game was re-released on the PlayStation Network as a PSone Classic in Japan on September 28, 2011, in Europe on November 21, 2012 and in North America on February 18, 2014 in Japanese version.  The English-language version of the game was released on the PlayStation Network in North America on November 5, 2015.
