Richa Chadda: Richa Chadda is an Indian actress who works in Hindi films.  After debuting with a bit role in the comedy film "Oye Lucky!  Lucky Oye! " (2008), she worked as a supporting actress in several films.  Her major breakthrough was a supporting role in the film noir gangster saga "Gangs of Wasseypur" (2012), where her role as the bellicose and extremely foul-tongued wife of a gangster earned her a Filmfare award.  Her sole foray into mainstream cinema has been as a supporting character in "Goliyon Ki Rasleela Ram-Leela" (2013).
