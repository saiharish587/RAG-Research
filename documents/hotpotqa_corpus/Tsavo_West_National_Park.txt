Tsavo West National Park: Tsavo West National Park is located in the Coast Province of Kenya.  The park covers an area of 9,065 square kilometres.  The A109 road Nairobi-Mombasa and a railway divides it from the adjoining Tsavo East National Park.  Together with adjoining ranches and protected areas, they comprise the Tsavo Conservation Area.  Tsavo West is a more popular destination on account of its magnificent scenery, Mzima Springs, rich and varied wildlife, good road system, rhino reserve, rock climbing potential and guided walks along the Tsavo River.  The park is operated by Kenya Wildlife Service.
