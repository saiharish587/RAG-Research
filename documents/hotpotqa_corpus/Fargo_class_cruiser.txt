Fargo-class cruiser: The "Fargo"-class cruisers were a modified version of the previous "Cleveland"-class cruiser design; the main difference was a more compact pyramidal superstructure with single trunked funnel, intended to improve the arcs of fire of the anti-aircraft (AA) guns.  The same type of modification differentiated the "Baltimore" and "Oregon City" class es of heavy cruisers. Changes were made to order to reduce the instability of the Cleveland-class light cruisers, especially their tendency to roll dangerously.  The main battery turrets sat about a foot lower and the wing gunhouses (the 5 inch, twin gun mounts on the sides of the ship) were lowered to the main deck.  The medium (40 mm) anti-aircraft mounts were also lowered.
