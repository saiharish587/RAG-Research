Wentworth, New South Wales: Wentworth is a small border town in the far south west of the state of New South Wales, Australia.  It lies at the confluence of Australia's two most important rivers, the Darling and the Murray, the latter forming the border with the state of Victoria to the south.  The border with the state of South Australia lies approximately 100 km to the west.  The town of Wentworth is in the local government area of the same name.
