Ramos Arizpe Assembly: The Ramos Arizpe Assembly is a General Motors automobile factory in Ramos Arizpe, Coahuila, Mexico.  It opened in 1981 and has manufactured vehicles from Buick, Cadillac, Chevrolet, GMC, Oldsmobile, Pontiac, Saturn, and Saab.  It currently produces the Chevrolet Cruze, Sonic and along with the San Luis Potosi Assembly produces the Chevrolet Equinox.
