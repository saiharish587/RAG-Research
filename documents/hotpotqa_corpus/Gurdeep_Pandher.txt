Gurdeep Pandher: Gurdeep Pandher (Punjabi: ਗੁਰਦੀਪ ਪੰਧੇਰ) is a Canadian artist and author.  He has published two books, a biography of Kalpana Chawla titled "Among the Stars! : Life and Dreams of Kalpana Chawla", and a Punjabi book of poetry called "Mitti De Ghar". .  He choreographs, teaches, and performs Bhangra to advocate for diversity and cultural acceptance
