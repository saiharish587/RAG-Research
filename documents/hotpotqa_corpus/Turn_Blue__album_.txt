Turn Blue (album): Turn Blue is the eighth studio album by American rock duo the Black Keys.  It was co-produced by Danger Mouse and the band, and was released on Nonesuch Records on May 12, 2014.  The record was the group's fourth collaboration with Danger Mouse, following their previous studio album, "El Camino" (2011), which was their biggest commercial and critical success to that point.  For "Turn Blue", Danger Mouse reprised his role from "El Camino" as an equal songwriting partner alongside guitarist Dan Auerbach and drummer Patrick Carney.
