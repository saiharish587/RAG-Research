Merv: Merv (Turkmen: Merw , "Мерв", مرو; Persian: مرو‎ ‎ , "Marv"), formerly Achaemenid Satrapy of Margiana, and later Alexandria (Margiana) (Ἀλεξάνδρεια) and Antiochia in Margiana (Greek: Ἀντιόχεια τῆς Μαργιανῆς ), was a major oasis-city in Central Asia, on the historical Silk Road, located near today's Mary in Turkmenistan.
