Muhammad Bek Abu El Dahab Complex: The Mosque of Abu Dahab is a mosque in Cairo, Egypt just beside Al Azhar Mosque.  The mosque was built by the Amir Mohamed Beh Abu El Dahab, one of the leaders of Egypt during the rule of the Ottoman Empire.  It is located in Al Azhar Street, beside the main entrance of the Azhar Mosque and it is the fourth mosque in Egypt to be built according to the Ottoman style of architecture.  It was originally built as a Madrasa to host the number of students that were coming to study in the Azhar University.
