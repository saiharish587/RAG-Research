Faraway, So Close!: Faraway, So Close!  (German: In weiter Ferne, so nah! )  is a 1993 German fantasy film directed by Wim Wenders.  The screenplay is by Wenders, Richard Reitinger and Ulrich Zieger.  It is a sequel to Wenders' 1987 film "Wings of Desire".  Actors Otto Sander, Bruno Ganz and Peter Falk reprise their roles as angels who have become human.  The film also stars Nastassja Kinski, Willem Dafoe and Heinz Rühmann, in his last film role.
