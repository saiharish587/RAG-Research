James Alfred Ernest Corea: Dr James Alfred Ernest Corea was a Sri Lankan public official.  He was born in 1870.  His father was Charles Edward Bandaranaike Corea who was a Proctor of the Supreme Court.  His mother was Henrietta Seneviratne.  J.A.E Corea's father died in 1872 when his youngest son Victor was just one.  His wife Henrietta was a widow from the age of 21.
