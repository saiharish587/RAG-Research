Ammunition (Krewella EP): Ammunition is the third extended play by American electronic music duo Krewella.  It was released on May 20, 2016 by Columbia Records on streaming and digital download music services.  Ammunition was preceded by the release of the single "Beggars", on April 28, 2016, shortly followed by the promotional single "Broken Record", which was released on May 10, 2016.  It is a six-track extended play, and the duo's first major release since the departure of former member Kris Trindl.  It is also their first major release since their debut album "Get Wet".
