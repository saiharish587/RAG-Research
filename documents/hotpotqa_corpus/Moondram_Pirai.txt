Moondram Pirai: Moondram Pirai (English: "The crescent seen on the third day following new moon day" ) is a 1982 Indian Tamil-language romantic drama film written, directed and filmed by Balu Mahendra.  The film features Kamal Haasan and Sridevi in the lead roles, while Silk Smitha, Poornam Vishwanathan and Y. G. Mahendra play supporting roles.  The music for the film was composed by Ilaiyaraaja, with lyrics written by Kannadasan, Vairamuthu and Gangai Amaran.  It also featured the last song written by Kannadasan to be recorded before his death in 1981.
