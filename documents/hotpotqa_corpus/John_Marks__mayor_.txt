John Marks (mayor): John Robert Marks III (born July 25, 1947) is an American lawyer, politician, and former Mayor of Tallahassee, Florida from February 2003 until November 2014.  Marks is the longest serving mayor in Tallahassee's history.  He was elected to three consecutive terms as the city's Mayor, spanning nearly twelve years.  He won a third term in 2010, but declined to seek re-election for a fourth term in 2014.  He was succeeded by Andrew Gillum on November 21, 2014.
