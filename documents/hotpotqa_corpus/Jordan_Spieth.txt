Jordan Spieth: Jordan Alexander Spieth ( ; ; born July 27, 1993) is an American professional golfer on the PGA Tour and former world number one in the Official World Golf Ranking.  He is a three-time major winner and the 2015 FedEx Cup champion.  In April 2016, "Time" magazine named Spieth to its list of the "100 Most Influential People", noting that he "exemplifies everything that's great about sports."
