Anne E. Russon: Russon is widely published in the fields of primate behavior and ecology, is executive director of the Borneo Orangutan Society of Canada, and is the author of several popular press books dealing with Great Apes including "Orangutans: Wizards of the Rainforest", "Reaching into Thought: The Minds of the Great Apes", and "The Evolution of Thought: Evolution of Great Ape Intelligence".
