6- and 12-String Guitar: 6- and 12-String Guitar is the second album by Leo Kottke, a solo instrumental steel-string acoustic guitar album originally released by John Fahey's Takoma Records in 1969.  It is popularly known as the Armadillo album after the animal illustrated in the distinctive cover art (by Annie Elliott).  Although Kottke has had a prolific career as a recording artist, "6- and 12-String Guitar" remains his best-known album.
