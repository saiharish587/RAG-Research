Repo! The Genetic Opera: Repo!  The Genetic Opera is a 2008 American musical horror film directed by Darren Lynn Bousman.  Based on the 2002 musical of the same name, which was written and composed by Darren Smith and Terrance Zdunich, the film stars Alexa Vega, Paul Sorvino, Anthony Stewart Head, Sarah Brightman, Paris Hilton, Bill Moseley, Nivek Ogre, and Terrance Zdunich.
