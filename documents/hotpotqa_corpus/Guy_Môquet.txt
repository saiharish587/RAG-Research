Guy Môquet: Guy Môquet ((1924--)26 1924 - 22 1941(1941--) (aged 17 ) ) was a young French Communist militant.  During the German occupation of France during World War II, he was taken hostage by the Nazis and executed by firing squad in retaliation for attacks on Germans by the French Resistance.  Môquet went down in history as one of the symbols of the French Resistance.
