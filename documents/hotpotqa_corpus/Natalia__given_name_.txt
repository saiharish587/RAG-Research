Natalia (given name): Natalia is a female given name with the original Late Latin meaning of "Christmas Day" (cf. Latin natale domini).  It is currently used in this form in Italian, Romanian, Spanish, Portuguese, Greek, Russian, Ukrainian, Bulgarian and Polish.  Other forms and spellings include Natalie/Nathalie (French, Dutch, English and German), Natálie (Czech), Natália/Nathália (Portuguese, Slovak and Hungarian), Natalya/Nataliya/Natalia (Russian: Наталья, Наталия ), Nataliya or Nataliia (Ukrainian, Belarusian), Natalija (Croatian, Latvian, Serbian, Slovene and Macedonian) and Natàlia (Catalan).
