2007 New York Mets season: The New York Mets' 2007 season was the 46th regular season for the Mets.  The Mets were defending their first divisional championship since 1988.  While the Atlanta Braves were counted as possible competition, the Philadelphia Phillies were predicted as the front-runners, albeit by their own star shortstop, Jimmy Rollins.  Ultimately, Rollins' prediction rang true, as the Phillies won the NL East Division title on the last day of the regular season.  With a seven-game division lead on September 12, the Mets suffered a historic collapse by losing 12 of their last 17 games and missing the postseason.
