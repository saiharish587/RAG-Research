Mike Conaway: Kenneth Michael "Mike" Conaway ( ; born June 11, 1948) is the U.S. Representative for Texas 's 11th congressional district , serving since 2005.  He is a member of the Republican Party.  The district is located in West Texas and includes Midland, Odessa, San Angelo, Brownwood and Granbury.  Conaway was asked to lead the investigation into Russian interference in the 2016 United States elections (with assistance from Trey Gowdy and Tom Rooney) after the Intelligence Committee chair, Devin Nunes, recused himself.
