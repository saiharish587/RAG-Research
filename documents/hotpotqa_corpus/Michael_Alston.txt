Michael Alston: Michael Alston is an Australian disabled fencer.  He competed in the 1998 FESPIC Games and won a silver team medal.  At the 1998 Stoke Mandeville Games, he won a gold medal in the foil event.  That same year, he also competed at the DEFI Sport Event held in Canada.  He won a silver medal in the épée event and a bronze medal in the sabre event.  He was supported by the Blacktown City Council and was coached by Sally Kopiec.
