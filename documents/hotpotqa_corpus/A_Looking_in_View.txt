A Looking in View: "A Looking in View" is a song by American metal band Alice in Chains, featured on their fourth studio album "Black Gives Way to Blue" (2009).  It was released as the lead single from the album on June 30, 2009, marking it as the band's first single since the previous decade.  The song was the band's first release with vocalist William DuVall, who replaced the band's original vocalist, Layne Staley.  Clocking in at a length of seven minutes and six seconds, it is the longest song Alice in Chains has released as a single.
