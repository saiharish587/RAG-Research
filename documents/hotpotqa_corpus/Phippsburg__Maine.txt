Phippsburg, Maine: Phippsburg is a town in Sagadahoc County, Maine, United States, on the west side of the mouth of the Kennebec River.  The population was 2,216 at the 2010 census.  It is within the Portland–South Portland–Biddeford, Maine, metropolitan statistical area.  A tourist destination, Phippsburg is home to Bates-Morse Mountain Conservation Area, Fort Popham State Historic Site; it is also home to Fort Baldwin which overlooks Fort Popham, and Popham Beach State Park, as well as Pond Island National Wildlife Refuge.  The town includes part of Winnegance.
