Farmers' Association: The Farmers' Association (Lithuanian: "Ūkininkų Sąjunga" , ŪS) was a political party in inter-war Lithuania.  Its leaders included Aleksandras Stulginskis, Vytautas Petrulis and Kazys Jokantas.  A political party, established in 1989 under the same name, claims historical heritage of the ŪS.
