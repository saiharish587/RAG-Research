Du riechst so gut: "Du riechst so gut" (German for "You smell so good") is a song by the German Neue Deutsche Härte band Rammstein.  It was the band's first single and was released on its first album, "Herzeleid".  It describes the inner thoughts of a predator hunting his prey.  The title is said to be inspired by Patrick Süskind's "Perfume", a favourite novel of Till Lindemann's, the singer of the band.
