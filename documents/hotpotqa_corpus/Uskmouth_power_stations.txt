Uskmouth power stations: The Uskmouth power stations (also known as the Fifoots Point power stations) refers to a series of two coal-fired power station at the mouth of the River Usk in the south-east of Newport, Wales.  The first of the two station, Uskmouth A power station, was built in the 1950s and demolished in 2002.  The second station, Uskmouth B power station, was built in the 1960s.
