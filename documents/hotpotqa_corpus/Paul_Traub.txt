Paul Traub: Paul R. Traub (born January 31, 1952, Brooklyn, New York) is an American lawyer, who specializes in business law, specifically bankruptcy, insolvency, and trial litigation.  He has participated in several large retail bankruptcies, among them Kmart, FAO Schwarz Inc., KB Toys Inc., Stage Stores, Office Max, and eToys.com.  Other clients have included Halston, Joan & David, Levitz, Bombay Company, Linens 'n Things, Zales, and Whitehall Jewelers.
