AACTA Award for Best Reality Television Series: The AACTA Award for Best Reality Television Series is an award presented by the Australian Academy of Cinema and Television Arts (AACTA), a non-profit organisation whose aim is to "identify, award, promote and celebrate Australia's greatest achievements in film and television."  The award is presented at the annual AACTA Awards, which hand out accolades for achievements in feature film, television, documentaries and short films.  The award was first introduced in 2012, for the 2nd AACTA Awards in 2013, due to the growth of reality television productions in Australia.  Reality television productions could previously be submitted in the Best Light Entertainment Series category.
