Bangiya Gramin Vikash Bank: Bangiya Gramin Vikash Bank (BGVB) is a premier Regional Rural Bank established in terms of provisions of Regional Rural Banks Act 1976 & is sponsored by United Bank of India.  BGVB has been established with the amalgamation of erstwhile Mallabhum Gramin Bank, Gaur Gramin Bank, Murshidabad Gramin Bank, Nadia Gramin Bank and Sagar Gramin Bank.  This bank is sponsored by United Bank of India & Owned by Govt.  of India (50% of Share Capital Deposit), Govt.  of West Bengal (15% of Share Capital Deposit) and United Bank of India (35% of Share Capital Deposit).
