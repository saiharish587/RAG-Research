One Day (2AM and 2PM song): "One Day" is a song by the South Korean boy groups 2AM and 2PM, Oneday.  It was released in July 4, 2012 as 2AM's third Japanese single and 2PM's fifth Japanese single.  The song is the main theme song for the documentary movie "Beyond the Oneday ~Story of 2PM & 2AM~", which broadcast in Japan started on June 30.
