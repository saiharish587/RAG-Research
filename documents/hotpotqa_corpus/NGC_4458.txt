NGC 4458: NGC 4458 is an elliptical galaxy located about 54 million light-years away in the constellation of Virgo.  It was discovered by astronomer William Herschel on April 12, 1784.  NGC 4458 is a member of Markarian's Chain which is part of the Virgo Cluster.  It is in a pair with the galaxy NGC 4461.  NGC 4458 and NGC 4461 are both Interacting with each other.
