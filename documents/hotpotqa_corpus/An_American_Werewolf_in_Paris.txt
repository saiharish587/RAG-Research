An American Werewolf in Paris: An American Werewolf in Paris is a 1997 comedy horror film directed by Anthony Waller, co-written by Tim Burns, Tom Stern, and Waller, and starring Tom Everett Scott and Julie Delpy.  It follows the general concept of, and is a loose sequel to, John Landis' 1981 film "An American Werewolf in London".  The film is an international co-production between companies from the Netherlands, Luxembourg, the United States, and France.
