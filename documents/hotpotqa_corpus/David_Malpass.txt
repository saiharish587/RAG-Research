David Malpass: David R. Malpass (born March 8, 1956) is an American economist who currently serves as Under Secretary of the Treasury for International Affairs.  He previously served as Deputy Assistant Treasury Secretary under President Ronald Reagan, Deputy Assistant Secretary of State under President George H. W. Bush, and Chief Economist at Bear Stearns for the six years preceding its collapse.  During the 2016 U.S. presidential election, Malpass served as an economic advisor to Donald Trump.  In March 2017, Trump announced that Malpass would be his nominee for undersecretary for international affairs in the United States Department of the Treasury.  Malpass was confirmed for the position by the United States Senate on August 3, 2017.
