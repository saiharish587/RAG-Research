Release (The X-Files): "Release" is the seventeenth episode of the ninth season of the American science fiction television series "The X-Files".  The episode originally aired on the Fox network on May 5, 2002.  The teleplay for the episode was written by David Amann, from a story by John Shiban and Amann, and was directed by Kim Manners.  The episode helps to explore one of the show's story arc involving John Doggett finding the truth behind his son's murder.  The episode earned a Nielsen rating of 5.1, being watched by 5.38 million households, and 7.8 million viewers in its initial broadcast.  The episode received largely positive reviews from critics.
