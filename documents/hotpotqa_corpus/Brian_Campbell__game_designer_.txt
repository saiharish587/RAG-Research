Brian Campbell (game designer): Brian "Chainsaw" Campbell is a Greater Seattle area game developer, author and editor who is credited for working in the role-playing game industry as far back as 1993.  Brian's notable work includes "", "", "", Ratkin, and other World of Darkness products for White Wolf, the d20 versions of "Call of Cthulhu" and "Star Wars" for Wizards of the Coast, indie games such as "Spaceship Zero" for Green Ronin and "Fading Suns" for Holistic Design, Inc., and a foray into board games that included "Betrayal at House on the Hill" from Avalon Hill.
