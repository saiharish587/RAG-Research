William Cantelo: William Cantelo (born 1839) was a 19th-century British inventor.  Credited with developing an early machine gun, he disappeared from his home in Southampton in the 1880s.  While trying to find Cantelo, his two sons saw a photograph of American born inventor Hiram Maxim, creator of the Maxim gun; his superficial similarity to their father led them to believe that he had re-emerged under a new name.
