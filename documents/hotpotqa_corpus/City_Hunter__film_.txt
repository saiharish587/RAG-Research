City Hunter (film): City Hunter (; "Sing si lip yan") is a 1993 Hong Kong action comedy film written and directed by Wong Jing, starring Jackie Chan, Joey Wong, Kumiko Goto and Chingmy Yau.  The film is based on the Japanese manga of the same name.  The film was released in the Hong Kong on 14 January 1993 along with Stephen Chow's "Fight Back to School III".
