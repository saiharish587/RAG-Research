Ice Cream Man (business): Ice Cream Man is a business entity whose stated goal is to give away 500,000 free units of ice cream throughout the United States.  Since its founding in 2004, Ice Cream Man has given away approximately 300,000 units of ice cream.  In the process the organization has become a fixture at music festivals across the United States.  As of November 2010, Ice Cream Man has over 100 volunteers committed to the idea of free ice cream for all.
