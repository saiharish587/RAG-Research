Think Like a Man Too: Think Like a Man Too is a 2014 romantic comedy film directed by Tim Story and the sequel to Story's 2012 film "Think Like a Man" based on Steve Harvey's book "Act Like a Lady, Think Like a Man".  The script is written by David A. Newman and Keith Merryman.  The film was released on June 20, 2014.  The cast from the first film returned to reprise their roles.
