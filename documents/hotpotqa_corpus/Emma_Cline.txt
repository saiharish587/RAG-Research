Emma Cline: Emma Cline is an American writer and novelist, originally from California.  She published her first novel, "The Girls", in 2016, to positive reviews.  The book was shortlisted for the John Leonard Award from the National Book Critics Circle and the Center for Fiction's First Novel Prize.  Her stories have been published in "The New Yorker", "Tin House", "Granta" and "The Paris Review".  In 2017 Cline was named one of "Granta's" Best Young American Novelists.
