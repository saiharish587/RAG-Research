The Pakistan Anti-Hero: The Pakistan Anti-Hero is a book written by Pakistani author, journalist, cultural critic and satirist, Nadeem F.Paracha.  It is his second book.  His first book, "End of the Past" was published in 2016 by Vanguard Publications.  His second book too is published by Vanguard.  "The Pakistan Anti-Hero" is an extension of Paracha's first book in which he mapped the political evolution of Pakistani society.  In his second book he attempts to navigate the evolution of Pakistani nationalism through the study of a number of Pakistani intellectuals, artistes, sportsmen, scholars and militants.
