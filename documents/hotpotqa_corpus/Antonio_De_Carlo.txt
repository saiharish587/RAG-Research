Antonio De Carlo: Antonio De Carlo, born (1967--) 4, 1967 (age 50 ) in Tijuana, Baja California, Mexico, is a Mexican actor also known as Antonio in the late 1980s and early 1990s.  He won an Emmy Award in 2005-2006.  He is president and founder of the "Fundación Cultura Sin Fronteras AC".  After twelve years out of show business, in 2012 he returned to the soap operas performing "Magic Dragon" one of the main characters for "Miss XV", a co-production from Televisa México and Nickelodeon directed and produced by Pedro Damian.  He is also part of the cast of "Muchacha Italiana Viene a Casarse", directed by Pedro Damian (Televisa, México).
