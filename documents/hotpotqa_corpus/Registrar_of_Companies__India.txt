Registrar of Companies, India: The Registrar of Companies (ROC) is an office under the Indian Ministry of Corporate Affairs that deals with administration of the Companies Act 1956 and Companies Act, 2013.  There are currently 22 Registrars of Companies (ROC) operating from offices in all major states of India.  Some states, such as Maharashtra and Tamil Nadu, have two ROCs each.  Section 609 of the Companies Act, 1956 tasks the ROCs with the primary duty of registering companies and LLPs floated in the respective states and the union territories under their administration.
