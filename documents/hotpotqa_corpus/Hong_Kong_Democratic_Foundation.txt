Hong Kong Democratic Foundation: The Hong Kong Democratic Foundation (; HKDF) is a Hong Kong public policy think tank established initially as a political party on 27 October 1989.  The Foundation was founded in the run up to the first Legislative Council direct elections of 1991 by a group of business people and professionals who endorsed democratic ideals led by Jimmy McGregor and Dr Leong Che-hung.  The Foundation later transformed itself into a think tank and is currently chaired by Southern District Councillor Paul Zimmerman.
