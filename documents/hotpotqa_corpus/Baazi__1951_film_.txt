Baazi (1951 film): Baazi (English: "Gamble", Hindi: बाज़ी, Urdu: بازی) is a 1951 Indian Hindi film directed by Guru Dutt.  This was the second film of Dev Anand's production house Navketan Films, and as per a commitment given by Dev Anand to Guru Dutt in their days of struggle, the movie was directed by Dutt.  It was inspired by the 1946 Hollywood movie Gilda.
