The Walker (song): "The Walker" is a song by the American neo soul band Fitz and The Tantrums.  It is the second single from the band's second album "More Than Just a Dream".  The song was used in the trailers for the movies "The Boxtrolls" and "Storks", in the trailer for video game "The Sims 4", in television commercials for the 86th Academy Awards that were hosted by Ellen DeGeneres, in the 2014 Major League Baseball postseason and in commercials for Sprite, Supercuts, Reebok ZQuick and Walmart.  In 2014, the song started being used as the opening theme to the MLB Network show "High Heat", hosted by Christopher "Mad Dog" Russo.  The song is also featured in the pilot episode of the FOX television sitcom "Grandfathered".  It has also been used in the soundtrack of the video game "NBA 2K18".
