Old German Shepherd Dog: Old German Shepherd Dog (German: "Altdeutscher Schäferhund" ) is a controversial name for the long-haired variation of the German Shepherd Dog (German: "Langstockhaariger Deutscher Schäferhund" ), which is not a separate breed recognized by the Fédération Cynologique Internationale.  Nonetheless, there are efforts to establish this variety as a separate breed.
