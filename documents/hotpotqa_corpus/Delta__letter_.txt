Delta (letter): Delta (uppercase Δ, lowercase δ or 𝛿; <a href="Greek%20language">Greek</a>: δέλτα "délta", ] ) is the fourth letter of the <a href="Greek%20alphabet">Greek alphabet</a>.  In the system of Greek numerals it has a value of 4.  It was derived from the Phoenician letter dalet 𐤃, Letters that come from delta include <a href="Latin%20alphabet">Latin</a> <a href="D">D</a> and <a href="Cyrillic%20script">Cyrillic</a> <a href="De%20%28Cyrillic%29">Д</a>.
