Krishnam Raju filmography: Krishnam Raju is an Indian actor who acted more than 190 films.  Krishnam Raju entered Tollywood in 1966 with the film "Chilaka Gorinka" directed by Kotayya Pratyagatma alongside Krishna Kumari.  The film won Nandi Award for Best Feature Film - Silver for that year.  Later he acted in the mythological film "Shri Krishnavataram" which also starsN. T. Rama Rao.  He acted in many films with the established actos N. T. Rama Rao and Akkineni Nageswara Rao.  He also acted in many films with the established actresses Krishna Kumari, Rajasulochana, Jamuna and Kanchana.
