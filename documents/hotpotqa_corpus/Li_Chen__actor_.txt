Li Chen (actor): Li Chen (born 24 November 1978), also known as Jerry Li is a Chinese actor.  He is best known for being a cast member in the variety show "Keep Running".  Li is also known for his roles in television series "Beijing Love Story" (2012), "Beijing Youth" (2012) and "The Good Fellas" (2016); as well as films "Ultimate Rescue" (2008), which won him the China Movie Channel Media Awards and "Aftershock" (2010).  Li made his directorial debut in 2017 with "Sky Hunter".
