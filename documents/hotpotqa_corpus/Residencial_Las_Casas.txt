Residencial Las Casas: Residencial Fray Bartolomé de Las Casas, more commonly known as Residencial Las Casas or Las Casas, is a public housing complex located in San Juan, Puerto Rico consisting of 417 housing units.  It is under the management of the Puerto Rico Housing Authority ("Administración de Vivienda Pública" in Spanish) and is under the federal housing program of the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development.  It was named after the famous Spaniard Roman Catholic Fray Bartolomé de Las Casas, who also has a town named after him in Mexico, namely San Cristóbal de las Casas.
