Mono Mills, California: Mono Mills (also, Mono) is a ghost town in Mono County, California.  It is located 9.5 mi east-southeast of Lee Vining, at an elevation of 7356 feet (2242 m).  Mono Mills has nearly vanished.  Its site is along California State Route 120, 9.1 mi east from the junction with U.S. Route 395.
