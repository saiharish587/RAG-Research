Shaun Butler: Shaun Butler (born Goldie LeShaun Butler) is an American BMX rider.  He is an 3X XGAMES BMX Professional Athlete, and MTV Sports Music Festival Winner.  He was born to Gwen Butler on June 29, 1976 in Fullerton, California.  Butler has been riding professionally since 1993, and is one of ten professionals featured in the Dave Mirra games on PlayStation and PlayStation 2.  His sponsors include KHE Bikes, TSA Clothing, DC Shoes, Fox Racing, Spy Eyewear and Bell Sports.
