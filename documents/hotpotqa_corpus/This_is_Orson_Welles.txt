This is Orson Welles: This is Orson Welles is a 1992 book by Orson Welles (1915–1985) and Peter Bogdanovich that comprises conversations between the two filmmakers recorded over several years, beginning in 1969.  The wide-ranging volume encompasses Welles's life and his own stage, radio and film work as well as his insights on the work of others.  The interview book was transcribed by Bogdanovich after Welles's death, at the request of Welles's longtime companion and professional collaborator, Oja Kodar.  Welles considered the book his autobiography.
