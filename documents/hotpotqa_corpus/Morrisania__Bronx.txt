Morrisania, Bronx: Morrisania ( ) is the historical name for the South Bronx in New York City, New York.  The name derives from the Manor of Morrisania, the vast 2,000 acre estate of the powerful and aristocratic Morris family, who at one time owned most of the Bronx as well as much of New Jersey.  The family includes Lewis Morris, 4th Lord of the Manor, and a signer of the United States Declaration of Independence, and Gouverneur Morris, penman of the United States Constitution.  Both are buried in the crypt at St. Ann's Church of Morrisania.
