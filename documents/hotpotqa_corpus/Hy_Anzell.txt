Hy Anzell: Hy Anzell (September 7, 1923 – August 23, 2003) was a Yiddish-speaking American theater, television, and movie actor.  He originated the role of the flower shop owner, "Mr. Mushnik", in the original off-Broadway production of "Little Shop of Horrors" with Ellen Greene and Lee Wilkof.  He was also in the original 1976 Broadway cast of "Checking Out".
