Shkëlqim Muça: Shkëlqim Muça is a former Albanian football player and current coach of Flamurtari.  He will be remembered by football fans as one of the finest Albanian footballers of the 1980s, skillful with the ball, but also distinguished as an extraordinary assist man, and a strong shooter.  Muça's skills have been shown many times on the pitch: he could play in the center or the right side of the midfield.
