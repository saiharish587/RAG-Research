Page 3: Page 3 is a colloquial term for a feature formerly included in the British tabloid newspaper "The Sun".  The phrase originates with the publication of a large photograph of a topless, bare-breasted female glamour model which was usually published on the print edition's third page.  The feature first appeared in the newspaper on 17 November 1970 and on the official Page 3 website since June 1999, where it still continues.  The terms "Page 3" and "Page Three" are registered trademarks of News UK, parent company of "The Sun", although the feature has been imitated in Britain's other 'red top' tabloids and by newspapers internationally.
