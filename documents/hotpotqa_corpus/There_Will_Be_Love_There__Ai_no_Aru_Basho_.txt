There Will Be Love There (Ai no Aru Basho): There Will Be Love There (Ai no Aru Basho) (愛のある場所 , Place with Love , pronounced "Zea Uiru Bī Rabu Zea Ai no Aru Basho") is The Brilliant Green's third single, released on May 13, 1998.  It was their first number-one single on Oricon charts.  It was used as the drama Love Again's theme song.
