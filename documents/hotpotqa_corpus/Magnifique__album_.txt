Magnifique (album): Magnifique is the fifth studio album by Ratatat, released on July 17, 2015.  Ratatat began touring in early 2015 with limited stops in the midwestern US and the Coachella music festival where new songs were debuted.  On April 12, 2015, the band released "Cream on Chrome", the first single from the album.  On June 16, 2015, "Abrasive" was released as the second single.  The album features a cover of the 1971 Springwater single "I Will Return" and cover artwork collage sketches by Evan Mast and Mike Stroud.
