Karolyn Grimes: Karolyn Grimes (born July 4, 1940) is an American actress.  She is best known for her role as "Zuzu Bailey" in the Frank Capra classic "It's a Wonderful Life".  She also played "Debbie" in the 1947 Christmas film, "The Bishop's Wife" starring Cary Grant, David Niven, and Loretta Young.
