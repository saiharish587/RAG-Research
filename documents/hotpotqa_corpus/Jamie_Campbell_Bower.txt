Jamie Campbell Bower: James Metcalfe Campbell Bower (born 22 November 1988) is an English actor, singer, and model.  He is best known for his roles as Anthony Hope in "", Caius in "The Twilight Saga", King Arthur in the Starz series "Camelot", the young Gellert Grindelwald in "Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows – Part 1", and Jace Wayland in "".  As of 2017, he portrays Elizabethan playwright Kit Marlowe in the TNT series "Will".
