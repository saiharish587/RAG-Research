Are You Right There Father Ted?: "Are You Right There Father Ted?"  is the first episode of the third series of the Channel 4 television sitcom "Father Ted", and the 18th episode overall.  It is notable for being the first episode aired after the death of Dermot Morgan, who had died the day after filming for the final episode had been completed.  As a mark of respect to Morgan, the original transmission of the first episode was delayed by a week.
