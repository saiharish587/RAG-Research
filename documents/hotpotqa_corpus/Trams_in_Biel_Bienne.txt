Trams in Biel/Bienne: The Biel/Bienne tramway network (Alemannic German: "Strassenbahnnetz Biel" ; French: "Réseau tramway de Bienne" ) was part of the public transport network of the bilingual city of Biel/Bienne, in the canton of Bern, Switzerland, and its environs for more than 70 years.
