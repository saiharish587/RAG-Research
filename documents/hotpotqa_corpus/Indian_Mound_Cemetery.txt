Indian Mound Cemetery: Indian Mound Cemetery is a cemetery located along the Northwestern Turnpike (U.S. Route 50) on a promontory of the "Yellow Banks" overlooking the South Branch Potomac River and Mill Creek Mountain in Romney, West Virginia, United States.  The cemetery is centered on a Hopewellian mound, known as the Romney Indian Mound.  Indian Mound Cemetery is also the site of Fort Pearsall, the Confederate Memorial, Parsons Bell Tower, and reinterments from Romney's Old Presbyterian Cemetery.  The cemetery is currently owned and maintained by the Indian Mound Cemetery Association, Inc.
