Firestorm (comics): Firestorm is the name of several fictional superheroes appearing in American comic books published by DC Comics.  Ronnie Raymond and Martin Stein debuted as the first incarnation in "Firestorm, the Nuclear Man" No. 1 (March 1978) and was created by Gerry Conway and Al Milgrom.  Jason Rusch debuted as a modern update of the character in "Firestorm" vol.  3 No. 1, (July 2004), and was created by Dan Jolley and Chris Cross.
