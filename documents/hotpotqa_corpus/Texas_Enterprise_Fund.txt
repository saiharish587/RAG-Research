Texas Enterprise Fund: The Texas Enterprise Fund is a business incentive fund that was created by legislation in 2003.  The fund, which had an initial $295 million investment, is used for ensuring the growth of business in Texas.  One of Texas’ most competitive recruitment tools, these funds are used primarily to attract new business to the state or assist with the substantial expansion of an existing business as part of a competitive recruitment situation.  Sources indicate that since 2003 the Fund has yielded up to $6.3 billion in capital investment in Texas by out-of-state companies .
