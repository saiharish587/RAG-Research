Shape risk: Shape risk in finance is a type of basis risk when hedging a load profile with standard hedging products having a lower granularity.  In other words a commodity supplier wants to pre-purchase supplies for expected demand, but can only buy in fixed amounts that are bigger than the demand forecasted.  This means it has to either over order or under order and make up the difference at the time of delivery at the spot price which might be much higher.  Shape risk is also related to commodity risk.
