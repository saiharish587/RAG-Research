Live from Faraway Stables: Live from Faraway Stables is a 2003 live album and concert film by Australian alternative rock band Silverchair.  It was recorded at their concert held on 19 April 2003, at Newcastle Civic Theatre in the band's home-town of Newcastle, New South Wales, and was the second show to be held there during the band's Across the Night world tour of March to June 2003.  It is Silverchair's first live release.
