The Namesake (film): The Namesake is a 2006 Indian-American drama film which was released in the United States on 9 March 2007, following screenings at film festivals in Toronto and New York City.  It was directed by Mira Nair and is based upon the novel of the same name by Jhumpa Lahiri, who appeared in the movie.  Sooni Taraporevala wrote the screenplay.  The film received positive reviews from American critics.  The film stars Tabu, Irrfan Khan, Kal Penn and Sahira Nair.
