List of James Bond films: James Bond is a fictional character created by novelist Ian Fleming in 1953.  Bond is a British secret agent working for MI6 who also answers by his codename, 007.  He has been portrayed on film by actors Sean Connery, David Niven, George Lazenby, Roger Moore, Timothy Dalton, Pierce Brosnan and Daniel Craig, in twenty-six productions.  Only two films were not made by Eon Productions.  Eon now holds the full adaptation rights to all of Fleming's Bond novels.
