Sam's Diner: Sam's Diner, also known as Millie's Diner and Victory Diner, is a historic diner located at Kill Devil Hills, Dare County, North Carolina.  It was built about 1940 by the Kullman Dining Car Co., and moved to its present location in 1996.  It is a one-story, Streamline Moderne style steel frame building sheathed in porcelain enamel panels and stainless steel trim.
