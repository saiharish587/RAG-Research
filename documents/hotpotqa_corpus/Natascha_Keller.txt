Natascha Keller: Natascha Keller (born July 3, 1977 in West Berlin) is a German retired field hockey striker.  She won a gold medal as a member of the German team at the 2004 Summer Olympics.  She also competed at the 1996 Summer Olympics, 2000 Summer Olympics, 2008 Summer Olympics and 2012 Summer Olympics.  In 1999 she received an award from the International Hockey Federation.
