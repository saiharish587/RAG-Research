Sweet '69: "Sweet '69" is a song by Babes in Toyland, released in 1995.  B-sides, "S.F.W." and "Swamp Pussy", are live recordings from the Danish Roskilde Festival in 1994 by Radiomafia.  "Sweet '69" is the first single by Babes in Toyland to get a lot of radio airplay in the US, reaching #37 on the "Billboard" Modern Rock chart.  The single also peaked at #173 on the UK's Official Singles Chart.  The song is unique in its extensive use of melodic cowbells by drummer Lori Barbero.  An accompanying video was also released.
