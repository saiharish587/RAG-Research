North Carolina Highway 132: North Carolina Highway 132 (NC 132) is a North Carolina state highway entirely in New Hanover County.  It travels from U.S. Route 421 (US 421) north of Carolina Beach through Wilmington to US 117/NC 133 just south of the main business district of Castle Hayne.  The road runs just to the east of downtown Wilmington, and runs along the western edge of the University of North Carolina Wilmington.
