Project 635 Dam: The Project 635 Dam () is one of the three dams constructed on the Irtysh River in China's Xinjiang Uighur Autonomous Region.  The embankment dam is located in Fuhai County, about 56 km east of Beitun.  It creates a reservoir (the Project 635 Reservoir, ), which serves as the source of water for the Irtysh–Karamay–Ürümqi Canal.
