Møllevang Church: Møllevang Church (Danish: "Møllevangskirken" ) is a church in Aarhus, Denmark.  The church is situated in the Fuglebakken neighborhood on the street Fuglesangs Alle, north of Ring 1, in Western Aarhus.  Møllevang Church is a part of the Church of Denmark, the Danish state church, and is the parish church of Møllevang Parish.  The church serves some 9000 parishioners and holds weekly sermons as well as weddings, burials and baptisms.
