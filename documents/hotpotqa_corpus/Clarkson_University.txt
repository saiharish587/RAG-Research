Clarkson University: Clarkson University is a private research university with its main campus located in Potsdam, New York, and additional graduate program and research facilities in New York State's Capital Region and Beacon, N.Y.  It was founded in 1896 and has an enrollment of about 4,300 students studying toward bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degrees in each of its schools or institutes: the Institute for a Sustainable Environment, the School of Arts & Sciences, the School of Business and the Wallace H. Coulter School of Engineering.  Clarkson University ranks #8 among "Top Salary-Boosting Colleges" nationwide.  The Carnegie foundation classified Clarkson University as a "High Research Activity" institution.
