The Vanishing American: The Vanishing American (1925) is a silent film western produced by Famous Players-Lasky in the United States, and distributed through Paramount Pictures.  The film was directed by George B. Seitz and starred Richard Dix and Lois Wilson, recently paired in several screen dramas by Paramount.  The film is based on the 1925 novel, "The Vanishing American", by Zane Grey.  It was remade as a 1955 film starring Scott Brady and Audrey Totter.
