Philomena McDonagh: Philomena McDonagh (also known as Phylomena McDonagh) is an English actress and writer best known for her roles as art teacher June Summers in "Grange Hill" and Carol Nelson in ITV soap opera "Emmerdale".  McDonagh acted in Phil Young's play, "Crystal Clear" at the Wyndham's Theatre in London, England with Anthony Allen and Diana Barrett in the cast.  Phil Young was also director.  She has also written films and for television, notably the film adaptation of "Far from the Madding Crowd".
