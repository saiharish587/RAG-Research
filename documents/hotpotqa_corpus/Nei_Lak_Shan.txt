Nei Lak Shan: Nei Lak Shan (Chinese: 彌勒山) is the sixth highest hill in Hong Kong.  With a height of 751 m on Lantau Island, it is situated immediately north of Ngong Ping where the Buddhist Po Lin Monastery is located.  "Nei Lak", or correctly "Mei Lak" is a Cantonese language translation of Maitreya, the future Buddha, in Buddhism.
