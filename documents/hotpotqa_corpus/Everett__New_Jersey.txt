Everett, New Jersey: Everett is an unincorporated community located along the border of Holmdel and Middletown townships in Monmouth County, New Jersey, United States.  The community is located on Everett Road (County Route 52), along which the township line runs, at its intersection with Stillwell Road and Sunnyside Road.  Most of the area in Everett is composed of homes with some farmlands nearby.  Holmdel's Bell Labs Complex is located to the west of Everett.
