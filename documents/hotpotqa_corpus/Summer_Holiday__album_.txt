Summer Holiday (album): Summer Holiday, is an album by Cliff Richard and The Shadows.  It is the soundtrack to the film of the same name.  The album topped the UK Albums Chart for 14 weeks.  Three singles from the album were released, beginning with the double A-side single, "The Next Time" and "Bachelor Boy", followed by "Summer Holiday" and lastly "Foot Tapper" (by the Shadows).  All three topped the UK Singles Chart.
