The Amazing Race Canada 3: The third season of The Amazing Race Canada is a reality game show based on the American series "The Amazing Race".  It features twelve teams of two, each with a pre-existing relationship.  The grand prize includes: a CA$ cash payout, “gas for life” from Petro-Canada, the opportunity to fly for a year anywhere Air Canada flies worldwide in Business Class, and two Chevrolet Colorado "Z71" Trucks.  For the third year in a row, Jon Montgomery returned as the host.
