Society of Texas Film Critics Awards 1995: The 2nd Society of Texas Film Critics Awards were given by the Society of Texas Film Critics (STFC) on December 28, 1995.  The list of winners was announced by STFC president Joe Leydon.  Founded in 1994, the Society of Texas Film Critics members included 18 film critics working for print and broadcast outlets across the state of Texas. " The Usual Suspects" received four awards, more than any other film.
