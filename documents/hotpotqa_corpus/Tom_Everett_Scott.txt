Tom Everett Scott: Thomas "Tom" Everett Scott (born September 7, 1970) is an American film, theatre and television actor.  His film work includes a starring role as drummer Guy Patterson in the film "That Thing You Do! ", the protagonist in "An American Werewolf in Paris" and notable roles in "Boiler Room", "One True Thing", "Dead Man on Campus", "Because I Said So", and "La La Land".
