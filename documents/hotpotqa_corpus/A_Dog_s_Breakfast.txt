A Dog's Breakfast: A Dog's Breakfast is a Canadian comedy independent film produced in 2006.  It was the first film to be written and directed by British-born Canadian actor David Hewlett, who is best known for his role of Dr. Rodney McKay in the TV franchise "Stargate".  Hewlett created the film as a private off-season project and stars alongside his real-life sister Kate Hewlett and "Stargate" actors Paul McGillion, Christopher Judge and Rachel Luttrell.  The film was produced by John Lenic and Jane Loughman.
