Major League Baseball uniforms: The uniforms worn by Major League Baseball teams have changed significantly since professional baseball was first played in the 19th century.  Over time they have adapted from improvised, wool uniforms to mass-produced team brands made from polyester.  The official supplier for Major League Baseball uniforms is Majestic Athletic, who has held the contract since 2005.  On December 5, 2016, it was announced that Under Armour would be the official on-field uniform provider for Major League Baseball, starting in 2019.
