Grindstone River: The Grindstone River is a 6.7 mi river of Minnesota, a tributary of the Kettle River.  Its name is derived either from the Dakota "Iŋswú watpá" (Small Stones River) or from the Ojibwe "Zhiigwanaabikokaa-ziibi" (River abundant with grind stones).  Sandstone taken from near the river was used to produce sharpening stones.  In Ojibwe, Hinckley ("Gaa-zhiigwanaabikokaag") is named after this river.
