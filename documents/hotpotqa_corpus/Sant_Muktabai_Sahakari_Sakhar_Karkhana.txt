Sant Muktabai Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana: Sant Muktabai Sahakari Sakhar Karkhana (SMSSK ) known as Sant Muktabai Cooperative Sugar Factory is a cooperative factory founded in 1988 by Pratibha Patil and others.  The factory is situated at Edlabad in Jalgaon district in Maharashtra.  It was inaugurated by Sonia Gandhi on 23 January 1999, and Patil remained chairperson until 1996.
