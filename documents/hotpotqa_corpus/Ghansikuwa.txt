Ghansikuwa: Ghansikuwa is a village development committee in Tanahu District in the Gandaki Zone of central Nepal.  At the time of the 1991 Nepal census it had a population of 6573 people living in 1264 individual households.It is named after the place Ghanshikuwa which named after a pond made by ghasi.The famous cities of this VDC includes Kamalbari,Ghanshikuwa,Deurali,Chabdibarahi,Chirkanne and so on.The popular temple chabdi barahi is also partially located here.
