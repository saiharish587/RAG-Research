Life During Wartime (film): Life During Wartime is a 2009 American comedy-drama film written and directed by Todd Solondz, which premiered at the 2009 Venice Film Festival.  It is a direct, but loose sequel to his 1998 film "Happiness", with new actors playing the same characters.  It stars Allison Janney, Shirley Henderson, and Ciarán Hinds, among others.
