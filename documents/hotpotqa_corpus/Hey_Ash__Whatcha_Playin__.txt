Hey Ash, Whatcha Playin'?: Hey Ash, Whatcha Playin'?  ("HAWP") is an independently produced series of short comedy videos created by siblings Anthony Burch and Ashly Burch.  The series utilizes surreal humor and comical sibling rivalry to examine the themes, industry trends, and societal impact of video games, with each episode typically focusing on a single game.  "HAWP" was hosted on Destructoid for its first year of production, and syndicated to GameTrailers until 2013, and has since became independently distributed.  s of December 2013 , the series has received 19,541,899 views.  The series is currently airing its fifth season.
