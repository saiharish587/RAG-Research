Riaño, León: Riaño ("Riañu", in Leonese dialect), is a former small municipality located along the Esla River in the mountains of the province of León, in the autonomous community of Castile and León, northern Spain.  The village was across the river from Peak Gilbo.  Located 1153 feet above sea level, it is in the Cantabrian mountains near the foothills of the Picos de Europe.
