Toby Amies: Tobias "Toby" Amies (born 27 June 1967 in Birmingham, Warwickshire) is a broadcaster, filmmaker and photographer, best known for his feature-length documentary "The Man Whose Mind Exploded", and his work on Radio4, MTV UK's "Alternative Nation", FilmFour, "Lonely Planet Six Degrees" and "The Rough Guides".  Toby has specialised in making programmes about art, music and travel, with a special emphasis on fringe culture and alternative perspectives.  In addition, he is a widely published portrait photographer and writer.
