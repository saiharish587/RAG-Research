San Diego State Aztecs football: The San Diego State Aztecs football team represents San Diego State University in the sport of American football.  The Aztecs compete in the Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) of the National Collegiate Athletics Association (NCAA) and the West Division of the Mountain West Conference (MW).  They play their homes games at SDCCU Stadium and are currently coached by Rocky Long.  They have won twenty-one conference championships and three national championships at the small college division.
