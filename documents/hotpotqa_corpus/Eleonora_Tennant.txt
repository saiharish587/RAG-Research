Eleonora Tennant: Born in Sydney as Eleonora Fiaschi, the daughter of Brigadier-General Thomas Fiaschi, she was sent to school in England.  In 1911, while in Australia, she met Ernest Tennant, a British merchant banker who did a lot of business with Germany.  They married soon afterwards, while Tennant was still seventeen, and settled in the UK, living at the Tennant family home of Orford House.  They had four children together The two came to know Joachim von Ribbentrop and were supportive of Nazism.  Ernest Tennant was a leading figure in the Anglo-German Fellowship, an organisation he helped to establish in 1935 which advocated closer relations between the UK and Nazi Germany.
