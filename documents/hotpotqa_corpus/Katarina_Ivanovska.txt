Katarina Ivanovska: Katarina Ivanovska (Macedonian: Катарина Ивановска ; born 18 August 1988) is a Macedonian model and actress.  She began her modeling career in 2004, appearing at Milan Fashion Week after winning the Look Models International model search in Macedonia.  In December, 2004, she appeared in a pictorial for "Elle" magazine and has also appeared in "Citizen K", "Stiletto" and the Italian and Russian "Vogue".  She has been featured on the covers of "Diva" and "Maxima" magazines and in advertisements for D&G in 2006.  She is considered the most successful Macedonian model.  In 2010, Ivanovska appeared in Serbian "Elle" magazine.  In 2011 she signed a contract for advertising Victoria's Secret products.  In 2011 she got her first acting job in the Macedonian World War II film, "The Third Half", landing the lead role of a young Jewish girl named Rebecca.
