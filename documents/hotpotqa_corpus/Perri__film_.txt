Perri (film): Perri is a 1957 film from Walt Disney Productions, based on Felix Salten's 1938 "Perri: The Youth of a Squirrel".  It was the company's fifth feature entry in their "True-Life Adventures" series, and the only one to be labeled a "True Life Fantasy".  In doing so, the Disney team combined the documentary aspects of earlier efforts with fictional scenarios and characters.
