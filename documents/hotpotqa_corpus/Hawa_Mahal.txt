Hawa Mahal: Hawa Mahal (English translation: "Palace of winds" or "Palace of the Breeze") is a palace in Jaipur, India, so it is named because it was essentially a high screen wall built so that the women of the royal family could observe street festivals while unseen from the outside.  It is Constructed of red and pink sandstone, the palace sits on the edge of the City Palace, Jaipur, and extends to the "zenana", or women's chambers.
