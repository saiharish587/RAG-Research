Hampden Park: Hampden Park (often referred to as Hampden) is a football stadium in the Mount Florida area of Glasgow, Scotland.  The 51,866 -capacity venue serves as the national stadium of football in Scotland.  It is the normal home venue of the Scotland national football team and amateur Scottish league club Queen's Park F.C. and regularly hosts the latter stages of the Scottish Cup and Scottish League Cup competitions.  It is also used for music concerts and other sporting events, such as when it was reconfigured as a athletics stadium for the 2014 Commonwealth Games.
