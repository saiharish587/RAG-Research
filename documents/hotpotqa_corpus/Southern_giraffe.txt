Southern giraffe: The southern giraffe ("Giraffa giraffa"), also known as two-horned giraffe, is a proposed species of giraffe native to Southern Africa.  The southern giraffe is proposed as one of the four discovered members of the genus "Giraffa".  The species was described and given its binomial name by German naturalist Johann Christian Daniel von Schreber in 1784.  Two subspecies are recognized.  However, the IUCN currently recognizes only one species of giraffe with nine subspecies.
