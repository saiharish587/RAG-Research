Pan Celtic Festival: The Pan Celtic Festival (Irish: "Féile Pan Cheilteach" ) is a Celtic-language music festival held annually in the week following Easter, since its inauguration in 1971.  The first Pan Celtic Festival took place in Killarney, County Kerry, Ireland.  Its aim is to promote the modern Celtic languages and cultures and artists from all six Celtic nations: Brittany, Cornwall, Ireland, Isle of Man, Scotland and Wales.
