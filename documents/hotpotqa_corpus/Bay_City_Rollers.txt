Bay City Rollers: The Bay City Rollers are a Scottish pop band whose popularity was highest in the mid 1970s.  The "British Hit Singles & Albums" noted they were "tartan teen sensations from Edinburgh", and were "the first of many acts heralded as the 'biggest group since the Beatles' and one of the most screamed-at teeny-bopper acts of the 1970s".  For a relatively brief, but fervent period (nicknamed "Rollermania"), they were worldwide teen idols.  The group's line-up had numerous changes over the years, but the classic line-up during its heyday included; guitarists Eric Faulkner and Stuart John Wood, singer Les McKeown, bassist Alan Longmuir, and drummer Derek Longmuir.
