Dharti Mata (film): Dharti Mata is a 1938 Hindi social film directed by Nitin Bose.  The film was also made and in Bengali as "Desher Mati" in the same year by New Theatres.  It starred K.L. Saigal, Uma Shashi, Jagdish Sethi, Kamlesh Kumari, and K. C. Dey.  The music was by Pankaj Mullick and lyricist and dialogue writer was Pandit Sudarshan.  The story, screenplay and cinematography was by Nitin Bose.  The story is about two friends Ashok and Ajay, one interested in agriculture and the other in technology.  Ashok goes to the village to help the farmers while Ajay goes to UK for higher studies in engineering.  The film highlights the need of technology and new concepts for effective farming.
