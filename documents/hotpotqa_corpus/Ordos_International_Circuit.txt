Ordos International Circuit: Ordos International Circuit(Chinese:鄂尔多斯国际赛车场), built in 2010, is a motorsport facility located in Kangbashi New Area, Ordos City, Inner Mongolia, China.  It hosted a round of the China Touring Car Championship, Scirocco Cup China and Superleague Formula in 2010.  The circuit is 3.751 km long with 18 corners.
