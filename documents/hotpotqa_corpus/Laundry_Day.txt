Laundry Day: Laundry Day is an American independent dark comedy/crime film set in New Orleans starring Kerry Cahill, Billy Slaughter, Dave Davis, and Samantha Ann.  It is the directorial debut of Randy Mack, who also wrote and produced via Armak Productions.  The original score was composed and performed by Peter Orr of legendary New Orleans band Sneaky Pete & The Fens.  It is currently represented by Circus Road Films and planning a 2017 film festival premiere.
