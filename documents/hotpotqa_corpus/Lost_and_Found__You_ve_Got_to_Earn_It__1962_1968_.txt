Lost and Found: You've Got to Earn It (1962–1968): Lost and Found: You've Got To Earn It (1962–1968) is a compilation album by The Temptations.  Released by Motown Records in 1999, it includes twenty unreleased Temptations records alongside unreleased mixes of "Ain't Too Proud to Beg" and "You've Got to Earn It".  Most of the songs were recorded during the group's "Classic 5" era with David Ruffin and Eddie Kendricks as lead singers, although there are some tracks present which were recorded with Ruffin's predecessor, Elbridge Bryant, in the lineup.  There's also one track that was recorded with Ruffin's successor, Dennis Edwards.
