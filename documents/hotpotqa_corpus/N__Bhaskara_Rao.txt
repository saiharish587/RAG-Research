N. Bhaskara Rao: Nadendla Bhaskara Rao (born 23 June 1935 in Guntur) was the chief minister of the Indian state of Andhra Pradesh for a brief period in 1984.  He servred the shortest term yet, as the Chief Minister of Andhra Pradesh – 31 days.  His son Nadendla Manohar is Ex MLA for Tenali Constituency who is also Ex Speaker for United Andhra Pradesh assembly.
