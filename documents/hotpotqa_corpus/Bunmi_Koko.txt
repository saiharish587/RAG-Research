Bunmi Koko: Bunmi Koko was a Luxury Fashion Brand, based in London, England.  Creative Director Bunmi Olaye and partner Francis Udom founded Bunmi Koko in 2009.  The name was inspired by the designer’s first name ‘Bunmi’ (which means 'God gave me') and the nickname given to her by her partner Francis; "Koko" (meaning my other half’).  The two phrases joined to mean, "God gave me my other half."
