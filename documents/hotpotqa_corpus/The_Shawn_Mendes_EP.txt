The Shawn Mendes EP: The Shawn Mendes EP is the debut extended play by Canadian singer Shawn Mendes, released on July 28, 2014 by Island Records.  It debuted at number 5 on the U.S. "Billboard" 200 chart with sales of 48,000 copies in its first week.  The EP includes the single "Life of the Party", which was a moderate success.  The extended play was later removed from the iTunes Store and "Show You", "One of Those Nights" and "The Weight" were released as separate promotional singles.  "The Weight" was included in the deluxe edition of Mendes' debut studio album "Handwritten".  As of April 2016, the EP has sold 103,000 copies in the United States.
