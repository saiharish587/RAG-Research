Mike DiNunno: Mike DiNunno (born January 29, 1990) is an American-Italian professional basketball player for the Cheshire Phoenix, playing the point guard position.  Born in Maywood, Illinois, he played high school basketball at Lake Park and Von Steuben.  Initially, he committed to Northern Illinois to play college basketball, where he received MAC All-Freshman team honors.  After two seasons, he transferred to Eastern Kentucky.  DiNunno was named in the OVC All-Newcomer team in his junior season and first team all-OVC in his senior season at Eastern Kentucky.  Following his graduation, he has played professionally in several leagues in Europe.
