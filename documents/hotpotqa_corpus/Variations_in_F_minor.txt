Variations in F minor: The Andante with variations in F minor (Hoboken 17/6), also known as Un piccolo divertimento, was composed for piano by Joseph Haydn in 1793, and is among his most popular piano works.  (The late British composer and pianist John McCabe, in his booklet note accompanying his boxed set of recordings of Haydn's complete solo keyboard music, was of the opinion that it was possibly inspired by the death of Maria Anna von Genzinger (1754-93, called "Marianne") [p. 23].  McCabe also says that this piece is Haydn's "most extended and most resourceful such work for the keyboard" [p. 22].)  The variations here are a set of double variations, the first theme is in F minor and the second theme in F major.  Two variations of each theme and an extended coda follow.
