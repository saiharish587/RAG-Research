Pulkovo Airport: Pulkovo Airport (Russian: Аэропорт Пулково ; ] ) (IATA: LED, ICAO: ULLI) is an international airport serving Saint Petersburg, Russia.  It consists of one terminal, "Terminal 1" which is located 23 km south of the city centre.  The airport serves as a hub for Rossiya Airlines (formerly Pulkovo Aviation Enterprise), and as focus city for Nordavia.  Pulkovo International Airport is responsible for serving the citizens of Saint Petersburg, as well as the Leningrad Oblast: a total of 6,120,000 people.
