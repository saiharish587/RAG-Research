1982 British Army Gazelle friendly fire incident: On 6 June 1982, during the Falklands War, the British Royal Navy Type 42 destroyer HMS "Cardiff" engaged and destroyed a British Army Gazelle helicopter, serial number "XX377", in a friendly fire incident, killing all four occupants.  "Cardiff", on the lookout for aircraft flying supplies to the Argentine forces occupying the Falkland Islands, had misidentified the helicopter as an enemy C-130 Hercules.  Although the helicopter's loss was initially blamed on enemy action, a subsequent inquiry found "Cardiff"' s missile to be the cause.
