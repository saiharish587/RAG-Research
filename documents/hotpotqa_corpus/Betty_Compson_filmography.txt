Betty Compson filmography: This page has most if not all of Betty Compson's known film appearances 1915–48.  Films from 1915 to 1919 are shorts, mostly for Al Christie unless otherwise stated.  A big breakout came in 1919 with "The Miracle Man" now lost.  In the early-to-mid 1920s Compson was a major player at Paramount with her name above the title of her films.  By the end of the decade she was free-lancing and appearing as feature and support with name below the title of the film.  Her star definitely took a fall somewhere after marrying director James Cruze, and she would remain a feature support into the sound era for the rest of her film career.
