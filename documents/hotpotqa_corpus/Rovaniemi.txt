Rovaniemi: Rovaniemi (] ) is a city and municipality of Finland.  It is the administrative capital and commercial centre of Finland's northernmost province, Lapland.  It is situated about 6 km south of the Arctic Circle and is between the hills of Ounasvaara and Korkalovaara, at the confluence of the river Kemijoki and its tributary, the Ounasjoki.  The city and the surrounding Rovaniemen maalaiskunta (Rural municipality of Rovaniemi) were consolidated into a single entity on 1 January 2006.
