Nachiket Dighe: Nachiket Dighe (Marathi: नचिकेत दिघे "Nacikēta Dighē") born 11 November 1987) is an Indian actor and dubbing voice actor, who dubs in Hindi, Marathi, and English.  He is best known for his Hindi voice-dubbing of Rupert Grint's role as Ron Weasley, in the Harry Potter film series.  He is the official Hindi voice dub-over artist for Kevin Jonas and Toby Amies.  Despite that Jonas and Amies are in different age groups, Dighe is able to perfectly match the quality of their voices when it comes to dubbing their roles in Hindi.  He is also married to Rucha Dighe.
