Lesser Matters: Lesser Matters is the 2003 debut of Swedish pop group The Radio Dept. It received rave reviews, achieving 'universal acclaim' status from Metacritic, and was ranked the 9th best album of 2004 by "NME" magazine.  It received favorable reviews from "UNCUT", "Mojo" and "Q Magazine" with a rating of 84/100 on Metacritic.
