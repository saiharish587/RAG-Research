Wayne Goss (make-up artist): Wayne Alan Goss (born 4 March 1978) is an English makeup artist, YouTube personality and entrepreneur.  Best known for his "Wayne Goss" channel on YouTube, he is the creator and owner of the self-eponymous makeup brush line launched in September 2013.  As of November 2016, Goss' main YouTube channel had over 2.7 million subscribers and had had 344 million views.
