University of the Littoral Opal Coast: University of the Littoral Opal Coast ("Université du Littoral Côte d'Opale"), also known as ULCO, is a French university, based in Boulogne, Calais, Dunkirk (Dunkerque) and Saint Omer.  The head office is in Dunkirk (Dunkerque in French).  It reports to the Academy of Lille and is a member of the European Doctoral College Lille-Nord-Pas de Calais.
