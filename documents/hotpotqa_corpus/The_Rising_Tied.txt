The Rising Tied: The Rising Tied is the debut studio album of hip hop ensemble Fort Minor, the side project by Linkin Park rapper Mike Shinoda.  The album was released on November 22, 2005 through Warner Bros.  Records and Shinoda's label Machine Shop Recordings. ""  is the first and only instrumental studio debut album of Fort Minor, which contains no lyrics and guest appearances, with music written and composed by Shinoda.
