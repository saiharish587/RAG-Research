Watery Grave (EP): The "Watery Grave" EP was Graham Van Pelt's first release under his solo project Miracle Fortress.  The self-released 5 song EP was recorded in 2005 at Friendship Cove, a recording studio/indie rock venue owned by Van Pelt and fellow friend Jack Dylan.  The success of "Watery Grave" gained Van Pelt the attention of Montreal, Canada-based record label Secret City, which led to a collaboration that yielded his first full-length album "Five Roses".
