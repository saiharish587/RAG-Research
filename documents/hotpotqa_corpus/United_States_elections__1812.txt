United States elections, 1812: The 1812 United States elections elected the members of the 13th United States Congress.  The election took place during the First Party System, and shortly after the start of the War of 1812.  The Federalist Party made a relatively strong showing, winning seats in both chambers while supporting a competitive challenge to the incumbent Democratic-Republican President.  However, the Democratic-Republican Party continued its control of the Presidency and both houses of Congress.
