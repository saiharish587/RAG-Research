Newsholme, Lancashire: Newsholme is a small village and civil parish in the Ribble Valley, Lancashire, England, but lies within the historic West Riding of Yorkshire.  The population of the civil parish was 50 at the 2001 census but with the inclusion of Horton had risen to 253 at the 2011 Census.  Today it lies near the boundary with North Yorkshire on the A682, 4 mi north of Barnoldswick and 12 mi west of Skipton.
