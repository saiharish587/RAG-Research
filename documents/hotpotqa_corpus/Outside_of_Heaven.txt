Outside of Heaven: "Outside of Heaven" is a popular music song written by Sammy Gallop and Chester Conn.  A recording by Eddie Fisher with Hugo Winterhalter's orchestra and chorus was made at Manhattan Center, New York City, on July 19, 1952.  It was released by RCA Victor Records as catalog number 20-4953 (in USA) and by EMI on the His Master's voice label as catalog number B 10362.
