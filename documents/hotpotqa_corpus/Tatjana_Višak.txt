Tatjana Višak: Tatjana Višak (born 12 December 1974), often credited as Tatjana Visak, is a German philosopher specialising in ethics and political philosophy who is currently based in the Department of Philosophy and Business Ethics at University of Mannheim.  She is the author of "Killing Happy Animals" (2013, Palgrave Macmillan) and the editor, with the political theorist Robert Garner, of "The Ethics of Killing Animals" (2016, Oxford University Press), an edited collection.  She is known for arguing that utilitarians should not accept that nonhuman animals can be replaced by other, equally happy, beings, meaning that utilitarians can oppose the routine killing of animals in agriculture.
