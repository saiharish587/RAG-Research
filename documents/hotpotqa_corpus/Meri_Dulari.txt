Meri Dulari: Meri Dulari (Urdu: میری دلاری‎ , English: "My favorite") is a 2013 Pakistani drama serial directed by "Amin Iqbal".  Serial will broadcast on Geo TV from 13 March 2013.  It is written by "Nabeela Abar Raja" and production by A & B Entertainment.  It stars Sami Khan, Yumna Zaidi, Sana Askari and Nazli Nasar.
