Janine Gibson: Janine Victoria Gibson (born 17 June 1972) is a British journalist who is editor-in-chief of the Buzzfeed UK website.  In the summer of 2014 she became deputy editor of Guardian News and Media and editor-in-chief of theguardian.com website in London.  She is a former editor-in-chief in New York City of Guardian US, the offshoot of "The Guardian", the British newspaper and online publication.
