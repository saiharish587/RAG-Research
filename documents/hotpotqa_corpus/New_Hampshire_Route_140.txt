New Hampshire Route 140: New Hampshire Route 140 is a 21.142 mi east–west state highway in central New Hampshire, running from Tilton to Alton.  The western terminus of NH 140 is in Tilton at an intersection with U.S. Route 3, New Hampshire Route 11 and New Hampshire Route 132, located at exit 20 on Interstate 93.  The eastern terminus is in Alton at NH 11 and New Hampshire Route 28A (Main Street).  In Alton, the road is named the Frank C. Gilman Highway.
