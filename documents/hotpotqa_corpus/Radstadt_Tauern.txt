Radstadt Tauern: The Radstadt Tauern (German: "Radstädter Tauern" ) are a subrange of the Central Eastern Alps in Austria.  Together with the Schladming Tauern, the Rottenmann and Wölz Tauern and the Seckau Tauern the Radstadt Tauern form the major range of mountains known as the Low Tauern.  The mountains are found in the southeast of the Austrian state of Salzburg, between the upper reaches of the Enns and Mur rivers.
