2013 NCAA Skiing Championships: The 2013 NCAA Skiing Championships were held in Middlebury, VT and Ripton, VT on March 6–9, 2013.  Middlebury College hosted the event with alpine events at the Middlebury College Snow Bowl and Nordic events taking place at the Rikert Nordic Center.  The competition was won by the Colorado Buffaloes after compiling the largest final-day comeback in NCAA Skiing Championships history.  Twenty-one teams from three regions sent skiers to compete for the NCAA collegiate team championship and individual titles.
