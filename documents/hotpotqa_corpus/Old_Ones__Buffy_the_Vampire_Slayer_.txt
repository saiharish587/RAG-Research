Old Ones (Buffy the Vampire Slayer): In the Buffyverse, the Old Ones are the extremely powerful, pure-breed demons that once dominated Earth before humankind appeared.  Illyria is one of these demons (though its real form was revealed only in an illustration) while it's more than likely that Jasmine and her kind (The Powers That Be) ascended to their higher plane because of the growing malevolence of these warring demons.  It's also possible that the Powers that Be were part of the same race but shared a different philosophy than the other Old Ones.
