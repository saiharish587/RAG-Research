Go Too: Go Too (1977) is Go's third album.  Go was founded by Stomu Yamashta, Steve Winwood and Michael Shrieve.  For this album, Jess Roden replaced Steve Winwood (who had left the group).  The style of the music became modified accordingly.  In addition, Linda Lewis was hired as a singer.  Together with Paul Jackson, Jr. and the orchestra of Martyn Ford the album unified various soundscapes.
