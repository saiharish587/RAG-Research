Hydrogen highway (Japan): Japan's hydrogen highway is a network of hydrogen filling stations placed along roadsides that provide fuel for hydrogen fuel cell vehicles (HFCV).  An HFCV is a vehicle that uses a fuel cell to convert hydrogen energy into electrical energy.  The hydrogen that is used in fuel cell vehicles can be made using fossil or renewable resources.  The hydrogen highway is necessary for HFCVs to be used.  HFCV reduce tailpipe emissions of greenhouse gases.  By May 2016, there were approximately 80 hydrogen fueling stations in Japan.
