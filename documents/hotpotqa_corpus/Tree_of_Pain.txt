Tree of Pain: "Tree of Pain" is a song by heavy metal band Soulfly, released in 2002 as the seventh track of the studio album numerically titled "3".  Like most other songs, "Tree of Pain" has never been released as a single.  This song is unique for Soulfly that it contains a pop ballad and a woman singer.  This song tributes Max Cavalera for his stepson's (Dana Wells') untimely death.
