Spanish Valley, Utah: Spanish Valley is a census-designated place (CDP) in San Juan County, Utah, in the United States.  As of the 2000 census, the CDP population was 181.  But on almost all maps of the area, and especially in local usage, the name identifies the geographic valley that extends south of the city of Moab.  The majority of the valley, and the majority of the population living in it, lies within Grand County the northern neighbor to San Juan County.  Only the southern-most third of Spanish Valley lies within San Juan County, and it is the least populated.
