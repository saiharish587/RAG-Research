Half Past Dead 2: Half Past Dead 2 is a 2007 American action film directed by Art Camacho, and starring Bill Goldberg and Kurupt.  It is a sequel to the 2002's "Half Past Dead" starring Steven Seagal, Ja Rule and Morris Chestnut.  None of the actors from the original film return, except Kurupt who returns to reprise his role of Twitch.  The film was released in the United States on May 15, 2007.
