Kamal Haasan: Kamal Haasan (born 7 November 1954) is an Indian film actor, director, screenwriter, producer, playback singer and lyricist who works primarily in Tamil cinema.  Kamal has won several film awards including three National Film Awards, the second-most by any Indian actor, and nineteen Filmfare Awards.  His production company, Rajkamal International, has produced several of his films.
