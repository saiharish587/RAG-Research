Vampires vs. Zombies: Vampires vs. Zombies is an independent horror film loosely based upon J. Sheridan Le Fanu's classic 1872 novel "Carmilla".  Unlike Le Fanu's story, however, most of the action in the film takes place inside a car.  The title and the cover were obviously inspired by the horror film "Freddy vs. Jason", it's unclear if it was intended as a mockbuster of that film or not.
