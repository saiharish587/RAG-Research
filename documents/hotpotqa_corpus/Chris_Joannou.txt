Chris Joannou: Christopher John Joannou (born 10 November 1979) is an Australian musician, best known as the bassist for the Newcastle-based alternative rock band Silverchair.  His real name is Christophoros John Joannou, and he was born in Newcastle, New South Wales, he has a twin sister and an older sister.  He has a nephew and two nieces.  He was the first of the three band members to cut his long hair short.  Joannou was nicknamed 'Lumberjack' by Silverchair fans for his love of trees, and plaid shirts.  Chris' bandmate Ben Gillies taught him how to play bass guitar, making him the only Band member who did not initially know how to play an instrument.
