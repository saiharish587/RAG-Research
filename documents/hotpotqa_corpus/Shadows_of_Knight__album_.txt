Shadows of Knight (album): Shadows of Knight is the third studio album by American garage rock band The Shadows of Knight, and was released on Super K Productions, SKS 6002, in 1969.  Recording for the album came after lead vocalist Jim Sohns revamped the Shadows of Knight's line-up and signed with Super K. Although "Shadows of Knight" did not chart and was the last album featuring new material by the group until "A Knight to Remember", a single taken from the effort, "Shake", became a moderate national success in the United States.
