The Wharf (Holdings): The Wharf (Holdings) Limited (), or Wharf (九倉) in short, () is a company founded in 1886 in Hong Kong.  As its name suggests, the company's original business was in running wharfage and dockside warehousing, and it was originally known as The Hong Kong and Kowloon Wharf and Godown Company, Limited and founded by Sir Paul Chater.  The company adopted its current name in 1986.
