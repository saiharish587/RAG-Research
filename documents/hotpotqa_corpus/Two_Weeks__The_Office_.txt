Two Weeks (The Office): "Two Weeks" is the twenty-first episode of the fifth season of the television series "The Office", and the 93rd overall episode of the series.  It originally aired on NBC in the United States on March 26, 2009.  In this episode, Michael, who has given his two weeks' notice to Dunder Mifflin, tries to convince others in the office to quit and join him in starting a new paper company.  Meanwhile, Pam spends her day trying to put together the new photocopier and becomes frustrated with her job.
