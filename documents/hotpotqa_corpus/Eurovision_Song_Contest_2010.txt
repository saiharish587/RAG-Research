Eurovision Song Contest 2010: The Eurovision Song Contest 2010 was the 55th annual Eurovision Song Contest, broadcast from the Telenor Arena in Bærum, Greater Oslo, Norway.  Norway gained the rights to host the contest after achieving a record breaking victory in Moscow the previous year.  It was the third time Norway had hosted the contest, having previously done so in 1986 and 1996.  The 2010 winner was Germany with Lena singing "Satellite", written by American Julie Frost and Denmark's John Gordon.  It was Germany's first win in twenty-eight years, its second since the Contest's inception, and its first win as a unified country.  It was also the first time a "Big Four" country won the contest since the rule's introduction in 2000.
