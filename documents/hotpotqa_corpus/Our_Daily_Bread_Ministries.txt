Our Daily Bread Ministries: Our Daily Bread Ministries (formerly RBC Ministries) is a Christian organization founded by Dr. Martin De Haan in 1938.  It is based in Grand Rapids, Michigan, with over 300 employees.  It produces several devotional publications, including "Our Daily Bread".  It also produces radio and television programs, and an online university program, Christian University GlobalNet.  The publishing arm of Our Daily Bread Ministries is called Discovery House.
