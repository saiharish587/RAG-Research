Wilkes-Barre Wyoming Valley Airport: Wilkes-Barre Wyoming Valley Airport (IATA: WBW, ICAO: KWBW, FAA LID: WBW) is a county owned, public airport three miles north of Wilkes-Barre, in Luzerne County, Pennsylvania, and 11 miles south of Scranton, in Lackawanna County, Pennsylvania.  The National Plan of Integrated Airport Systems for 2011–2015 categorized it as a "general aviation" facility.  The primary airport of the Scranton/Wilkes-Barre metropolitan area is the Wilkes-Barre/Scranton International Airport (AVP).
