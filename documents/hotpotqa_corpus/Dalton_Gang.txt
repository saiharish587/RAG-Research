Dalton Gang: The Dalton Gang was a group of outlaws in the American Old West during 1890–1892.  It was also known as The Dalton Brothers because three of its members were brothers.  The gang specialized in bank and train robberies.  During an attempted bank robbery in Coffeyville, Kansas in 1892, two of the brothers and two other gang members were killed; Emmett survived and was captured, tried, and convicted.  He was paroled after serving 14 years in prison.
