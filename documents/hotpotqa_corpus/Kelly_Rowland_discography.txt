Kelly Rowland discography: American recording artist Kelly Rowland has released four studio albums, two compilation albums, one box set, two extended plays and forty-four singles, including twenty as a featured artist and five promotional singles. Rowland began her career in 1997 with one of the best-selling girl groups, Destiny's Child, who have sold around 60 million records worldwide.  As a solo artist, Rowland has sold 40 million records worldwide.
