Nevisian dollar: The dollar was the currency of Nevis until 1830.  The currency consisted of counterstamped Spanish and French colonial coins.  The dollar was subdivided into 72 "black dogs", each of 1½ pence.  Around 1801, coins were issued for 1, 4, 6, 7 and 9 black dogs with the word "Nevis" and the denomination stamped on them.  The 1 black dog coins were countermarked on French Guianan 2 sous, whilst the 9 black dogs were made from Spanish colonial 1 real coins.  In 1830, sterling was established as the official currency of the island.
