1995 San Jose State Spartans football team: The 1995 San Jose State Spartans football team represented San Jose State University during the 1995 NCAA Division I-A football season as a member of the Big West Conference.  The team was led by head coach John Ralston, in his third year as head coach at San Jose State.  They played their home games at Spartan Stadium in San Jose, California.  The Spartans finished the 1995 season with a record of three wins and eight losses (3–8, 3–4 Big West).
