Summer Games Done Quick 2016: Summer Games Done Quick 2016 (sometimes abbreviated as SGDQ 2016) was a speedrun charity marathon hosted by the Speedruns Live community on 3–9 July, 2016.  Held at the Hilton Minneapolis Downtown in Minneapolis, Minnesota, the event consisted of 177 consecutive speedruns, spread over seven days.  Participants played through a large variation of video games, including games in the "Super Mario" and "The Elder Scrolls" series.  The event included multiple races, blindfolded play-throughs, and two "Tetris" competitions.  Summer games Done Quick 2016 raised $1,297,924.44 USD for Doctors Without Borders, a slight improvement over the 2015's Summer marathon.
