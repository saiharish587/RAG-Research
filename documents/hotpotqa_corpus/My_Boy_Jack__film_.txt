My Boy Jack (film): My Boy Jack is a 2007 television drama based on David Haig's 1997 play of the same name.  It was filmed in August 2007, with Haig as Rudyard Kipling and Daniel Radcliffe as John Kipling.  It does not include act three of the play, which extended to the 1920s and 1930s: instead it ends with Kipling reciting the poem "My Boy Jack".  The American television premiere was on 20 April 2008 on PBS, with primetime rebroadcast on 27 March 2011.  The film attracted about 5.7 million viewers on its original broadcast in the UK on Remembrance Day, 11 November 2007.
