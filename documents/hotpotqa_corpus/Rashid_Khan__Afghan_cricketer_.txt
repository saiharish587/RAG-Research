Rashid Khan (Afghan cricketer): Rashid Khan Arman (Pashto: ‎ ; born 20 September 1998), commonly known as Rashid Khan, is an Afghan cricketer who represents the national team.  Rashid played in the 2017 Indian Premier League for Sunrisers Hyderabad.  In June 2017, he took the best bowling figures for an associate nation in a One Day International (ODI) match.
