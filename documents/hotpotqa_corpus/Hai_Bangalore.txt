Hai Bangalore: Hai Bangalore is a mass circulation weekly Kannada language tabloid published in Bangalore.  Its editor is journalist Ravi Belagere, who received a Karnataka Media Award in 2007.  The tabloid articles include reports about scandals, scams, affairs, background politics, murders, crime to the public eye, although the accuracy of the reports is often questionable.  Apart from these reports, it contains columns and articles about various fields like psychology, sports, science and cinema news.
