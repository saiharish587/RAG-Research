The Jerky Boys 2: Jerky Boys 2 is the second comedy album released by prank call artists, the Jerky Boys.  The album was released in 1994 and was the final Jerky Boys album released on the Select Records label, distributed by Atlantic Records.  It went Platinum as well as the first album.  This album was also nominated for a Grammy Award at the 1995's Grammy's for Best Comedy Album, but lost to Sam Kinison's "Live From Hell".
