History of Newcastle Jets FC: The History of the Newcastle Jets FC starts at their inception in 2000 by businessman Con Constantine after the collapse of the regions previous football club the Newcastle Breakers.  The Breakers were dissolved when Soccer Australia revoked its NSL licence at the conclusion of the 1999/2000 season.  At the formation of Newcastle United the home ground was moved back to where Newcastle KB United played, now known as EnergyAustralia Stadium.
