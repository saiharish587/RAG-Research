Ronald F. Collins: Ronald F. Collins is an American politician from Maine.  A Wells resident, Collins represents the 2nd Senate District.  Prior to being elected a Maine State Senator, he was a member of the Maine House of Representatives representing Wells from 2002-2010.  He represents Acton, Berwick, Cornish, Lebanon, Limerick, Newfield, North Berwick, Parsonfield, Shapleigh and Wells in the Maine State Senate.
