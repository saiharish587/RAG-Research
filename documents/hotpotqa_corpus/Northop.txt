Northop: Northop (Welsh: "Llaneurgain" ) is a small village and an electoral ward situated in Flintshire, Wales, approximately 12 miles west of the city of Chester, midway between Mold and Flint, and situated just off junction 33 of the A55 North Wales Expressway.  At the 2001 Census, the population of Northop was 2,983, increasing to 3,049 at the 2011 census.  The community includes Sychdyn.
