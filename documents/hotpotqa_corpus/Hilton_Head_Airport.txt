Hilton Head Airport: Hilton Head Airport (IATA: HHH, ICAO: KHXD, FAA LID: HXD) is a public use airport located on Hilton Head Island, in Beaufort County, South Carolina, United States.  Also known as Hilton Head Island Airport, it is owned by Beaufort County.  Mostly used for general aviation, it is also served by a commercial airline and one public charter airline operating limited service.  It is the only airport on Hilton Head Island.
