Norlin Quadrangle Historic District: The Norlin Quadrangle Historic District comprises the core of the main campus of the University of Colorado campus in Boulder, Colorado.  The twelve buildings were designed to reflect a variety of architectural styles.  The quadrangle was named after University of Colorado president George Norlin.  Buildings on the quadrangle include the Norlin Library, Woodbury Arts and Sciences Building, Old Main, the Hale Science Building, University Theater, Macky Concert Hall and the Women's Studies Cottage.
