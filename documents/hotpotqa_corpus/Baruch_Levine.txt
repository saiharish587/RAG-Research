Baruch Levine: Baruch Levine (born December 28, 1977) is a Canadian-born American Orthodox Jewish composer and singer whose songs have become popular and classic throughout the Orthodox Jewish world.  His slow, soulful, heartfelt tunes have gained wide popularity at Shabbat tables and "kumzits" gatherings.  One of his most successful compositions is ""Vezakeini"" (Give Us Merit), derived from the ancient prayer recited at Shabbat candle lighting.
