King Faisal Babes F.C.: King Faisal Babes Football Club is a professional football club in Kumasi, Ashanti.  The club competes in the Glo Premier League.  They were represented at the FIFA World Cup 2006 by left-back Habib Mohamed, who was playing for Ghana national team.  King Faisal Babies were relegated at the end of the 2010–11 Glo Premier League season and played in the Poly Tank Division One League in the 2011–12 season.  Then were re-promoted to the 2012–13 Glo Premier League season.  The club have never won the Ghana Premier League.
