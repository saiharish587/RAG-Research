Jean-Loup Chrétien: Jean-Loup Jacques Marie Chrétien (born 20 August 1938) is a French retired "Général de Brigade" (brigadier general) in the "Armée de l'Air" (French air force), and a former CNES spationaut.  He flew on two Franco-Soviet space missions and a NASA Space Shuttle mission.  Chrétien was the first Frenchman and the first western European in space.
