Red Butte Creek: Red Butte Creek is a small stream whose headwaters are found in the northeast part of Salt Lake County, Utah.  It flows west through the Red Butte Garden and Arboretum, by the University of Utah, Fort Douglas and flows southwesterly through Salt Lake City’s Liberty Park before forming a confluence with the Jordan River.  Consisting of 7.25 mi2 of watershed, Red Butte Canyon is the smallest canyon of the seven canyons in eastern Salt Lake County.  Its elevation ranges from 5000 to above sea level.
