Andatee China Marine Fuel: Andatee China Marine Fuel Service Corporation ("Andatee") (NASDAQ: AMCF ) is a marine fuel wholesale and retail company that is based in Dalian, China.  The company sells oil fuel products mainly to one municipality directly under the central government and four big provinces in the Northeast and East of China, namely Tianjin, Liaoning, Shandong, Jiangsu and Zhejiang.  The company has subsidiaries including Goodwill Rich International Limited, Dalian Fusheng Petrochemical Company ("Fusheng"), Fusheng's variable interest entity, and Xing Yuan.  The company's subsidiary, Xing Yuan, founded in 2001, is a joint-venture with the Dalian subsidiary of CPC Corp.(China Petroleum and Chemical Corporation), which is the largest petroleum company in China.
