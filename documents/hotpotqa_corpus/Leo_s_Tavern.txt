Leo's Tavern: Leo's Tavern (Irish: "Tábhairne Leo" ) is a restaurant and pub in the Donegal Gaeltacht, best known as the home of music artists Clannad, Enya and Moya Brennan.  The pub opened in 1968 and held Irish traditional music sessions nightly, becoming the musical starting block for the children of Leo Brennan, the pub's founder.  The current proprietor is Bartley Brennan, one of the youngest of the Brennan siblings.
