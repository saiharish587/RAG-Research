Allan Simonsen: Allan Rodenkam Simonsen (born 15 December 1952) is a former Danish footballer and manager.  He most prominently played as a forward for German Bundesliga club Borussia Mönchengladbach, winning the 1975 and 1979 UEFA Cups, as well as for Barcelona from Spain, winning the 1982 Cup Winners' Cup.  Simonsen is the only footballer to have scored in the European Cup, UEFA Cup, and Cup Winners' Cup finals.  Simonsen was named 1977 European Footballer of the Year.
