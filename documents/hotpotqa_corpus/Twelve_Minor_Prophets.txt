Twelve Minor Prophets: The Minor Prophets or Twelve Prophets (Aramaic: תרי עשר‎ ‎ , "Trei Asar", "The Twelve"), occasionally Book of the Twelve, is the last book of the Nevi'im, the second main division of the Jewish Tanakh.  The collection is broken up to form twelve individual books in the Christian Old Testament, one for each of the prophets.  The terms "minor prophets" and "twelve prophets" can also refer to the twelve traditional authors of these works.
