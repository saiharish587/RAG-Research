Political Scrapbook: Political Scrapbook is a left wing political blog.  The site was described as "influential" by "The Independent" and is viewed, along with outlets such as "Left Foot Forward", as part of a cohort of British left-wing blogs which have attracted significant interest from the media.  The site has been likened to Paul Staines' anti-establishment "Guido Fawkes" blog, although Staines has claimed "It's hard to create a leftwing version of me because of political correctness."  In 2011 the site was ranked by Wikio as the 7th most influential political blog in the UK and was voted as "Total Politics'" 2nd best left-wing blog in 2011.
