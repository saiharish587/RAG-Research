To the Morning: "To the Morning" is a song written and performed by Dan Fogelberg, with strings arranged by Glenn Spreen.  It is the first song on Side 1 of his debut album, "Home Free".  It is about waking up every day and knowing that it's going to be a new day, regardless of anything else, no matter what happens in life.  (It's going to be a day/There's really no way to say no to the morning) Musically, it is built around the very subtle piano work, with emphasis placed on Fogelberg's vocals while he is singing and the piano or strings during instrumental sections.  The song is peculiar in his canon for featuring no guitar.
