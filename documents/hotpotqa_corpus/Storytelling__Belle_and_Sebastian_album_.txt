Storytelling (Belle and Sebastian album): Storytelling is a soundtrack by Scottish indie pop band Belle and Sebastian.  It is the score to the Todd Solondz movie "Storytelling".  Belle and Sebastian experienced many problems in communication with Solondz while scoring the film, and as such only about six minutes of their music was actually used in the movie .  The album contains five tracks that are recorded dialogues.  The instrumental track "Fuck This Shit" uses the prosody of the title phrase in a number of different keys but never the words themselves.  It was the band's final release on Jeepster, as they went on to sign a deal with Rough Trade the following year.
