A Klingon Christmas Carol: A Klingon Christmas Carol is the first play to be performed entirely in Klingon, a constructed language first appearing in the television series "Star Trek".  The play is based on the Charles Dickens novella, "A Christmas Carol".  "A Klingon Christmas Carol" is the Charles Dickens classic tale of ghosts and redemption, adapted to reflect the Klingon values of courage and honor, and then translated into Klingon, performed with English supertitles.
