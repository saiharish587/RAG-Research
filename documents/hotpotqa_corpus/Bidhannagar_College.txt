Bidhannagar College: Bidhannagar College (Bengali: বিধাননগর মহাবিদ্যালয়) Bidhannagar (Salt Lake), Kolkata, established in 1984, is a West Bengal State University affiliated college run by the Government of West Bengal.  It was formerly affiliated to the University of Calcutta.  Apart from undergraduate courses, the college offers postgraduate courses in Education, Microbiology, Chemistry and Zoology.
