John Conlee: John Conlee (born August 11, 1946) is an American country music singer.  Between 1978 and 2004 Conlee charted a total of 32 singles on the "Billboard" Hot Country Songs charts, and recorded 11 studio albums. His singles include seven #1 hits: "Lady Lay Down," "Backside of Thirty," "Common Man," "I'm Only in It for the Love," "In My Eyes," "As Long As I'm Rockin' with You" and "Got My Heart Set on You."  In addition to these, Conlee sent 14 other songs into the Top Ten.
