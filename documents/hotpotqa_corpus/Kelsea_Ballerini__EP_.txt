Kelsea Ballerini (EP): Kelsea Ballerini is the debut extended play (EP) by American country pop singer Kelsea Ballerini.  It was released on November 24, 2014, by Black River Entertainment.  The EP contains five tracks that would later be included on Ballerini's debut full-length album, "The First Time", in 2015, including the singles "Love Me Like You Mean It", "Dibs", and "Yeah Boy".
