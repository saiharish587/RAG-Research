Camellia sinensis: Camellia sinensis is a species of evergreen shrub or small tree whose leaves and leaf buds are used to produce tea.  It is of the genus "Camellia" () of flowering plants in the family Theaceae.  Common names include "tea plant", "tea shrub", and "tea tree" (not to be confused with "Melaleuca alternifolia", the source of tea tree oil, or "Leptospermum scoparium", the New Zealand teatree).
