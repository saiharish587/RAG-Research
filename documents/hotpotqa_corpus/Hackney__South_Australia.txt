Hackney, South Australia: Hackney is a suburb of Adelaide in the City of Norwood Payneham St Peters.  It is adjacent to the City centre and North Adelaide.  Part of the O Bahn system lies here and presently, this system is being upgraded.  Hackney Road and Payneham Road are main roads running through the suburb.  Hackney Road is part of the City Ring Route, Adelaide.  There are many expensive houses in this area.
