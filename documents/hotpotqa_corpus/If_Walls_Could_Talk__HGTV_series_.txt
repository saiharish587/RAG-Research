If Walls Could Talk (HGTV series): If Walls Could Talk is an American television show on HGTV.  Each episode lasts half an hour and usually airs at 5:00 and 5:30 Eastern Time.  Hosted by Mike Siegel, this show talks about mysteries and shocking discoveries found in houses.  Previous hosts include C. Van Tune, Grant Goodeve, and Elyse Luray.
