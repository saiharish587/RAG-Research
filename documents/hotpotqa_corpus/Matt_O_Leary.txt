Matt O'Leary: Matthew Joseph "Matt" O'Leary (born July 6, 1987) is an American actor who made his debut in the made-for-television Disney Channel Original film "Mom's Got a Date with a Vampire" (2000).  He would go on to star in the thriller "Domestic Disturbance" (2001) opposite John Travolta, and have supporting roles in "Frailty" (2001), and the independent neo-noir film "Brick" (2005).
