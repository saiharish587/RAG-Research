Hello Friend: Hello Friend, sometimes written as Hello, Friend, is a short comedy horror film directed by Graham Linehan and written by Linehan and Robert Popper.  The film was Linehan's directorial debut.  The film stars Martin Savage as a man, John Ward, but credited as the "Subject", who buys some new internet software for his computer, which begins to take over his life.  The film was produced by The Film Council, Film4 Productions and Shine Limited.
