Waddington, Lincolnshire: Waddington is a large rural commuter village and civil parish in the North Kesteven district of Lincolnshire, England, situated approximately 4 mi south of Lincoln on the A607 Grantham Road.  The village is known for its association with RAF Waddington.  At the 2001 Census Waddington had a population of 6,086, increasing to 6,122 at the 2011 census.
