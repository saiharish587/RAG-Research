Hreša: Hreša is a mountain village located near Sarajevo, capital of Bosnia and Herzegovina.  Hreša is located in Istočni Stari Grad (Serbian Cyrillic: Источни Стари Град), municipality in City of East Sarajevo, Republika Srpska, Bosnia and Herzegovina.  It was also known as Srpski Stari Grad (Serbian Cyrillic: Српски Стари Град), and was created from part of the pre-war municipality of Stari Grad (the other part of the pre-war municipality is now in the Federation of Bosnia and Herzegovina).  Hreša is the biggest place of Istočni Stari Grad (East Old Town).
