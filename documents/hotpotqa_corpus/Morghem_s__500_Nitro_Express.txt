Morghem's .500 Nitro Express: Morghem's .500 Nitro Express known as Frank's Khing at owner/call name registered events, is a UKC registered male American Pit Bull Terrier.  Who competes in dog sports and conformation shows earning titles in Dock Jump, Lure Course, Show Champion, Rally Obedience and Weight Pull.  Khing has been awarded the AKC Canine Good Citizen and USTTA temperament tested certifications.
