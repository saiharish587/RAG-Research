Jeffrey Wright: Jeffrey Wright (born December 7, 1965) is an American film, television and stage actor.  He is known for such roles as Belize in the HBO miniseries "Angels in America", Jean-Michel Basquiat in "Basquiat", Felix Leiter in the James Bond films "Casino Royale" and "Quantum of Solace", Valentin Narcisse in the HBO series "Boardwalk Empire", and Beetee in "The Hunger Games" films.  He currently stars as Bernard Lowe in the HBO series "Westworld".
