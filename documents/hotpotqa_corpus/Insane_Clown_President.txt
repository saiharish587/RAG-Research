Insane Clown President: Insane Clown President: Dispatches from the 2016 Circus is a non-fiction book by Matt Taibbi about Donald Trump and the 2016 United States presidential election.  The book contains illustrations by "Rolling Stone" artist Victor Juhasz.  Taibbi's choice of title for the book was motivated by Trump's marketing style.  His work was inspired by Hunter S. Thompson, who had previously published "Fear and Loathing on the Campaign Trail '72".
