Holy Key: "Holy Key" is a single by American musician DJ Khaled, featuring American rappers Big Sean, Kendrick Lamar and American singer Betty Wright.  It was released on July 22, 2016 by We the Best Music Group and Epic Records as the third single of DJ Khaled's ninth studio album, "Major Key".  The song was produced by Edsclusive with co-production by Cool & Dre.
