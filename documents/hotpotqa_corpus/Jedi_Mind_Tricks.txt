Jedi Mind Tricks: Jedi Mind Tricks (JMT) is an underground hip hop duo from Philadelphia, Pennsylvania, founded by two high school friends, rapper Vinnie Paz (Vincenzo Luvineri) and producer Stoupe the Enemy of Mankind (Kevin Baldwin).  In 1999 second rapper Jus Allah joined the group to record their second studio album, "Violent by Design".  Allah left the group shortly after, but returned in 2008 for the sixth studio album, "A History of Violence".  In 2011 Stoupe left the group because "his heart wasn't in making JMT records anymore".  In 2013, Allah left again, and Stoupe returned in 2015.
