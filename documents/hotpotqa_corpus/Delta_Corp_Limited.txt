Delta Corp Limited: Delta Corp Limited, previously known as Arrow Webtex Ltd., is an Indian real estate, gaming and hospitality corporation that owns and operates casinos and hotels under several brands.  The company was founded as Creole Holdings Company Pvt Ltd on 5 November 1990.  It is the largest and only gaming and hospitality industry in India.  Delta Corp is a public company listed on the Bombay Stock Exchange (BSE) and National Stock Exchange of India Limited (NSE).  It runs the majority of the offshore casinos on Panaji, Goa.  It is the largest gaming company in India with a revenue of ₹INR 2.39 billion (2016).  The company operates casinos in three Indian states: Goa, Daman and Sikkim.
