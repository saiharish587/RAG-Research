Electoral district of Adelaide: Adelaide is an electorate for the South Australian House of Assembly.  The 23.4 km² state seat of Adelaide currently consists of the Adelaide city centre including North Adelaide and suburbs to the inner north and inner north east: Walkerville, Gilberton, Medindie, Medindie Gardens, Thorngate, Fitzroy, Ovingham, most of Prospect up to Regency Road, and parts of Collinswood and Nailsworth.  The boundaries have been the same for the past three elections.  The federal division of Adelaide covers the state seat of Adelaide and additional suburbs in each direction.
