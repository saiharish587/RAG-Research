Hedgerows Regulations 1997: The Hedgerows Regulations 1997 of England and Wales came into effect on 1st June 1997 and is government legislation which falls under the Environment Act 1995.  It was created to protect hedgerows, in particular those in the countryside aged 30 years or older.  Since the legislation came into effect it is a criminal offence to remove a hedgerow in contravention to the regulations.
