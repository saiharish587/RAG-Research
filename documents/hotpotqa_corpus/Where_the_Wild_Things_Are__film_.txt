Where the Wild Things Are (film): Where the Wild Things Are is a 2009 fantasy drama film directed by Spike Jonze.  Written by Jonze and Dave Eggers, it is adapted from Maurice Sendak's 1963 children's book of the same name.  It combines live-action, performers in costumes, animatronics, and computer-generated imagery (CGI).  The film stars Max Records and features the voices of James Gandolfini, Paul Dano, Lauren Ambrose, Forest Whitaker, Catherine O'Hara, and Chris Cooper.  The film centers on a lonely eight-year-old boy named Max who sails away to an island inhabited by creatures known as the "Wild Things," who declare Max their king.
