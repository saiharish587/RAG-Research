After Death: After Death is a 1988 Italian zombie film.  The film is set on a remote island where a voodoo curse that raises the dead from their graves to feast on the flesh of the living.  When a boat containing a group of explorers which includes a young girl who experienced the zombie uprising years earlier, makes an emergency docking on the island, the crew find that their only hope for survival is a protective idol given to the young girl by her mother years ago.
