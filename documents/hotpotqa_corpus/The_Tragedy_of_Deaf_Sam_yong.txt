The Tragedy of Deaf Sam-yong: The Tragedy of Deaf Sam-yong (Korean: 비련의 벙어리 삼용 , translit.  Biryeonui beongeori samyong) is a 1973 South Korean drama film directed by Byun Jang-ho.  The film was selected as the South Korean entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 46th Academy Awards, but was not accepted as a nominee.
