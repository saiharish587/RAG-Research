Johnny Kerr: John Graham "Red" Kerr (July 17, 1932 – February 26, 2009) was an American basketball player, coach, and color commentator.  He played in the NBA from 1954 to 1966, mainly as a member of the Syracuse Nationals.  He later held several coaching and administrative positions before embarking on a thirty-three year career as a television color commentator for the Chicago Bulls.
