Delta 16: Delta 16 is a Finnish fixed shooter video game developed by Jyri Lehtonen and published by Amersoft in 1985.  It was originally released in 1985 for the Commodore 16 home computer and later ported to Commodore 64 for which it was released free of charge.  A total of 101 copies were sold.  A notable feature was an automated firing mechanism that the player could activate.
