On Broadway: Act One – October 28th, 1987: On Broadway: Act One – October 28th, 1987 is a three-CD live album by the Jerry Garcia Band and the Jerry Garcia Acoustic Band, two music groups led by Grateful Dead guitarist and singer Jerry Garcia.  It contains three complete sets of music, recorded at the Lunt-Fontanne Theatre in New York City on October 28, 1987.  It was released by ATO Records on June 23, 2015.
