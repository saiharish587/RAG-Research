Samoan Clipper: Samoan Clipper was one of ten Pan American Airways Sikorsky S-42 flying boats.  It exploded near Pago Pago, American Samoa, on January 11, 1938, while piloted by famous aviator Ed Musick.  Musick and his crew of six died in the crash.  The aircraft was carrying only airmail and express freight; no passengers were aboard.
