Marsupilami (band): Marsupilami were an English progressive rock band active in the early 1970s.  Their name was taken from a cartoon character created by Belgian artist André Franquin.  The band released two albums, "Marsupilami" (1970) and "Arena" (1971), on Transatlantic Records.  The albums were reissued on Cherry Red Records in 2007.  The band briefly reunited for gigs in 2011.
