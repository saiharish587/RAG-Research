Here &amp; Now (America album): Here & Now is the sixteenth original studio album by American folk rock duo America, released by Burgundy Records in January 2007 (see 2007 in music).  This is the first pop music studio album released by the group since "Human Nature" in 1998 (the group released a Christmas-themed studio album in 2002, "Holiday Harmony").  This album was a modest hit when it was released in 2007, reaching 52 on the Billboard album chart; America's best chart showing since 1982's "View from the Ground".
