Mikayil Alakbarov: Mikayil Mahammad oglu Alakbarov (Azerbaijani: "Mikayıl Məhəmməd oğlu Ələkbərov" ; 1924– 13 October 1943) was an Azerbaijani Red Army man and a posthumous Hero of the Soviet Union.  Alakbarov was posthumously awarded the title on 26 October 1943 for his actions during the Battle of the Dnieper, during which he reportedly repulsed 14 counterattacks and was seriously wounded.
