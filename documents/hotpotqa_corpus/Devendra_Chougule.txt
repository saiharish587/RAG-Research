Devendra Chougule: Devendra Chougule is a Marathi actor, who has acted in numerous Marathi dramas, movies and television serials for over 9 years.  He has been associated with Marathi Theatre since 2000 and till date has performed in 6 different plays and variety of roles.  In the journey of 9 years of performing arts, he has been honoured with many prestigious awards.  Till date he has received Vocational Award from RCO Kolhapur Heritage in his name.  He has also performed in 2 Marathi Feature Films and 4 marathi serials.  On January 6, 2013.
