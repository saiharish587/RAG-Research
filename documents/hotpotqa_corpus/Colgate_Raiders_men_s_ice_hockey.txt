Colgate Raiders men's ice hockey: The Colgate Raiders men's ice hockey team is a National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I college ice hockey program that represents Colgate University.  The Raiders are a member of ECAC Hockey.  They used to play at Starr Rink from its inauguration in 1959 until the 2015-16 season.  Starting with the 2016-17 NCAA season, the Raiders have started playing their home games in the Class of 1965 Arena.  The program is located in Hamilton, New York.
