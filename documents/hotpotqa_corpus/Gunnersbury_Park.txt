Gunnersbury Park: Gunnersbury Park is a park between Acton, Brentford, Chiswick and Ealing, West London, England.  Purchased for the nation from the Rothschild family, it was opened to the public by Neville Chamberlain, then Minister of Health, on 21 May 1926.  The park is currently jointly managed by Ealing and Hounslow borough councils.
