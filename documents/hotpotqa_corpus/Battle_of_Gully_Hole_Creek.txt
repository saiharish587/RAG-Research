Battle of Gully Hole Creek: The Battle of Gully Hole Creek was a battle that took place on July 18, 1742 (new style) between Spanish and British forces in the Province of Georgia, resulting in a victory for the British.  Part of a much larger conflict, known as the War of Jenkins' Ear, the battle was for control of St. Simons Island, the British fortifications of Fort Frederica and Fort St. Simons, and the strategic sea routes and inland waters they controlled.  After the victory, the Province of Georgia established undisputed claim to the island, which is now part of the U.S. state of Georgia.  The better-known Battle of Bloody Marsh, a skirmish also won by the British, took place on the island the same day.
