Dui Purush (1945 film): Dui Purush (Two Generations) is a 1945 Bengali language epic family drama film directed by Subodh Mitra.  Produced by New Theatres and adapted from Tarashankar Banerji's novel and popular stage play, it had screenplay by Binoy Chatterjee.  The editing was done by Mitra, who was popularly known as Kachi Babu, and cited as one of the best editors in the film industry.  Music director was Pankaj Mullick with lyrics by Sailen Roy.  The cinematographers were Sudhin Majumdar and Yusuf Mulji.  The cast included Chhabi Biswas, Chandravati, Sunanda Banerjee, Ahindra Choudhury, Tulsi Chakraborty, Jahar Ganguly, Naresh Mitra.
