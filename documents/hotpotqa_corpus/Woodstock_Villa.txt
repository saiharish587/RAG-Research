Woodstock Villa: Woodstock Villa is a 2008 Indian musical thriller directed by Hansal Mehta and produced by Sanjay Gupta and Ekta Kapoor.  It features newcomers Sikandar Kher, Neha Oberoi and Arbaaz Khan in the primary roles while Shakti Kapoor, Gulshan Grover, Sachin Khedekar, Boman Irani and Anupama Verma essay other significant roles.  The soundtrack was composed by Anu Malik.  It was filmed in Mumbai and Mauritius.  The film, which was released in India on 30 May 2008, had a poor box office opening but earned mostly good reviews.  Sanjay Dutt and Saif Ali Khan have cameo appearances.  The plot is a loose adaptation of the 2003 Japanese thriller "Game" which also went on to be remade in Tamil in 2014 as "Sarabham"
