Jeanne Glynn: Jeanne Glynn (1932 – June 8, 2007) was a writer.  Jeanne Glynn died of cancer at age 75.  Glynn was an actress who turned to writing.  Her writing earned her five Daytime Emmy Award nominations.  Glynn's nominations came for her work on "General Hospital", "Guiding Light", "As the World Turns", "One Life to Live" and "Port Charles".  Other writing credits include "Search for Tomorrow".
