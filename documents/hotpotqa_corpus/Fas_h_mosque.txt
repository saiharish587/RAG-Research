Fas'h mosque: Fash Mosque (Arabic: مسجد الفسح‎ ‎ ), or Masjid Uhud is a small mosque beneath the Mount Uhud, under the cave, in Medina, Saudi Arabia.  There are some accounts of the Islamic prophet Muhammad fulfilling the Zuhr prayer on the day of the Battle of Uhud after the battle.  Initial construction was already destroyed and there are only few remnants of the east, west and south wall, and the mihrab mujawwaf which is still visible.  The building is now surrounded by an iron fence to guard its sustainability.  It is 4.5 km from Al-Masjid an-Nabawi.
