Flying Gold: Flying Gold (Hungarian: Repülő arany or Repülö arany) is a 1932 Hungarian crime film directed by Steve Sekely and starring Steven Geray, Gyula Kabos and Lajos Gárday.  A shipment of gold being flown from Paris to Budapest is robbed in mid-air.  A French-language version "Rouletabille aviateur" was also released.
