Santa María del Oro, Nayarit: Santa María del Oro is a municipality and municipal seat in the Mexican state of Nayarit.  It is located in the south of the state and has boundaries with the municipalities of El Nayar, Tepic, Ahuacatlán, Jala, San Pedro Lagunillas, La Yesca, and Xalisco.  The population was 21,688 in 2005, with the municipal seat of the same name having 3,314 inhabitants.  The total area was 912.90 km².  See  for statistical information.
