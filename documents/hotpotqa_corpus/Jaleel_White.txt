Jaleel White: Jaleel Ahmad White (born November 27, 1976) is an American actor.  In 1989, he was cast in the role of Steve Urkel on the sitcom "Family Matters".  The character, which was originally intended to be a one-time guest appearance, was an instant hit with audiences and White became a regular cast member.  The series aired for a total of nine seasons, from 1989 to 1997 on ABC, and from 1997 to 1998 on CBS.  Aside from this character, White is also known as the voice of Sonic the Hedgehog in the animated series "Adventures of Sonic the Hedgehog", "Sonic (SatAM)" and "Sonic Underground".  He reprised the voice of Sonic in the 2013 fan film, "Sonic".
