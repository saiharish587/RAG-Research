Terry Norris (actor): Terence Richard "Terry" Norris (born 9 June 1930) is an Australian professional actor who interrupted his career for 10 years with a stint in state politics, for the Australian Labor Party with the Victorian Legislative Assembly.  As an actor, he has starred in TV Shows such as "Bellbird" & "Cop Shop", and in films like "Romulus, My Father", "" and "Paper Planes".
