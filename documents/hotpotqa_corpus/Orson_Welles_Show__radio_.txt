Orson Welles Show (radio): Orson Welles Show (1941–42), also known as The Orson Welles Theater, Orson Welles and his Mercury Theater and the Lady Esther Show (after its sponsor), is a live CBS Radio series produced, directed and hosted by Orson Welles.  Broadcast Mondays at 10 p.m. ET, it made its debut September 15, 1941.  Its last broadcast was February 2, 1942.
