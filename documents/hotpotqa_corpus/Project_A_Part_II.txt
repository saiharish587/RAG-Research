Project A Part II: Project A Part II (; aka Jackie Chan’s Project A II) is a 1987 Hong Kong action film written and directed by Jackie Chan, who also starred in the lead role.  It is the sequel to the 1983 film "Project A".  Jackie Chan plays "Sergeant Dragon Ma" once again, Sammo Hung and Yuen Biao, stars from the original film, are absent.  The film was released in the Hong Kong on 19 August 1987.
