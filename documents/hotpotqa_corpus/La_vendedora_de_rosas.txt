La vendedora de rosas: La vendedora de rosas is a 1998 Colombian film directed by Víctor Gaviria.  The film is loosely based on the fairy tale "The Little Match Girl" by Hans Christian Andersen; it was entered into the 1998 Cannes Film Festival.  The film was selected as the Colombian entry for the Best Foreign Language Film at the 71st Academy Awards, but was not accepted as a nominee.
