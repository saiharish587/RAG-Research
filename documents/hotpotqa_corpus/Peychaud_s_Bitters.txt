Peychaud's Bitters: Peychaud's Bitters is a bitters distributed by the Sazerac Company.  It was originally created around 1830 by Antoine Amédée Peychaud, a Creole apothecary from the French colony of Saint-Domingue (now Haiti) who settled in New Orleans, Louisiana in 1795.  It is a gentian-based bitters, comparable to Angostura bitters, but with a lighter body, sweeter taste, and more floral aroma.  Peychaud's Bitters is the definitive component of the Sazerac cocktail.
