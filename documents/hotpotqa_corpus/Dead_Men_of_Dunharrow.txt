Dead Men of Dunharrow: The Dead Men of Dunharrow (also referred as the Shadow Host, the Grey Host, the oathbreakers, or simply the Dead) are fictional characters in J. R. R. Tolkien's Middle-earth legendarium.  They appear in "The Lord of the Rings" as the ghosts of Men of the White Mountains ("Ered Nimrais"), who were cursed to remain in Middle-earth by Isildur after they abandoned their oath to aid him in the War of the Last Alliance.  They were formerly known as the Men of the Mountains, and they were related to the Dunlendings.
