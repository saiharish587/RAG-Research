Idiot (1992 film): Idiot is a 1992 Hindi film based on Fyodor Dostoevsky's novel, "The Idiot".  It was directed by Mani Kaul and starred Shah Rukh Khan and Ayub Khan-Din.  The film debuted at the New York Film Festival in October 1992.  In this version of the tale, placed in contemporary Mumbai, Prince Miskin (Khan-Din) is a man whose epilepsy is mistaken for idiocy.
