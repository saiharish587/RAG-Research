Epistolary novel: An epistolary novel is a novel written as a series of documents.  The usual form is letters, although diary entries, newspaper clippings and other documents are sometimes used.  Recently, electronic "documents" such as recordings and radio, blogs, and e-mails have also come into use.  The word "epistolary" is derived from Latin from the Greek word ἐπιστολή "epistolē", meaning a letter (see epistle).
