Konstantin Trenyov: Konstantin Andreyevich Trenyov (Константи′н Андре′евич Тренё′в, 2 June [O.S. 19 May] 1876 , Baksheevka, Kharkov Governorate, Russian Empire, now Ukraine - May 19, 1945, Moscow, USSR) was a Soviet Russian writer and playwright, USSR State Prize laureate (1941), best known for his Russian Civil War history drama "Lyubov Yarovaya" (1926).
