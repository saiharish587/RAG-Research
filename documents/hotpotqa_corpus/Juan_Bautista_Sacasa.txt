Juan Bautista Sacasa: Juan Bautista Sacasa (León, Nicaragua, 21 December 1874 – Los Angeles, California, 17 April 1946) is the 20th President of Nicaragua from 1 January 1933 to 9 June 1936.  He was the eldest son of Roberto Sacasa, 44th and 46th President of Nicaragua, and Ángela Sacasa Cuadra, the former's cousin twice removed.  He was a relative of Benjamín Sacasa, 67th President of Nicaragua.
