Mike Saunders: Michael Earl "Mike" Saunders (born May 1952), also known as Metal Mike, is a rock critic and the singer of the Californian punk band Angry Samoans.  He is credited with coining the music genre label "heavy metal" in a record review for Humble Pie's "As Safe As Yesterday Is" in the November 12, 1970 issue of "Rolling Stone."  (The original text is shown in the VH1 Classic documentary "" from 2007.)  Six months later in 1971, he used the phrase again while reviewing Sir Lord Baltimore's first album, "Kingdom Come", in the pages of "Creem" magazine.
