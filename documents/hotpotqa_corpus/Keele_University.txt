Keele University: Keele University, officially known as the University of Keele, is a public research university located about 3 miles (5 km) from Newcastle-under-Lyme, Staffordshire, England.  Keele was granted university status by Royal Charter in 1962 and was founded in 1949 as the University College of North Staffordshire.  Keele is the 24th oldest university in the UK, and the highest research-ranked university in Staffordshire.  A science park and a conference centre complements the academic buildings, making it the largest campus university in the UK.  The university's School of Medicine operates the clinical part of its courses from a separate campus at the Royal Stoke University Hospital.  The School of Nursing and Midwifery is based at the nearby Clinical Education Centre.
