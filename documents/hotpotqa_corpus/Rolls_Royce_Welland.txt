Rolls-Royce Welland: The Rolls-Royce RB.23 Welland was Britain's first production jet engine.  It entered production in 1943 for the Gloster Meteor.  The name Welland is taken from the River Welland, in keeping with the Rolls-Royce policy of naming early jet engines after rivers based on the idea of continuous flow, air through the engine and water in a river.
