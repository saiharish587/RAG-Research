Por Baazar: Por Bazaar (English: Child Trafficking ) is a 2014 Marathi language thriller film which is produced by video Palace and directed by Manva Naik.  It stars Sai Tamhankar, Swarangi Marathe, Satya Manjrekar, Anurag Worlikar, Dharmaj Joshi, Sakheel Parchure, and Ankush Chaudhari in negative role.  Swapnil Joshi and Farhan Akhtar does a cameo in the film.
