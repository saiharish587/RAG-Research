Golden Boy Promotions: Golden Boy Promotions, Inc. is a boxing promotional firm started by former boxer 10-time world champion in six weight divisions Oscar De La Hoya (whose nickname is "The Golden Boy").  De La Hoya owns a majority interest in the firm, with Bernard Hopkins being a minority owner.  Previously Winky Wright, Shane Mosley, Marco Antonio Barerra and Ricky Hatton held interest in the company.
