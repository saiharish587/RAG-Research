1980 Damascus Titan missile explosion: The Damascus Titan missile explosion was an incident in the United States in 1980 in rural Arkansas.  Liquid fuel in a U.S. Air Force LGM-25C Titan II intercontinental ballistic missile exploded at a missile launch facility on September 19, 1980.  It occurred at Launch Complex 374-7 in Van Buren County farmland just north of Damascus, approximately 50 mi north of Little Rock.
