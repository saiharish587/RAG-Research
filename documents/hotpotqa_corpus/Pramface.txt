Pramface: Pramface was a BBC Three comedy-drama series starring Scarlett Alice Johnson and Sean Michael Verey.  Written by Chris Reddy, the first series, comprising six half-hour episodes, piloted on 23 February 2012.  The second series, which was commissioned by the BBC before the first series had even aired, began transmission on 8 January 2013 with an hour-long special as the first episode.  The remaining six episodes of the series were of the usual half-hour length.  A third series was commissioned which began airing on 25 February 2014 with the double episode series finale, which aired on 25 March 2014, bringing the third series to a total of six half-hour episodes.  It was confirmed in August 2014 that a fourth series will not be commissioned.
