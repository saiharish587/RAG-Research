Hijokaidan: Hijōkaidan (非常階段 , emergency staircase) is a Japanese noise and free improvisation group with a revolving lineup that has ranged from two members to as many as fourteen in its early days.  The group is the project of guitarist Jojo Hiroshige (JOJO広重 ) , its one constant member, who is head and owner of the Osaka-based Alchemy Records.  Other regulars include Jojo's wife Junko and Toshiji Mikawa (also of Incapacitants).
