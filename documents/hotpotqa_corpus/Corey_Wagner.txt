Corey Wagner: Corey Wagner (born 23 March 1997) is an Australian rules footballer playing for the North Melbourne Football Club in the Australian Football League (AFL).  He is the younger brother of Melbourne defender, Josh Wagner.  He was drafted by the North Melbourne Football Club with their fourth selection and forty-first overall in the 2015 national draft.  He made his debut in the nine point loss against Hawthorn in round 13, 2016 at Etihad Stadium.
