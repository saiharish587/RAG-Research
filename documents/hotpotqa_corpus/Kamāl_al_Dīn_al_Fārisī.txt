Kamāl al-Dīn al-Fārisī: Kamal al-Din Hasan ibn Ali ibn Hasan al-Farisi or Abu Hasan Muhammad ibn Hasan (1267– 12 January 1319, long assumed to be 1320)) (Persian: كمال‌الدين فارسی‎ ‎ ) was a prominent Persian Muslim scientist.  He made two major contributions to science, one on optics, the other on number theory.  Farisi was a pupil of the great astronomer and mathematician Qutb al-Din al-Shirazi, who in turn was a pupil of Nasir al-Din Tusi.
