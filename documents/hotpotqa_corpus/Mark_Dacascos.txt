Mark Dacascos: Mark Alan Dacascos (born February 26, 1964) is an American actor and martial artist.  He won numerous karate and various styles of kung fu championships between the ages of 7 and 18.  Dacascos is perhaps best known for his roles as Mani in the French film "Brotherhood of the Wolf", Toby Wong in the 1997 film "Drive", and Ling in the 2003 film "Cradle 2 the Grave".
