Anthem (Chess song): "Anthem" is a song from the concept album and subsequent musical "Chess" by Tim Rice, Benny Andersson and Björn Ulvaeus.  The song describes the feelings of Soviet Russian challenger, Anatoly Sergievsky, when he defects.  The song was originally sung by Tommy Körberg on the original concept album and as Anatoly in the original West End cast.  It was later covered by various artists including Josh Groban on his album "Stages" and Kerry Ellis.
