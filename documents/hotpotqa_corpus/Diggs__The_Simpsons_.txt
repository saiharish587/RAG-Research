Diggs (The Simpsons): "Diggs" is the twelfth episode of the 25th season of the American animated sitcom "The Simpsons", and the 542nd episode of the series.  It premiered on the Fox network in the United States on March 9, 2014.  The episode was written by Dan Greaney and Allen Glazier and directed by Michael Polcino.  In the episode, Bart makes friends with a transfer student named Diggs, an expert in falconry who saves Bart from the wrath of the Springfield Elementary bullies – and who intends to take to the sky himself, which makes Bart worry about Diggs' sanity.
