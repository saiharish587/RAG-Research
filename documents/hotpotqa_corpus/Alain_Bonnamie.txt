Alain Bonnamie: Alain Bonnamie ( (1965--) 31 1965 (age (2017)-(1965)-((11)<(07)or(11)==(07)and(30)<(31)) ) ) is a Canadian professional light middle/middle/super middle/light heavyweight boxer of the 1980s, '90s and 2000s who the World Boxing Council (WBC) Continental Americas light middleweight title, World Boxing Council (WBC) International light middleweight title, and Commonwealth middleweight title, and was a challenger for the North American Boxing Federation (NABF) light middleweight title against Wayne Powell (twice), and Canada light middleweight title against Stephane Ouellet, his professional fighting weight varied from 151+3/4 lb , i.e. light middleweight to 169+3/4 lb , i.e. light heavyweight.
