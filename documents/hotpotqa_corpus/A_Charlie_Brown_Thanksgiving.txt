A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving: A Charlie Brown Thanksgiving is the tenth prime-time animated TV special based upon the popular comic strip "Peanuts," by Charles M. Schulz.  It was originally aired on the CBS network on November 20, 1973, and won an Emmy Award the following year.  Currently, the special is aired every November in prime time on the ABC network.  It was the third holiday special after "A Charlie Brown Christmas" in 1965 and "It's The Great Pumpkin, Charlie Brown" in 1966.
