Jeremy Renner: Jeremy Lee Renner (born January 7, 1971) is an American actor.  He appeared largely in independent films, such as "Dahmer" (2002) and "Neo Ned" (2005).  Renner appeared in supporting roles in bigger films, such as "S.W.A.T." (2003) and "28 Weeks Later" (2007).  He was nominated for the Academy Award for Best Supporting Actor for his much-praised performance in "The Town" (2010).
