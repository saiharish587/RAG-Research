Fred Niblo Jr.: Fred Niblo Jr. (January 23, 1903 – February 1973) was a successful Hollywood screenwriter.  His career began in 1930 and lasted a little over twenty years.  He died in Los Angeles, California, in February 1973, aged 70.  He was the son of director Fred Niblo and Josephine "Josie" Cohan Niblo (1874–1916), sister of George M. Cohan.
