Smiley (2012 film): Smiley is a 2012 American psychological slasher horror film directed by Michael Gallagher and made by Level 10 Films.  The film stars were Caitlin Gerard, Melanie Papalia, Keith David, Shane Dawson, Andrew James Allen, Toby Turner, and Liza Weil.  The film was released on October 12, 2012.
