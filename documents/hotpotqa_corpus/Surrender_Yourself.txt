Surrender Yourself: "Surrender Yourself" is a 1992 debut single by the American electronica dance duo The Daou, which they both co-produced, wrote, and performed the musical arraignments on, most notably on the keyboards, as performed by Peter Daou.  Taken from their debut album, "Head Music", the single reached number one on the "Billboard" Hot Dance Club Play chart on July 11, 1992.
