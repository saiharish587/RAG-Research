KAND: KAND (1340 AM) is a radio station that serves the Corsicana - Ennis - Waxahachie area, and is owned by New Century Broadcasting.  This station runs a country music format, and is also the home of Corsicana High School Tigers and Navarro College Bulldogs football games, and the latest news from the Texas State Network News, and CBS News.  As of July 11, 2008, it airs sports programming from Fox Sports Radio during overnights and weekends.  For many years, the KAND call sign was assigned to the station now called KWPW.
