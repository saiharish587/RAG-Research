Lights (Ellie Goulding album): Lights is the debut studio album by English singer Ellie Goulding, released on 26 February 2010 by Polydor Records.  Goulding collaborated with several producers on the album, including Starsmith, Fraser T Smith, Frankmusik, Richard "Biff" Stannard, Ash Howes, Liam Howe, Fred Falke and Mumford & Sons' Ben Lovett.  The album received generally positive reviews from music critics, who complimented Goulding's electronic edge, while less favourable reviews felt the sound was generic.  "Lights" debuted atop the UK Albums Chart with first-week sales of 36,854 copies.  In North America, the album charted at number 21 in the United States and number 66 in Canada.  It spawned four singles: "Under the Sheets", "Starry Eyed", "Guns and Horses" and "The Writer".
