Erek Hansen: Erek Hansen (born August 31, 1982) is an American professional basketball player currently playing for Kolejlíler.  Ranks third in career blocked shots with 212.  In 2006, he had 89 blocked shots, the third best single season total at Iowa.  His 83 blocked shots in 2005 ranks fourth best for a single season.  He led the Big Ten in blocked shots as both a junior and senior.
