Black Velvet Flag: Black Velvet Flag was a New York City-based comedy music trio, known for their humorous, lounge-styled covers of songs by Southern California punk rock bands, and for performing while wearing tuxedos.  They became known in 1994, after performing on the New York music scene, which suddenly propelled them to fame in just six months.  Their only full-length album, "Come Recline", was released in 1995 on Go-Kart Records.  In 2003, an interactive documentary of the band, entitled "The Rise and Fall of Black Velvet Flag", was released; it was directed by Sheldon Schiffer.
