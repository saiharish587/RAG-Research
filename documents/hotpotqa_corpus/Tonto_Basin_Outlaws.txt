Tonto Basin Outlaws: Tonto Basin Outlaws is a 1941 American film directed by S. Roy Luby, the tenth of the entries in the Range Busters series.  Despite the film's title, the action takes place in Montana, not Arizona's Tonto Basin.  Like the other of the Range Busters series, the film was shot at Corriganville Movie Ranch and used footage from silent Westerns.
