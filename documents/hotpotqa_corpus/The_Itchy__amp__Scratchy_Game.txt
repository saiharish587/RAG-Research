The Itchy &amp; Scratchy Game: The Itchy & Scratchy Game is a platform video game that was released for the Super NES, and Game Gear.  It stars the mouse and cat Itchy and Scratchy from the American animated television series "The Simpsons", and features the classic gory violence from the show.  The main character is Itchy, who has to fight Scratchy.  Reception of the game has been generally negative.
