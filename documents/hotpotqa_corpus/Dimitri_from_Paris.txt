Dimitri from Paris: Dimitri from Paris (born Dimitrios Yerasimos in October 1963) is a Turkish born French music producer and DJ of Greek descent.  His musical influences are rooted in 1970s funk and disco sounds that spawned contemporary house music, as well as original soundtracks from 1950s and 1960s cult movies such as "Breakfast at Tiffany's", "La Dolce Vita" and "The Party", which were sampled in his album "Sacrebleu".  Dimitri fused these sounds with electro and block party hip hop he discovered in the 1980s.
