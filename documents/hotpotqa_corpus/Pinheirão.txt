Pinheirão: The Centro Poliesportivo Pinheiro, also known as the Pinheirão is a football stadium inaugurated on June 15, 1985 in Curitiba, Paraná, Brazil, with a maximum capacity of 35,000 people.  The stadium is owned by the Paranaense Football Federation (meaning "Federação Paranaense de Futebol" in Portuguese), and was the home ground of Paraná Clube.  Its formal name means Pinheiro Multi-Modality Center and honors the Pine tree, which is a common tree in the state.
