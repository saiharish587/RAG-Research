Australopithecus garhi: Australopithecus garhi is a 2.5-million-year-old gracile australopithecine species whose fossils were discovered in 1996 by a research team led by Ethiopian paleontologist Berhane Asfaw and Tim White, an American paleontologist.  The hominin remains are believed to be a human ancestor species and possibly the direct ancestor to the human genus, "Homo".
