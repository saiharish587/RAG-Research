Tsunami (Manic Street Preachers song): "Tsunami" is a song by Manic Street Preachers, released as a single on July 5, 1999 through Epic.  It was the fourth and final single to be released from the album "This Is My Truth Tell Me Yours" All three members of the band - James Dean Bradfield, Sean Moore and Nicky Wire - share the writing credits.  The single managed to peak at number 11 in the UK charts.
