Khazar language: Khazar, also known as "Khazaric" or "Khazaris", was the dialect spoken by the Khazars, a group of semi-nomadic Turkic originating from Central Asia.  There are few written records of the language, and it is regarded as extinct.  Khazar was a Turkic language; however, there is a dispute among scholars as to which branch of the Turkic language family it belongs.  One consideration believes it belongs to the Oghur ("lir") branch of the Turkic language family, while another consideration is that it belongs to the Oghuz ("shaz") branch.
