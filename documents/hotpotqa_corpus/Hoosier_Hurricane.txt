Hoosier Hurricane: Hoosier Hurricane is a wooden roller coaster at Indiana Beach in Monticello, Indiana.  The ride was designed by Dennis McNulty and Larry Bill of Custom Coasters International.  It opened on May 27, 1994, as the park's largest wooden roller coaster and the first wooden roller coaster built in Indiana in fifty years.  The ride was Custom Coasters International's third roller coaster designed and the first modern wooden coaster built with a steel support structure, which would eventually become a trend on many wooden coasters designed by them.
