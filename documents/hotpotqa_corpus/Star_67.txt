Star 67: Star 67 is a 2012 crime thriller directed by Kathi Selvakumar and Triden V Balasingam, featuring Imman Kannan in the lead role.  The soundtrack of the film was composed by Senthuran Alagaiya, while cinematography and editing were handled by Kuhenthiran and Triden V. Balasingam, respectively.  It was produced by Wotar Sound Pictures.  It had a decent run at select cinemas and tasted success at the box office.
