Blanche Cornwall: Blanche Cornwall (born April 24, 1868 in New York City, New York), was an American silent film and stage actress.  She was particularly active between 1911 and 1914 as one of the leading stars in Solax Studios films directed by Alice Guy-Blaché.  Before 1911, she was part of The Shubert Organization and made a success playing in theatrical adaptations of "Charlotte Temple" and "David Copperfield".  But already in 1918, in a letter to the editor of the "Motion Picture Magazine", a fan evoked Blanche Cornwall as a delightful "reminiscence" and a forgotten "old star."
