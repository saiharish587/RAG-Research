Dog anatomy: Dog anatomy comprises the anatomical studies of the visible parts of the body of a canine.  Details of structures vary tremendously from breed to breed, more than in any other animal species, wild or domesticated, as dogs are highly variable in height and weight.  The smallest known adult dog was a Yorkshire Terrier that stood only 6.3 cm at the shoulder, 9.5 cm in length along the head and body, and weighed only 113 g .  The largest known adult dog was an English Mastiff which weighed 155.6 kg and was 250 cm from the snout to the tail.  The tallest known adult dog is a Great Dane that stands 106.7 cm at the shoulder.
