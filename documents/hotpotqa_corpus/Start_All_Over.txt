Start All Over: "Start All Over" is a song performed by American recording artist Miley Cyrus.  It was released as the second and final single of Cyrus' debut album "Meet Miley Cyrus", the second disc of the dual album "".  A live version is available on "Hannah Montana & Miley Cyrus: Best of Both Worlds Concert" and as a karaoke in Cyrus's .  The song was originally written by Fefe Dobson, who passed the song to Cyrus, as she did not desire to include it on "Sunday Love".  The up-tempo song is lyrically about having a second chance in a romantic relationship.
