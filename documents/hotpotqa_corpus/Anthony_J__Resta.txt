Anthony J. Resta: Anthony J. Resta is an American record producer and musician.  A multi-instrumentalist, he is a known for his use of vintage audio gear.  He has earned twelve RIAA certified gold and multi-platinum awards and has been featured in many articles for his innovative recording techniques.  Some of his past clients have included Duran Duran, Elton John, Needtobreathe, Collective Soul, Guster, Megadeth, Perry Farrell, Nuno Bettencourt, Andrea Surova, Lucia Moniz, Dale Bozzio, TV Mania, Shawn Mullins, Del Marquis of Scissor Sisters, Green River Ordinance, Sarah Evans, The Cinnamon Fuzz, New Collisions, Blondie, Boys Don't Cry, and The Making Of The Making Of.
