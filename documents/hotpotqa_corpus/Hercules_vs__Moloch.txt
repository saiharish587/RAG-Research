Hercules vs. Moloch: Hercules vs. Moloch (Italian: "Ercole contro Moloch" , French: "Hercule contre Moloch" , also known as "Hercules Against Moloch", "Hercules vs. the Molloch", "Hercules Attacks", "Conquest of Mycene" and "The Conquest of Mycenae") is a 1963 Italian-French peplum film written and directed by Giorgio Ferroni and starring Gordon Scott.
