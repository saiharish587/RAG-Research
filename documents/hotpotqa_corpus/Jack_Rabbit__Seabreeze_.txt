Jack Rabbit (Seabreeze): Jack Rabbit is an "out and back" wooden roller coaster located at Seabreeze Amusement Park in Irondequoit, New York.  At its opening in 1920, it was the fastest roller coaster in the world.  The Giant Dipper in Santa Cruz, California, superseded it in 1924.  Jack Rabbit is the fourth oldest operating roller coaster in the world and the second oldest in the USA.  The oldest, Leap-The-Dips in Altoona, Pennsylvania, was closed from 1985 to 1999, making Jack Rabbit the oldest "continuously operating" coaster in the country.
