Kelley Jakle: Kelley Alice Jakle (born June 27, 1989) is an American actress and singer-songwriter.  She is best known for her role as Jessica in "Pitch Perfect" (2012) and its sequel "Pitch Perfect 2" (2015).  She is also known for her appearances on the first and second seasons of "The Sing-Off" in 2009 and 2010.
