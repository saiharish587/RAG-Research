Yassmin Alers: Yassmin Alers is an American actress born in New York City's Spanish Harlem as the middle child of a close-knit family of five children.  She was dance captain and understudy in the original broadway cast of Jonathan Larson's "RENT" on Broadway.  She also appeared in the Broadway production of Paul Simon's "The Capeman" and in the revival of "The Rocky Horror Show".  Alers has appeared in the International Tour of "The Who's Tommy" and the National Tour of "RENT".
