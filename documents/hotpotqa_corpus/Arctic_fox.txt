Arctic fox: The Arctic fox ("Vulpes lagopus"), also known as the white fox, polar fox, or snow fox, is a small fox native to the Arctic regions of the Northern Hemisphere and common throughout the Arctic tundra biome.  It is well adapted to living in cold environments.  It has a deep thick fur which is brown in summer and white in winter.
