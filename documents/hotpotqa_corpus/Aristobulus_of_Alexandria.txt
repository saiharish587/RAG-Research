Aristobulus of Alexandria: Aristobulus of Alexandria (Greek: Ἀριστόβουλος ) also called Aristobulus the Peripatetic (fl.  181–124 B.C.E.) and once believed to be Aristobulus of Paneas, was a Hellenistic Jewish philosopher of the Peripatetic school, though he also used Platonic and Pythagorean concepts.  Like his successor, Philo, he attempted to fuse ideas in the Hebrew Scriptures with those in Greek thought.
