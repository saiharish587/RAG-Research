KMVR: KMVR (104.9 FM, "Magic 105") is a radio station licensed to serve Mesilla Park, New Mexico.  The station is owned by Bravo Mic Communications, LLC.  It airs a Hot Adult Contemporary music format featuring artists such as Nelly Furtado, Duran Duran, The Fray, The Cure, Green Day, Madonna, Lifehouse, and others.  Its sister stations are KOBE, KVLC, KXPZ.  KMVR-FM broadcasts on 104.9 from Mesilla Park NM.
