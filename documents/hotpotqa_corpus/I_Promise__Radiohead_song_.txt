I Promise (Radiohead song): "I Promise" is a song by the English rock band Radiohead, recorded during the sessions for their third album, "OK Computer" (1997).  The band felt it was not strong enough to release at the time, but included it on the 2017 "OK Computer" reissue, "OK Computer OKNOTOK 1997 2017", and released it as a download with a music video on 2 June 2017.
