Bolivia national football team 1997: The Bolivia national football team played a total number of eighteen international matches in the 1997 season under the guidance of manager Antonio López Habas, including ten games at the 1998 FIFA World Cup qualification.  The team reached the final of the 2007 Copa América, hosted in Bolivia, but failed to qualify for the 1998 FIFA World Cup, finishing in 8th place in the CONMEBOL-ranking.
