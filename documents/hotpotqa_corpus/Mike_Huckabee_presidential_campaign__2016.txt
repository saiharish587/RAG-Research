Mike Huckabee presidential campaign, 2016: The 2016 presidential campaign of Mike Huckabee, the 44th Governor of Arkansas, began on May 5, 2015 at an event in his hometown of Hope, Arkansas.  Huckabee's candidacy for the Republican nomination in the 2016 Presidential election is his second, after having previously run in 2008.  Following a disappointing showing in the Iowa caucuses, Huckabee ended his run on February 1, 2016.
