Sugar Ray Robinson: Sugar Ray Robinson (born Walker Smith Jr.; May 3, 1921 – April 12, 1989) was an American professional boxer widely considered the greatest pound-for-pound boxer of all time.  Robinson's performances in the welterweight and middleweight divisions prompted sportswriters to create "pound for pound" rankings, where they compared fighters regardless of weight.  He was inducted into the International Boxing Hall of Fame in 1990.
