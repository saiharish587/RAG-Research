La vacanza: La vacanza (AKA Vacation) is a 1971 Italian drama film by Tinto Brass.  It stars Vanessa Redgrave and Franco Nero.  It premiered at the Venice Film Festival on 4 September 1971 where it was awarded the 'Best Italian Film' prize.  This was followed by a theatrical release in Italy on 5 April 1972.  A year earlier, Brass, Redgrave and Nero had worked together on the romantic drama, "Dropout".
