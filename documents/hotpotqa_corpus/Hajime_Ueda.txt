Hajime Ueda: Hajime Ueda (ウエダ ハジメ , UEDA Hajime ) is a Japanese manga artist who created a two-volume adaptation of the Japanese animated OVA series "FLCL", and the original manga "".  He started his career as a dōjinshi artist, gaining a reputation for his quirky and unique style of art.  He also does some costume design and sculpting.  Ueda was also the artist behind the ending theme animation for the Bakemonogatari anime, from the "Monogatari" series of stories.
