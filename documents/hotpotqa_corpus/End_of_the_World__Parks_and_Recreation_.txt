End of the World (Parks and Recreation): End of the World is the sixth episode of the fourth season of the NBC sitcom "Parks and Recreation".  It originally aired in the United States on November 3, 2011.  "End of the World" was written by Michael Schur and was directed by Dean Holland.  Overall, it is the 52nd episode of Park and Recreation.  The episode features a doomsday cult, the Resonabilists (also known derogatorily as "Zorpies"), who predict that the world is coming to an end.
