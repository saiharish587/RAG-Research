Sergio D'Autilia: Sergio D'Autilia (born 20 August 1977) is an Italian footballer who plays as a forward for Brera Calcio.  He made his fully professional club debut for Inter in the 1996–97 Coppa Italia on November 6, 1996 against Cagliari.  While he never played a league match with Inter, he had 19 appearances on the bench during the 1996–97 Inter Milan season and also in the second leg of the 1997 UEFA Cup Final against FC Schalke 04.
