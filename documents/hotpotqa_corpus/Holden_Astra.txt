Holden Astra: The Holden Astra is a compact car marketed by Holden in Australia.  Spanning six generations, the original, Australia-only Astra of 1984 was a derivative of the locally produced Nissan Pulsar, as was the 1987 Astra.  It was succeeded by the Holden Nova in 1989—another unique-to-Australia model line.  From 1995, the Holden Astra name was used in New Zealand, for a badge engineered version of the Opel Astra, which had been sold locally as an Opel since 1993.  The following year, Holden discontinued the Nova line in Australia in favour of the Opel-based Holden Astra.  On 1 May 2014, Holden announced to import the Opel Astra J GTC and Opel Astra J OPC with Holden badges to Australia and New Zealand.
