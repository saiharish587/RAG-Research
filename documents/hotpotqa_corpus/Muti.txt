Muti: Muti is a term for traditional medicine in Southern Africa as far north as Lake Tanganyika.  In South African English, the word muti is derived from the Zulu word "umuthi", meaning "tree," whose root is "-thi".  In Southern Africa, cognates of the word "umuthi" (or the word "muti") is in widespread use in most indigenous African languages as well as in South African English and Afrikaans, which sometimes use it as a slang word for medicine in general.
