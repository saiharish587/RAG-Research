Exis (EP): Exis (stylized as EXIS) is the debut extended play by Canadian recording artist Roy Woods.  It was released on July 31, 2015, by OVO Sound and Warner Bros.  Records.  The EP's sole guest appearance comes from Canadian rapper and label-mate Drake.  The official cover of "Exis" contains a picture of a white sketched tree with a blue background (representing "night time") and also the yellow moon.  The back cover reveals the track list on one side of the moon.  The first music video to be put out from the album was for the song "Jealousy".  The video was directed by creative artist Jim Joe.  It has received over 2,000,000 views since its release.
