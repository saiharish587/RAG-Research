Patrick Bouvier Kennedy: Patrick Bouvier Kennedy (August 7, 1963 – August 9, 1963) was the last child of United States President John F. Kennedy and First Lady Jacqueline Bouvier Kennedy.  He was the younger brother of Caroline and John Jr., and had a third sibling who was stillborn.  Born prematurely, Patrick Bouvier Kennedy lived just over 39 hours before desperate attempts to save him failed, putting the First Family and nation into mourning.  Three months later, his death was eclipsed by his father's assassination, but eventually his short lifespan led to innovations in treatment of premature infants, which gave rise to the pediatric subspecialty neonatology.
