Major Stars: Major Stars is a critically acclaimed American psychedelic rock band from greater Boston, Massachusetts.  Their first live performance was in 1997 at the inaugural Terrastock Festival in Providence, Rhode Island.  They toured Japan in 2000 with Overhang Party.  In 2002, they toured with Acid Mothers Temple as an opening act and performed at the South by Southwest Festival in Austin, Texas.  In December 2006 they performed at the Thurston Moore curated All Tomorrow's Parties festival in the UK.
