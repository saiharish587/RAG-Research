Eduant Private Russian School: Eduant Private Russian School (Turkish: Uluslararası Eduant Rus Okulu ) is a Russian international school in Antalya, Turkey.  It was established in 2000.  As of 2014 it had 120 students in its compulsory education section.  Many Ukrainian students arrived in Antalya due to the 2014 pro-Russian unrest in Ukraine.  The school offers English, Turkish and German as foreign languages.
