Royce Jones: Royce Jones (born December 15, 1954) is a Grammy-winning American musician best known for his work as a touring vocalist with the bands Steely Dan (in 1973 and 1974) and Ambrosia (joined 1978).  In the studio, Jones contributed vocals to David Pack's "Anywhere You Go", Odyssey's self-titled release, Steely Dan's "Countdown to Ecstasy", Bruzer's "Round 1" and Stephan Cohn's self-titled release.
