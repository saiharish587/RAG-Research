The Collector (2009 film): The Collector is a 2009 American home invasion horror film written by Marcus Dunstan and Patrick Melton, and directed by Dunstan.  The script, titled "The Midnight Man", was originally intended to be a prequel to the film "Saw", but the producers opposed the idea and dismissed it.  The film has developed a cult following.  A sequel, "The Collection", was released in 2012.
