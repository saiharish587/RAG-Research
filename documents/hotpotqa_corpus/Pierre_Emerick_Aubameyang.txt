Pierre-Emerick Aubameyang: Pierre-Emerick Emiliano François Aubameyang (] ; born 18 June 1989) is a French-born Gabonese professional footballer who plays for German Bundesliga club Borussia Dortmund and the Gabon national team.  He is known for his speed, having clocked 30 metres in 3.7 seconds, and his capability of playing as a forward or a winger.
