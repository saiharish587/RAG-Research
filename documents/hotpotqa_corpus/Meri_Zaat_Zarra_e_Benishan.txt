Meri Zaat Zarra-e-Benishan: Meri Zaat Zarra-e-Benishan (Urdu: ‎ ) is a Pakistani drama serial, directed by Babar Javed, and produced by Abdullah Kadwani and Humayun Saeed.  It is based on Umera Ahmad's novel of the same name.  It is an emotional story of a girl named Saba and her daughter Sara.  The drama became very popular upon its release.  In 2001
