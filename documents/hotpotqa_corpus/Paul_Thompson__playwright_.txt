Paul Thompson (playwright): Paul Thompson O.C. (born May 4, 1940 in Charlottetown, Prince Edward Island) is a Canadian playwright and theatre director.  Best known for his term as artistic director of Theatre Passe Muraille in Toronto, Ontario from 1970 to 1982, Thompson was known for pioneering techniques of collective creation, in which actors, playwrights and directors would collaborate on the creation of a play through field research and acting improvisations.  Plays on which Thompson was credited as a primary or collaborating writer during this era included "Doukhobors" (1970), "The Farm Show" (1972), "1837: The Farmers' Revolt" (1973, with Rick Salutin), "I Love You, Baby Blue" (1975), "Far As the Eye Can See" (1977, with Rudy Wiebe) and "Maggie and Pierre" (1980, with Linda Griffiths).
