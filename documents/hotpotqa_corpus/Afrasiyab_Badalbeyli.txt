Afrasiyab Badalbeyli: Afrasiyab Badal oglu Badalbeyli (Azerbaijani: "Əfrasiyab Bədəlbəyli" ) (19 April 1907, Baku – 6 January 1976, Baku) was a Soviet Azerbaijani composer, with noble Iranian origins (he was the descendant of Bahman Mirza and Abbas Mirza, " conductor and music critic, author of the music and libretto of "Giz Galasi" ("The Maiden Tower"), the first Azerbaijani ballet and the first ballet in the Muslim Orient.
