Barnens brevlåda: Barnens brevlåda (""The Children's Letterbox"") was a Swedish children's radio programme, led by Sven Jerring.  It aired totally 1 785 times, between 11 September 1925 and 1972, making it the longest-lasting Swedish radio programme of that time.  The record was broken on 14 November 1999 by the programme "Smoke Rings".
