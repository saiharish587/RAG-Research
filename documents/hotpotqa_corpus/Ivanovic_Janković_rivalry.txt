Ivanovic–Janković rivalry: Serbian tennis players Ana Ivanovic and Jelena Janković are both former world No. 1 and have met 12 times so far.  Their overall head-to-head series is 9–3, in Ivanovic's favor.  Janković is two years older than Ivanovic, so their paths connect them to play each other.  They are the first female players to achieve major successes for independent Serbia.
