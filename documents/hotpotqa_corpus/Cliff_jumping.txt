Cliff jumping: Cliff jumping is jumping off a cliff as a form of sport.  When done without equipment, it may be also known as tombstoning.  It forms part of the sport of coastal exploration or "coasteering".  When performed with a parachute, it is known as BASE jumping.  The world record for cliff jumping is currently held by Laso Schaller, with a jump of 58.8 m (193 ft).
