2008 Sichuan earthquake: The 2008 Sichuan earthquake (), also known as the First Great Sichuan earthquake or Wenchuan earthquake, occurred at 14:28:01 China Standard Time on May 12, 2008.  Measuring at 8.0 M, the earthquake's epicenter was located 80 km west-northwest of Chengdu, the provincial capital, with a focal depth of 19 km .  The earthquake was also felt in nearby countries and as far away as both Beijing and Shanghai—1500 km and 1700 km away—where office buildings swayed with the tremor.  Strong aftershocks, some exceeding 6 M, continued to hit the area up to several months after the main quake, causing further casualties and damage.
