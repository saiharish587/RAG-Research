Mayor Boss: Mayor Boss (born 5 June 1986), is a Nigerian rapper, singer, songwriter and video producer based in Europe.  He is member of the Nigerian Hip-hop, R&B & Pop group called Young Paperboyz and also has developed a solo career for himself with a major debut release of his two solo single called “My Diva,” and Mi Meow.  Mayor Boss has also founded his own record label, Naijamayor Records
