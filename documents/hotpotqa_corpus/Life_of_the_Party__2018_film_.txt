Life of the Party (2018 film): Life of the Party is an upcoming comedy film directed by Ben Falcone and written by Falcone and Melissa McCarthy.  The film stars McCarthy, Molly Gordon, Maya Rudolph, Julie Bowen, Gillian Jacobs, Debby Ryan, Matt Walsh and Jacki Weaver.  Produced by On the Day, the film is scheduled to be released May 11, 2018 by Warner Bros.  Pictures.
