John Lennox: John Carson Lennox (born 7 November 1943) is a Northern Irish mathematician and scientist specialising in group theory, philosopher of science, Christian apologist, and Emeritus Professor of Mathematics at the University of Oxford.  He is a Fellow in Mathematics and Philosophy of Science at Green Templeton College, Oxford University.  He is also Pastoral Advisor of Green Templeton College, Oxford, a Fellow of Wycliffe Hall, Oxford and Associate Fellow of the Saïd Business School.
