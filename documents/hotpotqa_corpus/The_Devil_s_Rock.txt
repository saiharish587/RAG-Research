The Devil's Rock: The Devil's Rock is a 2011 New Zealand supernatural horror war film produced by Leanne Saunders, directed by Paul Campion, written by Campion, Paul Finch, and Brett Ihaka, and starring Craig Hall, Matthew Sunderland, Gina Varela, and Karlos Drinkwater.  It is set in the Channel Islands on the eve of D-Day and tells the story of two New Zealand commandos who discover a Nazi occult plot to unleash a demon to win World War II.  The film combines elements of war films and supernatural horror films.  The film was theatrically released on July 8, 2011 in the United Kingdom and September 22, 2011 in New Zealand.  The film received mixed reviews from critics with the majority rating it average to above average and with many audience viewers on IMDB giving it a higher than average review.
