Lafayette Afro Rock Band: Lafayette Afro Rock Band was an American funk rock band formed in Roosevelt, Long Island, New York that relocated to France in 1970.  Though almost unknown in their native United States, they are now universally celebrated as one of the standout funk bands of the 1970s and admired for their use of break beats.  The band also recorded as Ice and as Krispie and Company (or Crispy and Company).
