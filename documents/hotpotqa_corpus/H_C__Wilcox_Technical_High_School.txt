H.C. Wilcox Technical High School: H.C. Wilcox Technical High School, or Wilcox Tech, is a technical high school located in Meriden, Connecticut that first opened its doors to Connecticut residents in a downtown building in 1918.  Today it receives students from many nearby towns.  Wilcox Tech is part of the Connecticut Technical High School System.  Like many Connecticut Technical High Schools, their decades-old building was recently renovated and expanded, at a cost of $77.5 million, and reopened Fall 2014.
