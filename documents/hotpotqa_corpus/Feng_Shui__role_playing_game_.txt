Feng Shui (role-playing game): Feng Shui is a martial arts-themed role-playing game, designed by Robin Laws, published first by Daedalus Entertainment and now by Atlas Games.  The game shares its setting with the collectible card game "Shadowfist".  The system is simple, with most detail being in the game's combat system.  Combat is made to flow quickly, moving from one action scene to another very quickly.  It was inspired and based on Hong Kong style action movies.
