Marc Bolan: Marc Bolan ( ; born Mark Feld; 30 September 1947 – 16 September 1977) was an English singer-songwriter, musician, guitarist, and poet.  He was best known as the lead singer of the glam rock band T. Rex.  Bolan was one of the pioneers of the glam rock movement of the 1970s.  He died at age 29 in a car accident a fortnight before his 30th birthday.
