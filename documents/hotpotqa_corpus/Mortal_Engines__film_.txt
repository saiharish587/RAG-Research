Mortal Engines (film): Mortal Engines is an upcoming New Zealand-American science fiction action adventure film directed by Christian Rivers and written by Peter Jackson, Fran Walsh, and Philippa Boyens.  It is based on the novel of the same name by Philip Reeve.  It stars Robert Sheehan, Hera Hilmar, Hugo Weaving, Jihae, Leila George, Ronan Raftery, and Stephen Lang.  It is set to be released in theaters on December 14, 2018.
