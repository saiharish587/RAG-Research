Kermes (dye): Kermes is a red dye derived from the dried bodies of the females of a scale insect in the genus "Kermes", primarily "Kermes vermilio".  The "Kermes" insects are native in the Mediterranean region and live on the sap of the Kermes oak.  They were used as a red dye by the ancient Greeks and Romans.  The kermes dye is a rich red.  It has good colour fastness in silk and wool.  It was much esteemed in the medieval era for dyeing silk and wool.  Post-medievally it was replaced by other red dyes.
