Lee Lin Chin: Lee Lin Chin is an Indonesian-born Australian television presenter and journalist.  She is best known for her association with the Special Broadcasting Service (SBS) network and is the weekend presenter of "SBS World News".  She has been a news presenter and journalist for many years, and in more recent times, has become popular throughout social media.  In 2016, she was nominated for the Gold Logie becoming the first SBS personality to be nominated for the award in the station's 36-year run.
