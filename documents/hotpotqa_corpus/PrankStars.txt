PrankStars: PrankStars was a 2011 American 6-episode reality television series that aired monthly and employed the use of a hidden camera.  The series premiered on Disney Channel on July 15, 2011, and was hosted by "Pair of Kings" and "Hannah Montana" star, Mitchel Musso.  The television program portrayed scenarios in which children and teenagers met their favorite stars in "unpredictable and humorous" settings that had been engineered by their friends and family.  The show was Disney Channel's first reality series since the "Totally in Tune" show.  In Canada, Family Channel aired two episodes of "PrankStars" on September 30, 2011, and a total of four episodes were originally supposed to broadcast before the series was withdrawn.  The fourth episode aired a day before Musso's mid-October 2011 arrest on a charge of driving while intoxicated (and subsequent removal from the cast of "Pair of Kings"), and the remaining two episodes premiered solely in the United Kingdom and Latin America, they premiered in the U.S. in November 2016 on Disney XD.
