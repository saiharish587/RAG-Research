Sisters of the Good Samaritan: The Congregation of the Sisters of the Good Samaritan, colloquially known as the "Good Sams", is a Roman Catholic congregation of religious women commenced by Bede Polding, OSB, Australia’s first Catholic bishop, in Sydney in 1857.  The congregation was the first religious congregation to be founded in Australia.  The sisters form an apostolic institute that follows the Rule of Saint Benedict.  They take their name from the well-known gospel parable of the Good Samaritan.
