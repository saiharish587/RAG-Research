Grand River (Oklahoma): The Grand River is an alternate name for the lower section of the Neosho River, a tributary of the Arkansas River in Oklahoma.  "Grand River" refers to the section of river below the confluence of the Neosho and Spring rivers in Ottawa County near Miami.  It empties into the Arkansas northeast of Muskogee, just downstream from the confluence of the Verdigris River with the Arkansas.  The area of convergence of the three rivers Arkansas, Verdigris and Neosho is called "Three Forks".
