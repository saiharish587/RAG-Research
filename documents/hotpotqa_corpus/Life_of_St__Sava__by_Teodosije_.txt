Life of St. Sava (by Teodosije): The Life of St. Sava (Serbian: Žitije Svetog Save/Житије Светог Саве ) was the second biography of Saint Sava (1169–1236), the first Archbishop of Serbs (s. 1219–1235), written by Serbian monk Teodosije the Hilandarian (1246–1328), after the first biography written in 1254 by monk Domentijan.
