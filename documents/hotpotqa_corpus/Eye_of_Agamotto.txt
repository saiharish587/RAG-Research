Eye of Agamotto: The Eye of Agamotto ( ) is a fictional mystical item appearing in American comic books published by Marvel Comics.  The item appears in publications in particular those featuring Doctor Strange.  The Eye of Agamotto is the name commonly given to the amulet Strange wears on his chest, though the Eye actually resides within the amulet and is released from time to time.  Created by writer Stan Lee and artist Steve Ditko, it first appeared in "The Origin of Dr. Strange", an eight-page story in "Strange Tales" #115 (December 1963).  In designing the Eye, Ditko drew inspiration from the real world charm The All Seeing Eye of the Buddha, known among Buddhists as The Amulet of Snail Martyrs, a Nepali symbol meant to protect its wearer against evil.
