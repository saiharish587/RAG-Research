Era Vulgaris (album): Era Vulgaris is the fifth full length studio album by American rock band Queens of the Stone Age.  The album was completed in early April 2007 and released on June 8, 2007 in some countries, June 11, 2007 in the United Kingdom and June 12, 2007 in the United States.  The single "Sick, Sick, Sick" was released in May, a second single "3's & 7's" was released in early June, with a third single "Make It wit Chu" released around Halloween of 2007.
