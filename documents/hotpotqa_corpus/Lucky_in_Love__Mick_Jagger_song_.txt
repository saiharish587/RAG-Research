Lucky in Love (Mick Jagger song): "Lucky in Love" is a song written and performed by English singer-songwriter Mick Jagger released as the second single from his debut album, "She's the Boss", in 1985.  "Lucky in Love" was the seven track on "She's the Boss" and was one of two tracks from "She's the Boss" included on Jagger's greatest hits album, "The Very Best of Mick Jagger".
