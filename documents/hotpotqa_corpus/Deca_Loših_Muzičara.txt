Deca Loših Muzičara: Deca Loših Muzičara (Serbian Cyrillic: Деца Лоших Музичара; trans.  "Bad Musicians' Children"), often abbreviated to DLM, are a funk rock band from Belgrade.  They were one of the most popular Serbian bands in the early 1990s.  Most DLM songs are fast and furious funk rock, but melodic, adorned with trumpet and saxophone lines.
