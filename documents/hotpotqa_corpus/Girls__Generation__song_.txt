Girls' Generation (song): Girls' Generation (Hangul: 소녀시대; RR: "Sonyeo Sidae") is a Korean song sung by several artists.  The song was originally sung by Lee Seung-chul in 1989, released on his self-titled album "Lee Seung-chul: Part 2" (Korean: 이승철 1집 Part 2).
