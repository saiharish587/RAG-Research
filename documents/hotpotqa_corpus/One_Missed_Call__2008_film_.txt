One Missed Call (2008 film): One Missed Call is a 2008 supernatural horror film directed by Eric Valette and written by Andrew Klavan.  An international co-production between the United States, the United Kingdom, Japan, and Germany, it is a remake of the 2003 Japanese film of the same name directed by Takashi Miike, which itself was based on the Yasushi Akimoto novel "Chakushin Ari".  The film stars Shannyn Sossamon, Edward Burns, Ana Claudia Talancón, Ray Wise and Azura Skye.
