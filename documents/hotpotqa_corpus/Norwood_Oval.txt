Norwood Oval: Norwood Oval (currently known as Coopers Stadium due to sponsorship from the Adelaide-based Coopers Brewery) is a suburban oval in the western end of Norwood, an inner eastern suburb of Adelaide, South Australia.  Norwood, Payneham & St Peters Council own the Oval but rent it to the Norwood Football Club.  It has been used for a variety of sporting and community events including baseball, soccer, rugby league and American football, but its main use is for Australian rules football.  It is the home ground for the Norwood Football Club ("The Redlegs") in the South Australian National Football League (SANFL).
