Maputo International Airport: Maputo International Airport (IATA: MPM, ICAO: FQMA) , also known as Lourenço Marques Airport or Mavalane International Airport, is an airport located 3 km northwest of the center of Maputo, the largest city and capital of Mozambique.  It is the largest airport in Mozambique, and hub for LAM Mozambique Airlines and Kaya Airlines.  Most destinations served from the airport are in Africa but TAP Portugal operates a service to Lisbon, Portugal, Turkish Airlines operates flights to Istanbul, and Qatar Airways operates flights to Doha.
