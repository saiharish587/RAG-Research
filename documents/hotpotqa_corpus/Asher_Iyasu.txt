Asher Iyasu: Asher Iyasu (Hebrew: אשר איסו‎ ‎ , born in Ethiopia) is an Israeli golfer who was the first golfer to win the Caesarea Golf & Country Club's tournament from the Beta Israel community.  His first real success came in a doubles tournament in which he won with a top blind golfer, Israel's only blind golfer, Zohar Sharon.  The second was with the son of the famous writer Yigal Mossinson, Humi.
