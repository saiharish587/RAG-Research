Lasseter (musical): Lasseter is an Australian musical with book and lyrics by Reg Livermore and music by Sandra McKenzie and Patrick Flynn.  Described as a musical fable, it follows the spiritual journey of a disillusioned group of young people who leave the city and temptations of consumer society in search of a Utopia in the Australian outback.  The title of the musical was inspired by the explorer Harold Bell Lasseter (but the musical is not about him).
