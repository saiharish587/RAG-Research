Put (card game): Put is an English tavern trick-taking card game first recorded in the 16th century and later castigated by 17th century moralists as one of ill repute.  It belongs to a very ancient family of card games and clearly relates to a group known as Trut, "Truque", also "Tru", and the South American game Truco.  Its more elaborate version is the Spanish game of Truc, which is still much played in many parts of Southern France and Spain.
