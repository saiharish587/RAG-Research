2015 Accra floods: The 2015 Accra floods resulted from heavy continuous rainfall in Accra, the largest city in Ghana.  The rain started on 1 June 2015.  Other causes of this flood is as a result of the improper planning of settlement in Accra, choked gutters which block the drainage system and a few other human factors.  The floods have resulted in heavy traffic on the roads in the city and also a halt in commercial activities as markets were flooded and workers trapped.  Mayor of Accra Metropolitan Assembly, Alfred Oko Vanderpuije described the flooding as critical.  At least 25 people have died from the flooding directly, while a petrol station explosion caused by the flooding killed at least 200 more people.
