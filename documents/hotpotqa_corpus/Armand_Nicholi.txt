Armand Nicholi: Armand M. Nicholi, M.D., Jr. is a clinical professor of psychiatry at Harvard Medical School and the Massachusetts General Hospital.  His clinical work and research has focused on the impact of absent parents on the emotional development of children and young adults.  He is the editor and coauthor of the classic "The Harvard Guide to Psychiatry" (3rd edition, 1999).  He was also a founding board member of the Family Research Council.
