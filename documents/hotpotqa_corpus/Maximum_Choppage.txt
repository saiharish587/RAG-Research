Maximum Choppage: Maximum Choppage is an Australian television comedy series starring Lawrence Leung.  The six-part series premiered in 2015 on ABC2.  It is directed by Craig Melville and written by Leung, Duncan Sarkies and Josh Mapleston.  It is produced by Julie Eckersley, Sophie Miller and Linda Micsko with executive producers Tony Ayres and Debbie Lee.  The series is based on the film work of Timothy Ly which was developed into the TV series by Matchbox Pictures.
