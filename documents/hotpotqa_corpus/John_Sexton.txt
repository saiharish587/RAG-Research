John Sexton: John Edward Sexton (born September 29, 1942) is an American lawyer and academic.  Sexton served as the fifteenth President of New York University, from 2002 to 2015.  From 1988 to 2002, he served as Dean of the NYU School of Law, during which time NYU became one of the top five law schools in the country according to "U.S. News and World Report".  From January 1, 2003 to January 1, 2007, he was the Chairman of the Board of the Federal Reserve Bank of New York; in 2006, he served as chair of the Federal Reserve System's Council of Chairs.
