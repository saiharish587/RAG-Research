Hans Christian Andersen (film): Hans Christian Andersen is a 1952 Hollywood musical film directed by Charles Vidor, with lyrics and music by Frank Loesser.  The story was by Myles Connolly, screenplay written by Moss Hart and Ben Hecht (uncredited), and Samuel Goldwyn Productions were the producers.  It is a fictional, romantic story revolving around the life of the famous Danish poet and story-teller Hans Christian Andersen.  The film stars Danny Kaye in the title role.
