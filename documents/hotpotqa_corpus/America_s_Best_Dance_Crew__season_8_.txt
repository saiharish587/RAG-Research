America's Best Dance Crew (season 8): America’s Best Dance Crew All-Stars: Road to the VMAs was the eighth season of the competitive dance reality television series "America's Best Dance Crew".  The revival of the series was announced by MTV on January 10, 2015, following the show's cancellation in 2012.  The season, which premiered on July 29, 2015, was hosted by television presenter Jason Dundas.  The season consisted of six "Video Music Award"–themed episodes; the winner was crowned in a live episode finale airing before the 2015 MTV Video Music Awards.  Hip hop artist T-Pain, recording artist Teyana Taylor, and Broadway performer Frankie Grande formed the new judging panel.
