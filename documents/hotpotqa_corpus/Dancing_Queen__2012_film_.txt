Dancing Queen (2012 film): Dancing Queen (Hangul: 댄싱퀸 ; RR: "Daensing Kwin " ) is 2012 South Korean romantic comedy film starring Uhm Jung-hwa and Hwang Jung-min.  The film tells a story of a married couple, who in the midst of their mundane lives decides to pursue their lost dreams.  The husband finds himself accidentally running for Mayor of Seoul and his wife decides to become a pop singer.  It was produced by JK Film and distributed by CJ Entertainment, and released on January 18, 2012.
