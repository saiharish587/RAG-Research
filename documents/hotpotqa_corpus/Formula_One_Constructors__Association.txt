Formula One Constructors' Association: The Formula One Constructors' Association (FOCA) was an organization of the chassis builders (constructors) who design and build the cars that race in the FIA Formula One World Championship.  It evolved from the earlier "Formula 1 Constructors Association" (F1CA; the name was changed due to unfortunate connotations in some languages) and came to be dominated by Bernie Ecclestone and Max Mosley (originally a co-founder of March Engineering).  Frank Williams, Colin Chapman, Teddy Mayer, Ken Tyrrell were also significant members.  FOCA served to represent the interests of their privately owned teams – usually against the race organisers and later against the manufacturer-owned or supported teams such as Ferrari, Matra and Alfa Romeo.  Ecclestone became the organisation's chief executive in 1978, with Mosley taking on the role of legal advisor.
