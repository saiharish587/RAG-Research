Fort Minor Militia EP: Militia, also known as the Fort Minor Militia EP, is the debut extended play, which was released on November 22, 2006 via Machine Shop by Linkin Park co-vocalist Mike Shinoda for his well-known hip-hop-based side-project Fort Minor.  The EP was the first official debut EP by Fort Minor, and it is produced by Machine Shop.  Production was handled by Shinoda, and executive production was handled by Jay-Z, credited as Shawn Carter.
