Good Ol' Fashioned Love: Good Ol' Fashioned Love is the debut studio album by British-Irish doo-wop boy band The Overtones.  The album was released on November 1, 2010 in the UK by Rhino Entertainment.  The album was subsequently re-released on March 8, 2011 containing a number of new tracks.  The album peaked at number 4 on the UK Albums Chart and number 14 on the Irish Albums Chart.  The album was released in Germany and Austria on 23 March 2012, renamed Gambling Man.
