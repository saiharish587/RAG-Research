Monmouth Heights at Marlboro, New Jersey: Monmouth Heights at Marlboro is an unincorporated community located within Marlboro Township in Monmouth County, New Jersey, United States.  The housing development was created in the 1960s by Levitt & Sons though some protests occurred by long-time residents of Marlboro Township at the time of its creation.  The community is located along New Jersey Route 79 across the highway from Marlboro High School.
