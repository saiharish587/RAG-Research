Suzy Whaley: Suzy Whaley (born c. 1967) is a professional golfer, from Connecticut, who, in 2003, became the first woman in 58 years to qualify for a PGA Tour event when she qualified for the 2003 Greater Hartford Open, after winning the 2002 Connecticut PGA Championship.  She was also the first woman to win a PGA individual professional tournament.  She is currently recognized by Golf for Women as a top 50 female instructor and is a Board Member and Advisor for numerous organizations including Golfer Girl Magazine.  She is an LPGA Teaching and Club Professional (T&CP) member who played on the LPGA Tour in 1993.
