Carey Wilber: Carey Wilber (June 26, 1916 – May 2, 1998) was a Buffalo, New York born journalist and television writer who began his career in the live days of television, and wrote for a variety of programs over the next three decades, including "Captain Video and His Video Rangers", "The Asphalt Jungle", "Lost In Space", "The Time Tunnel", "Bonanza", and "Maverick".  He wrote the "Ice Princess" storyline for the daytime serial "General Hospital" in 1981.  He died in Seattle, Washington.
