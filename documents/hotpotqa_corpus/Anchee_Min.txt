Anchee Min: Anchee Min or Min Anqi (; born January 14, 1957 in Shanghai, China) is a Chinese-American author who lives in San Francisco and Shanghai.  Min has published two memoirs, "Red Azalea" and "", and six historical novels.  Her fiction emphasizes strong female characters, such as Jiang Qing, the wife of chairman Mao Zedong, and Empress Dowager Cixi, the last ruling empress of China.
