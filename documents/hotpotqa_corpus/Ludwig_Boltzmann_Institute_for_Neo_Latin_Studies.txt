Ludwig Boltzmann Institute for Neo-Latin Studies: The Ludwig Boltzmann Institute for Neo-Latin Studies (LBI) (German: "Ludwig Boltzmann Institut für Neulateinische Studien" ) in Innsbruck is a research institute of the Austrian Ludwig Boltzmann Gesellschaft.  Partner organizations of the LBI are the University of Innsbruck, the University of Freiburg, the Austrian National Library in Vienna, and the Pontificio Comitato di Scienze Storiche in Rome.
