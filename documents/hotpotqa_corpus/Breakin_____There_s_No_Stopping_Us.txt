Breakin'... There's No Stopping Us: "Breakin'... There's No Stopping Us" is a song by American music duo Ollie & Jerry.  Released in 1984 as a single from the soundtrack to the film "Breakin'", the song reached No. 9 on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot 100 and No. 5 on the UK Singles Chart.  It is the theme song to the film "Breakin<nowiki>'</nowiki>".  The drum was played with a Roland TR-808 and Linn LM-1.
