Songs and Dances of Death: Songs and Dances of Death (Russian: Песни и пляски смерти , "Pesni i plyaski smerti") is a song cycle for voice (usually bass or bass-baritone) and piano by Modest Petrovich Mussorgsky, written in the mid-1870s, to poems by Arseny Golenishchev-Kutuzov, a relative of the composer.
