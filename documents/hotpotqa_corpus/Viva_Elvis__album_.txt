Viva Elvis (album): Viva Elvis is the soundtrack remix album of the Cirque du Soleil show "Viva Elvis", which focuses on the life and music of American singer and musician Elvis Presley.  The album, though initially produced as a soundtrack to the show, does not include all of the songs featured in the show.  The CD tracks are rearranged and extended versions of songs heard in the show, and in fact the album includes two instrumental versions of the songs "Memories" and "You'll Never Walk Alone", neither of which is in the Cirque du Soleil show.
