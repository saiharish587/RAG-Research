Edmund Mortimer, 2nd Baron Mortimer: Edmund Mortimer, 2nd Lord Mortimer (1251 – 17 July 1304) was the second son and eventual heir of Roger Mortimer, 1st Baron Mortimer.  His mother was Maud de Braose.  As a younger son, Edmund had been intended for clerical or monastic life, and had been sent to study at Oxford University.
