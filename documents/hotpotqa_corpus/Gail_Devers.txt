Gail Devers: Yolanda Gail Devers ( ; born November 19, 1966) is an American retired track and field athlete.  A two-time Olympic champion in the 100 meters for the USA, her 1996 win made her only the second woman (after Wyomia Tyus) to successfully defend an Olympic 100m title.  She won a third Olympic gold medal in the 4 x 100m relay in 1996.  She is also the 1993 World champion in the 100m and a three-time World champion in the 100m hurdles.  In 2011, she was inducted into the National Track and Field Hall of Fame.
