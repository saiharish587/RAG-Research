Stade de France: The Stade de France (] ) is the national stadium of France, located just north of Paris in the commune of Saint-Denis.  Its seating capacity of 81,338 makes it the sixth-largest stadium in Europe.  The stadium is used by the France national football team and French rugby union team for international competition.  Originally built for the 1998 FIFA World Cup, the stadium's name was recommended by Michel Platini, head of the organising committee.  On 12 July 1998, France defeated Brazil 3–0 in the 1998 FIFA World Cup Final contested at the stadium.  It will host the opening and closing ceremonies and the athletics events at the 2024 Summer Olympics.
