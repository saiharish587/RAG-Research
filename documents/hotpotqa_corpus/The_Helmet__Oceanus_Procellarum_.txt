The Helmet (Oceanus Procellarum): The Helmet is an informal term for a highland mass within southern Oceanus Procellarum on the near side of the Moon.  The astronauts of Apollo 16 used the term to refer to the feature during the mission.  The name derives from the roughly semicircular shape of the border of the feature, resembling a helmet.  The feature is topographically higher and of a higher albedo than the surrounding mare lava plain.
