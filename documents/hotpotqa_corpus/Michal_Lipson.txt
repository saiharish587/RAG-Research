Michal Lipson: Michal Lipson (born 1970) is an American physicist known for her work on silicon photonics.  Lipson was named a 2010 MacArthur Fellow for contributions to silicon photonics especially towards enabling GHz silicon active devices .  Until 2014, she was the Given Foundation Professor of Engineering at Cornell University in the school of electrical and computer engineering and a member of the Kavli Institute for Nanoscience at Cornell.  She is now a Professor of Electrical Engineering at Columbia University.  According to Google Scholar, her publications have been cited over 22,500 times, and she has an h-index of 80 as of August 20, 2016.  In 2009 she co-founded the company PicoLuz, which develops and commercializes silicon nanophotonics technologies.
