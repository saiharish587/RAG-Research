Rocky Mount station: Helen P. Gay Rocky Mount Historic Train Station, often shortened to Rocky Mount, is an Amtrak train station in Rocky Mount, North Carolina.  It is served by four passenger trains, the "Carolinian", "Silver Meteor", "Silver Star", and "Palmetto."  The station is located just south of downtown Rocky Mount, and the street address is 101 Hammond Street.  In 2010, it was named for Helen P. Gay, a 90-year-old former member of the Rocky Mount City Council who was instrumental in the station's most recent restoration project.
