No Surprises: "No Surprises" is a song by the English alternative rock band Radiohead, released as the third and final single from their third studio album "OK Computer" (1997) on 12 January 1998.  The song was accompanied by a music video featuring a single shot of lead singer Thom Yorke singing karaoke lyrics of the song displayed inside a helmet as it fills up with water.  The single peaked at number 4 on the UK Singles Chart.
