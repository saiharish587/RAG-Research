Godrej BKC: Godrej BKC (Bandra-Kurla Complex) is a project by Godrej Properties Limited developed in partnership with Jet Airways located in Mumbai, India.  Of the 1.3mn sq. ft., 250,000 sq. ft. of the building would be used as the corporate headquarters of Jet Airways (India) Limited.  The Architectural Partner would be SOM (Skidmore, Owings and Merrill) and the construction would be undertaken by L&T.  Profits are to be shared in the ratio of 50:50 between Godrej Properties and Jet Airways.  Godrej BKC is LEED Platinum pre-certified.
