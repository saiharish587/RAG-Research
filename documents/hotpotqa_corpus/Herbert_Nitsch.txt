Herbert Nitsch: Herbert Nitsch (born 20 April 1970) is an Austrian freediver who has held world records in all of the eight freediving disciplines recognised by AIDA International.  He is the current assisted freediving world record champion and “the deepest man on earth”.  William Trubridge (NZ) is the current world unassisted free diving world record holder, diving to a depth of 90m (328 feet), without assistance.  This title was given to him when he set a world record in the "No Limits" discipline at the depth of 214 m .  To date, he has achieved 69 official World Records (see the table below), and one world record in the traditional Greek discipline of Skandalopetra 107 m .  He surpassed his own no limits depth with a world record dive in June 2012 to 253.2 m .
