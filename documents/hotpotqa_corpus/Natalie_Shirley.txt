Natalie Shirley: Natalie Shirley (born 1957) is an American lawyer businesswomen and university president from Oklahoma.  Shirley previously served as the Oklahoma Secretary of Commerce and Tourism under Governor of Oklahoma Brad Henry, having been in that position from 2007 to 2011.  She was Governor Henry's second Secretary of Commerce and Tourism, having succeeded Kathy Taylor.  Concurrent with her service as Commerce Secretary, Shirley was appointed by Governor Henry to serve as the Director of the Oklahoma Department of Commerce from 2007 to 2011.  In March 2012 she became the fourth president at Oklahoma State University-Oklahoma City.
