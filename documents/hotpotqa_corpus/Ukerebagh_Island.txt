Ukerebagh Island: The Ukerabagh Island is a river island, contained within the Ukerabagh Island Nature Reserve, a protected nature reserve, that is located in the mouth of the Tweed River, in the Northern Rivers region of New South Wales in eastern Australia.  The 125 ha reserve is situated near Tweed Heads and 1 km south of a section that defines the border between the states of New South Wales and Queensland.  The island and reserve is an important site for coastal birds.
