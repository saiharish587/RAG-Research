September (2007 film): September is a 2007 Australian drama film, directed by Peter Carstairs and produced by John Polson.  Set in Western Australia's wheatbelt in 1968 (though filmed at Harden, New South Wales), it stars Xavier Samuel and Clarence John Ryan as two teenagers whose interracial friendship struggles to withstand the expectations of their community.  The film sensitively documents the disparity and discrimination faced by the country's Aboriginal people.
