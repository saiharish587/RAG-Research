Tetradium: Tetradium is a genus of nine species of trees in the family Rutaceae, occurring in temperate to tropical east Asia.  In older books, the genus was often included in the related genus "Euodia" (sometimes written, ""Evodia"" from latin spelling), but that genus is now restricted to tropical species.  In cultivation in English-speaking countries, they are known as Euodia, Evodia, or Bee bee tree.
