Family policy in the Netherlands: Marriage in the Netherlands has very few rules that have changed throughout history.  The first rule was set in the 1811 Civil Code which stated that the groom must be at least 18 years of age and the bride must be at least 15 years of age.  This was changed in 1838 when brides were made to be at least 16 years of age and grooms still 18.  Recent additions to the Civil Code have changed the brides' age to 18, along with the grooms' age of 18.
