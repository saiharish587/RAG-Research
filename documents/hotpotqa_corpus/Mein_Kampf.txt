Mein Kampf: Mein Kampf (] , "My Struggle") is a 1925 autobiographical book by Nazi Party leader Adolf Hitler.  The work describes the process by which Hitler became antisemitic and outlines his political ideology and future plans for Germany.  Volume 1 of "Mein Kampf" was published in 1925 and Volume 2 in 1926.  The book was edited by Hitler's deputy Rudolf Hess.
