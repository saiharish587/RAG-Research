2007 BA-CA-TennisTrophy: The 2007 BA-CA-TennisTrophy was a tennis tournament played on indoor hard courts.  It was the 33rd edition of the event known that year as the BA-CA-TennisTrophy, and was part of the International Series Gold of the 2007 ATP Tour.  It took place at the Wiener Stadthalle in Vienna, Austria, from October 7 through October 14, 2007.
