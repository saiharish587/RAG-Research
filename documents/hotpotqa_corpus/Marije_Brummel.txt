Marije Brummel: Marije Brummel is a Dutch football defensive midfielder currently playing for Sandviken in the Toppserien.  She previously played for Bristol Academy in the FA WSL; SC Klarenbeek, SV Saestum and Be Quick '21 in the old Hoofdklasse; for FC twente and Heerenveen, in the women Eredivisie and BeNe League.  She also played the European Cup with Saestum.
