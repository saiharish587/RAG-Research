Foumban Royal Palace: The royal palace of Foumban, where the king of the Bamum still resides today, was built in 1917.  The Palace Museum tells the history of the dynasty of the Bamum kings from 1394 to the present day, with information on the most famous of the Bamum kings, Ibrahim Njoya, who died in 1933 and who created a writing system at the end of the 19th century called Bamum script.
