MTV Video Music Award for Best Direction: The MTV Video Music Award for Best Direction in a Video is a craft award given to the artist, the artist's manager, and the director of the music video.  In 2007, the award was briefly renamed Best Director, but it returned to its original name for the 2008 awards.  The biggest winners are Spike Jonze and David Fincher with three wins each, although one of Jonze's wins is credited as the "Torrance Community Dance Group".
