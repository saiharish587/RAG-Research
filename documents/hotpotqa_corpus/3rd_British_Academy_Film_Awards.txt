3rd British Academy Film Awards: The 3rd British Film Awards, known retroactively as the British Academy Film Awards, were given by the British Academy of Film and Television Arts (BAFTA) (known then as the British Film Academy) on 29 May 1950, and honoured the best films of 1948 and 1949.  The awards for Best British Film and Best Film from any Source was handed out to "The Third Man" and "Bicycle Thieves", respectively, and "The Third Man" was the most nominated feature film, with two.
