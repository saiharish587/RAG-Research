With Vision: With Vision is the second full-length album release by Place of Skulls.  The album was released in 2003 on the Southern Lord Records label.  The recording of this album was marked by numerous personnel changes before the final staff was assembled.  This is the only Place of Skulls album to feature Scott Weinrich (of The Obsessed, Saint Vitus, Spirit Caravan fame) on vocals and second guitar, as he left soon after to concentrate on The Hidden Hand.  Writing credits are far more distributed among the band members than its predecessor, wherein all songs were written solely by Griffin.
