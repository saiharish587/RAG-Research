Punks Not Dead: Punks Not Dead is the debut studio album by the Scottish punk rock band The Exploited, released in April 1981 on Secret Records.  Strongly working class and loyal to the first impulses of the 1970s punk movement, the album was a reaction to critics who believed the punk rock genre was dead, and went against popular trends such as new wave and post-punk.  The album – and especially the title track – is a reaction to the punk rock band Crass' song "Punk is Dead".  It contains the double A side singles "Army Life/Fuck the Mods" and the later follow up "I Believe in Anarchy".  "Army Life" details the experiences of Wattie Buchan when he was a 17-year-old squaddie on a tour of duty in Belfast in the 1970s.
