Ali Ahamada: Ali Ahamada (born 19 August 1991 in Martigues) is a Comorian footballer who plays as a goalkeeper for Turkish club Kayserispor in Süper Lig.  He made his professional debut on 20 February 2011 appearing against Rennes appearing as a substitute for the injured Marc Vidal.  In his eight starts in the 2010–11 season, Ahamada recorded six clean sheets, notably against Lens, Lyon, Bordeaux, and Lorient.  In the 2011–12 season, Ahamada opened the campaign as the team's first-choice goalkeeper.  He recorded ten clean sheets in the team's first 19 matches of the season.
