Ingles Bottom Archeological Sites: Ingles Bottom Archeological Sites is a set of archaeological sites, and national historic district located along the New River near Radford, Montgomery County, Virginia.  The district encompasses a variety of archaeological sites relating to human occupation from 8000 B.C. to the present.  It includes the site of a log cabin built about 1762, as the home of William and Mary Draper Ingles (1732-1815).  The property also includes the site of a stable, family cemetery, and Ingles Ferry.
