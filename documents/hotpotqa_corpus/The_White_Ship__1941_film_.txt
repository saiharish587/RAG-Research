The White Ship (1941 film): The White Ship (Italian:La nave bianca) is a 1941 Italian war film directed by Roberto Rossellini.  Its cast was made up entirely of amateur actors, many of them the real crew of a hospital ship of the Italian navy.  The production was a work of propaganda intended to support the war aims of the Fascist Italian regime during the Second World War.  It was made with the close co-operation of the Italian Navy, particularly Francesco De Robertis.  Vittorio Mussolini, the son of the Italian dictator, was also a supporter of the project.
