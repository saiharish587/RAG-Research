Aeroflot: PJSC AeroflotRussian Airlines (Russian: ПАО "Аэрофло́т-Росси́йские авиали́нии" , "ПAO Aeroflot-Rossiyskiye avialinii" ) (), commonly known as Aeroflot ( or ) (Russian: Аэрофлот , English translation: "air fleet", ] ), is the flag carrier and largest airline of the Russian Federation.  The carrier is an open joint stock company that operates domestic and international passenger and services, mainly from its hub at Sheremetyevo International Airport.
