Te3n: Teen (stylised as Te3n; तीन is Hindi for "Three") is an Indian Hindi-language thriller film written and directed by Ribhu Dasgupta.  This remake of the 2013 South Korean film "Montage" stars Amitabh Bachchan, Nawazuddin Siddiqui, Sabyasachi Chakraborty, Padmavathi Rao, and Vidya Balan in the lead roles.  It was released on 10 June 2016.  Though praised for acting and storyline by critics and audience, the film was a commercial failure at the box office.
