New Jack City II: New Jack City, Pt.  II is the sixth studio album by American rapper Bow Wow.  It was released on March 31, 2009, by LBW Entertainment and Columbia Records.  This is Bow Wow's first album to be released on his new label LBW Entertainment, and his first album to be receiving a parental advisory label for "Adult Language".  The album features guest appearances from Swizz Beatz, Jermaine Dupri, Nelly, Trey Songz, T-Pain, Ron Browz, Dondria and T.I..
