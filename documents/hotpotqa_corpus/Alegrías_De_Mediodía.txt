Alegrías De Mediodía: Alegrías De Mediodía (English Midday Happiness) was a TV show broadcast by Canal de las Estrellas.  It is a TV show aimed to young audience, set in Mexico.  This show was broadcast in 1980.  This was the first time ever Lucero starred in front of the TV cameras.  She went to the casting for this show and she immediately started working on it.  It started on 17 February 1980, and was broadcast from Monday to Friday from 3:30 to 4 in the afternoon through the TV channel Canal De Las Estrellas ("The Star's Channel").
