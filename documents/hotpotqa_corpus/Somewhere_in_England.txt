Somewhere in England: Somewhere in England is the ninth studio album by George Harrison, released in 1981.  The album was recorded as Harrison was becoming increasingly frustrated with the music industry.  The album's making was a long one, during which conflicts with Warner Bros.  Records arose.  "Somewhere in England" was the first Harrison album to be released after the death of his former Beatles bandmate John Lennon; the lyrics of its first single, "All Those Years Ago", pay tribute to Lennon.
