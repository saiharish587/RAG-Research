Miley Cyrus &amp; Her Dead Petz: Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz is the fifth studio album by American singer Miley Cyrus, which was independently released for free online streaming on SoundCloud on August 30, 2015.  Opting for an album relying less on computerized elements than her previous release, "Bangerz" (2013), Cyrus began planning the project in 2013 before "Bangerz" was released.  Work continued into 2014 and 2015, when she befriended and began collaborating with the Flaming Lips.  In addition to the psychedelic rock band, Cyrus worked with producers Mike Will Made It and Oren Yoel (with whom she had collaborated on "Bangerz").  The album features guest vocals by Big Sean (who had appeared on "Bangerz"), Sarah Barthel of Phantogram and Ariel Pink.
