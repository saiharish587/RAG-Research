Cinestill: Cinestill Film creates photographic film for still cameras from Kodak's motion picture cinema film.  Cinestill Film uses a modified version of Kodak motion picture cinema film, allowing it to be developed with the C-41 process rather than the Eastman Color Negative process.  Beta testing of Cinestill Film's "800Tungsten Xpro C-41" began in late 2012, the film is based on Kodak's "Vision 3 5219" motion picture cinema film.  Although the "Vision 3 5219" film is normally rated for 500ISO, the conversion to C-41 processing allows the film to be rated at 800ISO.
