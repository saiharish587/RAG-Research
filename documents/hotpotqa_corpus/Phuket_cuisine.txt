Phuket cuisine: In the past, Phuket was one of Thailand’s commercial ports which traded with other countries.  These cultural influences are reflected in the cuisine and local foods.  Phuket cuisine and local food (Baba Peranakan food) is the combination of many cultural food habits, whether they be Chinese, Malay or Thai.  Some Phuket local food tastes sweet, such as Chinese Hakka cuisine, but it can also be spicy, such as in Thai cuisine and Malay cuisine.
