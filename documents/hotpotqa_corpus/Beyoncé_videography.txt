Beyoncé videography: American singer, songwriter and actress Beyoncé has released various music videos.  After her film debut as the lead in the direct-to-video musical "" (2001), Beyoncé's first solo music video was the soundtrack single "Work It Out" for the 2002 film "Austin Powers in Goldmember", her first theatrical film.  After Destiny's Child—a girl group which Beyoncé was a member of—went on hiatus, she released in 2003 her first music video as a solo artist for "Crazy in Love" (featuring Jay-Z) from "Dangerously in Love".  It won three awards at the 2003 MTV Video Music Awards, including Best Female Video.  Other videos from the album included for the singles "Baby Boy", "Me, Myself and I" and "Naughty Girl".
