Ralph Rodríguez: Ralph Rodríguez (born 29 July 1941) is a Puerto Rican former sport shooter who competed in the 1968 Summer Olympics, in the 1976 Summer Olympics, in the 1992 Summer Olympics, in the 1996 Summer Olympics, in the 2000 Summer Olympics.  He was one of the torch lighters of the 2010 Central American and Caribbean Games.
