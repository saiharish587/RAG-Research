Ballaios: Ballaios (Ancient Greek: Βαλλαῖος ; ruled c.167–c.135 BC) or (c.195–c.175 BC) was an Illyrian king of the Ardiaei.  Ballaios was not mentioned by any ancient writers.  Ballaios was a powerful and influential king testified by the abundance of his silver and bronze coinage found along both coasts of the Adriatic.
