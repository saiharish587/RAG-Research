Russian International School in Dubai: The Russian International School in Dubai (Russian: Русская Международная Школа в Дубае ; Arabic: المدرسة الحولیة الروسیة‎ ‎ ) is a Russian private school located in Muhaisnah 4, Dubai, United Arab Emirates.  It follows the Russian Curriculum, the only school in Dubai to do so.  Originally established in 1996 as the Dubai Russian School; it was a villa-based school with 60 students, founded by Najibullah Najib.
