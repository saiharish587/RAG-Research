Starrcade (1983): Starrcade (1983) was the first annual Starrcade professional wrestling event.  It was produced under the National Wrestling Alliance (NWA) banner by Jim Crockett Promotions (JCP).  The event took place on November 24, 1983 at the Greensboro Coliseum in Greensboro, North Carolina and was broadcast on closed-circuit television around the Southern United States.  Eight professional wrestling matches were featured.
