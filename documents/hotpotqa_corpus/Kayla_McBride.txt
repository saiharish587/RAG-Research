Kayla McBride: Kayla McBride (born June 25, 1992) is an American professional basketball player for the San Antonio Stars of the Women's National Basketball Association (WNBA).  She was drafted 3rd overall by the San Antonio Silver Stars in the 2014 WNBA draft.  McBride played shooting guard for Notre Dame, where she led Notre Dame to four consecutive Final Fours and three NCAA championship appearances.
