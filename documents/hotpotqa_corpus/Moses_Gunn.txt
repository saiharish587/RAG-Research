Moses Gunn: Moses Gunn (October 2, 1929 – December 16, 1993) was an American actor of stage and screen.  An Obie Award-winning stage player, he co-founded the Negro Ensemble Company in the 1960s.  His 1962 Off-Broadway debut was in Jean Genet's "The Blacks," and his Broadway debut was in "A Hand is on the Gate," an evening of African-American poetry.  He was nominated for a 1976 Tony Award as Best Actor (Play) for "The Poison Tree" and played Othello on Broadway in 1970.
