Behind Enemy Lines (2001 film): Behind Enemy Lines is a 2001 American war film directed by John Moore in his directorial debut, and starring Owen Wilson and Gene Hackman.  The film tells the story of Lieutenant Chris Burnett, an American naval flight officer who is shot down over Bosnia and uncovers genocide during the Bosnian War.  Meanwhile, his commanding officer is struggling to gain approval to launch a combat search and rescue mission to save Burnett.  The plot is loosely based on the 1995 Mrkonjić Grad incident that occurred during the war.
