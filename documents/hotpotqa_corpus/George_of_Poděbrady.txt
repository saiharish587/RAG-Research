George of Poděbrady: George of Kunštát and Poděbrady (23 April 1420 – 22 March 1471), also known as Poděbrad or Podiebrad (Czech: "Jiří z Poděbrad" ; German: "Georg von Podiebrad" ), was King of Bohemia (1458–1471).  He was leader of the Hussites.  He is known for his idea and attempt to establish common European institutions.  It is seen as the first historical vision of European unity.
