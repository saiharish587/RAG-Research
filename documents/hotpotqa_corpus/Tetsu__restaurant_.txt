Tetsu (restaurant): Tetsu is a modern Japanese restaurant located in the Tribeca neighborhood of New York City.  Conceived by Michelin-starred chef Masa Takayama, Tetsu serves a Yakitori-based menu with an emphasis upon grilled fare.  Similar to Takayama’s other restaurant ventures, Tetsu features both a la carte and Omakase menus.  The restaurant will feature two floors, with a casual ground floor space featuring cocktails and small plates, and a cellar space which will feature a twenty-course tasting menu.
