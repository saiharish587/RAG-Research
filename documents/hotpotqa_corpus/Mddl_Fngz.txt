Mddl Fngz: Mddl Fngz, also known as Middle Fingaz, is a South-West Houston-based rap group formed before 2000 as side project by southern rapper Bun B when partner rapper Pimp C was incarcerated.  The group has appeared on several mixtapes as well as UGK's album UGK (Underground Kingz), and Bun B's second album II Trill.  Members Band.I.T, Bad Azz Bam, and Young Kilo are signed to UGK Records.
