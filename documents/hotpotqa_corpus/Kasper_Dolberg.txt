Kasper Dolberg: Kasper Dolberg (born 6 October 1997) is a Danish professional footballer who plays as a forward for Ajax and the Denmark national team.  Dolberg made his senior debut at Silkeborg IF in May 2015.  He joined Ajax in July 2015 and made his debut for the club in July 2016.  He represented Denmark at under-16, under-17, under-19 and under-21 level before making his senior international debut in November 2016.
