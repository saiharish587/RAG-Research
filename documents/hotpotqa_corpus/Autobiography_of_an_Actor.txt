Autobiography of an Actor: Autobiography of an Actor: Sivaji Ganesan, October 1928-July 2001 is the autobiography of Indian actor Sivaji Ganesan published by Sivaji Prabhu Charities Trust.  It is a compilation of interviews between Ganesan and journalist T. S. Narayanaswamy.  The book was originally published in Tamil under the title Enathu Suya Sarithai (English: "My Autobiography" ) in 2002, and the English translated version by Sabita Radhakrishna was released in 2007.
