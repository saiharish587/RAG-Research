Parks in Lyon: The city of Lyon and its urban area have several parks.  The main parks in the inner city include Jardin des Chartreux in the 1st arrondissement, Parc Bazin, Parc Chambovet and Parc Sisley in the 3rd arrondissement, Parc de la Cerisaie, Parc Francis Popy and Jardin Rosa Mir in the 4th arrondissement, Jardin des Curiosités and Parc des Hauteurs in the 5th arrondissement, Parc de la Tête d'Or in the 6th arrondissement, Parc de Gerland, Parc des berges du Rhône and Parc Sergent Blandan in the 7th arrondissement, and Parc de Montpellas and Parc du Vallon in the 9th arrondissement.
