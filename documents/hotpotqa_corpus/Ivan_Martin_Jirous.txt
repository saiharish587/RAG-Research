Ivan Martin Jirous: Ivan Martin Jirous (23 September 1944 – 10 November 2011) was a Czech poet, best known for being the artistic director of the Czech psychedelic rock group The Plastic People of the Universe and later one of the organizers of the Czech underground during the communist regime.  He is also known more frequently as Magor, which can be roughly translated as "loony" or "fool" and is supposedly derived from "phantasmagoria".  This nickname was given to him by the "experimental" poet Eugen Brikcius.  His wife, Věra Jirousová, wrote a good deal of the Plastics' early lyrics.
