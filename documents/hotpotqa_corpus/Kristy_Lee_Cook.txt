Kristy Lee Cook: Kristy Lee Cook (born January 18, 1984) is an American country singer who was the seventh place finalist on the seventh season of "American Idol".  In 2005, Cook released her first album called "Devoted".  In June 2008, Cook signed to 19 Recordings and Arista Nashville.  She released her post-"Idol" album, "Why Wait", on September 16, 2008.  This album has produced her first chart single, "15 Minutes of Shame", a Top 30 hit on the "Billboard" country charts.  Her first single for Broken Bow Records, "Airborne Ranger Infantry", was released on October 16, 2012.
