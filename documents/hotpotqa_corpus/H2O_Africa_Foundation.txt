H2O Africa Foundation: The H2O Africa Foundation was an NGO founded by Matt Damon to raise awareness about clean water initiatives in Africa.  It was part of the Running the Sahara expedition and documentary project undertaken by Damon, James Moll, LivePlanet, and the Independent Producers Alliance.  In 2009, the H2O Africa Foundation merged with WaterPartners to form Water.org, an organization co-founded by Matt Damon and Gary White of WaterPartners in July 2009.
