Kinston Free Press: The Free Press is an award-winning daily newspaper based in Kinston, North Carolina.  It has served the city of Kinston and Lenoir County, North Carolina since 1882.  "The Free Press" was owned by Freedom Communications until 2012, when Freedom sold its Florida and North Carolina papers to Halifax Media Group.  In November 2014, Halifax announced the sale of The Free Press and its other properties to New Media Investment Group Inc.  In 2015, Halifax was acquired by New Media Investment Group.
