Eddie Collins: Edward Trowbridge Collins Sr. (May 2, 1887 – March 25, 1951), nicknamed "Cocky", was an American professional baseball player, manager and executive.  He played as a second baseman in Major League Baseball from 1906 to 1930 for the Philadelphia Athletics and Chicago White Sox.  A graduate of Columbia University, Collins holds major league career records in several categories and is among the top few players in several other categories.  In 1925, Collins became just the sixth person to join the 3,000 hit club – and the last for the next 17 seasons.
