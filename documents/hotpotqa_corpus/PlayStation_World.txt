PlayStation World: PlayStation World (PSW) was a monthly video games magazine that covered the PlayStation brand.  The pages were featured news, previews, reviews and letter pages.  It was launched in 2000, published by Computec Media.  The magazine had a circulation of 17,132 along with a readership of 210,000.  The UK portion of Computec Media was acquired by Future Publishing in 2003, with the title added to Future's portfolio.  At the time of the acquisition, the magazine had an average monthly circulation of 53,349.  By 2007, the magazine's circulation had decreased to 17,132.  On 13 August 2009, Future Publishing announced it was to close the PSW magazine, citing "decreasing demand" as the reason for the publication's closure.
