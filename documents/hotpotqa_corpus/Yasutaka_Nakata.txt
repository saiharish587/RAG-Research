Yasutaka Nakata: Yasutaka Nakata (中田 ヤスタカ , Nakata Yasutaka , born February 6, 1980 in Kanazawa, Ishikawa) is a Japanese DJ, record producer, composer and songwriter.  He formed the band Capsule in 1997 with vocalist Toshiko Koshijima and himself as composer and record producer when both were 17.  They formally debuted in 2001 with the song "Sakura."
