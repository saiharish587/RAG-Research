Liquid Snake: Liquid Snake (Japanese: リキッド・スネーク , Hepburn: Rikiddo Sunēku ) is a fictional character from the "Metal Gear" franchise.  He is the twin brother of series' protagonist Solid Snake and Solidus Snake as well as the second product of the "Les Enfants Terribles", a top-secret government project to artificially create soldiers through the DNA of Big Boss.  He first appears as the antagonist in the original "Metal Gear Solid", where he leads the now rogue FOXHOUND unit in a hostile takeover of a nuclear disposal facility in Alaska.  The character returns in the prequel "" as a child mercenary nicknamed the White Mamba (ホワイトマンバ , Howaito Manba ) with his real name revealed to be Eli (イーライ , Īrai ) .
