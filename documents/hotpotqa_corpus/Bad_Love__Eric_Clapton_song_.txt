Bad Love (Eric Clapton song): "Bad Love" is a rock song written and recorded by the British rock musician Eric Clapton, who co-wrote the title with Foreigner's lead guitarist Mick Jones.  The track was released as the third single from Eric Clapton's 1989 studio album "Journeyman" on March 10, 1990 for Reprise Records a sublabel of the Warner Music Group company.
