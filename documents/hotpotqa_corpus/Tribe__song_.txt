Tribe (song): "Tribe" is the fourth single by metal band Soulfly, released in January 1999.  It is played as the ninth track of the eponymous debut album "Soulfly", after "Bleed" and before "Bumba".  "Tribe" is the fourth and last single of the album.  As with every other Soulfly songs with vocals, the lyrics were written by Max Cavalera.
