Camouflage (2007 game show): Camouflage is a television game show airing on GSN.  Hosted by Roger Lodge, and billed as "the hidden word game where the answer is always right in front of you", "Camouflage" originally aired for 40 episodes from July 2 to 27, 2007.  The show is a word game, with contestants searching for a hidden word or phrase in a string of jumbled letters.  The show is produced by Enjoy the Ride Productions in association with McB Entertainment.
