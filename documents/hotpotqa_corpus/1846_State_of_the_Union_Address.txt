1846 State of the Union Address: The 1846 State of the Union Address was presented to the 29th United States Congress, containing both the United States Senate and United States House of Representatives on Tuesday, December 8, 1846.  It was the 56th address given.  President James K. Polk, the 11th president, had written it.  It was written during the Mexican-American War, and speaks a lot of it.  "The existing war with Mexico was neither desired nor provoked by the United States."
