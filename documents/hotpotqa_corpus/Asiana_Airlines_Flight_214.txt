Asiana Airlines Flight 214: Asiana Airlines Flight 214 was a scheduled transpacific passenger flight from Incheon International Airport near Seoul, South Korea, to San Francisco International Airport (SFO) in the United States.  On the morning of Saturday, July 6, 2013, the Boeing 777-200ER crashed on final approach into SFO.  Of the 307 people aboard, two passengers died at the crash scene, and a third died in a hospital several days later; all three of them were teenage Chinese girls.  Another 187 individuals were injured, 49 of them seriously.  Among the injured were four flight attendants who were thrown onto the runway while still strapped in their seats when the tail section broke off after striking the seawall short of the runway.  It was the first crash of a Boeing 777 that resulted in fatalities since that aircraft model entered into service in 1995.
