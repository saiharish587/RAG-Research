Muslim Magomayev (musician): Muslim Magometovich Magomayev (Azerbaijani: "Müslüm Məhəmməd oğlu Maqomayev", 17 August 1942 – 25 October 2008), dubbed the "King of Songs" and the "Soviet Sinatra" was a Soviet Azerbaijani baritone operatic pop singer.  He achieved iconic status in Russia and the post-Soviet countries for his vocal talent and charisma.
