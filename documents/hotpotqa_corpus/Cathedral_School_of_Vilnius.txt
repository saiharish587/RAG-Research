Cathedral School of Vilnius: The Cathedral School of Vilnius was a cathedral school attached to the Vilnius Cathedral.  It is believed to be the earliest school in the Grand Duchy of Lithuania.  For about a hundred years it was the only Catholic school in Vilnius (possibly due to a royal privilege prohibiting establishment of other schools).  The cathedral school was merged with Vilnius Academy (now Vilnius University), established by the Jesuits in 1570.
