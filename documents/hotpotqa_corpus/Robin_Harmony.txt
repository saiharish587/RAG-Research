Robin Harmony: Robin Harmony (born October 20, 1961) is an American college basketball coach.  She currently serves as head coach of Lamar University women's basketball team.  From 2005 to 2013, she served as head coach at St. Thomas University.  Prior to that, she served as assistant coach (six years) and associate head coach (twelve years) at Miami Hurricanes.  She split her stay at the University of Miami by serving as an assistant coach at Fairleigh Dickinson University for one season prior to her return to the Hurricanes.
