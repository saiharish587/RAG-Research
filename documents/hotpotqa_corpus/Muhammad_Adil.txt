Muhammad Adil: Muhammad Adil Iqbal (Urdu: ‎ ; born 9 July 1992) is a Pakistani footballer who plays as a midfielder for Shoro Top League club Dordoi Bishkek and the Pakistan national team.  A right-footed player, he usually plays as central midfielder.  Earlier in his career he primarily played as a wide midfielder.  Adil is known for his dribbling skills, speed, crossing ability and his accurate right foot long-range shots from the centre of midfield.
