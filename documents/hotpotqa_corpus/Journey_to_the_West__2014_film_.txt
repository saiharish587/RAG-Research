Journey to the West (2014 film): Journey to the West () is a 2014 French-Taiwanese film directed by Tsai Ming-liang.  The title is inspired by the 16th century Chinese literary classic of the same name.  It had its world premiere at the "Panorama" section of the 64th Berlin International Film Festival in February 2014.  It is an entry in Tsai's "Walker series" of films.
