Marcus Livius Drusus Claudianus: Marcus Livius Drusus Claudianus ("fl. " 1st century BC) was a senator of the Roman Republic.  He was born with the name "Appius Claudius Pulcher", into the patrician family of the Claudii.  According to Suetonius, Drusus was a direct descendant of the consul and censor Appius Claudius Caecus.  He was descended from Caecus via the first Appius Claudius Pulcher, who was consul in 212 BC and Caecus's great-grandson.  His daughter Livia became the wife of the first Roman Emperor Augustus, and he was thus a direct ancestor of the Julio-Claudian emperors Tiberius, Caligula, Claudius and Nero.
