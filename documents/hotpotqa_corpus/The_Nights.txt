The Nights: "The Nights" is a song by Swedish DJ and music producer Avicii.  It features uncredited vocals by Nicholas Furlong, who co-wrote the song with Jordan Suecof, Gabriel Benjamin, and John Feldmann.  On 1 December 2014, it was released as a digital download by PRMD Music and Universal Island on Avicii's "The Days / Nights", then on 11 January 2015 in the United Kingdom.  The song peaked at number 6 on the UK Singles Chart and number 1 on the UK Dance Chart.  On 23 January 2015, Avicii released "The Nights (Avicii by Avicii)", his own remix of the song.  The song appears on the UK version of Avicii's second studio album "Stories" (2015).
