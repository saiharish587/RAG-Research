Bose L1 Portable Systems: Bose Corporation makes the L1 Portable Systems.  When introduced in 2003 they were called Personalized Amplification Systems for Musicians.  Bose has been actively encouraging the use of the term L1 for this product line as the L1 portable line array.  Colloquially it was known as PAS (Personalized Amplification System) but the acronym is a registered trademark of another company.
