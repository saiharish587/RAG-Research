Barbara New: Barbara New (9 May 1923 – 24 May 2010) was an English character actress, well known for playing Mabel the scullery maid in the David Croft sitcom "You Rang M'Lord? ".  Following this role, she appeared as Vera Plumtree in "Oh, Doctor Beeching! ".  She had previously played smaller parts in Croft's earlier sitcoms "Dad's Army" and "Hi-de-Hi! ".
