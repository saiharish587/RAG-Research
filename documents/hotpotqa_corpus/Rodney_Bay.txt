Rodney Bay: Rodney Bay is a bay on Saint Lucia; it is along the northwestern coast of the island to the north of Choc Bay.  It is named after British naval officer George Brydges Rodney.  Admiral Rodney won many battles against France by using a Fort built at Pigeon Island - which is the outer boundaries of Rodney Bay- to view any movements in France's naval base in Martinique.  On a cloudless day, one can clearly see Martinique.  The Fort remains in pristine condition at Pigeon Island and still has many cannons positioned, as if ready for battle.
