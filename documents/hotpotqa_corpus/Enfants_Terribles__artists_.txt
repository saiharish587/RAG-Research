Enfants Terribles (artists): Enfants Terribles also Nana ET Matvey is an artist duo consisting of Nana Rosenørn Holland Bastrup (short: Nana Bastrup) (born 1987 in Copenhagen, Denmark) and Matvey Slavin (born 1987 in Leningrad - subsequently renamed St.Petersburg -, Russia).  The duo was founded in Hamburg in 2012 and named after their installation "Enfants Terribles" which, in May 2012, was exhibited on the large paved area outside the Hamburger Kunsthalle in Hamburg.  The installation "Enfants Terribles" was a homage to the spider sculpture "Maman" by Louise Bourgeois, and consisted of sixteen baby spiders around Bourgeois' existing sculpture.
