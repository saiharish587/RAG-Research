Struan's: The Struan family and their company Struan's (also called the Noble House) is a fictional family featuring heavily in many of the Asian Saga novels by writer James Clavell.  The family plays an important role in the novels "Tai-Pan", "Noble House", "Gai-Jin", and "Whirlwind".  "Tai-Pan" is the first book about the Struan family, although it is preceded by "Shōgun" in the Asian saga.
