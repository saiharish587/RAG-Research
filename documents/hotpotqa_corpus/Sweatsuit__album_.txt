Sweatsuit (album): Sweatsuit is a compilation album by American rapper Nelly, released on November 22, 2005.  The album consists of tracks from his 2004 simultaneous album releases, "Sweat" and "Suit".  The US edition of the compilation also includes four extra tracks, including "Fly Away" from the soundtrack of the 2005 film "The Longest Yard", the single "Grillz" featuring rappers Paul Wall and Ali & Gipp, The Notorious B.I.G. single "Nasty Girl" and "Tired" featuring Avery Storm.
