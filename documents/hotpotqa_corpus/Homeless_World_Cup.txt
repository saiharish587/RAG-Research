Homeless World Cup: The Homeless World Cup is an annual football tournament organized by the Homeless World Cup organization, a social organization which advocates the end of homelessness through the sport of association football (or soccer).  The organization puts together an annual football tournament where teams of homeless people from each country compete.  The fifteenth edition of the Homeless World Cup will take place in Oslo, Norway in 2017.  The defending champions are Mexico, who won in Glasgow in 2016.
