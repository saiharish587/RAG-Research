Will It Be Love by Morning: "Will It Be Love by Morning" is a song written by Lewis Anderson and Fred Koller, and recorded by American country music artist Michael Martin Murphey.  It was released in January 1984 as the second single from the album "The Heart Never Lies".  The song peaked at number 7 on the U.S. "Billboard" Hot Country Singles and at number 5 on the Canadian "RPM" Country Tracks chart.
