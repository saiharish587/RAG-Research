Genna crime family: The Genna crime family, was a Prohibition era crime family in Chicago.  From 1921 to 1925, the family was headed by the six Genna brothers, known as the "Terrible Gennas".  These brothers were Sicilians from the town of Marsala and operated from Chicago's Little Italy and maintained control over the Unione Siciliana.  They were allies with fellow Italian gang the Chicago Outfit.  After a bloody war led to their demise in the 1920s, the gang was eventually absorbed by the Chicago Outfit.
