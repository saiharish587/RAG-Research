Adelaide City FC: Adelaide City Football Club is an Australian football (soccer) club based in Adelaide, South Australia.  They are also known as "The Zebras" and "the Black and Whites".  The club, which competed in the National Soccer League for 27 seasons and is one of Australia's most decorated sides, has won three national championships and 17 South Australian championships.  Since it withdrew from the NSL just before the league's final season began in 2003, Adelaide City has competed in the National Premier Leagues South Australia.
