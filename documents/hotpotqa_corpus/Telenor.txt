Telenor: Telenor Group (; ] or ] ) is a Norwegian mostly government-owned multinational telecommunications company headquartered at Fornebu in Bærum, close to Oslo.  It is one of the world's largest mobile telecommunications companies with operations in Scandinavia, Eastern Europe and Asia.  It has extensive broadband and TV distribution operations in four Nordic countries, and a 10-year-old research and business line for Machine-to-Machine technology.  Telenor owns networks in 13 countries, and has operations in 29 countries if their 33% ownership in VimpelCom Ltd is included.
