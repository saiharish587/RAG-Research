Ghana Nuclear Society: The Ghana Nuclear Society (GNS) is a nonprofit organization that advocates for the introduction of nuclear energy in Ghana.  It is headquartered at the Ghana Atomic Energy Commission (GAEC) in Accra.  With the establishment of The Ghana Nuclear Society, Ghana has joined the league of those countries with National Nuclear Societies.  Its head office is located at the Ghana Atomic Energy Commission (GAEC) in Accra.  The current national president is Prof. John Justice Fletcher.  The society is not for science inclined persons alone.
