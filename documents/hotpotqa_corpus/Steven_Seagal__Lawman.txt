Steven Seagal: Lawman: Steven Seagal: Lawman is an American reality television series that aired on A&E for its first two seasons and Reelz for its third.  It stars actor and martial artist Steven Seagal performing his duties as a reserve deputy sheriff in Jefferson Parish, Louisiana (season 1–2) and Maricopa County, Arizona (season 3).  It premiered on December 2, 2009.
