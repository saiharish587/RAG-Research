Hong Kong Island: Hong Kong Island () is an island in the southern part of Hong Kong.  It has a population of 1,289,500 and its population density is 16,390/km², as of 2008 .  The island had a population of about 3,000 inhabitants scattered in a dozen fishing villages when it was occupied by the United Kingdom in the First Opium War.  In 1842, the island was formally ceded in perpetuity to the UK under the Treaty of Nanking and the City of Victoria was then established on the island by the British Force in honour of Queen Victoria.
