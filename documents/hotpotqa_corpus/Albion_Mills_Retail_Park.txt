Albion Mills Retail Park: Albion Mills Retail Park is a retail park in central Wakefield, West Yorkshire, England.  The park, which contains a branch of PC World, was built in 2000.  It was acquired by CB Richard Ellis Realty Trust in July 2008 at a cost of £10.5 million (US$20,805,750).  The shopping complex covers an area of 55,294 square feet.
