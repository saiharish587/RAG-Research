Cornell School of Nursing: The Cornell University School of Nursing was founded in 1877 as the New York Hospital Training School for Nurses, in New York City.  As a part of New York Hospital, the school began its connection with Cornell University when Cornell's Medical College affiliated with New York Hospital in 1927.  In 1932, the school moved to the joint campus on the upper east side of New York when both institutions co-located.  The school became affiliated with Cornell and renamed as the Cornell University-New York Hospital School of Nursing in 1942.
