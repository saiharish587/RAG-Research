Tobacco Factory: The Tobacco Factory is the last remaining part of the old W. D. & H. O. Wills tobacco factory site on Raleigh Road, Southville, Bristol.  It was saved from demolition by the architect and former mayor of the city George Ferguson and through his vision has become a model of urban regeneration.  It is now a multi-use building which houses Thali Cafe, animation and performing arts school, loft-style apartments, a café bar, offices and a theatre.
