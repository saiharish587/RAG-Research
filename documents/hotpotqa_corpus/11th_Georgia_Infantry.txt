11th Georgia Infantry: The 11th Georgia Infantry Regiment was a Confederate States Army unit during the American Civil War.  The regiment was part of the Army of Northern Virginia.  Apart from a period when it was detached as part of Longstreet's forces in Georgia and Tennessee, the regiment remained with the army and took part in several major battles including the Seven Days Battles, Sharpsburg, Gettysburg, Cold Harbor and the siege of Petersburg until it surrendered at Appomattox on 9 April 1865.
