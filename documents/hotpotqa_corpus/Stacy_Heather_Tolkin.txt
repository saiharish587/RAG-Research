Stacy Heather Tolkin: Stacy Heather Tolkin is a former child actress who played the part of Irina in "The Concorde ... Airport '79", and voiced a number of 1983 television productions featuring Charles Schulz Peanuts characters.  Tolkin also voiced Sally Brown and Truffles in "The Charlie Brown and Snoopy Show".  She also played Herb Tarlek's daughter in a few episodes of "WKRP In Cincinnati".  As a child actress, Tolkin was in over a hundred commercials including McDonald's, Grandma's Cookies, and Kellogg's Corn Flakes.  She was also on the cover of the Kellogg's Corn Flakes box.  Tolkin played Bonnie Franklin's daughter in the TV movie, "Portrait of a Rebel: Margaret Sanger".
