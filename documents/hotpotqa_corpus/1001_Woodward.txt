1001 Woodward: 1001 Woodward is an office building in Downtown Detroit, Michigan.  It replaced the Majestic Building, a 14-story high rise on the same site.  The building is located just south of the neighboring David Stott Building, at the corner of Woodward Avenue and Michigan Avenue overlooking Campus Martius Park.  Constructed from 1963 to 1965, the 25-story building is designed in the International Style.  It was listed on the National Register of Historic Places in 2013.
