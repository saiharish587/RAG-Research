The Arrangement (novel): The Arrangement is a 1967 novel by Elia Kazan, narrated by a successful Greek-American advertising executive and magazine writer living in an affluent Los Angeles suburb who suffers a nervous breakdown due to the stress of the way in which he has lived his life – the "arrangement" of the title.  In 1969 Kazan made it into a film.  "The Arrangement" was a best-seller and garnered generally favorable reviews but it has been out of print since the 1980s.
