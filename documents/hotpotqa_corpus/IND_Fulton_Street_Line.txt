IND Fulton Street Line: The IND Fulton Street Line is a rapid transit line of the IND Division of the New York City Subway, running from the Cranberry Street Tunnel under the East River through all of central Brooklyn to a terminus in Ozone Park, Queens.  The IND Rockaway Line branches from it just east of Rockaway Boulevard.  The A train runs express during daytime hours and local at night on the underground portion of the line; it runs local on the elevated portion of the line at all times.  The C train runs local on the underground portion of the line at all times except late nights.
