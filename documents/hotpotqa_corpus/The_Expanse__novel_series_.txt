The Expanse (novel series): The Expanse is a series of science fiction novels, novellas and stories by James S. A. Corey, the pen name of authors Daniel Abraham and Ty Franck.  The first novel, "Leviathan Wakes", was nominated for the Hugo Award for Best Novel in 2012.  In 2017 the series as a whole was nominated for the 'Best Series' Hugo Award.
