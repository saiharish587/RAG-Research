SnapDragon: SnapDragon is a contemporary jazz band based in San Antonio, Texas.  The group released their debut CD, "Stealing a Moment" on Humbug Records in 2008, and the 10-song CD soon broke the Top 100 in U.S radio airplay (Mediabase Smooth Adult Contemporary chart).  SnapDragon also had three tracks on the compilation CD "HumBug Christmas" (HumBug Records, 2008).
