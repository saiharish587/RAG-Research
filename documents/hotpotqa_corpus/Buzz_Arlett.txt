Buzz Arlett: Russell Loris Arlett (January 3, 1899 – May 16, 1964), also known as Buzz Arlett, was an American baseball player of German descent, sometimes called "the Babe Ruth of the minor leagues."  Like Ruth, Arlett was a large man (6'4" and 230 pounds (104 kg)) who began his career as a pitcher before becoming his league's dominant home run hitter.  He was for many decades the all-time minor league home run king in the United States, until being surpassed by Mike Hessman on August 3, 2015.  He spent one season with the Philadelphia Phillies of Major League Baseball (MLB).
