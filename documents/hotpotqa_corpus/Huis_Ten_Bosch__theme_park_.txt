Huis Ten Bosch (theme park): Huis Ten Bosch (ハウステンボス , "Hausu Ten Bosu" ) is a theme park in Sasebo, Nagasaki, Japan, which recreates the Netherlands by displaying life-sized copies of old Dutch buildings.  The name Huis Ten Bosch translates into English as "House in the Woods/bush".  It is named after Huis ten Bosch, one of the three official residences of the Dutch Royal Family, located in The Hague in the Netherlands and former home to Princess Beatrix.
