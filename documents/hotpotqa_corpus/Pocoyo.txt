Pocoyo: Pocoyo ("Pocoyó" in Spanish) is a Spanish pre-school comedy animated television series created by Guillermo García Carsí, Colman López, Luis Gallego and , and is a co-production between Spanish producer Zinkia Entertainment, Cosgrove-Hall Films and Granada International.  Two series have been produced, each consisting of 52 seven-minute episodes.  English actor and comedian Stephen Fry narrates the English-language version and José María del Río narrates the Castilian Spanish version of the first two seasons, while Stephen Hughes narrates the third season, called Let's Go Pocoyo.  In 2016, a new spin-off show called "Pocoyo Planet" was created.
