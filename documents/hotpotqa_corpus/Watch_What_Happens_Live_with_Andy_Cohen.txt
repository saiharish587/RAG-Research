Watch What Happens Live with Andy Cohen: Watch What Happens Live with Andy Cohen (previously named Watch What Happens Live until December 19, 2016) is an American pop culture-based late-night talk show hosted by Andy Cohen that premiered on July 16, 2009.  It features Bravo's own reality programming in the manner of an aftershow, and celebrities' recent news.  The show is produced live from New York City, making possible interaction with viewers by phone and social media.  In November 2013, Bravo renewed "Watch What Happens: Live" for two additional seasons.  On March 2, 2016, the show aired its 1000th episode.
