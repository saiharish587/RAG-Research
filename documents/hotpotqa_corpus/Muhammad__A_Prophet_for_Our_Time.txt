Muhammad: A Prophet for Our Time: Muhammad: A Prophet For Our Time is a 2006 non-fiction book by the British writer Karen Armstrong.  It is part of the "Eminent Lives" series, which are short biographies of famous people by well-known writers.  It is Armstrong's second biography of Muhammad.  Her first biography "" earned her the Muslim Public Affairs Council Media Award.  "Muhammad: A Prophet For Our Time" is a short biography that shows how most Muslims understand Muhammad and their faith.  In the book, Armstrong depicts Muhammad as both a mystic and a wise political and social reformer.
