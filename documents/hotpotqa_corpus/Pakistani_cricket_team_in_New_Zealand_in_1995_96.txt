Pakistani cricket team in New Zealand in 1995–96: The Pakistan national cricket team toured New Zealand in December 1995 and played a Test match against the New Zealand national cricket team, winning by 161 runs.  New Zealand were captained by Lee Germon and Pakistan by Wasim Akram.  In addition, the teams played a four-match series of Limited Overs Internationals (LOI) which was tied 2–2.
