Ghulam Rasool Gangi: Ghulam Rasool Gangi was a direct descendant of Genghis Khan and the son of Pir Bukhsh.  His family moved from Yarkand and Kashgar to India in 1598, where he lived in the Siakolt district of Langrewali.  For three generations Abdul Rashid Khan's descendants were rulers of Khotan and Yarkand, but were scattered geographically until 1707.  Quresh Sultan, one of Abdul Rashid Khan's sons, and other Chugtai royal family members were relocated to India during the time of Emperor Akbar.
