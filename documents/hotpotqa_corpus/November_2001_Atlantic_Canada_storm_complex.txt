November 2001 Atlantic Canada storm complex: The November 2001 Atlantic Canada storm complex was a powerful coastal storm that included the remnants of North Atlantic hurricanes Michelle and Noel.  The low intensified as it moved westward into Atlantic Canada on November 6, reaching a minimum pressure of 946 mbars.  The storm turned to the northeast and emerged into the Atlantic Ocean on November 8.  It produced strong winds throughout Atlantic Canada, including gusts of up to 96 mph (155 km/h) at the Confederation Bridge in Prince Edward Island.  High waves caused damage along the coastlines, while high winds left up to 100,000 without power.  Overall damage was minor, and no casualties were reported.
