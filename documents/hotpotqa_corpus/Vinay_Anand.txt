Vinay Anand: Vinay Anand (Hindi: विनय आनंद ) is an Indian actor, having featured in around 60 Hindi and Bhojpuri movies.  Known for his roles in movies like Dil Ne Phir Yaad Kiya, Sautela, Jahan Jaaeyega Hamen Paaeyega, Aamdani Atthani Kharcha Rupaiyaa, Vinay Anand is also related to Bollywood actor Govinda.
