Manjolai Labourers massacre: Manjolai Labourers massacre (Tamil:மாஞ்சோலை தொழிலாளர்கள் படுகொலை) or Thamirabarani massacre of 23 July 1999 was the death of 17 labourers, including two women and a two-year-old child, when they got into the river to escape Tamil Nadu Police lathi-charge.  Public were going in procession to Tirunelveli Collectorate to submit a memorandum demanding wage settlement for the tea plantation workers of Manjolai estate.  An altercation between the police and the marchers resulted in lathi-charge by police.  When the marchers ran helter-skelter, many fell into the river and died.
