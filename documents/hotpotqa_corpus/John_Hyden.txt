John Hyden: John Hyden (born October 7, 1972 in Pensacola, Florida), and raised in San Diego, California, where he attended Mt. Carmel High School, is an American volleyball player, who was a member of the United States men's national volleyball team that finished in ninth place at the 1996 Summer Olympics in Atlanta, Georgia.  A 2-time All American at San Diego State University he was named to 1995 World Cup team and played as an outside hitter.  At the 2000 Summer Olympics he finished in 11th place with "Team USA".
