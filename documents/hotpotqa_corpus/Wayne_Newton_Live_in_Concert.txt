Wayne Newton Live in Concert: Wayne Newton Live in Concert was a Pay-Per-View concert special starring Wayne Newton that aired live from the Westgate Las Vegas Resort & Casino (then called the Las Vegas Hilton) on May 23, 1989.  The concert was one of only a handful of times that Newton has starred in a concert special featuring his entire Las Vegas show, and was also a way of promoting his then-new album "Coming Home."  The show was directed by Steve Binder, the same man who directed Elvis Presley's '68 Comeback Special.  Select pieces of the show were put together in a video cassette titled "Wayne Newton: Live In Concert at the Las Vegas Hilton" & was released in 1998.
