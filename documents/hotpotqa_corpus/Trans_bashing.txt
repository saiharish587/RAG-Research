Trans bashing: Trans bashing is the act of victimizing a person emotionally, physically, sexually, or verbally because they are transgender or transsexual.  Unlike gay bashing, it is committed because of the target's actual or perceived gender identity, not sexual orientation.  However, a trans person may be gay bashed if the person perceives them as gay rather than transgender.  The term has also been applied to hate speech directed at transgender people and at depictions of transgender people in the media that reinforce negative stereotypes about them.
