Walt Disney World Railroad: The Walt Disney World Railroad (WDWRR) is a 3-foot ( ) narrow-gauge heritage railroad and attraction located within the Magic Kingdom theme park of Walt Disney World in Bay Lake, Florida, in the United States.  Its route is 1.5 mi in length and encircles most of the park, with train stations in three different park areas.  The rail line, constructed by WED Enterprises, operates with four historic steam locomotives originally built by Baldwin Locomotive Works.  It takes about 20 minutes for each train to complete a round trip on the WDWRR's main line.  On a typical day, the railroad has two trains in operation; on busy days, it has three trains.
