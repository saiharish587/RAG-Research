Å: Å (lower case: å) — referred to as a Swedish "a" or volle — represents various (although often very similar) sounds in several languages.  It is considered a separate letter in the Swedish, Danish, Norwegian, Finnish, North Frisian, Walloon, Chamorro, Lule Sami, Skolt Sami, Southern Sami, and Greenlandic alphabets.  Additionally, it is part of the alphabets used for the Alemannic and the Bavarian-Austrian dialects of German.
