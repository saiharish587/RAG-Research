70th Armor Regiment: The 70th Armor Regiment is an armored (tank) unit of the United States Army.  It was constituted as the 70th Tank Battalion in July 1940, an independent tank battalion intended to provide close support to infantry units.  In this role, it saw action in the Mediterranean and European Theater of Operations, making assault landings and fighting with the 9th Infantry Division in North Africa, and with the 1st Infantry Division in Sicily.  The battalion supported the 4th Infantry Division on Utah Beach during the D-Day landings in France, and fought with the 4th Infantry Division through the remainder of World War II.  The 70th Tank Battalion was one of the first three tank battalions to deploy to Korea in the Korean War, where it saw significant action in, primarily with the 1st Cavalry Division.
