Bank of Canton: Bank of Canton () was established in 1912 in Canton but registered as a British company in Hong Kong.  This made it the first Chinese-owned bank in Hong Kong.  At the time, all the other banks in Hong Kong were foreign, primarily British, including the locally incorporated but British-run Hongkong and Shanghai Banking Corporation.  Security Pacific National Bank (SPNB) bought a majority share in Bank of Canton in 1971.  In 1988, Security Pacific succeeded in wholly acquiriing Bank of Canton, which became Security Pacific Asia Bank.  When Bank of America acquired Security Pacific Corporation in 1993 it changed Security Pacific Asia Bank’s name to Bank of America (Asia).  China Construction Bank acquired Bank of America (Asia) in 2006.
