The Rumble Fish 2: The Rumble Fish 2 (ザ・ランブルフィッシュ2 ) is a 2D fighting game for the Atomiswave arcade platform.  It was produced by Dimps and published by Sammy.  It is the sequel to "The Rumble Fish", bringing in several new additions.  A re-release on Taito's NESiCAxLive arcade digital delivery service was made available in 2012.
