Russian LGBT Network: The Russian LGBT Network (Russian: Российская ЛГБТ-сеть ) is a non-governmental LGBT rights organization working for the social acceptance of and protection of the rights of LGBT people in Russia.  Founded in 2006, it was reformed into the first (and only) Russian inter-regional LGBT rights organization on October 19, 2008.  The organization is a member of the International Lesbian and Gay Association (ILGA) and is led by Russian LGBT rights activist Igor Kochetkov.
