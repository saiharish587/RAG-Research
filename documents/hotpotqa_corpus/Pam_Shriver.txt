Pam Shriver: Pamela Howard Shriver (born July 4, 1962) is an American former professional tennis player known primarily as a doubles specialist with success also as a singles player.  She currently is a tennis broadcaster for ESPN.  During the 1980s and 1990s, she won 133 titles, including 21 women's singles titles, 111 women's doubles titles and one mixed doubles title.  In Grand Slam tournaments, Shriver won 21 doubles titles and one mixed doubles title.  She also won a women's doubles gold medal at the 1988 Olympic Games in Seoul with Zina Garrison as her partner.
