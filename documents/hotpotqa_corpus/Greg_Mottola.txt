Greg Mottola: Gregory J. "Greg" Mottola (born July 11, 1964) is an American film director, screenwriter, and television director.  Mottola wrote and directed the 1996 independent film "The Daytrippers", then concentrated for several years on directing in television for series such as "Undeclared" and "Arrested Development".  More recently, he has directed the feature films "Superbad", "Adventureland", and "Paul".
