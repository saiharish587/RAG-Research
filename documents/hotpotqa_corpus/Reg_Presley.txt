Reg Presley: Reginald Maurice Ball (12 June 1941 – 4 February 2013), known professionally as Reg Presley, was an English singer-songwriter.  He was the lead singer with the 1960s rock and roll band The Troggs, whose hits included "Wild Thing" and "With a Girl Like You" (they reached number one in the US and the UK respectively).  He wrote the song "Love Is All Around", which was featured in the films "Four Weddings and a Funeral" and "Love Actually".
