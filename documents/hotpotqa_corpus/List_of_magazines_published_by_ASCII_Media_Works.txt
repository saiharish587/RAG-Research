List of magazines published by ASCII Media Works: This is a list of magazines published by the Japanese publishing company ASCII Media Works.  After the merger of ASCII and MediaWorks on April 1, 2008, the two company's active magazines continued publication.  Most of their magazines center on anime, manga, bishōjo games, or video games.  A large number of ASCII Media Works' magazines carry the title "Dengeki" (電撃 , meaning "electric shock") which precedes the title of a given magazine; the "Dengeki" label is also used on publishing imprints, and contests held by the company, making it a well-known trademark for ASCII Media Works.  Other magazines center on computers and information technology.
