Miami MLS team: The Miami MLS team is a proposed professional soccer team to be based in Miami, Florida.  The year the team is scheduled to join Major League Soccer (MLS) is pending negotiations over stadium financing and location.  The ownership group, known as Miami Beckham United, is led by David Beckham, his business partner Simon Fuller, Miami-based Bolivian businessman Marcelo Claure, American sports executive Tim Leiweke and CEO of Eldridge Industries and Los Angeles Dodgers owner Todd Boehly.
