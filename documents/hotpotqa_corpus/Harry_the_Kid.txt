Harry the Kid: Harry Head (died 1881) known as Harry the Kid, was a cattle rustler and stagecoach robber in southern Arizona.  He is known to have died in 1881; however, his birth year and date weren’t recorded.  One of his better known robberies occurred in Contention City, Arizona.  He was with three outlaws named Bill Leonard, Jim Crane, and Luther King, when they all attempted to rob a stagecoach that was carrying 26,000 dollars in Wells Fargo money.  (The Complete List of Old West Outlaws)
