Muehlenbeckia adpressa: Muehlenbeckia adpressa, commonly known as climbing lignum, is a prostrate or climbing plant, native to Australia.  It has thin red-brown stems up to 1 m in length.  The leaves are 1.5 - long and 1.5 - wide.  It occurs in coastal areas of Western Australia, South Australia, Tasmania, Victoria and New South Wales.
