Justice (Rev Theory album): Justice is the third studio album by the rock band Rev Theory, released on February 15, 2011 by Interscope Records.  The first single, also titled "Justice," was released to iTunes and radio on October 25, 2010.  "Justice" peaked at #5 in "Billboard" Hard Rock Albums chart and #75 in the "Billboard" 200.  The album has sold 54,000 copies in the United States to date.  The song Justice was used for WWE's Extreme Rules (2011).  The song Hangman was used for the secondary theme song for WWE's SmackDown from 2009 to 2012.
