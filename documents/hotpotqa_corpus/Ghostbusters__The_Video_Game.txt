Ghostbusters: The Video Game: Ghostbusters: The Video Game is a 2009 action-adventure game based on the "Ghostbusters" media franchise.  Terminal Reality developed the Windows, PlayStation 3, and Xbox 360 versions, while Red Fly Studio developed the PlayStation 2, PlayStation Portable, and Wii versions, and Zen Studios developed the Nintendo DS version.  The game was released after several delays in development and multiple publisher changes.  In North America, all versions of the game were published by Atari, while publishing in Europe for the PlayStation 2, PlayStation Portable and PlayStation 3 versions was handled by Sony Computer Entertainment.
