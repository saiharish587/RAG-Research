TerreStar Corporation: TerreStar Corporation ("TSTR"), formerly "Motient Corp." (MNCP - 2000-2007) and "American Mobile Satellite Corp." (AMSC - 1988-2000), was the controlling shareholder of "TerreStar Networks Inc.", "TerreStar National Services, Inc." and "TerreStar Global Ltd.", and a shareholder of SkyTerra Communications.
