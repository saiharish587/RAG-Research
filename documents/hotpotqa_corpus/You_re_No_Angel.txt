You're No Angel: "You're No Angel" is a song written in 1942 by Francis E. Tucher (born in Indianapolis, Indiana in 1924).  It was recorded by "Nora Lou and her Pals of the Golden West," with lead singer Nora Lou Martin.  This female singing group appeared in "The Silver Bullet" (1942), "Stage to Chino" (1940), "Rancho Grande" (1940) with Gene Autry, and "Rovin' Tumbleweeds" (1939), also with Gene Autry.
