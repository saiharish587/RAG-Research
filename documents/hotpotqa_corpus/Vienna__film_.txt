Vienna (film): Vienna (also known as Orson Welles' Vienna or Spying in Vienna) is a 1968 short film directed by Orson Welles.  It was originally produced as part of his abandoned television special, Orson's Bag, which was made for CBS; but in 1969, with the project close to completion, CBS withdrew their funding over Welles' long-running disputes with US authorities regarding his tax status.  The film remained uncompleted.  Despite its name, "Vienna" freely mixes footage shot in Vienna, Zagreb, and in a Los Angeles studio.  The 8-minute segment was restored by the Munich Film Museum in 1999.
