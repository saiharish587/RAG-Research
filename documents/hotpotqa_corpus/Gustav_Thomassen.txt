Gustav Thomassen: Gustav Thomassen (1862 – 1929) was a Norwegian actor and theatre director.  He made his stage debut in 1881 at Den Nationale Scene in Bergen.  He worked at Den Nationale Scene until 1905, and served as theatre director from 1900 to 1905.  From 1905 to 1929 he worked as an actor and instructor at Nationaltheatret in Oslo.
