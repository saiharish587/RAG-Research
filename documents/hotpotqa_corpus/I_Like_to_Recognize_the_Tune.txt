I Like to Recognize the Tune: "I Like to Recognize the Tune" is an American popular song written by composer Richard Rodgers and lyricist Lorenz Hart.  The song was introduced by Eddie Bracken, Marcy Wescott, Mary Jane Walsh, Richard Kollmar and Hal Le Roy in the 1939 Broadway musical "Too Many Girls".  The lyrics lament the distortions of melody inherent in Jazz and Swing: "I like to recognize the tune / I want to savvy what the band is playing / I keep saying, "Must you bury the tune?"  In his autobiography, "Musical Stages", Richard Rodgers described the motivations that inspired the song: "we voiced objection to the musical distortions, then so much a part of pop music because of the swing-band influence."
