Colgems Records: Colgems Records was a record label that existed from 1966 to 1971.  It was a joint venture between Screen Gems, the television division of Columbia Pictures, and RCA Victor to issue records by the Monkees and other artists affiliated with Columbia/Screen Gems.  The label would also issue soundtrack recordings for Screen Gems and Columbia Pictures productions.  RCA acted as manufacturer and distributor for Colgems.  (Outside of the United States, Colgems productions appeared on the RCA Victor label.)
