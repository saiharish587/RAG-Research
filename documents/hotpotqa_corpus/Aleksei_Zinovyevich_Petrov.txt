Aleksei Zinovyevich Petrov: Aleksei Zinovyevich Petrov (Russian: Алексе́й Зино́вьевич Петро́в ; 28 October (15 October, Old Style) 1910, Koshki, Samara Governorate, Russian Empire – 9 May 1972, Kiev, Soviet Union) was a mathematician noted for his work on the classification of Einstein spaces, today called Petrov classification.
