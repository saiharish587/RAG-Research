The Bitch Is Back (Roxanne Shanté album): The Bitch Is Back is the second and final album released by rapper Roxanne Shanté.  It was released on October 5, 1992, on Cold Chillin' Records sub-label Livin' Large, was distributed by Tommy Boy/Warner Bros.  Records, and featured production by Kool G Rap, Grand Daddy IU, Large Professor, Mister Cee, Trackmasters, and Grandmaster Flash.
