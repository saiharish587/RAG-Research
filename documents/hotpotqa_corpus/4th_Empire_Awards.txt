4th Empire Awards: The 4th Empire Awards ceremony, presented by the British film magazine "Empire", honored the best films of 1998 and took place in 1999 at the Park Lane Hotel in London, England.  During the ceremony, "Empire" presented Empire Awards in nine categories as well as three honorary awards.  The honorary Movie Masterpiece award was first introduced this year.  The awards were sponsored by Stella Artois for the second consecutive year.
