Bajaga i Instruktori: Bajaga i Instruktori (Serbian Cyrillic: Бајага и Инструктори; trans.  "Bajaga and the Instructors") are a highly popular Serbian and former Yugoslav rock band.  Founded in Belgrade in 1984 by composer, lyricist and guitarist Momčilo Bajagić "Bajaga", the group possesses a rich discography and a plethora of hit songs that placed them at the top of the former Yugoslav rock scene, alongside other mega-selling bands such as Bijelo Dugme and Riblja Čorba.
