Israelites of the New Universal Pact: The Israelites of the New Universal Pact are a South American religious sect, mostly concentrated in Peru.  The evangelical Christian sect was founded in the Junin province of Peru in 1960 by Ezequiel Ataucusi Gamonal, following a break with the Seventh Day Adventist church of which he and his followers had been members., The end-times sect, which postulates Peru as a promised land, and its founder as the messiah, has gained a large following among indigenous people of the Peruvian jungle.
