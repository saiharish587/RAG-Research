1908 News of the World Match Play: The 1908 News of the World Match Play was the sixth News of the World Match Play tournament.  It was played from Tuesday 6 to Thursday 8 October at Mid-Surrey Golf Club.  32 players competed in a straight knock-out competition, with each match contested over 18 holes, except for the final which was over 36 holes.  The winner received £100 out of a total prize fund of £240.  J.H. Taylor defeated Fred Robson by 2 holes in the final to win the tournament.
