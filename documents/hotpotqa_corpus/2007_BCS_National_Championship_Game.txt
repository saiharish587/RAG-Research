2007 BCS National Championship Game: The 2007 Tostitos BCS National Championship Game was an American football game played at the University of Phoenix Stadium in Glendale, Arizona, on January 8, 2007.  It was the first time that the BCS had staged its own standalone national title game (previously the four BCS bowls each took turns serving as the title game).  The #1 Ohio State Buckeyes lost to the #2 Florida Gators, 41–14.
