Shehzad Roy: Shehzad Roy is a Pakistani singer, social worker and humanitarian.  He started his singing career in 1995 and has recorded six albums since.  He has recorded many hit songs such as "Saali," "Teri Soorat" and "Kangna," but is most famous for his 2008 socio-political album "Qismat Apney Haath Mein".  Roy is also the president and founder of Zindagi Trust, a non-government charitable organisation, that strives to improve the quality of education available to the average Pakistani.
