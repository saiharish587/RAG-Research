So You Think You Can Dance (U.S. season 11): So You Think You Can Dance, an American dance competition show, returned for its eleventh season on Wednesday, May 28, 2014.  The commission of an eleventh season was first announced by series creator Nigel Lythgoe on the September 10, 2013, telecast of the season 10 finale.  The season again features Lythgoe, who also serves as executive producer, and ballroom expert Mary Murphy as the two permanent members of the judge's panel while Cat Deeley continues in her role as host for a tenth consecutive season.
