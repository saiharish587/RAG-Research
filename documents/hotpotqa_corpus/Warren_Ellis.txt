Warren Ellis: Warren Girard Ellis (born 16 February 1968) is an English comic book writer, novelist, and screenwriter.  He is best known as the co-creator of several original comics series, including "Transmetropolitan" (1997–2002), "Global Frequency" (2002–2004), "Red" (2003–2004)—adapted into the feature films "Red" (2010) and "Red 2" (2013)—"Trees" (2014–present), and "Injection" (2015–present).  Ellis is the author of the novels "Crooked Little Vein" (2007) and "Gun Machine" (2013), and the novella "Normal" (2016).
