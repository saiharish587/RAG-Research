Honda CB125: The Honda CB125 was a 122 cc motorcycle made by Honda from 1971-1975 (1973-1985 in the US).  It had an overhead camshaft (OHC) engine with a 9500 rpm redline.  The "S" model was produced from 1971 to 1975 and was replaced in 1976 by the "J" model (the US bikes retained the S designation).  The newer model sported a two piece head, 124 cc displacement, and a larger carburetor.
