Milky Milky Milk Tour: The Milky Milky Milk Tour (initially called the Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz Tour) was the fifth concert tour by American recording artist Miley Cyrus, launched in support of her fifth studio album, "Miley Cyrus & Her Dead Petz" (2015).  The limited-run tour visited eight cities across North America.  She was joined by The Flaming Lips and Dan Deacon throughout the tour.
