List of England Test cricketers: This is a list of England Test cricketers.  A Test match is an international two-innings per side cricket match between two of the leading cricketing nations.  The list is arranged in the order in which each player won his Test cap by playing for the England cricket team.  Where more than one player won his first Test cap in the same Test match, those players are listed alphabetically by surname.  In the text, the numbers that follow the players' names correspond to their place in the chronological list of English Test cricketers.  Current players have their chronological number on the front of their shirts.
