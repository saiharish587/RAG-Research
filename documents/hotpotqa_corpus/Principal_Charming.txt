Principal Charming: "Principal Charming" is the fourteenth episode of "The Simpsons"<nowiki>'</nowiki> second season.  It originally aired on the Fox network in the United States on February 14, 1991.  In the episode, Marge's sister Selma is looking for a husband, so Marge orders Homer to help her find one.  Things go wrong, however, when Homer invites Principal Skinner over for dinner and Skinner instead falls for Selma's twin sister Patty.
