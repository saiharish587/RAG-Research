Rymill Park: Rymill Park, also known as Park 14 or Mullawirraburka in Kaurna, is a recreation park located in the East Park Lands of the South Australian capital of Adelaide.  It is bordered by East and Dequetteville Terraces, and Rundle and Bartels Roads.  It is in close proximity to the Adelaide Botanic Garden, Hutt Street, and Victoria Park.
