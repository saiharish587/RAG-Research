Hill Mansion: Hill Mansion is a historic home located at Culpeper, Culpeper County, Virginia.  It was built in 1857-1858, and is a two-story, four bay, brick dwelling in the Italianate style.  It measures 39 feet by 38 feet, 7 inches, and rests on a high brick foundation.  The front facade features a one-story porch consisting of an arcade, supported on Tuscan order piers, with a bracketed cornice.  It was the home of Edward Baptist Hill, whose brother, General A. P. Hill, was a frequent visitor during the American Civil War.  It also served as a Confederate hospital and later as headquarters for Union officers.
