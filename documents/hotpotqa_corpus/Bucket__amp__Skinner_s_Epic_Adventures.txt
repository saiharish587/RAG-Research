Bucket &amp; Skinner's Epic Adventures: Bucket & Skinner's Epic Adventures is an American teen sitcom that premiered on Nickelodeon on July 1, 2011.  The first half of the first season ended on October 7, 2011.  After a five-month hiatus, the second half premiered March 17, 2012.  Ashley Argota confirmed on July 5, 2012 that Nickelodeon has cancelled the show with several episodes yet to air in the USA; however all episodes were broadcast in other countries.  On December 22, 2012 TeenNick aired the previous never before seen in the US, Christmas episode.
