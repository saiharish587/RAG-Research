The Call of the Wild (2007 film): The Call of the Wild is a 2007 documentary film by the independent filmmaker Ron Lamothe.  The premise details the odyssey of Christopher McCandless as Lamothe takes a road trip across North America to the places McCandless visited.  Within the film, Lamothe reaches conclusions about McCandless' death which contradict both Sean Penn's film "Into the Wild" (2007) and Jon Krakauer's book "Into the Wild" (1997), on which it was based.
