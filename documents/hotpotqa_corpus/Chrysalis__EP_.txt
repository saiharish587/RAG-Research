Chrysalis (EP): Chrysalis is the debut extended play by South Korean girl group I.O.I, a special group created through the 2016 Mnet survival show, "Produce 101", composed of eleven trainees from different entertainment companies that will promote for a year under YMC Entertainment.  The album was released digitally on May 4, 2016 and physically on May 9 with the single "Dream Girls" in two versions, a standard edition and a special edition.
