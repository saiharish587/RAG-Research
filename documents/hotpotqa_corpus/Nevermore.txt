Nevermore: Nevermore is an American progressive metal band from Seattle, Washington, United States.  Formed in 1991, they are known to incorporate elements from various styles such as power metal, modern hard rock, classic heavy metal and technical thrash metal.  The band has been on hiatus since 2011, due to personal issues between the band members.  In April 2015, lead singer Warrel Dane confirmed that Nevermore has not disbanded, and there is a possibility for them to continue in the next two years with another album.
