Steve Griffin: Steven Leroy Griffin (born December 24, 1964) is a former American football wide receiver who played one season with the Atlanta Falcons of the National Football League.  He was drafted by the Atlanta Falcons in the twelfth round of the 1986 NFL Draft.  Griffin played college football at Purdue University and attended Miami Norland High School in Miami Gardens, Florida.  He was also a member of the Washington Commandos, Detroit Drive and Columbus Thunderbolts of the Arena Football League.  He was the MVP of ArenaBowl II after accumualting six receptions, two passed deflected and two interceptions in the game.
