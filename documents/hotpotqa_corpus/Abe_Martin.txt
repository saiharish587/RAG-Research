Abe Martin: Othol Hershel "Abe" Martin (October 18, 1908 – January 11, 1979) was an American football player, coach, and college athletics administrator.  He served as the head coach at Texas Christian University (TCU) from 1953 to 1966, compiling a record of 74–64–7.  Martin was also the athletic director at Texas Christian from 1963 to 1975.
