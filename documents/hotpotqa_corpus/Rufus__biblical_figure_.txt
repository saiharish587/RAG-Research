Rufus (biblical figure): Rufus ("Red") was a first-century Christian mentioned in with his brother Alexander, whose father "Simon a Cyrenian" was compelled to help carry the cross on which the Lord Jesus Christ was crucified.  According to Easton he was probably the same Rufus mentioned in , whose mother, (as well as Paul's mother,) were among those to whom Paul sent greetings in his epistle to the Romans.
