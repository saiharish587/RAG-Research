Sharif Badmash (1975 film): ′′Sharif Badmash (1975 film)′′ (Punjabi: ) is 1975 सामाजिक और संगीतमय फिल्म Pakistani Punjabi language action film, directed by Iqbal Kashmiri and produced by Asim Ilyas.  Film starring actor Mumtaz in the lead role and with Yousuf Khan , Aasia, Sultan Rahi and Asad Bukhari as the villain.  It was a super-hit musical movie and celebrated its Golden Jubilee in Pakistani cinemas in 1975.
