Need for Speed: Porsche Unleashed: Need for Speed: Porsche Unleashed, released as Need for Speed: Porsche 2000 in Europe, is a racing video game released in 2000.  It is the fifth installment in the "Need for Speed" series.  Unlike other "NFS" titles, "Porsche Unleashed" centers on racing Porsche sports cars, with models ranging from 1950 to 2000.  The game is noted for its extensive information regarding Porsche and its cars.  Unlike the previous four "Need for Speed" games, "Porsche Unleashed" was not released in Japan.
