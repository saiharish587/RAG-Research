William J. Weaver: William Joseph Weaver (1759-1817) was an artist born in London who came to prominence in North America.  He is perhaps most famous for his portrait of Alexander Hamilton which hangs in the United States State Department, and his full length portrait of Prince Edward, Duke of Kent and Strathearn, which hangs in Province House (Nova Scotia), Canada.  He also worked for Joseph Booth's Polygraphic Society.
