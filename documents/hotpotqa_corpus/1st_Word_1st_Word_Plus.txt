1st Word/1st Word Plus: 1st Word and 1st Word Plus are word processors developed by GST Computer Systems in the 1980s.  The original package, 1st Word, was given away free with all Atari STs.  The later 1st Word Plus was sold by GST and was more advanced.  Atari ST disk magazine ST News was written entirely and exclusively using 1st Word and, later, 1st Word Plus.  The first Volume (1986) was distributed as a plain 1st Word . DOC file, after that a custom shell was produced that enabled the 1st Word documents to be displayed in a userfriendly disk magazine shell.
