Let's Get Ready to Rhumble: "Let's Get Ready to Rhumble" is a 1994 song by British duo Ant & Dec, who at the time were known as PJ & Duncan.  The song was released in the United Kingdom on 11 July 1994 as the third single from their debut studio album "Psyche".  The song was written by Nicky Graham, Deni Lew and Mike Olton, and produced by Nicky Graham.  It peaked at number 9 in the UK Singles Chart in 1994, and was later #1 almost two decades after its original chart appearance.  The song includes a repeatedly-used sample of Michael Buffer saying his trademark catchphrase.
