1957 NBA Finals: The 1957 NBA World Championship Series was the championship series of the 1956–57 National Basketball Association season, and was the conclusion of the 1957 NBA Playoffs.  The best-of-seven series was played between the Western Division champion St. Louis Hawks and the Eastern Division champion Boston Celtics.  This was the first trip to the Finals for each team, the first Finals in which both teams competing were making their first appearances since 1951.  The Celtics won the series over the Hawks, 4–3.  It remains the only Game 7 in NBA history to be decided in double-overtime.
