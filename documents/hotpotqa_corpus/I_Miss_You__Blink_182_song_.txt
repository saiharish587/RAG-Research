I Miss You (Blink-182 song): "I Miss You" is a song by American rock band Blink-182, released on February 9, 2004 as the second single from the group's fifth studio album, "Blink-182" (2003).  Co-written by guitarist Tom DeLonge and bassist Mark Hoppus, they employed a method of writing separately and bringing their two verses together later.  The song, produced entirely acoustic, features an acoustic electric bass, a cello, and a brushstroked drum loop.  The song was inspired by The Cure song "The Love Cats" and contains references to "The Nightmare Before Christmas" (1993).
