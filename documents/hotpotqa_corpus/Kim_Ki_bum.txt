Kim Ki-bum: Kim Ki-bum (born August 21, 1987) is a South Korean actor and singer.  He was a member of the South Korean boy band Super Junior.  After the release of Super Junior's third studio album "Sorry, Sorry" in 2009, Kim announced his leave from the group to pursue his acting career.  In 2015, Kim announced he ended his contract with S.M. Entertainment and departure from the group.  Kim made his television debut in the Korean television drama, "April Kiss", in 2004.
