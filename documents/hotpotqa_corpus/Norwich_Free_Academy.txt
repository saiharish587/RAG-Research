Norwich Free Academy: The Norwich Free Academy ("NFA"), founded in 1854 and in operation since 1856, is a coeducational independent school for students between the 9th and 12th grade.  Located in Norwich, Connecticut, the Academy serves as the primary high school for Norwich and the surrounding towns of Canterbury, Bozrah, Voluntown, Sprague, Lisbon, Franklin, Preston, and Brooklyn.  It was recognized by the U.S. Department of Education as a National Blue Ribbon School of Excellence in 2001.  The class of 2016 graduated in NFA's 160th year of operation.
