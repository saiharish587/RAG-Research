Fair Antigua, We Salute Thee: "Fair Antigua, We Salute Thee" is the national anthem of Antigua and Barbuda.  Written by Novelle Hamilton Richards and composed by Walter Garnet Picart Chambers, it was adopted as the anthem in 1967 while Antigua and Barbuda were still a British colony.  It was adopted as the national anthem upon independence in 1981. " God Save the Queen" is still the royal anthem.
