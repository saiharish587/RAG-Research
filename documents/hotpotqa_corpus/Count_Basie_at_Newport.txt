Count Basie at Newport: Count Basie at Newport is a live album by jazz musician Count Basie and his orchestra.  It was originally issued as Verve MGV 8243 and included only the tracks 1-8 and 13.  Tracks 9-12 originally included in "Count Basie & Joe Williams/Dizzy Gillespie & Mary Lou Williams at Newport" (Verve MGV 8244).
