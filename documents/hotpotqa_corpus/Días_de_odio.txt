Días de odio: Días de odio, literally translated as Days of Hate, is a 1954 Argentine film.  It is based on the short story "Emma Zunz" by Jorge Luis Borges. Días de odio is a film Argentina in black and white directed by Leopoldo Torre Nilsson scripted himself about the story of Jorge Luis Borges entitled Emma Zunz, which was included in the book The Aleph and first released in 1949.  It was first performed the 3 of June 1954 and had as main protagonists Elisa Galvé , Nicolas Freguês, Raul del Valle, Enrique de Pedro, Duilio Marzio and Virginia Romay .  The producer of the film was Armando Bó .
