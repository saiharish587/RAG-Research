Miss Teen USA 2016: Miss Teen USA 2016 was the 34th Miss Teen USA pageant, was held at The Venetian Theatre in Las Vegas, Nevada on July 30, 2016.  Katherine Haik of Louisiana crowned her successor Karlie Hay of Texas, at the end of the event.  The pageant was webcast on the Miss Universe website, Facebook page, and mobile app as well as on Xbox 360 and Xbox One consoles via Xbox Live.  It was hosted by Miss USA 2015 Olivia Jordan and social media star Cody Johns, while "American Idol" winner Nick Fradiani performed.
