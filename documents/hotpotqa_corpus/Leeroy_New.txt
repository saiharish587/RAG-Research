Leeroy New: Leeroy New is a contemporary Filipino fine artist whose works overlap with theatre, film, fashion, and visual arts.  He is a Visual Arts graduate of Philippine High School for the Arts and a graduate of the University of the Philippines College of Fine Arts.  Currently, he is known as one of the designers alongside Kermit Tesoro for the muscle dress, a dress worn by Lady Gaga in her music video, Marry The Night.
