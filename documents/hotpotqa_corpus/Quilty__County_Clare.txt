Quilty, County Clare: Quilty (Irish: "Coillte" , meaning "woods" ), historically "Killty", is a small fishing village between Milltown Malbay and Doonbeg in County Clare, Ireland.  Lobster, salmon, bass, herring and mackerel are landed at Quilty, formerly known for its curing industry.  The area was officially classified as part of the West Clare Gaeltacht, an Irish-speaking community, until 1956.
