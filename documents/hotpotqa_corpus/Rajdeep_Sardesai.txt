Rajdeep Sardesai: Rajdeep Sardesai (born 24 May 1965) is an Indian news anchor and author.  Sardesai is currently a consulting editor at the India Today group, and hosts India Today Television.  He was the Editor-in-Chief of Global Broadcast News, that included CNN-IBN, IBN7 and IBN-Lokmat—which he resigned in July 2014.
