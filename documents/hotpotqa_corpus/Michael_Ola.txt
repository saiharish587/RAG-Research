Michael Ola: Michael Ola (born April 19, 1988) is an American football offensive tackle for the Buffalo Bills of the National Football League (NFL).  He played college football at Hampton University and attended Riverdale High School in Riverdale, Georgia.  He has also been a member of the Jacksonville Sharks, Montreal Alouettes, Miami Dolphins, Chicago Bears, San Diego Chargers, Detroit Lions, Seattle Seahawks and New York Giants.
