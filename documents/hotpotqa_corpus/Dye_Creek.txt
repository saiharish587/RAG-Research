Dye Creek: Dye Creek is an 18.2 mi watercourse in Tehama County, California, United States, that is tributary to the Sacramento River.  Dye Creek's watershed is situated in north-central California.  The Dye Creek watershed contains rugged terrain areas of oak-studded forest, and also provides habitat for numerous understory flora and fauna.  An example forb found in the watershed is the poppy "Calochortus luteus", which is at its northern limit around the Dye Creek watershed.
