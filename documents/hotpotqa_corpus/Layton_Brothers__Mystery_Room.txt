Layton Brothers: Mystery Room: Layton Brothers: Mystery Room is a puzzle adventure video game for iOS and Android, published by Level-5.  It is a spin-off of the "Professor Layton" series, starring rookie investigator Lucy Baker, who works with genius investigator Alfendi Layton, son of Hershel Layton, in the Scotland Yard's top investigation unit, the "Mystery Room".  The game was released in Japan on September 21, 2012, and in English on June 27, 2013.  An Android version was released on September 5, 2013.
