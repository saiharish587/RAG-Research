New Jersey Tidelands Resource Council: The Tidelands Resource Council is a body of twelve Governor-appointed members.  The Council meets monthly and makes decisions to sell or rent state tidelands.  All of the decisions must then be approved by the Commissioner of the Department of Environmental Protection, the Attorney General and the Governor.  The council meets the first Wednesday of every month, with the exception of January, at NJ Civil Service Commission, 44 S. Clinton Ave., Trenton, NJ.  Council meetings begin at 10 am and are open to the public.
