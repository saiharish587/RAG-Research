Central tire inflation system: A central tire inflation system (CTIS) is a system to provide control over the air pressure in each tire of a vehicle as a way to improve performance on different surfaces.  For example, lowering the air pressure in a tire creates a larger area of contact between the tire and the ground and makes driving on softer ground much easier.  It also does less damage to the surface.  This is important on work sites and in agricultural fields.  By giving the driver direct control over the air pressure in each tire, maneuverability is greatly improved.
