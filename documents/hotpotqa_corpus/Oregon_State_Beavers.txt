Oregon State Beavers: The Oregon State Beavers are the athletic teams that represent Oregon State University in Corvallis, Oregon.  The Beavers compete at the National Collegiate Athletic Association (NCAA) Division I (Football Bowl Subdivision (FBS) for college football) level as a member of the Pac-12 Conference.  Oregon State's mascot is Benny the Beaver.  Both the men's and women's teams share the name, competing in 7 NCAA Division I men's sports and 10 NCAA Division I women's sports respectively.
