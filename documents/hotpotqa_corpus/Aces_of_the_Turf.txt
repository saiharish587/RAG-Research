Aces of the Turf: Aces of the Turf (French: Les as du turf) is a 1932 French comedy sports film directed by Serge de Poligny and starring Paul Pauley, Alexandre Dréan and Josyane.  It was made at Joinville Studios by the French subsidiary of Paramount Pictures.  In 1935 it was released in the United States with the alternative title of Racetrack Winners.
