Withlacoochee River (Suwannee River): The Withlacoochee River originates in Georgia, northwest of Nashville, Georgia.  It flows south through Berrien County where it joins the New River and forms part of the boundary between Berrien and Cook counties.  It then flows south into Lowndes County, Georgia.  At Troupville, Georgia the Little River joins the Withlacoochee River flows continues to flow south and forms part of the boundary between Lowndes and Brooks counties in Georgia.  The river then flows into Florida for 1.34 miles Florida before returning into Georgia for an additional 2.44 miles.  It then returns to Florida, forming the northeast boundary of Madison County, Florida and the western boundary of Hamilton County, Florida and eventually merges with the Suwannee at Suwannee River State Park west of Live Oak.  The river is 115 mi long.  It is believed to be the source for the name of the central Florida river of the same name.
