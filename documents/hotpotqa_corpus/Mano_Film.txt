Mano Film: Mano Film (formerly Mano Produktion) is a Swiss film production company located in Zurich, Switzerland.  It was founded in 1987 by Anka Schmid, Agnes Barmettler and Rachel Schmid and produced several feature films.  In 2001 Anka Schmid became the sole proprietress.  The production company has since specialized in producing interdisciplinary film and art projects.
