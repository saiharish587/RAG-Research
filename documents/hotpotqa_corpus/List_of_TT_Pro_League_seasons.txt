List of TT Pro League seasons: The TT Pro League (formerly known as the Professional Football League) is the Trinidad and Tobago professional league for association football clubs.  The league serves as the top division in the Trinidad and Tobago football league system and is the country's primary football competition.  Contested by ten clubs, the league is one of the world's few football leagues that does not operate on an automatic system of promotion and relegation.  Seasons run from September to May, with teams playing 27 games each, totaling 135 games in the season.  Most games are played in the evenings of Fridays ("Super Fridays") and Saturdays ("Fiesta Saturdays"), with a few games played during weekday evenings.  The Pro League headquarters is located in northwest of Trinidad and Tobago in St. Augustine.  The league is currently sponsored by Digicel and thus officially known as the Digicel Pro League.
