Susan M. Gordon: Susan M. Gordon is an American government official who currently serves as Principal Deputy Director of National Intelligence.  Prior to assuming her current role, she was the Deputy Director of the National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency (NGA), having assumed the position on January 1, 2015.  Before joining the NGA, she served as director of the CIA's Information Operations Center and senior cyber adviser to the Director of the Central Intelligence Agency.  Gordon worked for the Central Intelligence Agency for over 25 years.
