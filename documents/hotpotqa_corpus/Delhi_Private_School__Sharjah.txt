Delhi Private School, Sharjah: Delhi Private School, Sharjah (DPS, Sharjah), (in Arabic: مدرسة دلهي الخاصة ، الشارقة) is a for-profit private senior secondary school in Sharjah, UAE.  The school was established in 2000 and is located in the school zone, in Sharjah industrial area No .16 Affiliated to CBSE New Delhi, it is the first school in the UAE to have branched out from its parent institute, Delhi Public School, New Delhi and runs under the aegis of Delhi Public School Society which now also operates Delhi Private School, Dubai.
