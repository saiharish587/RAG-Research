Liza Weil: Liza Rebecca Weil (born June 5, 1977) is an American actress, known for her role as Paris Geller in the WB/CW series "Gilmore Girls" and its Netflix revival "".  She is also known for her roles as White House aide Amanda Tanner in the first season of ABC's "Scandal" and as attorney Bonnie Winterbottom in ABC's "How to Get Away with Murder".
