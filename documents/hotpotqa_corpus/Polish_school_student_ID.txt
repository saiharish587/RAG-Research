Polish school student ID: The Polish school student ID (Polish: "legitymacja szkolna" ) is the document certifying that the person is attending a primary or secondary school in Poland.  For student of age at which no ID card or passport is issued, this document certifies his identity.  With this you can have official discounts on public transport.  Polish student ID contains first name, last name, birth date, address, PESEL and photo.  Document size: 72×103 mm and photography: 30×42 mm.
