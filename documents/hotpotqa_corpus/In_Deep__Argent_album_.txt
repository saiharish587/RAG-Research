In Deep (Argent album): In Deep, released in 1973, is the 4th studio album by Argent; originally released by Epic Records, KE 32195.  It features the original full-length recording of "God Gave Rock and Roll to You", which reached No.18 in the UK charts (U.S. #114); when released as a single in edited form later the same year.  It was later remade by Kiss as "God Gave Rock 'n' Roll to You II" for the film "Bill & Ted's Bogus Journey" in 1991, when it was a massive worldwide hit.
