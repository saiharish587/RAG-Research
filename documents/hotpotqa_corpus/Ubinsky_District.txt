Ubinsky District: Ubinsky District (Russian: Уби́нский райо́н ) is an administrative and municipal district (raion), one of the thirty in Novosibirsk Oblast, Russia.  It is located in the center of the oblast.  The area of the district is 13760 km2 .  Its administrative center is the rural locality (a "selo") of Ubinskoye.  Population: 16,297 (2010 Census);  (2002 Census) ;  (1989 Census) The population of Ubinskoye accounts for 35.8% of the district's total population.  The district's name comes from Lake Ubinskoye, which is mostly located inside the district.
