Now Summer 2007 (Australian series): Now Summer 2007 is a compilation CD released by EMI Music Australia in late 2006.  Like most Australian summer compilations, it is released in December and is meant to carry on throughout January and February (the three months of summer).  It has two CDs which no other NOW CD in Australia has.  "Now Summer 2007" is the 15th CD of the Australian Now!  series.
