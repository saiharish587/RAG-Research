Kevin Magee (executive): Kevin Magee is an American television executive with MSNBC, serving as Executive-in-Charge of the morning news show, Morning Joe.  He came there from the Fox Business network.  He joined the FOX Broadcasting group in 2001.  He began his term as Senior Vice Preisent of FOX News Radio in 2005.  Chairman of FOX News Programming Council.  Prior to Fox, he served as business news executive producer for CNBC from 1997-2001.  He also worked for ABC News, as senior programming producer of Good Morning America from 1991-1997.
