Joy (Mick Jagger song): "Joy" was the second track from English singer-songwriter Mick Jagger's fourth solo album, "Goddess in the Doorway" (2001). " Rolling Stone" called it "a rocking, gospel-tinged collaboration with Bono of U2" - featuring Pete Townshend on guitar.  "Joy" was one of three tracks from "Goddess in the Doorway" to be featured within Jagger's greatest hits album, "The Very Best of Mick Jagger".
