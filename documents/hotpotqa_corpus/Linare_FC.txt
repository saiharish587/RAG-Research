Linare FC: Linare FC is a Lesotho football club based in Leribe.  It is based in the city of Leribe in the Leribe District.  It was established in 1931 and it has been in the Lesotho Premier Division from the early 1930s to date.  Its one of the soccer power house in Lesotho with most of the players been able to play in foreign countries like South Africa.
