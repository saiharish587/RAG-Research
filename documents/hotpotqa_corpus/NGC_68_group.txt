NGC 68 group: The NGC 68 galaxy group is a cluster of at least 40 galaxies around the elliptical galaxy NGC 68.  The main group was discovered in 1784 by William Herschel, who listed the galaxies as a single object.  Later, in the 1880s, John Louis Emil Dreyer managed to discern the main galaxies and cataloged them each as NGC 68, NGC 70, and NGC 71.
