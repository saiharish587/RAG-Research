Songs of David Byrne and Brian Eno Tour: The Songs of David Byrne and Brian Eno Tour is a 2008–2009 promotional concert tour of music co-written by David Byrne and Brian Eno with performances by Byrne.  In addition to being a retrospective of the duo's collaborations, the tour promoted the album "Everything That Happens Will Happen Today".  The musical performers were accompanied by dancers who were choreographed to several songs.  Performances were held across the world and later documented on a tour EP and a concert film.
