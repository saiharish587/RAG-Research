Newton's Grace: Newton’s Grace (working title: "But Now I See") is an American historical drama film about John Newton, a slave ship captain and later Church of England pastor who wrote many hymns, including Amazing Grace.  The film, directed by the award-winning Christian filmmaker John Jackman, is based on Newton’s autobiography, "Out of the Depths".  Jackman used the tall ship "Hector" in Pictou, Nova Scotia, for filming many seafaring scenes.  Scenes set on the coast of Sierra Leone were filmed at various locations on the North Carolina coast, particularly Fort Anderson and Fort Macon.  Interiors and green screen special effects shots were filmed in Winston-Salem, North Carolina.  The film stars Erik Nelson as the young John Newton.  The film premiered at the deCoste Centre in Pictou, on May 13, 2017.
