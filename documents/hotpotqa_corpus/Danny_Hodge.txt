Danny Hodge: Daniel Allen Hodge (born May 13, 1932) is a retired American wrestler and boxer.  He is renowned for his wrestling career, both amateur and professional.  He was born and raised in Perry, Oklahoma, where he continues to live.  He is famous for the ability to crush apples with one hand, a feat which he demonstrated live on ESPN during the 2006 NCAA Wrestling Championships.  He said his strength was due to having double tendons in his hands.
