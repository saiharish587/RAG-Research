Hate Story 3: Hate Story 3 is a 2015 Indian erotic thriller film directed by Vishal Pandya.  Produced by T-Series, it stars Karan Singh Grover, Sharman Joshi, Zareen Khan and Daisy Shah in lead roles, and Priyanshu Chatterjee in a pivotal role.  Written by Vikram Bhatt and Madhuri Banerji, the film belongs to the "Hate Story (film series)".  The film was released on 4 December 2015.
