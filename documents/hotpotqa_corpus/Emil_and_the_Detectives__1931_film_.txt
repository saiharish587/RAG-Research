Emil and the Detectives (1931 film): Emil and the Detectives (German: Emil und die Detektive ) is a 1931 German adventure film directed by Gerhard Lamprecht and starring Rolf Wenkhaus.  It is based on the 1929 novel by Erich Kästner, who also contributed to the film's script.  The film script was written by Billy Wilder.  Until today, this film is generally considered to be the best film adaption of "Emil and the Detectives.
