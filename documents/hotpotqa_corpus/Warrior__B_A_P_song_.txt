Warrior (B.A.P song): "Warrior" (Korean: 워리어) is a song recorded by South Korean boy group B.A.P.  It is released as a digital single on January 26, 2012 through TS Entertainment.  The song served as B.A.P's debut single, the first from their self-titled debut album.  "Warrior" was written and produced by Kang Ji-on and Kim Ki-bum, the same producers of Song Ji-eun's Going Crazy and Bang & Zelo's "Never Give Up".  B.A.P's leader, Bang Yong-guk also participated in the song's production by co-writing "Warrior".  The song's lyrics describes the injustice of the current society and how the protagonist's desire to end it.
