Agent Carter (season 1): The first season of the American television series "Agent Carter", which is inspired by the film "" and the Marvel One-Shot short film of the same name, features the Marvel Comics character Peggy Carter as she must balance doing administrative work and going on secret missions for Howard Stark while trying to navigate life as a single woman in 1940s America.  It is set in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), sharing continuity with the films of the franchise, and was produced by ABC Studios, Marvel Television, and F&B Fazekas & Butters.  Tara Butters, Michele Fazekas, and Chris Dingess served as showrunners.
