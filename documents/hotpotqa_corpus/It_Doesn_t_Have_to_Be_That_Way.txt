It Doesn't Have to Be That Way: "It Doesn't Have to Be That Way" is a song written and recorded by Jim Croce for his 1973 album "Life and Times".  Originally released early that year as the B-side of the "One Less Set of Footsteps" single, it was reissued that December as the third and final single from the album as well as Croce's second posthumously-released single.  It reached a peak of #64 on the "Billboard" Hot 100, spending five weeks on the chart.
