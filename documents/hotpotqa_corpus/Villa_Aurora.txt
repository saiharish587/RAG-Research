Villa Aurora: The Villa Aurora at 520 Paseo Miramar is located in Pacific Palisades, Los Angeles and has been used as an artists residence since 1995.  It is the former home of the German-Jewish author Lion Feuchtwanger and his wife Marta.  The Feuchtwangers bought this Spanish-style mansion in 1943 for only $9,000, the annual salary of a school teacher.  The house was a popular meeting place for artists and the community of German-speaking émigrés.  Lion Feuchtwanger wrote six of his historical novels in this house: "Der Tag wird kommen", "Waffen für Amerika", "Die Jüdin von Toledo", "Narrenweisheit oder Tod und Verklärung des Jean-Jacques Rousseau", "Jefta und seine Töchter", and "Goya oder der arge Weg der Erkenntnis".
