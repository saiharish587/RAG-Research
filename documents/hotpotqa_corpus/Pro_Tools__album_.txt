Pro Tools (album): Pro Tools is the fifth studio album by American rapper and Wu-Tang Clan member GZA.  The album was released August 19, 2008 on Babygrande Records.  It serves as his first release since his collaboration album with DJ Muggs, "Grandmasters" (2005), and follows six years after his last solo effort, "Legend of the Liquid Sword" (2002).  Production for the album took place during 2008 and was handled by several record producers, including RZA, Bronze Nazareth, Preservation, Mathematics, Black Milk, Arabian Knight, True Master
