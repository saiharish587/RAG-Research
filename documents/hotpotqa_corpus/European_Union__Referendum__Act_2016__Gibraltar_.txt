European Union (Referendum) Act 2016 (Gibraltar): The European Union (Referendum) Act 2016 is an Act of the Gibraltar Parliament, which implements the United Kingdom’s European Union Referendum Act 2015 in Gibraltar.  It was the first time a referendum has been held in Gibraltar on the issue of continued EU membership since the territory joined along with the United Kingdom in 1973 and was also the first time the British Overseas Territory had participated in a UK-wide referendum.  The Act commenced on 26 January 2016, and received assent from the Governor of Gibraltar on 28 January 2016.
