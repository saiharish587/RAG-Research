Tottenham Hotspur L.F.C.: Tottenham Hotspur Ladies F.C. is an English women's football club affiliated with Tottenham Hotspur F.C..  The club is set to compete in the FA WSL 2 for the 2017–18 season.  The club currently play at Cheshunt F.C..  Tottenham Hotspur Ladies F.C. was founded in 1985, under the name Broxbourne Ladies.  The first team have won twelve trophies, the most recent being the overall FA Women's Premier League in the 2016-17 season.
