2016 Football League Championship play-off Final: The 2016 Football League Championship play-off Final was a football match contested between Sheffield Wednesday and Hull City.  The match was played at Wembley Stadium on 28 May 2016 at 17:00.  Hull City won 1–0 after a Mohamed Diamé goal in the 72nd minute, and was therefore promoted to the Premier League for the next season.  Success in the final was estimated to be worth up to £170 million to the winning team.
