Patrick W. Kenny: Patrick W. Kenny (died 22 April 1931) was an Irish politician.  He was a Cumann na nGaedheal member of the Free State Seanad Éireann from 1922 to 1931.  He was elected to Free State Seanad in 1922 for 9 years and served until his death in office on 22 April 1931.  He was elected Leas-Chathaoirleach (Deputy chairman) of the Seanad on 12 December 1928.
