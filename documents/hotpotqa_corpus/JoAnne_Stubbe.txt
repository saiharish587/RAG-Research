JoAnne Stubbe: In 1946, JoAnne was born in Champaign, Illinois.  Stubbe received a BS degreewith high honors in chemistry from the University of Pennsylvania and worked as an undergraduate in the laboratory of Professor Edward R. Thornton.  After she received her PhD degree in organic chemistry under the guidance of Professor George Kenyon from the University of California, Berkeley in 1971, she did a very brief stint (1971-1972) as a postdoc at UCLA, where she worked on synthesizing LSD from tryptophan with Julius Rebek.  Then, Stubbe taught at Williams College (1972-1977) discovered she didn’t want to teach, but wanted to do research.  Her realization sent her to Brandeis University (1975-1977), where she did a second postdoc with Bob Abeles.  This is where she learned the art and science of creating mechanism-based enzyme inhibitors.  She also taught at Yale School of Medicine (1977-1980) as an assistant professor in the department of pharmacology.
