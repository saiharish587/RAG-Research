Battlestar Galactica: Blood &amp; Chrome: Battlestar Galactica: Blood & Chrome is a prequel to the reimagined "Battlestar Galactica" series and is the latest installment in the franchise.  A web-series that became a pilot for a possible series chronicling the early adventures of a young William Adama, but the series was not picked up.  It stars Luke Pasqualino, Ben Cotton, and Lili Bordán.  Michael Taylor wrote the teleplay from a story by Taylor, David Eick, Bradley Thompson and David Weddle, with Jonas Pate as director.  Distribution of "Blood & Chrome" began as a 10-episode online series in conjunction with Machinima.com on  9, 2012 (2012--) , and also aired as a televised movie on  10, 2013 (2013--) on Syfy.
