Spencer Reiss: Spencer Reiss (born 1952 in New York) is a former Newsweek foreign correspondent in Asia, Africa, Middle East and Latin America, now a contributing editor at Wired magazine. He began working for Wired as a senior editor in San Francisco in 1996.  He is responsible for covering energy issues, new media, commercial space travel, and the impact technology is having on humans.  He is also a frequent contributor to "The Wall Street Journal" and "MIT Technology Review".  Reiss also directed the program for the annual Monaco Media Forum held in Monte Carlo.
