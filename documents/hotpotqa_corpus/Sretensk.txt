Sretensk: Sretensk (Russian: Сретенск ; ] ) is a town and the administrative center of Sretensky District in Zabaykalsky Krai, Russia, located on the right bank of the Shilka River (Amur basin), 385 km east of Chita, the administrative center of the krai.
