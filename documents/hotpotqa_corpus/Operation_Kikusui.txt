Operation Kikusui: Operation Kikusui (菊水作戦 , Kikusui sakusen ) was a series of suicidal air attacks by Imperial Japanese forces during the Battle of Okinawa against Allied fleets in the waters around Okinawa, as part of Operation Ten-Go.  The name of the operation, "Kikusui", comes from the hata-jirushi of the samurai Kusunoki Masashige.
