In His Eyes: "In His Eyes" is a song performed in the musical "Jekyll and Hyde", composed by Frank Wildhorn with lyrics by Frank Wildhorn, Leslie Bricusse and Steve Cuden.  "Jekyll and Hyde" premiered on Broadway in 1997 and has since seen many subsequent international, as well as regional, productions.  In 2013, the show was revived on Broadway.  The song appeared in the Original Broadway production performed by Linda Eder and Christiane Noll.
