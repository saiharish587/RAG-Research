Cacareco: Cacareco (1954-1962) was a female black rhinoceros exhibited in Brazilian zoos.  She became famous as a candidate for the 1958 São Paulo city council elections with the intention of protesting against political corruption.  Electoral officials did not accept Cacareco's candidacy, but she eventually won 100,000 votes, more than any other party in that same election (which was also marked by rampant absenteeism).  Today, the term "voto Cacareco" (Cacareco vote) is commonly used to describe protest votes in Brazil.  Cacareco's candidacy inspired the Rhinoceros Party of Canada, nominally led by the rhinoceros Cornelius the First.
