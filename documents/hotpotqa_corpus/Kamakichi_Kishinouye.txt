Kamakichi Kishinouye: Kamakichi Kishinouye (岸上 鎌吉 , Kishinoue Kamakichi , 29 November 1867 – 22 November 1929) was a Japanese fisheries biologist and cnidariologist and a professor of the Imperial University of Tokyo (Faculty of Agriculture) between 1908 and 1928.  Kishinouye died in Chengtu of a sudden illness while on a collecting expedition to China.
