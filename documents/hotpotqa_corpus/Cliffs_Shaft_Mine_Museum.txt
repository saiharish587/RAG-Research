Cliffs Shaft Mine Museum: The Cliffs Shaft Mine Museum is a former iron mine, now a heritage museum, located on Euclid Street between Lakeshore Drive and Spruce Street in Ishpeming, Michigan.  The museum, operated by "Marquette Range Iron Mining Heritage Theme Park Inc.", celebrates the history of the Marquette Iron Range.  The site was designated a state of Michigan historic site in 1973 and placed on the National Register of Historic Places in 1992.
