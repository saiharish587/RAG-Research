Partners Harvard Medical International: PHMI was founded in 1994 as a subsidiary of Harvard University.  From 1994 until 2008, the organization was known as Harvard Medical International, or HMI.  Operated as a division of Harvard Medical School, HMI’s original focus was to work with institutions around the world – primarily medical schools and health care delivery organizations – interested in developing education programs and health care programs with the help of Harvard professors and the staff of Harvard Medical School.  In April 2008, HMI became Partners Harvard Medical International and became part of Partners HealthCare.  Since 1994, the company has developed collaborative relationships with institutions in more than 40 countries.
