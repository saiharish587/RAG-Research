Village School (Great Neck, New York): Village School (also known as VS) is an American long-established public alternative school.  It is a member of Coalition of Essential Schools and is located in the village of Great Neck, New York, serving students in grades 8 through 12.  Village School is one of three high schools in the Great Neck School District, which includes Great Neck North High School and Great Neck South High School.  Village School offers its 39 students an outdoor education program, college preparatory program, and inclusion of students with disabilities.
