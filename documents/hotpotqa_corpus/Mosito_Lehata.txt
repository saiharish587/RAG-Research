Mosito Lehata: Mosito Lehata (born 8 April 1989 in Maseru) is a Mosotho athlete competing in sprinting events.  He is the current holder of the Lesotho national record for the 100-meter at 10.13 seconds, and has consistently won the national track championships on shorter tracks.  He was eliminated in the first round of the men's 200 m event at the 2012 Summer Olympics.  Lehata found success in the 200 m event at the 2013 World Championships in Athletics when he finished in the first round ahead of Jamaican sprinter Usain Bolt and advanced to the semifinals.
