1929 Philadelphia Athletics season: The 1929 Philadelphia Athletics season involved the A's finishing 1st in the American League with a record of 104 wins and 46 losses.  After finishing in second place to the New York Yankees in 1927 and 1928, the club won the 1929 pennant by a large 18-game margin.  The club went on to win the World Series over the NL champion Chicago Cubs, four games to one.
