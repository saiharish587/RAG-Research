The Perfect Murder (film): The Perfect Murder is a 1988 English language Indian film directed by Zafar Hai and produced by Merchant-Ivory.  The film is based on the 1964 novel "The Perfect Murder" by British crime fiction writer HRF Keating and stars Naseeruddin Shah as Inspector Ghote, the leading character in Keating's novels.  Swedish actor Stellan Skarsgård as well as many noted Indian actors such as Madhur Jaffrey, Amjad Khan, Dalip Tahil, Ratna Pathak, Annu Kapoor and Johnny Walker also appear in the film.
