Mississippi gubernatorial election, 1947: The 1947 Mississippi gubernatorial election took place on November 4, 1947, in order to elect the Governor of Mississippi.  Incumbent Democrat Fielding L. Wright, who had succeeded to the governorship a year prior following the death of Thomas L. Bailey, ran for election to a first full term.  As was common at the time, the Democratic candidate won in a landslide so therefore the Democratic primary was the real contest, and winning the primary was considered tantamount to election.
