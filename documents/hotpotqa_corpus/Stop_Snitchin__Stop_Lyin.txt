Stop Snitchin, Stop Lyin: Stop Snitchin–Stop Lyin is the sixth mixtape album/DVD by rapper The Game.  It features artists like Lil' Kim, Ice Cube, E-40, Chingy, WC, Paul Wall and others.  This mixtape is the third of three diss mixtapes aimed at the G-Unit members.  It is quite possibly the most popular of the three due to the DVD advertisement.  It also features disses by other Black Wall Street Members.  Repeatedly DJ Clue says "Come on Game it's the last one" suggesting there would be no more diss tracks or mixtapes aimed at G-Unit any more.  The track "Niggaz Bleed" is rumoured to be a subliminal diss to Suge Knight, however, there is no solid evidence to support this.
