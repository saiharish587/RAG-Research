Main Prem Ki Diwani Hoon: Main Prem Ki Diwani Hoon (English: "I am crazy about Prem"), is a 2003 Indian Hindi romantic comedy drama film by Sooraj Barjatya and produced by Rajshri Productions.  The film is a remake of the 1976 film "Chitchor" and features Hrithik Roshan, Kareena Kapoor and Abhishek Bachchan in the lead roles.  The movie had a worldwide release on 26 June 2003.
