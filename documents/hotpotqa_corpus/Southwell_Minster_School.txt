Southwell Minster School: Southwell Minster School is a Church of England school and sixth form located in Southwell, Nottinghamshire, England for children aged 8 to 18.  In December 2011 the School was graded Outstanding by Ofsted, in 30 out of 31 areas, among only 3% of schools in the country.  The school provides both boy and girl choristers to Southwell Minster.
