2018 Premier League Darts: The 2018 Unibet Premier League Darts will be a darts tournament organised by the Professional Darts Corporation – the fourteenth edition of the tournament.  The event will begin on Thursday 1 February at the 3Arena in Dublin and will end with the Play-Offs at The O2 Arena in London on Thursday 17 May.  This is the first year that the tournament is sponsored by Unibet.
