Trimble Township Community Forest: The Trimble Community Forest or Trimble Township Community Forest is a 1200 acre forest preserve owned by the Appalachia Ohio Alliance, a land trust located in southeast Ohio.  Formerly called Taylor Ridge, it was purchased in 2006 from the Sunday Creek Coal Company, and was formerly part of the Sunday Creek State Wildlife Area.  The land has a long history of coal, oil, natural gas, and timber production.  The AOA negotiated with the Trimble Township Trustees on its use before purchase.  It is located in Athens County, Ohio, south of Ohio State Route 78, between Glouster, Ohio and Murray City, Ohio.
