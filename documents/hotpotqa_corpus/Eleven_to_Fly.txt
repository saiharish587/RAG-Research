Eleven to Fly: Eleven to Fly is the second studio album by British electronic music group Tin Tin Out.  The album includes singles such as "Here's Where the Story Ends" with Shelly Nelson, peaking at number 7 in the UK Singles Chart in early 1998, and "What I Am" featuring Emma Bunton from the Spice Girls, which peaked at number two in the UK Singles Chart in 1999.
