Hwang Bo-ra: Hwang Bo-ra (born October 2, 1983) is a South Korean actress.  Hwang made her acting debut in 2003 and became popular after she played a quirky-looking "cup noodle girl" in a ramyeon commercial.  In 2007, Hwang played the daughter/narrator in black comedy "Skeletons in the Closet" (also known as "Shim's Family"), for which she won Best New Actress at the Busan Film Critics Awards and Director's Cut Awards.
