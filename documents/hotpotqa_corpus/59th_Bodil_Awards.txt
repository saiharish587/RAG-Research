59th Bodil Awards: The 59th Bodil Awards were held on 5 March 2006 in Imperial Cinema in Copenhagen, Denmark, honouring the best national and foreign films of 2005.  Peter Mygind og Mette Horn hosted the event.  Per Fly's "Manslaughter" won the award for Best Film.  Best Actor in a Leading Role went to Jesper Christensen, the film's protagonist.  Trine Dyrholm won Best Actress in a Leading Role for her performance in "".
