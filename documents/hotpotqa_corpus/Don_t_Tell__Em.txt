Don't Tell 'Em: "Don't Tell 'Em" is a song by American singer Jeremih from his third studio album, "Late Nights".  It features American rapper YG, and was released as the first single from the album.  The song peaked at number six on the "Billboard" Hot 100.  Outside the United States, "Don't Tell 'Em" peaked within the top ten of the charts in the United Kingdom, as well as the top twenty of the charts in Australia and Belgium.
