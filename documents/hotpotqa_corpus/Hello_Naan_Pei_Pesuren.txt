Hello Naan Pei Pesuren: Hello Naan Pei Pesuren (English: "Hello, This is ghost speaking" ) is a 2016 Indian comedy horror film written and directed by S. Baskar and produced by Sundar C., under the banner Avni Cine Makers.  Music composed by Siddharth Vipin, cinematography handled by Banu Murugan and editing done by N. B. Srikanth.
