68th Tony Awards: The 68th Annual Tony Awards were held June 8, 2014, to recognize achievement in Broadway productions during the 2013–14 season.  The ceremony was held at Radio City Music Hall in New York City, and was televised live on CBS.  Hugh Jackman was the host, his fourth time hosting.  The 15 musical Tony Awards went to seven different musicals, and six plays shared the 11 play Tony Awards.
