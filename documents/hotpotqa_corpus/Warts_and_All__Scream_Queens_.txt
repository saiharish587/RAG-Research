Warts and All (Scream Queens): "'Warts and All'" is the second episode of the second season and the fifteenth overall, of the horror black comedy series Scream Queens.  It was directed by Bradley Buecker and written by series co-creator Brad Falchuk.  It premiered on September 27, 2016 on Fox Broadcasting Company.  The episode centers on Chad's attempt to win Chanel back and facing competition from Dr. Brock.  Meanwhile, Chanel #5 finds love with a patient with severe warts around his body.
