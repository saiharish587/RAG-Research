Sandy Hollow–Gulgong railway line: The Sandy Hollow–Gulgong railway line is a railway line in eastern New South Wales, Australia.  The line forms a cross country connection from the Main North line in the Upper Hunter region to the Gwabegar line in the Central West region.  The line is approximately 125 kilometres in length.  From the Gwabegar line, trains can then ultimately reach the Main West line creating a circuitous bypass of Sydney for freight traffic heading between the west and north of New South Wales.  The line was opened in 1985.
