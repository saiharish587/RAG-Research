Night School (1981 film): Night School (released in the United Kingdom as Terror Eyes) is a 1981 American slasher film, directed by Ken Hughes and starring Rachel Ward, in her feature film debut.  The plot revolves around a series of gruesome decapitation murders targeting mostly college coeds in Boston, Massachusetts.  The film was originally to be directed by Alfred Sole, but Sole passed on the project.  Ken Hughes was ultimately brought in to direct, and "Night School" was his final film.  The music score was composed by Brad Fiedel.
