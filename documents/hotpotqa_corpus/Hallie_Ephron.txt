Hallie Ephron: Hallie Elizabeth Ephron (born March 9, 1948) is an American novelist, book reviewer, journalist, and writing teacher.  She is the author of mystery and suspense novels.  Her novels "Never Tell a Lie," "There Was an Old Woman", "Come and Find Me", and "Night Night, Sleep Tight" were finalists for the Mary Higgins Clark Award.  In 2011, "Never Tell a Lie" was made into a Lifetime television movie entitled "And Baby Will Fall", starring Anastasia Griffith, Brendan Fehr, and Clea DuVall.
