4192: The Crowning of the Hit King: 4192: The Crowning of the Hit King, a 2010 documentary film, follows the exploits and achievements of Pete Rose, a baseball player.  The film, Directed by Terry Lukemire, is narrated by J. K. Simmons.  The film stars Marty Brennaman, Tony Pérez, Mike Schmidt, and Pete Rose himself, who relay the struggle and effort it took to make history through America's favorite pastime.
