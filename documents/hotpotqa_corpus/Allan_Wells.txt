Allan Wells: Allan Wipper Wells (born 3 May 1952) is a former British track and field sprinter who became the 100 metres Olympic champion at the 1980 Summer Olympics in Moscow.  Within a fortnight of that, he also took on and beat America's best sprinters at an invitational meeting in Koblenz.  In 1981, Wells was both the IAAF Golden Sprints and IAAF World Cup gold medallist.  He is also a three-time European Cup gold medallist among many other sprint successes.
