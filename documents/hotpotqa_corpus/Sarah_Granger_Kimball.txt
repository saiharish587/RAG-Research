Sarah Granger Kimball: Sarah Melissa Granger Kimball (December 29, 1818 – December 1, 1898) was a 19th-century Mormon advocate for women's rights and early leader in the Relief Society of The Church of Jesus Christ of Latter-day Saints (LDS Church).  Kimball's involvement in the Church led to the establishment of the women's Relief Society as well as participation in the national suffrage movement.
