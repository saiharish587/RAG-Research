Debbie Harry: Deborah Ann Harry (born Angela Tremble; July 1, 1945) is an American singer-songwriter and actress best known as the lead singer of the new wave band Blondie.  Her recordings with the band reached the number one position in the United States and the United Kingdom on multiple occasions from 1978 to 1981.  She is considered the first female rapper to chart at number one in the U.S. owing to her work on "Rapture".
