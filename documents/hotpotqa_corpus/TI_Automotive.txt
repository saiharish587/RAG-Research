TI Automotive: TI Automotive develops, manufactures and supplies automotive fluid storage, carrying and delivery systems.  The company has 23,000 employees at 120 locations in 29 countries, and supplies all of the world's major automobile manufacturers.  TI Automotive serves the automotive aftermarket through Bundy, Walbro and Marwal brands.  The company's headquarters are located in Oxford, UK, with Corporate Offices based in Auburn Hills, Michigan.  In 2015 TI Automotive was acquired by Bain Capital.
