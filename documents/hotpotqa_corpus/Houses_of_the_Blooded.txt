Houses of the Blooded: Houses of the Blooded is a roleplaying game designed by John Wick released in July 2008.  Its author has described it as the "anti-Dungeons and Dragons roleplaying game," emphasising elements of the fantasy genre that D&D overlooks.  Taking a more swords and sorcery approach (rather than "generic fantasy"), the game focuses on romance, intrigue, courtly dangers, and domain management.
