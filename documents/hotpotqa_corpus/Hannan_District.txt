Hannan District: Hannan District () is one of 13 districts of Wuhan, the capital of Hubei province, People's Republic of China.  It is the least-populous of Wuhan's districts, and is situated on the northern (left) bank of the Yangtze River.  It borders the districts of Caidian to the north and Jiangxia to the east across the Yangtze, as well as the prefecture-level cities of Xianning and Jingzhou (for a sliver) to the south; it also borders the directly administered county-level city of Xiantao to the west.
