Derrill Osborn: Derrill Radcliff Osborn is a retired American fashion executive.  He descends from pioneers and ranchers who settled in the Portales area.  After serving in the U.S. Army, Osborn started working at Saks Fifth Avenue in New York City in 1964.  He stayed for ten years, working his way up from a sales person to a buyer.  Osborn briefly ran a men's clothing and antiques boutique in Beverly Hills before joining Neiman Marcus where he also worked as a buyer for six years before being appointed vice-president of men's tailored clothing.  In 2002, Osborn retired from his position at Neiman Marcus.
