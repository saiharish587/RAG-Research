Desi Arnaz: Desiderio Alberto Arnaz y de Acha III (March 2, 1917 – December 2, 1986), better known as Desi Arnaz or Desi Arnaz, Sr., was a Cuban-born American actor, musician, and television producer.  He is best remembered for his role as Ricky Ricardo on the American television series sitcom "I Love Lucy".  He co-starred on that show with Lucille Ball, to whom he was married at the time.  He and Ball are generally credited as the inventors of the syndicated rerun, which they pioneered with the "I Love Lucy" series.
