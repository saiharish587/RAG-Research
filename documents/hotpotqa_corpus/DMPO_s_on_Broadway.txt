DMPO's on Broadway: Live at DMPO's on Broadway is a VHS/DVD of the Dead Kennedys' performance at the old San Francisco avant-garde theater, DMPO's on Broadway.  It was filmed on June 16, 1984, the last night the theater was open before it was torn down.  The show is about an hour long and includes 14 songs.  Originally released on VHS in 1985 as "Live In San Francisco", it was released on DVD on August 29, 2000, under its current title.
