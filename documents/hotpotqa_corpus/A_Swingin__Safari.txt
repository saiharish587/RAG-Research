A Swingin' Safari: "A Swingin' Safari" is an instrumental composed by Bert Kaempfert (using his alias, Bernd Bertie) in 1962.  It was recorded by Kaempfert on Polydor Records and released in the United States on Decca Records, but failed to chart.  That same year, Billy Vaughn recorded it, and his cover reached number 13 on the "Billboard" charts that summer.
