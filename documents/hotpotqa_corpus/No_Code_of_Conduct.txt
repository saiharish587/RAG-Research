No Code of Conduct: No Code of Conduct is a 1998 action crime thriller film directed by Bret Michaels.  The film stars Charlie Sheen, and Martin Sheen as father-and-son vice unit detectives, along with Mark Dacascos who portrays Charlie Sheen's partner.  The film was released as a direct-to-video feature in some countries, including: Australia, Sweden, Japan, the Czech Republic, Argentina, Brazil, Azerbaijan, Russia and Turkey.  Bret Michaels is credited as Director, Screenwriter, Composer (Music Score), Actor and Executive Producer.  Charlie Sheen's credits in this release include Actor, Screenwriter and Executive Producer.
