2007 South Korea oil spill: The MT "Hebei Spirit" oil spill was a major oil spill in South Korea that began on the morning of 7 December 2007 local time, with ongoing environmental and economic effects.  Government officials called it South Korea's worst oil spill ever, surpassing a spill that took place in 1995.  This oil spill was about one-third of the size of the "Exxon Valdez" oil spill.
