Rue Lhomond: Rue Lhomond is a street in the 5th arrondissement of Paris, France.  It is located in the quartier du Val-de-Grâce and has existed since the 15th century.  It was once known as "rue des Poteries" after its Gallo-Roman pottery workshops (re-discovered in the 18th century), then from around 1600 as "rue des Pots" and finally "rue des Postes".  It was given its present name in 1867 after the priest, grammarian and scholar Charles François Lhomond (1727-1794).
