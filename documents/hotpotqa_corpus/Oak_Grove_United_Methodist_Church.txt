Oak Grove United Methodist Church: Oak Grove United Methodist Church is located in Chesapeake, VA, and is known as “the mother of all Methodist congregations in Hampton Roads”.  It has helped found four other Methodist churches in the area: Community United Methodist, Great Bridge United Methodist, Messiah United Methodist, and Oaklette United Methodist It is located on the corner of North Battlefield and Great Bridge boulevards, and was the fourth Methodist church established in the Northampton County.  The church’s history dates back to the 1700s, and since then the church has established a wide variety of ministries and has been declared a historical structure.  The church follows Methodism, a movement started by John Wesley in an effort to reform the Church of England from within.
