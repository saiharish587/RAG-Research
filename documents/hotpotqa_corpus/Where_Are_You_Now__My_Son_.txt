Where Are You Now, My Son?: Where Are You Now, My Son?  is an album by Joan Baez, released in early 1973.  One side of the album featured recordings Baez made during a US bombing raid on Hanoi over Christmas 1972.  Included on the recording are the voices of Barry Romo, Michael Allen and human rights attorney Telford Taylor, with whom Baez made her famous 1972 visit to North Vietnam.
