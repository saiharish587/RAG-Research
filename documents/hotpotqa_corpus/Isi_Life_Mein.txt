Isi Life Mein: Isi Life Mein (English: In This Life ) is a 2010 Bollywood film directed by Vidhi Kasliwal and starring Akshay Oberoi alongside Sandeepa Dhar in both their debut film.  It was released on December 24, 2010.  The movie's storyline is a play, "The Taming of the Shrew - Reborn," with "Reborn" added to underscore that the original play was modified to remove alleged misogyny.  This film was a moderate success at the box office and also earned Filmfare Award for Best Female Debut nomination for Sandeepa Dhar.
